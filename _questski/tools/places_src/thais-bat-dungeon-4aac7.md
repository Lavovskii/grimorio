 This cave is located to the south of Thais , below of the Minotaur Camp .
It isn't a really good place to gain experience points, but a rather good place to get Bat Wings since there spawn 21 Bats . 
It is an optional part of the first mission in The Scatterbrained Sorcerer Quest . Irmana will point you to this cave if you say you don't have the required materials. 
In the west of the entrance, there is a Minotaur Guard encampment, and to the east, there is a Cyclops encampment, so it's possible to found them lured in the entrance.
