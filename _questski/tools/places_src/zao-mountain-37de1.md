 The Zao Mountain is a mountain dividing Vengoth from the Zao Steppe and is located on top of Farmine . It is housed by a primitive tribe living in caves, thus Cavemen , which are led by Lazaran . They all have the Barbarian Outfits except for Ulala , who has the shaman outfit because of her job as priestess . With the Summer Update 2012 one house was added to this location, the Caveman Shelter .

Desce a montanha com {spell:Levitate} (ou parcels/caixas) e, logo depois de desceres, vai para **norte** até às trepadeiras (vines): usa-as para subir até à tribo.
