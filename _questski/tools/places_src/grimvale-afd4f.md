 Grimvale is a small island located north-east of Stonehome and Edron . It can be reached by taking the Small Boat with Cornell , located here [COORD x=33304 y=31720 z=7] .
 According to Lionet : 
 "the moon will be full around the 12th-14th each month (the 13th of each month being the prime time for the events on Grimvale). We introduced these "moon phases" to be a constant phenomenon around the 13th of each month during the whole year." ( source )
 This means depending on the time of the month you are visiting Grimvale, you either encounter fairly harmless creatures such as Badgers and Bandits , or a lot stronger creatures such as Werebadgers , Werebear and Wereboars . Also, you will either encounter Purple Nightshade or Blooming Purple Nightshade .
 The Grimvale Quest takes place here. Grimvale is also one of the spots you have to cartograph during the Cartography 101 Quest .
