Some time ago, a group of soldiers from the Royal Army , along with their commander, travelled by ship to the Greenclaw Swamp . They thought that they had landed on an island, and the commander found it to be a good location to build his home.
 During the construction of his new home, there was an earthquake, and most of the structure disappeared into the swampy ground. Sadly, the commander was in the cellar when the earthquake occurred, and the remainder of the troops left by boat, assuming he had died.
 The commander had tried to escape, but the cellar door was locked. He then decided to try and dig his way out, but that quickly proved impossible, as the ground above him was unstable swampland, and could collapse and drown him at any time.
 During his time underground, he encountered a family of Bonelords that seemed to ignore him. Over time, he became bitter about his men leaving him to die, and he now has a strong hatred for all humans. His affinity for the Bonelords has grown, and they now live in peace with each other underground.
 The man who was once the commander of the royal army is now known as the " Black Knight ", and the ruins of his unfinished home are therefore often referred to as the "Black Knight's Villa" ( here [COORD x=32821 y=31964 z=7] , behind a door opened by Key 5010 ).
 Even though the Black Knight respawns quite slowly, this is still a good place to hunt at because of the loot he drops. The Bonelords and the single Wyvern can drop some nice items too.
 Black Knight Quest takes place here. Also, you visit him during Secret Service Quest .

No andar dos {creature:Bonelord|Bonelords}, vai para **leste** até à parede e depois para **norte** até às escadas; desce. A **norte** passa o {item:Gate of Expertise} de nível 50 e, na sala grande, entra no teleporte a **norte**: leva-te à sala do {creature:Black Knight}.
