In the southeast part of Darashia , next to the magic carpet and the boat , there is an entrance to the Deeper Catacombs.
 The Catacombs hold the coffins of many old darashian families.
 In order to reach the lower levels, you must turn off some lights, use the sundial and go down the secret stairs inside a coffin in the room to the south-west. 
After going down, the stairs will dissapear and the lights will turn on again.
 A part of the Tibia Tales Quest can be found here.
