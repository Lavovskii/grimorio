## Maps 
The main area is filled with swamp trolls, with a single ghoul spawn, and skeletons behind door in the southern part of the cave. A few bats spawn in middle of the cave
Swamp Trolls and slugs spawn to the north, rats and cave rats spawn in the small rooms, and rotworms can be found in the cave to the south.
