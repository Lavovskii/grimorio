In this dungeon you'll travel through Tibia's history, starting at version 12.80 until the very early days of Tibia when only the city of Thais existed. On each area you'll find one or two creatures corresponding to the update they were implemented in, as well as references to other content implemented in that time.
 The entrance of the dungeon is through a time machine located under Thais' wall, here [COORD x=32429 y=32168 z=8] .
 This is the main area of the 25 Years of Tibia Quest .
 All of the monsters have normalized stats, so don't be surprised! Whether it's a Carnisylvan or a Wolf they have similar strength to each other.
As a hunting place, this location may be of interest mainly for FACC players with level 100+, as the exp/h is fairly representative for such a level range.
Moreover, as most of the locations contains only one kind of a monster at a time, their weaknesses may be exploited even by lower level characters.
However, the mobs drop mostly gold, basic supplies, and only sometimes jewelry – thus the hunt would be at loss most of the times.

[formal-dress-outfits-quest 0-0] Chegar ao Time Travel Dungeon

[formal-dress-outfits-quest 0-2] Sair do Time Travel Dungeon
Durante a luta há um teleporte a **norte** da sala que te tira do combate (não entres nele antes de acabar). Depois do boss, volta pelos teleportes do dungeon até à entrada e usa a máquina do tempo para regressares à torre nordeste de **Thais**; a máquina só funciona para sair depois de a teres reparado durante a {wiki:25 Years of Tibia Quest}. Da próxima vez, o teleporte roxo da sala da {creature:Memory of a Werelion} serve de atalho até perto do teleporte do Lord Retro.
