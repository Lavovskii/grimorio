## Notes 
 Senja Castle is located on the north-east side of the Ice Islands of Senja . It was once inhabited by Alawar, a perhaps evil sorcerer . 
Alawar has his treasure locked away beneath the castle. It is guarded two ways: by mercenary Minotaurs from below, and a room of fire , poison , and energy fields from above. 
Once in the treasure room, the teleports there take you to the roof of Senja Castle. There is a room below the room with fire , poison , and energy fields that contains two chests, which can be opened for a prize.
