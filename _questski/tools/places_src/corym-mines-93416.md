The Corym Mines is a dungeon where a group of Coryms are forced to mine precious metals by the Corym Ratter . This can be a good place for lower level players to get both Gold and Experience , because Stonerefiners cannot see invisible , give fair amount of experience points, and they drop up to 4 Platinum Coins . Even level 20 characters can hunt there safely with Stealth Rings , once they run through a few Corym Vanguards on the path to the mines. 
 To enter the mines, you have to leave Venore through the east exit, enter the Corym Cave here [COORD x=33073 y=32085 z=7] , go down 4 floors and then go into the teleport to the north here [COORD x=33055 y=32012 z=11] . You need permission from the locals to pass through Sealed Doors . 
 The Hidden Threats Quest takes place here. Caution: if you proceed with the quest line far enough, upon your first exit through the teleport, you will have to face miniboss Cave Spider .
