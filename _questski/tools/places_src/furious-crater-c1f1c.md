The Furious Crater is a hollow rock that lies south of Kilmaresh and east of Oramond . It cannot be reached by land, but only through a magical teleport located in the Soul War Quest hub. It has 5 floors, of which 3 are the main hunting grounds. The 4th floor (from to to bottom, as you go deeper into the crater) has a lever that leads to the ground floor, where Goshnar's Cruelty can be found. In order to travel between the floors, players must use regular and Fog portals. Access to the lower floors is obtained by collecting charges left by the creatures on the previous floor, and this access must be repeated every time the player enters the hunting ground. 
 It is also possible to reach the ground floor by levitating here [COORD x=33841 y=31867 z=6] . On this floor there are also creatures, as well as a Chest on the western corridor, here [COORD x=33810 y=31868 z=7] . By opening this chest you can get different items:
- 3 Gold Ingots 
- 1 White Gem 
- 1 Diabolic Skull 
- 1 Unicorn Figurine 
- 1 Bar of Gold 
- and possibly more.

No hub há uma fila de **Fog Portals**, cada um para uma zona com um desafio que tens de completar para chegar ao boss dessa zona.
