 Near the city of Port Hope there is a cave with many Swamp Trolls suited for players at level 8 to 20. It is also a good place for any player who wishes to collect some food. There's a cave with a single Crab also known as the Holy Crab and a Bonelord spawn so be careful when exploring the area. You can enter the cave using a hole here [COORD x=32662 y=32742 z=7] or here [COORD x=32678 y=32611 z=7] . The cave furthest south, with the Rats , Bugs and Spiders , is also connected to the Tiquanda Bandit Caves , accessible through the hole located here [COORD x=32555 y=32679 z=7] .
