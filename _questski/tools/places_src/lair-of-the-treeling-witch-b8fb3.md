 The Lair of the Treeling Witch is a dungeon north-east on Cormaya , accessed here [COORD x=33338 y=31972 z=7] . You can find Starlight Buttercups near the entrance.
 The cave is ruled by a single witch, she has a book laying around explaining her secret: How to Make Trees Come Alive (Book) .
 The Lair of the Treeling Witch is also one of the spots you have to cartograph during the Cartography 101 Quest .
