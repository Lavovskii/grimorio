The Femor Hills are located east of Carlin , south-west of Ab'Dendriel and north of Kazordoon , the city under which the hills ownership resides. Of the many creatures which can be located in the hills are Goblins , Cyclops , a Hunter , a Black Sheep and a Demon Skeleton at the peak center of the hill under ground. Also, occasional but rare orc invasions near Whitewatch Tower leave Orc Shamans , Orc Berserkers , and various other strong orcs near the tower. There is a famous tower in the heart of the hills that had many rumors about it prior to the Winter update 2007 , it is surrounded by Jungle Grass that grows under your feet if you walk on it, and there was no known way to get passed it. After the update, however, it was a part of a quest . Before the Summer update 2006 , the Demon Skeleton would spawn if you roped it, making it possible to have multiple Demon Skeletons , even though there is only one spawn. This is not possible anymore.
