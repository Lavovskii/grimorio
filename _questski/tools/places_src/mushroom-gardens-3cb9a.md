 The Mushroom Gardens are located on the lower floors of the Gnomebase Alpha . To reach them first travel to Gnomegate , take the teleporter here [COORD x=32796 y=31780 z=10] (rank II or higher in the Bigfoot's Burden Quest ) and walk to the south of Gnomebase Alpha and go down a ramp here [COORD x=32997 y=31901 z=9] . Walk a few steps north and go down some Mushroom Corkscrew Stairs here [COORD x=32991 y=31885 z=10] .
