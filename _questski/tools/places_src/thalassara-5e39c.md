 Thalassara is a distant and secluded kingdom, long isolated from the outside world by treacherous coral reefs and shifting coastal formations. Its people developed a distinct culture centred around art, scholarship, craftsmanship, and magical research under the rule of monarchs seated upon the ancient Coral Throne.
## Geography 
 Thalassara is known for its unusual coral formations, fossilised reefs, and ancient stone structures. Massive coral growths cover hillsides and cliffs, while marine fossils and remnants can be found far inland. These features have led scholars to speculate that the land may once have been beneath the sea.
## Culture and Civilization 
 Coral is deeply integrated into Thalassaran life. Through traditional techniques and magic, it is shaped into tools, ornaments, buildings, and arcane devices. In Coralmar, polished coral structures are said to glow faintly beneath the moonlight.
## The Moonstone Fragment 
 Thalassara's isolation was disrupted by the fall of a moonstone fragment in the southern part of the kingdom. The event attracted explorers, scholars, mercenaries, and other outsiders, while the discovery of Moonsilver brought further attention to the region.
## The Moonborn Threat 
 The fallen moon fragments spawned strange creatures that began spreading throughout Thalassara. The once-isolated kingdom now faces a growing threat while becoming a centre of international interest and conflict.
## Legacy and Significance 
 Thalassara's ancient traditions, mysterious landscape, and connection to coral continue to make it a unique kingdom. The arrival of the moonstone fragment and the discovery of moonsilver have begun a new chapter in its history, bringing the secluded realm into contact with the wider world.
