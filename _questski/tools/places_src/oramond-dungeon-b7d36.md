 In order to reach this dungeon, you must go through a Slime Slide in Rathleton Magistrate here [COORD x=33669 y=31887 z=5] . To be able to enter, you must have cast at least 50 votes in the Rathleton Quest . Paladins can hunt here alone, but Knights and Mages are recommended to hunt in teams. This dungeon is also erroneously called Catacombs .
 This cave has three different set-ups. The current set-up in your world depends on which one gets the most votes in the Rathleton Quest . The dungeon can be filled with either:
 A) Glooth Golems , Rustheap Golems , Worker Golems and War Golems , 
 B) Oramond Minotaurs , 
 C) Destroyers , Hellspawns , Grim Reapers , Dark Torturers , Demons , Hellhounds and Juggernauts . 
 Every 14 days, the votes will be reset and the option which was the most popular at that time will take over. You decide.
 All three possible set-ups are listed below.
 Be cautious when hunting here however, as the respawn in the dungeon is random , which means that not always the same monsters appear in the same place. After clearing a room, you will meet another creatures from current set-up when they spawn the next time you enter that room. For example a place that had only 5 Destroyers inside, next time may have 2 Hellhounds, 2 Juggernauts and a Demon!
