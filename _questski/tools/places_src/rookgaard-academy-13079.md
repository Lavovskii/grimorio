## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
The Rookgaard Academy 
 The Rookgaard Academy is the building directly north of the Rookgaard temple . It serves as a valuable location for new players, offering essential NPCs and resources.
## Ground floor 
 Seymour & Vascalir 
 Here, players can find NPCs Vascalir , who offers missions for the Rookie Guard Quest , and Seymour , who provides knowledge about Tibia to newcomers. For the library located here, see Rookgaard Libraries .
## First floor 
 The Oracle 
 Upon reaching level 8, players can speak with The Oracle and be teleported to the Island of Destiny .
## Underground floor 
## Bank 
 Paulie and the bank 
 On this floor, Paulie manages the local Bank . Additionally, players can interact with Amber , an adventurer with captivating tales. 
## Exhibition 
 The Exhibition 
 The area also features an exhibition showcasing objects and creatures found on the main continent.
## Historic 
 Before the revamp of Rookgaard and the introduction of The Rookie Guard Quest , this floor housed a training room with a Spider , Bug , Wolf , and a Troll , accessible with Key 4600 . However, this room no longer exists, and the Key 4600 is no longer obtainable.
