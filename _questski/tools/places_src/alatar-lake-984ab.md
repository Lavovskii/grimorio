 Alatar Lake is a nice location to hunt your first Bonelord without getting surrounded by other creatures like Mummies or Ghosts . 
The lake is named after Alatar , the Sage.
 It is a place of spirituality and magic. Also, one of the few Green Frog outside premium account area spawns here with the boss monster The Frog Prince . 
 The cave is divided in four rooms with a total of two Bonelords and one Ghoul . In the northern room there is also a hidden pick hole which leads to nothing but some squares in between two barriers.
