 Horestis Tomb is a tomb north-west of Ankrahmun . It is comprised of 6 hunting floors, each floor progressively more difficult. It is normally populated with Undead creatures specific to the tomb, e.g. Grave Guards , and a boss, Horestis . Each floor has a Ornate Canopic Jar that, once broken, changes the spawn of the floor to be weaker and reduced on the next Server Save . Once all jars are broken, the players who successfully broke at least one of them are granted access to the boss room. After Horestis is killed, on the next server save, the tomb's creatures will be replaced by non-undead weaker ones that are also specific to the tomb, while the undead creatures will no longer spawn, and Honour Guards will spawn all over the desert between Ankrahmun and Darashia .
 It is not known how to repopulate the tomb but it is thought that Honour Guards or Scarabs must be killed. When a Scarab is killed a phenomenon called Horestis' Curse may be witnessed: a scorpion spawns from the scarabs' body. This event can happen even if the Scarab is killed in other areas, such as Yalahar 's Arena and Zoo Quarter .

[opticording-sphere-quest 1-14] (Opcional) Como os códigos são descodificados
Os símbolos são código binário: cada grupo de 8 bits é uma letra (ASCII/UTF-8).

**Venore:** o 1.º símbolo dá *Venore*; o 2.º dá *0c041a07* e o 3.º *000a220d* (hexadecimal). Os bytes 0c 04 1a 07 00 0a 22 0d, passados a binário e combinados por **XOR** com as letras de *Venore* (repetindo V e e no fim), dão **Zathroth**.

**Gnomebase:** o binário dá *RZQALTQDE*; troca cada letra pela seguinte do alfabeto (*SARBMUREF*) e inverte: **FERUMBRAS**.

**Gray Island:** o binário dá diretamente **QJ-((**.

**Ankrahmun:** os hieróglifos dão **ZORALURK**.

**Ab'Dendriel:** o código está escondido em pixels do Menhir (descrito no livro {wiki:Tassondis's Discoveries (Book)}, na forja de Oramond).
