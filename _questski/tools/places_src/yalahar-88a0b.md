 During the God Wars the Yalahari withdrew to the northern islands and founded their city, Yalahar. The Yalahari were very intelligent and constantly sought more knowledge. They discovered an entity, Variphor , that fascinated them, but soon led to their destruction. Many died in the fighting and the rest fled. The minions of the Yalahari, who remained behind, tried to run the city, but it did not take long for it to fall into disrepair. Soon all the quarters around the Inner City fell into ruin and became occupied by lawless creatures.
 Today the Inner City is still safe, at least on the ground level. The surrounding quarters are also accessible, but adventurers should be careful of the many dangers and stay on the main roads. Yalahar is located here [COORD x=32805 y=31234 z=7] .
 There are two isles close to Yalahar: Mistrock and Fenrock . Yalahar is also the only way to access to the west side of Zao , a place called Vengoth .
 Some Yalahari, or Yalahari minions, still live in the central building in the city. They mostly keep to themselves, but may need help from visitors.

## Guards 
 There are 7 NPCs in Yalahar which guard the passages between two separate quarters. They all have the same uniform. You will need to talk to them in the In Service of Yalahar Quest . To pass between two quarters just say pass to the guard and then your destination.
