## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
The Christmas Bakery doesn't lack the season's spirit. 
 The Christmas Bakery is the only place where you can bake a Gingerbreadman from a Lump of Cake Dough , if you've learnt the Gingerbread Recipe already. Use the lump on any oven, as if you were baking a Cake .
## How to get there 
 Go past Ruprecht at Vega , barely south from Santa's House . You will find a loose ice pile, here [COORD x=31944 y=31721 z=7] .
Use a shovel to open the way down. Follow the short path to the bakery, there're no monsters. You will soon find yourself at the Christmas Bakery.
