Helheim is a small island to the north-east of Hrodmir . It is inhabited by The Undead due to a bizarre necromantic experiment led by an even more fearsome variant of the bonelord, the Braindeath . The very existence of this necromantic experiment is causing turmoil in the spirit's realm, and for that reason Hjaern is evidently disturbed. To access this island, you must have completed The Ice Islands Quest up to the Secret of Helheim. There is also a small quest on Helheim , the Berserk Potion Quest . 
 This level somewhat resembles a Skull .
