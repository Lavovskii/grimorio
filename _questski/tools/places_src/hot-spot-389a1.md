 See also: Hunting Places Help 
 Through one the Crystal Teleporters in Gnomebase Alpha . You will face Massive Fire Elementals , Dragon Lords and Vulcongras . This is the most dangerous of the regular five hunting areas in Gnomegate . 
 Knights: If solo hunting here only lure 3 to 4 monsters at a time and be ready to use health potions as the Vulcongras beams are very strong and can combo out a knight very easily. Highly recommend duo with a druid for larger pulls and more experience. Try to avoid having multiple Vulcongras able to beam you in the lure. 
 Mages: Would not recommend hunting here solo at the level 90 suggestion, go with a knight and use magma set with avalanche runes. Avoid the beams of the Vulcongras and the Dragon Lords as well as the area damage caused by all monsters here. 
 Paladins: Would not recommend hunting here as you cannot lure as many as a knight solo and you cannot run the monsters killing them 1 by 1 as there is always more monsters nearby. If hunt here avoid beams from Vulcongras and Dragon Lords .
