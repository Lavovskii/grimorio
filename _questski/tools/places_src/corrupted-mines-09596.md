The Corrupted Mines are an abandoned coal mine in the northeastern region of Newhaven . Once a source of prosperity for the island, the site was abandoned after all the ore had been extracted. Legend has it that a tragic accident buried a group of miners alive. Since then, their souls have haunted the tunnels. 
It is a hunting ground for novice adventurers who have learned the basics of combat against the Muglex goblin clan and now seek greater challenges and stronger foes.
