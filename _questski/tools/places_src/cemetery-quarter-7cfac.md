In the cemetery quarter, the dead have taken over completely and the former buildings and crypts now only serve as a place for necromantic rituals. Even though there are not normally creatures on the main road or on the ground floor of the big building some creatures like Nightmares can shoot you over the fence and occasionally Tormented Ghosts may be summoned in the building who will attack those there and on the roads.
