## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
The Shattered Isles are a group of islands west of the island of Darama , and south of the Mainland . Some centuries ago, the isles belonged to a single landmass, from which strong magic flowed. Then, dark powers from the depth of the seas attacked the peaceful land dwellers, for the control of this magic. The war shattered the isles and destroyed both civilizations, and turned the magic of the isles into chaos.

 Recently, the Shattered Islands were occupied by Thaian officials and troops. The largest of these islands, Vandura , is the location of the relative metropolis of Liberty Bay . The Shattered Islands are Vandura , Meriana , Nargor , Treasure Island , the Forbidden Islands and the Laguna Islands .
 The area is based on the Caribbean.
