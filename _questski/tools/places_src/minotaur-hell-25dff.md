 Minotaur Hell is a cave that lies north of Rookgaard . It can be accessed through the main Rookgaard cave, here [COORD x=32094 y=32137 z=7] . You will meet Snakes , Wolves , Trolls , Orcs and Minotaurs on the way to Minotaur Hell. It is a dangerous place to hunt alone, therefor it is recommended to bring a team. But if hunting alone, a good idea to take a box and let only one or two attack at a time. There is a Minotaur Mage visible in a room to the west, although it is unreachable. There is a book regarding his presence there.
 Since the Christmas Update 2006 a boss creature, Apprentice Sheng has a chance of spawning.
 The Minotaur Hell Quest is located here.
