 The Falcon Bastion , also known as the Falcon Castle, is the secret hideout of the Falcon Knights. Forgotten for a long time, the location and steps necessary have recently been discovered by heroes seeking information about The Secret Library .
 The Bastion consists of the Castle, which is in ruins, and also of an underground area. The Castle is very tricky to hunt at, because it has many small rooms and cracks that send you to the floor below it. The underground area is wider and more suitable to hunting.
 Part of The Secret Library Quest takes place here and you must be level 250 or higher to enter it.
 Many secrets are hidden inside the Falcon Bastion:
- You can find the 9 volumes of The Grand Master of Verbal Debate hidden around the castle. 
- You will find several Brass-Shod Chests in the Bastion, which can be used once every 20? hours. From them you can get a Gold Ingot , a Death Ring , Roasted Dragon Wings and possibly more. 
- You can find the Key 0909 on the upper floors and use it to unlock this [COORD x=33360 y=31350 z=7] and this [COORD x=33368 y=31331 z=7] doors on the ground floor. 
- You can find a Can of Grease here [COORD x=33364 y=31335 z=7] and use it to open the gate here [COORD x=33369 y=31316 z=3] . 
- You can break the wall in the basement here [COORD x=33375 y=31343 z=8] to access the area to the east. In it you will find the NPC Crowned Tree . Talking to it will grant you the chance to fight the Thawing Dragon Lord . 
- After talking to the Crowned Tree , you can use the Frozen Dragon here [COORD x=33361 y=31315 z=5] to wake the Thawing Dragon Lord . If the Stone Wall (Falcon Bastion) blocks your path to the Thawing Dragon Lord simply use it to break it down.
