 Meriana is a small island north of Vandura in the Shattered Isles . A small group of rebels have created the settlement of Sabrehaven here to escape the oppression of the Thaian guard in Liberty Bay .
 There is a steep mountain range along the southern coast, which serves as a disguise to prevent detection by the Thaian Guard that has occupied Vandura . Left to themselves, the small rebel group are generally undisturbed by the officials of Liberty Bay .
 The island itself is fairly safe, with only small frogs, crabs and wasps and the occasional centipede found wandering on the beaches and in the palm trees. There are spiders and tarantulas in the mountain as well as a tiger.
 On this island you can find the Meriana Gargoyle Cave here [COORD x=32389 y=32604 z=7] , a large cave that features Gargoyles , Tarantulas , Stone Golems , Blood Crabs and Thornback Tortoises 
 This island can be accessed via the Access to Meriana quest .
