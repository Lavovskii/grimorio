 The Poacher Caves/Gloomy are some caves north of the Green Claw Swamp , south in the Ferngrims Gate accessed here [COORD x=32754 y=31928 z=7] .
 Sometimes you will not find this cave here, but Poacher Caves/Game or Poacher Caves/Poachers instead. This is because they are all part of a Poacher Caves Mini World Change .
