 The Underground Glooth Factory is a the modern Glooth Factory build under Oramond to replace the old and abandoned one. Although it is directly below the old Glooth Factory there is no connection between the two. You enter the Underground Glooth Factory via the Slime Slide in the Workshop Quarter , the building east of the stairs to Upper Rathleton , here [COORD x=33651 y=31942 z=7] .
 The Oramond Factory Raids also take place here on the second floor, see its article for more details about each raid. Jaccus Maxxen's Dungeon can be accessed on the third floor of the Underground Glooth Factory. 
 To use the Slime Slide leading into the Underground Glooth Factory, you must have at least the rank of Citizen in the Rathleton Quest (300 votes cast).
 The following quests take place in this area:
- Several missions of the Oramond Quest . 
- Hero of Rathleton Quest (in Jaccus Maxxen's Dungeon ).
