 See also: A Visit to Kazordoon Kazordoon is a dwarven city located inside The Big Old One in the middle of the Tibian Continent , here [COORD x=32614 y=31923 z=7] . While the name Kazordoon is very similar to Khazad-dûm (which means "Mine of the Dwarves" in accordance with the Khuzdul, the language of the Dwarves in the Lord of the Rings universe), Knightmare has stated that it was named after the musical Brigadoon .
 Mostly inhabited by Dwarves , the city expands several levels above ground; however, the undergrounds is where it excels. The underground section of the city has entrance to seven Dwarf Mines , and numerous Ore Wagons are designed especially for transporting minerals to and from mines. There are also a number of furnaces in the area to produce Metals from the ore.
 Although not designed for human (or dwarven) transport, the carts are also capable of carrying such loads to and from main attractions, such as the entrance, depot , temple , steamship and shops . To operate the ore wagons, you will need to buy a license for 250 gold from Lokur (the Kazordoon postman), or any other ticker seller NPC .
 Gramlok Silverbeard was the first emperor of Kazordoon. Presently, the emperor is Kruzak Dustbeard , who usually stays in his throneroom high above ground.
 The city received a makeover in Update 8.2 and expansion of its hunting places and transportation system in Update 9.8 .
