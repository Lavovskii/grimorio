 The Muggy Plains is an area after the Dragonblaze Peaks accessible through an ancient lizard teleport located on the Zao Steppe . This teleporter is only available to players who have completed the first eight missions of The New Frontier Quest .
 Those plains are ruled by all kind of High class lizards. In the Muggy Plains , you can also find Chaochai , the Stampor Cave , the Temple of Equilibrium , the Fire Dragon Dojo , the Corruption Hole , one of the Brimstone Bug Caves and the ferry to Chazorai .
 The surface is a great hunting spot for paladins until they are able to hunt with Diamond Arrows . They must be wary though, as the location is occasionally infested with hundreds of Lancer Beetles notorious for their distance skill reduction.
