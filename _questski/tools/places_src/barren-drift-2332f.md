## Access 
 The Barren Drift is accessed during the Feaster of Souls Quest . 
A portal leading to the area can be found inside a rock formation north of Edron here [COORD x=33221 y=31704 z=7] .
## Notes 
- Evil Prospectors spawn after mining a small number of Luminescent Crystals . 
- When triggered, they appear in large numbers throughout the cave as part of a raid. 
- Once defeated, they do not respawn unless another raid is triggered by mining more crystals.
