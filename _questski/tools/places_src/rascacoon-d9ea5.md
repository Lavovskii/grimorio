 Rascacoon is a small island just north of Kilmaresh . The island inhabitants are called the Rascoohan and migrated to this island from a place called Washingstone . They are under constant attack by the Pirats .
 Rascacoon can be accessed through the Pirat Mine and the Exotic Cave Spider Cave , which is accessed from Issavi . It's also possible to take a shortcut there with Eustacio if he trusts you. 
 Access to the island is obtained through A Pirate's Tail Quest . Most of the quest also takes part here.
