 Nargor is the second largest island in the Shattered Isles . The island is surrounded by jagged reefs and mountains, making it particularly difficult to reach. This provides a distinct advantage to the skilled sailors that call themselves Pirates .
 Nargor is home to the largest known pirate base, and the island is riddled with caves and traps to prevent unauthorized access. There is also a cave haunted by a couple of undead pirates.
 Accessed via the Meriana Quest . 
If you have completed the quest, you can get to Nargor by going to Liberty Bay , using the password "peg leg" with Captain Waverider and asking Sebastian to bring you to Nargor.
