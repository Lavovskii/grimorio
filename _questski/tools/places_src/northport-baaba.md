 Northport is located north-east of Carlin and west of Ab'Dendriel . It is a small village known for its fish . Ask Marlene about the rumours of the village. Northport may sound a remote deserted place, but it can be handy when hunting Chickens for the addon quest , for fishing or for a hand on the Explorer Society Quest . Northport is located here [COORD x=32488 y=31610 z=7] on Tibiawiki Mapper.
