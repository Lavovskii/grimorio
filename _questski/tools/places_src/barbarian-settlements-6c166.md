 The Barbarian Settlements are located south-west of Svargrond . There are three settlements, named after the major clans of raiders: Ragnir (located in an inactive volcano), Bittermor (located under the glacier) and Krimhorn (located on a peninsula).
 You can travel to these fields talking with Buddel , asking for passage , then raider camp and confirm the answer. There is a chance he will take you to a different island, but the chances are lower after a patch. You can also reach the settlements by walking through the glacier and a small bridge to the west of Svargrond - it is a faster and cheaper way of getting there.
 These settlements are profitable when you're talking about Loot . Barbarians drop lots of addon items and you can get some Tusks , Bear Paws and Furry Clubs when you're trying to avoid Mammoths and Polar Bears .
 Bordering these 3 settlements, the glacier Formorgar hosts countless animals like Mammoths and Polar Bears and dangerous and fierce creatures such as Ice Golems and Crystal Spiders . Deep under the glacier, a strange cult has set up their camp in an old mine. It is rumoured that they are melting ancient demons there, hoping to be able to conjure a mighty archdemon one day.
