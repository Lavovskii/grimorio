## Notes 
 A number of NPCs work in the outpost to assist new adventurers: Oressa is the temple priestess; Wentworth runs the bank past the vocation rooms; Hamish , Richard , Coltrayne , and Inigo runs the shops; Garamond and Ser Tybald teach spells ; Plunderpurse runs the bank; Mr Morris is involved with the Dawnport Quest .
 There are 4 exits from the outpost: each exit will change a character's vocation and provide that character with a specific set of items the first time through and certain items each time a character walks through. See the Dawnport article for further details.
