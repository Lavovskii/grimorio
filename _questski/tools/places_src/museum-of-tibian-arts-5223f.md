The Museum of Tibian Arts (MOTA) is an exhibition of several Tibian art pieces, curated by Gareth . Inside the museum, however, strange things may happen. 
 On its second floor, the statues will come to life at Night , making this a rather dangerous place because of the Animated Guzzlemaw in there. For this reason, low level players visiting the museum should avoid wandering around this floor at night. On the third floor, Goldhanded Cultists and Goldhanded Cultist Brides seem to be up to something dark. 
 One mission of the Cults of Tibia Quest takes place here.
