 Zao Orc Land is found on the Zao Steppe . It is accessible from Farmine by way of the mountain pass. The land is split into three sections. The northwest end has some small rocky hills bordering a wooden fort that holds weaker and strong Orcs. Central base contains many broken down buildings and the weaker Orcs. The east end is a semi-circle of siege equipment run by stronger Orcs. The Orcs must always be prepared for war and are well equipped with personal weapons and siege weapons to fight the Lizards. 
 Be warned that the east end is full of stronger orcs on the ground level and that the ones that appear during raids and can be lured to the central base. 
It was implemented in the Christmas Update 2009 .
 The Orcs are led by Minotaurs of the Mooh'Tah philosophy, particularly by Curos . Ever since arriving on Zao have been embattled against the Lizards and have fought for the ground that they now live in. While they mostly fight against the Lizards they have also attacked the Cavemen who have since fled to the mountains. Despite this violence they have also formed a symbiotic relationship with some of the native creatures such as the Gnarlhound with whom they hunt for food and provide mutual protection, and occasionally eat. 
 The Fort is always accessible with a backdoor entrance reachable after levitating up here [COORD x=33066 y=31306 z=6] .
 The first mission of Unnatural Selection Quest takes place in front of the main gate of the fortress. One Tome of Knowledge is located in the basement.
