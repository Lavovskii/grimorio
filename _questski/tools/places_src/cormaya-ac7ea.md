 Cormaya is a small island south of Edron ( here [COORD x=33307 y=31999 z=7] ). It can be accessed either from the steamship in Kazordoon or Farmine or the ship in Edron . It can also be accessed by traveling through the Edron Rotworm Cave . Because of access to experience in the two hunting locations, a large shoreline, and relatively few surface monsters, Cormaya is a place of relaxation and mana sitting. Pemaret , the local fisherman, can also take players to the sage Eremo who sells a blessing , Amulets of Loss , and promotional spells . Cormaya is home to several individual houses and a series of flats, the prices of which have risen slightly since the new hunting location.
 Raiding parties of Dwarf Soldiers from Kazordoon and its new mining site occasionally appear and roam the eastern part of the island.
## Cormaya Garden 
 Cormaya Garden is a lovely place to be on the island of Cormaya . It can be found south of the boat , around the backside and just east of the Castle of the White Dragon Guildhall . Below the garden is a staircase to the steamship . The garden is a place for rest and relaxation. It can be used as a place to take a break from hunting, as creatures can't get to it.
