## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
The Maze of Bazir is a part of the Pits of Inferno in which Bazir 's throneroom is found. Bazir is known as the lord of lies , thus the principal theme of the maze is deceit . This theme is exhibited in a few ways, such as:
- There are many levers throughout the maze, but all of them work against the player except the final one, concealed beneath an orc corpse, which opens a portal to the throneroom (the goal). 
- There are many other leads that serve to steer explorers off-course, for example the locked door which has a hole leading down to a teleport. 
- The goal's lever itself is concealed beneath a non-decaying orc corpse. 
- The maze has many "fake" monsters: "Demon" goblins , fake phantasms . 
 Although the maze has only 3 floors, on the top floor of the maze there are rope holes. These will lead to the Pits of Inferno Dragon Lair and there is no way back.
