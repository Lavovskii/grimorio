 Fenrock is an island, west of Yalahar . There are also other islands nearby: Mistrock and Vengoth . It can be reached via the NPC Maris who is located on the docks in the Foreigner Quarter of Yalahar. Underneath this island, you can find the hidden city of Beregar . There is no mailbox near the surface, but one is just a short boat ride away in Mistrock . The second floor is probably the best place to hunt Tortoises (for their creature products ).
