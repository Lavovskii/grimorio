 The entrance to Thais Dragon Lair is situated north from Thais on third level under ground ( here [COORD x=32349 y=32112 z=10] ). Although the route is a little bit complicated you probably won't encounter any creatures apart from Trolls and Rotworms . The route is explained in Naginata Quest spoiling information. If you're the owner of Dark Mansion this is a great hunting ground for you, since it's easily accessible from the basement so you can make a quick trip to the house to drop loot and gather supplies. 
 In the Thais Dragon Lair you can find 3 Dragon Lords (one spawns near the center of the cave, this one spawns REALLY fast, and two in the quest room), 2 Dragon Lord Hatchlings spawning in the northeast corner and 3 Dragons and 4 Dragon Hatchlings spawning all over the room. The dragon lord boss Demodras may rarely spawn as well. 
 It's a good hunting place for mid-level Free account players. 
 Naginata Quest takes place there.
