 The Isle of the Mists is a small island, located in the sea east of the Plains of Havoc . Also known as "Druid Island", since only Druids are able to go there. The monks' boss, Fernfang , spawns on this island. After that, you can enter the island by stepping on the Magic Forcefield situated south of the Plains of Havoc temple, here [COORD x=32831 y=32295 z=7] .
 There is also a quest , and some red roses spawn on the second floor of the island.
