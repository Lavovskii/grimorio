 The Dryad Gardens is a dungeon south-west of the boat on Cormaya , accessed through using the Stony Pond here [COORD x=33264 y=32014 z=7] . There is a Warning Sign at the entrance of the cave: 
 Citizens, beware: Don't drink from the pond! Survivors tend to hallucinate strongly and tell of strange gardens infested with evil tree creatures. - The Magistrate of Cormaya 
 The Dryad Gardens is also one of the spots you have to cartograph during the Cartography 101 Quest .
