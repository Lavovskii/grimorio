The Killer Caiman Cave can be found southwest of the central Orc camp in Zao , here [COORD x=33137 y=31448 z=7] .
 A perfect hunting place for a knight at level 60+ with 80/75 skills. You can easily get over 150k exp/h by using Brutal Strike . Bring Mana Potions and some Strong Health Potions that might come in handy if you encounter 3 or more Killer Caimans . You can make some profit even if you aren't picking up the Obsidian Lance .
