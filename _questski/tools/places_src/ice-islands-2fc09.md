 The Ice Islands are two sets of islands to the north of the main Tibia continent, in the Nordic Ocean. Because of the islands' low temperature, they are permanently covered with snow and ice. They are located:
- Southern: Northwest of Carlin, here [COORD x=32075 y=31669 z=7] ; 
- Northern: North of Carlin, here [COORD x=32287 y=31271 z=7] . 
 One set of islands is the Southern Ice Islands, which are very small and accessible to players with free accounts. The Northern Ice Islands (or just Ice Islands) were added in the Summer 2007 update and include many larger islands and the city of Svargrond .
## Southern Ice Islands 
 The Southern Ice Islands consist of Folda , Senja ,  Vega , and the Arctic Faun's Island . Folda has a large dungeon with Frost Trolls , Rotworms , Minotaurs and if you go deep enough, Slimes , Minotaur Archers , Minotaur Mages , minor Orcs and Water Elementals . There are even Bonelords , Scorpions , Snakes and all kinds of minotaurs in the hidden cave. 
 You can go to any of the three main islands taking the Nordic Ferry [COORD x=32235 y=31678 z=7] located at the northwest of Carlin . The price is 20 gp from the Continent to any island, 10 gp , island-to-island and the passage back to the Continent is free.
 The Arctic Faun's Island can be accessed if you take a shortcut from captain Charles in Port Hope and end up shipwrecking there (this is random). 
 Locations of Senja [COORD x=32163 y=31656 z=7] , Folda [COORD x=32023 y=31579 z=7] , Vega [COORD x=31991 y=31700 z=7] , Arctic Faun's Island [COORD x=32115 y=31739 z=7] and Nimmersatt's Lair [COORD x=32248 y=31588 z=7] .
## Northern Ice Islands 
 These were added for premium players in the Summer Update 2007 and consist of Grimlund , with the Chakoya settlement of Inukaya , Helheim , Hrodmir , with the city of Svargrond , Krimhorn , Nibelor , Okolnir and Tyrsung . Many new creatures and quests can be found in these areas.
