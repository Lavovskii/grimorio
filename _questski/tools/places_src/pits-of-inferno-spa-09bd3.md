 PoI Spa (sometimes called DT City) is a large room that is found after passing Hall of the Four Ways and Pits of Inferno Levers . It contains creatures such as Diabolic Imps ,  Nightmares ,  Defilers ,  Destroyers ,  Hellfire Fighters ,  Phantasms ,  Dark Torturers ,  Plaguesmiths ,  Blightwalkers  and  Fire Devils .
 The creatures here, especially Dark Torturers, drop really great loot, which makes this a good place to gain some profit.
