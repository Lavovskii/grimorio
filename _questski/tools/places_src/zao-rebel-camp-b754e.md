 The rebel camp in Zao is the headquarters of the Lizard resistance that wants to overthrow the Emperor of Razachai and hinder the spread of the corruption in the Zaoan environment. It is located below the Souleater Mountains in northern Zao ( here [COORD x=33213 y=31058 z=9] ). The rebel camp has a Royal Mailbox as well as a postman NPC, Ezean .
