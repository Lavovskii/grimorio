 The Drillworm Caves can be found in the southern part of the Kazordoon city mine , near the Steamship ( here [COORD x=32657 y=32010 z=13] ).
 Browse Map [COORD x=32658 y=32012 z=13] 
 Upper floor of the cave can be considered as a free account alternative to Edron Earth Elemental Cave .
Lower floor consists of two separate caves, western and eastern. Western cave is the easier one - you will encounter only two Wyverns next to the entrance. Paladins can hunt here with ease from level 75, using Fire Wall Runes if necessary. Eastern cave is more tricky. There are three Drillworms close to the entrance, which can be lured one by one, but it will limit the room for maneuver. What's more, there are no Giant Spiders in that cave.
