 Cyclopolis is a very large dungeon located behind the mountains north of Edron ( here [COORD x=33251 y=31698 z=7] ). It was built by the Cyclopes , and reaches several floors below surface.
 Many creatures share this city with the Cyclopes , including Elves , Wild Warriors , Minotaur Archers , Dwarf Soldiers , Elf Scouts , Minotaur Mages , Minotaur Guards , an Elf Arcanist , Orc Berserkers and Orc Leaders ! Further below the surface you can even find Fire Elementals and the fast and mighty Behemoth . There is a ramp up to the Behemoth Quest on floor 8.
 On floor 4 you can see a lot of Giant Swords north-east of the magic forcefields.

[behemoth-quest 3-4] Sair de Cyclopolis
Volta pelo mesmo caminho: sai da sala final pela porta de nível 80, desce as escadas e regressa pela passagem estreita até às escadas da plataforma pequena. Daí sobe os 5 andares de Cyclopolis seguindo os mapas da secção 1 ao contrário, até à entrada a **norte de Edron**.
