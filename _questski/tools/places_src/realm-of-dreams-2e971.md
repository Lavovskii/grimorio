 This area can be accessed during the Realm of Dreams Quest . It consists of a small island [COORD x=32821 y=31504 z=7] between Vengoth and Mistrock with an underground system. North-east of the island there is a house with someone sleeping on a bed. To the north-east there is a portal which teleports visitors to where they came from.
 There are no creatures or NPCs in the Realm of Dreams.
