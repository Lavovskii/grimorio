 The Mal'ouquah or Green Djinn Fortress is the gloomy fortress of the Green Djinn located in the Kha'zeel (Ankrahmun Desert). This is where Malor maintains his troops to fight Gabel and his Blue Djinns and Marids , hoping to eventually defeat them entirely and overcome the Ashta'daramai . Because of the war between the Marids and Efreets , they will not trade with anyone they do not trust entirely. 
 Once you have gained their trust by completing the Green Djinn Quest , you can buy and sell from Alesar (weapons) and Yaman (magical items). 
 Also housed in the Mal'ouquah with the Green Djinns and Efreets are Gargoyles , Stalkers , Mummys , Scorpions and Hyaenas . 
 To reach the Mal'ouquah, simply follow the passage near the oasis into the mountains ( here [COORD x=33101 y=32657 z=7] ).
 Note: only players with level 30+ may pass the entrance doors.
