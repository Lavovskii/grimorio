The Nomad cave is a quite large cave located below the mountain pass between Darashia and Ankrahmun . To enter it, you must start Arito's Task . The cave is a good hunting place for low level players.
 In a room, you can find a spawn of some coconuts , bananas , grapes and 2 Small Oil Lamps . All over the cave you can also stumble over different pillows , and Water Pipes .
 Once you have performed Arito's task you can dig for the secret entrance, similar to the way you would look for a tomb. You may pass through the back door to enter the cave, without sacrificing a scimitar. (this is after going back to muhad ).
## Location 
 There are two entrances, the first official entrance is in the mountains between ank and dara, just east of the passage to Darashia , here [COORD x=33205 y=32544 z=7] . 
 To access this entrance you need to start Arito's Task . After that you'll need to place a scimitar on that crack, and use a vial of water 2sqm east from the crack, and a secret entrance will appear. 
 The second "secret" entrance is near the first entrance, on the desert. You'll need a shovel to open the hidden hole. Inside there is a door that can only be opened if you have completed Arito's task . So completing it is necessary to use this entrance. The hole can be found around here [COORD x=33214 y=32553 z=7] NOTE: To open the hole you might have to dig multiple times on the correct sand tile.
