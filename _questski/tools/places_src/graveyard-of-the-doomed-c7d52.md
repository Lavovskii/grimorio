An overview of the graveyard 
 The Graveyard of the Doomed is a small graveyard deep inside the underground tunnels of Mount Sternum . Skeletons , Rats and Ghouls roam here. A long time ago, it was a municipal graveyard of Thais .
 Some graves are connected to the Royal Archives , whereas others serve as hints for a quest or are nothing but jokes.
