North-east of Liberty Bay on the island Vandura there's a cave in which voodoo cultists are hiding. The entrance is hidden in the yard of one of the sheds, here [COORD x=32359 y=32721 z=7] . The cultists have planted a Jungle Maw near the entrance to prevent anyone from getting in. As well as cult members, other fearsome monsters have made their home inside the cave. 
 This area is one of the few areas home to the Voodoo Cultists , and as such is a good place to hunt for Rope Belts . The cave is also home to the Mana Draining creatures from Bonelords and Elder Bonelords , which may discourage low-level Mages from hunting there as the damage dealt can easily overwhelm them as they struggle to heal whilst their mana is being drained.

[the-shattered-isles-quest 6-3] Chegar aos Black Sheep da Cult Cave
A entrada da {wiki:Cult Cave} fica em Vandura, a **nordeste** de Liberty Bay: é um buraco escondido no pátio de um dos barracões, guardado por uma {wiki:Jungle Maw|Jungle Maw}. Desce pelo buraco; no andar de baixo, sobe pela escada (*Up the Ladder*) até ao piso térreo da gruta, onde os {creature:Black Sheep} estão numa sala redonda (D no mapa), com uma {creature:Witch}. Para saíres, volta pelo mesmo caminho até ao buraco da entrada (leva a {item:Rope}).
