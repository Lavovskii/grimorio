 A mountain made of rock and ice. It is located in Tyrsung . Giants inhabitate this place. This place is a good location for mid level players (40 to 60) to hunt. Without bonus you can easily make 40k/h exp, and with bonus, 65k/h. If you have capacity to carry all the Frost Giant Pelt , you can make a good profit, every one can be sold for 160gps, and hunting for two hours you can get about 20 to 25 Frost Giant Pelts .
 It is the home of the Skeleton Decoration Quest .
