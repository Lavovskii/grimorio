 The Poacher Caves/Poachers are located north of the Green Claw Swamp , south in the Ferngrims Gate accessed here [COORD x=32754 y=31928 z=7] .
 Sometimes you will not find this cave here, but Poacher Caves/Game or Poacher Caves/Gloomy instead. This is because they are all part of a Poacher Caves Mini World Change .
 If you skip the Wyvern cave, this is one of a few places that can sustain paladins for almost unlimited hassle-free hunt time. This is due to the abundance of dropped arrows and food.
Because neither Poachers nor Hunters can sense invisible , with a continuous use of Stealth Rings , you will not receive any damage. This fact makes the hunt hassle-free in contrast to nearby Orc Fortress , where you have to be wary of Orc Leaders . Moreover, since Hunters have very high exp/hp ratio and low armor, it is an ideal place for low-level characters to quickly gain lots of experience.
