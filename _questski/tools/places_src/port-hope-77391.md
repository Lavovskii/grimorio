 Port Hope is a beautiful city in the jungle west of Ankrahmun . The city was at first settled by a few sailors who shipwrecked here when traveling from Venore to Thais , and is now inhabited with colonizers from all over the Tibian continent .
Surrounded by the hostile jungle full of enemies and threats people have never seen before, the town is in constant need of valiant protectors and explorers. Hunters and adventurers alike meet here for their expeditions into the jungle and share the tales of their latest missions. For the junior hunter, in town there is a hole to a cave with minor monsters.
It's part of the Kingdom of Thais
