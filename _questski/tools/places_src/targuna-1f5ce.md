 Targuna is the island that new players progress to after leaving Newhaven . It is intended for levels 8 to 20. The island leads to three hunting grounds:
- Hidden Lizard Temple , down a staircase to the north. 
- Crimson Court , through the portal in Emiliana 's house. 
- Aragonia , via the sea turtle on the west shore.
