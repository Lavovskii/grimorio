This is the cave you will find in the northern part of the Edron Troll-Goblin Peninsula . A quite good start hunting place for any vocation and for Paladins even better, because they can hunt with the spears dropped by trolls. There is also a little quest in this cave.
In the second level underground, southwest, there is a small room that seems to be dedicated to Goblins's worship. There is a Goblin statue in the middle of it, and rubbish all around. Northwest, there is a room with some fishes on the ground; possibly, they are preys of the bears that live there.
