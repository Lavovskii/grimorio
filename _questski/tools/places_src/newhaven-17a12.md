 Newhaven was first mentioned on October 8, 2025 as the new beginner's island. It was implemented in Update 15.12.c7d92c .
 Newhaven is a peaceful coastal settlement located just east of Bounac . It serves as the first home for new adventurers entering the world of Tibia. Here, players meet a handful of helpful residents: a trader who buys local loot and sells hunting supplies such as potions and ammunition, a banker, quest givers and a spell trainer who offers a few basic spells for each vocation.
 The village has two exits: 
- to the north lies the hunting ground, where adventurers can face various monsters to gain experience and prepare for the challenges ahead 
- to the west, the exist remains sealed by a special level door, only opening for those who have reached level eight. Behind it waits a boat that sails most vocations to Thais peninsula, a one-way journey into the true dangers of the mainland. Only Monks are taken to Blue Valley instead. 
 Once adventurers leave Newhaven , they can never return. The island is a safe haven of learning, guidance and preparation for a new generation of Tibian heroes.
