 Hellgorge is deep cave on Goroma housing some of the Ruthless Seven's most brutal minions. The cave is riddled with the same notorious cult worshipping the archdemon Ghazbaran , and their involvements in the melting of the Formorgar Glacier may be of similar efforts to the planned resurrection of the archdemon Morgaroth .
 Hellgore was officially named on the Autumn Patch 2011 private testing board by a CipSoft member. It was then the only location outside of the Pits of Inferno and Demon Forge which contained a Hellhound : part of this update was to make hunting hellhounds more accessible and adding Paw and Fur Society tasks for hunting them. On the teaser thread that introduced new Mini World Changes that were going to be implemented on Winter Update 2011 which included Fire from the Earth , this location was again referred as Hellgore . However, with the introduction of Cyclopedia's Map with Summer Update 2018 , this was officially named Hellgorge .
 It is not exactly known what the Hellgorge area includes. It may refer to the entire cave, the final demonic area or just the Hellhound spawn. In this article we will assume it refers to the whole cave.
 Deep Hellgorge (commonly referred to as Goroma Demons ) is arguably the most time- and cost- efficient place to complete the Paw and Fur Society demon task, with two spawns of four Demons and around 10 demons in total, and with few little creatures in between spawns.
 Note that this cave does not lead to the Forbidden Island Ramoa ; the Goroma Volcano is the only dungeon connecting Goroma to Ramoa.
