This is the cave you will find in the southern part of the Edron Troll-Goblin Peninsula . It is inhabited by goblins , bugs and wasps . The goblins that live there cultivate mushrooms in some muddy fields. Oddly enough, many piles of bones of trolls are found in one of the caves. Possibly, trolls had inhabited there before and were killed by the goblins. There is also a little quest in this cave. 
It is a quite good start hunting place or if you got bored from the trolls in the north. Good for Paladins , because they can skill with the Small Stones dropped by goblins.
