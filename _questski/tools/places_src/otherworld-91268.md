 The Otherworld (a.k.a. The Void ) is a collection of dungeons introduced in the Summer Update 2016 . To reach it you have to enter one of the vortices, located in various places around Tibia. These vortexes are located:
- North of Ankrahmun , here [COORD x=33168 y=32638 z=7] 
- South-west of Svargrond , here [COORD x=32165 y=31293 z=6] 
- West of Zao Steppe , here [COORD x=33034 y=31370 z=7] 
 They will take you to the same dungeon, only changing the location where you arrive. Only 1 of these 3 portals stays open at any time, the location changes randomly every 2 hours (starting at Server Save ) but it's possible that the current location is "drawn" and hence the vortex won't change. 
 See also: Route:Otherworld 
 There are also two separate otherworlds with weaker creatures. Their entrances are near the Dwarf Bridge (see Otherworld (Dwarf Bridge) ) and north of Edron (see Otherworld (Edron) ).
 The main Otherworld is where the Heart of Destruction Quest takes place.

## ROUTE PAGE (Route:Otherworld)
The Otherworld is a large area where it's easy to get lost, specially since there are three different entrances and some passages may change and prevent you from leaving through the same place you came in. A Spying Eye can be used to check which one of the three Glowing Vortexes are available to access or leave the Otherworld. On this page you'll find some of the common routes necessary to move around this area, specially if you're doing missions of the Heart of Destruction Quest .
 If you get lost in the Otherworld , you can either go to the Vortex Hub and use a Temple Teleport there to leave, or use the routes to the hub to backtrack your way to the exit that is available at the moment. There are also other vortexes around the world that are not mentioned in this page, for example here [COORD x=32103 y=31402 z=13] . These can be used if you get trapped in a room during an entrance change, however, the exactly locations in the Otherworld where they will send you to must be checked.

## The Vortex Hub 
 There is a vortex hub where players are sent after killing one of the 5 charge bosses, here [COORD x=32162 y=31328 z=11] . This hub is a safe area which you can go to in case you want to leave using a Temple Teleport or if you need to wait for rescue. 
 The northern red vortex marked as the exit will send you to the vortex shown below as the Hub's entrance, here [COORD x=32162 y=31327 z=11] . To leave the Otherworld from the Hub, you can take this vortex and then follow the path backwards towards the Darama entrance below, even if the entrance isn't there.

Se tiveres um {item:Spying Eye}, usa-o: diz-te onde está aberto o vórtice. Sem ele, vai à entrada mais próxima e confirma se o vórtice lá está. [heart-of-destruction-quest 1-3] Como sair do Otherworld (vale para toda a quest)
A forma mais segura é ir para a **sala de teleportes (hub)** do Otherworld (secção 7), que é zona segura: daí podes usar um {wiki:Temple Teleport} (comprado na Store) para voltares ao templo da tua cidade. Se não tiveres um, entra no vórtice vermelho **norte** do hub (a saída): apareces no vórtice de entrada do hub ({coord:32162,31327,11}). Daí segue o caminho da imagem: abre caminho para **sul** e **desce 2 andares**, em direção à entrada de **Darama** (o vórtice a norte de Ankrahmun, {coord:33171,32633,7}). É o mesmo caminho que usas quando começas em Ankrahmun. Este caminho serve **sempre** para sair, seja qual for a entrada aberta nesse momento. Já no deserto, caminha para **sul** até Ankrahmun e apanha o barco do {npc:Captain Sinbeard}.
