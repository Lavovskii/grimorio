 The Hrodmir Stone Golem Caves are two caves implemented during Updates/9.6 , a lot later than the initial release of Svargrond . They were updated during Updates/9.86 . They were released and extended because the Killing in the Name of... Quest tasks for Stone Golems and Gargoyles drove the need for good hunting places in their respective level ranges.
 One cave is located here [COORD x=32065 y=31194 z=7] and has a second level since Updates/9.86 . The other one is located here [COORD x=32081 y=31375 z=8] .
