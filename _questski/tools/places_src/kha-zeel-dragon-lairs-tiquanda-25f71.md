 The Kha'zeel Dragon Lairs/Tiquanda are two sets of dragon lairs much like the ones accessed through Kha'labal , but then accessed through Tiquanda instead. The northern lairs can be accessed here [COORD x=33009 y=32604 z=7] , and the southern ones here [COORD x=32983 y=32646 z=7] .
 The northern set of lairs consists of three lairs filled with 8 Dragons , 4 Dragon Hatchlings , and 1 Dragon Lord .
 The southern set of lairs consists of four lairs filled with 21 Dragons , 9 Dragon Lords , 4 Dragon Hatchlings , and 2 Dragon Lord Hatchlings .
 Home of The Great Dragon Hunt Quest . The Kha'zeel Dragon Lairs/Tiquanda are also one of the spots you have to cartograph during the Cartography 101 Quest .
