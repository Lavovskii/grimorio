Goroma is the largest of the Forbidden Islands and is home to a curious Voodoo Cult . The island is dry and hot, with a large volcano being the most distinguishable landmark.
 The waters surrounding the island are extremely dangerous and any ship sailing nearby may easily get caught in a storm or tidal wave, and become shipwrecked on this dangerous island.
 Traveling from Liberty Bay to Thais one who is at least level 20 may eventually get lost in a tropical storm and become stranded on this island.
 If you are shipwrecked, the only way to leave is to collect 30 Wood to repair your ship, but be careful! You might become the next sacrifice of " The Cult ", or be murdered by the lost souls of previous shipwrecked crews.
 It is advisable to hunt in groups here, to reduce the supply cost.
 The deeper part of Goroma is known as Hellgorge . 
 Note: the volcano contains demonic creatures such as Hellfire Fighters , Demons , Diabolic Imps and Dragonlings during the Fire from the Earth Mini World Change . To reach Ramoa it is advisable to wait for the mini world change to end.
 To pass the electric sparks to deeper Goroma you must complete the VoodooMaster Quest .
