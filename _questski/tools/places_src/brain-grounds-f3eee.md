The Brain Grounds consist of an organic environment which seems to be controlled by a central Brain. It can be accessed through an invisible teleport north of the Jakundaf Desert . It is a popular hunting spawn due to the big amount of creatures that can be lured into the bigger rooms at the same time and the fact that it can be hunted in several different party combinations or just solo.
 There are three floors with increasing difficulty, and a portal [COORD x=31904 y=32347 z=9] on the second floor that brings players to the Netherworld , which is sometimes used as part of the hunting area if the player or party hunting is too strong for the Brain Grounds alone.
 Access to this area is obtained as part of the Feaster of Souls Quest .
