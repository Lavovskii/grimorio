 Dawnport was one of the former beginner's island, first mentioned on August 21, 2014 and implemented in Update 10.55 . It was located southwest of Ghostlands , here [COORD x=32062 y=31895 z=7] , until it was removed from the game. 
 Dawnport aimed to introduce the player to the game by allowing them to test each vocation to get a feel before they make their final decision. In addition, there were many areas to explore, a few quests and tasks to complete and a wide variety of creatures to hunt.
 Dawnport replaced Rookgaard as the beginner's island, but Rookgaard still remained accessible to accounts with at least one character on Mainland already, with Inigo bringing players there.
 Unlike Rookgaard , there was no premium area in Dawnport. All players could access all areas. Players had to leave Dawnport when they hit level 20, but they could leave as early as level 8. When you died on Dawnport, you would keep all your items, unless you were level 8 or higher, in which case the usual death penalty applied.
 Upon reaching level 20, the player would be teleported to the Temple underneath the starting area after 5 minutes. If the player walked into the starting area before the timer ended, they would also get teleported downstairs and would be unable to go back up to visit the shops. Therefore, it was advised to return to the starting area just before reaching level 20 to take care of errands, such as selling and buying items as well as organizing the items you wanted to take with you to the mainland.
 Dawnport offered a quick way to reach level 20, as one could reach from 25k exp/h (as Knight ), up to 55k exp/h (as Sorcerer ), depending on chosen class and if free or premium account. Especially for free account players, it was recommended to stay in Dawnport until almost level 20, as getting to that level on mainland could prove to be tough, way more dangerous and way slower in comparison.
 A total of 2000 Gold Pieces could be taken with you to the mainland, 1500 deposited in your bank account and 500 cash in your backpack. Any extra gold had to be either thrown away or donated to the NPC that warps you to the mainland. To maximize your starting funds on the mainland, filling your backpack with Brass Helmets (0.9 g/oz.) or Plate Shields (0.7 g/oz) was recommended, as they offered the best "Gold to Weight"-ratio of the farmable items in Dawnport.
## Vocations 
 Dawnport was created to allow new players to test the different vocations before venturing onto the mainland. Changing vocations was possible and done by stepping onto specific pedestals designated for each vocation. To choose or switch vocations, players needed to step onto any of the designated Vocational Pedestals , which could be found in the following directions:
- Heading north to test Druid by stepping on the Druid Pedestal ( here [COORD x=32063 y=31882 z=5] ). 
- Heading east to test Knight by stepping on the Knight Pedestal ( here [COORD x=32074 y=31890 z=5] ). 
- Heading south to test Sorcerer by stepping on the Sorcerer Pedestal ( here [COORD x=32063 y=31901 z=5] ). 
- Heading west to test Paladin by stepping on the Paladin Pedestal ( here [COORD x=32056 y=31890 z=5] ). 
- Heading upstairs and to the west to test Monk by stepping on the Monk Pedestal ( here [COORD x=32060 y=31895 z=4] ). 
 Each vocation received a starting set of items. When changing vocations, players would lose their original equipment (weapon and shield, and also the Jacket when changing to the Monk) and gain new items specific to their new vocation. The items provided were as follows:
- Druids received a Spellbook of the Novice and The Chiller , along with 1 Meat , 2 Light Stone Shower Runes , 2 Lightest Missile Runes , 10 Mana Potions , and 2 Small Health Potions . 
- Knights received a Dagger and a Wooden Shield , along with 1 Meat , 2 Mana Potions , and 10 Small Health Potions . 
- Sorcerers received a Spellbook of the Novice and The Scorcher , along with 1 Meat , 2 Light Stone Shower Runes , 2 Lightest Missile Runes , 10 Mana Potions , and 2 Small Health Potions . 
- Paladins received a Bow and 100 Simple Arrows , along with 1 Meat , 5 Mana Potions , 7 Small Health Potions , 1 Light Stone Shower Rune , and 1 Lightest Magic Missile Rune . 
- Monks received a Light Jo Staff , 5 Mana Potions , 7 Small Health Potions , 2 Light Stone Shower Runes , and 2 Lightest Missile Runes . 
 Players could choose to become each vocation and collect a total of 4 Meat , 7 Light Stone Shower Runes , 7 Lightest Missile Runes , 32 Mana Potions , and 28 Small Health Potions . However, note that you could not claim more than one set of supplies per vocation.
 The key weapons and shields, such as the Light Jo Staff , Dagger , Wooden Shield , The Scorcher , The Chiller , Spellbook of the Novice , Bow , and a Quiver with 100 Simple Arrows , would be lost when changing vocations and replaced with the items for the newly chosen vocation. This applied even if those items were obtained through looting; they would still be removed from your inventory during the vocation change.
 Paladins could leave the pedestal and either throw their arrows on the ground and return immediately for 100 additional arrows, or use the arrows they had and return when they need more at any time.
 All vocations would receive basic spells , which would be carried over to the mainland. More spells could be tried out for free from level 8 onward from the NPCs in the Adventurers' Outpost. 
## Spells 
 The following spells are learned by all players, when they become a vocation. These spells would be swapped upon leaving the Adventurers' Outpost through the respective vocation gate. This was done to address the issue of characters leaving Dawnport with spells irrelevant to their vocation, as well as to allow older characters fair access to those spells .
- Knights learned Bruise Bane . 
- Paladins learned Magic Patch , Arrow Call . 
- Sorcerers learned Magic Patch , Buzz , Scorch , Lightest Missile Rune and Light Stone Shower Rune . 
- Druids learned Mud Attack , Chill Out , Magic Patch , Lightest Missile Rune and Light Stone Shower Rune . 
 In addition, all players could learn spells for free on Dawnport, if they met the required level, from 8 to 18. Druids and Sorcerers could try out spells offered by Garamond , while Paladins and Knights could try out spells offered by Ser Tyb
