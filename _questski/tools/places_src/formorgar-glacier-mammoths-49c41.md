There are two places on/near the Formorgar Glacier where you can hunt Mammoths . One is west of Svargrond , near The Bloodtusks lair. The other one is south-west of the city and has more Mammoths .
 To reach the smaller Mammoth hunting area you can take two routes.
- Walk west from Svargrond , cross a small bridge and levitate up 5 times here [COORD x=32172 y=31161 z=7] 
- Walk north from Svargrond by going up two ramps here [COORD x=32245 y=31112 z=7] and walk west to a frozen waterfall here [COORD x=32148 y=31080 z=5] . Levitate up 3 times. 
 To reach the bigger Mammoth area you can also take two routes.
- Walk west from Svargrond , cross a small bridge and go up a ramp here [COORD x=32180 y=31230 z=7] . This is the fastest route. 
- Find Buddel in the south of Svargrond here [COORD x=32255 y=31197 z=7] and ask him for a passage to the raider camp . He will take you here [COORD x=32153 y=31375 z=7] for 50 gp . From there walk north and go up a ramp here [COORD x=32158 y=31355 z=7] . 
 It is a good idea to find Grizzly Adams in Port Hope and ask him for a task to hunt mammoths, before hunting Mammoths . Optionally you could combine the mammoth task with the Badger task, since they can be found in the bigger Mammoth area.
