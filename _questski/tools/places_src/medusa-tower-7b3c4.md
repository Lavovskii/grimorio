 The Medusa Tower is a tower southwest in Tiquanda , here [COORD x=32860 y=32829 z=7] , south of the Hunter / Dworc Camp . It can be reached by going down a hole north of the tower and entering it at the basement through a tunnel. The creatures become gradually stronger as you go higher and higher.
 The tower is in the style of the Lizards , as becomes clear from objects like Giant Lizard Claws , Lizard Statues , Pagodas , and Snake Walls . However, there's a large variety of statues all around the tower, such as a Dwarven Statue (Mace) , a Pirate Statue , and a Vampire Statue .
 The Medusa Tower is also one of the spots you have to cartograph during the Cartography 101 Quest .

## Ground floor 
This floor is immediately challenging as you can be hit from a distance by creatures beyond the wall. Go to the south side of the stairs immediately and protect yourself with Fire Bomb Runes . The coins represent the number of tiles that a monster can launch a distance attack at players who do not move away from the stairs. A player's first experience with the tower may cause them to lose enthusiasm for their attempt to conquer the tower due to the amount of damage received in the first instant. A bold player will realize that the monsters are vulnerable to retaliation and area attack runes can clear the area by players standing on any of the tiles marked by coins in the south-west of the picture. Fire Bomb Runes are most effective at protecting a player on the ground floor. On floors above you will find Behemoths which do not respect the flames. You need to be supplied with a quantity of Energy Bomb Runes to defeat them and protect yourself. You start to move clockwise around the room using Fire Bomb Runes for protection. Keep up your courage and move on. They cannot defeat you.

## First floor 
 This floor is immediately challenging as you can be hit from a distance by creatures beyond the wall. Go to the south side of the stairs immediately and protect yourself with Fire Bomb Runes . 
 You have the same challenges on the first floor as you did on the ground floor. Monsters can attack from beyond the wall to the south. The lines of attack are as the coins indicate. You should move north as soon as you can. There is only one Behemoth at the top of the stairs. You need to prepare an Energy Bomb Rune for safety and use it as far north and east of the stairs as you can. Clearing the first floor south of the wall. The monsters can only attack using line of sight. You can use area runes and easily clear monsters who cannot attack you directly.
