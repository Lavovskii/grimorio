 The Ice Witch Temple is a cave located below the western peninsula in the Barbarian Settlements area, southwest of Bittermor ( here [COORD x=31923 y=31360 z=7] ). It is inhabited by several Ice Witches , Ice Golems and Crystal Spiders . Under the temple itself, there are pits containing Frost Dragons and Frost Dragon Hatchlings , which can be accessed by levitating .
 For the first level it is enough to be lvl ~70.
