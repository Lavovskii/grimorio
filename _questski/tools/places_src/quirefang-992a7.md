 This area was implemented in the Winter Update 2011 . Quirefang is a large island where two races are fighting for supremacy. It is only accessible through the Gray Island , a small isle to the west of Cormaya , that can only be reached by the eastern harbours. Once one arrives on the Gray Beach , a small town located on the southern part of the island, they may find a Depot , a Temple and some Rockboys . To the north, there is the Hive a land of insectoids known as the Hive Born . In the depths below Quirefang one can find Fiehonja , where the Deeplings reside. 
 There are two quests here called the Liquid Black Quest and War Against The Hive Quest .
