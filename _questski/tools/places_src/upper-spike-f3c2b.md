 The Upper Spike is a Premium Account hunting area. It is advisable to have a Stealth Ring in case of getting trapped.
 On the first hunting floor you will meet: Gargoyles, Mummies, Ghosts, Crypt Shamblers, Demon Skeletons and Zombies.
 If you are a mage of level 30+ and have good economics, this is a great place to hunt with Avalanche Runes .
