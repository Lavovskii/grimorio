## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
The Krailos Mines are mines located below the island of Krailos . The entrance can be found in the southwest of the Krailos Steppe here [COORD x=33693 y=31775 z=7] .The Ogres ruling the island have enslaved Goblins to work in these mines under the leadership of the fierce Grubokk . This is because these mines are a great source of Opals and Onyx Chips . Unfortunately, the goblins are treated very cruelly and you will find many of their corpses scattered around the mines. You will also be able to find the NPC An Imprisoned Goblin here. Sometimes you will find an unannounced raid of Crystalcrushers and Drillworms here, so be careful!

[krailos-quest 4-3] Entrar nas Krailos Mines
Desce pelo buraco à esquerda do Grubokk. Na gruta, caminha para **oeste** entre as pedras até ao portão no ponto indicado e abre-o com a {item:Key 0555}. Desce, e desce de novo pela rampa. Caminha para **nordeste** e desce pelo buraco indicado: estás nas minas.

Para saíres das minas, volta pelo mesmo caminho: sobe pelos buracos com a {item:Rope}, sobe a rampa e passa o portão até à gruta por baixo do Grubokk; sobe com a corda pelo buraco junto a ele.
