These towers are a great source of Honeycombs .
## Rookgaard wasp tower 
 Ground level: 1 wasp 
+1: 2 wasps 
+2: 3 wasps

 The +4 level contains a honey flower patch, search it and you'll obtain a Honey flower , used for the Studded Legs Quest
