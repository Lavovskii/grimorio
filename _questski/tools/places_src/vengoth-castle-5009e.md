 The Vengoth Castle is a decaying castle that was erected by some brother vampires in the Vengoth northeast mountain. Its ample garden is guarded by living Gargoyles , and its rooms are haunted by many creatures, living and undead. 
It is an imponent building that goes many floors above the mountain. Access to its entrance is reached through narrow haunted passages in the southeast Vengoth mountain. A bottomless pit separates the two mountains, and a bridge connects them.
The focal part of the Blood Brothers Quest takes place here.
