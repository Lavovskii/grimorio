 The Seacrest Grounds are hunting grounds deep below Oramond . You can arrive by talking to Mr Brandon west of Rathleton , here [COORD x=33550 y=31869 z=7] . You will endure drowning damage if you aren't wearing a Helmet of the Deep . The helmet isn't needed to enter the hunting grounds.
 There is a main quest, Twenty Miles Beneath the Sea Quest , which unlocks the Seacrest Grounds in parts.
- First mission - unlocks the first Seacrest Grounds area ( Lost Mountains ; the western teleport). 
- Second mission - unlocks the second Seacrest Grounds area ( Sunken City ; the eastern teleport). 
- Third mission - unlocks the third Seacrest Grounds area ( Birthing Grounds ; the northern teleport). 
 In addition, when 2,000 Seacrest Serpents have been slain (this does not have to be on the same day), The Fire-Feathered Serpent World Change will be activated and a further mission of the quest can be started ( Twenty Miles Beneath the Sea Quest - The Fire-Feathered Sea Serpent ). The world change returns to its default state when 2,000 Renegade Quaras have been slain.
