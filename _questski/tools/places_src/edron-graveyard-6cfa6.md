## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
Edron Graveyard is located north of Stonehome , here [COORD x=33315 y=31734 z=7] . One of the few graveyards only accessible by Premium players. There is a  Daily Respawn of  Grave Flowers . There are twenty graves and a crypt with a single Stone Coffin . A single Skeleton spawns in the crypt, and a few more in a hole north of the gaveyard.
