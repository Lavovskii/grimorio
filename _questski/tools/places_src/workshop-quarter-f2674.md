 The Workshop Quarter is a part of the city Rathleton on the island Oramond with Rustheap Golems and Metal Gargoyles roaming freely. It has the city east gate to the east, bounded by a stream of glooth with a bridge to Lower Rathleton to the west and stairs to Upper Rathleton to the north. It also houses the entrance to the Underground Glooth Factory .
