 Gnomebase Alpha is the base for elite soldiers of the Bigfoot army and is located here [COORD x=32989 y=31884 z=9] . From here you can access the Mushroom Gardens , the Hot Spot , the Golem Workshop and the Warzones . Only premium players can access this area, with a Large Crystal Teleporter here [COORD x=32796 y=31780 z=10] . You need to have rank II (more than 120 reputation points) in the Bigfoot's Burden Quest to use the teleporter.
