 This area is only accessible once by finishing The Beginning Quest . Once players leave the bridge and take a few steps they will be teleported here. They will then be killed by Kraknaknork's Demon unless they transfer equipment and enough supplies from another character. The chances of killing it are insanely low however. It is the first place you will die.
