 The Quara Grotto is a large quara cave, south-west of Liberty Bay , here [COORD x=32208 y=32887 z=7] .
 On the first floor there are three places where you can jump in the water, you can swim without Diving Equipment . You will find Water Channels (You see a water channel. A cold maelstrom pulls you down.) but you need a diving helmet to enter them. Afterwards, you can go up by entering the Hot Geysers (You see a hot geyser. A hot current buffets you upward.)
 Near the entrance of the quara grotto you will find a Warning Sign :
 DANGER. Quara sighted below. Experienced adventurers only! Do not swim into a water channel unless you are prepared to meet your death! 
 Furthermore, you will find Small Fish in various places, as well as Large Seashells on all floors.
 The Quara Grotto is also one of the spots you have to cartograph during the Cartography 101 Quest .
