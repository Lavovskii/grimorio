The Dark Grounds is a small enclosed area in Upper Roshamuul , north of the Roshamuul depot. It's accessible only during an unannounced world change triggered by completing the Roshamuul Quest - Soothing Bad Dreams task 20 times in an unknown amount of days. Due to the unpopularity of this task, the Dark Grounds are not often accessible. In response to this, CipSoft lowered the requirements to open the area in a patch on March 3, 2015 , but even after this change this place is still rarely accessible.
 This area is a hunting place for Sights of Surrender , divided in two sections with 3 Sights of Surrenders each. It doesn't have any other monsters, which is the primary reason one would prefer hunting here over the Guzzlemaw Valley . Otherwise, there is nothing of interest here.
