## North Plains 
 This small area is home to some of the stronger minotaurs. The creatures here spawn very closely and this place is ideal for hunts using area attacks. It can be reached by going over the hill here [COORD x=33725 y=31919 z=7] . 
## Hills 
 There are two places on the east plains where it's possible to go up the mountain. You'll find the stronger minotaurs here.
