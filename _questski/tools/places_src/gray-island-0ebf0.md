 Gray Island is a small isle located [COORD x=33189 y=31985 z=7] south of Edron and west of Cormaya which can only be reached by Boat from Ab'Dendriel , Venore , Darashia and Edron for 150 gp . On its surface one can find a Sundial , a hole and Scrutinon , who will transport you back Ab'Dendriel, Darashia, Edron or Venore for free. There is an underground passage leading to Quirefang accessible through the only Hole in this island.
 Gray Island was implemented at the Winter Update 2011 .
