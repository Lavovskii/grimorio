## Notes 
 In the Magistrate the voting process takes place. Citizens of Rathleton can cast votes on different subjects. There is also a Slime Slide to a hunting ground filled with various creatures, depending on what the majority voted on in the previous weeks. You can find the following NPCs in the Magistrate:
- Ezebeth , +2 (at building entrance) 
- Marvin , +2 (entrance level) 
- Barazbaz , +2 (entrance level) 
- Jondrin , +3
