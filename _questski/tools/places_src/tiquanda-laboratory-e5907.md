 The Tiquanda Laboratory is an abandoned, overgrown facility under a hill southeast of Banuta , here [COORD x=32886 y=32590 z=7] . It seems to be a plant incubator that have been taken over by hostile plant life. Only one sole survivor, a single Mad Scientist , remains in the laboratory. 
The laboratory is mostly inhabited by Carniphilas and Mutated Humans . It is a good hunting spot for those who seek to complete a carniphila task .
