## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
The Plague Spike is the aptly-named mountain range separating the Devourer from Drefia . It is known by many names, including Mountain of Poison and Scorpions Rock .
 Browse Map [COORD x=33057 y=32436 z=7] 
 All throughout the Plague Spike are poisonous creatures, Hyaenas and Nomads .
 Just on the western side of the mountains is Drefia , the hide-out of a horde of undead and a cult of necromancers .
