 The dwarf mines are a group of caves located near or inside the city of Kazordoon . They can be divided in mines reachable outside Kazordoon and mines which can only be accessed by going through Kazordoon . The mines outside Kazordoon have 6 entrances resulting in 6 different caves. On the third floor 4 of those tunnels are connected through the Mine Hub .
 In those places, you'll mainly find all kinds of Dwarves , but there are also some less frequent creatures dwelling deep in the caves. While exploring the mines, you should be careful as you can easily access the Caves of the Lost to fight harder dwarves in the deepest floor of some mines.
 It is also known that there is a tunnel that takes from Mount Sternum to the mines and the other way around.
 The mines have many mysteries, including, but not limited to, the Basilisk and some quests .
 The Steam Boat is located deep inside the mines under the city of Kazordoon .
 You can find a mysterious site that was built by the Planegazers.
 Crusader Helmet Quest , Circle Room Quest and Iron Ore Quest take place here. This place is also a part of Sam's Old Backpack Quest , Kissing a Pig Quest , Opticording Sphere Quest and To Outfox a Fox Quest .
## Maps 
## Outside Kazordoon 
 The mines outside Kazordoon can be accessed through 6 entrances. Since December 2012 the mines have names (after player suggestion), on these maps they are still distinguished from each other with different colors.
 Color Name | A Yellow Old Ore Mine | B Pink Splintered Stone Mine | C White Bright Ledge Mine | D Red Diggers Depths Mine | E Green Pick 'N Shovel Mine | F Blue Drunken Fellow Mine
 ground floor map with 6 entrances first floor map, tunnel A (yellow) contains the Iron Ore Quest second floor map, tunnel E (green) has an entrance to the Sunken Mines third floor map, tunnel A, B, E and F are connected through the Mine Hub fourth floor map, tunnel E (green) has a passage to the Mount Sternum/Undead Cave . fifth floor map, tunnel B (pink) contains the Grothmok Tunnel . sixth floor map, to go further down from tunnel F (blue) you need to pass a level 32 Gate of Expertise . To go further down from tunnel E (green), you need to pass a level 35 Gate of Expertise . seventh floor map, tunnel E (green) contains the Crusader Helmet Quest and tunnel F (blue) contains the Circle Room Quest .
