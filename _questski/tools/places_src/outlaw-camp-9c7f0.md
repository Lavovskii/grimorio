The Outlaw Camp can be found east of Thais and Blood Bay , and west of Venore . This is the home of those who forsook their land and people for the wayward life. Wild Warriors , Hunters and other creatures live in this land. H.L. is the lone shopkeeper who is no less a crook than the outlaws about him. He will buy and sell for ridiculous prices. Also, for some odd reason, he won't keep conversations with ladies. 
 There are a few houses and guilhalls, on the Outlaw Camp Street . Because of their distance from a depot, houses here are generally cheap and on some worlds are entirely free to claim (0 gp bids will often go uncontested). Despite being closer to Venore, these houses are found in the Kazordoon list on the official website. As such Outlaw Camp 1 is the fourth largest house in all of the Kazordoon list, at 39 sqm. 
 Prior to the 7.4 Update, which restricted luring possibilities, it was possible to lure Giant Spiders in to the Outlaw Camp, but now such practices are entirely limited to Minotaur Mages and the odd Hunter . However, it may still be a little dangerous for inexperienced players. 
 After the 8.7 update the entire Outlaw Camp area was revamped and new quests were added there with the Married Men and their leader, Rottin Wood .
 See also Loot/Outlaw Camp .
## Description 
 When you walk inside Outlaw you will find a stone path that leads to the main buildings. There you will find some small Houses for people that are running away from something or just want to remain forgotten. Placed in the middle of these small flats is H.L. A man of short sentences who will never carry on a conversation with a woman. In the northernmost building of the village is Rottin Wood . He is the leader of The Married Men. There you can help him by performing some tasks... 
On the underground floor of this main building is the tavern , where Snake Eye will sell you a nice drink to forget about the problems that you are trying to leave behind. You will also find a mailbox , which will come in handy living in a reclusive place like the Outlaw Camp. There are some shops for rent as well if you wish to start your own business! (but don't expect too many clients!)
Going exactly to the south of Outlaw's flats you will find the Outlaw Castle , a mysterious castle owned by guilds that you would probably not like to face. 
If you walk around the corners of this little town you will find observatory towers. Going any further will lead you to the wild people and creatures that live there, so beware! 
 The Bright Sword Quest and the Rottin Wood and the Married Men Quest can be found here.
