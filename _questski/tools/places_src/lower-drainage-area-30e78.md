The Lower Drainage Area is not very suitable for hunting, since the Fungi creatures hit considerably hard and go invisible often, and their loot isn't very good. 
 The Oramond Quest and the Opticording Sphere Quest have missions that take place here.
 To reach this dungeon, enter the Sewer Grate at the Workshop Quarter here [COORD x=33622 y=31978 z=7] , then get to the ramp here [COORD x=33660 y=31892 z=9] (you can use the teleport one floor down of the ladder, here [COORD x=33614 y=31985 z=10] , as a shortcut).
