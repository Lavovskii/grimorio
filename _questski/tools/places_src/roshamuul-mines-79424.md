 The Roshamuul Mines are a small mine infested with Terrorsleeps and Feversleeps .
 Hunting here is strongly discouraged because the Dreamhaunter inhabiting the mines are really unattractive and annoying to hunt.
 Some of the Roshamuul Quest takes place here.
