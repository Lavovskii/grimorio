The Hell Hub is a dungeon that connects seven locations which are realms of The Ruthless Seven . The Hub leads to the following Grounds (numbered according to the map to the right):
- Grounds of Plague 
- Grounds of Deceit 
- Grounds of Fire 
- Grounds of Destruction 
- Grounds of Undeath 
- Grounds of Despair 
- Grounds of Damnation 
 This is the central area of the Ferumbras' Ascension Quest .
