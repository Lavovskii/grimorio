 This temple is buried under the sands of the Kha'labal . It is inhabited by Low Class Lizards and Cult members, who worship a lizard god there. This hunting place was introduced in update 10.8 together with a lot of other new hunting places.
 The entrance is located here [COORD x=33315 y=32675 z=7] , hidden by the tree. You may need to dig a few times before it opens. On the final floor, Incompetent Idiots (Book) can be found here [COORD x=33344 y=32688 z=10] .
 The third level underground has many Voodoo Cultists and Low Class Lizards , being easy to get swarmed. On the fourth floor, there are 5 Enlightened of the Cult , 11 Adepts of the Cult , a Lizard Snakecharmer , 3 Cobras , and sometimes the boss Tzumrah the Dazzler . Overall, the third and fourth floor of this dungeon are entirely full of ranged monsters, making those floors more wasteful for Knights . This hunting spot is good for Paladins , and using a Shield and Royal Stars or Enchanted Spears , and Recovery .
 On most occasions, the majority of profit will come from Rope Belts , which are used for imbuing .
