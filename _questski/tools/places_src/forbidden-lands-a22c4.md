 The Forbidden Lands are located in Tiquanda , east of Banuta ( here [COORD x=32973 y=32549 z=7] ) behind a great palisade . There are large and mysterious footprints, and some terrible and giant monsters beyond the wall. In this dangerous land, you can find Behemoths , Giant Spiders , Stone Golems , Massive Earth Elementals , Spit Nettles , Carniphilas and Hydras and if you listen carefully, you can hear whispers of The Blind Prophet .
 The Forbidden Lands - The Blind Prophet - Teleport . 
 You can find there a Giant Ape's Hair used in The Ape City Quest
