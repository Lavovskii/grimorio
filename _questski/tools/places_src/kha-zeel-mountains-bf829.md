## Introduction 
 The Kha'zeel Mountains are a sub-are of the Cyclopedia Map that belongs to Tiquanda area, even though it reaches to the eastern ocean and borders two deserts, leaving the jungle far behind. It has three branches, the northeastern one (number 1 in image below), known as the Plague Spike , that separates the Devourer Desert around Darashia from Drefia , the eastern one (number 2) separating the Devourer and the Kha'labal desert around Ankrahmun , and the south and main branch (number 3), that divides the Kha'labal and the jungle of Tiquanda around Port Hope . ==
 As a Cyclopedia Map subarea, the geography of the mountain range will lead you to a lot of different paths, mainly above the ground level, but some of them underground. This sometimes will make you walk long distances, and will make you pay more attention when looking for possible Levitate spots. The zones that can be discovered are mixed with zones that do not count for discovery, in a strange mix of patches.
 While discovering the subarea, the strongest creatures the player will possibly face are Massive Energy Elementals , that can easily kill an unaware player around level 80-90 with an unfortunate combo. The areas where Hydras and Dragons can be found are not part of the terrain to be discovered.
 The following sections include tips when discovering the subarea.
