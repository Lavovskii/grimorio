## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
The Temple of Xayepocax is a room deep down in the Edron Hero Cave . It houses a quest and many, many different creatures . This is a great place to try and kill your first Warlock . A good strategy would be to lead him through the entrance's narrow corridor and then trap him in a corner.
 After the lvl 70 Gate of Expertise in the south of the room, you will find a teleporter which will take you to the Demon Forge (you will need lvl 100 to be able to enter it though).
 Home to the Vampire Shield Quest .
## Creatures 
 In total, this room contains 3 Demon Skeletons , 3 Monks , 3 Hunters , 2 Heroes , 2 Priestesses , 2 Witches , 2 Dark Magicians , 2 Dark Apprentices , an Orc Shaman , a Stalker and a Warlock . Note: they respawn fairly quickly, so be careful.
