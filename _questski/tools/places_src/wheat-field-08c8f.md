## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
Wheat Fields are found all around Tibia. Players can use a scythe on wheat to harvest a bunch of wheat from these fields. A bunch of wheat can be ground into flour at a millstone which can be used to bake bread , cake , or cookies . Wheat can only be harvested when it is fully grown and bright yellow. Once it is cut it takes a day before it starts regrowing at which point it gets a little higher and turns green, and another whole day until it is fully matured at normal height and turns bright yellow again.
