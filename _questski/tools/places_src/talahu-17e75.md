 Talahu , otherwise known as Hydra Island, is as the alternate name would suggest, crawling with Hydras . Talahu is much smaller than Fibula, yet contains around 20-30 Hydras on its surface making travel on the island very tricky for groups trying to get to Malada and later Kharos . Talahu is a popular destination spot for high level mages seeking fast experience and decent cash. Talahu is also the only island with a mailbox inside the Teleportation Hub . Talahu is the second island in the teleportation chain. 
Below Talahu one can find a maze of caves with all different Quara scouts and Bog Raiders. Two floors below there is a square maze containing Quara scouts which makes a nice hunting spot for players of level 120 and above.
To the north of this maze is a gate of expertise leading to a cave with Serpent Spawns and Medusae .
 To reach the spawn of Medusae and Serpent Spawns follow the line.
