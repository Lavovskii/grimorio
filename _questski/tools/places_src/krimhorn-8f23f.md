 One of the savage Norsir barbarians camps, and the widest for that matter. The camp is located in the "Raider Camp" to the west side. You can access the place via the mountain to the east with lots of mammoths on, or by foot through the Formorgar Glacier . This place has the Ice Witch Tower where higher level players can hunt. It is a very good place for paladins to level after 35+, and any knights can easily hunt on the Ice Witch tower on higher levels.
