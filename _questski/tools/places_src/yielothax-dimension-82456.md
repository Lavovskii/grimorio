This location is part of The Mage's Tower World Change and is only accessible depending on the state of the world change.
 South-west of the Zao Steppe , a vicious Raging Mage has created a dimensional portal on top of a tower in order to access the energy buzzing there. The wizard himself is keeping the dimensional rift open. So as long as he is alive you are able to enter Another Dimension where you will encounter Yielothax , strange and tough creatures with rare loot . Also, you might come across the Inoperative Uniwheel , which can be obtained as mount .
