## Other Properties 
## Version 
 10.10 July 17, 2013 Summer Update 2013 Part 1 
 See also: Hunting Places Help 
The Fury Dungeon , located deep below Edron here [COORD x=33303 y=31829 z=14] , can only be accessed through a Fury Gate as part of the Fury Gates Mini World Change . This gate appears close to the Tibian towns  and like any other Mini World Change , they appear randomly. Only promoted players of level 60 or more may enter it, and from Update 10.56 on, you also need to be premium. This hunting place was implemented in Summer Update 2013 - I , and used to serve as a way to give free account players a taste of usually just premium account creatures .
