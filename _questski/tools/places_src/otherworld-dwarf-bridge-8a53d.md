 The Otherworld is a collection of dungeons introduced in the Summer Update 2016 . This article is about the Otherworld reachable from a cave near the Dwarf Bridge here [COORD x=32520 y=32021 z=8] . There is also an Otherworld north of Edron and a main one .
 Please note that the entrance to this otherworld is not permanently there. Some times it disappears, but it comes back after some some minutes/hours. 
 A mission of the Opticording Sphere Quest takes place here.
