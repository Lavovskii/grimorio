## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
A small view of the Well of Life The Well of Life , or the Fountain of Life, as it is more known, completely refills your health .
 It is located at the Pits of Inferno entrance here [COORD x=32806 y=32321 z=11] . This place has some butterflies and flowers which are used in a quest, but isn't the only spot where player can find these creatures/flowers.
 It's a beautiful place to take screenshots with friends, although it's not easy to get here as a low-leveled player.
 When you use it for the first time, you'll get the following message: 
 You feel very refreshed and relaxed. 
 When you use it again, you'll get the following message: 
 You have already drunk from the fountain of life. 
If you drink water from this fountain, you may get the achievement Fountain of Life .
