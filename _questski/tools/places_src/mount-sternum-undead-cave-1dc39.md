## Version

 See also: Hunting Places Help 
A new cave after the winter update 2007 , CipSoft probably wanted to compensate the free account people for the big undead hunting caves ( Ankrahmun Tombs ) the premium account players have. With every floor you go down the monsters become more challenging and stronger. This is an excellent hunting ground for a number of reasons. Firstly, it boasts excellent gold drops as well as good amount of experience (even when there are many players hunting in the cave), mace/sword lootbags can be made and sent using the mailbox near NPC Melfar (if you have done the Postman Missions Quest ). Last but not least, there is a nearby food source at the northern exit of the cave(near the witch tower), there are plenty wolves and bears, as well as water for those carry a fishing rod.
 Caution is advised however, it's great distance from cities makes it a popular PK hangout in PVP worlds.
There are a few entrances, one is here [COORD x=32418 y=32017 z=7] , close to the witch tower south of Kazordoon, the other one is through the Mount Sternum Cyclops cave. 
 There is one quest in this cave, the Geomancer Quest .
