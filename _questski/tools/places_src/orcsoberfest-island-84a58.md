 The Orcsoberfest Island is the location where the Orcsoberfest event takes place, here [COORD x=33771 y=31059 z=7] . To access it use a teleport just south of Thais ( here [COORD x=32361 y=32294 z=7] ). The Great Schnitzel 's lair is also located under this island. 
 This island shares a location with the Percht Island , which is why it may look odd on the Automap if the player has visited both places. Which area is active depends on the event that is running. You can see the map that corresponds only to the Orcsoberfest Island on the images to the right. The region that hosts the island is called Secluded Island , and that's the name that appears in e.g. the Bestiary of the creatures.

[orcsoberfest 3-0] The Great Schnitzel Hunt — encontrar o portão
O portão do Great Schnitzel aparece perto de uma de 17 cidades e **muda de sítio a cada 2 horas**. Para o encontrares, compra ao {npc:Xaver} os {item:All Knowing Sausages} (100 Festive Points; não se gastam com o uso) e usa-os. Viaja até à cidade indicada (o portão pode estar perto de Thais, Venore, Kazordoon, Port Hope, Liberty Bay, Svargrond, Yalahar, Issavi, Rathleton e outras) e procura o {item:Omnious Cauldron} perto dela. O caldeirão leva-te ao covil do Great Schnitzel, que fica debaixo da Orcsoberfest Island.
