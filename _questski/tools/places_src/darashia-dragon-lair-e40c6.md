 The Darashia Dragon Lair is a cave to the north of Darashia . Only players of level 40 or higher are allowed to pass the Gate of Expertise which marks the entrance.
 The Lair contains many Dragons of all kind, including Hatchlings and Dragon Lords . On the bottom level you could even find Demodras .
 This is a great spot to hunt Dragons, as the respawn is great. Even with many players online, there is a dragon for everyone. It's also a great place for Paladins , as there is much space for you to run them.
 However, if you're unexperienced, you should enter the lair with caution. If the spawns are completely full and you're unfamiliar with hunting Dragons, their numbers can surprise you.

[darashia-dragon-quest 2-0] Voltar à superfície
O covil não tem teleporte de saída: volta pelo mesmo caminho. Do terceiro nível sobe as escadas para o segundo (Dragon Lords), atravessa-o até às escadas para o primeiro e sobe outra vez até à entrada, a norte de Darashia. Os Dragons e Dragon Lords que deixaste para trás podem ter voltado a aparecer, por isso não subas sem vida e mana cheias.
