It is one of the areas of the Rotten Blood Quest . The creatures here deal 3 different elemental damages: Holy , Fire and Earth (and Energy Damage for characters under the influence of at least three Bakragore's Taints ), while being sensitive to Physical , Death , and Energy .
 Characters absorb dark energy here, which is displayed as a separate counter next to the character's name (represented by the icon). If the counter of a character reaches 100, a painful chain spell triggers on top of them, with an initial distance of 3 tiles and jump distance of 6 tiles. If there are other characters nearby, the chain spell can hit up to 5 targets with an increase of damage per jump: [ 1 ] 
- when a Knight or Paladin is the initial target, the base Energy Damage is 2,250 and it increases 37% per jump 
- when a Sorcerer or Druid is the initial target, the base Energy Damage is 900 and it increase 90% per jump.

[decaying-defender-outfits-quest 0-5] Matar os quatro filhos do Bakragore
Dentro do Blood Vestibule, fala outra vez com o {npc:A Bloodshade} ({key:quest}): tens de matar dois pares de gémeos malignos antes de chegares ao Bakragore. Há quatro salas com {item:Spheric Teleporter|Spheric Teleporters}; cada um leva a uma zona de caça. Em cada zona, toda a equipa tem de encher o contador de **Rotten Charges** até **100** para entrar na sala da alavanca do boss. Podes matá-los por qualquer ordem, mas a ordem mais usada é a da tabela. Cada boss morto dá um {wiki:Bakragore's Taints|Bakragore's Taint} à equipa, sempre pela mesma ordem.
