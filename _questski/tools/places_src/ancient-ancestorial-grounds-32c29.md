The Ancient Ancestorial Grounds is a sacred site of the Deeplings which is under attack by their enemies, the Deathlings . 
 It's a good hunting place for teams or Druids + Knights duo, yielding both good experience and enough loot to pay all supplies. Even though the creatures here do not drop any very valuable item, they drop Warrior's Axes and Shields very often. All the loot obtained here can be sold nearby to a Rock In A Hard Place , which is also very convenient. The spawn is big enough in a way that two separate teams can hunt at the same time, one on each floor (it's possible to levitate down from the first to the second floor on the spots marked on the map). 
 To reach this place, go down this hole [COORD x=33514 y=31323 z=7] on Gray Beach , then go down several steps of stairs and go two floors up to the east, until you find a teleport here [COORD x=33584 y=31388 z=13] .
 Access to this place is obtained through The Secret Library Quest which partially takes place here.

[the-secret-library-quest 3-10] Sair das Ancient Ancestorial Grounds
A wiki não descreve a saída das Ancient Ancestorial Grounds nem da sala do Brokul: volta pelo mesmo caminho até ao teleporte e ao buraco a leste de Gray Beach (sobe com a {item:Rope} onde for preciso) e faz a passagem subterrânea ao contrário até à Gray Island. Se não encontrares a saída, uma {item:Temple Teleport Scroll} leva-te ao teu templo (não funciona em zonas No-Logout nem com Logout Block ou Protection Zone Block). Na Gray Island, o {npc:Scrutinon} leva-te **de graça** a Ab'Dendriel, Darashia, Edron ou Venore: diz {key:hi}, {key:travel} e o nome da cidade (por exemplo {key:edron}).
