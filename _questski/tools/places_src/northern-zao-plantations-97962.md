Coming out of the Souleater Mountains , you will stumble upon the tainted plantations of Northern Zao . Many perverted bushes, plants and trees can be sighted at this botanically exclusive area. A pathway leads to Razachai , but the guard won't let just anyone enter the city. One of the Brimstone Bug Caves can also be found here. There is also a tower in the far east portion of the area ( here [COORD x=33324 y=31090 z=7] ), housing a group of lizards . This tower is considerably easier to hunt than the plantations outside as there are at most 6 lizards per floor.
