It is one of the areas of the Rotten Blood Quest . The creatures here deal 3 different elemental damages: Energy , Physical and Ice (and Fire Damage for characters under the influence of at least three Bakragore's Taints ), while being sensitive to Earth , Fire , and Physical .
 Creatures in the area can put a Darkfield below characters. It can be compared to a Fire Field but it deals considerable Agony Damage instead. A darkfield can only be destroyed through Destroy Field Runes , but will always inflict damage on the initial target.
