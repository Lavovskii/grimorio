## Sections of the Pits of Inferno 
 The Pits of Inferno (also known as the Inferno Pits) is a legendary place below the Plains of Havoc , where the Nightmare Knights established a base to fight evil. The Ruthless Seven have taken it over and turned it into a horrifying stronghold of evil, filled with demons and horrors from beyond this world. 
 The entrance to the Pits of Inferno is located in a small chamber, sealed by a magic wall along with the Well of Life and the Shrine of Wonders, as shown in this screenshot. As shown in the picture, Arieswar (who was accompanied by Taghor) had discovered the entrance to Goshnar 's grave and was able to get down to the Place of Wonders, but could not get past the magic wall. He was teleported past it during the winter '04 test server. After that, the pickhole leading to Goshnar 's grave was removed. The Pits of Inferno were re-opened during the December '06 update. 
 The pits of inferno are full of difficult creatures and powerful demons. Only the bravest adventurers dare to invoke the wrath of the beasts that lurk there. The area is now based around the Pits of Inferno Quest .
 If you walk to the far bottom left corner of the bottom left room you can find empty mapping.
 Hunting Tips 
 The pits of inferno are massive. There are many hunting grounds although some are more viable than others.
- Dragon Lair - waste is almost inevitable, but each vocation could hunt here for decent experience rates. 
- Spa - the spa is mainly hunted for the Dark Torturers . Paladin duos work great here, so do knight+druid teams. 
- Levers - the levers are great for loot if you avoid the annoying parts. Mainly for knight+druid teams. 
- Tafariel 's throneroom - hunted for Dark Torturers for loot. Mainly for knight+druid teams. 
 Keep in mind, for any throneroom hunts, you will almost surely need a spawn blocker for the hub.
## Dragon Lair 
 The Pits of Inferno Dragon Lair is the biggest dragon lair in the game. There you can find Dragons and Dragon Lords .
## Levers 
 The Pits of Inferno Levers (also called levers are a major part of the Pits of Inferno Quest but also a significant hunting area. While the entire area is focused around the quest, a small part of it is desirable to hunt - namely, the north and parts of the east. With the introduction of the first Pits of Inferno#Hub_Shortcut , it becomes viable to hunt here as 4 players (1 of each vocation) are no longer necessary to enter.
## Spa 
 The Pits of Inferno Spa (also commonly called DT city or dark torturer city , although not a city by any means) is a popular hunting ground and minor part of the quest . The spa is notable as the first part of the quest where Dark Torturers are found, which are known for their loot. The extreme north end of the spa contains a few Phantasms , but they are easily avoided.
## Maze 
 Map showing all invisible teleports in the maze. 
 The Pits of Inferno Maze (often shorten to PoI maze ) is a small part of the pits of the inferno, the part between the spa and the hub room. There are monsters before the maze, but none in them.

## Point of no Return 
 The Pits of Inferno point of no return is a minor part of the quest after the maze which prevents turning back. It is a part where players make the decision to quit now (north hole, leads to Plains of Havoc slime cave with no return) or proceed to the Pits of Inferno#Hub (south hole). Between the maze and the point of no return is a room with few Hellfire Fighters and Flamethrowers . After the room but before the hub is the first installment of the quest rewards and a room with Phantasms and Nightmares .
