It is one of the areas of the Rotten Blood Quest . The creatures here deal 3 different elemental damages: Death , Ice and Energy (and Earth Damage for characters under the influence of at least three Bakragore's Taints ), while being sensitive to Holy , Earth , and Fire .
 Indestructible Radicular Totems are scattered in this hunting ground. They unleash large Earth Damage wave spells hitting clockwise in cardinal directions (east, south, west, then north). If a creature dies near a Radicular Totem, its power counter is raised (represented by a icon next to its name) and the damage that is dealt by the next totem's wave increases.
