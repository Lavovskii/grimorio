 The Sea Serpent Area is split into two locations west of Hrodmir ( here [COORD x=31929 y=31069 z=8] and here [COORD x=31931 y=31251 z=9] ).
You can get there with Captain Haba . You will need Diving Equipment (i.e. either a Helmet of the Deep or a Depth Galea ) to dive underwater; once underwater, you can remove the helmet but lacking full drown resistance will incur the usual Drown Damage .
 There are 3 floors in the first area. On the first floor there are Deepsea Blood Crabs and Young Sea Serpents , along with at least 2 Sea Serpents spawns. You can Levitate or use Parcels , Pots or Ice Cubes to get down to levels -2 and -3 to fight Young Sea Serpent and Sea Serpents in a dozen or so pits. Some of the pits have caves so beware that there can be more serpents present than you can see from above. It was said a profitable hunt here was hard before Updates/8.5 , as Sea Serpents droped little loot whose price was stable.
 You will need to have completed The Hunt for the Sea Serpent Quest in order to access these hunting grounds.
