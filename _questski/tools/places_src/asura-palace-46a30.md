 The Asura Palace is an opulent building near the Kha'zeel Pass in Tiquanda , here [COORD x=32953 y=32685 z=7] . The main area houses many Dawnfire Asuri , Midnight Asuri , Frost Flower Asuri and Hellspawns . There are also some other creatures here, but these can only be reached through some hidden entrances. The Palace also has a secret basement, in which the Asuri hide in their true form — True Dawnfire Asura , True Midnight Asura and True Frost Flower Asura — along with Hellspawns and Hellhounds (only players at level 250+ can enter this area). 
 To reach the two Liches , go to the westernmost part of the ground floor and step on Some Cracks . 
To reach the two Massive Fire Elementals , fall through some cracks in the northeasternmost part of the fourth floor. 
To reach the two Massive Energy Elementals , ascend the western part of the tower as far as you can and then fall through some cracks in front of a chest. 
To reach the three Hydras , go to the little patch of green on the ground floor and fall into the Pitfall . 
To reach the two Destroyers , fall through some cracks to the southwest of the green patch on the third floor. 
 A Blackboard near the Massive Energy Elementals says:
 You read: A golden goblet filled with fresh and innocent blood.
A silver chalice filled with snake and scorpion poison.
An ivory vessel filled with a wailing lover's tears.
These are the paraphernalia to perform the ritual of youth and beauty.
Another instructional text comes in the form of a Large Book and is found near the Destroyers :

 How to take on and keep a beautiful human appearance By eating the powder of the purple kiss, the blue lotus and the pearl flower, with cinnamon and honey, an asura becomes lovely in the eyes of mortal men. An asura may also carry with her the bone of a peacock or of a hyena covered with gold. This makes her hair silky and her skin fresh and vivid. The application of a mixture of jungle rose petals, a flower from a fresh grave and peacock bone powder produces the same effect. Drinking mare's milk is also a good way to keep an appealing human form for an asura. Thereafter she should take black sesame seeds and soak them with the eggs of sparrows, boil them in milk and mix them with sugar. If she drinks this mixture and eats three fruits of the dragon nest tree she will keep her human form for five days. But if she takes a bath in a young man's blood she may keep a lovely human form for the time of a full month.

 The Asura Palace Quest , The Lost Brother Quest and part of The Secret Library Quest take place here. The Asura Palace is also one of the spots you have to cartograph during the Cartography 101 Quest .
