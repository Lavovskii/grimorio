 The Cake Keep Isle is an island [COORD x=33308 y=32083 z=7] south of Cormaya .
 It is only accessible a few days a year, during the World Quest A Piece of Cake . A large cake can be found on the island once the event starts, and players have to eat it in order to succeed.
