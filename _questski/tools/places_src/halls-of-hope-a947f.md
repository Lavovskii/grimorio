## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
The Halls of Hope is a temple located north of Thais , here [COORD x=32326 y=32090 z=7] . It was implemented in the Winter Update 2016 .
## Unfinished state 
 Initially the temple will be in an unfinished state. By progressing in the Forgotten Knowledge Quest it can be finished. In this state it will contain: Ruined Columns , Overgrown Monuments , Empty Waterway , Plant Tub , Empty Ruined Plant Tub and an Imbuing Shrine .
## Finished state 
 When the temple is finished rebuilding, it will contain six coloured portals.
- Death Portal leading to the Old Masonry , here [COORD x=32805 y=31657 z=8] with boss Lady Tenebris . 
- Energy Portal leading to the Replica Dungeon , here [COORD x=32786 y=32818 z=13] with boss Lloyd . 
- Earth Portal leading to near the Desecrated Glade , here [COORD x=32635 y=32257 z=7] with boss Mounted Thorn Knight . 
- Fire Portal leading to the Temple Complex , here [COORD x=33339 y=31168 z=7] with boss Soul of Dragonking Zyrtarch . 
- Ice Portal leading to the Formorgar Mines , here [COORD x=32206 y=31027 z=10] with boss Melting Frozen Horror . 
- Holy Portal leading to the Astral Shaper Dungeon , here [COORD x=32780 y=32686 z=14] with boss The Time Guardian . 
 Furthermore there is a seventh teleporter, an Energy Gate leading to the Astral Shaper Boss Dungeon , here [COORD x=32905 y=32847 z=13] .
