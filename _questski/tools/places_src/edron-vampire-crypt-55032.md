 The Edron Vampire Crypt is a vampire crypt, similar to the Vampire Crypts in Drefia , north of Edron , here [COORD x=33109 y=31722 z=7] .
 The teleporter here [COORD x=33019 y=31605 z=11] will teleport you to the Stone Blocks near the entrance of the cave, here [COORD x=33052 y=31698 z=8] . On the final floor, Bleed the Snake (Book) can be found here [COORD x=32999 y=31593 z=11] .
 The Edron Vampire Crypt is also one of the spots you have to cartograph during the Cartography 101 Quest .
