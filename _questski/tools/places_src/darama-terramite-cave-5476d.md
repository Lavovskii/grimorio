 The Darama Terramite Cave is a cave located under the Kha'zeel mountains, connecting Kha'labal desert ( here [COORD x=33104 y=32697 z=7] ) with the Tiquanda jungle ( here [COORD x=32976 y=32686 z=7] ). It consists of two Terramite chambers separated by a small empty area inside the mountain, where players can catch their breath after hunting. If you have a pick , you can reach at least one deeper cave with Terramites (use it here [COORD x=33027 y=32708 z=8] ).
 If you are just trying to cross the mountain, you can also walk the above ground route via the Kha'zeel Pass .
 It is advised to bring your own food (e.g. loot from Elephants in the jungle) as Terramite Eggs are quite valuable and not recommended for consumption.
