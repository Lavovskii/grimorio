 Vampire Crypts (originally called Vampire Hell by players) is a massive Vampire dungeon below the sands of Drefia . Historically it was nothing more than a small section of the main Drefia dungeon, and although the connection with the main dungeon remains, the Vampire Crypts have their own entrance and have finally been recognized as an independent area (in The Gravedigger of Drefia Quest ), since July 2013 . To the south-east of the main floors are the Drefia Grim Reaper Dungeons , although the entrance to them has also moved with the revamp of Drefia. The Vampire Crypts also contain the resting location of The Pale Count , a rare boss whose appearance opens up the final two floors of the crypts until he is slain.
 Because of the abundance of Vampires , it is advised to bring a Blessed Wooden Stake with you when hunting at the Vampire Crypts. Dusting will make your hunting trip a lot more profitable.
