Also known as Kazordoon Prison or Dwacatra Prison Isle . It's name is an reference to the Alcatraz Prison Isle in San Francisco . Dwacatra is the prison island deep below Kazordoon . This is where all of the law-breakers are locked away. The general of the dwarven army has the only known key , and he is not likely to let just anyone have it. He keeps it safely locked away in his chambers.
 Sorcerers and Druids of Level 27 and above can hunt here with little waste with 2 Monks as summons, other summons don't work so well because they can't heal themselves. Don't care about Food , because Cyclopses and Dwarves drop enough Meat and White Mushroom . Some Stalagmite Runes would be good too, to deal with Orc Berserkers .
 Dwacatra has some small holes and open holes with Creatures . Don't go there without Ropes nor Shovels . Obsidian Knife will help you a lot, because there's lots of Minotaurs .
