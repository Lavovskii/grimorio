The Halls of Ascension is the secret location where Ferumbras is prepairing his Ascension . It's accessed through a teleport here [COORD x=33279 y=32392 z=9] .
 This is the area of the final part of the Ferumbras' Ascension Quest , and only players who have successfully slain the quest bosses before this part can access it.
