The Muglex Camp is a fortified goblin settlement located in the northwestern region of Newhaven . It is occupied by members of the Muglex Clan, a group of goblins that has arrived in Newhaven by ship and has been terrorizing the human village to the south for quite some time. The camp provides the clan with a fortified position from which they can launch attacks on the village. 
The camp is intended for young adventurers. The Muglex Clan is the first threat that adventurers are asked to deal with.
