 Robson's Isle (also known as Underground Isle ) can only be accessed via the Steamship at the Sunken Mines and the Steamship deep under the north of Thais when the Steamship World Change is active, operated by Junkar . It's inhabited by Robson and his Goblin guard Lunch .
