 The Venore Corym Cave is located east of Venore in the swamp, here [COORD x=33073 y=32085 z=7] . You have to go up the stairs in the building there and then down the ladders, to prevent roping. The quickest way there is through the east Venore city entrance [COORD x=33002 y=32096 z=7] .
 As a paladin of level 55+, skill above 80 and Royal Spears you can make good profit and experience (even 100k/h with stamina bonus) when hunting here with relatively low risk of dying. Just remember not to run like a mindless bot through the caves - just focus on one area filled with as many Corym Vanguards as possible. Particularly good spot is here [COORD x=33010 y=32075 z=11] (with the large cave north-east, but you will probably not need go there, Coryms respawn is fast).
 Level 25+ knights and paladins with decent skill (45+) and plenty of Stealth Rings can try to run past the Vanguards to a portal on the bottom floor and access an area filled with Stonerefiners , which give extremely good experience, are weak to physical attacks, and cannot sense invisibility.
