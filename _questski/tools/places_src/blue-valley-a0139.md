 Blue Valley is a secluded and mystical island north of Krailos , accessible here [COORD x=33603 y=31469 z=7] . For generations, it has been home to the warrior monks known as the Enpa, who preserved their ancient disciplines through times of chaos, isolation, and interdimensional strife.
## Overview 
 Once part of a forgotten continent, Blue Valley was severed from its homeland by a rift in reality, sending the island adrift through time and space. The Enpa, remnants of a broken civilization, adapted their traditions of balance and combat to survive in this shifting void. In time, they learned to manipulate the unstable rift, unintentionally tethering their world to the shadow realm of Yng , a connection that brought endless conflict.
 For centuries, the Enpa resisted brutal invasions from the dark forces of Yng, forging a culture defined by discipline, harmony, and spiritual resilience. Guided by leaders like Enpa-Deia Pema and mystics who transcended physical form, they found serenity in struggle and balance in war.
 Eventually, Sempi Enpa Gaan succeeded in sealing the rift, stabilizing the valley and returning it to Tibia. Though the island resurfaced in its original location, the Enpa emerged as changed people, hardened, wise, and detached from the world they once knew. Now, they cautiously open their sanctuary to outsiders, seeking to preserve their wisdom by sharing it with those who prove worthy.
## Geography 
 The landscape of Blue Valley reflects its spiritual essence: calm yet treacherous, ancient yet otherworldly. Terraced monasteries, weather-worn shrines, and sacred groves fill the mountain valleys, while remnants of rift energy ripple through the terrain. A few smaller interdimensional tears still dot the region, hidden from untrained eyes, leading to unstable outworld pockets.
## Culture 
 The Enpa live by a strict philosophy rooted in inner balance, control of the body, and detachment from worldly desire. Their teachings evolved under pressure from the dark world of Yng, combining martial arts with spiritual enlightenment. Many Enpa regard their continued survival as a form of resistance against chaos itself.
