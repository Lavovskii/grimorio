 Gnomprona , also known as Primal Gnome Hub , is an excavation site near a volcano under Marapur . There, the Gnomes are executing the Operation Challenger , trying to stabilize the newly found volcano while investigating the odd progenitor race that once must have lived there.
 The Gnome Hub is a safe area which can be used to reach three different areas with very strong creatures:
- Monster Graveyard 
- Crystal Enigma 
- Sparkling Pools
