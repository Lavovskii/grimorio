 See also: A Visit to the Plains of Havoc The Plains of Havoc , called the Fair Plains before the battles were fought (according to Oldrak ), are located in the southern part of the Tibian Continent , south-west of Venore and south-east of Kazordoon , here [COORD x=32747 y=32258 z=7] . They were once the best hunting grounds for Paladins , to hunt Cyclopses at lower levels and Giant Spiders at higher levels. They are home to many assorted and dangerous creatures. There is a Temple where you can rest after fighting. Just east of this temple is the Abandoned Monastery . There is also the Necromant House , which has a portal that leads to The Void . Also, the Island of the Mists is a peaceful island reserved just for druids. The famous Knightwatch Tower can be found in the southern areas of PoH. West of the temple you can find the PoH Dragon Lair . Recently an area has been infected by a talking tree, the Demon Oak .
