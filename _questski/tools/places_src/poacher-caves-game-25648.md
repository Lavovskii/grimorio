 The Poacher Caves/Game are some caves north of the Green Claw Swamp , south in the Ferngrims Gate accessed here [COORD x=32754 y=31928 z=7] . It is a really good place to hunt for Bear Paws and Wolf Paws , although you can also find some Wyverns at the bottom.
 Sometimes you will not find this cave here, but Poacher Caves/Poachers or Poacher Caves/Gloomy instead. This is because they are all part of a Poacher Caves Mini World Change .
