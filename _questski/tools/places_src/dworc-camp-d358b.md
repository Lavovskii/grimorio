 The Dworc Camp is a square area bordered by strands of Bamboo Fence and plants in the Tiquanda jungle located here [COORD x=32900 y=32792 z=7] , north of the Medusa Tower . It is one of many settlements of the Dworcs , who also perform some of their rituals here. You may come across their boss, Oodok Witchmaster .
 Sometimes, this camp won't be there and you will find a Hunter Camp instead. This is because these camps make up the Jungle Camp Mini World Change together.
 In the middle of the camp, you will be able to find a Pile of Bones (Quest) . Use it to obtain the Dworc Misadventure (Book) .
