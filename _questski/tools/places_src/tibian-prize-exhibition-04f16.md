## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
The Tibian Prize Exhibition was introduced to display Contest Prizes , mostly Fansite Items to the normal Tibia player. The exhibition is opened every Sunday (on other days Cillia isn't there). You can buy a ticket by talking to Cillia . The ticket price is 50 gold. It is located underground near the Thais' Game Hall ( here [COORD x=32387 y=32210 z=7] ). In the hall richly decorated with banners, crossed swords and tapestries, you can find the following items, walking counter-clockwise.
