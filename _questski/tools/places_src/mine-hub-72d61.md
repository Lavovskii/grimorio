# PLACE: Mine Hub
slug: mine-hub-72d61

## WIKI PAGE (location, how to get there, exits; [COORD] = map link)
float The Mine Hub is a room three levels below ground in the Kazordoon mines outside the city. You might need Key 3802 in order to open it. The only Dwarf Guards on this third floor are in this room to guard the passages. 
 Four different caves connect here. There are also three royal mailboxes, accessible at the end of each passage. It is courteous to close all doors after traveling between passages, to prevent the Dwarf Guards from overwhelming those who cannot handle them. On the other hand, this room is also good for trying to solo your first guard. Using distance weapons it is easy to open and shut the door, firing on the guard while it is open .
