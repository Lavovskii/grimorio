The Green Claw Swamp is a dangerous place for the unwary adventurer. It is a good place for mid-levels, however. 
 The swamp is home to many creatures, including Amazons , Valkyries , Witches , and many poisonous creatures such as Poison Spiders and Scorpions . In a few places you might even run into a Mummy , Black Knight or even a Giant Spider .
 Points of interest in Green Claw Swamp are the Venore Amazon Camp , Dusalk's Troll Clan Cave and Villa Scapula . There are also rumours of a reclusive witch named Wyda .
 Before venturing into Green Claw Swamp, be sure to have some Antidote ready, and certainly bring a Machete to cut down the prominent Jungle Grass that can easily block your path.
