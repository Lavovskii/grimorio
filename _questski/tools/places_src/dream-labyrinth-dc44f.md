The Dream Labyrinth is a magical place that protects the Dream Scar from unwanted intruders. Despite its name, the Labyrinth isn't very confusing, but in order to move around it freely you must have found the keys to 7 different doors . 
 The Labyrinth has three floors. On the first floor, there are three separate areas: one with Summer Dreamelves (west), one with Winter Dreamelves (north-east) and the third one with both types (south-east). On the second floor, Summer and Winter Dreamelves are also found on the different regions of the Labyrinth, but they are connected. The smaller third floor also has both types of Dreamelves. 
 Access to and through the Dream Labyrinth is obtained during The Dream Courts Quest .
