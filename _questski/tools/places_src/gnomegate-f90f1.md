Hidden under the earth for centuries lies the town of an underground dwelling race, the Gnomes . Amidst crystals and mushrooms, they have always led a quiet life in seclusion, suspicious of outsiders, and hesitant to give their trust. Accessible only by the GCTS , the Gnomebase is in fact part of Kazordoon . Gnomegate is physically located below Ferngrims Gate and the ocean between Venore and Edron , here [COORD x=32795 y=31771 z=10] .
 Gnomebase Alpha is a separate part of Gnomegate .
