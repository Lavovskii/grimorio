 This is the desert surrounding Ankrahmun . It was once a lush and fertile region until the great battle during the civil war of the Djinns left the entire area barren and dry. The Ashta'Daramai Library retains a chronicle of the ancient war.
 On the surface, there are very few structures, and sparse packs of Hyaenas , bands of Nomads , and small herds of Deer , but under the surface are vast hidden tombs of Undead creatures and the resting places of the great Pharaohs . There are also large caves with Larva and Scarabs under the sand.
