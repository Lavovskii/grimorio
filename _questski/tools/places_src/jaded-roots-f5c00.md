It is one of the areas of the Rotten Blood Quest . The creatures here deal 3 different elemental damages: Earth , Physical and Fire (and Death Damage for characters under the influence of at least three Bakragore's Taints ), while being sensitive to Energy , Ice , and Holy .
 There is a chance that the death of a monster will spawn a Decaying Totem . This totem sets off four consecutive ring spells in expanding ripples, each of them dealing considerable damage, before vanishing.
