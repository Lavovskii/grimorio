 The Meriana Gargoyle Cave is a cave below Meriana , accessed via the meriana garden here [COORD x=32389 y=32604 z=7] . It is one of the most accessible and large enough places to hunt Gargoyles for e.g. the Killing in the Name of... Quest , besides Stone Golems , Tarantulas and Thornback Tortoises which can be hunted for this quest as well.
