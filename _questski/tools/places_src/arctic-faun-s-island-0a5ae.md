 The Arctic Faun's Island is one of the small southern Ice Islands , located between the mainland (northwest of Carlin ) and the islands of Senja and Vega , here [COORD x=32115 y=31739 z=7] . 
It can only be accessed by shipwrecking there when taking the shortcut route with Captain Charles from Port Hope . Since this is a random event, you cannot freely visit the island — it can only be reached once every 20 hours . However, you can repeatedly request the shortcut until you end up there. 
 Tip: The quickest method to trigger a shortcut is to stand near Charles on the Port Hope boat and repeatedly say: 
 Hi, Shortcut, Yes, Edron to Charles and Hi, Port Hope, Yes to Captain Seahorse 
until you are stranded on a random island.
 A barrel on the island can be used to obtain two Small Enchanted Sapphires and possibly other minor loot.
 Although the island existed since the introduction of the Ice Islands in June 2001 , it only became accessible to players with the Summer Update 2018 .
