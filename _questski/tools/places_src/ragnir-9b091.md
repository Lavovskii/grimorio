 One of the three homes for the savage barbarians , where the Norsir tribe resides. The camp is located in an inactive volcano. You can access it by foot over the Formorgar Glacier from Svargrond or by boat from the south. The island is a good place for mid to low levels to hunt at. It is not recommended for mages to hunt here, it is a better place for paladins.
