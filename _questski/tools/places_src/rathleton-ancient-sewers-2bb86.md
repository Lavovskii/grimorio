The Rathleton Ancient Sewers are located under Lower Rathleton , in the abandoned side of the city. There are many entrances to the sewers from the ground floor. The most commonly used is the one here [COORD x=33569 y=31920 z=7] , since it can be quickly reached by levitating down from the town on the upper floor. Despite being just next to the New Sewers , they are not directly connected. 
 The sewers are not frequently used as a hunting area since the number of creatures and the map design to not favor a good experience ratio or profit. Thus, it's mostly visited by those looking to complete missions for the Oramond Quest .
