 The Gardens of Night are situated in Drefia , here [COORD x=33056 y=32346 z=10] and play a role in The Gravedigger of Drefia Quest . They were introduced in Updates/10.1 , with the revamp of Drefia and the introduction of said quest. The gardens contain some items which are needed in various quests. According to Omrabas the gardens are located:
 Omrabas : East of the Necromancer Halls, you can't miss it, just look for a lot of dark and white sand, dried trees, and priestesses! [[{{{NPC}}}/Transcripts|Read more...]] 
 The entrance of the gardens could be considered to be here [COORD x=33037 y=32357 z=10] , where a Blackboard reads "Apothecary and Holy Grounds of the Dark Priestesses.". Behind a Counter you can find several (unreachable) items such as a Rough Red Gem , a Leechbloom a Reagent Flask , a Bottle with Rat Urine , a Fleshy Bone together with an Infernal Frog .
 More to the south between a basin and a Large Basin there are 2 Stone Herbs , a Troll Green and Leaves , a Love Flower , an Icy Skull , a Skull , Bones and a Bone Claw.
 A little to the east is a Sign saying "Path to the Gardens Of Night. Tread with care, pilgrim. The Dark One listens for you." To the south is a pond with Palms , Agave , Palm Lettuce Coral , an Alga , Swamp Tentacles , Small Glowing Mushrooms, a Yellow Glowing Mushroom , a Frog Leaf , a Sponge, a Bog Fingers Plant .
 To the west behind some Fences you will find Black Sheep , a Horse , Rabbits , Flamingos and a Larva and Pigs . There are some Pigeons and Bog Frogs which can be reached.
 To the east are a Leechbloom , a Hairy Violet, a Love Flower , Corms and a Dreadcoil , as well as Troll Mushrooms , Bog Wart, a Dark Blue Mushroom , an Infected Throne , a Large Boletus Mushroom and an Apocalypse Mushroom .
 More to the north you will find several Trees , including Blooming Cocoon Trees , Blooming Jade Spike Plants , Dragonbone Trees , Sunholder Trees and Dead Trees .
 There are also two Monuments , one reading "Eternal Life" and one reading "To the Dark God". Furthermore there are several Strange Shrines saying "There is something slightly worrying about it." or "You feel uneasy in its presence." with Stone Circles around them or a Strange Bonepile next to it. There are also Pillars with Hallowed Bonepiles next to them.
 There are also Books to be found, see Drefia Library#Gardens of Night .
