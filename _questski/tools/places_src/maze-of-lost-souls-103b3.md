 The Maze of Lost Souls is an intricate system of tunnels north of Carlin with many different monsters. Despite the name, the maze contains no Lost Souls . The maze is located a few levels below the Fields of Glory . It was constructed by trolls under the command of warlocks to protect the city of Demona from those who would intrude on the warlocks and try to steal from their treasure and knowledge. You need to be level 30 to enter there. The maze houses many different creatures and it is also very easy to become lost. You may enter one tunnel but cannot go back the same way. To enter the city of Demona you must be at least level 60.
 To enter, go down here [COORD x=32490 y=31697 z=7] and proceed to the hole closest to you. After going down, walk to the little patch of grass to the east and enter the concealed hole in the northwest corner. Turn the lever to the right and get back up. If the lever is already turned to the right, turn it to the left and them again to the right, as the hole can be reset due to a Floor Reset . Walk to this hole [COORD x=32483 y=31633 z=9] and enter. You are now in the Maze of Lost Souls.
## Strategy 
 If you become lost and have no Temple Teleport Scroll , remember that you can always place map markers for dead ends and dangerous creatures, however, maximum zoom is necessary for precision.
 If hunting a Fiendish Creature or you find map markers unreliable, the decorative stones scattered around the maze, as well as junk you might've brought with you, either accidentally or deliberately, can be organized to mark places you've already explored, as well as known dead ends without creatures. For example, placing a smaller stone over a larger one at the entrance of a corridor, over a Sewer Grate , or either at the base or top of a Ladder , can help you avoid re-exploring the same areas. Some holes might lead to places you've marked as dead ends, though, so reorganizing the stones into an arrow pointing toward the exit in such cases is also a good idea.
