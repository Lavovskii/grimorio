The Rathleton New Sewers are located under Oramond 's Workshop Quarter , which is the newer part of the city. Inside it, you'll find teleports that work as shortcuts from one area to another, but you can also walk around it. The entrance can be found south of the Workshop Quarter, here [COORD x=33621 y=31981 z=7] . Despite being just next to the Ancient Sewers , they are not directly connected. 
 The sewers are not frequently used as a hunting area since the number of creatures and the map design to not favor a good experience ratio or profit. Thus, it's mostly visited by those looking to complete missions for the Oramond Quest .
