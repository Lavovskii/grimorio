 The Desert Dungeon caves form a maze in which even experienced players can get lost. It is located under the Jakundaf Desert . Once in the caves, there are only two possibilities to exit the Desert Dungeon: by completing the Desert Dungeon Quest or by using the teleporter near entrance to the quest room. There are many different rooms in this maze containing different kinds of creatures which are dangerous for unprepared and inexperienced players.
 The Desert Dungeon Library , the Hall of Lost Doors , and the Teleport Room are all located here and part of the Desert Dungeon Quest . The dungeon also houses three NPCs, the necromancer Adrenius , the adventurer Hagor , and the blind Nemal , who also play their roles in the quest.
 There are still many mysteries and possibly unsolved puzzles found here. It is where you will find the famous unreachable room with a lever and a teleport, guarded by a single Banshee ( here [COORD x=32619 y=32100 z=12] ), as well as mysterious books, keys, levers and switch tiles.

[panpipe-quest 2-3] Sair do Desert Dungeon pelo teleporte
A partir do andar das aranhas segue o caminho curto da {wiki:The Desert Dungeon Quest|Desert Dungeon Quest} (Vocation Quest) até ao {item:Gate of Expertise}:
- Vai para **norte**, vira para **leste** assim que puderes e segue a gruta para **sul** até à escada; sobe.
- Vai para **oeste** até outra escada e sobe (**não** desças o buraco a sul com a placa de aviso).
- Vai para **sul** e sobe a escada seguinte.
- Vai para **leste** e **norte** (há um buraco: **não** desças), depois **leste** e **norte** outra vez até à última escada; sobe.
Ao lado do Gate of Expertise está o **teleporte de saída**: entra nele e apareces junto ao {npc:Adrenius}, no templo do Jakundaf Desert. Daí sai para a superfície do deserto e volta a pé para Venore (a leste).
