 Inukaya is the settlement of the Chakoyas . Their locomotion method mainly consist in extenses long tunnels built underneath the igloos . The camp is situated on the island Grimlund , just east of Nibelor and can be accessed through a Dog Sled from Nibelor .
 To use the sled from Nibelor you must finish The Ice Islands Quest mission 5. You also need to carry Ham with you.
 This place is home of the Inukaya Quest .
