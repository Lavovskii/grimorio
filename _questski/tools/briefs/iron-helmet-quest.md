## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Iron Helmet Quest}}

There is no real difficulty to this quest. There is simply a dead body partially obscured by a tree in the [[Plains of Havoc]].

The dead body is just west of the [[Cyclops]] / [[Orc]] / [[Minotaur]] camp ([COORD x=32787 y=32232 z=7 | here]).

There will usually be a lot of [[Leather Armor]]s, [[Letter]]s, and [[Worn Leather Boots]] lying on the ground near the tree.
