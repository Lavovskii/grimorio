## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Nightmare Teddy Quest}}

[[File:Full map to Nightmare Teddy.png|thumb|363x363px]]
When the [[Nightmare Isles Mini World Change]] is active, travel to [[Darama]] in order to find a portal which, depending on the specific message, will have appeared [COORD x=33037 y=32404 z=7 | here], [COORD x=33265 y=32653 z=7 | here] or [COORD x=33216 y=32273 z=7 | here]. You will be teleported to the [[Nightmare Isles]], which are filled with [[Silencer]]s, [[Retching Horror]]s, [[Choking Fear]]s, and [[Terrorsleep]]s.

Make your way onto the islet containing the only [[Terrorsleep]]s. You will find a [[Chest of Drawers (Quest)]] here, which can only be opened by players who have taken part in killing all six Terrorsleeps. After doing so, [[use]] the drawers to open them and receive your reward, a [[Nightmare Teddy]].
