## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=The Exterminator Quest}}

==Method==

Go to [[Padreia]] in [[Carlin]]s [[Druid]] guild, [COORD x=32301 y=31815 z=7 | here]. Ask her about a mission. Your [[Quest Log]] should update and you will receive [[Special Flask (Padreia)|an special flask]].
<gallery mode="nolines">
Exterminator_flask.png
</gallery>

Go to the sewer gate, [COORD x=32327 y=31816 z=7 | here] and go down, walk south and be prepared to fight some [[Slime]]s. Go down another floor and fight more Slimes. Finally use the poison flask on the water, this will prompt to update your Quest Log.

<gallery mode="nolines">
Exterminator_sewer_entrance.png
Exterminator_flask_drop.png
Exterminator_slimes.png
</gallery>

Go back to [[Padreia]] to claim your reward.
<gallery mode="nolines">
Exterminator_reward.png
</gallery>
