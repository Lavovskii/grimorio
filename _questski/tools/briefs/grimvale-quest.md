== Overview ==

This quest has 3 separate missions which are independent from each other. '''The Curse of the Full Moon''' can only be done during full moon nights which happen from the [[Server Save]] of the 12<sup>th</sup> to the server save of the 15<sup>th</sup> of each month, when the [[Grimvale Mini World Change]] is active.

The other missions, '''The Curse Spreads''' and '''An Ancient Feud''', can be started at any time even if you haven't started or completed The Curse of the Full Moon yet. 

== The Curse of the Full Moon ==

This mission starts at [[Grimvale]] and its reward is gaining access to [[Feroxa]]'s lair, which will open on the 13th of every month. To reach Grimvale, travel to [[Edron]] and walk to [[Stonehome]]. To the north of [[Stonehome]] is a ferry run by [[Cornell]]. Ask him for a {{KW|passage}} to Grimvale.

Grimvale is an island under quarantine because it contains were-sickness. Humans who exhibit the were-sickness are forbidden to leave the island, although this quarantine is not very effective because the were-sickness is not restricted to Grimvale. Werebeasts must seek [[Purple Nightshade Blossoms]] within Grimvale to have a hope of curing their illness. If you are bitten by a werebeast you will eventually change into one of them, depending on which beast you were bitten by. You cannot leave the island except by dying or teleporting. NPCs on the mainland will not react to your condition except [[Cornell]] at [[Edron]] who will suggest you go to [[Grimvale]] to find a cure.

:'''Warning:''' Because you cannot leave as a werebeast, it's not recommended to come here unless you are well prepared. To cure yourself you can either wait for a day that doesn't experience full moons, or eat [[Purple Nightshade Blossoms]] which can be found on the 2nd floor of the dungeon.

=== Purple Nightshade Blossoms ===
The were-sickness is curable by eating [[Purple Nightshade Blossoms]] {{Ilink|Purple Nightshade Blossoms}}. These flowers are found exclusively in the Grimvale dungeon. To obtain [[Purple Nightshade Blossoms]], go into the Grimvale dungeon by falling down the [[Pitfall]] at the north-east of the island. In this dungeon, there are '''3''' [[Blooming Purple Nightshade]]s to harvest from:
* From the start, head south and follow the cave south-east, where in the next room you will find the first flower ([COORD x=33353 y=31687 z=8 | here]).
* Head west to the end of the cave and drop down the pitfall to the next floor. Walk north to find the second flower ([COORD x=33314 y=31638 z=9 | here]).
* Head east and follow the path south-east to find the third flower ([COORD x=33384 y=31635 z=9 | here]).

When you harvest [[Purple Nightshade Blossoms]] you will receive the message:
:{{Message|type=server|You pick the large blooming blossom from the stem of this purple nightshade.}}

However, you can only harvest these during ''full moons'' because when it is not a full moon, you will see [[Purple Nightshade]]s in place of the [[Blooming Purple Nightshade]]s. Attempting to harvest a non-blooming [[Purple Nightshade]] will display a server message:
:{{Message|type=server|This purple nightshade is currently not blooming. No blossoms can be harvested.}}

Note that there's a '''30-minute cooldown per location''' to harvest new nightshades, however you will only need blossoms during full moons because the symptoms of were-sickness are only exhibited during these days. That is, if you cannot harvest blossoms because of the world state, the NPCs will react as if you aren't sick.

After harvesting [[Purple Nightshade Blossoms]] three times, deliver them to [[Maeryn]] by saying '''nightshade'''. 

=== Killing Were-creatures ===

In order to enter Feroxa's lair, you will also need to help the citizen of Grimvale by killing 50 were-creatures. The number of creatures you have killed will be registered in your [[Quest Log]] in case you need to check. 

=== Obtaining the Liquid Silver Trap ===

Before going to Feroxa's room when its [[Grimvale Mini World Change|raid]] starts, you should also prepare some [[Closed Silvered Trap]]s {{Ilink|Closed Silvered Trap}}, which are necessary for the fight against the boss. A total of 20 traps will be needed for the fight (not for each player, but for the whole server). Usually, every players bring a few traps to help, since creating them can be very time consuming.

To obtain the traps, you will need to bring [[Maeryn]] a [[Closed Trap]] {{Ilink|Closed Trap}} and [[Vial of Liquid Silver]] {{Ilink|Vial of Liquid Silver}}. You can buy the [[Closed Trap]] at general equipment stores, even from [[Gladys]] at Grimvale, for 280 gold. Obtaining [[Vial of Liquid Silver]] is a bit more difficult. Go into the Grimvale dungeon, third floor, and '''use''' a [[Pick]] (Secret Agent tools will also work) on any [[Silver Vein]] in the mountain walls. You may not be able to obtain [[Vial of Liquid Silver]] at every try, in which case you will receive the message:
:{{Message|type=server|Even after a thorough and frustrating search you could not find enough liquified silver in this vein to fill a flask.}}

This does not mean that particular vein is empty - you could repeat the process and succeed. When you have attempted to mine a vein, you will be unable to try again for '''2''' minutes. Mining again before this period is up will result in the following message:
:{{Message|type=server|You are still exhausted from earlier attempts. Getting liquid silver out of the mountain needs concentration and a steady hand.}}

When you are successful you will see the following message and obtain a [[Vial of Liquid Silver]] that lasts for 24 hours.
:{{Message|type=server|Carefully you gather some of the liquified silver from this vein in a small flask. You now feel strangely affected to the moon.}}

Each time you successfully mine [[Vial of Liquid Silver]], you gain a permission to use either [[Strangely Glowing Mark (Blue)|blue]] or [[Strangely Glowing Mark (Green)|green]] glowing marks for various effects:

[[Strangely Glowing Mark (Green)|Green Strange Glowing Mark]]:
:{{Message|type=server|The mythic fires beneath your feet heal you.}}
::Character is healed by 250 [[Hit Point]]s.

[[Strangely Glowing Mark (Blue)|Blue Strangely Glowing Mark]]:
:{{Message|type=server|The mythic fires beneath your feet strengthen you.}}
::Character's speed is boosted by 90 for about 1 minute.

If it's not a ''full moon'', you will be unable to mine the [[Silver Vein]]:
:{{Message|type=server|Only in the light of the full moon, these strange veins of unique silver will liquify and be soft enough to be mined.}}

=== Feroxa ===

After having delivered 3 [[Purple Nightshade Blossoms]] and killed 50 were-creatures, you will have access to Feroxa's lair. The lair will open when the raid starts on the 13th of every month, and it can start at any time between the Server Save and 12 hours after it. 

{{Seealso|Grimvale Mini World Change}}

At her first human stage you need to fight her with normal attacks, both spell and physical. When you reduce her health to about half, she will transform into [[Feroxa (Werewolf)|her werewolf form]]. 

In this second phase she is immune to almost everything except for [[Silvered Trap]]s and [[Life Drain]] damage. When [[Feroxa (Werewolf)|Feroxa]] walks on these traps, she will be damaged for 1,000 health points and the traps will vanish. The traps can be stacked and she does not heal, so it's a matter of having enough traps to bring the boss to half health. As an alternative, sorcerers and druids can summon either [[Gozzler]]s or [[Ghoul]]s to deal [[Life Drain]] damage, but this process takes a lot longer.

At half health, she will change into [[Feroxa (Killable Werewolf)|her killable werewolf form]]. Now you must attack her with all your firepower.

When she reaches 0 health she will either turn into her weak human form or [[Feroxa (Gloom Wolf)|her gloom wolf form]], both of which are quite weak and must be taken down with sheer firepower.

You will receive two sets of loot for the whole battle. Only the final form of Feroxa (Gloom Wolf) will grant you a kill count in the [[Bosstiary]].

== The Curse Spreads ==

=== Edron ===

Talk to [[Daniel Steelsoul]] inside the [[Edron]] Castle and ask him about the '''curse'''. He will explain how they have been able to cure some of the sick citizens of Edron, but not all of them, and they are not sure why, but someone at the [[Noodles Academy of Modern Magic]] must know. Go to the Ivory Towers and talk to [[Milos]] at the upper floors of the north-west tower and ask him if he knows anything about the '''curse'''.

Milos will say that he has made some written notes about this disease and that you should find them. There are 4 notes:

[[File:Grimvale Nightshade Parchment.png|thumb|right]]
* [[Piece of Parchment (Gold)]], located in one of the bookcases of the central tower floor +4;
* [[Piece of Parchment (Silver)]], located in one of the bookcases of the central tower floor +3;
* [[Piece of Parchment (Shadow Bite)]], located in one of the bookcases of the central tower floor +3;
* [[Hastily Scribbled Note]], located inside a [[Box]] near NPC [[Puffels]] (south-tower, floor -1).

After getting all 4 notes, go back to [[Milos]] and say '''notes'''. He will give you a [[Stone Tablet with Ley Lines]] and [[Crimson Nightshade Blossoms]] and instruct you to use his notes to obtain the other ingredients, which are [[Shadow Bite Berries]], [[Gold Dust]] and [[Silver Dust]].

[[File:Grimvale Tuft of Shadow Bite.png|thumb|right]]
The [[Shadow Bite Berries]] can be harvested from [[Shadow Bite Plant]]s, growing in the dungeon located south-west of Edron, [COORD x=33179 y=31922 z=7 | here] (you have to enter the [[Sewer Grate]] inside the Edron Flats building to cross to the other side). Inside this cave you will meet all types of were-creatures. Go down to the second floor, there will be a [[Shadow Bite Plant]] just next to the rope spot. Alternatively, there is a [[Shadow Bite Plant]] also on the second floor of [[Edron Earth Elemental Cave]], [http://tibia.wikia.com/wiki/Mapper?coords=129.133,123.227,9 here].

To obtain the metals, you'll need to visit the [[Venore Corym Cave]], [COORD x=33072 y=32088 z=7 | here]. After entering the cave, go down the hole north-east, down the next hole, south and down again, then go to the north-west side of the cave and enter the teleport [COORD x=33054 y=32008 z=11 | here]. Just north of you there will be some mined [[Gold Nugget (Object)|Gold Nuggets]] and [[Silver Nugget]]s ready to be shipped away, however, you shouldn't steal the [[Corym Ratter]]'s belongings right in front of him, so you will have to find some inside the mine. If you haven't done so yet. talk to the NPC to start the [[Hidden Threats Quest]] and obtain access to the interior of the [[Corym Mines]]. You can find [[Gold Nugget (Object)|Gold Nuggets]] on the room south-east of the entrance, [COORD x=33052 y=32085 z=12 | here]. Use your pick on the nuggets to mine them. To mine the [[Silver Nugget]]s, you'll have to go down the stairs north to the flower floor (talk to [[Corym Servant]] if you don't have access yet). You'll find the Silver Nuggets in a room south-west, [COORD x=33040 y=32036 z=13 | here].

After getting the nuggets, go back to Edron. Two floors below [[Milos]] you will find a [[Wooden Grinder]]. Use the nuggets on the grinder to obtain [[Gold Dust]] and [[Silver Dust]].

With all ingredients in hand, you are ready to prepare the cure for the were-sickness. You'll need an [[Alchemical Apparatus]], there are multiple in [[Sinclair]]'s lab on the central tower,  use the one in the north of the room. Use the [[Crimson Nightshade Blossoms]] first, followed by the [[Silver Dust]], the [[Gold Dust]] and finally the [[Shadow Bite Berries]] on it to create your [[Nightshade Distillate]]. Deliver the '''potion''' to [[Daniel Steelsoul]] so he can start using it on Edron's citizens.

=== Cormaya ===

The were-sickness has also reached [[Cormaya]], and [[Daniel Steelsoul]] wants you to help [[Yoem]] with it. Travel to the small island and talk to him at the center of the village about the '''curse'''. Yoem suspects that the village's water supply is being tainted, and will ask you to investigate the [[Last Sanctum]] however, to access it, you must perform a ritual in the [[Grimvale]] dungeon first. Go back to Edron, find [[Cornell]] north of Stonehome [COORD x=33304 y=31720 z=7 | here] and ask him for '''passage''' to Grimvale.[[File:Grimvale Puzzle Configuration.png|thumb|right|This puzzle configuration seems to have some meaning. If any levers are pulled at this configuration, a {{Message|type=sound|*click*}} will be heard.]]
Go down [COORD x=33353 y=31671 z=7 | here], and down twice more to the fourth floor, you'll be in the ruins where you must solve the 5x5 puzzle in the center room, [COORD x=33448 y=31577 z=11 | here]. Pull each lever to move the white tile south of it and create a V-shape starting at the top, as pictured. After that, pull any other lever. You will hear a {{Message|type=sound|*click*}} noise, at which point an array of light will shoot downward from the roof and the white tiles will return to the top.

Now you can enter the [[Last Sanctum]], which is behind a [[Mystic Flame]] in a dungeon east of the village in Cormaya, [COORD x=33353 y=32000 z=7 | here]. It is also inhabited by were-sick creatures. Follow the cave to the south-west and go down the [COORD x=33353 y=32093 z=9 | ramp]. Go to the room to the north, where you'll find some Statues. When approaching the water, you'll receive a message about how the water is being tainted by the magical statues that were built there. Report this to [[Yoem]] (say '''curse'''), he will instruct to seek the help of the wizards from Edron. Again, find [[Milos]] and tell him about your discoveries. 

:{{Message|type=server|The clean water changes as soon as it flows into the basin. Obviously this is the spot where Cormaya's water gets tainted.}}

Milos will explain to you his theory that the cure is not working because the were-creatures have tampered the Ley Lines that meet under Cormaya. In order to fix this, you must remove the curse that has been applied to them by using a [[Golden Symbol of Suon]] on the other end of the Ley Line, which is marked by a [[Dark Stone Totem]]. There are three Ley Lines: one towards [[Krailos]], one towards [[Banuta]] and one towards [[Vengoth]]. This is indicated by the text written on the statues inside the [[Last Sanctum]]. 

[[File:Grimvale Dark Stone Totem.png|thumb|Removing the curse from Banuta's Ley Line]]
* On Krailos, the [[Dark Stone Totem]] is north-east of the village around [COORD x=33680 y=31630 z=7 | here].
* On Banuta, the [[Dark Stone Totem]] is locates inside the southern most building, [COORD x=32809 y=32583 z=7 | here].
* On Vengoth, the [[Dark Stone Totem]] is located west before the Castle's gate, [COORD x=32949 y=31489 z=6 | here]. 

You should receive this message after using the Suon Symbol next to a totem:

:{{Message|type=server|You remove the curse that was put on this statue.}}

[[File:Grimvale Last Sanctum Clean.png|thumb|right]]
After removing the curse from all 3 totems, go back to the [[Last Sanctum]]. You'll notice that now the Water is clean again. Report this discovery to [[Yoem]], who will be pleased with your help and instruct you to talk to [[Daniel Steelsoul]] once again.  

:{{Message|type=server|The water that flows into the basin stays fresh and clean now. Obviously the curse is lifted.}}

=== Were-beasts leaders ===

[[Daniel Steelsoul]] will explain to you that some were-beast leaders have been found to be responsible for spreading the disease, and he needs your help killing them. You'll find them behind magical gates located in Edron's and Cormaya's dungeons. 

* [[Bloodback]], the [[Wereboar]] leader located under Edron's cave, [COORD x=33166 y=31977 z=8 | here].
* [[Darkfang]], the [[Werewolf]] leader located under Edron's cave, [COORD x=33055 y=31909 z=9 | here].
* [[Sharpclaw]], the [[Werebadger]] leader located under Edron's cave, [COORD x=33129 y=31971 z=9 | here].
* [[Black Vixen]], the [[Werefox]] leader located under Cormaya's cave, [COORD x=33442 y=32051 z=9 | here].
* [[Shadowpelt]], the [[Werebear]] leader located under Cormaya's cave, [COORD x=33403 y=32097 z=9 | here].

These bosses must be fought alone, but they are relatively easy. Mages and Paladins can use [[Fire Wall Rune]]s to keep a distance. 

After killing all 5 bosses, report back to [[Daniel Steelsoul]]. In gratitude for your help, he will gift you with a [[Foxtail Amulet]]. From now on you will also be allowed to travel between the [[Last Sanctum]] and Edron's dungeon using the [[Death Portal]]s existing in both caves ([COORD x=33064 y=31977 z=9 | here] and [COORD x=33429 y=32083 z=9 | here]), which can be very useful for going back to the mini-bosses (you can fight them every 20 hours).

=== Help Rhargu ===

Follow the path south and go down the hole where you'll find [[Rhargu]]. Ask him about the '''curse'''. In order to help you, [[Rhargu]] will ask for some food. 

# One {{Ilink|Haunch of Boar}} [[Haunch of Boar]] which you can loot from [[Boar]]s. 
# One {{Ilink|Hard-Boiled Hydra Egg}} [[Hard-Boiled Hydra Egg]]. Use a regular [[Hydra Egg]] on the campfire located in the Hyaena Lairs, [COORD x=33162 y=32397 z=11 | here].
# Five {{Ilink|Lion's Mane}} [[Lion's Mane]]s. If you don't have them already, you can get 5 from the {{Ilink|Stump}} [[Stump]] on top of the entrance to Lion's Rock, [COORD x=33136 y=32353 z=5 | here].
# Five {{Ilink|Salted Cave Rat}} [[Salted Cave Rat]]s, for which [[Rhargu]] will give you an {{Ilink|Empty Cask}} [[Empty Cask]]. 
## Leave the Hyaena Lairs, and when back in [[Darashia]] buy a '''{{Ilink|Net (Fishing)}} [[Net (Fishing)|Net]]''' from [[Halif]] and five {{Ilink|Salt}} [[Salt]] from [[Mugluf]], at the central market.
## Use the [[Net]] on [[Cave Rat]]s to capture them. Mages can [[Summon Creature|summon]] and capture those, but if you aren't a mage, just go to a place with many [[Cave Rat]]s such as Thais' sewers. There's a 20% chance that you'll get a [[Cave Rat (Item)]]. Use [[Salt]] on it to get a [[Salted Cave Rat]]. Use it on the Cask to store it there. Once the cask is full with 5 [[Salted Cave Rat|Salted Cave Rats]], return to [[Rhargu]].

=== Ancient Ritual ===

Meet [[Domizian]] again and ask him about the {{KW|curse}}. You learn from him that only those who have strong will and a good heart can control the curse through the use of a complex ancient ritual involving the conjuration and activation of secret moon runes, the playing of two holy instruments and the sacrifice of a creature touched by the curse.

For the runes he tells you the aspects of the moon and of the sun should be reconcilied thus the words "SUN AND MOON" should be written with the runes and that king [[Heliodor]] should know more about them. For the instruments they shouldn't be mere musical instruments but rather items of great significance: an ocarina made from a scarab shell and a horn made from a conch shell. As for the sacrifice, a were creature that has not yet harmed or even killed someone has to have its life taken for a greater good.

=== Curse Under Control ===
Return to [[Niral]] and inform her of your mission's completion by greeting her and saying {{KW|mission}}. Niral will express gratitude and request that you return the next day. On your return, she will provide a status update.

Once 20 hours have passed, you will be able to speak to [[Niral]] about the status of the curse and she'll inform you the curse is under control. As a token of appreciation for your efforts, Niral will grant you the following rewards:

[[File:Foxmouse (Mount).gif|right]]
* {{Ilink|Toy Ball}} [[Toy Ball]]: a special item that can be used to tame a [[Foxmouse (Mount)|Foxmouse]]. Taming the foxmouse will also grant you the [[Like Fox and Mouse]] achievement. Note that the foxmouse can sense the curse, even outside of full moon cycles. Cursed characters can not tame it, and should consume [[Purple Nightshade Blossoms]] to get rid of the curse before trying.

* In the northern area beyond the Foxmouse, there is a room accessible by using a [[Moonlight Ipomea's Stamen]] by using it on the [[twines]] between the [[Symbol of Fire]] and [[Symbol of Sula (Silver)]]. '''Note: this will consume the Moonlight Ipomea's Stamen.''' Within this room, you will find a [[Treasure Chest (Oskayaat)]]. Opening the chest presents an opportunity to obtain a [[Werewolf Painting (Howling)]], [[Werewolf Painting (Moon)]], or other assorted items. For detailed information on the likelihood of obtaining each item, refer to the [[Treasure Chest (Oskayaat)/Statistics|statistics]] page.

* Access to locked doors: In addition, Niral will bestow upon you the ability to access the previously locked doors within the city of [[Oskayaat]]. This privilege allows players to have access to NPCs on the island as well as [[Training School|training statues]].
