== Messages ==

== Method ==
The [[Merchant NPCs|merchant]] [[Yasir]] will drop anchor off the harbour of one of these [[towns]]: [[Ankrahmun]], [[Carlin]] or [[Liberty Bay]]. During this time you are able to sell almost all [[Creature Products]] to him.

Say ''ariki'' or ''trade'' to [[Yasir]] to get the [[achievement]] [[Si, Ariki!]].
