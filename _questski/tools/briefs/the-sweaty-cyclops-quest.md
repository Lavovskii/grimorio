== Ability to deal with A Sweaty Cyclops ==

=== Method ===

He'll ask you 3 [[Bast Skirt]]s for a gift to his "girlfriend", you can collect by hunting [[Dworc Venomsniper]]s.

=== Transcripts ===
{{Infobox Transcript|
Player: '''hi'''<br>
A Sweaty Cyclops: Hum Humm! Welcume lil' Player.<br>
Player: '''uth'lokr'''<br>
A Sweaty Cyclops: Firy steel it is. Need green ones' breath to melt. Or red even better. Me can make from shield. Lil' one want to trade?<br>
Player: '''yes'''<br>
A Sweaty Cyclops: Wait. Me work no cheap is. Do favour for me first, yes?<br>
Player: '''yes'''<br>
A Sweaty Cyclops: Me need gift for woman. We dance, so me want to give her bast skirt. But she big is. So I need many to make big one. Bring three okay? Me wait.<br>
Player: '''bast skirt'''<br>
A Sweaty Cyclops: Lil' one bring three bast skirts?<br>
Player: '''yes'''<br>
A Sweaty Cyclops: Good good! Woman happy will be. Now me happy too and help you.<br>
''Player'': '''bye'''<br>
A Sweaty Cyclops: Good bye lil' one.
}}

You can now ask the Sweaty Cyclops to forge metals and items for you.

In case you don't have enough capacity for the bolts, Big Ben will reply with:
{{Infobox Transcript|
''Player'': '''yes'''<br>
A Sweaty Cyclops: No can do. Make some room in your inventory first.<br>
}}
Note, that as a smith by trade, he's not great at math and won't subtract the weight of soul orbs first.
This means, if you want to make the bolts in batches of 1000 soul orbs, you have to start with 2900&nbsp;oz of free capacity, not 3000*0.9=2700 oz.
Alternatively, to simplify calculations, just assume each bolt weigths exactly 1 oz.
