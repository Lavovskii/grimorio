== Method ==

=== Welcome to Newhaven ===

This mission will be automatically assigned to your character upon arriving at the main island of Newhaven, immediately after you select your character's vocation. The goal is to reach level 8 and travel to [[Targuna]]. Try to complete the other missions in this quest; your character will likely reach level 8 before finishing them.

=== Protect the Village ===

Greet [[Gustavo, The Guard]] and say '''goblins'''. He will ask you to kill '''20''' goblins of the Muglex Clan.<br>
Talk to him again after you have killed enough goblins. He will reward you with a Guardian Ring and 800 experience points.

You can start this mission and '''Lost Wisdom''', and complete them in a single run.

=== Lost Wisdom ===

Greet [[Viola]] and say '''mission''', and '''yes'''. She will ask you to retrieve her [[Book of Wisdom]], which was stolen by a ghost.<br>
Talk to her again after you have obtained the book. She will reward you with a Scarf and 800 experience points.
