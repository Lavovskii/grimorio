== Fungus Cleaning ==
{{Ilink|Servant Sentry}}

Travel to [[Edron]] and head south of [[Stonehome]]. You’ll find a ruined tower [COORD x=33314 y=31837 z=7 | here]. Cross the bridge and talk to the [[Servant Sentry]]. 

{{Infobox Transcript|
''Player'': hi<br>
Servant Sentry: The. '''Slime'''. Has. Entered. Our. '''Master'''. Has. Left! We. Must. '''Help'''.<br>
''Player'': help<br>
Servant Sentry: Defeat. '''Slime'''. We. Will. Why. Did. You. Kill. Us? Do. You. Want. To. Rectify. And. Help?<br>
''Player'': yes<br>
Servant Sentry: Then. Take. This. Gobbler. Always. Hungry. Eats. Slime. Fungus. Go.
}}

[[File:TMV1.png|thumb|350px|Slime Gobbler being used to remove fungus]]<br />
You will receive a {{Ilink|Slime Gobbler}} [[Slime Gobbler]]. Go down two floors to reach the main area [COORD x=33312 y=31853 z=9 | here]. Depending on your [[World Changes|world status]], there will be {{Ilink|Slime Fungus}} [[Slime Fungus]] scattered throughout the dungeon all over.

Use the Slime Gobbler on the Slime Fungus. When done correctly, the tile will be cleaned and you’ll receive 20 experience points.

'''Note: You can only remove one fungus every 5 seconds.''' There are '''350 fungus tiles''' in total, so it’s highly recommended to bring friends to speed up the process.

After the [[Updates/10.52|10.52 Update]], each player must "gobble" at least '''25 fungus tiles''' to be eligible to face the [[Mad Mage]]. Once you reach the threshold, you'll see this message:<br />
{{Message|type=server|You gobbled enough slime to get a good grip on this dungeon's slippery floor.}}

If you're solo and clear the entire dungeon, you won’t be able to leave — otherwise, the fungus will regenerate and the Servants will stop spawning. You can re-enter the dungeon if someone else is still inside. In this case, the next wave will resume from where it left off.

If you were part of a team and didn’t kill the Mad Mage but another player did while you were present, you’ll get the message:<br />
{{Message|type=server|The mage has escaped, better start gobbling to face him again!}}

You’ll need to clean the fungus again in order to trigger a new Mad Mage spawn.

== Servant Raids ==

[[File:TMV3.png|thumb|left|350px|Iron Servants spawning south of the entrance]]<br />

Once the dungeon is fully cleaned, waves of Mage Servants will begin to spawn. These appear in groups of [[Iron Servant|Iron]], [[Golden Servant|Golden]], and [[Diamond Servant]]s.

A typical solo session contains approximately:
* 250 [[Iron Servant]]s
* 200 [[Golden Servant]]s
* 60 &nbsp;&nbsp;[[Diamond Servant]]s

A new wave will begin '''immediately''' after all Servants from the previous wave were killed.  
'''Important: All Servants must be killed for the next wave to spawn.'''

After clearing all waves, the [[Mad Mage]] will appear in one of three random locations:<br />
[[File:Mad Mage.gif|thumb|left|link=Mad Mage]]
* The northern corridor [COORD x=33355 y=31855 z=9 | here]
* The far eastern corridor [COORD x=33368 y=31874 z=9 | here]
* The south eastern corridor [COORD x=33349 y=31902 z=9 | here]

Once the [[Mad Mage]] is slain, the fungus will return to the dungeon — awaiting the next player willing to help the Servants again.
