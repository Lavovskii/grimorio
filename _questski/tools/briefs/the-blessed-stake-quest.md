{{Spoiler|name=The Blessed Stake Quest}}

== Foreword ==
* The first 5 lines of the prayer can be received by free accounts, however, the quest then sends you to [[Edron]] and therefore a premium account is required.
* This quest can take 20–30 minutes, but you can stop and continue at another time. Each step of the quest is recorded in your [[Quest Log]].
* You don't require the [[Meriana Quest]] to get the main reward ([[Blessed Wooden Stake]]), however you do need it to get the ''[[Blessed!]]'' achievement, and [[Chondur]] can bless you additional stakes (one immediately, each one after has one week cooldown)

=== Required Equipment ===
* 5,000 gp
* Travel money. Bring 1,100 gp for travel at least as you will be sent to many major cities.
*Optionally, another 5000 gp (see Method)

== Method ==
Go to [[Thais]] temple ([COORD x=32369 y=32238 z=7 | here]) and ask [[Quentin]] for a '''stake'''.

He will ask you to get a simple stake from [[Gamon]]. Go to the north-eastern store on [[Mill Avenue]] ([COORD x=32408 y=32172 z=7 | here]) and ask [[Gamon]] for a stake. He will charge you 5,000 gp.

'''Note: It is advised to buy two stakes right away, as you can make two Blessed Wooden Stakes on the same day for the first time (one from Tyrias, one from Chondur). That way you can keep one, and sell the other.'''

Go back to [[Quentin]] with the stake. Say '''Wooden Stake''' and receive his blessing. He will send you on your way to Carlin for the next blessing, who will send you to Ab'Dendriel, etc.. The following is the suggested route:

== Ability to deal with [[Chondur]] ==

After you have completed this quest, you can then take a [[Wooden Stake]] directly to [[Chondur]] ([COORD x=32360 y=32551 z=7 | here]), and he'll bless it. You can do this unlimited times but you must wait a week real time since your last blessing before repeating it.
