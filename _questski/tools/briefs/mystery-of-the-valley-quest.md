== Requirements ==
* Completed up to [[The_Ice_Islands_Quest#Nibelor_5:_Cure_the_Dogs|Cure the Dogs]] mission of the [[The Ice Islands Quest]] to access [[Inukaya]];
* 2x {{Ilink|Ham}} [[Ham]]s (access to [[Grimlund]] area in [[Nibelor]] via sled);
* Completed up to [[The_New_Frontier_Quest#Mission_08:_An_Offer_You_Can't_Refuse|An Offer You Can't Refuse]] mission of the [[The New Frontier Quest]] to access [[Muggy Plains]];
* ''Optional:'' access to [[Azzilon Castle]] (check [[No Rest for the Wicked Quest]]).
** <small>{{Post|39507367|It is possible}} to proceed in this quest without going to Azzilon and speaking to [[Lai]].</small>

== Mystery of the Valley ==
Travel to [[Azzilon Castle]] from [[Edron]] and meet with [[Lai]]. Ask her about {{KW|statue}} and she will tell you about an ancient secret related to Merudri civilization and a mysterious valley. She tells you the valley was once forbidden to outsiders, and about a legend on a trespassing adventurer who saw a Merudri pilgrim using a statue with three dragons holding a crystal to travel back to the valley.

Find the three fragments and assemble the {{ilink|Guardian Statue (Quest)}} [[Guardian Statue (Quest)]].

=== First Fragment ===
<div style="float:right;">[MINIMAP x=32458 y=31089 z=7 marks=none]</div>
Head to [[Nibelor]] and use the sled ([COORD x=32367 y=31058 z=7 | here]) to access the east part of the island, [[Inukaya]]. You need to have {{Ilink|Ham}} [[Ham]]s in your backpack to go to and from this area, so it is recommended to have at least two with you.

Walk a bit more east and [[levitate]] up this mountain ([COORD x=32458 y=31089 z=7 | here]) twice. At the top, use the shrine and you will find the {{Ilink|Fragment_of_a_Statue_(1st)}} [[Fragment_of_a_Statue_(1st)|first fragment of a statue]].

=== Second Fragment ===
<div style="float:right;">[MINIMAP x=33198 y=31184 z=7 marks=none]</div>
[[Lai]] tells you the second shrine is in an area with lots of green, insects buzzing, heat shimmering, bamboo trees, and grass. Head to [[Muggy Plains]] in [[Zao]] and go up the stairs [COORD x=33198 y=31184 z=7 | here].

Use the shrine and you will find the {{Ilink|Fragment_of_a_Statue_(2nd)}} [[Fragment_of_a_Statue_(2nd)|second fragment of a statue]].

=== Third Fragment ===
<div style="float:right;">[MINIMAP x=33838 y=31710 z=7 marks=none]</div>
[[Lai]] tells you the third shrine is in a dry area with large rocks and cliffs, where a large river flows through the land and saturates the barren landscape, and with someone walking around not far from it.

Head to the Southern Steppe in [[Kilmaresh]], go up the stairs [COORD x=33838 y=31710 z=7 | here], and [[levitate]] up a platform west from NPC [[Tefrit]]. Use the shrine and you will find the {{Ilink|Fragment_of_a_Statue_(3rd)}} [[Fragment_of_a_Statue_(3rd)|third fragment of a statue]].

=== Crystal Fragments ===
<div style="float:right;">[MINIMAP x=33094 y=32690 z=7 marks=none]</div>
[[Lai]] congratulates you for gathering all missing pieces of the statue but tells you need a few more crystal fragments from the original statue found atop a rock in [[Kha'zeel]].

Head to [[Ankrahmun]] desert and [[levitate]] up the mountains to the west where you can find some [[Energy Elemental]]s ([COORD x=33094 y=32690 z=7 | here], and then [COORD x=33082 y=32704 z=6 | here]). Walk a bit north and levitate a last time ([COORD x=33084 y=32669 z=4 | here]).

Use one of the three pieces of the statue from your backpack in the crystal fragments on the floor and the {{ilink|Guardian Statue (Quest)}} [[Guardian Statue (Quest)]] is assembled.
