## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Mad Mage Room Quest}}

===Required Equipment===
*[[Rope]]
*[[Shovel]]
*[[Key 3620]] (to get to [[A Prisoner]] in order to get [[Key 3666]])
*7 [[Red Apple]]s (for [[Key 3666]])

===Method===
* The first thing you need to do is go to the Prison in [[Mintwallin]] and talk to [[A Prisoner]], to get the [[Key 3620|key]] to his room, you need to go to the barracks, south of [[Markwin]]. You then need to give 7 apples to A Prisoner to change them for the [[Key 3666|key]] to the quest room.

* Ask him for the '''riddle'''. There is more than one answer to this, but the answer he is looking for can be found in a [[The Riddle (Book)|book]] in the mad mage room (in a bookcase to the north-east). You need to give the correct order in which a [[Knight]], [[Druid]], [[Paladin]] and [[Sorcerer]] can cross a bridge when being hunted by orcs. The correct answer is "PD-D-KS-P-PD". You receive silver [[Key 3666]] after giving the apples.

* '''This is the path to the Mad Mage room from the surface. You will walk past this room on your way to [[Mintwallin]].'''
* Go to the [[Ancient Temple]], north of [[Thais]] ([COORD x=32356 y=32171 z=7 | here]).
* Go down two levels, and head as far north as you can go.
* Go down to the level with the [[Rotworm]]s.
* Follow this path to the east and south, and go down the ladder:
[[Image:Route Mintwallin 1.png|450px]]

* On this level, there will be a lot of [[Poison Field]]s. You can either run through them and then use [[Antidote]], or you can use [[Destroy Field]] to clear a path.
* Go down another level.
* Follow this path to the east and north. Be careful of [[Minotaur]]s. Go down the hole that is circled on the map. You will face a couple [[Cyclops|Cyclopes]] down this hole:
[[Image:Route Mintwallin 2.png]]

* Head south down the left most passage. Go up the hole to a small room and up again to another room. Here go to the end of this passage and down the hole to a small room and down again:
[[Image:Route Mintwallin 3.png]]

* Head south then west then north then take a right heading towards the level door.
[[Image:Mad Mage Room Quest Map 04.png]]

* Head down the stairs BUT BEWARE!!!! There is at least one [[dragon]], one [[Giant Spider|giant spider]], one [[Bonelord]], 3 [[Demon Skeleton|demon skeletons]], one [[ghoul]], one [[scorpion]], and one [[skeleton]]. After clearing this room head to the southeast and through the locked door, using a key taken from [[A Prisoner]].
[[Image:Mad Mage Room Quest Map 05.jpg]]

* Once you are through the door, go up the ladder and face 3 [[Demon Skeleton]]s. The rewards are in [[Chest (Quest)|chest]]s in this room.

* You can look around there, once a day you can find [[Energy Ring]], [[Silver Dagger]], [[Strong Mana Potion]]s, [[Blank Rune]]s, [[Throwing Star]]s and food.
