## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Dragon Tower Quest}}
First, you need to make your way to [[Shadowthorn]] in the swamps south-east of [[Venore]] (follow the directions on the [[Shadowthorn]] page).

From here you can either go 1) above ground or 2) underground:
----
'''1. Above Ground'''<br>
Enter the city and go immediately to the west and up the ladder.

[[Image:Dragon Tower Quest Map 01.jpg]]

Follow the path west and south until you can see the large tower in the middle of the encampment, then go down the ladder.

[[Image:Dragon Tower Quest Map 02.jpg]]

'''2. Underground'''<br>
Enter the city and go immediately to the east and down the ladder.

[[Image:Dragon Tower Quest Map 03.jpg]]

Follow the passage all the way south-west and go up the ladder.

[[Image:Dragon Tower Quest Map 04.jpg]]
----
Head east to the large tower and start going up.

[[Image:Dragon Tower Quest Map 05.jpg]]

The monsters are as follows:

First floor : 3 Elf Scouts and 1 Elf

Second floor : 4 Elf Scouts and 2 Elf Arcanists

Third floor : 2 Dragons (only accessible from above, they cannot reach you)

Fourth floor : 6 Elf Scouts and 3 Elf Arcanists

Fifth floor: 3 Elf Scout and 2 Elf Arcanists

The rewards will be in two [[box]]es in the last floor.
