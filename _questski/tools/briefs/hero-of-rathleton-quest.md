## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Hero of Rathleton Quest}}

== Requirements ==
* Rank of ''Squire'' in the [[Rathleton Quest]]

== Method ==
Go to the [[Underground Glooth Factory]], walk west and go all the way down in the "pit". Find the [[Strange Pipe]] in the west and talk to him.

=== A voice in the darkness ===
<div style="float: right; padding: 0 0 0.5em 0.5em;">[MINIMAP x=33640 y=31963 z=14 marks=(33598,31929) (33598,31960) (33614,31989) (33635,31958) (33661,31937) (33674,31989) (33693,31929) (33711,31961) (33726,31953)]</div>
You will need a moderately leveled team. You can take as many or as few team members as you like but you are unlikely to make it far with fewer.

Exit the plant through the eastern [[Door (Sealed)|quest door]] to enter [[Jaccus Maxxen's Dungeon]]. To the far east there is a [[Magic Forcefield]]. To enter the forcefield, your team needs to turn on all '''8''' [[Strange Machine]]s and enter before they reset. The [[Strange Machine]]s are found on the way to the forcefield. There are 3 on the north side, 3 in the middle and 2 on the south side. A team of 2 could reasonably do this by running through a freshly cleared spawn.

When you have used all the [[Strange Machine]]s, enter the portal to the far east. You will be in a small room with a teleporter to the east. You have 10 minutes to kill all the waves '''and''' the boss. Wait a while and [[Glooth Anemone]] will start spawning. There will be 5-7(?) waves of [[Glooth Anemone]]s and the waves will occur even before you clear the last wave. Eventually, 4 [[Tentacle of the Deep Terror|Tentacles of the Deep Terror]] will spawn. They have a lot of health and will retreat at 1000-2000(?) health. You must kill them all at once. When the first one dies, there is a 10-second window before it spawns again with full health. If you can kill them all within that 10 second window, the [[Deep Terror]] will spawn. Kill the [[Deep Terror]] to progress...

=== The plot thickens ===
<div style="float: right; padding: 0 0 0.5em 0.5em;">
[MINIMAP x=33660 y=31947 z=15 marks=(33704,31963) (33679,31949) (33675,31931) (33650,31945) (33641,31964) (33636,31932) (33606,31935) (33582,31960) (33570,31949)]
</div>
After the [[Deep Terror]] defeat, you must enter the portal to the east of his room. You will be teleported to another large hall with another 8 [[Strange Machine]]s. Before you do anything, talk with the [[Strange Pipe]] to ensure you can enter the next boss room. The deal is the same: turn the machines on and enter the teleport. The teleport is on the far west. When you enter the portal a few waves of 1 [[Rot Elemental]], 1 [[Devourer]] and 1 [[Glooth Blob]] will spawn. Then, a single [[Feeble Glooth Horror]]s will spawn. Kill it, and it will multiply into 2 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]]s (2 total). Kill each of those and 2 [[Glooth Horror]]s will spawn (4 total). Kill each of those and 2 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s will spawn (8 total). Finally, kill those and each of them will turn into 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (16 total). Kill all of these to activate the portal to the west. When all of the Glooth Horror variations have been slain, enter the portal to the west.

You do not need to kill all of the horrors at once, which is extremely deadly without a very large team, and in fact the [[Strange Pipe]] discourages that. Unfortunately heeding the NPC's advice is impractical because the 10 minute limitation is usually not long enough to execute a safer strategy. The safest strategy would be to kill the strongest first, meaning single-target combat all the way, thus:
# Kill 1 [[Feeble Glooth Horror]] (2 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 1 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]] (1 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]], 2 [[Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 1 [[Glooth Horror]] (1 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]], 1 [[Glooth Horror]], 2 [[Strong Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] ('''1 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]], 1 [[Glooth Horror]], 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]], 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]''')
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (1 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]], 1 [[Glooth Horror]], 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (1 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]], 1 [[Glooth Horror]], 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (1 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]], 1 [[Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 1 [[Glooth Horror]] (1 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]], 2 [[Strong Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (1 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]], 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (1 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 1 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]] (2 [[Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 1 [[Glooth Horror]] (1 [[Glooth Horror]], 2 [[Strong Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (1 [[Glooth Horror]], 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]], 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (1 [[Glooth Horror]], 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (1 [[Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 1 [[Glooth Horror]] (2 [[Strong Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]], 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s

This strategy is the safest but also takes the longest. If your team has insufficient single-target firepower they will be kicked out after 10 minutes. Because of this the usual strategy is to just kill them all at once, so:

# Kill 1 [[Feeble Glooth Horror]] (2 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 2 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]]s (4 [[Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 4 [[Glooth Horror]]s (8 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 8 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s ('''16 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s''')
# Kill 16 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s

You could possibly mix strategies. The first one is too slow and the second one is too hard, so you could also try, for example:
# Kill 1 [[Feeble Glooth Horror]] (2 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 2 [[Weakened Glooth Horror]]s (4 [[Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 4 [[Glooth Horror]]s (8 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] ('''7 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s, 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s''')
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (7 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (6 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s, 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (6 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (5 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s, 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (5 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (4 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s, 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (4 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (3 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s, 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (3 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (2 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s, 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (2 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s, 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s (1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]])
# Kill 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] (2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s)
# Kill 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s

The idea with this strategy is to utilize area attacks as much as possible while also keeping the [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s away. The blockers will need to handle, at worst, 7 [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s and 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s. The team can use area attacks until the [[Strong Glooth Horror]]s have low health, and then change to attack 1 [[Strong Glooth Horror]] and the 2 [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s that it creates.

=== Into the fire ===
<div style="float: right;">
[MINIMAP x=33461 y=31955 z=15 marks=(33514,31957) (33455,31932) (33469,31965) (33442,31981) (33427,31948) (33382,31928) (33375,31981) (33369,31955) (33515,31933)]
</div>
After slaying the Glooth Horrors, you must enter the portal to the west of his room. You will be teleported to yet another large hall with another set of [[Strange Machine]]s. Talk to the [[Strange Pipe]] who is a little south of where you appear enter, and clear your way to the portal to the far west. Use the [[Strange Machine]]s to activate the portal and enter to arrive in a large room with a short lava border.

In a few moments, the lava will spread and [[Raging Fire]]s will spawn. You must kill these and use their [[Magma Chunk|bodies]] {{Ilink|Magma Chunk}} on the [[Crucible]] {{Ilink|Crucible}} to the north-west with the limited time and space you have. For the first wave, you have 60 seconds to kill 6; for the second, you have 50 seconds to kill 5; for the third, you have 40 seconds to kill 4. 30 seconds after killing the third wave you will be teleported to the next area and receive the achievement [[Go with da Lava Flow]].

''Note'': The number of [[Raging Fire]]s that spawn is random, so it may happen that not enough of them spawn when all rounds finish. If this happens or if your team fails to use enough bodies on the [[Crucible]] in time, you will be sent back to the western teleport in the previous area, through which you came. You'll then have to turn on all [[Strange Machine]]s of this area again to restart.

=== The root of all evil ===
<div style="float: right; padding: 0 0 0.5em 0.5em;">
[MINIMAP x=33640 y=32065 z=15 marks=(33607,32031) (33619,32055) (33629,32043) (33610,32094) (33673,32095) (33670,32033) (33642,32082) (33662,32060)]
</div>
You will have been teleported to another area after succeeding in the last mission. Talk to the [[Strange Pipe]] which is very close to the teleport, before turning on all the [[Strange Machine]]s in the area and entering the teleport to the east.

Turn the [[Strange Machine]]s on and enter the portal to the east.

You will arrive in a large square room with 4 glooth generators away from the wall at each corner. A few seconds after entering, there will be a few waves of [[Glooth Golem]]s before the final boss, [[Professor Maxxen]], spawns.

The 4 generators on each corner will become [[Glooth-Generator]]s after a certain period of time. The south-west will spawn some time after [[Professor Maxxen]] spawns; the south-east will spawn 35 seconds after the first spawns, the north-east will spawn 30 seconds after the second spawns, and the north-west will spawn 25 seconds after the third spawns. You must plan ahead of time how to deal with them. As soon as the [[Glooth-Generator]]s spawn, and a player is around them, they will announce {{Message|type=sound|THE GLOOTH GENERATOR CHARGES UP FOR A LETHAL EXPLOSION}}; 14–15 seconds later, if the [[Glooth-Generator]]s is still alive, 4 [[Energy Pulse]]s will spawn at the generator locations and explode immediately after ({{Message|type=sound|The fully charged generator explodes in a blast!}}), dealing a massive amount of energy damage (5600) to everyone in a 6 squares radius before also disappearing.

The recommended strategy to deal with the [[Glooth-Generator]]s is have the whole team attack it as soon as it spawns. Despite of that, all players should be prepared to reduce the 5600 damage and absorb the remaining using Energy protection equipment and [[Magic Shield]], for example. 

'''Note:''' The [[Glooth-Generator]]s do '''not''' activate their "charging up" phase if players are not around them. This can be used as a strategy if the whole team stays at the southern side of the room, but it can also be very dangerous since if one player gets into an active [[Glooth-Generator]] sight it will immediately summon the [[Energy Pulse]]s which in turn will explode. 

[[Professor Maxxen]] will summon 2 [[Walker]]s, and has 3 accomplices fight alongside him: [[Glooth Masher]], [[Glooth Slasher]], [[Glooth Trasher]] (not summons). During the fight, [[Professor Maxxen]] will be teleported to a different part of the room a few times.

=== Justice served ===

When [[Professor Maxxen]] is defeated, the team can enter the portal to the east to arrive in the reward room. Talk to the [[Strange Pipe]] there and obtain the rewards from the [[Box]] to the north. You will receive a [[Bag]] with the items listed below. You'll also receive the achievement ''[[The Professor's Nut]]''. Now that you've obtained the rewards, you may use the teleporters on the north-east side of the first area ([COORD x=33716 y=31931 z=14 | here]) which will allow you to skip some of the quest (to redo a specific area). The team can now leave by entering the [[Slime Slide]] to the east, which will bring you back to [[Rathleton]].

=== Shortcuts ===

After finishing the quest you can use the three [[Magic Forcefield]]s [COORD x=33716 y=31932 z=14 | east] of the first room as shortcuts to skip some of the puzzles and bosses if you want to go to a specific part of the dungeon to hunt or kill a boss. 

* The west teleport takes you to the entrance of the second room (where you go after defeating the [[Deep Terror]]), [COORD x=33743 y=31941 z=15 | here].
* The center teleport takes you to the entrance of the third room (where you go after defeating the [[Empowered Glooth Horror]]s), [COORD x=33536 y=31956 z=15 | here].
* The east teleport takes you to the entrance of the fourth room (where you go after the lava room puzzle), [COORD x=33610 y=32055 z=15 | here].
