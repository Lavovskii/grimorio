## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Targuna Quest}}
== Requirements ==
* To begin '''A long lost treasure''' with [[Sterling]] you must be Level 10.
* A rope and a shovel (or equivalents).

* To begin '''Burning heat''' with [[Emiliana]] you must be Level 12.

== Method ==

=== An ancient enemy ===
Talk to [[Lizzie]] in south part of [[Targuna]]. Head north to the and enter the [[Hidden Lizard Temple]] through a staircase [COORD x=31936 y=31863 z=7 | here]. Go one level lower and defeat the [[Lizard Commander]]. Enter the portal to return to [[Targuna]] and talk to [[Lizzie]]. Go to the depot and go up through the stairs to talk to [[Camilla]]. You will receive 2 platinum coins and 1500 experience points.

=== A long lost Treasure ===
Speak to [[Sterling]], you will find him at the west side of [[Targuna]]. Travel to [[Aragonia]] using the sea turtle. In [[Aragonia]] kill at least 20 pirates and collect 10 tortoise eggs.The eggs are scattered around the island and you just need to click on them. Then talk to [[Morla]] in the [COORD x=33516 y=32747 z=7 | south cave of Argonia]. You will receive an [[Old Treasure Map]]. Return to Targuna and talk to [[Sterling]]. You receive 3000 experience points and a mark appears on the map. Go to the mark [COORD x=31948 y=31924 z=7 | here], use a shovel and grab the [[Adventurer Backpack]] from the chest. 

=== Burning heat ===
Talk to [[Emiliana]] in the room above the training dummy in [[Targuna]]. Accept the mission and go to the room above. Enter the portal to get to the [[Crimson Court]]. Talk to [[Emiliana]] again and say "mission". Descend to through the [[Crimson Court]] until you reach the portal to fight [[Herald of Fire]]. Before reaching the portal there are fire walls that can be disabled by pressing the pressure plates that are on the sides of the dungeon, but you can just cross through the fire by taking burning damage. Enter the portal, stand in the center of the room and press the lever. You will be teleported to a boss fight with [[Herald of Fire]]. Defeat him and enter the purple portal to exit the dungeon, then talk to [[Emiliana]] again. You will receive 5000 experience points and [[Bunch of Turnips]]. Give the [[Bunch of Turnips]] to the ram in the [COORD x=31926 y=31896 z=7 | west side of] [[Targuna]] in order to obtain the [[Reliable Ram]] mount and the [[A reliable Friend]] achievement.

In case you lost the [[Bunch of Turnips]] you can talk to [[Emiliana]] and pay 1000 gold to receive them again.
