## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler|name=The Order of the Lion Quest}}

== Requirements ==

* Completed the [[Order of the Falcon]] access as part of [[The Secret Library Quest]]
* Completed the [[Order of the Cobra]] access as part of the [[Grave Danger Quest]]
* 20 pieces of {{Ilink|Ham}} [[Ham]]
* 10 pieces of {{Ilink|Bread}} [[Bread]]
* 20 {{Ilink|Broken Longbow}} [[Broken Longbow]]s

== Method ==

''Note'': This quest is dependent on the Tibian day/night cycle and will often require you to wait for the next cycle or a full day, so completing it may take a few hours even if the missions themselves don't require that much time. 
''Note²'': In [[Bounac]] you will encounter both [[Members of the Order of the Lion]] and Usurpers which are trying to take control of the Order. These two groups will fight each other, and the Lions will not attack you, which makes moving around the castle considerably easier. Note, however, that if you kill one of the Lions you'll be sent to [COORD x=32411 y=32483 z=7 | jail] for a few seconds.

Travel to [[Edron]] and find the small boat north of the mountain pass on the location shown to the right.

=== Bounac Residents ===

==== Evrard the Miller ====

'''Note''': This first part can only be done during the Tibian [[day]] (between hh:15 and hh:45 in real-life time). 

<div style="float:right">[MINIMAP x=32421 y=32463 z=7 marks=none]</div>
Find the NPC [[Evrard the Miller]] in the mill-house (shown on the map) and answer '''yes''' to his questions. He will ask for your help looking for two things in the Usurpers camp.

Go down the stairs [COORD x=32427 y=32461 z=7 | here], then follow the cave south-east and go up to the surface again. Here you'll have to find two items:

* The ''Ledger'' (a Parchment) is located inside a book in the small house south of the boat:
[MINIMAP x=32469 y=32503 z=7 marks=none]

* The ''Map'' is in the boat, on the lower floor (levitate down in front of the stairs), in a box. 
[MINIMAP x=32483 y=32494 z=7 marks=none]

Return to Evrad the Miller and tell him of your findings. You will now be granted access to the southern side of the island. Go down the same stairs as before, but this time follow all the way south and go up the stairs. Around here, you'll have to find 5 NPCs, and each one will ask you for a small task.

==== NPCs Locations ====

All Bounac NPCs have two locations depending whether it's day or night. For the 5 NPCs below, you can use the following maps to find them based on the time of the day. For this part, the NPCs will deliver and receive the tasks regardless of their position.

[[File:The Order of the Lion Quest - NPCs Day.png]]<br/><br/>
[[File:The Order of the Lion Quest - NPCs Night.png]]

==== [[Wes the Blacksmith]] ====

After greeting him, use the keyword '''Yselda''' to receive and deliver your task, which is to bring him 20 {{Ilink|Broken Longbow}} [[Broken Longbow]]s that you have to loot from the [[Usurper Archer]].

==== [[Jehan the Baker]] ====

Say hi, then '''Yselda'''. Jehan needs the 10 loafs of {{Ilink|Bread}} [[Bread]].

==== [[Onfroi]] (Wizard) ====

Say hi, then '''Yselda''' and he will ask you to kill 20 [[Crypt Warrior]] that roam the ground floor of the island at night. 

Note that the kill count is assigned to whom did the most damage, so if you are doing this quest as a group each player should kill the whole quantity independently.

==== [[Dal the Huntress]] ====

Say hi, then '''Yselda''' and she will ask you to kill 20 [[Deer]] (there are some around Bounac as well). 

If you are doing this part as a group, the same rules of Onfroi's mission applies to the kill count.

==== [[Fral the Butcher]] ====

Say hi, then '''Yselda''' and deliver the 20 {{Ilink|Ham}} [[Ham]].

=== Kesar the Younger ===

After gaining the trust of Bounac residents, you will be allowed into the castle. To do so, go to the third floor using the stairs where [[Onfroi]] is during day, follow north, then west until you find the [[Bounac Guard]]. Say '''hi''', '''pass''', '''yes'''. 

Go two floors upstairs, where [[Kesar the Younger]] stands on the left side of the room (but only during [[Time|day time]]). Ask him about the {{KW|siege}}. Players then have to wait at least 10 minutes for him to take care of their request. Note, that if you talk to Kesar before that time has passed, the timer will reset and you will have to wait another 10 minutes. So it is advisable to wait 11 minutes just to be sure the timer will not reset. When the required time has passed, talk to Kesar about {{KW|siege}} and say {{KW|yes}} to his proposal. Now you have to wait for [[Time|one Tibian day]] (one hour in real life) for him to take care of your request. After this time has passed, during the next night cycle, when players stay or enter Kesar's room, then [[Fugue]] will spawn along with 5 [[Usurper Warlock]]s. Kill [[Fugue]] and wait until it is day time again to talk to [[Kesar the Younger]]. 

''Note'': To leave the castle, go to the floor below the throne room, then walk the ramp south and walk into the elevator. You can now also use this elevator as a shortcut to the castle. 

<gallery mode="nolines">
The Order of the Lion Quest - Fugue.png

Talk to Kesar again about the '''siege'''. He will ask you to accompany him to the fields on the eastern side of the island to meet with Drume. Kesar will teleport you to a small room where you'll watch his meeting with Drume. After that, you'll have to talk to Kesar about the '''siege''' once more. Seeing as the peace negotiations were not successful, you'll have to fight [[Drume]] in the field.

'''Note:''' If you have not completed [[Order of the Falcon]] and [[Order of the Cobra]] access quests until this point, Kesar will instead ask you to investigate about the other Orders.

=== Drume ===

[[File:The Order of the Lion Quest - Drume|thumb]]
{{FightTips
|{{FightTip|type=use|element=Death|text=Specially on Commanders}}
|{{FightTip|type=avoid|element=Earth}}
|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Earth}}
|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Death}}
}}

Go to the area east of the island again and up the ramp, where you can team with up to 4 other players to fight [[Drume]]. 

After entering the fields, you'll find yourself among several members of the Order of the Lion. There are three squads of each side (Lions and Usurpers). Your first objective is to kill the 3 [[Usurper Commander]]s. The regular Usurpers and Lions will attack each other, but the [[Usurper Commander]] will always target you. Since they do not spawn at the exact same place, you can move south-west slowly as to only lure one of them at a time. Once a Commander dies, its squad will disappear.

In order to help you find the Commanders in the battlefield you can set your [[Battle Window]] to ''Sort Descending by Name'', and this way the Commanders will always be the first in it. 

''Note'': If all three [[Lion Commander]]s die, you will fail the battle and be kicked out. 

After killing the Usurper Commanders, [[Kesar]] and [[Drume]] will spawn. [[Kesar]] won't move, but can be targeted by Drume. Drume will target any Lion or you, and retarget somewhat frequently. You can use that to your advantage and use the Lions as blockers to mitigate the damage you take. It's also possible that [[Grand Chaplain Gaunder]], [[Grand Canon Dominus]], [[Grand Commander Soeren]] or [[Preceptor Lazare]] spawn with Drume. If this happens, beware that the Falcon aid will always target the same creature (or player) that [[Drume]] does. 

After that, talk to [[Kesar the Younger]] again. You'll receive the [[Lionheart]] [[Achievement]] and the '''Hero of Bounac''' [[Title]] (upon next login). You will also be granted access to all areas within Bounac, including the [[Depot]] ([COORD x=32391 y=32489 z=7 | here]) and the ability to trade with Bounac NPCs.
