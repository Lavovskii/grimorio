== Method ==
When this Mini World Change is active it's possible to enter the [[Energy Gate]] in [[Vengoth]] [COORD x=32949 y=31520 z=7 | here]. In there you can get the chance to fight [[Devovorga]]'s incarnations in exchange for [[Tentacle Piece]]s. Talk to the [[Dread Guardian]] and ask him about '''essence''', then '''yes'''. Next, say the name of the boss you want to fight and enter the [[Energy Gate]] to the south. 

Note that unlike during the Devovorga event, only one person can enter the teleport at at time, so you can't kill the bosses with a team during this Mini World Change.

For those focusing on [[Bosstiary]] points, it's recommended to only kill the three weakest bosses [[Anmothra]], [[Irahsae]] and [[Teneshpar]], which will require 75 [[Tentacle Piece]]s in total. [[Chikhaton]] and [[Phrodomo]] require more pieces and are harder to kill, with a risk of wasting the tentacles depending on your level. Furthermore, they can be easily killed several times with teams when the [[Rise of Devovorga]] event is active.
