==Required Equipment==
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Rope]]

==Method==
'''Note:''' At the level range of 8~40, this cave has some difficult creatures, and the rewards are not terribly exciting. It is recommended to get a team together and complete all of the quests in this cave at one time, instead of making multiple trips.

* Go down into the [[Edron Orc Cave]]. The entrance is southwest of the castle ([COORD x=33173 y=31900 z=7 | here]). You can access this area via a drain in the basement of the Edron Flats.
* Once in the hole, you will meet only [[Orc]]s, [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Orc Spearmen]] and [[Orc Warrior]]s. Follow the cave to the west and then south. There are a couple of side rooms, but basically there is only one way to go.
[[Image:Dark Armor Quest Map 01.jpg]]
* On the next level, go west and take the passage north. You will meet [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Orc Spearmen]], [[Orc Warrior]]s and probably one or two lured [[Orc Berserker]]s. Go into the first room on the east. Drop down the hole.
[[Image:Dark Armor Quest Map 02.jpg]]
* As soon as you go down this hole, you will be surrounded by [[Orc Shaman]]s. Remember, [[Energy Damage|energy attacks]] do not work on [[Orc Shaman|shamans]], so use AoE runes to clear the room (the summoned snakes make it hard to aim, and your knights can't get through them to attack the Shaman).
* On this level, go north, meeting more [[Orc Shaman|shamans]] and one or two [[Orc Berserker]]s. You will walk over a tile-floor area - this means you are on the right track. At the northern end of the floor, drop down another hole.
[[Image:Dark Armor Quest Map 03.jpg]]
* On this floor you will meet [[Orc Shaman]]s, [[Orc Berserker]]s, [[Orc Leader]]s and alot of [[Poison Spider]]s, in the chest - as shown on the picture - you can find a [[backpack]] with 2 [[Poison Dagger]]s and 30 [[Poison Arrow]]s.
[[Image:Poison Daggers Quest Map 04.jpg]]
