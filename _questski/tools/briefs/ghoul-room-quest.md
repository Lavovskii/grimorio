## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Ghoul Room Quest}}
===Required Equipment===
* Recommended [[Destroy Field]] or using [[Antidote]] for [[Poison Field]]s

===Method===
* Go to [[Thais]] [[Ancient Temple]]
* Down 2 levels and head North - all the way to the northern end of the large room
* Go down to the [[Rotworm]]s, and follow this path to the east, south, and east, and go down the ladder. Watch out for [[Orc]]s, [[Orc Spearman|Orc Spearmen]], [[Orc Warrior]]s and possible lured [[Fire Devil]]:
[[Image:Route Mintwallin 1.png|450px]]

* This room will be filled with [[Poison Field]]s. You can either run for it or use [[Destroy Field]] to get through.
* Once through the poison fields, go down the ladder
* Follow a small tunnel to the east and then north:
[[Image:Ghoul Room Quest 2.png]]

* After you come to a room with [[Skeleton]]s and [[Rotworm]]s, "Open" a dead skeleton in the South-East of the room - you will get [[Key 3600]]
* When you have the key, "Use" the well in that room to go down it.
* Kill 3 [[Ghoul]]s, unlock the door with [[Key 3600]], get your reward.
