==Required Equipment==
* [[Rope]]
* [[Shovel]]

==Method==
* Find the loose stone pile west of [[Kazordoon]] ([COORD x=32448 y=31993 z=7 | here]). Use your shovel, and go down the hole. On the surface you will find [[Dwarf|Dwarves]], [[Dwarf Soldier]]s, [[Minotaur]]s, and [[Minotaur Archer]]s.

* Once down the hole, you will encounter [[Minotaur]]s, and [[Minotaur Archer]]s. Follow the passage to the north-west until you find a room with beds in it. The quest box is located between a couple of beds:
