## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=The Ancient Tombs Quest}}
== Requirements ==
* At least 8 [[Scarab Coin]]s (bring extra for safety).
* Strong team recommended: ~5 players level 75+ or 3 players level 150+.
* [[Dwarven Ring]] (important for [[Dipthrah]]).

== Introduction ==
This quest involves slaying all of the original [[Pharaohs]] to obtain all the helmet pieces used to reconstruct the [[Helmet of the Ancients]]. Each pharaoh except [[Ashmunrah]] drop an item each time they are slain, a pass item which will alter the destination of the [[Magic Forcefield]] in the pharaoh's room. Normally when entering the [[Magic Forcefield]] the player will be taken to the start of the tomb; when this pass item is held by a character, the portal will instead lead to a small room with the pharaoh's [[Sarcophagus]], from which the player can obtain the pharaoh's helmet piece. Each of these helmet pieces can only be obtained once per character. '''Quest teams will have to kill every pharaoh at least once for each person needing a helmet piece'''. The final boss, [[Ashmunrah]], guards the chamber where the helmet must be reconstructed, and thus does not possess a piece of the helmet, does not drop any pass item, and only needs to be slain once.

The [[Pharaohs]] reside deep down below the [[Ankrahmun Desert]], each in their own [[Ankrahmun Tombs|tomb]]. To enter the tombs, you must find the right place to dig in the sand with a [[Shovel]] or equivalent functioning tool. Finding the hole is only the first challenge. You must make a sacrifice, a [[Scarab Coin]], to be able to access the deeper parts of each tomb. These can be obtained by digging specific locations in the sand north of [[Ankrahmun]], or as loot from various monsters. To use the [[Scarab Coin]], step on top of the [[Mystic Flame]] in a tomb, usually below the second hunting floor, and place the [[Scarab Coin]] on the empty [[Coal Basin]].

This quest involves exploring all tombs and as such, a great variety of monsters will be encountered. Simple monsters like [[Skeleton]]s, [[Scarab]]s, [[Scorpion]]s, etc. are common to most tombs while [[Dragon]]s, [[Ancient Scarab]]s, [[Warlock]]s, [[Behemoth]]s and [[Lich]]es will be encountered in specific tombs.

The order that you slay the pharaohs in does not matter, with the exception of [[Ashmunrah]] which must be killed last. If your team is unsure they can handle it, attempt the pharaohs in order from least to most challenging. As a general guideline, that would be:

====Morguthis====
To find [[Morguthis]] enter the [[Tarpit Tomb]] that is located [COORD x=33232 y=32704 z=7 | here]. You can find details about that tomb [[Tarpit Tomb|here]]:<br />
[[File:Tombtar.jpg]]<br />

Go down to the flame teleport and enter it, then throw a [[Scarab Coin]] on the basin. The ramps to descend are immediately spotted when you reach each floor until the flame.

After entering the flame teleport follow the maps to the final hunting floor and another teleport.

[[File:Morguthis Map 1.jpg|200px]]

[[File:Morguthis Map 2.jpg|200px]]

Enter the level 75 door to arrive at Morguthis' arena, but there's a puzzle before you can reach Morguthis. There are [[Deathslicer]]s all around which cannot be killed (but they can be pushed). The arena also contains trapdoors. Trapdoors lead to caves which will all lead to different parts of the maze. To complete the puzzle you must step in each blue flame in the maze. This involves entering every single cave. Note that all flames, teleports and trapdoors are safe to enter. The only exceptions are the final teleport (which is the only teleport not inside a cave) and the first teleport (just before the level 75 doors). Each teleport will take you upstairs of a nearby 'sandstone switch tile' found nearby it - these correspond to trapdoors (i.e. you drop down to these by entering them) and each position you are taken to is safe (blocked off to prevent [[Deathslicer]]s from attacking). The only time you will face [[Deathslicer]]s is when moving through the sandstone part of the maze to enter a different trapdoor.

The following is the quickest route to take. Note that it is not imperative that you take this route, you can enter the flames in any order at all as long as you enter them all at least once.
