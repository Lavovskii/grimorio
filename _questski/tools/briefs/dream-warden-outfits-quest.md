## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler|name=Dream Warden Outfits Quest}}

== Requirements ==
* Access to [[Eruaran]] (server must have killed [[Gaz'haragoth]] on the central mountain)
* [[Dream Warden Mask]] (for addon 1 - mask)
* Addon 1 - to obtain addon 2 - claw
* [[Unrealized Dream]]s or [[Dream Warden Claw]] (for addon 2 - claw)

== Base Outfit ==
[[File:Outfit_Dream_Warden_Male.gif]][[File:Outfit Dream Warden Female.gif]]

The following missions of the [[Roshamuul Quest]] need to be completed in order to gain the base outfit:
*[[Roshamuul_Quest/Spoiler#A_Cup_of_Tea|A Cup of Tea]] ([[Morax]])
*[[Roshamuul_Quest/Spoiler#Ancient_Dreams|Ancient Dreams]] ([[Morax]])
*[[Roshamuul_Quest/Spoiler#Waters_of_Life|Waters of Life]] ([[Morax]])
*[[Roshamuul_Quest/Spoiler#Mend_What.27s_Broken|Mend What's Broken]] ([[Dream Butterfly]])

After completing those missions go to [[Eruaran]] to receive the outfit.

== Addon 1 (Mask) ==
[[File:Outfit_Dream_Warden_Male_Addon_1.gif]][[File:Outfit Dream_Warden Female_Addon_1.gif]]

Obtain a [[Dream Warden Mask]], dropped by the [[Roshamuul Prison]] bosses [[Gaz'haragoth]], [[Horadron]], [[Prince Drazzak]], [[Terofar]], and [[Zavarash]].

Bring the [[Dream Warden Mask]] to [[Eruaran]] to receive the addon.

== Addon 2 (Claw) ==
[[File:Outfit_Dream_Warden_Male_Addon_2.gif]][[File:Outfit Dream_Warden Female_Addon_2.gif]]

Obtain a [[Dream Warden Claw]] from [[Unrealized Dream]]s or from [[Gaz'haragoth]] and bring it to [[Eruaran]] to receive the addon.

== Reward ==
Besides the [[Dream Warden Outfits]] you will also achieve the [[Dream Warden]] achievement after completing this quest.
