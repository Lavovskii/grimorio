== Base Outfits ==

Finish [[Soul War Quest]] by defeating [[Goshnar's Megalomania]]. 

== First Addon ==

Use {{Ilink|The Skull of a Beast}} [[The Skull of a Beast]].<br />
You will get the following message:<br />
{{Sound|Now the beast is unleashed!}}

== Second Addon == 

Use a {{Ilink|Bracelet of Strengthening}} [[Bracelet of Strengthening]].<br />
You will get the following message:<br />
{{Sound|Wild power flows though your body!}}

Upon using both items you will get the achievement [[Unleash the Beast]].
