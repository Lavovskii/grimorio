## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name= Vampire Shield Quest}}

Go to the [[Hero Cave]], north of the city of [[Edron]] (1). You will face three [[Hunter]]s at the entrance. Go down the hole there. Watch out for [[Demon Skeleton]]s, [[Priestess]]es, and some minor [[creatures]]. Go down the next hole (2), and north to another hole in that room (3). This room contains a [[Monk (Creature)|Monk]], and some [[Demon Skeleton]]s, [[Dark Magician]]s, and [[Wild Warrior]]s. Here go east and north to the next hole (4). This room contains 3 [[Dragon]]s and some [[Dragon Hatchling]]s. The hole is in the middle of room (5). Here you can find a [[Hero]] and some minor creatures. Go around to the main floor (6).<br />

Be prepared to be swamped with creatures. Kill them, and head south to the end and then east. At the next crossroads are some [[Minotaurs]]. Take the south path and down the stairs (7).

[[File:Vampire_Shield_Quest_map.png]]<br />

This is the [[Temple of Xayepocax]]. Here you will face 3 [[Demon Skeleton]]s, 3 [[Monk (Creature)|Monk]]s, 3 [[Hunter]]s, 2 [[Hero]]es, 2 [[Priestess]]es, 2 [[Witch]]es, 2 [[Dark Magician]]s, 2 [[Dark Apprentice]]s, an [[Orc Shaman]], a [[Stalker]] and a [[Warlock]]. Once you have killed them, go to the back of the room. Enter the level 70 gate of expertise, and retrieve your reward of a [[Vampire Shield]] and a [[Dragon Lance]] from the [[Chest (Quest)|chest]]s.

There is also a quest box ''outside'' of the level 70 door. This quest box contains the [[Strange Symbol]], [[Black Pearl]] and [[Mysterious Fetish]] (8).
