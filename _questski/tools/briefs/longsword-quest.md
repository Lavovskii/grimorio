## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Longsword Quest}}

==Required Equipment==
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Rope]]

==Method==
* Go to the Troll cave east of the [[Dwarf Bridge]] ([COORD x=32662 y=31966 z=7 | here]). The cave entrance is hidden behind a tree.
* Once in the cave, head to the south and look for stairs down. Be careful of the [[Slime]]s to the north of the cave entrance. They are down a drain, but could be roped up to the main level.
[[Image:Longsword Quest Map 01.jpg]]
* Once on the next level, head north. The [[Minotaur Archer]] and [[Minotaur Mage]] are in the room to the north, but they might be lured to a different location.
[[Image:Longsword Quest Map 02.jpg]]
*The quest boxes are on the north end of the large room.
