==Required Equipment==
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Rope]]
* [[Machete]] (depending upon route)
* [[Pick]]
* Recommended [[Antidote]] or [[Antidote Rune]] for fighting poisonous creatures

==Method==
'''Note:''' There are a number of ways to reach this cave. Described here is the safest route.

* Make your way to the cave entrance in [[Green Claw Swamp]] ([COORD x=32700 y=31992 z=7 | here]). The quickest way is to come from the west (by the [[Dwarf Bridge]]). You'll need to fall down a partially hidden pitfall.
* Once in the cave, follow this route to the east. You will face [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Rotworm]]s and sometimes lured [[Bonelord]]. Go down the hole:
[[Image:Silver Brooch Quest Map 01.jpg]]
* Take this short passage to the south (there are no monsters here), and go down the hole:
[[Image:Silver Brooch Quest Map 02.jpg]]
* Follow this path to the east and south. There will be 3 [[Bonelord]]s in the larger room (you can also lure them 1 by 1 if you don't wish to waste). Once you have killed the [[Bonelord]]s, use your [[pick]] in the circled area on the map:
[[Image:Silver Brooch Quest Map 03.jpg]]
* When you go down into the pick hole, you will be faced with up to 5 [[Mummy|Mummies]] in a very small room. kill the [[Mummy|mummies]] and "Use" a coffin on the north end of this room to get your reward.
