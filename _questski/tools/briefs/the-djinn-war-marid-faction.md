## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name= The Djinn War - Marid Faction}}

'''Note''': Before starting ''The Djinn War'' quest, consider doing the quest [[To_Appease_the_Mighty_Quest/Spoiler#Method|To Appease the Mighty]]. Since this quest can only be completed before starting The Djinn War and rewards 20 [[Platinum Coin]]s.

== Introduction ==
There are two warring Djinn factions, the [[Blue Djinn]]s & [[Marid]]s up against the [[Green Djinn]]s & [[Efreet]]s. As part of the war both engage in conflict while also trading strong [[Equipment]] at competitive prices. However, they are selective in their dealings, preferring to trade only with those they trust. To gain their trust, you must pledge allegiance to either the Blue or Green Djinn faction (each offering distinct items). Once accepted, you can engage in buying and selling equipment with them.

=== Required Equipment ===
* {{Ilink|Cheese}} [[Cheese]] - Used to interact with [[Rata'mari]] in the [[Green Djinn Fortress]].
* {{Ilink|Cookbook}} [[Cookbook]] - Needed for a mission with [[Bo'ques]].

== Method ==
'''Note: You can only align with one of the Djinn factions (Blue or Green). If you prefer to side with the [[Efreet]] (Green), refer to the [[The_Djinn_War_-_Efreet_Faction/Spoiler#Method|Efreet Faction Quest Guide]].'''

* In [[Ankrahmun]], locate [[Melchior]] around [COORD x=33192 y=32820 z=7 | here] wandering the streets. Speak with him to learn about Djinn customs and the greeting word "{{KW|DJANNI'HAH}}". This word allows entry into either Djinn Fortress.

* To reach the [[Ashta'daramai|Blue Djinn Fortress]], take the ramp west of the Giant Spider hill ([COORD x=33120 y=32578 z=7 | here]) north of Ankrahmun.

* Navigate northwest until you encounter the Level 30 [[Door of Expertise]].

* Inside the fortress, greet [[Umar]] with "{{KW|DJANNI'HAH}}" and inform him you seek passage.

* Note: After initial contact, "Hi" suffices for subsequent interactions.

=== Marid Mission 1 ===
* After listening to him, proceed to the upper floor and speak with [[Bo'ques]]. Ask him for a '''{{KW|Mission}}'''; and he will ask you to retrieve a [[Cookbook]] from [[Maryza]] in [[Kazordoon]].

* Go to [[Kazordoon]] (if you do not have the cookbook already), and talk to [[Maryza]] in the Tavern (near the depot) by saying {{KW|Hi Maryza}}. The cookbook will cost you 150[[Gold Coin|gp]]. Also note that she will only sell one copy per player.

* Return to [[Bo'ques]], talk to him, and give him the [[Cookbook]]. {{Ilink|Cookbook}} He will reward you with 3 [[Small Sapphire]]s and tell you that [[Fa'hradin]] might have some work for you.

=== Marid Mission 2 ===
* Talk to [[Fa'hradin]] he is on the balcony on the same floor as [[Bo'ques]] [COORD x=33106 y=32541 z=5 | here], and ask for a '''{{KW|Mission}}'''. He will tell you to get a report from a spy they have in the Green Djinn fortress, and he will give you the password '''{{KW|PIEDPIPER}}'''.
'''Note: This is the first part; you must invade the opposing Djinn Tower. You ''cannot'' enter the back way of the Green Djinn Tower ''until'' you have acquired this mission.'''

* You will need to go to the [COORD x=33033 y=32620 z=6 | secret entrance] to the [[Green Djinn]] fortress. Besure to bring the [[Cheese]] {{Ilink|Cheese}} with you.

* First, go up the ramp west of the Oasis [COORD x=33101 y=32657 z=7 | here].

* Instead of going west to the front door of the Green Djinn fortress, follow the path [COORD x=33060 y=32594 z=6 | north], around to the back of the fortress. You will go up and down a few ramps and face some [[Lion]]s, [[Hyaena]]s, [[Cobra]]s, [[Scorpion]]s, and a [[Wyvern]].

* When you reach the back door, go inside.

* After killing the [[Hyaena]]s and the [[Green Djinn]], go down the steps.

* Here you will face 3 [[Green Djinn]]s. Kill them all and talk to the rat NPC [[Rata'mari]]. Say the password {{KW|PIEDPIPER}} instead of saying ''Hi''. Then ask him for the {{KW|[[Spy Report]]}}, then say {{KW|Yes}} to give him the [[Cheese]].

* Take the [[Spy Report]] {{Ilink|Spy Report}} back to [[Fa'hradin]] in the [[Blue Djinn]] fortress. When you give him the spy report, he will tell you to go speak with [[Gabel]] upstairs.

=== Marid Mission 3 ===
* Go upstairs and talk to [[Gabel]] [COORD x=33102 y=32520 z=1 | here]. Ask him for a {{KW|Mission}}, and he will tell you that he needs you to retrieve a [[Gemmed Lamp]] from the [[Orc King]] deep in [[Ulderek's Rock]].

* Go to [[Ulderek's Rock]][COORD x=32874 y=31772 z=7 | ] [[The Orc Fortress]], and speak with the [[Orc King]] found [COORD x=32982 y=31736 z=9 | here]. To enter the chamber a min. level 40 is required. Be careful because if you have never spoken to [[The Orc King]], he will summon some [[Orc Warlord]]s, [[Orc Leader]]s, [[Orc Berserker]]s and ''possibly'' other things. Ask him for the {{KW|lamp}} {{Ilink|Gemmed Lamp}}, and when asked {{Message|type=chat|Who do you want to trap in this cursed lamp?}} reply by saying {{KW|Malor}}.<br />

'''NOTE: The safest way to do this is to throw a [[Poison Bomb]] rune under the [[Orc King]], then greet him twice, grab the lamp and run away.'''

* Take the lamp into the Green Djinn fortress and stealthily make your way to [[Malor]]'s personal bedchamber. Re-enter through the secret passage to reach the top floor, where you'll encounter an increasing number of [[Green Djinn]]s, [[Efreet]]s, and [[Gargoyle]]s. It's advisable to bring [[Dwarven Ring]]s and enlist strong shooters and blockers for assistance. Previously, stairhopping was feasible before the [[Updates/8.41|spring patch]], but this method is no longer viable, making it preferable to proceed with capable allies. Knights may use exori mas, paladins exevo mas san, and mages can rely on runes for sufficient damage. Always wear a dwarven ring and consider casting mana shield for added protection.

* When you reach [[Malor]]'s bedchamber, "use" the lamp THAT'S ON THE COUNTER, next to his bed.
* Stand in front of the lamp (bed) and click use on your lamp once (to be sure, check your Quest log, the third mission, it should say "You successfully changed...").
* Return to [[Gabel]] in the Blue Djinn fortress and tell him you completed the {{KW|Mission}}. You will now be able to trade with [[Haroun]] and [[Nah'Bob]]. It is now also possible to begin a [[Killing in the Name of... Quest#Unranked Tasks|Green Djinn - Hunting Task]] by mentioning {{KW|task}} to [[Gabel]].
