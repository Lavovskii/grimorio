== Required Equipment ==
* [[Shovel]], or equivalent
* [[Rope]], or equivalent
* [[Cough Syrup]] (recommended to purchase in advance)
* [[Hydra Egg]] (recommended to purchase in advance)
* [[Key 3940]]
* [[Crowbar]]
* Melee weapon (for breaking [[Amphora]]s, the crowbar also works)
* [[Dwarven Ring]] (1 recommended for all team members for last mission)
* A lot of traveling money.

== Method ==
To begin the quest, you must first go to [[Hairycles]]' temple in south-eastern [[Banuta]] ([COORD x=32827 y=32577 z=7 | here]). On the way there you will meet [[Carniphila]]s and other usual [[Tiquanda]] monsters, as well as many [[Apes]] of all kinds. On the first floor above ground of the temple you will find [[Hairycles]], the main NPC of the quest, who is willing to entrust players in such dire times.

=== Mission 1: Whisper Moss ===
Ask [[Hairycles]] for a '''mission'''. He will tell you about a plague affecting the ape people. He wants for you to infiltrate the [[Dworc Caves]] and steal some [[Whisper Moss]] which is said to be a cure.

In [[Trapwood]], the southern part of [[Tiquanda]], fall down the south-easternmost [[Pitfall]] leading to the [[Dworc Caves]] ([COORD x=32773 y=32912 z=7 | here]). Use a melee weapon on the damaged [[Wooden Bars]]. If you picked another pitfall you will have to make your way to the correct room. In the eastern part of this room there is a [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]]. Go in and open the box inside it to obtain [[Whisper Moss]].

Bring it to [[Hairycles]] and report about your '''mission'''.

=== Mission 2: The Cure ===
[[Hairycles]] now needs [[Cough Syrup]] that humans use. Hairycles will send you to the local barkeeper [[Brewster]], but Brewster would send you to [[Ustan]] who would explain that his cough syrup supply was stolen in an ape raid. Ultimately, you would be sent to [[Carlin]] to buy it from the local druid there, [[Padreia]]. To buy it, ask Padreia for '''cough syrup'''. It will cost you 50gp. You can buy this in advance to save a trip to and from [[Banuta]] or accept syrup from another player, you don't need to purchase it yourself.

Bring the [[Cough Syrup]] to [[Hairycles]] to complete the '''mission'''.

=== Mission 3: Lizard Parchment ===
[[Hairycles]] thinks the plague is the work of the [[Lizards]]. Hairycles wants you to collect an [[Old Parchment]] from the lizard city of [[Chor]]. When you are ready, enter Chor from the '''south-western''' log ([COORD x=32906 y=32855 z=7 | here]) and go south, cross the bridge and then turn east. You should find a building with a [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]] on the eastern side of it. Go in the door and open the box in it to get the [[Old Parchment]].

'''Note''': Prepare your team because there are a lot of lizards in Chor. It might help to bring a mage with [[Great Fireball]] because of their numbers. Kill the [[Lizard Snakecharmer]] first because their summoned [[Cobra]]s can be annoying. You can use a [[Stealth Ring]] for the [[Lizard Templar]]s, but the other two lizards will see through it. Templars are the only monsters that will try to surround you, so it can help get out of a sticky situation.

Bring the [[Old Parchment]] back to [[Hairycles]] and report about your '''mission'''.

=== Mission 4: Parchment Decyphering ===
[[Hairycles]] cannot read the scroll. He needs you to find a way to decipher it.

Go to [[Ankrahmun]] and leave the city from the north-east exit. Follow the town walls south-east and dig for the [[Shadow Tomb]] ([COORD x=33255 y=32834 z=7 | here]). Go down into the tomb to the second main floor, the floor with the blue flame (not through it). Go south from the ramp and east all the way to the end, to find an [[Obelisk]] and 4 [[Red Shrine Stone]]s. Step close to the obelisk to decipher the scroll. The text {{sound|<nowiki>!-! -O- I_I (/( --I Morgathla</nowiki>}} will appear above your head.

In the tomb you will encounter [[Skeleton]]s, [[scarab]]s, [[Ghoul]]s, [[Crypt Shambler]]s and 1 [[Fire Devil]]. There might also be lured [[Stalker]]s, [[Ghost]]s, [[Vampire]]s and [[Mummy]]s.

When you have deciphered the scroll, report back to [[Hairycles]] about your '''mission'''.

'''Note: '''You don't need to carry the scroll with you to decipher it.

=== Mission 5: Hydra Egg ===
[[Hairycles]] needs a [[Hydra Egg]] to prepare his charm. Your mission is to bring him one.

You can bring him any [[Hydra Egg]]. It is suggested to simply buy one from the market because they are common loot from [[Hydra]]s. If that is not possible, you can loot one yourself or receive it from the [[Hydra's Nest]], on the [[Hydra Egg Quest]] ([COORD x=32947 y=32702 z=7 | here]). You can expect to fight one or multiple [[Hydra]]s in either case if purchasing the egg is not possible.

Bring a [[Hydra Egg]] to [[Hairycles]] to complete this '''mission'''.

=== Mission 6: Witches' Cap Spot ===
[[Hairycles]] needs a sample of the [[Witches' Cap Spot]] for his ''Charm of Life''. This can only be found in the [[Fibula Dungeon]], the [[Bonelord]] section.

You will need [[Key 3940]]. Bring it if you have it. If you don't, you will need 800 gp ([[Simon the Beggar]]) or 2,000 gp ([[Dermot]]) to purchase it.

Go to [[Fibula]] which is through a tunnel far south of [[Thais]], [COORD x=32261 y=32385 z=7 | here]. Purchase [[Key 3940]] if you need to, and then '''use''' the [[Draw Well]] in the middle of the small town to enter the [[Fibula Dungeon]]. Follow the path south, east and then north to cross a bridge and find the door to use the key, if it's locked. Open the door and go in and down the stairs inside. Go directly south through the door and down one of the two ladders at the end of the passage. There are several [[Bonelord]]s here. Keep going south until the end and then turn west until you hit a wall. You should see a [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]]. Go in the door and use the [[Witches' Cap Spot]] to obtain a sample of it.

Bring the sample back to [[Hairycles]] and report about your '''mission'''. You are now a ''friend of the apes'' and can purchase [[Banana]]s for 2gp. Hairycles will also heal you if you are low in health.

The [[Witches' Cap Spot]] will last 24 hours (as of [[Updates/10.63|10.63]]). If you lose your [[Witches' Cap Spot]] you can obtain another one 24 hours after you obtained it.

=== Mission 7: Destroying Casks ===
[[Hairycles]] wants you to destroy the [[Small Cask]]s below the city left by the [[Lizard]] ancestors so that the [[Apes]] cannot drink from them.

In order to destroy the small casks you will need a [[Crowbar]] {{Ilink|Crowbar}}. From [[Hairycles]], go down twice to enter the basement. Go north and follow the corridor to a large room full of [[Apes]]. Turn west and go down the stairs at the end of the hall. Now go east, north, west and north again. Ignore the first chance to turn east but take the second. Follow the passage and you will reach a large room with lots of [[Apes]] and 7 [[Cask]]s on the east side. Enter each door and destroy the casks until you are done.

You need to destroy 3 casks per person; if you are in a large group you may need to wait for them to respawn (3–5 minutes?).

'''Note''': There are lots of [[Apes]] underground. If you are a mage it is recommended to bring [[Avalanche Rune]]s to deal with them.

=== Mission 8: Evidence of Holy Ape ===
[[Hairycles]] aims to strengthen the spirit of the apes. He wants for you to find evidence of the holy ape figure ''Bong'' existing within the [[Forbidden Lands]].

Save for the last mission, this is the hardest part of the quest. East of [[Banuta]] you will find [[The Blind Prophet]] ([COORD x=33020 y=32605 z=7 | here]). Ask the Blind Prophet to '''transport''' you to the [[Forbidden Lands]]. The Forbidden Lands contain a lot of massive monsters, like [[Behemoth]]s, [[Hydra]]s, [[Giant Spider]]s and [[Massive Earth Elemental]]s. Expect to face 2-3 [[Behemoth]]s, 2-4 [[Giant Spider]]s, many [[Massive Earth Elemental]]s and other [[Tiquanda]] monsters, like [[Carniphila]]s and [[Spit Nettle]]s. [[Hydra]]s spawn on the north-east side of the Forbidden Lands, it is unlikely that you will encounter them.

From the ramp at [[Forbidden Lands]], go all the way west, staying close to the southern [[Palisade]] to avoid most of the monsters. If you stick south you should be able to avoid the [[Behemoth]] on the surface by the ramp to [[Forbidden Lands]]. When you reach the corner, turn north and continue following the path until you find a hole. You will most likely encounter a [[Behemoth]] as you walk north. The hole leads to the [[Forbidden Lands]] Hydra Cave. Go north-east of the hole and you should find two [[Giant Footprint]]s ([COORD x=32933 y=32496 z=7 | here]). '''Use''' the north-western tile of the left footprint to obtain the [[Giant Ape's Hair]].

Now go back to [[Hairycles]] and report about your '''mission'''. If you lose your [[Giant Ape's Hair]] you can purchase it from [[Black Bert]]. Note, however, that you must have obtained it once in order to turn it in.

=== Mission 9: The Deepest Catacombs ===
'''Note''': Remember to bring your [[Snake Destroyer]]. If you forget the snake destroyer, you cannot complete the quest and will have wasted a lot of time on this mission.

'''Note''': Remember to bring any melee weapon for breaking amphoras, [[Snake Destroyer]] cannot be used for that. 

'''Note: '''It is recommended to bring [[:en:Egg of The Many|Egg of The Many]] in order to unlock a secret tunnel from Tiquanda to Deeper Banuta. This egg is used on a snake head directly east of the teleporter once inside Deeper Banuta.

[[Hairycles]] wants you to go deep under [[Banuta]] and find the [[Lizards|Lizards']] serpent god monument and destroy it. On accepting this mission you will receive a [[Snake Destroyer]] (you will need this) and gain entry to [[Deeper Banuta]].

Go [COORD x=32839 y=32533 z=9 | here] and enter the [[Magic Forcefield]] to appear at the first floor of [[Deeper Banuta]]. Here you will encounter [[Green Djinn]]s and [[Efreet]]s, [[Bonelord]]s and [[Elder Bonelord]]s, among minor monsters. You must go further down, but in order to do so you must open the teleporter on this floor. There are 4 [[Canopic Jar (Object)|Canopic Jar]]s that must be broken in order to open the teleporter. The jars will recover after a certain period so you must be fast.

The best way is to send the group to the teleporter in the center and have one person run and destroy the jars. There is plenty of time to do that if the jar breaker ignores all the monsters. A second player breaking the jars will make it even quicker, one player working on the south side and the other on the north side.

When the jars are broken, everyone in the group should jump in the [[Magic Forcefield]] in the center of the floor. When you arrive at the lower floor, you may immediately be chased by a [[Serpent Spawn]].

Now you are at the quest floor. This floor is significantly harder than the floor above. On this floor there are [[Bonebeast]]s, [[Hydra]]s, [[Serpent Spawn]]s and [[Medusa]]e. You must find and destroy [[The Statue of the Snake God]], which is in the northern room. However, the room is sealed and can only be accessed if you open the wall.

Throughout the floor there are 7 levers. All of these 7 must be pulled to the '''right'''. The order in which they are pulled does not matter except the last one. The last lever to pull is by the wall that will be removed. You have a limited amount of time to pull the levers.

The quickest strategy is to have a high level (200+ recommended) run the levers, ignoring the monsters. This can be dangerous for lower leveled players, mostly due to the constant [[Paralysis]] from [[Hydra]]s. Another strategy is to have 2 people pulling the levers, splitting the floor in half, both starting on the south-west side and working in opposite directions.

When all the levers are pulled, the wall will disappear. Go through the hole in the wall and find [[The Statue of the Snake God]] north. There are no creatures in this room. Use your [[Snake Destroyer]] on the statue to complete your mission. It takes 40 seconds for the [[Smashed Stone Head]] to repair itself.

While in this room, you have the opportunity to receive [[Damaged Steel Helmet]] (north) for the [[Knight Outfits Quest]], if you have the quest to do so. You can also see the level 999 [[Door of Expertise]] (east).

When your team is done, you can leave the floor by stepping back through the portal at the start. If the wall returns and you are inside it, you can use the lever by the wall to open it again and leave. When you enter the portal at the start of the floor, you will appear at the second floor of [[Banuta]], with [[Apes]], not the last floor you came from.

Report back to [[Hairycles]] about your '''mission''' and then ask for '''shamanic powers''' to receive your [[Shaman Outfit]]. Now you can also buy three [[Monkey Statue]]s ([[Monkey Statue (No Hearing)|No Hearing]], [[Monkey Statue (No Seeing)|No Seeing]], and [[Monkey Statue (No Speaking)|No Speaking]]) in [[Furniture Kit]]s to unpack in your house.
