## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Power Bolts Quest}}
==Required Equipment==
*[[Shovel]] 
*[[Rope]]

==Method==
Go into the hole just south of the [[Plains of Havoc]] [[Temple]], [COORD x=32815 y=32280 z=7 | here].

Kill the [[Giant Spider]] and look around. Hidden below several corpses you will be able to find two human bodies and a [[Pile of Bones (Quest)]].

*The body directly below the [[teleporter]] contains the [[The Dreammaster (Book)|The Dreammaster Book]].
*The northern body contains a [[Brown Bag]] with in it 5 [[Power Bolt]]s and 12 [[Burst Arrow]]s.
*The pile of bones contains the [[Two Handed Sword]].

To the east of the room, you can also find a [[daily respawn]] of 6 [[Throwing Star]]s and in the southwestern corner one of a [[Talon]].
