## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=Plate Armor Quest}}
== Equipment ==
* Travel money (variable amount)

== Method ==
* Go to [[Venore]] boat, and travel [[Venore]]&rarr;[[Darashia]] until you are hijacked by the [[Ghostship]].
* Go down and fight your way across the gangplank to the southern boat.
* Go down to 2 [[vampire]]s, 1 [[Demon Skeleton|demon skeleton]] and a [[ghost]]. Kill them and use the coffin on the east to receive your reward. '''Warning''': when you go down you will land on a [[fire]] field.
* Go back up, and up again to a single [[ghost]] and a [[Magic Forcefield|magic forcefield]]. Enter this portal to appear at [[Darashia]]'s boat.

'''Note''': When it is night in Tibia, you are more likely to go to the Ghostship. (unconfirmed)<br />
