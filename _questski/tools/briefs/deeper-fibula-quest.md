## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler | name = Deeper Fibula Quest }}
===Required equipment===
* [[Key 3940]]
* [[Key 3980]]
* [[Destroy Field]] runes (or lure a [[Dragon Lord]] to cast its [[Fire Bomb]] attack on the [[Fire Field]]s)

===Method===

*Head to [[Fibula]] and enter the [[Fibula Dungeon|dungeon]] through the well.
*Follow the path shown on the map. If you are faced with a locked door, you need [[Key 3940]] to open, it can be bought on the island.
[[Image:Entering_Fibula.gif]]
*Upon coming down, head east and through a teleporter.
*You will now be facing two teleporters, go through the level 50 gate of expertise and the right one.
*You will be teleported to a room with 2 [[Minotaur Guard]]s and 4 [[Minotaur Archer]]s.
*Continue west through [[Fire Field|fire]], and [[Energy Field|energy]] fields to get [[Key 3980]], be prepared to face 3 [[Minotaur Mage]]s.
*[[Key 3980]] is found by "using" on a hole to the west (marked on the map).
*Go back and then south, kill some [[Minotaurs]] (normal and archers if you go east, or normal and mages if you go west), unlock the door with you newly found key, and in the next room, prepare to face much [[Minotaurs]] of all sorts.
*Continue further and now prepare to face ~16 [[Dragon]]s and 4 [[Dragon Lord]]s (one at the entrance of the cave, three at the exit).
*The quest rewards are found in scattered human and skeleton corpses at the end of the cave. Note that some corpses may require you to manually open them, since Tibia may attempt to [[Quick Looting|Quick Loot]] the corpses instead of opening them depending on what controls you use. 
** [[Tower Shield]] is in the north-west corner of the cave, under a [[Fire Field]], inside a skeleton. If you don't have any [[Destroy Field Rune|Destroy Field runes]], you can lure a [[Dragon Lord]] to cast its [[Fire Bomb]] attack onto you, and then wait for the 7:26 minutes for the [[Fire]] to disappear.
** [[Warrior Helmet]] is south of the Tower Shield, in a skeleton that is hidden behind a rock.
** [[Dwarven Ring]] is north of the exit portal, inside a skeleton.
** [[Elven Amulet]] is directly east of the Dwarven Ring, in a fresh-looking dragon corpse.
** [[Knight Axe]] is south-east of the Elven Amulet, in a dead human body.
*The portal in the south-west part of the last room leads back to the dungeon entrance.
[[Image:Deeper_Fibula_Quest_Map_New.jpg]]

'''Note:''' It is possible to get the [[Tower Shield]] and [[Knight Axe]] without using [[Destroy Field]]. Simply lure the [[Dragon Lord]] and have him make a [[Fire Bomb]] over the existing [[Fire Field]]. It will go away in a few minutes.
