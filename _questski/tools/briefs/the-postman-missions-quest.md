## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=The Postman Missions Quest}}
This quest takes a great deal of time and money. It can be worked on at any level but certain parts need 500 [[oz]] cap and at the end, the [[Player]] needs to be able to make it to and through [[Mintwallin]], which can be done with others help.

This quest can be done in stages, depending upon your abilities or time constraints.

There are a total of 10 missions in five groups: ''Assistant Postman'' (cheaper [[parcel]]s and [[letter]]s), ''Postman'' ([[Post Officer's Hat]]), ''Grand Postman'' ([[boat]] discounts), ''Grand Postman for Special Operations'' (Waldo's [[Post Horn]]), and ''Arch Postman'' (use of royal [[Mailbox]]es).

==Required Equipment==
Since there is so much walking on "safe" paths, you should bring plenty of [[Food]]. [[Brown Mushroom]]s are the best. If you are a mage or paladin, be sure to bring plenty of [[Blank Rune]]s also.

'''Assistant Postman'''
* Approximately 700 [[gp]]. More or less depending upon the routes that you take.
* [[Crowbar]]

'''Postman'''
* 20 [[Bone]]s (for every player)

'''Grand Postman'''
* 1 [[Mouldy Cheese]], 1 [[Banana Skin]] and 1 [[Dirty Fur]] (for the whole party)

'''Grand Postman for Special Operations'''
* Approximately 200 [[gp]]. Maybe more or less depending on your [[Dice]] Rolls.
* 12 [[Arrow]]s
* [[Grapes]]

'''Arch Postman'''
* 500 [[oz]] of available capacity (500 [[oz]] is needed to take the [[Letterbag]] out of the box, someone can carry it for you).
* Travel money to [[Vega]]
'''Note:''' A [[Stealth Ring]] is advisable lower levels attempting last mission.

==Method==
Start out by talking to [[Kevin]] at his post office between [[Thais]] and [[Kazordoon]] ([COORD x=32568 y=32022 z=7 | here]). Ask him for a mission and tell him you are interested in joining his guild (does not prevent you from joining a "normal" Tibia guild). If you forget where you were going, or if you have completed a mission, you can check the quest log or to [[Kevin]] and ask about a mission.

===Assistant Postman===
====Mission 1 - Check Postal Routes====
# Head to [[Thais]] and take the [[Boat]] to [[Carlin]] (110 [[gp]])
# Walk to [[Femor Hills]] and carpet to [[Edron]] (60 [[gp]]). The ramp to get [[Uzon]] is located [COORD x=32511 y=31840 z=7 | here] (You can also purchase a [[crowbar]] which is needed for next mission, from [[Beatrice]] for 260 [[gp]]).
# Boat to [[Venore]] (40 [[gp]])
# Walk to [[Kazordoon]] and in deepest cave, ride the [[Steam Boat]] to [[Cormaya]] (160 [[gp]], for the Kazordoon route to the ship, see [[Route: Kazordoon Steam Ship|here]]) - Alternately go back to Edron, to Cormaya, and boat to Kazordoon and back.
# Return to Kevin's Post office (cheapest way is to walk through [[Rotworm]] caves to [[Edron]], boat to [[Venore]] and walk to Kevin's Post Office)
# Tell [[Kevin]] you completed the mission.

====Mission 2 - Fix Mailbox====
# Get a [[Crowbar]] (can be purchased from [[Edron]], [[Darashia]], [[Kazordoon]], North of [[Thais]] ([[Lubo]]) and [[Venore]])
# Take the ferry to [[Folda]] (20 [[gp]]) - North-West of [[Carlin]] ([COORD x=32233 y=31677 z=7 | here])
# Climb to the top of the mountain ([COORD x=32001 y=31567 z=7 | here]) and 'use' the crowbar on the mailbox
# Return to Kevin and ask about advancement.

You are now an '''Assistant Postman'''! (cheaper parcels and letters)

===Postman===
====Mission 3 - [[Bill]] Delivery [[Image:Bill.gif]]====
# Ask Kevin for a new mission and receive a [[Bill]] for one Magician Hat and a bunch of rabbits to be delivered to a famous magician. Don't bother skipping to the next step if you don't have the bill in your possession.
# Walk to [[Venore]] and seek out [[A Strange Fellow]] below the [[Depot]] ([COORD x=32911 y=32077 z=7 | here])
# Talk to him and keep asking about his 'hat' (4 times) until he gives in and takes his 'bill', which is when he says: "Ok, ok, I'll take it. I guess I have no other choice anyways. And now leave me alone in my misery please."
# Return to Kevin.
''Bring the 20 bones with you now. You'll hand them on the next mission.''

====Mission 4 - Aggressive Dogs [[Image:Bone.gif]][[Image:Dog.gif]]====
# Kevin will tell you the classic post officer's plight - aggressive dogs. He will also tell you that they are running low on [[bone]]s in the "Post Officer's Fund".
# Get 20 [[bone]]s (the easiest way is to go to Mount Sternum and kill skeletons).
Note: Bones weigh 9.50oz each, so 20 will weigh 190oz. Make sure you have enough capacity.
# Talk to Kevin and tell him that you completed the "mission" and that you brought "all" of the bones. He will take them one at a time if you cannot carry all 20 at once.
# Ask about an "advancement".

You are now a '''Postman'''! ([[Post Officer's Hat]])

===Grand Postman===
====Mission 5 - Present Delivery [[Image:Present.gif]]====
# Ask about another mission. Kevin will tell you to deliver a [[Present (Postman)|present]] to [[Dermot]] on [[Fibula]].
# As directed, open the right blinking door and take the [[Present (Postman)|present]] from the chest. '''''DO NOT OPEN THE PRESENT!!''''' If you try to open it, you will get nothing and the [[Present (Postman)|present]] will disappear, then you will have to either buy another from NPC [[Black Bert]] for 16,000gp or get it with another character.
# Travel to [[Fibula]] to [[Dermot]] and give him the [[Present (Postman)|present]].
# Return to Kevin.

====Mission 6 - New Uniforms====
# Travel to [[Venore]] and talk to [[Hugo]], [COORD x=32955 y=32108 z=6 | here]. He is located upstairs where dresses and other wears are being sold.
# Ask Hugo about new 'uniforms' and new 'dress pattern'. He will say that he has no idea where Kevin got it from, and send you back.
# Return to Kevin to say 'dress patterns'. He will send you to [[Talphion]] in [[Kazordoon]].
# Go north to Kazordoon and talk to Talphion the technomancer. In Kazordoon, on the level with the [[rat]]s, go to the west end of the passage west of the shops. Talphion is down two staircases and North. [COORD x=32563 y=31896 z=12 | here]
# Say 'dress patterns' a few times until he understands you. He will send a letter to Kevin.
# Go Back to Kevin and ask about 'dress pattern'. He will acknowledge the message from Talphion, and send you to [[Queen Eloise]] for the perfect colors.
# Go north to [[Carlin]] and find [[Queen Eloise]], [COORD x=32315 y=31755 z=7 | here]. Say ''''Hail Queen'''' and ask about 'uniforms.' She will send some samples to Kevin.
# Return to Kevin and ask about ''''dress patterns''''.'''DON´T SAY MISSION''' He will mention the samples from Queen Eloise, and tell you to figure out what smell dogs hate the most. He says that [[Noodles]], [[King Tibianus]]'s dog has the best sense of smell.
# Gather the [[Mouldy Cheese]], [[Banana Skin]] and a [[Dirty Fur]]
# Go to Thais Castle, and talk to [[Noodles]], the [[King Tibianus|king's]] dog.
# Say "hi", "sniff banana skin", "do you like that?", "hi", "sniff dirty fur", "do you like that?", etc. Continue doing this until Noodles spits. It could happen even with the first sniffed item.
# Return to Kevin and mention "dress pattern". He will tell you he is mailing the patterns to Hugo, and ask you to tell Hugo that they are ordering them.
# Return again to Hugo, 'dress pattern.' He will insult the patterns, but ultimately agree to making them.
# Return to Kevin. Ask about advancement.

You are now a '''Grand Postman'''! (This grants you travel discounts on [[boat]]s)

====Mission 8 - Missing Courier====
# Kevin tells you that the mail courier [[Waldo]] is missing, and that you need to find him, or at least his posthorn.
# "Where's Waldo?" you ask ... He is in the [[Thais Troll Cave]]s ([COORD x=32489 y=32260 z=7 | here]). You will need a [[shovel]] to open the hole.
:* Once in the caves, go east and down the hole
:* Then go north and east past the [[Orc]]s, and up the ladder
:* Waldo will be directly west, behind a [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]] [COORD x=32514 y=32248 z=8 | here]. '''Note:''' You will not be able to open the door until you have talked to [[Kevin]] about [[Waldo]], so do not bother trying to find Waldo before you have received the assignment from Kevin.

:* If you don't have the required 500 [[capacity]] you need for the next mission, it is a good idea to buy a [[parcel]] from [[Wally]] and send whatever you do not need to yourself.

You are now a '''Grand Postman for Special Operations'''! ([[Post Horn]])

===Arch Postman===
====Mission 9 - Dear Santa====
# This time Kevin asks you to deliver some letters to [[Santa Claus]] on [[Vega]].
# Open the left [[Sealed Door]] and get a [[Letterbag]] (it weighs 500 [[oz]], and no one else can carry it for you, since it won't come out of the chest unless you have the capacity)
# Take this letterbag to [[Vega]] by [[Nielson]]'s raft and go west to Santa's house ([COORD x=31955 y=31712 z=7 | here]).
# If you lost the letterbag you can buy a replacement from NPC [[Black Bert]] at the price of 8,000 [[gp]]s.
# [[Use]] the letterbag on [[Santa's Mailbox]] (you cannot use it to send regular mail)
# After you deliver the letters, it will become a normal [[Red Bag]].
# Return to Kevin for the last mission!

====Mission 10 - Mintwallin====
# Now Kevin gives you a [[Letter to Markwin|letter]] to deliver to [[Markwin]] [COORD x=32420 y=32152 z=15 | here] in [[Mintwallin]] (it's a [[Letter to Markwin|letter]] from his mother). A [[Stealth Ring]] is advisable for this mission as he summons 4 [[Minotaur Archer]]s, 2 [[Minotaur Guard]]s, and 2 [[Minotaur Mage]]s summoned at once! (And also more on the way to [[Markwin]]). He summons his guards only '''the first time''' you talk to him.
# When you are ready, go to [[Thais]], [[Ancient Temple]], [COORD x=32356 y=32170 z=7 | here] and follow the [[Route:Mintwallin|route here]] to the [[Minotaur]] city of [[Mintwallin]].
# Before saying "hi", its highly recommended to cast the [[invisible]] spell or the use a of stealth ring. As soon as you say "hi" to Markwin, he will call for his 8 bodyguards. You can also say 'hi' to him from the outside of the castle. This way you can lure a few at a time, or simply ignore them.
# Say hi again, and give him his [[Letter to Markwin|letter]].
# Return to Kevin and ask about advancement.

'''Tip:''' If you don't have stealth rings and are barely above lvl 30, you can push Markwin with furnitue to the right and talk through the level 30 door, no bodyguards will spawn there when you talk to him the first time.

You are now an '''Arch Postman'''! (You may now use royal mailboxes).

You will earn the achievement [[Archpostman]].

For a list of royal mailboxes, read the page about [[Mailbox]]es.
