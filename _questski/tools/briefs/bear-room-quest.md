## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Bear Room Quest}}
===Required Equipment===
*[[Rope]]
*[[Key 4601]] to bear room, or a [[Pick]] to get it (for methods on how to obtain a [[Pick]] on Rookgaard, see the [[Pick Quest]] article).

===Method===
*Go to the abandoned building a little north of [[Rookgaard]] and down the hole.
[[Image:Bear Room Quest 1.png]]

*Go east and down ([[Troll]]s here).
[[Image:Bear Room Quest 2.png]]

*Go down again like it is shown on this map. There are a few [[Orc]]s here.
[[Image:Bear Room Quest 3.png]]

*Go down again. There are 2-4 [[Orc]]s here. It's fairly easy to out run them.
[[Image:Bear Room Quest 4.png]]

*Final level - There are 1-2 [[Minotaur]]s and [[Orc]]s in this room.

To retrieve the key, use a [[pick]] on the mud floor square directly south of the big table. A hole will appear for you to go down. Downstairs is a chest with [[Key 4601]]; take it and go back up the ladder.

[[Image:Bearroom6.jpg]]

North of the big table is a switch which controls the stone which can block the door to the bear room further north. If the stone is blocking the door, remove it by using the switch. Go through the door and kill the [[Bear]], afterwards open all three boxes to get your reward.<br>'''Note:''' If someone has blocked the way back you can use the sewer grate to go down to a room with 4 [[Spider]]s, and then up the ladder to the south in order to get back into the central room with the [[Minotaur]]s and [[Orc]]s.

[[Image:Bear Room Quest 5.png]]

'''Note:''' Next to the stone switch is the chest for the [[Present Box Quest]].
