## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Barbarian Test Quest}}
=== Required Equipment ===
* [[Honeycomb]] (Probably more than one. The number of honeycombs that you will use depends on your luck).
* Three [[Mug]]s of [[Mead]]. Can be bought near [[Sven]].
* Advisable : [[Dwarven ring]], but read the second mission

===Method===
====Barbarian Test 1: Barbarian Booze====
Go to [[Sven]] west of the [[Svargrond]] depot, and then go south at the crossroad (it's a large building with lots of stools); ask him about taking the barbarian test.
Give him a [[Honeycomb]] by saying "mead". For each honeycomb you will be allowed 20 sips, drink from the bucket until you drink 10 sips in a row without passing out. If you pass the sip this message will appear: {{Sound|The world seems to spin but you manage to stay on your feet.}} And if you fail: {{Sound|The mead was too strong. You passed out for a moment.}} (your sprite becomes that of a [[Dead Human]] for a couple of seconds). You can drink as fast as humanly possible. If you don't pass, try again, by saying "mead" again and giving him another [[Honeycomb]]. If you did it this message appears: {{Sound|You already passed the test, no need to torture yourself anymore.}}

[[File:Svenbarbtest.jpg]]

====Barbarian Test 2: The Bear Hugging====
[[File:Sleeping Bear.gif|right|The bear whilst unconscious]]When you have done that he will give you a [[Mead Horn (Empty)]]. Fill it with mead from the bucket behind [[Sven]] (brown contents) and then proceed to the sleeping bear. He is [COORD x=32252 y=31051 z=5 | here], up a set of ramps, to the east of the [[Svargrond Arena|arena]] ramps; the Carpet fly-er has set up his business up these ramps however you want to go west then north past the Carpet fly-er inside a small ice cave. Once you get through the giant ice area entrance go north until you hit a wall and follow it east. Use the [[Mead Horn|Full Mead Horn]] on the bear so it becomes unconscious, then use the bear to hug it. Doing this will grant you the achievement "[[Bearhugger]]". 

{{Server Message|Congratulations! You earned the achievement "Bearhugger".}}

Report back to [[Sven]] by saying {{KW|Bearhugging}} to complete the mission.

[[File:Sleepingbearbrabian.jpg]]

====Barbarian Test 3: The Mammoth Pushing====
For the next mission you need to buy 3 [[mug]]s of [[mead]] from [[Dankwart]] (if you find some empty mugs you can fill them with [[beer]] from the cask underneath [[Sven]], it will work too) nearby [[Sven]]. It is advisable to take a [[Dwarven Ring|dwarven ring]] with you. Find the [[Mammoth (Object)|mammoth]], which resides in the same icy area as the bear, but farther to the west [COORD x=32154 y=31031 z=5 | here]. Once you have found the mammoth, drink all 3 mugs of mead/beer and then use the mammoth to knock it over. 

Report back to [[Sven]] by saying {{KW|Mammoth}} to complete the mission.

[[File:Mammoth1.jpg]]

*Finishing this quest, will earn you the achievement "[[Honorary Barbarian]]".

You have now completed the Barbarian Test Quest, which means you can now become a citizen of [[Svargrond]], travel with [[Buddel]], start the [[The Ice Islands Quest]] and [[Barbarian Arena Quest|fight in the Arena]].
