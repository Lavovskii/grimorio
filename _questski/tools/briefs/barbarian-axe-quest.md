## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name= Barbarian Axe Quest }}

===Required Equipment===
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Rope]]
* [[Stealth Ring]] (Advisable, not necessary)

===Method===
* Go to the [[Edron Orc Cave]] through a hole in the basement of Edron Flats.
* Head south, facing [[Snake]]s, [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Orc]]s, [[Orc Spearmen]],[[Orc Warrior]]s on the surface and go down the hole leading into a cave near the southern most coast.
[[Image:Dark Armor Quest Map 01.jpg]]
*On this floor you will find [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Orc]]s, [[Orc Spearmen]] and [[Orc Warrior]]s. Follow the arrow down the hole.

[[Image:Barbarian Axe Quest Map 2.jpg]]
*On this floor you will encounter [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Orc]]s, [[Orc Spearmen]], [[Orc Warrior]]s and probably one or two lured [[Orc Berserker]]s.

[[Image:Barbarian Axe Quest Map 3.jpg]]
*Once down this hole you will face [[Orc Berserker]]s, [[Poison Spider]]s and [[Orc Warrior]]s. To save supplies, it is best to stand in the bottom left corner, so that only 3 monsters can hit you at a time. It is advisable to kill the [[Orc Berserker]]s first.

[[Image:Barbarian Axe Quest Map 4.jpg]]
*'''Continue with caution!''' Right after going down the hole, you will encounter 3 - 5 [[Orc Berserker]]s, so it is advisable to use a [[Stealth Ring]]. Follow the arrow, and beware of more [[Orc Berserker]]s. As you head south into the final room, you will have to face 3+ [[Orc Berserker]]s, 2 [[Orc Leader]]s and 2-3 [[Orc Warrior]]s. Open the box at the southern end of the room to claim your reward.
