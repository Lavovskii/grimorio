==Required equipment==
* [[Rope]]
* Prefered [[Antidote]] and [[Invisible]] spell.

==Method==
* Head to [[Drefia]] west of [[Darashia]] and enter the main dungeon [COORD x=32996 y=32413 z=7 | here]. It is advised to use the [[Invisible|Invisibility]] spell here due to the overwhelming undead force.

[[File:Drefia Main Dungeon Entrance.png|Follow the green lines to the third level of the main dungeon.]]
* Follow the map above, or just descend to the third floor of the main dungeon.
* On the third floor, head straight north approximately 18 steps. To your left will be a hole. Open it with a [[Shovel]] and go down.

[[File:Drefia Medusa Shield Quest Path.png|300px|Follow the green lines through the level 60 gates]]

* Follow the map above, or head east and through the [[level]] 60 [[Gate of Expertise]].

* Prepare yourself as there will be an army of undead up here ([[Demon Skeleton]]s, [[Ghost]]s, [[Zombie]]s, [[Vampire]]s, several [[Necromancer]]s, several [[Priestess]]es and their summons ([[Ghoul]], [[Ghost]]s and [[Mummy|Mummies]])). '''Note''': with luck, the room will already be clear. Beyond the quest room is [[Drefia Wyrm Lair]], a popular hunting ground.
* A good strategy at lower levels would be to get either a mage or paladin to go invisible and [[Great Fireball]] the entire spawn a few times to clear out most of the monster spam so that you can take down the [[Demon Skeleton]]'s and [[Necromancers]] fast.

* The [[Medusa Shield]], [[Blue Robe]] and [[Skull Staff]] are located in a coffin in the easterly section of the room. There are also [[Daily Respawns]] on the sacrificial stones that you may take if no other players have done so (1 [[White Pearl]], 1 [[Black Pearl]], 1 [[Small Ruby]], 1 [[Small Emerald]]).
