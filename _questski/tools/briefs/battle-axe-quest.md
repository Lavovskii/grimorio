## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=Battle Axe Quest}}
== Requirements ==
* [[Rope]], or equivalent.
* [[Pick]], or equivalent.

== Method ==
Enter the [[Thais Sewers]] near the depot. You will face [[Rat]]s here. Navigate to the south-west end of the cave ([COORD x=32302 y=32257 z=8 | here]). '''Use''' a pick on the tile 2 sqm north of the dead end on the eastern side. A hole should open. Go down, kill the 4 [[Cave Rat]]s and get your reward in the [[Pile of Bones (Quest)]].
