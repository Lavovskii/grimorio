## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=Nobleman Outfits Quest}}
=== Outfit ===
These outfits can be selected by all [[Premium Account]] players. Only the addons are included in this quest.

=== Top Hat for Males ===
[[Image:Outfit Nobleman Male Addon 2.gif]]
==== Required Equipment ====
* 150,000[[gp]]

==== Method ====
* Go to [[Irmana]] in [[Venore]] and ask about a "top hat".
* You will need to simply purchase it.

==== Transcripts ====
{{Infobox Transcript|
''Player'': '''Hi'''<br>
Irmana: Welcome to the house of fashion, <name>!<br>
''Player'': '''Addon'''<br>
Irmana: Currently we are offering accessories for the nobleman - and, of course, noblewoman - outfit. Would you like to hear more about our offer?<br>
''Player'': '''Yes'''<br>
Irmana: Especially for you, mylord, we are offering a fashionable top hat and a fancy coat like the one Kalvin wears. Which one are you interested in?<br>
''Player'': '''Top Hat'''
}}
