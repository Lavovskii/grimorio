== Required Equipment ==
*[[Shovel]]
*[[Rope]]

== Method ==
This quest is located through the [[Maze of Lost Souls]] (MoLS). Exploring this place without a map can be very dangerous, as you may encounter [[Giant Spider]]s, [[Dragon Lord]]s and more dangerous [[creatures]].

There are several caves north of [[Carlin]], in the [[Fields of Glory]], that lead to the MoLS. For the case start at the cave a bit south of [[Northport]] [COORD x=32488 y=31610 z=7 | here], a small village north of [[Carlin]].
Use the shovel to open a hole [COORD x=32512 y=31624 z=7 | here] and get into it.

Follow the blue line until the next hole.

[[Image:Route Demona.png]]

Keep following the blue line and you'll find a small forest inside the cave. In the red spot, behind the trees, there is a hole and inside that hole there is a switch that you must turn right to open the entrance to the [[Maze of Lost Souls|MoLS]].
<br>'''Note:''' If the switch is already turned right, then turn it left and right again.

[[Image:Route Demona (1).png]]

Get out the hole and follow this way to get to the entrance of the [[Maze of Lost Souls|MoLS]]. You will have to cross some [[Energy Field]]s:

[[Image:Route Demona (2).png]]

Now you are inside of the [[Maze of Lost Souls|MoLS]] and I suggest you that you follow the next the map step by step or you'll face [[dragon]]s, [[Dragon Lord|dragon lords]] and [[Giant Spider|giant spiders]]. In this path you'll face all the creatures in the list above ([[rat]]s ~ [[orc leader]]s). Keep in mind: don't get out of the path if you can't face dangerous creatures.

[[Image:Route Demona (3).png]]

In the first entrance room is the [[Griffin Shield Quest]]. Here you will meet 3 [[Elf Arcanist]]s, 2 [[Stone Golem]]s, 2 [[Dwarf Guard]]s and 2 [[Minotaur Guard]]s. If you have not yet done it you can do so now. Keep going to the end of the passage and through the Gate of Expertise for [[level]] 60.

[[Image:Demona_Gate.GIF]]

Go north and take the right passage all the way to the stairs. Right above here is [[Demona]].

[[Image:Demona.GIF]]

In Demona are several [[Warlock]]s, a couple of [[Stone Golem]]s and a few [[Elf Arcanist]]s. Go to the top of the room. Down the stairs is the quest room with 4 [[Warlock]]s and 5 [[Dragon]]s.

Once you kill the warlocks and dragons or hold them off, go to the north side of the room and open the [[Chest (Quest)|chest]]s for your [[Crystal Wand]] and a bag with [[Silver Rune Emblem (Sudden Death)|Silver Rune Emblem]] and [[Twinkiller Rune (Book)]].
The [[book]]s might also be worth taking from the quest room.

Anyhow you'll have to get out of [[Maze of Lost Souls|MoLS]], so you'll have to follow the path in the next map.
<br>Note: there are other ways to get out of [[Maze of Lost Souls|MoLS]], but this one is the safest and shortest one.

[[Image:Route Demona (4).png]]

That's all! Now you can either follow the same path you used to go to [[Maze of Lost Souls|MoLS]] to get out of the cave. 
If you are comfortable hunting on Demona, you can go north west of the Star Room with warlocks, go down [COORD x=32451 y=31655 z=14 | this stairs] (nothing spawns right at the stairs). From the stairs you walk north, then west. Here you will meet 2 [[Warlock]]s and 2 [[Elf Arcanist]]s. Keep walking west until you reach the end of the tunnel, from there head south. At the end of that corridor you will find a [COORD x=32400 y=31657 z=15 | teleport]. It will bring you back to the [[Fields of Glory]].
