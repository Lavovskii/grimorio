## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=To Blind the Enemy Quest}}
==Required Equipment==
* [[Destroy Field Rune]]
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Rope]]
* [[Mirror]] (provided by [[Eroth]])

==Method==
Go to [[Ab'Dendriel]]. Go up one floor in the [[depot]] and head west. Talk to the NPC [[Eroth]] about a mission. He will say you have to destroy an orb in the top of the tower of [[Elvenbane]] with a [[Mirror]].
[[File:To Blind the enemy.png|thumb|259x259px]]
Enter the [[Elvenbane Fortress]] [COORD x=32579 y=31680 z=7 | here] (you'll need a [[Destroy Field Rune]]) and go into the central tower. Go up to the last floor (be prepared to face many monsters, listed above) and use the [[Mirror]] on the [[Scrying Ball]] you see there.

Report back to [[Eroth]] to get your reward.
