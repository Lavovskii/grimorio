## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler| name=Orc Fortress Quest}}
===Required equipment===
*None

===Method===
*Head to the [[Orc Fortress]] and enter the hole.
[[File:Orc_Fortress_Map_01.png]]
*Head east and be prepared to face all kinds of Orcs. (The safest route its marked with a blue line)
[[File:Orc_Fortress_Map_AB.png]]
*Down the stairs you will face several [[Orc Leader]]s, [[Orc Warlord]]s, a [[Dragon]] and probably some [[Orc Shaman]]s and [[Stone Golem]]s coming from a room west of the staircase.
*When they are dead, head east and north through the [[level]] 40 gate of expertise.
[[Image:Orc_Fortress_Quest_Map_02.gif]]
*In this room you will find several [[Orc Berserker]]s, [[Orc Warlord]]s, [[Slime]]s, [[Orc Leader]]s and [[Orc Rider]]s.
*The quest boxes are located in the north end of this room.
*'''Note: You will meet the [[Defiler]]-shaped [[NPC]] [[The Orc King]] here. Do not say 'hi' to him,''' if you do he will summon a lot of Orcs of all types including Berserks, Leaders and Warlords.
**If you were smart enough to start the [[Blue Djinn Quest]] or the [[Green Djinn Quest]] before this quest, you could get your reward, and then say "hi" to [[The Orc King]] and then "complete" those quests; the same goes for the part of [[Jester Outfits Quest]] that asks you to give a [[Exploding Cookie]] to [[The Orc King]].
