## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler|name=Rotten Blood Quest}}

== Requirements ==
* Level 250
* 2x [[Bloody Tears]] {{Ilink|Bloody Tears}} or 5,000,000 [[gp]]s

== Method ==

Go to [[Drefia]] and talk to [[Torkada]] [COORD x=33033 y=32428 z=7 | here]. He will reveal that there is an expedition working on an important mission for the [[Inquisition]]. Asking him about the {{KW|expedition}} reveals that some adventurers, while exploring, found the entrance to a temple of evil. That temple seemed to be too foul, even for Drefian standards. They have warded the entrance so that the corruption can be kept at bay, but has a {{KW|mission}} for players who want to eradicate all evil inside. If you say {{KW|yes}}, he will bless you and allow you to enter the temple, which is accessed through a [[Levitate]] spot to the north west, [COORD x=32989 y=32393 z=7 | here]. Levitate up, pass the 2 ghouls, go downstairs and then go down the hole. Then go down yet again through the stairs immediately to the north east, and start heading south west until you reach a door between two limestone pillars. Pass through the door.

Moving into the room, players will find [[A Bloodshade]] right at the entrance to the [[Blood Vestibule]]. Talking to it will reveal that the temple holds the entrance to the holy sanguine realm of the blood god. That realm is currently being corrupted by spawns that are festering, which is a mockery of his master's former glory. By asking it for a {{KW|quest}}, it reveals that to enter the sanguine realm, one must either sacrifice two {{Ilink|Bloody Tears}} [[Bloody Tears]] or 5,000,000 [[gold]] (the latter is chosen by the '''detour''<nowiki/>' prompt). Players who choose to sacrifice the tears can use the sacrificial plate twice while holding them (you might have to click it multiple times for the tears to be consumed). After the sacrifice, players will be granted passage into the pool of blood that works as a portal. Do not attempt to go into the pool before doing this, or you will take [[Agony Damage]] that can hit for a percentage of your health.

Inside the Blood Vestibule, talk to A Bloodshade again, and ask for {{KW|quest}}. It will tell you that you must slay two pairs of evil twins that lurk in the areas, before being able to reach [[Bakragore]], the foul presence that de-sanctifies this realm of blood. Exploring the area ahead, players will find four rooms with {{ilink|Spheric Teleporter}} [[Spheric Teleporter]]s. Clicking on each of them will teleport players to one of the four hunting areas. For each area, characters must accumulate enough [[Rotten Charge (Counter)|Rotten Charges]] to be able to enter the boss lever rooms that will take them to face one of the progenies of [[Bakragore]].

=== Rotten Charges ===
For each of the areas, and each time a team wants to fight a boss, the [[Rotten Charge (Counter)|rotten charge counters]] of all characters have to be filled to 100. The charge counter shows up beside each character's name (represented by the [[File:Quest Condition Arrow Up.png]] icon), right above the [[Bakragore's Taints]] counter (represented by the [[File:Quest Condition Red Blood.png]] icon), and will increase slowly on its own while they are within the hunting grounds, filling up in 33 minutes (one charge every 20 seconds). However, each hunting ground also offers a few mechanics which players can use to speed it up.

=== Strategies for all bosses ===

You can kill the bosses in any order you want, as the taints will always be received on the order listed above. Due to the difficulty of the accesses and bosses, however, there is an order which most players consider best, which is the following:

# [[#Jaded Roots|Jaded Roots]] - [[Murcion]]
# [[#Darklight Core|Darklight Core]] - [[Vemiath]]
# [[#Gloom Pillars|Gloom Pillars]] - [[Chagorz]]
# [[#Putrefactory|Putrefactory]] - [[Ichgahal]]

The lower level or more unexperienced a team is, the more applicable some knowledge becomes for all bosses:

* Mechanics are essential. All the bosses have mechanics that, if failed to succeed or handle, can make make the boss harder to kill or negatively impact the party.
* Differently from most boss fights up until [[Summer Update 2023]], this quest features battles designed for [[Paladin]]s to be the main blockers.
* [[Knight]]s should consider wearing [[Necklace of the Deep]] for most of the time, due to the high [[Life Drain Damage]] of the [[Elder Bloodjaw]]s in all fights.
* All bosses include at least two [[Elder Bloodjaw]]s in the rooms. The final amount depends on the team's [[Bakragore's Taints|taints]].
* [[Paladins]] should avoid diagonal steps as much as possible and use [[Powerful Vibrancy]], since all bosses have [[Paralyse]] spells that they use constantly, and some of them heal a lot if standing still.

==== Murcion ====
{{FightTips
|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Life Drain}}
|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Death}}
}}

{{ilink|Murcion}} [[Murcion]] is the twin of [[Ichgahal]], and both share similarities.

The boss will leave {{ilink|Mould (Field)}} [[Mould (Field)|mould tracks]] on the ground wherever it passes or stands. If players step or stand on the tracks, they take 12,5% of their maximum [[HP]] per second as [[Agony Damage]]. If the boss steps or stands on its own tracks, it will heal significantly (15,000+). 

The boss fights together with two {{Ilink|Elder Bloodjaw}} [[Elder Bloodjaw]]s, not considering modifiers from [[Bakragore's Taints|taints]]. During the whole fight, the boss will keep summoning {{ilink|Mushroom}} [[Mushroom]]s randomly around itself. Up to 8 Mushrooms can exist at the same time, exploding after a while or if players come close to them, dealing 2,500+ [[Life Drain]] damage in a small area. 

This is one of the harder fights due to the small room, which leaves very little place to run around. The common strategy is to have the [[Paladin]] immediately grab the target from [[Murcion]], then run around in a circle as wide as possible. The movement flow is very important, because if the [[Paladin]] moves too fast they will complete a whole lap before the [[Mould (Field)|mould tracks]] have disappeared, but if they move too slow the boss will end up healing from standing on its track.

All shooters should avoid getting close to the boss to avoid drawing its target. If the boss switches target to any shooter, they should immediately run on the same direction that [[Paladin]] was moving towards, to keep [[Murcion]] moving on the correct direction. Failing to react quickly will cause the boss to stand on its [[Mould (Field)|mould tracks]] and heal. Lower level teams can not afford to have the boss heal this way multiple times, or they will run out of time to kill it.

The [[Knight]] should [[Challenge]] the [[Elder Bloodjaw]]s as soon as they spawn, and run around the room in the same direction as the [[Paladin]], but falling behind around half a lap, so that they are always at the opposite side of the room. Knights should try to step near the [[Mushroom]]s when running their lap, to help clear the path for the Paladin's next lap. The clearer the path is for the [[Paladin]], the easier it will be to keep the movement flow of [[Murcion]]. However, a balance should also be found, because the boss has a limit to how many [[Mushroom]]s can be active at the same time, and leaving a few behind might be desirable to avoid situations where the boss summons enough mushrooms to trap itself in them, causing it to stand still and significantly heal multiple times in a very short period.

==== Vemiath ====
{{FightTips
|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Energy}}
}}

{{ilink|Vemiath}} [[Vemiath]] is the twin of [[Chagorz]], and both share similarities. During the fight, players have a counter next to their names ([[File:Quest Condition White X.png]] icon), increasing by two per second to a maximum of 21. When under maximum stacks, characters will take 15% [[HP|maximum health]] [[Agony Damage]] per second. Each time a player moves, they "drop" these charges on the ground, leaving {{ilink|Darklight Spark}} [[Darklight Spark]] as tracks and reducing the counter by one. Stepping or staying over the Darklight Sparks causes 3% [[HP|maximum health]] per second as [[Agony Damage]]. After three seconds, the Darklight Sparks turn into their second stage, {{ilink|Darklight Field}} [[Darklight Field]]s, which last for two seconds but also cause 3% maximum health agony damage immediately upon being stepped on. Therefore, players can take take up to 18% maximum health per second if not handling these carefully. [[Vemiath]] heals itself by 200 [[HP]] for each Darklight Spark it steps on, or by 400 [[HP]] for each Darklight Field.

The boss fights together with two {{Ilink|Elder Bloodjaw}} [[Elder Bloodjaw]]s, not considering modifiers from [[Bakragore's Taints|taints]]. It can summon up to 8 {{ilink|Pillar of Dark Energy}} [[Pillar of Dark Energy|Pillars of Dark Energy]]. Each one of these Pillars can chain players in its melee range for [[Energy Damage]]. Players should avoid standing next to one, and it is specially important to avoid standing next to many, as multiple chains can be triggered at the same time, dealing very high spikes of [[Energy Damage]]. When taking damage from this chain spell, a different counter ([[File:Quest Condition Red Ball.png]] icon) is applied to each character hit by it. This counter is reduced at a rate of one per second and deals 1,200~ [[Energy Damage]] with every tick. The maximum counter value is 11. Players should be extra careful when having these because getting hit by [[Vemiath]]'s beam spell will trigger all charges at once, dealing a high Energy Damage outburst.

Every 90 seconds, a {{ilink|Rotten Charge}} [[Rotten Charge]] will appear at the center of the room, lasting for 15 seconds. Players must lure an [[Elder Bloodjaw]] into it. 

If no [[Elder Bloodjaw]] steps on it:
* The room will make an announcement after 6 seconds ({{sound|The rotten charge is going to explode!}}), and again after 10 seconds ({{sound|THE ROTTEN CHARGE WILL EXPLODE SOON!}}). After 15 seconds, the charge will explode, and each player will take 40% of their character's maximum [[HP|hit points]] as [[Agony Damage]].

If an [[Elder Bloodjaw]] steps on it:
* all players in a small radius of the charge will not generate [[Darklight Spark]]s or [[Darklight Field|fields]] for 15 seconds
* the boss will not heal itself on players' tracks for 15 seconds
* all [[Elder Bloodjaw]]s will be removed from the boss room for 15 seconds
* the resistances of the boss will be noticeably lowered for 15 seconds
* the boss will prepare a devastating spell that hits its current target after 6 seconds and 12 seconds, respectively. When the boss strikes, [[Agony Damage]] in form of percentage of maximum hit points will be dealt for both strikes. The damage depends on the current target and their [[vocation]]:
** [[Knight]]s will lose 5% maximum health from the first strike and 10% maximum health from the second strike
** [[Paladin]]s, [[Druid]]s, and [[Sorcerer]]s will lose 20% maximum health from the first strike and 40% from the second strike
** Note: the boss will yell {{Sound|WILL ... PUNISH ... YOU!}} once at the start of the mechanic, then repeat it after the first strike. Finally, it yells {{Sound|RAAAR!}} when doing the second attack, which should help players identify the moment it happened

The most common strategy is to have the [[Paladin]] run in circles with the boss at the northern part of the room, while shooters split on the west and east sides. The [[Knight]] takes the [[Elder Bloodjaw]]s and runs in circles with them at the southern part of the room. Once the Rotten Charge appears, the Knight should lure the Bloodjaws on it, then take the boss to block the two hits. Once they are done, the Paladin should take the boss again and the whole strategy is repeated until the boss is dead.

{{ilink|Ichgahal}} [[Ichgahal]] is the twin of [[Murcion]], and both share similarities. This is arguably the easiest boss in the quest, and that is why it is left for last, to be fought with three [[Bakragore's Taints]].

The boss will leave {{ilink|Spores}} [[Spores|spore tracks]] on the ground, wherever it passes or stands. If players come into contact with the tracks in this first stage, they receive minor [[Agony Damage]] and a [[Hexed|lesser hex]]. After a few seconds, the spore tracks turn into their second stage, which is {{Ilink|Volatile Funghi}} [[Volatile Funghi]]. If players come into contact with this second stage, they receive considerable [[Agony Damage]] and a [[Hexed|greater hex]] for 15 seconds, and the funghi becomes an even more dangerous {{Ilink|Spore Cloud}} [[Spore Cloud]]. [[Ichgahal]] heals itself by 13,500 [[HP]] for each Volatile Funghi it steps on, and by 27,000 [[HP]] for each Spore Cloud.

The boss fights together with two {{Ilink|Elder Bloodjaw}} [[Elder Bloodjaw]]s, not considering modifiers from [[Bakragore's Taints|taints]]. During the whole fight, the boss will keep summoning {{ilink|Mushroom}} [[Mushroom]]s randomly around itself. Up to 8 Mushrooms can exist at the same time, exploding after a while or if players come close to them, dealing 2,500+ [[Life Drain]] damage in a small area. 

There are two common strategies used on the fight. One is to have the [[Knight]] use [[Challenge]] on all [[Elder Bloodjaw]]s and stand in a corner, with one [[Druid]] staying nearby to heal them. This is because the room is big enough that the [[Paladin]] can then run a half-lap on the other side of the room, having basically three shooters staying away from the Elder Bloodjaws vision the entire fight and avoiding swapping caused by the [[Bakragore's Taints|first taint]]. Another is to repeat the strategy used in [[Murcion]], where the [[Knight]] just runs the full lap as well, staying half a lap behind the [[Paladin]].

The erased progress of the two bosses, while random, should be the same for the whole team. Players have 25 minutes to finish this fight. The following information considers a team fighting the boss on the easiest mode possible. For harder modes, players must consider the effects of having [[Bakragore's Taints#Bakragore's_Final_Taint|the final taint]] and [[Bakragore's Burden|burdens]]. The information also specifies the base damage values, this is important to know because players will always be under the influence of at least four [[Bakragore's Taints|taints]], so be aware and take into consideration that all base damage below will be further increased by 14% (or more, when under effect of final taint and burdens).

Staying over the Darkfields causes 3% [[HP|maximum health]] per second as [[Agony Damage]]. Therefore, players can take take up to 18% maximum health per second if not handling these carefully. [[Bakragore]] heals itself by 500 [[HP]] for each Darkfield it steps on.

The boss can summon up to four {{ilink|Pillar of Dark Energy}} [[Pillar of Dark Energy|Pillars of Dark Energy]]. Each one of these Pillars can chain players in its melee range for [[Death Damage]]. Players should avoid standing next to one, and it is specially important to avoid standing next to many, as multiple chains can be triggered at the same time, dealing very high spikes of [[Death Damage]]. When taking damage from this chain spell, a different counter is applied to the characters hit by it ([[File:Quest Condition Red Ball.png]] icon). Every 90 seconds, Bakragore will drop a [[Rotten Charge]] at the center of the room that deals 1,250 [[Agony Damage]] to everyone in the room and lasts for 15 seconds. If no [[Elder Bloodjaw]] steps on it:
* The room will make an announcement after 5 seconds ({{sound|The rotten charge is going to explode!}}), and again after 10 seconds ({{sound|THE ROTTEN CHARGE WILL EXPLODE SOON!}}). After 15 seconds, the charge will explode ({{sound|THE ROTTEN ENERGY EXPLODES!}}), and each player will take 80% of their character's maximum [[HP|hit points]] as [[Agony Damage]].

If an [[Elder Bloodjaw]] steps on it:
* all players in a small radius of the charge will not generate [[Darkfield]]s for 15 seconds
* the boss will not heal itself on fields for 15 seconds
* all [[Elder Bloodjaw]]s will be removed from the boss room for 15 seconds
* the resistances of the boss will be noticeably lowered for 15 seconds
* the boss will prepare a devastating spell that hits its current target after 6 seconds and 12 seconds, respectively. When the boss strikes, [[Agony Damage]] in form of percentage of maximum hit points will be dealt for both strikes. This strike hits differently, depending on the target's [[vocation]]:
** [[Knight]]s will lose 5% maximum HP from the first strike and 10% maximum HP from the second strike
** [[Paladin]]s, [[Druid]]s, and [[Sorcerer]]s will lose 40% maximum hit points from the first strike and 80% from the second strike
** Note: the boss will not yell to indicate when the strike has happened, so players must pay extra attention to detect it.

==== Bakragore's Burden ====
:{{Mainarticle|Bakragore's Burden}}

The fight against [[Bakragore]] can be made harder and more rewarding if players sacrifice at least one of its minion's essence before starting. For each essence they sacrifice, an {{ilink|Elder Bloodjaw}} [[Elder Bloodjaw]] will be replaced by an echo of the corresponding boss, but rewards will be applied for that fight. See [[Bakragore's Burden]] for all the details.

=== Rewards ===

Upon defeating [[Bakragore]] for the first time, players have an increased chance to drop a {{Ilink|Bag You Covet}} [[Bag You Covet]]. This chance will keep on increasing further with every kill until they loot a bag from it. Then, the drop chance returns to its original value which is considerably lower than the increased first-kill chance. Each time they subsequently defeat the boss without receiving a bag, the chances increase again until their next drop.

Players who defeated Bakragore will receive the base [[Decaying Defender Outfits]], and also be granted permission to make [[Crimson Offering]]s to the [[Altar (NPC)|Altar]] in the [[Blood Vestibule]].
