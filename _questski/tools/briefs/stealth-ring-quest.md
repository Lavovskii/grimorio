## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Stealth Ring Quest}}
This [[quest]] is not at all complicated, just dangerous.

==Method==
* Head to the [[Minotaur Pyramid]] north-east of [[Darashia]] ([COORD x=33312 y=32282 z=7 | here]). You will only have to face [[Minotaurs]] on the ground level.
* Go down the staircase. Down these steps you will face [[Minotaurs]] and one or two [[Minotaur Guard]]s.
* Follow this path to the south-west, and go down another staircase:
[[Image:Stealth Ring Quest Map 01.jpg]]

* Head to the East/North-East. There will be several [[Minotaur Guard]]s and [[Minotaur Archer]]s on this floor. There will be another staircase to go down. On the next level are all kinds of [[Minotaurs]], including about 4 [[Minotaur Mage]]s:
[[Image:Stealth Ring Quest Map 02.jpg]]

* On this level, head North. Look for another staircase down. Down this staircase, you will face one [[Mummy]]:
[[Image:Stealth Ring Quest Map 03.jpg]]

* After killing the [[Mummy]], the [[Stealth Ring]] can be found in the south-east coffin, and the [[Protection Amulet]] can be found in the north-east coffin:
[[Image:Stealth Ring Quest Map 04.jpg]]
