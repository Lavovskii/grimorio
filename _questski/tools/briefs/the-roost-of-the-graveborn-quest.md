== Method ==
=== Visit Draconia ===

{{Message|type=server|You arrived. Great! Now investigate the Dragon Cemetery for a hint about what is going on there.}}

Go north to investigate Dragon Cemetery by going down the hole [COORD x=32814 y=31562 z=7 | here] twice and speak with [[Dragon Ancestor Spirit]].

{{KW|hi}} > {{KW|spirit}}

You learn that four ancestral dragons' spirits are being manipulated by someone. To free them, you need to seek out the places and relics to which their essences are bound.

Go back to [[S'Zallar M'Andar]] in Ab'dendriel to report the mission:

{{KW|hi}} > {{KW|mission}}

====Location====
[[Pits of Inferno Dragon Lair]] ([COORD x=32755 y=32308 z=12 | here])

====Boss mechanics====
There are 4x {{ilink|Pulsating Lava}} [[Pulsating Lava]]s, one in each corner of the room. After restoring enough health points, {{ilink|Pulsating Lava}} [[Pulsating Lava]] will transform into {{ilink|Eruption}} [[Eruption]] and then into {{ilink|Flame Totem}} [[Flame Totem]]. Each totem does AoE fire damage that heals the boss with range increasing with every stage. When [[Flame Totem]] is active, the [[Eldritch Dragon Lord]] takes reduced damage, and with at least 3 active totems, the boss becomes invulnerable. As the [[Eldritch Dragon Lord]]'s health decreases, there will be times when [[Pulsating Lava]]s restore health and transform into [[Flame Totem]]s. The goal of the fight is to keep at least 2 totems in their [[Pulsating Lava]] stage so the boss is vulnerable.

====After the fight====
Enter a {{ilink|Magic Forcefield (Green)}} '''green teleport''' south-west of the room and use the {{ilink|Molten Throne}} [[Molten Throne]]

'''BEWARE''': You cannot use the green teleport to escape before the boss is defeated, even if you have previously completed the encounter.

We then report our progress by using the {{ilink|Jade Dragon Head}} [[Jade Dragon Head]], we should see the following message:

{{Message|type=server|S'Zallar: Well done. Now seek the hoard in the deadly ice caves around the island of Okolnir. Attune to the prize possession there while while having the jade talisman with you.}}

=== {{ilink||Ice Horror}} [[Ice Horror]] ===
{{FightTips
|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Ice}}
|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Death}}
}}

====Requirements====
* [[Eldritch Dragon Lord]] defeated and relic touched
* {{ilink|Jade Dragon Head}} [[Jade Dragon Head]]
* {{ilink|Fire Bomb Rune}} [[Fire Bomb Rune]]

====Location====
[[Okolnir]] ([COORD x=32151 y=31453 z=7 | here])

====Boss mechanics====
Beware of the frequent [[Rooted]] and [[Fear]] conditions during the fight. Use [[Fire Bomb Rune]] to deal with the [[Fear]] condition. Each player is also afflicted with a [[Drunk]] condition that cannot be removed.

Once in the boss room, players will be randomly targeted by cannons on the ship to the right. This is indicated by a red target effect left on the ground and the following message:

{{Message|type=server|You are targeted!}}

Roughly 3 seconds after being targeted, a small AoE explosion will occur, damaging all creatures nearby and temporarily leaving a hole in the ice. Stepping onto a hole will freeze players and afflict them with a [[Rooted]] condition.

With this mechanic in mind, the first step is to use it to get past two {{ilink|Ice Blockade}} [[Ice Blockade]]s to the north. Position yourself near an [[Ice Blockade]] so that the explosions destroy it and create a passage. Additionally, {{ilink|Ice Crawler}} [[Ice Crawler]]s will spawn occasionally along the way. Don't waste time killing them, as they are dealt with by the cannon mechanic.

Upon reaching the [[Ice Horror]], it will initially be almost invulnerable, and party must use the cannon mechanic to damage it. When the boss reaches roughly half health, it spawns a large wave (approx. 20) of {{ilink|Ice Crawler}} [[Ice Crawler]]s and becomes vulnerable to normal attacks. This is indicated by the following message {{Message|type=sound|The ice carpace breaks and the Ice Horror becomes vullnerable to ordinary attacks!}}

====After the fight====
Enter a {{ilink|Magic Forcefield (Green)}} '''green teleport''' north of the room and use the {{ilink|Draconic Rattle}} [[Draconic Rattle]]

'''BEWARE''': After entering the green teleport, stop moving until the [[Drunk]] condition wears off to avoid accidentally leaving the room before using the '''Draconic Rattle'''.

'''BEWARE''': You cannot use the green teleport to escape before the boss is defeated, even if you have previously completed the encounter.

We then report our progress by using the {{ilink|Jade Dragon Head}} [[Jade Dragon Head]], we should see the following message:

{{Message|type=server|S'Zallar: Again you were successful. The last hoard is located in the Mountain that seperates destert and jungle in Darama. Attune to the prize possession there while while having the jade talsiman with you.}}

* A player going through the wave will increase their stack count. Each stack increases damage taken. '''Getting a 21th stack causes instant death.'''
* [[Gravedigger]] going through the wave will increase its stack count. Each stack increases its damage and reduces damage taken.

=== {{Ilink|Bone Overlord}} [[Bone Overlord]] ===

====Requirements====
* All previous bosses defeated
* {{ilink|Fire Bomb Rune}} [[Fire Bomb Rune]] use suggested

====Location====
Below [[Draconia]], in the [[Forgotten Crypt]], [COORD x=32925 y=31779 z=11 | here]

====Phase 1: Soulcages====

{{FightTips
|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Death}}
|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Energy}}
}}

Upon entering the room, four different Soulcages can be found, each linked to a different {{ilink|Myzareth's Spirit}} dragon Spirit and a different [[Special Condition]] that is randomly applied to all players in the room during the fight: {{Icon|Feared}} [[Feared]], {{Icon|Rooted}} [[Rooted]], {{Icon|Slowed}} [[Slowed]], and {{Icon|Drunk}} [[Drunk]]. Players must be aware and ready to handle any of these at any time during the fight. The dragon Spirits are one of the sources for these conditions, but not the only source. 

The dragon Spirits are immune to all damage, but their Soulcages can be killed, and their [[HP]] pool is shared. It is important to not let a dragon Spirit near its Soulcage, or the Soulcage can heal up to 5,000+ per turn. At some points when the [[HP]] of the Soulcage is lowered, the linked dragon Spirit will teleport to it, making it harder to avoid the healing.
