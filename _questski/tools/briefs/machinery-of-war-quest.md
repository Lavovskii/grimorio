## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Machinery of War Quest}}
==Required Equipment==
*Up to 44 [[Bricklayers' Kits]]
*1 [[Fire Bug]] (but it is advisable to carry more than one, since it can explode in your hands)

==Method==

Go to [[Baxter]], he is outside the [[Rain Castle]], in [[Thais]].

Ask him about the "Orc War", and he will tell you that there are 3 tasks you can do:
*Establishing outposts
*Burning war equipment
*Getting war plans

=== Task 1: Establish outposts ===
[[Image:Broken_wall.jpg|thumb]] [[Image:orcs_outpost_attack.jpg|thumb]]
'''''NOTE: The outpost can only be repaired once every 6~7 hours.'''''
Buy 44 [[Bricklayers' Kit]]s from [[Baxter]]. Go to the Wyvern nest in [[Ferngrims Gate]], outside the [[Orc Fortress]], [COORD x=32775 y=31831 z=7 | here]. 
Your mission is to repair 4 outposts and 2 walls. To repair a broken [[Brick Wall]] you have to use a [[Bricklayers' Kits]] on it. It's fully repaired when an orange warning appears and the orcs spawn. Orcs will spawn in waves every 30 seconds for five minutes. During these attacks orcs can drop [[Orc Tusk]]s. For the quest it does not matter if you kill the orcs or not, when you see the warning, you can just go to the next place (if nobody kills the summoned [[orcs]], they will dissapear after 10 minutes).<br />

[[Image:Outposts.PNG]]<br />
''This image will help you to locate the walls and outposts.''

Orcs spawn at each outpost:

* 1 - 8 Warriors, 14 Berserkers, 9 Shamans, 4 Riders, 1 Leader, 1 Warlord
* 2 - 10 Warriors, 13 Berserkers, 10 Shamans, 5 Riders, 1 Leader, 1 Warlord
* 3 - 12 Warriors, 15 Berserkers, 9 Shamans, 6 Riders, 1 Leader, 1 Warlord
* 4 - 9 Warriors, 9 Berserkers, 5 Shamans, 4 Riders, 1 Leader
* W North - 8 Warriors, 15 Berserkers, 7 Shamans, 5 Riders, 1 Leader, 1 Warlord
* W South -  10 Warriors, 14 Berserkers, 11 Shamans, 4 Riders, 2 Leaders, 1 Warlord

=== Task 2: Burning war equipment ===
[[Image:Burned_ballistaes.jpg|thumb]] [[Image:Burned_catapults.jpg|thumb]] [[Image:Burned_towers.jpg|thumb]]
'''''NOTE: The Orc equipment can only be set on fire once every 6~7 hours.'''''<br />
For this mission you have to set the following war machines on fire:
*8 [[Ballista]]es
*8 [[Catapult]]s
*2 [[Siege Tower]]s

Buy [[Fire Bug]] for this task and go here:<br />
[[Image:Balistaes.PNG]]

Find the Ballistaes (8) and set them all on fire with the fire bug. When you are done, go to the Catapults:<br />
[[Image:Catapults.PNG]]

Find the catapults (8) and use fire bug on them as well to set them on fire. Finally, head south of the Orc Fortress to the [[Siege Tower]]s:<br />
[[Image:Siege_Towers.PNG]]

When you found the [[Siege Tower]]s, put all the wheels (3 on each) of it on fire.

After setting all the equipment on fire, report back to [[Baxter]] and tell him you have made an '''achievement'''.

=== Task 3: War plans ===
[[Image:War_plans.jpg|thumb]]
When you've done the other 2 missions, go to Baxter and ask him about War Plans. Go to Orc Fortress and go down 1 floor.

[[Image:War plans 1.png]]
[[Image:War plans 2.png]]

The plans are at the northern wall. Use them and an orange text should appear saying you should report back to Baxter. Go back to him and tell him about the 'war plans' to be awarded 1000 experience points.

When you complete this mission you need to wait some days before you can do it again:<br />
Baxter: We already know their current plan! Come back in a few days. By then they might have changed their plans and I will have a mission for you.

You can complete the war plans part of the quest as many times as you like, independent of the repairing/burning parts.

==Ranks==

To raise your rank, complete multiple achievements ('''NOTE:''' You can either complete the ''Task 2: Burning war equipment ''(once) or ''Task 1: Repairing the outposts ''(twice) to increase your rank):

Make 3 achievements to become a Henchman and get additional 2,000 experience as reward.

Make 6 achievements to become a Mercenary and get 3,000 experience for this advancement.

Make 12 achievements to become an Orc Butcher and get 5,000 experience for this advancement.

Make 20 achievements to get the highest rank - War Hero. You will get 7,000 experience for this advancement and from now you will get 2,000 (instead of 1,000) experience each time you finish an achievement.
