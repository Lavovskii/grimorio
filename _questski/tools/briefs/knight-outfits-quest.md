## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Knight Outfits Quest}}

=== Outfit ===
These outfits may be selected by any player. Only the addons are included in this quest.

=== Sword ===
[[File:Outfit Knight Male Addon 1.gif]]
[[File:Outfit Knight Female Addon 1.gif]]

==== Required Equipment ====
* 100 [[Iron Ore]]
* 1 [[Huge Chunk of Crude Iron]]

==== Method ====
You can obtain all of the items listed above before you start this quest. You are advised to collect the items before you talk to the NPC.
* Collect [[Iron Ore]] either from all kind of [[Dwarves]] or specific [[Geo-Elementals]], such as [[Stone Golem]]s.
* Obtain the [[Huge Chunk of Crude Iron|crude iron]] by trading a [[Giant Sword]] to [[A Sweaty Cyclops]] in [[Ab'Dendriel]].
* Talk to [[Morgan]] in [[Sabrehaven]] and ask him about '''forge'''. He will tell you that he can forge you either a Knight's Sword or a Warrior's Sword. Tell him you want a '''Knight's Sword'''.
* He will tell you to bring him 100 pieces of iron ore and one piece of crude iron. Once you bring him the items, he will give you the sword addon.

=== Helmet ===
[[File:Outfit Knight Male Addon 2.gif]]
[[File:Outfit Knight Female Addon 2.gif]]

==== Required Equipment ====
* 100 [[Perfect Behemoth Fang]]s
* 1 [[Damaged Steel Helmet]]
* 1 [[Flask of Warrior's Sweat]]
* 1 [[Piece of Royal Steel]]

==== Method ====
All of the items listed above may be collected before you start the mission (except the [[Damaged Steel Helmet]]). You are advised to collect these items before you visit the NPC.
* Talk to [[Gregor]] and ask him about his outfit. He will tell you that you must prove your worthiness to wear such an adorned helmet.
* First you must collect 100 [[Behemoth Fang]]s by looting and/or [[Skinning]] [[Behemoth]]'s.
* You are then sent to retrieve the [[Damaged Steel Helmet|helmet of Ramsay the Reckless]] found behind a sealed door in [[Banuta]]'s final quest room, [COORD x=32862 y=32522 z=11 | here].
:'''Warning:''' If you didn't talk to Gregor first about the [[Damaged Steel Helmet|Ramsay the Reckless Helmet]] in Banuta you will not be able to open the door to get it!
* You must now obtain a [[Flask of Warrior's Sweat]] by trading 4 [[Warrior Helmet]]s to the [[Shopkeeper NPCs|Magic Shopkeeper]] Djinns in the [[Djinn]] Towers or looting from [[Undead Gladiator]]s.
* Lastly you are asked to bring [[Royal Steel]]. It can be obtained by trading a [[Crown Armor]] to [[A Sweaty Cyclops]] in [[Ab'Dendriel]] or looted from [[Plaguesmith]]s.
* Once you have all of the items needed, return to [[Gregor]] and he will send you to [[Sam]] to adorn your helmet. Wait 2 hours to get your new helmet.
