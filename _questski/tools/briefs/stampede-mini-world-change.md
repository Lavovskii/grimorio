== Method ==
This change happens in the jungle of [[Tiquanda]], south of [[Banuta]], [COORD x=32856 y=32673 z=7 | here].

For one day you can find [[Terrified Elephant]]s, which have a higher chance to drop [[Elephant Tusk]]s than normal [[Elephant]]s. The area where they spawn is small and mostly cleared of vegetation. It contains up to 16 Terrified Elephants.

Kill 5 of them to get the [[achievement]] [[Trail of the Ape God]].
