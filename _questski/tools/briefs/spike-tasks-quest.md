## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler|name=Spike Tasks Quest}}
To reach [[The Spike]], enter the northern teleporter near [[Xelvar]], at the temple area of [[Kazordoon]], [COORD x=32623 y=31857 z=11 | here] ([[Teleport Crystal]]s are not needed).

Depending on your level, you must go to different divisions of the Spike.
* Level 25-49 goes to the [[Upper Spike]].
* Level 50-79 goes to the [[Middle Spike]].
* Level 80+ goes to the [[Lower Spike]].

Each division has 4 daily tasks which can be completed for 1 fame point each. These fame points can be accumulated and eventually used to purchase the [[Cave Explorer Outfits|Cave Explorer outfits and addons]]. You require 100 fame to complete the full outfit, which will take a minimum of 25 days since the tasks have a 20 hours cooldown. You can check your current amount of fame points by asking [[Gnommander]] about '''reward''' and then checking your questlog. 

Because of its difficulty, it is recommended to bring a team if you're going into Upper or Middle spike, or if you are going to the Lower spike near its minimum allowed level.

If you lose a quest item, the NPC [[Gnomux]] will sell all items required.

== Upper Spike Tasks ==
These tasks are available if your character is in the level range 25-49. Ask [[Gnomilly]] on the 2nd floor of the Spike for '''missions'''. Receive all missions ('''pacifiers''', '''release''', '''track''', '''kill'''), and then leave the area using the [[Large Crystal Teleporter (Berg)|large crystal teleporter]].

Having received the '''pacifiers''' tasks from [[Gnomilly]], search the first 3 floors of the Spike for [[Ghost Pacifier]]s. Use the [[Tuning Fork]] on the ghost pacifiers 7 times. You can use the same pacifier once every 2 minutes.

If you fail, you will receive one of these messages instead:
* ''You've found nothing special'' &mdash; Nothing happens.
* ''You unearthed some not-so-death creature'' &mdash; [[Zombie]] or [[Demon Skeleton]] spawns.
* ''Your crude digging has angered some ancient ghost'' &mdash; [[Ghost]] spawns.

== Middle Spike Tasks ==
These missions are available if you are in the level range 50-79. Receive all the missions from [[Gnombold]] on the 4th floor.

It is strongly recommended for mages to bring a [[Terra Set]] and use [[Fire Damage|Fire]] attacks because all of the monsters in this division deal earth damage and most are weak to fire. Be very careful of the [[Crystalcrusher]]s, the hardest monsters in the division.

==== Destroy Nests ====
Having received the '''nests''' task, go to the south side of the inner Spike (the safe zone) and step between the crystal structures to disguise as a [[Mutated Bat]]. Now enter the cave and search for nests. You can only use nests when disguised,you need to destroy 8 nests. A nest will "repair" itself after one to two minutes and can then be used again.

Nests can be found at the {{Map Marker|red exclamation mark}}

Turn in the task for 2000 experience and 1 fame point.

==== Fertilising Mushrooms ====
Having received the '''fertilise''' task, search the caves for large mushrooms. Use the fertiliser on the mushrooms. You must do this 4 times on different mushrooms, it isn't possible to fertilise the same mushroom twice. This counts per player, so both you and your friend can fertilise the same mushroom.

Mushrooms can be found at the {{Map Marker|orange star}}

Turn in the task for 2000 experience and 1 fame point.

==== Geomantic Charges ====
For this task you need to find magnetic monolithes and use the [[Lodestone]] on the charged monolith. Using it once, gives you a [[Partially Charged Lodestone]]. Using it on two charged monolithes, gives you a [[Highly Charged Lodestone]]. On the third use, you will get the message: ''You have charged you body with geomantic energy and can report about it.''

Monolithes will recharge themself in around one to two minutes and can be used again then. '''It is possible that you get teleported to a random place in the cave!'''

A Monolith can be found at the blue question mark {{Map Marker|blue question mark}}.

==== Killing Crystal Crushers ====
Having received the '''kill''' task, enter the caves and kill 7 [[Crystalcrusher]]s.

Turn in the task for 2000 experience and 1 fame point.

==== Temperature Gauging ====
Having received the '''temperature''' task, enter the caves and search for lava pools. The hot spot is the same for each player, but changes each day with [[Server Save]] and it can be on any of the 3 Lower Spike floors. Use the [[Thermometer]] to check for a hotspot near the lava pools. When you receive the message ''Whew! That was that hot, it melted the thermometer! At least you've found the hot spot!'', report back to [[Gnomargery]].
