==Required equipment==
*[[Rope]]

==Method==
*Go to [[The Jolly Axeman Tavern]] in [[Kazordoon]].([COORD x=32637 y=31888 z=9 | here])
*In upper side of room you will see 4 sewer gates.
*Go down, kill rat and take [[Explorer Brooch]] from body.
[[File:ExplorerBroochQuest.gif]]

You can trade the brooch with Mortimer in the explorer society building in Northport for 50 gp.
