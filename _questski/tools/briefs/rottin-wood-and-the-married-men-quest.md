## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Rottin Wood and the Married Men Quest}}

== Required Equipment ==
*[[Obsidian Knife]] or [[Kitchen Knife]] (one [[Daily Respawns|spawns daily]] on the [[Timber Table]] next to [[Rottin Wood]]))
*[[Hammer]]

== Method ==
=== Mission 1: Lucky Charms ===
[[File:Outlaw_camp_map_RW.png]]<br/>
Go to [[Rottin Wood]]'s house in [[Outlaw Camp]]. Ask him for a mission and agree to help him. He will hand you 7 [[Handcrafted Ribbon]]s and ask you to get some [[Rabbit's Foot|Rabbit's Feet]]. Kill some [[Rabbit]]s around the camp and use a [[Kitchen Knife]] or an [[Obsidian Knife]] on their bodies (you have to wait 10 seconds before [[skinning]]). You will have a chance of getting 1, 2 or failure to get any Rabbit's Feet. You can skin successfully up to 7 Rabbits (total of 7-14 Rabbit's Feet), afterwards a message appears telling that you have skinned enough for today. Once you have 7 feet or more, use the Handcrafted Ribbons on the feet to make [[Rabbit's Foot Lucky Charm]]s. Return to Rottin Wood and tell him that you completed this mission. '''Note:''' If you lose the Handcrafted Ribbons, you will need to wait approximately 30 minutes to get more from Rottin Wood.<br/>
[[File:Rottin Wood and the Married Men Quest_bunny_1.png]]
*You will receive 100 [[experience]] and 500 [[gp]].

=== Mission 2: In a State of Disrepair ===
[[File:Rottin Wood and the Married Men Quest_repair_1.png]]<br/>
Ask Rottin Wood for another mission. He will tell you to repair broken walls around the camp. To repair, use a [[Hammer]] on a broken wall. Once you fixed a wall it will become broken again after 2 minutes so you can repair it again (you can fix the same wall 6 times). There are 4 walls in need of repair in the vicinity of [[Rottin Wood]] - just look on the walls on some of of the buildings around him. There is also a wall which needs repairing on the top floor of his house. When you have repaired all 6 walls go back to Rottin Wood and report that you completed this mission.

*You will receive 500 experience and 500 gp.

=== Mission 3: Bushwhacking ===
[[File:Rottin Wood and the Married Men Quest_thief_2.png]]<br/>
Ask Rottin Wood about the next mission. He needs your help trapping [[Travelling Merchant|passing merchants]] and will mark the spot on your [[Automap]]. Head north, place all the 5 [[Net]]s ([[use]] them on the ground) and you will be kicked from the area. You will be blocked out of it by a [[Small Fir Tree]]. Wait until 5 of the Travelling Merchants enter the nets and after 5 minutes the tree will disappear.<br/>
[[File:Rottin_Wood_and_the_Married_Men_Quest_thief_3.png]]<br/>
Re-enter the area and 'USE' the [[Captured Merchant]]s to loot them. After looting all 5, head back to Rottin Wood. '''Note:''' If you fail to collect all 5 of the valuables from the merchants, you will need to wait 30 minutes to get more from Rottin Wood.
*You will receive 1000 experience, 500 gp and the [[Achievement]] [[Merry Adventures]].

=== Daily Task: Lucky Loop ===
This task is the same as the first mission. Collect the 7 Rabbit's Feet and use the Handcrafted Ribbons to make Rabbit's Foot Lucky Charms.

'''Note:''' You may also hand [[Fake Rabbit's Foot|7 Fake Rabbit's Feet]] and receive the Achievement [[Bunny Slipped]]. To make Fake Rabbit's Feet, use the Handcrafted Ribbons on [[Wooden Spoon]]s to get [[Spoon with a Ribbon|Spoons with Ribbon]] and then use them on [[Wool]]. Once you are done, return to Rottin and tell about your task.

*You will receive 100 experience and one of the following: 500 gp (90% of probability), 300 [[gp]] and [[Time Ring]] (9%), or [[Hunting Horn]] (1%?).(When you receive the horn [[Rottin Wood]] tells you: ''Good hunt. That will be enough to help us uhm... get through the winter yes. And here is a very special reward for you. I hope you know how to use something like that, heh.'').

{{Seealso|Rottin Wood and the Married Men Quest/Statistics|usage=article}}

=== Daily Task: Handyman ===
This task is the same as the second mission, he will tell you to repair broken walls around the camp using a Hammer. When you have repaired 6 walls go back to Rottin Wood and tell him that you completed the task.
*You will receive 500 experience and 500 gp.
