==Method==
From April 1 until May 1 every year, the island of [[Chyllfroest]] will be accessible. Visit it and go to the very top of the mountain (sometimes you will need the [[Levitate]] spell) to find a [[Pile of Bones (Quest)]]. Use this pile to obtain a [[Tinder Box]]. This can be repeated every 20 hours.
