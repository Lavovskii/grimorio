## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name= Spike Sword Quest}}
==Required Equipment==
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Rope]]
* [[Pick]]
* [[Invisible]] spell or [[Stealth Ring]]s for [[Fire Devil]]s

==Method==
* Go to the small cave east of [[Mount Sternum]] ([COORD x=32593 y=32068 z=7 | here]), and go down.

* You will be in a small room, go down another level:
[[Image:Spike Sword Quest Map 01.jpg]]

* In this room, you will need to pick a hole just to the south-west, as indicated on the map. Go down the hole:
[[Image:Spike Sword Quest Map 02.jpg]]

* In this room is one [[Fire Devil]]. Follow the passage around to the west, and go down another level. You may want to use your [[Invisible]] spell or [[Stealth Ring]] now:
[[Image:Spike Sword Quest Map 03.jpg]]

* On this level, there will be 5 [[Fire Devil]]s. Head to the south-west corner of the room, and pick another hole, then go down:
[[Image:Spike Sword Quest Map 04.jpg]]

* Now you will be surrounded by lava, and there will be a large number of [[Fire Devil]]s, 4 of them are reachable, 10 of them are on islands, but they can still reach you with their [[Fireball]] attacks. It is recommended for someone to go [[Invisible]] and clear this room. The reward is inside a body that is hidden behind a pillar.
[[Image:Spike Sword Quest Map 05.jpg]]

* Note: You will likely hear the roar of three [[Dragon]]s. These dragons are caged at the far north end of the room. It is unknown how or why they are there.
