== Requirements ==

Since this quest revolves around exploring all Tibia, several quests will be required to access different locations of the map.

== Method ==

This quest tracks your progress in discovering the Tibia Map using the [[Cyclopedia#Map|Cyclopedia Map]]. Tibia is currently divided into 20 '''areas'''. Each area is subdivided in several subareas, some of which are discoverable. For example, the Thais area is divided into [[Thais|Thais City]], Thais Surroundings, [[Greenshore]], [[Fibula]], [[Mount Sternum]] and Thais Troll's Cave.

The quest is split into two "missions":
* '' A Speck of Land'': tracks the progress of discovery of the active subarea.
* ''The Wide World'': tracks the number of areas fully discovered.

=== A Speck of Land ===

To discover a subarea, open the [[Cyclopedia]], switch to the Map tab, click the subarea you want to discover and then click the ''Start Discovering'' button. Once you confirm your choice, several ''Points of Interest'' will be randomly placed across the map. The Points of Interest are placed differently for each player, which means you will have to search for them yourself. In order to complete this subarea, you have to find 7 Points of Interest within it. Note that you cannot discover multiple subareas simultaneously, i.e., if you start to discover a new area, your current progress will be reset. Once 7 Points of Interest of a subarea has been discovered, this subarea will be permanently unlocked for that character and you can start discovering another one.

Here are some tips regarding discovering subareas:

* A message will be shown on the bottom of the game window every time you enter or leave the subarea you are currently discovering. Pay attention to it to avoid searching outside of the valid zone.
* A subarea can span across multiple floors. For example, city subareas usually also include their sewers as well as the upper floor of public buildings, walls and watchtowers.
* Points of Interest are invisible if your character is on a different floor. For this reason, you should still go up or down a level even if it seems like there are no Points of Interest there.
* Even though more than 7 Points of Interest are generated for each subarea, you may find yourself stuck. If this happens, you can choose to reset their locations, but this will also reset you progress for this subarea, so think well before doing so.
* It is recommended that you activate discovering a subarea while outside of it. That is because Points of Interest will not be generated in the current game window, which may lead to the exclusion of one or more points that would have been easy to spot otherwise.
* If you are exploring a large open area, it is over 35% more efficient to scan across the area from top to bottom rather than side to side. This is because the game window is 15x11 squares, so if you walk vertically, you are seeing more squares for every step you take.
* Some tiles, for example quest doors or breakable-walls, are not part of the subarea. Because of this, when walking through them, you will get the message that you left the subarea, even if the area ahead of you is also part of it. Always go into the room past these locations to make sure you are not discarding an active searching area. An example of this is the level [COORD x=31928 y=32633 z=10 | door] that guards the [[Medusa]] and [[Serpent Spawn]] dungeon under [[Talahu]].
* The waypoints are actually randomly chosen from a predefined pool. [[TibiaMaps]] ([https://tibiamaps.io/map/poi here]) collects and already includes most of the points on their map.

Some subareas can be tricky because of special situations:

* The hall and watching areas of the [[Duelling Arena]]s and [[Monster Arena]]s of each town are also part of the respective City subarea.
* The [[Tibian Prize Exhibition]] is part of the Thais City subarea. Thus, unless it is Sunday, it may be harder to find all 7 points around Thais and you might have to reset the points to find all 7.
* The Exhibition area of the [[Museum of Tibian Arts]] is also considered part of [[Thais Surroundings]].
* In order to fully explore the ''Ice Islands'' subarea, it is advised to shipwreck in the [[Arctic Faun's Island]] which happens by chance. Otherwise, it might be needed to reset your points several ''(hundred)'' times in order to discover this subarea only on the isle reached through [[Folda]]. In order to speed up this method, it is advised to bind action "Dialogs: Open Cyclopedia - Map" for quick resets.
* You can only explore [[Zzaion]] when its open due to the Orc Raid there.
* The small mountain side east of [[Vengoth]] is also part of its subarea, and you may find a point of interest here by going up the [COORD x=33010 y=31437 z=7 | ramp] from the Zao Steppe.
* The [[Dragonblaze Peaks]] subarea also contains the [[Zao Palace Antechambers]] and the [[Zao Palace]] itself, though you can find 6 or even 7 points without entering the Palace, but if you're missing one you may have to face some Drakens.
* The [[Emperor's Castle]] includes the [[Razachai/Inner Sanctum|Inner Sanctum]], which is the area of the [[Wrath of the Emperor Quest]] with [[Ghastly Dragon]]s and [[Undead Dragon]]s.
* [[Ragnir]] [[Stone Golem]] cave ([COORD x=32081 y=31375 z=8 | here]) is part of [[Krimhorn]] (Barbarian Camp) subarea.
* When discovering [[Folda]], which has a huge underground cave system, waypoints are also generated inside the cave from [[Alawar's Vault Quest]]. It is highly advisable to combine that quest (the long path) with exploring [[Folda]], since that cave can only be reached by acquiring certain keys.
* When discovering the [[Ankrahmun]] [[Pharaoh]] [[Ankrahmun Tombs|Tombs]], Points of Interests can also be generated inside the boss rooms.
* Points of Interest for [[Fenrock]] may be generated in an area, to which access is obtained during the last main mission of [[Shadows of Yalahar Quest]].
* [[Banuta]] region has Points of Interest inside [[Deeper Banuta]], which require [[The Ape City Quest]].
* The entrance area to [[Gray Island]] (before the vortex) is considered part of [[Edron]] Surroundings.

=== The Wide World ===

As you discover more subareas of a given area, you will unlock some new map features for this area:

* After unlocking 30% of the area, locations of [[NPC]]s within that respective area will be shown on the map.
* For discovering 70% of the area, you will be able to see passages to subareas on other map layers and teleports will be displayed as stair symbols on the map.
* Once you discover 100% of an area, you will receive the following perks:
** Creatures that occur in those subareas are listed when you click on a subarea's name or on a passages symbol that leads to this subarea. Creatures for which the Bestiary entry has not yet been unlocked are displayed as a silhouette.
** You will be notified one hour before a [[Raid]] is due to take place in that area. 15 minutes before the raid, you will be notified of the specific subarea, unless the raid happens below the ground floor. For example, if a raid in Nothern Darama has no 15-minute imminent raid icon show up, that means it is a raid in [[Drefia]].
** You will receive an [[Achievement]] corresponding to this area and a progress point towards the [[Discoverer Outfits]].

== Rewards ==

Besides the reward received for completing subareas and areas, you can also receive the [[Discoverer Outfits]] and its two addons for completing 10, 15 and 20 areas, respectively. 

When you complete 10 discovered Areas, you will receive a message: {{Sound|Awesome! You have already discovered 10 areas completely! Go and find Charos. He might have a little reward for such an ambitious discoverer like you.}}

To receive the outfit and addons, talk to [[Charos]] in the [[Adventurers' Guild]] about your {{KW|outfit}}. You will get the [[Achievements|achievement]] [[Widely Travelled]]. When you complete 20 areas and ask for the second addon, you will get the [[Measuring the World]] achievement as well.

You will also receive the [[Dedicated Entrepreneur]] character [[title]] after exploring 50% of the map areas, and the [[Globetrotter]] title after exploring 100%.
