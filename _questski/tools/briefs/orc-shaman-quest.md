==Required Equipment==
* [[Rope]]
* [[Shovel]]

==Method==
* Make your way through the swamp to a small cave ([COORD x=33055 y=32030 z=7 | here]). You will need to go east from [[Venore]]'s south gate.
<gallery mode="nolines">
Image:Venore_shaman_quest.jpg|Correct path
</gallery>
* Use your [[Shovel]] to open a hole, and go down into it.
* Once in the cave, head directly east and go down a level:
[[Image:Orc Shaman Quest Map.jpg]]

* On this level you will find a large number of [[Snake]]s, [[Orc]]s, [[Orc Spearman|Orc Spearmen]], and one [[Orc Shaman]]. The quest box is in the south-east corner of the room.
