== Requirements ==
* A [[Melee]] weapon, to break a wall.
* [[Levitate]], 3-6 [[Parcel]]s or equivalent.
* Completed 6 missions of [http://tibia.wikia.com/wiki/The_New_Frontier_Quest The New Frontier Quest]
* A [[Bucket]] of [[Milk]] ([[#Task 1 - Milk Delivery|Task 1 - Milk Delivery]])
* An [[Obsidian Knife]] or [[Crocodile Steak]] ([[#Task 2 - To Feed a Beast|Task 2 - To Feed a Beast]])
* A [[Wooden Flute]] ([[#Task 4 - Foul Spirits|Task 4 - Foul Spirits]])

== Method ==
=== Mission 1 - The Wrath of the Kahn ===
Go to the central [[Orc]] camp in the center of [[Zao Steppe]]s, and find the minotaur guard leader, [[Curos]], in the north-east section of the camp. Curos will give you the '''mission''' to infiltrate the north-western [[Zao Orc Land]] and kill a rebel, the [[Renegade Orc]].

Go to the north-western part of the [[Zao Steppe]] to reach the [[Zao Orc Land]]. Follow the path through the mountain to reach the last ramp ([COORD x=33060 y=31328 z=7 | here]) and go up. Along the east side of the mountain, look for a mountain to levitate up. It is concealed below the floor above, but if you hug the eastern side of the mountain you will see it. Levitate up and follow the mountain behind a bamboo fortress. Use your melee weapon on the damaged part of the wall until it breaks. Walk through and go downstairs. Go south-west and find the next ramp. Go down and then south-east to find a [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]]. Find the resistance leader, [[Renegade Orc]], in the room and kill him.

While you are here, it is recommended to get the [[Tome of Knowledge]] on the floor below. Go north from the ramp to find the entrance to the underground. From the ramp below, go north and west at the first chance you get to reach the room with the tome ([COORD x=33011 y=31252 z=8 | here]). You should only fight [[Orc Leader]]s, [[Orc Berserker]]s and smaller [[Orcs]] at the ramp and a lone [[Orc Warlord]] where the chest is. Get the [[Tome of Knowledge]] and leave.

Now report back to [[Curos]]. You will receive no reward from Curos for this mission but you have proven yourself worthy to the orcs.

=== Mission 2 - The Maw of the Dragon ===
'''Note:''' to save time, it is recommended to do this alongside [[The New Frontier Quest]], ''Mission 07: Messengers Of Peace.''

Ask [[Curos]] for a further mission. He will instruct you to find the entrance to the underground lizard base and destroy a magical scrying device. Go north through the small slits in the mountain and find the only rope hole on the surface within the [[Dragonblaze Peaks]] ([COORD x=33175 y=31306 z=7 | here]). Rope up twice but beware that [[Dragon Lord]]s may shoot you from other mountains. Go south-east and follow the mountain to the other side. Go down the ramp and north-west to find a [[Large Hole]]. Open the hole and drop down. From here on you will face strong [[High Class Lizards]]. Open the hole and go down to 1 [[Lizard Legionnaire]]. Kill it and go down again to 1-3 [[Lizard High Guard]]s. Fight your way through the hallways to reach the stairs. Go down to 1 [[Lizard High Guard]] and enter the [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]] a little west of the stairs.

You are now in the basement of the tower. You must ascend to the top of the tower and break the [[Scrying Ball]]. The tower is very small and has little room to run. Each floor has the following monsters:

== Curos Daily Tasks ==
After completing [[Curos|Curos']] 2 missions, you may request additional tasks for him. These are simple tasks that can be completed once per 20 hours. The 20 hour counter starts counting when you turn in your task. He will give out a task at random, and will reward you with experience and gold.

=== Task 1 - Milk Delivery ===
[[Curos]] may ask for a [[Bucket]] of [[Milk]]. Buy a [[Bucket]] from any [[Equipment Shopkeeper NPCs|equipment shopkeeper]] and [[Milk]] from a specialist food store. Use the milk on the bucket to change the containers. Bring the bucket of milk back to Curos.

'''Reward''': 800 experience, 650 gp and ''[[The Milkman]]'' achievement.

=== Task 2 - To Feed a Beast ===
[[Curos]] sends you to retrieve a [[Crocodile Steak]] from a [[Killer Caiman]]. Travel to [[Muggy Plains]] and kill a [[Killer Caiman]]. Then use an [[Obsidian Knife]] to obtain a [[Crocodile Steak]]. You might have to kill a few [[Killer Caiman]]s before you get a steak. Report back to [[Curos]] with the [[Crocodile Steak]] to complete the task.

'''Reward''': 1800 experience and 2250 gp.

=== Task 3 - Honour The Dead ===
Curos sends you to meditate on the Master Grave. You must stand on the grave on the northern side of the [[Dragonblaze Peaks]] ([COORD x=33139 y=31276 z=7 | here]) to complete the task.

'''Reward''': 1100 experience and 1000 gp.

=== Task 4 - Foul Spirits ===
[[Curos]] sends you to appease a tree spirit in an infested grove to the east by using a [[Wooden Flute]]. You should stand on the sqm in front of the tree spirit (with blood spot - [COORD x=33298 y=31388 z=7 | here]) when using the flute. There's a chance of failing. If you fail, 2 [[Haunted Treeling]]s or 2 [[Carniphila]]s will spawn. Kill the spawned creatures and wait for the tree spirit to calm down (it has to return to its initial form), then try playing the flute again. Sometimes you will be [[Cursed]] for 20 turns (300 total damage).

You will receive a red message once you have appeased the tree spirit. Report back to [[Curos]] to turn in the task.

'''Reward''': 1400 experience and 750 gp.
