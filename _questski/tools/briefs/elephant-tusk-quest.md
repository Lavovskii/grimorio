==Required Equipment==
None

==Method==
* Leaving from [[Port Hope]], follow the river on the south side until you reach the poacher's camp ([COORD x=32925 y=32754 z=7 | here]). The quest box is on the surface.
* A faster route is from Ankrahmun. Head south-west of the city until you find the bridge that connects to the Hydra spawns. Go south-west from the ladder, pictured just north-east of the [[Hydra]], but be careful!

* When travelling, stay close to the river to avoid the [[Carniphilia]]s and most of the [[Spit Nettle]]s.

* The [[Hydra]] spawn is north of the camp ([COORD x=32952 y=32730 z=7 | here]), so be careful when approaching, since there may be a lured [[Hydra]] waiting for you.
