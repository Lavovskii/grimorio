## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler|name=Sinatuki Quest}}

== Requirements ==
* Access to [[Nibelor]] (complete the [[Barbarian Test Quest]] and [[The Ice Islands Quest]], up to ''Nibelor 5: Cure the Dogs'').

== Method ==
You will need to give a combined total of 99 [[Green Perch]]es or [[Rainbow Trout]]s to [[Sinatuki]] and after that give her one more [[Green Perch]] or [[Rainbow Trout]] (100 fishes in total). There are multiple ways to obtain the fish:
*[[Ice Fishing]] in and around [[Svargrond]].
*Loot them from [[Chakoyas]] or other creatures.
*Buy from other players.

After obtaining the required amount of fish, go to [[Sinatuki]] and talk to them. After delivering all fish you will get your reward of 5 [[Shard]]s and the achievement [[Jamjam]].
