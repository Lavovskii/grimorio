## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Obsidian Knife Quest}}

== Required Equipment ==
* Completed [[The Sweaty Cyclops Quest]]
* [[Rope]]
* [[Scythe]]
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Dragon Shield]]
* [[Obsidian Lance]]

== Method ==
* Get a [[Dragon Shield]] and an [[Obsidian Lance]]. Once you have both of these items go to [[A Sweaty Cyclops]] who's in [[Ab'Dendriel]].
* If you haven't completed ([[The Sweaty Cyclops Quest]]) he will ask you for 3 [[Bast Skirt]]s so he can make a grass skirt for his wife. If you have already completed the quest, he will not ask you for them again.
* Ask him for the metal '''"uth'lokr"''' (commonly known as a [[Piece of Draconian Steel]] to humans). To make the metal, he will take your [[Dragon Shield]] and in return he will give you a [[Piece of Draconian Steel]].
* Walk to [[Kazordoon]] and take the steel and the obsidian lance to [[Uzgod]] below the [[Depot]].
* Ask him for '''"draconian steel"'''. He will take the draconian steel and an [[Obsidian Lance]] to make the knife.

{{DASH|NOTE: You can change the Piece of Draconian Steel and the Obsidian Lance to Uzgod without having offer the 3 bast skirt to the A Sweaty Cyclops}}
{{DASH|NOTE: You can trade these items as many times as you want to get more [[Obsidian Knife|Obsidian Knives]], but you must wait 20 hours between each one.}}

* [[Dragon Shield]] + [[Obsidian Lance]] = 4.5k (assuming you can buy them for djinn price}}
* [[Obsidian Knife]] = 5 - 12k (different on each server)
