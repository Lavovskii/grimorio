== Method ==
Different [[Nomads]] can be found at specific places in [[Kha'labal]], the desert surrounding [[Ankrahmun]]. It's not active every day and only one [[Nomad Camp]] out of four possible camps can be found when it's active. Three out of four of these camps have chests that can be claimed once per active world change. The creatures you'll find in this world change and only during this world change are [[Nomad (Blue)|blue-clothed nomad]]s and [[Nomad (Female)|female nomad]]s.

=== Rewards ===
The rewards are very simple and distributed as follows:

* South of the [[Tarpit Tomb]]: This camp has no chest, thus no rewards.
* Northeast of the [[Ancient Ruins Tomb]]: 10x [[Ham]]s {{Ilink|Ham}}
* Northeast of the [[Shadow Tomb]]: 50x [[Fish]]es {{Ilink|Fish}}
* South of the [[Ancient Ruins Tomb]]: 1x [[Fur Bag]] {{Ilink|Fur Bag}} containing:
** 3x [[Small Emerald]]s {{Ilink|Small Emerald}}
** 1x [[Black Pearl]] {{Ilink|Black Pearl}}
** 1x [[Small Topaz]] {{Ilink|Small Topaz}}
** 1x [[Silver Amulet]] {{Ilink|Silver Amulet}}

By opening all three chests you will be awarded the [[Chest Robber]] [[achievement]]. This means you'll have to check this world change at least three different days in order to achieve it.
