===Useful Items===
*[[Destroy Field]] Runes [[Antidote Rune]]s or [[Antidote]] Spell

===Method===

*Start off at the gate of [[Ab'Dendriel]]. Go south, west and north to Elvenbane Castle.<br>
*Drop down the hole surrounded by four stones. You are now in the basement of Elvenbane.

[[Image:Elvenbane Entrance.png]]

Run north and west to the opening. Here you may meet an [[Orc Berserker]] and a [[Minotaur Guard]]. Also a few [[Minotaur]]s and an [[Orc Spearman]] which may break your shield.<br>
Run around and enter the middle tower.

[[Image:Elvenbane1Floor.jpg]]

The first floor of the tower is entirely covered by poison fields. Walk over them or use [[Destroy Field]] runes to get rid of them. Prepare for the dragon upstairs.

[[Image:Elvenbane2Floor.JPG]]

On this floor you will face a [[Dragon]]. There are nothing to run around so 1 or 2 fast [[Energy Wave]]s are recommended for sorcerers. Anyway, kill the dragon or run past it to the ladder.

[[Image:Elvenbane3Floor.JPG]]

On this floor there is an [[Orc Shaman]] and a [[Minotaur Mage]]. Kill them and continue climb the stairs.

[[Image:Elvenbane4Floor.JPG]]

The final floor contains 2 quest chests and 2 quest drawers.<br>
There are also 2 [[Demon Skeleton]]s and an [[Elf Arcanist]], also known as the "playerhunter team".<br>
Kill them and open the quest chests/drawers to receive your stuff.
