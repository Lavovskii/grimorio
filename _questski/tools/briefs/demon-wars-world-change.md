## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Demon Wars World Change}}

Go to the [[Hero Cave]], north of the city of [[Edron]], [COORD x=33164 y=31640 z=7 | here]. Follow the common path to the main floor where many of the Hero Cave Quests are found. Go through the portal [COORD x=33162 y=31650 z=13 | here] to get into a safe room with the NPC [[A Dark Priestess]]. In this room there are stairs which will lead you to the middle of the Demon Wars cave.

To the east the [[Askarak Demon]]s reside, and to the west the [[Shaburak Demon]]s.

You can ask [[Guide NPCs]] about the '''demon war''' in order to check its current state. The possible messages/stages are:

* ''"The demon war is in a stalemate once again."'' — No faction has any advantage.
* ''"The Shaburak demons are in advantage right now."'' — [[Shaburak Lord]]s spawn on the upper floors of the western tower.
* ''"The Shaburak have summoned their leaders and dominate the complex."'' — [[Shaburak Lord]]s and [[Shaburak Prince]]s spawn on the upper floors of the western tower.
* ''"The Askarak demons are in advantage right now."'' — [[Askarak Lord]]s spawn on the upper floors of the eastern tower.
* ''"The Askarak have summoned their leaders and dominate the complex."'' — [[Askarak Lord]]s and [[Askarak Prince]]s spawn on the upper floors of the eastern tower.

In order to change the state of this world change, there must be a difference in kills between the two demon factions at the [[Server Save]]. The difference needs to be at least 100 to progress to the next stage. If the war is on a stalemate and the difference is greater than 400, however, the Demon War will go directly to the stage where the Princes spawn.
