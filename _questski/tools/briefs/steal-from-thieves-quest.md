== Method ==
'''Note:''' You can collect [[File:Bag (Ahmet).gif]] [[Bag (Ahmet)|Ahmet's belongings]] from the [[Box|box]] found ([COORD x=33131 y=32662 z=7 | here]) prior to speaking with him.

Begin by speaking with [[Ahmet]]. He can be found in [[Ankrahmun]] ([COORD x=33126 y=32810 z=6 | here]). Ask him about a {{KW|Mission}}, and he will inform you that he was robbed and will ask for your assistance in recovering his [[Bag (Ahmet)|belongings]].

Head north to the Oasis in [[Darama]] and find the box that is hidden behind a [[Palm]] south of the Oasis ([COORD x=33131 y=32662 z=7 | here]). Open it to get [[Ahmet|Ahmet's]] robbed things. '''Note''': you will need '''400oz''' capacity to collect his [[Bag (Ahmet)|belongings]].

[[File:Ahmets_Hidden_Bag.png]]

''You see a bag. It weighs 400.00 oz. It contains Ahmet's stolen property.''<br>

Return the [[Bag (Ahmet)|bag]] to [[Ahmet]] to collect your reward by saying {{KW|Hi}} - {{KW|Mission}} - {{KW|Yes}}.
