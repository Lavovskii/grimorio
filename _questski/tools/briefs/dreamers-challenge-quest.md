## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler|name=Dreamer's Challenge Quest}}
=== Required Equipment ===
* [[Rope]]
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Pick]]
* [[Life Ring]] (does not have to be new)
* 1-3 [[Liquid Containers]] with [[Water (Liquid)|Water]]
* 1-2 [[Fire Field]] Rune
* 2 [[Death Ring]]s (do not have to be new)
* [[Fire Bug]]
* [[Carrot]]
* It is also advisable to have [[Orichalcum Pearl|orichalcum pearls]] on you. So you can use the teleporters to escape without crossing the [[Plains of Havoc]]
Only for advancing in Ranks:
* 500 [[Demonic Essence]]s
* 1000 additional [[Demonic Essence]]s
* 1500 additional [[Demonic Essence]]s

=== Method ===

=== Mission 1 - Getting there ===
[[File:Pickhole.jpg|right]]Go to the [[Plains of Havoc]] Temple. Now walk east and go into the ruined building ([COORD x=32837 y=32253 z=7 | here]). Use your shovel on the northeast hole and drop in. Walk east into the other room. Use your pick until you find a hole. You will encounter 2 [[Ghoul]]s.

Walk towards the trees. You will be [[teleport]]ed to the other side and your [[carrot]] will be gone. Free account players are not allowed to pass this area.<br />[[File:Dreamer_Hugo_Free.jpg]].<br />Continue walking up and turn left. There will be a section with [[Poison Field]]s. Past the [[Poison Field]]s is one empty space. Use the [[Firebug]] here.

[[File:PoisonFields Dreamer'sChallenge.jpg]]

A [[Magic Forcefield|teleport]] will appear, walk into it and inside is a room full of trees. Above the trees is a floor-switch. Throw a [[Death Ring]] on it and the trees will clear open for you to enter. You must make it in before anyone else does or it will close.

[[File:DreamersChallengeDeathRing.jpg]]

=== Mission 2 - Matching color game ===

In here is the beginning of the Dreamer's Challenge. The first Challenge is a Matching color game. You must use the switches to match the 4 colors into their designated spots, much like how a Rubik's Cube works.

After completing it, run south and walk to the '''right [[Magic Forcefield|teleport]]''' before someone else does.

[[File:Dreamer Challenge Quest Solution.jpg|300px]]
[[File:Listolawl.jpg|300px]]

=== Mission 3 - Levers and Walls ===

Now you are in a long curved hallway and you can see into a weird room. Enter the South [[Magic Forcefield|teleport]] and you will be in a small room with 4 switches and a [[Magic Forcefield|teleport]]. Flick all 4 switches in the order described in the image below (SE, SW, NE then NW), and all the walls will disappear. Begin healing yourself or cast [[mana shield]], you will take more and more damage from the 12 [[Ominous Pillar]]s that surround you.

[[File:DreamersChallengeSwitches.jpg|420px]]

Enter the [[Magic Forcefield|teleport]] next to you, after all 4 walls are down, as quickly as possible. If you go in [[Magic Forcefield|teleport]] before the walls are all down, you return to the previous [[Magic Forcefield|teleport]]. If you die here, you are not able to recover your body.

It should only take you a few seconds to pull the levers and jump back through the [[Magic Forcefield|teleport]], so you may take up to 700 damage. If you're a low level mage, use manashield.

=== Mission 4 - Tic Tac Toe puzzle ===

[[File:Positions for tibiawiki.JPG|300px|right]]

Begin by walking south west, move the switch north of the white pillars. There is an [[NPC]] south here that is a [[Ghostly Woman]]. She will tell you about the Tic Tac Toe puzzle. She says she is cold and wants [[Boots]]. You do not need to get [[Boots]] though. Go east and enter the room with the [[bed]]s. Open the only [[Chest (Quest)]] in the room to get the [[Book of the Lost Heroes (Book)|Book of the Lost Heroes]]. Walk north to the Tic Tac Toe place. There will be 36 spaces, 6 rows and 6 columns. Each name in the [[Book of the Lost Heroes (Book)|Book of the Lost Heroes]] is 6 letters long and there are six names. Move the switch to make an X and O appear. Any name with an X or O in it, pretend you are writing it on the line. Say the first name was something like—X-O-, you would leave the first two spots blank, the third would have an X, the fourth would be blank, the fifth would be O and the sixth would be blank.

Book of the Lost Heroes:<br/>
<span style="font-family:monospace;">
Askur'''<u>o</u>'''<br/>
Mantar<br/>
Ild'''<u>o</u>'''ni<br/>
Ma'''<u>x</u>'''m'''<u>o</u>'''f<br/>
'''<u>X</u>'''em'''<u>o</u>'''d'''<u>o</u>'''<br/>
'''<u>Ox</u>'''anti</span>

After positioning all of these, run down to the [[Ghost]] NPC's Room. Move the switch and the pillars will return on the north side of that room. Move it again and a [[Magic Forcefield|teleport]] will appear. Go into it. The puzzle here, only needs to be solved by one person, each person can just move the switch to activate the [[Magic Forcefield|teleport]].

=== Mission 5 - Dream room ===

There are 4 tiles in this room.
* The '''north''' one requires a [[Life Ring]]
* The '''south''' one a [[Death Ring]]
Now go to the north-west and move the switch to make the well fill with [[Slime (Liquid)|Slime]] and turn the electricity on. Go back to the four tiles and:
* Pour your [[Water (Liquid)|water]] on the '''west''' tile
* Shoot a [[Fire Field|fire field]] on the '''east''' tile
A [[Magic Forcefield|teleport]] will appear in the center.<br />
You may be [[Protection Zone|PZ]] Locked. Wait til its gone and run in.

[[File:Dreamers11.JPG]]

Now you see a room with a [[bed]] and [[Magic Forcefield|teleporters]] around it. ''(Right image)''

[[File:Dreamers5.JPG|300px|right]] 

Step into a [[Magic Forcefield|teleporter]], this, according to the tibian history is when you start to [[dreamwalk]]. The paths you will find throughout the way are places who are called Dreampaths to the [[Dream Realm]]. As you are asleep, the monsters in there are just decorative and won't do you any harm. Note, though, that unless you are wearing [[Diving Equipment]], it is possible to drown if you stand still for too long when passing through the [[Underwater]] part [COORD x=32976 y=32271 z=14 | here].

Just walk around and you will jump through all of them, like your dream is wandering. Finally, you will end up in a room with an [[NPC]] [[A Ghostly Guardian]]. You will now receive the achievement [[Lucid Dreamer]]. There will be 4 boxes in this room. All boxes contain a [[bag]] with a vial of [[wine]], [[bread]], and a [[certificate]]. Continue north until you face a crossover. Now it's time to choose your fate. You can choose between:
*[[Brotherhood of Bones]] (west teleport) - speak to NPC [[The Bone Master]]
*[[Nightmare Knights]] (north teleport) - speak to NPC [[The Dream Master]]

You must go through the [[Magic Forcefield|teleport]] and talk to one of the two leaders to join their ranks. Upon accepting their membership, you will now be able to access the first floor of [[Magic Forcefield|teleports]]. You will also receive either the [[achievement]] [[Nightmare Knight]] or [[Bone Brother]] depending on the side you chose.

At the [[Dream Realm]] talk to your leader from the [[Brotherhood of Bones]] '''or''' the [[Nightmare Knights]]; they will have additional missions for you. (Say "join" to NPC) You can leave the [[Dream Realm]] by stepping into the teleporter near [[A Ghostly Guardian]].

Note: now you can use a [[Document of the Follower]] to obtain Nightmare/Brotherhood Outfits. [[File:Outfit_Nightmare_Knights_Male.gif]] [[File:Outfit_Nightmare_Knights_Female.gif]] [[File:Outfit_Brotherhood_of_Bones_Male.gif]] [[File:Outfit_Brotherhood_of_Bones_Female.gif]]

=== Mission 6 - Prove your deeds ===

Whether you joined the [[Nightmare Knights]] or the [[Brotherhood of Bones]] the next missions will be the same, just the reward is different depending on which side you chose.

If you are on the Dream Master's side your rank is '''Novice''', otherwise your rank is '''Maggot'''. Your privileges are the usage of the teleporters of the first floor of the [[Knightwatch Tower]]: ([[Carlin]], [[Thais]], [[Dream Realm]]).

Your mission is to collect 500 [[Demonic Essence]]. They can be obtained easily by hunting [[Nightmare]]s on [[Formorgar Mines]] or in [[Nightmare]]s from [[Yalahar]] in [[Cemetery Quarter]].

Bring them to [[The Dream Master]] if you are of the Nightmare Knights, say '''Demonic Essence''' and ask for an '''advancement''' and you will be promoted to the rank of '''Initiate''', otherwise bring them to [[The Bone Master]], you will be promoted to the rank of '''Hyaena'''. You are able now to use teleporters to [[Ab'Dendriel]], [[Fibula]] and [[Kazordoon]]. You may, also, use a [[Document of the Officer]] to obtain the first addon. [[File:Outfit_Nightmare_Knights_Male_Addon_2.gif]] [[File:Outfit_Nightmare_Knights_Female_Addon_2.gif]] [[File:Outfit_Brotherhood_of_Bones_Male_Addon_1.gif]] [[File:Outfit_Brotherhood_of_Bones_Female_Addon_1.gif]]

=== Mission 7 - Slaying more demons ===

Your mission is to collect 1000 more [[Demonic Essence]]s. They can be obtained easily by hunting [[Nightmare]] in either [[Yalahar]] or the [[Formorgar Mines]].

Bring them to [[The Dream Master]] if you are of the Nightmare Knights, ask for an advancement and you will be promoted to the rank of '''Dreamer''' and be granted a [[Nightmare Shield]], otherwise bring them to [[The Bone Master]], you will be promoted to the rank of '''Death Dealer''' and be granted a [[Necromancer Shield]]. You may also use a [[Document of the Leader]] to obtain the second addon. After using all three outfit scrolls, you will either receive the [[achievement]] [[Nightmare Walker]] or [[Skull and Bones]] depending on the side you chose. '''NOTE: If you gave all 1500 essence on the first mission, don't worry, you can still advance.'''

[[File:Outfit_Nightmare_Knights_Male_Addon_1.gif]] [[File:Outfit_Nightmare_Knights_Female_Addon_1.gif]] [[File:Outfit_Brotherhood_of_Bones_Male_Addon_2.gif]] [[File:Outfit_Brotherhood_of_Bones_Female_Addon_2.gif]]

=== Mission 8 - Slaughtering ===

Your mission is to collect 1500 more [[Demonic Essence]]s. They can be obtained easily by hunting [[Nightmare]]s on [[Formorgar Mines]].

Bring them to [[The Dream Master]] if you are of the Nightmare Knights, ask for an '''advancement''' and you will be promoted to the rank of '''Lord Protector''' and receive the [[achievement]] [[Lord Protector]]. If you are part of the [[Brotherhood of Bones]], bring them to [[The Bone Master]] to be promoted to the rank of '''Dread Lord''' and receive the [[achievement]] [[Dread Lord]]. You are now able to use teleporters to [[Darashia]], [[Ankrahmun]], and [[Mintwallin]]. You are also able to create your own [[Document of the Follower|Documents of the Follower]], [[Document of the Officer|Documents of the Officer]] and [[Document of the Leader|Documents of the Leader]] now.

=== Creating Documents ===

Once you have gained the rank Lord Protector, or Dread Lord, you are able to create your own documents very easily. You just need to go back to the [[Knightwatch Tower]] (through a hole hidden behind a tree [COORD x=32816 y=32322 z=7 | here]). There is a book in the Nightmare Knights officers quarters, behind the [[Gate of Expertise]] in the [[Dream Realm]], which tells you the following:

"''Know this, Lord Protector, yours is the power and privilege to grant the permission to wear an outfit appropriate to someone's status.''
''Put a small diamond on the northern altar and pull the lever. An enchanted scroll will appear. Used by a member of the rank Novice, the document will grant him the right to wear the armor of the Nightmare Knights.''
''Put a small diamond on the western altar and pull the lever. An enchanted scroll will appear. Used by a member of the rank Initiate, the document will grant him the right to wear the headgear of the Nightmare Knights.''
''Put a small diamond on the southern altar and pull the lever. An enchanted scroll will appear. Used by a member of the rank Dreamer, the document will grant him the right to wear the regalia of the Nightmare Knights.''
''Choose carefully who is worthy in your eyes.''"

'''NB:''' You need 1 [[small diamond]] for each document you wish to create.

[[File:Docufollwer1.jpg|250px]]

Place a [[small diamond]] on the counter in front of you.

[[File:Docufollwer2.jpg|250px]]

Pull the lever and the [[small diamond]] will transform into...

[[File:Docufollwer3.jpg|250px]]

A [[Document of the Follower]] (north altar), [[Document of the Officer]] (west altar), or a [[Document of the Leader]] (south altar). All three documents are created in the same way.
