## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Insectoid Invasion World Change}}
==Legend==
Strange insectoids have been spotted west of Greenshore every now and then. If you do not kill enough of them they will eventually eat up leaves and grass leaving behind an insectoid wasteland. Kill enough of these crawly creatures to stop the invasion and let the land turn green again.
==Method==
Go to [[Greenshore]] (north of [[Thais]] ([COORD x=32262 y=32074 z=7 | here]). Enter the village by the ramp ([COORD x=32304 y=32119 z=7 | here]) and head into town. Follow the first path to the west and there is a small bridge. Cross this bridge and the [[Insectoid Scout]]s may be in this general area and also down the hole where 2 [[Rotworm]]s spawn, although they may be able to be lured into the village.
