== Method ==
The dried up river in the [[Zao Steppe]] will run. During this time you will have the chance to catch several [[Sandfish]].

When fishing a [[Sandfish]] you will earn the [[achievement]] [[Desert Fisher]]. It might take some time before you catch one, though. Consider [[Hotkeys|hotkeying]] your [[Fishing Rod]] and eating a [[Northern Fishburger]] to make the [[Fishing]] go quicker.
