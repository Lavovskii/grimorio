## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Overhunting World Change}}

In the forests around [[Ab'Dendriel]] [[White Deer]] roam as far as [[Carlin]] to the west and [[Ulderek's Rock]] to the east. They are hunted by their natural predator, the [[wolf]], as well as poachers who seek their [[White Deer Antlers|antlers]] and [[White Deer Skin|skin]] to trade. The [[Elves]], on the other hand, seek to protect the deer.

If there are White Deer around, you can kill many of them to loot their their antlers and skin. You can sell these to [[Cruleo]] who lives near [[Ferngrims Gate]], [COORD x=32723 y=31788 z=7 | here]. Killing the deer reduces their population and if enough are killed that day, they may not appear the next day (after Server Save).<br />
You can try to [[Kingly Deer|mount]] a White Deer using a [[Golden Fir Cone]].

When the deer are overhunted the wolves will starve and you will find [[Starving Wolf|Starving Wolves]] roaming the east and west sides of [[Femor Hills]]. [[Magic Wolf Trap]]s can be found on the ground around [[Femor Hills]] which the starving wolves can be lured onto in order to [[Captured Wolf|capture]] them. The most useful of these [[Magic Wolf Trap]]s are found east ([COORD x=32619 y=31845 z=7 | here]) and south-west ([COORD x=32515 y=31887 z=7 | here]) of the [[Femor Hills]] [[Magic Carpet]]. When you have caught a [[Captured Wolf]] return it to [[Benevola]], who can be found [COORD x=32596 y=31746 z=7 | here].<br />
Trapping them allows the deer population to grow again by the next day. You can also kill [[Wolf|wolves]], but this is 10 times less effective than trapping them.
