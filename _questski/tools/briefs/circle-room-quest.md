## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=Circle Room Quest}}
==Required Equipment==
* [[Rope]]

==Notes==
* Do not underestimate this quest's difficulty, as the circle room has a fast respawn and [[Dwarf Geomancer]]s, [[Dwarf Guard]]s and [[Dwarf Soldier]]s will come to you from all sides.

==Method==
* Go to [[Kazordoon]], and enter the Dwarf mine located [COORD x=32495 y=31975 z=7 | here].
* You will be in a very small cave with one or two [[Dwarf|Dwarves]]. Go down a level.
* Follow this path to the north-east, and go down the hole:
[[Image:Circle Room Quest Map 01.jpg]]

* Follow this path to the north, and then east, and go down the hole:
[[Image:Circle Room Quest Map 02.jpg]]

* Follow this path to the south, west, and then north. Watch out for groups of [[Dwarf Soldier]]s. Go down the hole:
[[Image:Circle Room Quest Map 03.jpg]]

* This level has many small groups of [[Dwarf Guard]]s. Even though it is a short walk, they come at you from both directions. Go directly south, and go down the hole:
[[Image:Circle Room Quest Map 04.jpg]]

* Follow this path to the south, west, and north through the [[Dwarf Guard]]s. Here you will have to go through the Level 32 [[Door of Expertise]]. Before going down the hole, get ready with your [[rope]], in case you need to make a quick exit. You will be swarmed with [[Dwarf Soldier]]s, [[Dwarf Guard]]s and [[Dwarf Geomancer]]s down this hole:
[[Image:Circle Room Quest Map 05.jpg]]

* This room is where the quest boxes are. Work your way through the [[Dwarves]] to the far south end of the room to get your reward. Be very careful, there are a lot of [[Dwarf Geomancer]]s and [[Dwarf Guard]]s waiting for you! By staying to the eastern side of the room, you will avoid most of the [[Dwarves]] or save mana for the quest since [[Dwarf Geomancer]]s will use mana drain (0-80). Kill the geomancers fast using distance weapons or runes, but be careful when using melee, as they heal quickly and might lure you into a group of dwarves.   
[[Image:Circle Room Quest Map 06.jpg]]
