* [[Shovel]]

* Travel money
* [[Melee]] weapon or [[Firebug]] to break the [[Spiderwebs]]
* Completed first mission of [[The New Frontier Quest]]
* Completed first mission of [[The White Raven Monastery Quest]]
* 

== Mission ==
First of all, go to [[Edron]] and find the NPC [[Tereban]]. He is above the depot, at [[Mirabell]]'s tavern ([COORD x=33168 y=31811 z=6 | here]). Ask him for a '''mission''' and accept it with '''yes'''.

You are asked to find rare components of items. These items are found in dungeons. You can do these missions at any time, in any order, and you don't have to report a completed mission before doing another. You will receive 2500 experience for each item you turn in. When you hand in the last of the items, you can finally receive the [[Old Cape]] as well as 8,000 additional experience and gold.

=== The Magic Bow - Sinew ===
Go to the [[Edron Wyvern Hill]] ([COORD x=33241 y=31767 z=7 | here]) and fight your way to the far east. On the north side there is a [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]]. Go in and up the ramp, kill [[Heoni]] (who is about as strong as a [[Wyvern]]) and '''use''' the dead Heoni in order to obtain [[Strong Sinew]]. You do not have to kill [[Heoni]] yourself, just use the corpse.

{{Item Obtained|Strong Sinew}}

Bring it back to [[Tereban]] by saying '''strong sinew'''.

=== The Magic Bow - Wood ===
Go to [[Svargrond]] and head west out of town, continue west over the mountain, down the stairs and continue west to the raider camp or if you have completed the Barbarian Test travel with [[Buddel]] to the '''raider camps'''. Proceed north across the thin sheet of ice to [[Bittermor]], the northern glacial camp. In this camp, directly north of the entrance, is a [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]] ([COORD x=31987 y=31226 z=7 | here]). Go in it and open the box to receive the [[Exquisite Wood]].

{{Item Obtained|Exquisite Wood}}

Bring it back to [[Tereban]] by saying '''exquisite wood'''.

=== The Magic Robe - Cloth ===
Go to the [[Isle of the Kings]] and descend to the 6th floor of the catacombs. Go north-west until the end and use a [[Pick]] in the shadow of a [[Stone Pillar]] ([COORD x=32162 y=31921 z=13 | here]). Go down the hole to the 7th floor and go west until you find the [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]]. Go in it and open the chest for the [[Spectral Cloth]].

{{Item Obtained|Spectral Cloth}}

Bring it back to [[Tereban]] by saying '''spectral cloth'''.

=== The Magic Robe - Silk ===
Go to the [[Zao Wailing Widow Cave]], the entrance to which can be found north-east of the central orc camp ([COORD x=33222 y=31398 z=7 | here]). From the first floor ramp, go south-west and open the [[Spider Webs]] with your melee weapon or [[Firebug]]. Go through the web opening and down the ramp at the end of the passage. Now go south-west and follow the cave south, then south-east before it turns north-east. You will need to open another set of [[Spider Webs]] with your weapon or firebug before continuing north-east in search of a ramp along the southern wall of the cave. You are now at the final floor. Proceed west and go through the [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]] to a room with 4 [[Wailing Widow]]s. Kill them and '''use''' the [[Spider Victim]] in the middle of the room to get the [[Exquisite Silk]].

{{Item Obtained|Exquisite Silk}}

Bring it back to [[Tereban]] by saying '''exquisite silk'''.

=== The Magic Rod - Crystal ===
Go to [[Ankrahmun]] and exit the city from the north-eastern ramp. Follow the city's walls south to the south-eastern part of [[Darama]]. Dig for the [[Shadow Tomb]] ([COORD x=33255 y=32833 z=7 | here]), go in and down to the 5th floor. Go to the northern part of this floor ([COORD x=33235 y=32808 z=12 | here]) and find a [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]]. Go in and '''use''' the sarcophagus at the northern side of the room to obtain the [[Magic Crystal]].

{{Item Obtained|Magic Crystal}}

Bring it back to [[Tereban]] by saying '''magic crystal'''.

=== The Magic Rod - Root ===
Go to [[Port Hope]] and head to [[Banuta]]. If you have a high enough [[Paw and Fur Society]] rank you can travel directly to Banuta via [[Lorek]]'s ferry. When you reach Banuta, go down the southern-most staircase ([COORD x=32826 y=32576 z=7 | here]). You will face a lot of [[Apes]] here. Go north all the way (to [COORD x=32815 y=32505 z=8 | here]) and through the [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]]. Open the box to obtain the [[Mystic Root]].

{{Item Obtained|Mystic Root}}

Bring it back to [[Tereban]] by saying '''mystic root'''.

=== The Magic Shield - Iron ===
Go to [[Kazordoon]] and find the [[Steamship]]. You can travel directly from [[Cormaya]] or [[Farmine]], walk through the city's mine or take the [[Ore Wagon]]s. When you reach the steamship, go up from the steamship twice to reach the main floor with [[Dwarf|Dwarves]] and [[Dwarf Soldier]]s. Go south-west and down the hole to a single [[Dwarf Guard]]. Kill the guard and open the [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]] to the west of the room. Open the box to find the [[Old Iron]].

{{Item Obtained|Old Iron}}

Bring it back to [[Tereban]] by saying '''old iron'''.

=== The Magic Shield - Scale ===
Go to [[Thais]] and follow the dirt path north to [[Greenshore]]. When you cross the bridge to Greenshore, search for a hidden pitfall behind a tree a few steps north of the ramps ([COORD x=32300 y=32097 z=7 | here]). Down there are [[Orc]]s and [[Orc Spearman|Orc Spearmen]]. Kill them and go down again, but be mindful of the traps on the floor which may damage you. Follow the cave south and kill the various [[Orcs]] before going down again. Go south a bit and then west all the way to reach the Greenshore [[Dragon Lair]]. Go up and kill the [[Dragon]] who is accompanied by a [[Dragon Hatchling]]. Find a thin corridor on the western part of the lair which leads to a [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]]. Go in and kill [[Glitterscale]], who has about the same strength of a [[Dragon]]. When slain, '''use''' the [[Dead Glitterscale]] to obtain the [[Flexible Dragon Scale]].

{{Item Obtained|Flexible Dragon Scale}}

Bring it back to [[Tereban]] by saying '''flexible dragon scale'''.

=== Accepting the Mission ===
{{Infobox Transcript|
Player: '''hi'''<br />
Tereban: Greetings, friend.<br />
Player: '''mission'''<br />
Tereban: I have four sons which are very dear to me. Though they were born on the same day and even in the same hour, they took quite different paths in life. ...<br />
Tereban: Each of them chose a different vocation, one will become a knight, one a sorcerer, one a druid, and the other a paladin. In a few weeks they will reach adulthood and I am holding a birthday party for them. ...<br />
Tereban: It should become a day to remember and so I want to give them something special as a present. I searched the land for the finest craftsmen so they could create suitable presents for my sons. ...<br />
Tereban: But something of fine craftsmanship will just not cut it. So I asked them what they would need to create something special. They all came up with lists of rare and expensive items necessary for the task ahead. ...<br />
Tereban: I spent a small fortune to buy most of the materials but in the end the key components are that rare that they cannot be simply bought somewhere. ...<br />
Tereban: As far as I understood it, the places where you can get these items are quite dangerous and so it would take some adventurer to get them. ...<br />
Tereban: That would be your mission if you are interested. Uhm, so are you interested?<br />
Player: '''yes'''<br />
Tereban: I am relieved someone as capable as you will handle the task. Well, I need the parts of a sorcerer's robe, a paladin's bow, a knight's shield, and a druid's wand.<br />
Player: '''bye'''<br />
Tereban: Farewell.<br />
