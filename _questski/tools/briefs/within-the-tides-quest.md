== Requirements ==
* Completed [[The Lost Brother Quest]]
* {{ilink|Helmet of the Deep}} [[Helmet of the Deep]] or {{ilink|Depth Galea}} [[Depth Galea]]
* [[#Mountainous Matters|Mountainous Matters]] mission
** {{ilink|Hammer}} [[Hammer]]
** Some {{ilink|Ice Cube}} [[Ice Cube]]s
** One {{ilink|Honeycomb}} [[Honeycomb]]
** A {{ilink|Mango}} [[Mango]] and a {{ilink|Juice Squeezer}} [[Juice Squeezer]]

== Method ==

=== Of Feathers and Flowers ===
To begin, head to the northeast area of [[Tiquanda]] around [COORD x=32976 y=32715 z=7 | here] and speak with [[Tarun]]. Ask him about the {{KW|Asuri}}, and he will explain that these vile demons sometimes visit a faraway place. Tarun, curious but unwilling to investigate himself, will ask for your assistance.

==== Mission Objectives ====
After accepting Tarun's mission, your goal is to explore the palace and obtain three feathers by defeating the following minibosses:

* [[The Flaming Orchid]] {{Ilink|The Flaming Orchid}} drops the {{Ilink|Flamingo Feather}} [[Flamingo Feather]].
* [[The Moonlight Aster]] {{Ilink|The Moonlight Aster}} drops the {{Ilink|Peacock Feather}} [[Peacock Feather]].
* [[The Winter Bloom]] {{Ilink|The Winter Bloom}} drops the {{Ilink|Swan Feather (Asura)}} [[Swan Feather (Asura)|Swan Feather]].

In order to acquire them, you need to defeat three Asuri minibosses: [[The Flaming Orchid]], [[The Moonlight Aster]], and [[The Winter Bloom]]. Note that you don't have to kill or loot the items if someone can lend you the feathers; having one of each feather is sufficient for any number of players. You may bypass looting the [[Flamingo Feather]] and [[Peacock Feather]] by purchasing these items on the marketplace. <u>However, the swan feather available on the marketplace is not the correct one needed for the "Golden Hand Location." Therefore, you must defeat the [[The Winter Bloom]] boss to loot the proper [[Swan Feather (Asura)|Swan Feather]].</u>

All three minibosses are located within rooms behind sealed doors. To access them, you need to eliminate specific non-Asuri creatures in the palace. The requirements are:

* [[The Flaming Orchid]]: 2x [[Greater Fire Elemental]]s ([COORD x=32960 y=32676 z=4 | here]) and 3x [[Cave Hydra]]s ([COORD x=32940 y=32679 z=8 | here]).
* [[The Moonlight Aster]]: 2x [[Vicious Lich]]es ([COORD x=32923 y=32680 z=8 | here]) and 2x [[Vile Destroyer]]s ([COORD x=32936 y=32687 z=5 | here]).
* [[The Winter Bloom]]: 2x [[Greater Energy Elemental]]s ([COORD x=32961 y=32673 z=7 | here]) and 1x [[Solitary Frost Dragon]] ([COORD x=32943 y=32664 z=8 | here]).

Once you're done with these and have all feathers in hand, go [COORD x=32956 y=32674 z=3 | upstairs] and find an [[Ornate Case]]. Use it to get a [[Parchment]] then go downstairs towards [[Gilmak Copperbeard]] and ask him about the {{KW|nagas}}. He'll tell you where to find three treasure chambers to build an artefact that grants you access to a portal high in the [[Kha'zeel Mountains]].

The three parts of the artefacts are in the following locations:

NOTE - You will need to have the 3 feathers in your backpack inventory in order for the locations to appear and be opened on the floor.
* {{ilink|Silver Hand}} [[Silver Hand]]: [[Ankrahmun]] ([COORD x=33279 y=32785 z=7 | Here])
* {{ilink|Golden Hand}} [[Golden Hand]]: [[Folda]] ([COORD x=32082 y=31528 z=7 | Here])
* {{ilink|Bronze Hand}} [[Bronze Hand]]: [[Edron]] ([COORD x=33266 y=31768 z=10 | Here]) (Must first get the handwritten message from [COORD x=33270 y=31734 z=10 | here])

Once you have the parts, simply use the items on each other until you have a completed {{ilink|Asuri Talisman}} [[Asuri Talisman]]. Finally, head to [[Kha'zeel Mountains]] between Tiquanda and Ankrahmun to step in the portal ([COORD x=33037 y=32738 z=2 | here]) where you'll then be teleported to [[Marapur]].

=== Coastal Concerns ===

The Nagas at the coast may be in need of some help, but they'll only help you if they know you have good will. 

==== Cure for the Beavers ====

Once in Marapur, head west from the Silvertides depot and talk to [[Dhira]]. Ask him for a {{KW|mission}} and he will ask for your help to cure many giant beavers that help the island by harvesting and transportation. Accept the task and he'll ask you to gather a {{ilink|Moon Mint}} [[Moon Mint]] and a {{ilink|Coral Branch}} [[Coral Branch]].

* {{Ilink|Moon Mint}} [[Moon Mint]] can be harvested from {{ilink|Moon Mint (Immobile)}} [[Moon Mint (Immobile)|Moon Mint]] plants in the Fading Isles. The nearest one is [COORD x=33632 y=32801 z=7 | here].
* {{Ilink|Coral Branch}} [[Coral Branch]] can be looted from [[Foam Stalker]] in the [[Great Pearl Fan Reef]], [COORD x=33743 y=32779 z=7 | down here].

With both items in hand, walk to a building just south of the Silvertides depot, [COORD x=33771 y=32855 z=7 | here]. In the laboratory, use your [[Coral Branch]] in the {{Ilink|Mortar}} [[Mortar]] located on a table west in the room and you'll obtain {{ilink|Coral Powder}} [[Coral Powder]]. Then go east and use your [[Moon Mint]] and [[Coral Powder]] in the {{Ilink|Refiner of Magic}} [[Refiner of Magic]]. If done correctly you'll obtain a {{ilink|Cure for Sick Beavers}} [[Cure for Sick Beavers]].

Report back to [[Dhira]] then head just north from him to talk to [[Nilavarna]].

==== Hunt Foam Stalkers ====

Ask [[Nilavarna]] for a {{KW|mission}} and he'll ask you to kill a total of 300 [[Foam Stalker]]s and [[Two-headed Turtle]]s. Walk to the west island of the Stardance Mountains with a [[Helmet of the Deep]] or a [[Depth Galea]], then jump in the vortex to [[Great Pearl Fan Reef]] once again.

With the 300 kills achieved, report it back to [[Nilavarna]] and he'll give you a new task to help [[Timira]] as she suddenly became irascible and aggressive. Go to the northeast subarea of Emerald Gardens to find a portal to the boss fight.

==== Timira the Many-Headed ====

[[File:Timira the Many Headed - Within the Tides Quest|right|350px]]
Go to the small portal located in a house at the Emerald Gardens, [COORD x=33803 y=32701 z=7 | here]. This fight can be fought by up to 5 players, and you have 10 minutes to defeat Timira. 

<small>'''Stage 1 - Sparkles'''</small>

On the first stage, you have to use the special water to heal the Shattered Water in the room. Pick up an Empty Bucket from the floor and wait until the water starts to glimmer. When it does, use the bucket on the water to fill it. Make sure you're next to the water or the bucket won't be filled. You should get a [[Bucket Full of Sparkles]] {{Ilink|Bucket Full of Sparkles}}. Then, use the bucket on the Shattered Water on the eastern wall of the room.

While doing this, you'll constantly have to deal with [[Corrupt Naga]]s and [[Rogue Naga]]s, which quickly respawn in the room. You can try to block them or run around them or simply kill them while doing the mechanic. 

If you get a [[Bucket Full of Water]] {{Ilink|Bucket Full of Water}} by mistake, just empty the water on the floor so you can reuse your bucket. You have to use four buckets for each player in the room, but it doesn't matter who uses them. Once you're done, an energy effect will appear across the room. Then, click on the Trapped Water to use its energy to open the portal to the next stage. Enter the portal to the '''south'''. Make sure to open the Ornamented Chest near the portal for an extra reward!

<small>'''Stage 2 - Corrupted Water'''</small>

Similar to the previous stage, you have to use the trapped water to open the portal. This time, however, all you have to do is use the Corrupted Trapped Water until its cleansed and becomes Trapped Water, which you can use to open the portal. You can only use the corrupted water once every 30 seconds, so you'll have to deal with the Nagas while you wait.

Be careful, you lose health and mana when you successfully use the Corrupted Trapped Water.

Once the portal is open, make sure to open the chest to the north and move to the final room.

<small>'''Stage 3 - Timira'''</small>

Now you'll finally face [[Timira the Many-Headed]]! There are no mechanics here, and all you have to do is kill her. You can trap the boss in a corner north-west of the room to make the fight easier.

After killing the boss talk about your mission to [[Nilavarna]]. He will reward you with [[Colourful Water Lily]].

=== Mountainous Matters ===

The Mountain Nagas also have some problems they need help with.

==== Fix the Observatory ====

Go southeast from the Moonfall subarea's depot and upstairs in a big building until you find [[Udu]] at the last floor ([COORD x=33849 y=32788 z=2 | here]). Ask her for a {{KW|mission}} and she'll tell you she needs help with her observatory as something is blocking the water-powered mechanism downstairs in the same building.

Go downstairs until you find a water wheel. In this room, walk north to use the lever to slow it down, then use a {{Ilink|Hammer}} [[Hammer]] on the water wheel to fix it and get a quest log update.

Report back to Udu, and continuing with the mission she'll ask you to find an important piece of her telescope, the {{ilink|Glass Lens}} [[Glass Lens]], which was stolen by a moa and hidden in a nest. To find the nest, you have to go to the west part of Stardance Mountains, close to where prince [[Nihaka]] is, and use a [[Jungle Moa Nest]] located a bit south from there ([COORD x=33717 y=32777 z=2 | here]).

Bring the lens back to [[Udu]], and as a part of the mission, she'll give you a [[Celestial Chart]] {{Ilink|Celestial Chart}}. She'll ask for your help to find three constellations on her celestial chart: The Turtle, The Southern Crab, and The Smaller Rose. In order to chart these constellations, you'll have to visit the specific sites on the island and "use" your [[Celestial Chart]], as described below:

==== Stolen Naga Eggs ====

'''Note''': To finish this mission you must have helped Udu, but also completed the [[#Star-Crossed Lovers|Star-Crossed Lovers]] mission below. You should now jump to that part of the quest and return here once you have finished it. 

Find [[Saraki]] ([COORD x=33722 y=32765 z=4 | here]) for a new {{KW|mission}}. She will tell you the Asuri have stolen some naga eggs and ask you to talk to [[Kaumudi]], the hatch-mother who is in charge of the eggs.

Kaumudi doesn't have much information to assist you. She asks you then to talk to shaman [[Vrisaki]], so you can go straight to him.

Vrisaki is located on top of a mountain ([COORD x=33780 y=32735 z=2 | here]). Meet him and ask about the {{KW|naga eggs}}. He will ask you three items to protect you from the Asuri on your search for the eggs:

* {{Ilink|Parder Fur}} [[Parder Fur]], loot from [[Parder]]s.
* {{Ilink|Canary Feather}} [[Canary Feather]]. Go to Candra and pick the feather from the bird cage near her. (You must have completed the ''Star-Crossed Lovers'' task for that.)
* An Ice flower carved from {{Ilink|Ice Cube}} [[Ice Cube]]. You can use the [[Icicle Chisel]] {{Ilink|Icicle Chisel}} you'll receive to carve the Ice Cubes. You need either [[Pretty Ice Flower|Pretty]] {{Ilink|Pretty Ice Flower}}, [[Beautiful Ice Flowers|Beautiful]] {{Ilink|Beautiful Ice Flowers}} or [[Marvelous Ice Flowers]] {{Ilink|Marvelous Ice Flowers}}. 

With all 3 items go back to Vrisaki and use them on the [[Stony Face]] near him.

==== Secret Room ====

Talk to Vrisaki about {{KW|naga eggs}} once more, he'll tell you about a vision and stolen eggs in a secret room deep down in the sunken temple of the Moon Goddess.

Bring the [[Gemmed Lotus Brooch]] {{Ilink|Gemmed Lotus Brooch}} (from the [[Kusuma]]'s room) and go to the second floor of the sunken temple. Use the brooch on wall [COORD x=33627 y=32776 z=9 | here] and then use the wall to enter the room.

Use the big stone located north in the room and you'll find a hidden [[Bag with Naga Eggs]] {{Ilink|Bag with Naga Eggs}} there.

Bring the eggs back to Kaumudi and tell her about your {{KW|mission}}. You'll receive a [[Naga Nest]] {{Ilink|Naga Nest}} as a reward.

Return to Saraki at the mountain to report your {{KW|mission}} success. She will ask you for help with [[Timira the Many-Headed]]. If you have already defeated her during the [[#Coastal Concerns|Coastal Concerns]] mission then you don't have to do it again. Just say {{KW|mission}} once more to complete the quest.

=== Star-Crossed Lovers ===

'''Note''': You should only start this mission after having finished ''Coastal Concerns'' and the Observatory part of ''Mountainous Matters'', otherwise you won't be able to progress.

Go up the stairs twice in the coastal palace to find [[Candra]], [COORD x=33729 y=32828 z=4 | here]. To help Candra and [[Nihaka]] get married you need to go '''talk with Candra first''' and ask for a {{KW|mission}}. She says to talk with shaman [[Tarisu]]. He can be found on the temple south-east of Silvertides, [COORD x=33797 y=32858 z=6 | here].

==== Talk with the Naga Shamans ====

Tarisu believes the people will benefit from this marriage. If you haven't started the ''Coastal Concerns'' mission yet, he'll ask you to first Cure the beavers. He also tells you to talk with [[Vrisaki]], the mountain nagas' shaman. 

Vrisaki can be found at Moonfall's temple, just north of the depot up 3 sets of ramps, [COORD x=33779 y=32734 z=2 | here]. He wants you to help [[Udu]] first, which means you also need to have started the ''Mountainous Matters'' mission. 

After having helped both Udu and Dhira on the other missions, you can talk to [[Vrisaki]] and [[Tarisu]] once again. Depending on the order you did the missions, you may have to ask each NPC about {{KW|mission}} twice.

==== The Four Good Omens ====

Each of the Shamans asked you for two good omens to prove that this marriage is not ill-fated.

Tarisu wants a vial of holy water from the pond on our cemetery island and a moonshine pearl. Vrisaki wants  a coral vow flower and a damselfly.

* {{Ilink|Blessed Water}} [[Blessed Water]]

Go up the ramp on the eastern side of the eastern ramp, [COORD x=33781 y=32722 z=7 | here]. Follow the path south-east until you find a {{Ilink|Pile of Dry Wood}} [[Pile of Dry Wood]]. Use it to get a {{Ilink|Splint}} [[Splint]].

Then go back north and turn left to the other path of this passage, go south until you find a fireplace. Use the splint on it to make a {{Ilink|Burning Splint}} [[Burning Splint]]. 

Finally, go to a small island all the way north-east of Marapur, [COORD x=33792 y=32674 z=7 | here], where you'll find a frozen pond. Using the Burning Splint on it to melt the water, and then use the water to receive a vial with {{Ilink|Blessed Water}} [[Blessed Water]].

* {{Ilink|Moonshine Pearl}} [[Moonshine Pearl]]

To get this pearl, go to the eastern side of Marapur and down a vortex [COORD x=33883 y=32740 z=7 | here]. You'll need to wear a diving helmet. Use the {{Ilink|Large Seashell (Moonshine)}} [[Large Seashell (Moonshine)|Large Seashell]] to get the pearl.

'''Note''': If you're doing this mission with more players you'll have to wait 1 minute for the pearl to respawn for the other players.<br/>
'''Note²''': Initially Tarisu will tell you the pearl is at the [[Temple of the Moon Goddess]], where you'll find a [[Large Seashell (Shimmering)]] and a [[Shimmering Pearl]]. This is not the real pearl, however, but you can only take it during this mission of the quest. If you wish to have it, you should take it before the real pearl. 

* {{Ilink|Butterfly Conservation Kit (Damselfly)}} [[Butterfly Conservation Kit (Damselfly)|Living Damselfly]]

Go to [[Nihaka]] at the Mountain, [COORD x=33721 y=32770 z=3 | here], and ask her about {{KW|damselfly}} to get an empty {{Ilink|Butterfly Conservation Kit (Empty)}} [[Butterfly Conservation Kit (Empty)|Butterfly Conservation Kit]]. 

Go to the Big Naga Statue between Silvertides and Moonfall, [COORD x=33703 y=32809 z=5 | here]. You'll be informed that someone has killed all the damselflies. <br/>
{{Server Message|Someone has killed all the damselflies! Just some legs and eyes and broken wings are scattered here.}}<br/>
Report back to Nihaka and he'll suggest luring more damselflies with honeyed mango juice. Use a [[Juice Squeezer]] {{Ilink|Juice Squeezer}} on a [[Mango]] {{Ilink|Mango}} to get a [[Flask of Mango Juice]] {{Ilink|Flask of Mango Juice}}. Then use the flask on a [[Honeycomb]] {{Ilink|Honeycomb}} to make a [[Flask of Mango Honey Juice]] {{Ilink|Flask of Mango Honey Juice}}.

Use the Honey Juice on the water of the pond to spawn some [[Emerald Damselfly|Emerald Damselflies]], and use the Butterfly Kit on a {{KW|living}} Damselfy to catch it. You should end with a {{Ilink|Butterfly Conservation Kit (Damselfly)}} [[Butterfly Conservation Kit (Damselfly)|Butterfly Conservation Kit]] containing a Damselfly.

* {{Ilink|Coral Vow Flower}} [[Coral Vow Flower]]

You'll need [[Foam Stalker Spume]] {{Ilink|Foam Stalker Spume}}, which is a guaranteed drop from the [[Venerable Foam Stalker]], a special Foam Stalker that can be found [COORD x=33762 y=32778 z=9 | here].

With the spume, go to the mountain at the Murmuring Wilderness, [COORD x=33738 y=32682 z=5 | here]. Use the spume on the ground next to a Burnt Coral Vow Flower and pick the [[Coral Vow Flower]] {{Ilink|Coral Vow Flower}}. 

Return to Candra and give her the Pearl and the Blessed Water.

Go to Nihaka and give him the Damselfy and the Flower.

==== Kusuma ====

The final task is to defeat [[Kusuma]], an Asura that was disguising herself as a Naga. You can find her on the western mountain, [COORD x=33716 y=32767 z=5 | here]. You can enter the room by stepping on the Energy Vein symbol near the wall.

After defeating her, open the chest and take the [[Gemmed Lotus Brooch]] {{Ilink|Gemmed Lotus Brooch}} from it.

After that you can report back to Nihaka or Candra, you'll receive a [[Lilypad Backpack]] and the [[Wedding Planner]] achievement.
