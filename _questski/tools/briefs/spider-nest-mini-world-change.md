== Method ==
West of [[Venore]] some [[Poison Spider]]s run around and inside a small nest ([COORD x=32750 y=32105 z=7 | here]) you can fight [[Mamma Longlegs]]. Kill her three times to obtain the [[achievement]] [[Nestling]]. After having done so, you will not be able to enter the area any more.
