## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Spring into Life}}

'''Spring into Life''' replaced the Easter event but all Easter events occur in the Spring into Life event: during the event, [[Coloured Egg]]s  are dropped by [[Rabbit]]s and bought from some [[Food Shopkeeper NPCs]]. 

== Dragon nests ==
For the duration of the event, [[Dragon Hatchling]]s provide 25% more experience.

In many [[Dragon Lairs]] throughout Tibia, dragon nests will appear. Over time, these will hatch [[Dragon Hatchling]]s. They do not restore until the next [[Server Save]].

== Nest hunt ==
[[Hidden Nest]]s can be found scattered through Tibia. Find them to obtain [[Surprise Nest]]s, which can be used in order to get different rewards.
