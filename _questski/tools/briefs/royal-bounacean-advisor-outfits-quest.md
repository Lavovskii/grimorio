## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Royal Bounacean Advisor Outfits Quest}}

== Base Outfits ==

Finish the [[Adventures of Galthen Quest]] by defeating [[Megasylvan Yselda]] and report to [[Kesar the Younger]]. 

== First Addon ==

[[File:Outfit Royal Bounacean Advisor Male Addon 1.gif]][[File:Outfit Royal Bounacean Advisor Female Addon 1.gif]]

Defeat [[Megasylvan Yselda]] while keeping her Saplings alive (see [[Adventures of Galthen Quest]]) and talk to [[Kesar the Younger]]. Talk to [[John (Bounac)]] in his [COORD x=32442 y=32488 z=3 | house], at night, about '''Yselda''' to receive the addon.

== Second Addon == 

[[File:Outfit Royal Bounacean Advisor Male Addon 2.gif]][[File:Outfit Royal Bounacean Advisor Female Addon 2.gif]]

Complete [[The Order of the Stag Quest]]

Upon getting both addons you will get the achievement [[King's Council]].
