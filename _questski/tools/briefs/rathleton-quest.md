## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Rathleton Quest}}

==Method==

Do tasks in the [[Oramond Quest]] to obtain voting points. To spend them, go visit [[Marvin]] in the [[Rathleton Magistrate]] and talk to him about '''voting''' and then '''topic'''.

The cast votes are tallied on every other Monday, starting with the first Monday after [[Tibiadrome]] resets, during the [[Server Save]] and displayed on [[The Voting Results]] board.

===No Rank===
At this point, you will only be able to vote for the location of a '''shortcut''' leading back into the city of [[Rathleton]]: do you want it to be located in the [[Minotaur Hills]] (''minotaurs''), in the [[Lower Drainage Area]] (''fungi''), or in the [[Abandoned Sewers]] (''catacombs'')? You decide. The shortcut with the most votes will be established and can then be used. Every 14 days, the votes will be reset and the option which was the most popular at that time will take over.

After casting 50 successful votes, you will be able to ask for a '''promotion'''.

===Commoner Rank===
After your promotion to '''Commoner''' you will earn the [[achievement]] [[Rathleton Commoner]] and you will gain voting rights on one more topic: the so-called '''wish list'''. Do you want the nearby [[Oramond Dungeon|dungeon]] to be filled with [[Worker Golem]]s and [[War Golem]]s (''golems''), [[Oramond Minotaurs]] (''minotaurs'') or a combination of [[Destroyer]]s, [[Hellspawn]]s, [[Dark Torturer]]s, [[Grim Reaper]]s, [[Demon]]s, and [[Juggernaut]]s (''the wrath of evil'')? You decide. The [[creatures]] with the most votes will fill the dungeon. <span style="font-size:14.4444446563721px;">Every 14 days, the votes will be reset and the option which was the most popular at that time will take over.</span>

<span style="font-size:14.4444446563721px;">You will also be able to sell </span>[[Oramond]]<span style="font-size:14.4444446563721px;">-specific </span>[[Creature Products]]<span style="font-size:14.4444446563721px;"> to </span>[[Alaistar]].

After casting another 100 successful votes (150 in total), you will be able to ask for another '''promotion'''.

===Inhabitant Rank===

After your promotion to '''Inhabitant''', you will earn the [[achievement]] [[Rathleton Inhabitant]] and you will gain voting rights on another topic, namely which '''boss''' will be available to kill. Do you want to kill the furious moohtant [[Bullwark]], the poisonous [[Lisa]] or the mighty [[Glooth Fairy]]? You decide. The [[boss]] with the most votes will spawn. <span style="font-size:14.4444446563721px;">Every 14 days, the votes will be reset and the option which was the most popular at that time will take over.</span> 

You can now also sell weapons and armor looted from local [[creatures]] to [[Flint]].

After casting another 150 successful votes (300 in total), you will be able to ask for your third '''promotion'''.

===Citizen Rank===

After your promotion to '''Citizen''', you will earn the [[achievement]] [[Rathleton Citizen]]. As of yet, no new topics to vote on have been uncovered. However, you will still be able to do tasks in the [[Oramond Quest]] and cast votes. 

Rewards for becoming a citizen:

* When you use the [[Slime Slide]] at the boat you will end up in [[Rathleton]] without having to go past the [[Minotaurs]]. Also you may use the slide back to the boat from Rathleton.
* Ability to travel to [[Edron]], [[Venore]], [[Port Hope]], [[Krailos]], [[Issavi]] or [[Travora]] from Oramond
* Access to [[Underground Glooth Factory]].

After casting another 200 successful votes (500 in total), you will be able to ask for your final '''promotion'''.

===Squire Rank===

After your promotion to '''Squire''', you will earn the [[achievement]] [[Rathleton Squire]]. Now you will be able to start a new quest for the magistrate, namely the [[Hero of Rathleton Quest]].

== Recommendation ==
In order to get the full [[Glooth Engineer Outfits]] later it is recommended to vote at least once for each '''shortcut''', '''dungeon''' and '''boss''' [[Marvin]] is telling you. You don't need to vote for the raids. For the base outfit you need to complete [[Dark Trails Quest]]. Note: Voting topics depend on your rank, as described above.

In total, '''9 voting rights''' are needed for the Glooth Engineer Outfit addons, i.e to vote at least once for each shortcut, dungeon and boss.

=== Shortcut Votings ===
Simply say to [[Marvin]]

hi - voting - topic - shortcut - minotaur (or) fungi (or) catacomb  - 1 - yes

=== Dungeon Votings ===
Simply say to [[Marvin]]

hi - voting - topic - dungeon - golem (or) minotaur (or) wrath - 1 - yes

=== Boss Votings ===
Simply say to [[Marvin]]

hi - voting - topic - boss - Bullwark (or) Lisa (or) Glooth Fairy - 1 - yes

=== Raid Votings ===
Simply say to [[Marvin]]

hi - voting - topic - raid - minotaurs (or) mechanical creature (or) wild life - 1 - yes
