## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Citizen of Issavi Outfits Quest}}

== Base Outfits ==

Finish the Wanted mission of the [[Kilmaresh Quest]]. 

== First Addon ==

Use an [[Eye-Embroidered Veil]] {{Ilink|Eye-Embroidered Veil}}.

{{Server Message|You are now allowed to wear an eye-embroidered veil together with your robes of an Issavi citizen!}}

== Second Addon == 

Use a [[Tagralt-Inlaid Scabbard]] {{Ilink|Tagralt-Inlaid Scabbard}}.

{{Server Message|You are now allowed to wear a tagralt blade veil together with your robes of an Issavi citizen!}}

Upon getting both addons you will get the achievement [[Citizen of Issavi]].
