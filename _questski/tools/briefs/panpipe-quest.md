## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Panpipe Quest}}

==Required Equipment==
* [[Rope]], [[Elvenhair Rope]] or [[Magic Rope]] spell
* [[Shovel]] or [[Light Shovel]]
* [[Key 4055]]. You get this here close to the entrance of the Desert Dungeon. "Use" the hollow rock ([COORD x=32652 y=32108 z=7 | here]).

==Method==

'''Note''': before embarking upon this quest, make sure you know how to reach the exit portal for the [[Vocation Quest]]. There is no other safe way out of the [[Desert Dungeon]] (you cannot get out the way you got in).

* Go to the [[Desert Dungeon]] ([COORD x=32648 y=32094 z=7 | here]). Don't go in without [[Key 4055]]!
* To get the key, "use" the [[Stone (Long)|hollow stone]] ([COORD x=32652 y=32108 z=7 | here]). It's very close to the entrance to the dungeon.
* Go back to the [[Desert Dungeon]] entrance, use your shovel, and go inside.
* Take a [[Ladder]] down 5 times, to the bottom, you will face [[Spider]]s.
* Follow this path around to the west and south, possible meeting one [[Bonelord]], and go up a level:
[[File:Panpipe Quest Map 01.jpg]]
* Next, follow this path north-west (meeting a few [[Spider]]s again) past the [[Desert Dungeon Library]], and rope up the hole:
[[File:Panpipe Quest Map 02.jpg]]
* Rope up another level.
* On this level, follow this path south, and go up one more level:
[[File:Panpipe Quest Map 03.jpg]]
* Finally, follow this path to the south-east to the locked door, one [[Fire Devil]], and the quest box containing a [[Bag]] with 1 [[Power Ring]], [[Panpipes]] and 2 [[Small Amethyst]]s:
[[File:Panpipe Quest Map 04.jpg]]
* To get out, you need to go back to the entrance, and find your way to the [[Vocation Quest]] exit portal.
