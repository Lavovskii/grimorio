== Requirements ==
* 100x {{Ilink|Demonic Core Essence}} [[Demonic Core Essence]]s;
* Some {{Ilink|Mummified Demon Finger}} [[Mummified Demon Finger]]s ''(required amount is luck dependent)'';
* 1x {{Ilink|Fishing Rod}} [[Fishing Rod]].

:<small>'''Note:''' The quest progress is influenced by a world change, and the subterranean levels of [[Azzilon Castle|Azzilon]] are be blocked until enough {{ilink|Demonic Core Essence}} [[Demonic Core Essence]]s have been handed over to [[Lai]] by players. The exact amount of core essences is unknown.
:Once enough core essences are handed over, a server-wise message is reported to players, and the mist blocking the stairs fades away ({{Message|type=server|The mists in Azzilon have retreated!}})</small>

== To boldly go... ==
Meet NPC [[Fernlor]] in [[Edron]], at the [[Noodles Academy of Modern Magic]] ([COORD x=33281 y=31848 z=8 | here]). Talk to him and he'll tell you he needs your help to investigate an anomaly that he discovered. Ask him what is the {{KW|mission}} and he'll tell you he found a disturbance at some remote, uncharted isle to the east and that he set a teleport to such isle.

Enter the orange teleport and you'll be teleported to the [[Azzilon Castle]]. Go down the stairs and up another set to enter the castle, and keep moving north until you reach the inner southeast tower ([COORD x=33843 y=32360 z=6 | here]). Go up the tower and meet [[Lai]] at the center of floor +3.

Ask her for a {{KW|mission}} and she'll tell you need to collect {{Ilink|Demonic Core Essence}} [[Demonic Core Essence]]s which she'll use to drive away the lethal mist in the catacombs and even provide you with some sort of protection from its lingering effects.

== Evil to the core ==
Collect 100 {{Ilink|Demonic Core Essence}} [[Demonic Core Essence]]s from inferniarchs in the area and return to [[Lai]]. Give her the {{KW|essences}} and she'll explain to you the more essences you bring, the more resilient against the demonic corruption you will become. Additionally, you can now use the essences yourself without needing to run back to [[Lai]].

From this point you'll have a rank based on how many essences you have delivered to [[Lai]]. As you can see in the table below, not every rank provides a reward to the player but every rank will decrease the amount of [[Agony Damage]] a player will receive.

Note that the damage is continuously inflicted to the players every 20 seconds they are in any of the underground floors and the damage amount is calculated by the player's current health points. For example, a player with rank 1 and currently with 10,000 health points will receive 450 damage from the demonic corruption.

== A ward winning ==
Ask [[Lai]] for a {{KW|mission}}, and she will ask you to activate ancient crystals located in the towers of the castle.

Start with the northwest tower and activate the remaining three (northeast, southeast, and southwest towers) before the northwest crystal runs out of energy.

== A troubled soul ==
Ask [[Lai]] for a {{KW|mission}} and you'll learn that the poisonous mist is retreating, and she also sensed an untainted soul in great despair somewhere downstairs. Be aware that the creatures on lower floors are stronger!

Go downstairs to the -1 hunting area and walk southeast ([COORD x=33883 y=32406 z=8 | here]). Enter the teleport, and you'll meet [[Ortelio]], who will immediately ask you to help him {{KW|out}} of his prison. He tells you that the key to {{KW|open}} his cell could be anywhere but that he has seen a {{Ilink|Mummified Demon Finger}} [[Mummified Demon Finger]] used to open it before, and he suggests you find some of these fingers.

== The key person ==
Hunt the creatures in the area and loot some {{Ilink|Mummified Demon Finger}} [[Mummified Demon Finger]]s. The exact number of fingers you need depends on luck, but around 10 to 20 fingers should be enough to free [[Ortelio]]. Note that the fingers will break regardless of success or failure.

Use the fingers on the cell door until you are teleported inside the cell, then head back to [[Lai]]. When reporting your {{KW|mission}}, she will say there's a lot to learn from what [[Ortelio]] discovered about the inferniarchs. She also tells you that she has synchronized herself with the resonances of the past of the castle and is willing to share her vision with you of what she's learned so far.

== Vision of the Past ==
Ask [[Lai]] for a {{KW|mission}} again and accept her offer to harmonize you with the Essence of Azzilon . You will then be teleported to a new room where you can meet NPC [[A Vision of the Past]].

Say {{KW|hi}} to the vision, then return to [[Lai]] by entering the portal located south in the room.

== Blast from the past ==
Once again report your {{KW|mission}} to [[Lai]] and you'll learn that, based on [[Ortelio]]'s information, you need to disrupt the demon's operations. The most effective way to do this, he says, is by freeing their troll slaves.

Head to the catacombs and meet [[Ortelio]] on the -2 floor.

== Feeding the trolls ==
:''This task has a maximum duration of 600 seconds (10 minutes). Cooldown for it is around 10~12 minutes.''
Once you get to the -2 floor, go southeast and enter the teleport [COORD x=33893 y=32386 z=9 | here]. From this point, all tasks in this quest use a lever for 5 players, and it's highly recommended to have as many players as possible.

Use the food dispenser in the southwest corner of the room to get {{Ilink|Some Troll Food}} [[Some Troll Food]], then use it on a [[Hungry Slave Troll]]. Once this is done, it will turn into a [[Fed Slave Troll]] for a few seconds and start moving north, then get hungry again. Repeat the process until 20 trolls have reached the green portal in the north area, and the mission will be successful.

Return to [[Lai]] and ask for your next {{KW|mission}}.

== Imp-ossible Mission ==
:''This task has a maximum duration of 600 seconds (10 minutes). Cooldown for it is around 10~ minutes.''
[[File:Charged Imp.gif|right]]
Next, head to the northeastern part of the cave on floor -2, where a portal can be found ([COORD x=33868 y=32287 z=9 | here]). Activate the lever and assemble a team of up to 5 players.

The task is to kill 25 [[Charged Imp]]s while preventing the counter of the [[Powercollector]] in the middle of the room from reaching 25.

Each time a [[Charged Imp]] is killed, the counter will increase by 1. Eliminate the [[Imp Warden]]s to increase the chance of new [[Charged Imp]]s spawning. When a [[Charged Imp]] spawns, it heads straight for the [[Powercollector]], thus you and your team must block its way to the collector by standing in front of it, or by having two players surround themselves with [[Imp Warden]]s close to the collector. If it crosses the floor surrounding the [[Powercollector]], it will disappear, and the counter will increase by 1.

As the team kills the [[Charged Imp]]s and the counter increases, more enemies will spawn, increasing the difficulty. Protection against [[Energy]] attacks is recommended.

Once enough [[Charged Imp]]s have been killed, report the progress back to [[Lai]].

== Teach a man to fish... ==
Next, head to floor -2 and find the teleport to the fishing task. This time, use the {{Ilink|Fishing Rod}} [[Fishing Rod]] to fish up 25 [[Energy Sphere]]s. Each energy sphere caught will add 1 point to the team’s total, and you'll get the message "GOT ONE!".

Be aware of [[Worker Imp]]s in the room; they don’t need to be killed, but be wary of their attacks.

Once completed, return to [[Lai]] and report your mission.

== With friends like these... ==
[[File:Arbaziloth.gif|right]]
:'''''Note:''' Do not forget to bring a {{ilink|Fishing Rod}} [[Fishing Rod]] for this boss fight!''
Finally, gather your team and head to the final boss fight. Go to floor -3 and use the portal to enter [[Arbaziloth]]'s lair.

Before starting it is worth noting that the boss has two distinct modes with two distinct loot tables:
* ''Easy'': if players decide to kill [[The Forgemaster (Creature)|The Forgemaster]], [[Arbaziloth]] loot table will include [[Inferniarch Set]] weapons and {{ilink|Demon Claws}} [[Demon Claws]], the first addon item for the [[Fiend Slayer Outfits]].
* ''Hard'': if players decide to spare [[The Forgemaster (Creature)|The Forgemaster]]'s life, [[Arbaziloth]] will fully heal and become stronger, and the loot table will include helmets ({{ilink|Demonfang Mask}} [[Demonfang Mask]], {{ilink|Dreadfire Headpiece}} [[Dreadfire Headpiece]], {{ilink|Hellstalker Visor}} [[Hellstalker Visor]], {{ilink|Maliceforged Helmet}} [[Maliceforged Helmet]], and {{ilink|Demon Mengu}} [[Demon Mengu]]),  the second addon item for the [[Fiend Slayer Outfits]]: {{ilink|Demon Skull}} [[Demon Skull]], and the {{ilink|Demon in a Green Box}} [[Demon in a Green Box]] item for the mount. Players are also granted access to the [[Doomforge]].

Upon entering the boss fight, players will face [[Arbaziloth]], two [[Overcharged Demon]]s, and [[The Forgemaster (Creature)|The Forgemaster]]. The Forgemaster, as mentioned before, is there so players can decide which kind of fight they will face, while the demons are summons that will keep on respawning as they die, however, note that killing them will heal [[Arbaziloth]], so two common strategies are to lure them away in the room and lose their target, or have a player other than the main blocker to tank them.

As players attack [[Arbaziloth]], at four points during the fight the boss will yell {{Sound|Open fire you fools!}}, becoming invulnerable and having the [[Imp Minion]]s shoot the slingshot to generate a purple fire field in a random spot in the room. Players should pay attention because even though the boss takes damage, its [[HP]] will not go down. Lure the boss over it until the {{sound|CRAAAACK!}} sound appears, then use {{Ilink|Fishing Rod}} [[Fishing Rod]]s on the boss to make him vulnerable again. It is recommended that all players use their fishing rods as the boss can {{ilink|Ruse|Ruse Effect}} [[Dodge]] the use of the rod. When the boss turns vulnerable, a {{ilink|Powerful Hearts Effect}} [[Powerful Hearts Effect|heart effect]] appears on it, players lose target, and the boss makes a sound for each turn: first {{Sound|UH? WHAT ... ? HOW... ?}}, second {{Sound|AHHH! HOW DARE YOU!?}}, third {{Sound|YOU WILL PAY FOR THIS INSOLENCE}}, and last {{Sound|NOOO! AHHHH! THE POWER!!}}. Each time this process is done, one of the energy spheres are removed from [[Arbaziloth]]'s arm.

If the team did not kill [[The Forgemaster (Creature)|The Forgemaster]] during the fight, it will leave the fight shortly after the fourth mechanic is done, and [[Arbaziloth]] will heal himself fully. At this point, no mechanics are left and players just need to attack him until he dies. Note that the boss will deal more damage in this phase. 

Once the fight is over, players can use the orange teleport in the room to access the [[Doomforge]]. In case [[The Forgemaster (Creature)|The Forgemaster]] died during the bossfight, [[Arbaziloth]] will not heal himself, and the Doomforge will not be accessible.

== Reward ==

After defeating [[Arbaziloth]], report a last time to [[Lai]] and you will receive the base [[Fiend Slayer Outfits|Fiend Slayer Outfit]]. <br />[[File:Outfit Fiend Slayer Male.gif|link=Fiend Slayer Outfits]] [[File:Outfit Fiend Slayer Female.gif|link=Fiend Slayer Outfits]]

Additionally, killing the boss 10 times in the ''Hard'' difficulty grants you access to an additional room in the [[Doomforge]] where you can get the base version of any [[Inferniarch Set]] weapon of your choice.
