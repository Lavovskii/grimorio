==Required Equipment==

==Method==
'''Note:''' At the level range of 8~40, this cave has some difficult creatures, and the rewards are not terribly exciting. It is recommended to get a team together and complete all of the quests in this cave at one time, instead of making multiple trips.

* Go down into the [[Edron Orc Cave]]. The entrance is southwest of the castle ([COORD x=33173 y=31900 z=7 | here]). You can access this area through a drain in the basement of the Edron Flats.
* Once in the hole, you will meet only [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Orc]]s, [[Orc Spearmen]] and [[Orc Warrior]]s. Follow the cave to the west and then south. There are a couple of side rooms, but basically there is only one way to go.
[[Image:Dark Armor Quest Map 01.jpg]]
* On the next level, you will face [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Orc]]s, [[Orc Spearmen]], [[Orc Warrior]]s and probably one or two lured [[Orc Berserker]]s. Go west and take the passage north. Go into the first room on the east and drop down the hole.
[[Image:Dark Armor Quest Map 02.jpg]]
* As soon as you drop down this hole, you will be surrounded by [[Orc Shaman]]s. Remember, [[Energy Damage|energy attacks]] do not work on [[Orc Shaman|shamans]], so use [[GFB]]s to clear the room (the summoned snakes make it hard to aim, and your knights can't get through them to attack the Shamans).
* On this level, go north, facing more [[Orc Shaman]]s and one or two [[Orc Berserker]]s. You will walk over a tile-floor area - this means you are on the right track.
[[Image:Dark Armor Quest Map 03.jpg]]
* When you go to the next level, you will face [[Poison Spider]]s, multiple [[Orc Leader]]s, [[Orc Shaman]]s and probably one or two lured [[Orc Berserker]]s. Head north and you will see a large pit and a [[Giant Spider]] waiting for you inside of it. The hole to get down is on the north-east side of the pit.
[[Image:Dark Armor Quest Map 04.jpg]]
* Just go down the hole and kill the [[Giant Spider]]. You can find the [[Dark Armor]] inside a dead body on the ground. Instead of killing the Giant Spider you can also put on [[Defensive Fighting]] mode, run to the body, grab the loot, and go back.
