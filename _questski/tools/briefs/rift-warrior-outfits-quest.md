==Method==
=== Base Outfit ===
[[File:Outfit_Rift_Warrior_Male.gif]][[File:Outfit Rift Warrior Female.gif]]

Complete the [[Ferumbras' Ascension Quest]].

=== Addon 1 ===
[[File:Outfit_Rift_Warrior_Male_Addon_1.gif]][[File:Outfit Rift_Warrior Female_Addon_1.gif]]

Bring 100 [[Silver Token]]s to [[Cledwyn]] and ask for this addon.

=== Addon 2 ===
[[File:Outfit_Rift_Warrior_Male_Addon_2.gif]][[File:Outfit Rift_Warrior Female_Addon_2.gif]]

Bring 100 [[Silver Token]]s to [[Cledwyn]] and ask for this addon.

=== Reward ===
Besides the [[Rift Warrior Outfits]] you will also earn the [[Rift Warrior]] [[achievement]] after completing this quest.
