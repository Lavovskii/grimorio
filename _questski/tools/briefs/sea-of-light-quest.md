## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

== Requirements ==
* [[Rope]], or equivalent
* [[Shovel]], or equivalent
* [[Pick]], or equivalent
* 2gp to donate to [[Shoddy Beggar]].
* Travel money

== Method ==

=== Mission 1: The Plans ===
Go to [[Edron]] and ask [[Spectulus]] for a '''mission'''. Spectulus is in the central tower of [[Noodles Academy of Modern Magic]], the top floor.

[[Spectulus]] wants your assistance in identifying an inventor of a magic device which can sail the ''sea of light''.

Find the [[Shoddy Beggar]] in [[Edron]] and '''spare''' him at 1gp to get him to talk. Answer his questions adequately (see [[#Mission 1: The Plans 2|transcripts]]) and he will give you the [[Plans for a Strange Device]].

Bring the [[Plans for a Strange Device]] to [[Spectulus]] and give him 30 minutes (real time) to study them.

</gallery>

=== Mission 2: The Collector ===
[[Spectulus]] needs a [[Rare Crystal]] which can be found in the [[Lost Mines]], which is between [[Edron Troll Cave]] and [[Edron Goblin Cave]] ([COORD x=33103 y=31811 z=7 | here]). Inside it you will encounter [[Rat]]s, [[Undead Mine Worker]]s and [[Undead Prospector]]s.

When you reach the end of the mine, use a [[Pick]] on the large cyan crystal to obtain the [[Rare Crystal]].

Bring the [[Rare Crystal]] back to [[Spectulus]] and report about your '''mission'''.

<gallery mode="nolines">
File:Sea of Light Quest Mission 2 The Collector.jpg
</gallery>

=== Mission 3: The Mirror Crystal ===
[[Spectulus]] will give you the [[Carrying Device]] and [[Rare Crystal]]. From [[Edron]] boat, sail to [[Carlin]] and head north to ferry to [[Vega]]. When you arrive at Vega, follow the shore south-west until you find a [[Stony Pond]]. Use the rare crystal on the [[Ice Cube]] and you will be teleported into a cave. Go to the end of the cave and kill [[The Collector]]. Use the [[Carrying Device]] on its corpse.

Bring the [[Filled Carrying Device]] back to [[Spectulus]].
