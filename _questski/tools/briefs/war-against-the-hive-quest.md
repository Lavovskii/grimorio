== Equipment ==
* [[Rope]] or equivalent items or spells

== Introduction ==
[[Orockle]] is trying to fight against the destructive [[Hive Born]]. You can do the following missions for him. You can accept all missions at once, and each mission counts as an action against the Hive Born, required to advance the [[Hive Born World Change|hive stage]]. 200 actions in one day (by the entire server) will increase the stage of the hive, where the stronger areas will be accessible. During stage 3, the hive will be open for 5 days. On the last day of stage 3 the points must be accumulated in order to revert to stage 2, otherwise it goes back to stage 1.

'''Note:''' You can ask the [[Guide NPCs]] about the '''Hive''' world change to find out what stage is active and how many actions have been taken today.

Orockle will give you the items you need for the missions, but if you lose them you will need to buy them from him for 150-200gp each. Alternatively, you could borrow the mission items from someone else, as you don't lose them when turning missions in.

== First Stage ==
{{NPC Quote|link=no|NPC=Guide NPCs|msg=The hive is well defended and prepared for war. ...}}
The first stage missions are to ''blind'' the hive ([[#Antenna Blinding|Antenna Blinding]]), disrupt its ''digestion'' ([[#Disrupt The Hives Digestion|Disrupt The Hives Digestion]]), gather ''acid'' ([[#Dissolved Chitin|Dissolved Chitin]]), or ''disable'' swarm pores ([[#Pore Stuffing|Pore Stuffing]]). Each stage 1 mission gives 1 war experience, 1 favour and 1000 experience points.

=== Antenna Blinding ===
Orockle will give you a [[Strange Powder (Hive)|Strange Powder]]. Travel around the east side of the [[Hive]], search for small hill-like structures and use the powder on any of their antennae to disrupt their communications.

=== Disrupt The Hives Digestion ===
Orockle will give you a [[Blob Bomb]]. Travel to the far eastern side of the [[Hive]] and in a small hive pyramid go down 2 floors. Use the [[Blob Bomb]] (like a rune or a fishing rod, from a distance) on 7 [[Floor Blob]]s.

* [[Hickup]] (10)
* [[Heartburn]] (50)
* [[Stomach Ulcer]] (100)

=== Dissolved Chitin ===
Orockle will give you 8 [[Flask of Chitin Dissolver]] and a [[Swarmer Drum]]. You must kill 8 [[Crawler]]s and use the [[Flask of Chitin Dissolver]] on their fresh corpses to dissolve their chitin. When done, you will have [[Flask of Dissolved Chitin|flasks of dissolved chitin]].

On the surface of the hive, use the swarmer drum. This will summon a [[Swarmer Hatchling]] which you should then feed the [[Flask of Dissolved Chitin]] to (use the flask on it). Immediately the Hatchling will travel to Orockle who will collect acid from it. Do this until all flasks have been used. You must wait 30 seconds to reuse the drum.

'''Tip:''' You are given 8 [[Flask of Chitin Dissolver]]s but you are not limited in how many times you can receive [[Flask of Dissolved Chitin]]. You can buy a lot of chitin dissolver and dissolve chitin in bulk. In addition, you can use the drum as long as you have the task and haven't poisoned 8 [[Swarmer Hatchling]]s. You can ask other players to summon swarmers for you, making it even quicker.

Return to Orockle when you are done and you will receive your rewards. By repeating this mission you can get achievements:
* [[Gatherer]] (10)
* [[Supplier]] (50)
* [[Chitin Bane]] (100)

=== Pore Stuffing ===
Orockle will give you a [[Gooey Substance]]. Travel to the east side of the [[Hive]] area and use the Gooey Substance on 7 [[Hive Pore]]s. They summon three [[Lesser Swarmer]]s from time to time so beware of being trapped.

== Second Stage ==
{{NPC Quote|link=no|NPC=Guide NPCs|msg=The defences of the hive are breached. The hive structure to the east is open ...}}
The second stage missions are to ''kill'' the guards ([[#Kollos Killing|Kollos Killing]]) or ''break'' the control crystals ([[#Destroy The Hive Crystals|Destroy The Hive Crystals]]). During this stage you can also complete missions from stage 1.

Each stage 2 mission gives 2 war exp, 2 favour and 1500 experience points.

=== Kollos Killing ===
[[Orockle]] will instruct you to kill the Hive's guards. Kill 9 [[Kollos]] which can be found on higher floors of the eastern Hive structure (2nd stage) or in all levels (including ground) of the western Hive structure (3rd stage).

Return to Orockle when you are done and you will receive your rewards. By repeating this mission you can get achievements:
* [[Guard Killer]] (10)
* [[Hive Infiltrator]] (50)
* [[Exterminator]] (100)

=== Destroy The Hive Crystals ===
Orockle will give you a [[Reagent Flask]]. Travel into the Hive and use the flask on the corpse of a [[Spidris]]. You will get a flask of [[Aggressive Fluid]], this fluid last few minutes only (2?). Use it on [[Insectoid Monolith]] to destroy it, the monoliths recovers in 5 minutes. You have to refill the flask on another [[Spidris]]' corpse and repeat breaking 5 times in total. Note: it is a good idea to have a few [[Reagent Flask]]s with you for this mission (either buy them or reuse them from previous times you did the mission). This way you don't have to wait to break a crystal before obtaining another [[Aggressive Fluid]].

Return to Orockle when you are done and you will receive your rewards. By repeating this mission you can get achievements:
* [[Headache]] (10)
* [[Confusion]] (50)
* [[Manic]] (100)

== Third Stage ==
{{NPC Quote|link=no|NPC=Guide NPCs|msg=The hives defences have fallen. Its armies are confused and in shambles. All structures are open for invaders. ...}}
In this stage you can do missions from 1st and 2nd stages as well as access the reward room which is located on top floor of western Hive structure, going up [COORD x=33518 y=31209 z=7 | here]. No new missions are available during stage 3.

=== Rewards ===
Doing the missions earns War Exp points and Favour points: the first stage missions are worth 1 point each, and the second stage missions are worth 2 points each. To enter reward rooms you have to buy special pheromone from Orockle (say "reward", "yes") for 50 favour (it will last for one week) which will grant you access to the areas behind [[Hive Gate]]s, except those guarding the eastern and western towers).

The [[Insectoid Outfits|Insectoid outfit and addons]] are obtained by doing tasks. You can get the outfit with 300 war exp, the first addon with 500 war exp, and the second addon with 1000 war exp. The war exp is not consumed, so you only need 1000 war exp, not (300 + 500 + 1000).

There is 1 [[Hive Gate]] on every floor on the eastern structure, and every floor on the western structure. Behind each structure you can find [[Hive Cell]]s which can each be used weekly to obtain 1 [[Gooey Mass]]. This means every week you can get up to 16 [[Gooey Mass]]es just behind the gates. However, be prepared because there are many enemies waiting for you behind each of the hive gates.

At any time, you can check your war exp and favour by checking the quest log. You can also check the amount of each task completed this way.

You can get the following achievements by completing any mission:
* [[Hive Fighter]] (300 war exp)
* [[Hive War Veteran]] (500 war exp)
* [[Bane of the Hive]] (full [[Insectoid Outfits|Insectoid Outfit]])

The underground of the western tower is strongly guarded with many [[Hive Overseer]]s, and is home to the [[Hive Born Bosses]]: [[Chopper]], [[Fleshslicer]], [[Maw]], [[Mindmasher]], [[Rotspit]] and [[Shadowstalker]]. You can find them [COORD x=33479 y=31238 z=8 | here] [MINIMAP x=33479 y=31238 z=8 marks=(33463,31246,8) (33507,31245,8) (33424,31268,8) (33452,31225,8) (33525,31263,8) (33479,31238,8)].
