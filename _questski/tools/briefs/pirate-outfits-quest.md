=== Outfit ===
[[Image:Outfit Pirate Male.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit Pirate Female.gif]]

==== Required Equipment ====
* Must have completed the [[The_Shattered_Isles_Quest/Spoiler#Access_to_Meriana | Access to Meriana Quest]] to gain access to [[Meriana]] Island
* Must have completed the [[Meriana Quest]] to gain access to [[Nargor]] island

==== Method ====
Complete the [[Meriana Quest]]. Once you have done it the only thing you have to do is report back to Raymond and say:<br>

'''Pirate outfit'''

Now you have a Pirate Outfit.

''Player'': '''Bye'''<br>
Raymond Striker: May there always be wind in your sails.

=== Pirate Sabre ===
[[Image:Outfit Pirate Male Addon 1.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit Pirate Female Addon 1.gif]]

==== Required Equipment ====
* 100 [[Eye Patch]]es
* 100 [[Peg Leg]]s
* 100 [[Hook]]s 
(dropped by all kind of pirates)

==== Method ====
Talk to [[Duncan]] and say tasks, he will ask you to give him 100 [[Eye Patch]]es, 100 [[Peg Leg]]s and 100 [[Hook]]s, in that order. When you bring them to him, he will tell you a secret word. Go to [[Morgan]] and tell him that word, he will give you a sabre for pirate outfit.

=== Pirate Hat ===
[[Image:Outfit Pirate Male Addon 2.gif]] [[Image:Outfit Pirate Female Addon 2.gif]]
==== Required Equipment ====
* {{ilink|Ron the Ripper's Sabre}} [[Ron the Ripper's Sabre]]
* {{ilink|Deadeye Devious' Eye Patch}} [[Deadeye Devious' Eye Patch]]
* {{ilink|Lethal Lissy's Shirt}} [[Lethal Lissy's Shirt]]
* {{ilink|Brutus Bloodbeard's Hat}} [[Brutus Bloodbeard's Hat]]

==== Method ====
Talk to [[Ariella]] in [[Meriana]] and ask for a {{KW|task}}. She will ask you to kill four pirate leaders, [[Lethal Lissy]], [[Ron the Ripper]], [[Brutus Bloodbeard]] and [[Deadeye Devious]], and bring to her a unique item from each as a proof that you have killed them.

Despite of her request, you can buy the required items and deliver them without killing the pirate leaders. If you plan on killing the pirate leaders you'll have to wait for the [[Liberty Bay Raids#Pirate Bosses Raid|pirate leaders raid]] to take place in [[Liberty Bay]], or complete the [[Killing in the Name of... Quest#Unranked Tasks|Killing in the Name of... Quest pirate task]] with [[Raymond Striker]].
