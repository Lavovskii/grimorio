'''Note: this outfit quest is special, because you will only be able to obtain one of two available addons, not both.'''

=== Outfit ===
[[Image:Outfit_Yalaharian_Male.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit_Yalaharian_Female.gif]]

Complete the [[In Service of Yalahar Quest]].

=== Sage Addon ===
[[Image:Outfit_Yalaharian Male Addon_1.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit_Yalaharian Female Addon 1.gif]]

==== Required Equipment ====
*[[Vampiric Crest]] (obtained through the [[Blood Brothers Quest]]).

==== Method ====
After completing the [[Blood Brothers Quest]], bring the [[Vampiric Crest]] to [[Palimuth]] in [[Yalahar]], if you choose Palimuth's side in mission 9 of the [[In Service of Yalahar Quest|Yalahar main quest]] you will get this addon. To obtain the addon say '''addon''' to Palimuth while holding the crest.
