## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler|name=Children of the Revolution Quest}}

=== Required Equipment ===
* 6 [[Box]]es or [[Parcel]]s or [[Levitate]] spell.
* Possibly [[Shovel]] and [[Rope]] or [[Magic Rope]].
* [[The New Frontier Quest]] mission 8.
* A melee weapon to break some [[Wooden Bars]] for mission 2.

To start this quest, find NPC [[Zalamon]] in the very east of [[Zao]], one floor down.

[MINIMAP x=33340 y=31415 z=7 marks=none]

==Missions==
=== Prove Your Worzz! ===
You need to go through the teleport [COORD x=33198 y=31350 z=7 | here] to the area north of the [[Dragonblaze Peaks]] and go to the far east. There is a little camp of lizards and the [[Tactical Map]] is on the shelves inside the first building you see. Be ready to face [[Lizard Zaogun]]s along with other [[High Class Lizards]]. The camp is located [COORD x=33280 y=31221 z=7 | here].

You have to deliver the map in LESS THAN 1 HOUR to complete the mission, otherwise you'll have to start it again.

After you have the map, go back to Zalamon and say 'mission'.<br />
[[File:Childrenofrevolution1.PNG]]

=== Mission 1: Corruption ===

Go to the Temple of Equilibrium (it's marked on your map - [COORD x=33177 y=31194 z=7 | here]) and follow the stairs up to the top and back down to the bottom on the other side, you'll face many [[Lizard Zaogun]]s along with other lizards you have battled already. There are hidden stairs behind the double set of stairs which you came down ([COORD x=33178 y=31192 z=6 | here]), when you go down there are 5 [[Lizard Chosen]]. Red text comes up saying you passed.<br />
'''Note:''' You can jump back up immediately to avoid being hit.<br />

[[File:Childrenofrevolution2.PNG]]

'''Hint:''' if you don't want to waste a lot of supplies killing the [[Lizard Zaogun]] fighting your way up, it is possible to go up the first floor and use [[Levitate]] to get to the other side, on the red circle in the image:<br />

[[File:Temple_of_equilibrium.jpg]]

You'll find 2 [[Lizard Zaogun]] and 1 [[Lizard Dragon Priest]] going down, but you can run to the stairs and go immediately up afterwards.

You can exit the temple of equilibrium [COORD x=33186 y=31205 z=6 | here], by using [[Vines]] [[File:Vines.gif]]<br /> 
Report back to Zalamon.

=== Mission 2: Imperial Zzecret Weaponzz ===
Go into the small camp [[Chaochai]] to the north of the [[Dragonblaze Peaks]] ([[Zalamon]] marks the entrance on your map, the camp is [COORD x=33238 y=31166 z=7 | here] [MINIMAP x=33238 y=31166 z=7 marks=(33226,31165,7) (33229,31156,7) (33257,31167,7) (33233,31181,7)]). There are 3 buildings which you have to enter (marked with stars on the map):
*a food storage room on ground floor, [COORD x=33230 y=31154 z=7 | here]
*weapon storage room in a basement '''(which you need a melee weapon to break through [[Wooden Bars]])''', [COORD x=33228 y=31149 z=8 | here]
*a barracks on ground floor, [COORD x=33258 y=31170 z=7 | here]

You'll get a red message on your screen for each one. '''Don't forget to grab the Tome of Knowledge located in the north-eastern-most building, one flight up. You will find a chest that contains the Tome.'''<br />
[[File:Revolutionmission3.jpg]]

Once you have found them return to [[Zalamon]].

=== Mission 3: Zee Killing Fieldzz ===

First, get the [[Flask of Poison]] {{Ilink|Flask of Poison}} from [[Zalamon]]'s storage room west of the stairs. 

Then, go to the teleporter to the [[Muggy Plains]] and head east from there to the rice fields ([COORD x=33281 y=31142 z=7 | here] [MINIMAP x=33281 y=31142 z=7 marks=(33226,31165,7) (33229,31156,7) (33257,31167,7) (33233,31181,7)]). Go to the very top rice field and use the poison anywhere on the water. '''After you use the poison, go to the north-east and find the building for the next part, Mission 4. You can enter the building and receive the red message when you reach the basement (pictured below) to report back to Zalamon, saving a trip and resources.''' Then head back to NPC [[Zalamon]] to finish the mission.

[[File:Children of the revolution - Poison.png]]
[[File:Childrenofrevolution3.PNG]]

=== Mission 4: Zze Way of Zztonezz ===
''Note: You can do this part during the previous mission and also talk to [[Zalamon]] about symbols while giving report of mission 3.''

Head east from the teleport in [[Muggy Plains]], past the rice fields located in the north-east. There will be a lot of [[High Class Lizards]] along the way and some [[Mutated Tiger]]s, [[Mutated Bat]]s and [[Death Blob]]s when you get close to the right place. Go up the stairs ([COORD x=33311 y=31135 z=7 | here] [MINIMAP x=33311 y=31135 z=7 marks=(33311,31135,7)]) and follow the mountain pass (where there's 1 [[Mutated Tiger]]). Keep going up and into a building, go inside and make your way as far down the stairs as possible to a room with some strange symbols. A red message will appear.

[[File:Strange symbols.jpg]]

Head back to NPC [[Zalamon]] and say to him {{KW|strange symbols}}. He will tell you to take a [[Flask of Extra Greasy Oil]] {{Ilink|Flask of Extra Greasy Oil}} from a [[Small Bamboo Shelf]] in his storage room. Go back to the building in [[Muggy Plains]] to the room with the levers and use the grease on them. Then '''use''' the levers to arrange the symbols in the center of the room to match the symbols on the bottom floor. You will get a red message when you have done it right. Report back to [[Zalamon]]. 

You will receive a [[Tome of Knowledge]] as a reward for completing this mission.

=== Mission 5: Phantom Army ===

Note¹: Players who have previously completed this part can ask NPC "Zalamon" for "Task" and choose "'''Zztrike'''", which allows the person to enter again but their mission is to kill the [[Lizard Gate Guardian]].<br/>
Note²: If you are receiving help from a player that has already completed the [[Wrath of the Emperor Quest]], that player must get the '''Zztrike''' task from [[Chartan]], as he can no longer access [[Zalamon]].

To do this mission you must have 4 players in your group. In the building where you were on the previous mission you can now go down to access a room with a blue flame teleport. Walk into the teleport. In the area past the teleport the players must each stand in a square that is missing a lizard statue for 10–30 seconds. You will all see a message that you hear "distant clicks" when standing on your square.

[[File:Mission 5 Phantom Army distant clicks.jpg|300px|center]]
[[File:Children of Revolution - Mission 5 Phanthom Army's Firebomb Spot NE.png|thumb|220px|One of the spots where you can protect yourself with with Fire Bomb Runes]]
After this all players need to walk south where you will find some stairs leading up. '''There's a time limit and if you take too long to go up the stairs you won't be able to continue.''' When you go up you are in the area where the Phantom Army will spawn, which consists of [[Eternal Guardian]]s and [[Lizard Chosen|Lizards Chosen]]. It is suggested to hide away in the south-western or north-eastern part of the area and block the way with [[Fire Bomb Rune]]s to avoid fighting the army entirely. After surviving for 10 minutes you will be teleported out by [[Zalamon]] and you can report the mission to him.
{{clear}}[[File:Phanthom Army Levitate Spot.png|thumb|220px|Levitate here to hide yourself from the monster raids]]
The most eastern house has [COORD x=33277 y=31111 z=7 | a spot] where you are able to use levitate (''Exani Hur Up'') and stay completely out of the way of spawned creatures.

'''NB:''' There is a [[Tome of Knowledge]] in a chest in the building on the east side of the path ([COORD x=33264 y=31130 z=7 | here]) leading to the gate (south from where you begin).

[[File:Childrenofrevolution4.PNG]]<br />

[[File:Childrenofrevolution6.PNG]]<br />

Report back to Zalamon about your mission; you need to have the [[Weapons Crate (Set)|Weapons Crate]] and the other [[Corned Fish]] with you and then he will reward you with one of the following: [[Red Gem]], [[Green Gem]], [[Blue Gem]], or [[Zaoan Armor]].

=== Weekly Task: Zze Art of War ===
Ask [[Zalamon]] for the '''strike''' mission, or if you have lost access to Zalamon through the [[Wrath of the Emperor Quest]], ask [[Chartan]] at the [[Rebel Camp]].

This task is the same as '''Mission 5: Phantom Army'''. With the weekly task you can help other players with mission 5, collect the [[Tome of Knowledge]] there and/or kill the [[Lizard Gate Guardian]] boss.

This task can be repeated after 6 days and 20 hours.

The reward of this task is 10000 experience.
