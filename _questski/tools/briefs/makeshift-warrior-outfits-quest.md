== Base Outfit ==
[[File:Outfit_Makeshift_Warrior_Male.gif]][[File:Outfit_Makeshift_Warrior_Female.gif]]

Use a [[Plan for a Makeshift Armour]] to obtain the base outfit. It is dropped by the [[Warzone 4]] boss, [[The Baron from Below]].

{{Server Message|By using the plan you knock together a makeshift armour out of wooden planks, rusty nails and leather rags.}}

== Addon 1 (Shield and Weapon) ==
[[File:Outfit_Makeshift_Warrior_Male_Addon_1.gif]][[File:Outfit_Makeshift_Warrior_Female_Addon_1.gif]]

Use [[Crude Wood Planks]] to obtain the first addon. It is dropped by the [[Warzone 6]] boss, [[The Duke of the Depths]]

{{Server Message|You use the wooden planks to knock up a makeshift shield and weapon.}}

== Addon 2 (Tinged Pot) ==
[[File:Outfit_Makeshift_Warrior_Male_Addon_2.gif]][[File:Outfit_Makeshift_Warrior_Female_Addon_2.gif]]

Use a [[Tinged Pot]] to obtain the second addon. It is dropped by the [[Warzone 5]] boss, [[The Count of the Core]]. 

{{Server Message|You use the tinged pot as a makeshift helmet.}}

== Reward ==
Besides the [[Makeshift Warrior Outfits]] you will also receive [[Cobbled and Patched]] [[Achievement]] which was implemented one month after the quest and outfits.
