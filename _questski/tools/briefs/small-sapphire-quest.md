## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Small Sapphire Quest}}
== Requirements ==
* [[Rope]], or equivalent.

== Method ==
Follow the path to [[Drefia]] (you will encounter [[Snake]]s, [[Scorpion]]s, [[Nomad (Basic)|Nomad]]s, [[Ghoul]]s, and [[Tarnished Spirit]]s) and you should appear [COORD x=33018 y=32429 z=7 | here]. The chest containing the reward is located [COORD x=32962 y=32430 z=7 | here], and on your way there you should encounter [[Skeleton]]s, [[Ghoul]]s, [[Stalker]]s, and [[Demon Skeleton]]s. The island itself contains two [[Pirate Skeleton]]s and one [[Pirate Ghost]]. Contrary to how it may seem, you can simply walk over to the island and open the chest.
