== Requirements ==
* Access to [[Helheim]] ([[Barbarian Test Quest]] and [[The Ice Islands Quest]], up to ''The Secret of Helheim'').

== Method ==
'''Note''': To save time, you may want to do this with the [[Ice Islands Quest]] mission ''The Secret of Helheim''.

From [[Svargrond]], go to the south and find [[Buddel]]. Ask him for a trip to [[Helheim]]. When you arrive at Helheim, go south-east from Buddel's boat and up the mountain. Go north-east and down the 2 ramps to enter the dungeon.

You will face [[Mummy|Mummies]] and [[Ghoul]]s on the first floor. Go to the south end of the cave and down the ramp. On this floor you will face [[Demon Skeleton]]s, [[Crypt Shambler]]s and [[Mummy|Mummies]]. Go west to find another staircase and go down. You will find [[Demon Skeleton]]s, [[Crypt Shambler]]s and [[Bonebeast]]s on this floor. Go north and find a [[Pile of Bones (Quest)]]. Be careful as you may find up to 3 [[Bonebeast]]s together with a [[Demon Skeleton]]. Open the pile of bones to receive your reward.
