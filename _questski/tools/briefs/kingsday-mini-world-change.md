== Method ==
[[File:Kingsday Tibia.jpg|thumb|Cursing the King]]

The day is dedicated to celebrating the [[King Tibianus|King]]. This Mini World Change takes place in [[Thais]], and the following changes take place:
* Thais will be decorated for the day (as it was for [[Tibia's 15th Anniversary]]).
* [[Thais NPCs|Thaian NPCs]] will talk differently during the day.
* If it's Sunday, you can enter the [[Tibian Prize Exhibition]] for free.
* [[Raids]] will occur in the [COORD x=32395 y=32193 z=7 | Knights' Guild arena] every hour (approximately [[Server Save|2-20 minutes]] after the hour) in 2 waves. The second wave is triggered 2 minutes after the first one. Raid creatures will despawn 30 minutes after spawning if they are not killed.
* Cursing at [[King Tibianus]] will have you sent to the [[Thaian Jail]], where you will remain until the next [[raid]] in the Knights' Guild arena. '''You cannot logout in the jail, there is no way to leave before the next raid.'''
* Greeting the king will reward players with the [[achievement]] [[Loyal Subject]].
