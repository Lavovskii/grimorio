== Missions ==
=== Mission 1: Skulled ===

[[File:Findingcavemanskull.png|right]]
Go to [[Lazaran]] ([COORD x=32992 y=31471 z=3 | here]) located in the mountain between the city of Farmine and the Zao plains. Ask him about a mission and he will provide you with the first mission of the Unnatural Selection Quest.

Your mission is to find the [[Skull of a Caveman]] {{Ilink|Skull of a Caveman}}. Go to the northern orc settlement on the Zao Steppe, up the northern ramp and then down [COORD x=33042 y=31302 z=6 | here]. Pass through the sealed door to find the skull in a small cave, [COORD x=33052 y=31312 z=8 | here].

"Use" the [[Bone Pile]] to get the skull, and then return to Lazaran for your next mission.
{{Clear}}

=== Mission 2: All Around the World ===
You will get back the [[Skull of a Caveman]] and need to explore the world, letting the skull see everything.

You need to stand at the highest place in some specific locations and then use the skull.

You have to do it in the order below. If you forget which one is next, check your Quest Log.

*[[Edron]] - [COORD x=33268 y=31836 z=7 | Here], top floor of [[Ivory Towers]] in the fourth tower, where [[Spectulus]] is.<br />{{Sound|You hold the skull up high. You can resist the urge to have it look through a telescope though.}}
*[[Ab'dendriel]] - [COORD x=32711 y=31669 z=7 | Here], the tower west of the boat.<br />{{Sound|You hold up the skull and let it take a peek over the beautiful elven town through the dense leaves.}}
*[[Femor Hills]] - [COORD x=32525 y=31774 z=6 | here] , at the tower, with [[Thanita]].<br />{{Sound|Thanita gives you a really strange look as you hold up the skull, but oh well.}}
*[[Darashia]] - [COORD x=33213 y=32453 z=7 | Here], temple with [[Kasmir]] (above depot).<br />{{Sound|That was a real easy one. And you are used to getting strange looks now, so whatever!}}
*[[Ankrahmun]] - [COORD x=33150 y=32844 z=7 | Here], at the highest pyramid.<br />{{Sound|Wow, it's hot up here. Luckily the skull can't get a sunburn any more, but you can, so you better descend again!}}
*[[Port Hope]] - [COORD x=32580 y=32744 z=7 | Here], the western watch tower.<br />{{Sound|Considering that higher places around here aren't that easy to reach, you think the view from here is tolerably good.}}
*[[Liberty Bay]] - [COORD x=32347 y=32809 z=7 | Here], [[Ivory Circle]] at [[Malunga]]'s magic shop (north of depot).<br />{{Sound|Yep, that's a pretty high spot. If Lazaran ever sees what the skull sees, he'd be pretty satisfied with that nice view.}}
*[[Yalahar]] - [COORD x=32790 y=31237 z=7 | Here], [[Streets of Yalahar|shopping tower]], with [[Vincent]] (north of depot).<br />{{Sound|Well, there are higher spots around here, but none of them is as easily reachable as this one. It just has to suffice.}}
*[[Svargrond]] - [COORD x=32236 y=31096 z=5 | Here], the Watchtower west of the magic carpet.<br />{{Sound|Nice! White in white as far as the eye can see. Time to leave before your fingers turn into icicles.}}
*[[Thais]] - [COORD x=32345 y=32265 z=7 | Here], the tower down [[Harbour Street]].<br />{{Sound|That's definitely one of the highest spots in whole Tibia. If that's not simply perfect you don't know what it is.}}
*[[Carlin]] - [COORD x=32318 y=31749 z=7 | Here], in [[Queen Eloise]]'s castle in the western tower.<br />{{Sound|What a beautiful view. Worthy of a Queen indeed! Time to head back to Lazaran and show him what you got.}}
After completing all these steps, return to [[Lazaran]].

=== Mission 3: Dance Dance Evolution ===
Ask for a mission to Lazaran. He will tell you to go to [[Ulala]], who is located above Lazaran (up a ladder and up some stairs, [COORD x=32996 y=31470 z=1 | here]). Ulala will tell you to dance to please their god [[Krunus]].

[[File:Dance_Evolution.png|thumb|right|You can follow this numbers for the steps order.]]
On the south mountain ([COORD x=32991 y=31499 z=1 | here]) in the camp you will find the Krunus altar, there are lots of leaves on the ground. Step on them and there will be different effects on the ground. Move to the [[Plant Effect]] {{Ilink|Plant Effect}} which will only appear on one of the tiles. You can stop when you see "{{Sound|Krunus should be pleased.}}"

Also, you will earn the achievement [[Talented Dancer]] when finished.

When you are done, return to [[Ulala]].
{{Clear}}

=== Mission 4: Bits and Pieces ===
Now the god [[Krunus]] is pleased, another god called Pandor needs to be pleased. [[Ulala]] wants you to collect 5 [[Orc Tooth|Orc Teeth]], 5 [[Minotaur leather]]s and 5 [[Lizard Leather]]s. Bring them to her.

=== Mission 5: Ray of Light ===
The third god which needs to be pleased is called Fasuon. To do so you need to use a red [[Large Crystal]] [COORD x=32981 y=31431 z=1 | here]. Go downstairs where [[Lazaran]] is. There is a staircase in the top left corner, go down and make your way through the caves fighting nothing stronger then a single [[Demon Skeleton]]. Pass the quest door and make your way to the crystal on the top. Use it to complete this mission. {{Sound|A ray of sunlight comes down from the heaven and hits the crystal. Wow. That probably means Fasuon is supporting.}}<br />
While here, you will also notice a chest to the north on the level below, go to it to get a [[Tome of Knowledge]].

=== Mission 6: Firewater Burn ===
[[Lazaran]] has asked you for a [[Pot]] of [[Beer]], in his own words. Buy a [[Pot]] and mug of beer in Venore. You can then fill the pot with this beer and return to complete this mission and gather your [[Tribal Crest]].
* Take the [[Tribal Crest]] back to NPC [[Cael]] to receive your [[Warmaster Outfits]] addon. Just say "tribal crest" to [[Cael]].

== Daily Tasks ==

=== Daily Task: Colour Me Good ===
Depending on what colour is missing, you will need a [[Bucket]] of [[Blood]], [[Orange Star]], 100 [[Blueberry|Blueberries]], [[Goat Grass]], [[Some Leaves]] and possibly more to gain 500 experience. The [[Orange Star]], [[Goat Grass]] and [[Some Leaves]] are daily spawns around the [[Zao]] Steppe. They're quite easy to find if you do a little exploring.

=== Daily Task: Magic Mushrooms ===
You have to harvest mushrooms from the small cave just north of [[Lazaran]] and he will reward you with 50 experience points for each [[Brown Mushroom]], up to 10 for 500 points. You also get to keep the other mushrooms you harvest, except for the [[Brown Mushroom]]s which [[Lazaran]] will take from you when you speak to him again to complete the task. (If you haven't harvested 10 [[Brown Mushroom]]s, you can bring more [[Brown Mushroom]]s with you, he will take just 10, and reward you for all 10 he took.)
