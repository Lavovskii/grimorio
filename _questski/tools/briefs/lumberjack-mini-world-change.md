'''Note:''' These messages will only appear before anyone has told the Queen about the lumberjack.

== Method ==
This Mini World Change happens in the [[Fields of Glory|fields]] between [[Ab'Dendriel]] and [[Carlin]].

Sometimes the [[trees]] north of Carlin are cut down by the [[Lumberjack NPCs|lumberjack]] [[Chip]]. He will buy [[Wood|pieces of wood]] for 50 [[gp]]. The [[Queen Eloise|Queen of Carlin]] will not be very amused about that.

Mention ''Chip'' or ''lumberjack'' to [[Queen Eloise]] to get the [[achievement]] [[Whistle-Blower]].

'''Note:''' You need to talk to [[Chip]] before talking to [[Queen Eloise]], or else she will just reply with ''How would you know?'' when you mention him.
