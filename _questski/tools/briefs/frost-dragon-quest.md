## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler|name=Frost Dragon Quest}}

===Method===

Note: You must have completed the [[Barbarian Test Quest]] before starting this quest.

Travel to [[Okolnir]], using the boat south of [[Svargrond]] ([[Buddel]]) ('''hi''', '''passage''', '''okolnir'''). Make sure you answer '''no''' to his first question if you are carrying a [[Dwarven Ring]].

''(WARNING: You might end up on the wrong island. If you do, keep asking to travel to [[Okolnir]] until you end up at the place depicted in the screenshot.)''

[[Image:Frost Dragons Quest 1.png|right]]

Walk up the ramp, and you'll encounter one to three [[Frost Dragon]]s.

{{Clear}}

[[Image:Frost Dragons Quest 2.png|right]]

Green dots are possible [[Frost Dragon]]s and purple dots are possible [[Frost Dragon Hatchling]]s. Now follow the red arrow to the next hole.

{{Clear}}

[[Image:Frost Dragons Quest 3.png|right]]

Once again, green dots are possible [[Frost Dragon]]s, and purple dots are possible [[Frost Dragon Hatchling]]s. As soon you step down, you will notice a hole. You might encounter one [[Frost Dragon Hatchling]] and possibly a lured [[Frost Dragon]].

[[Image:Frost Dragons Quest 4.png|right]]

Black dots are possible [[Frost Dragon]]s and purple dots are possible [[Frost Dragon Hatchling]]s. The blue dot is the quest box.

Now you have reached the hardest part. When you go down the hole, you will encounter at least one [[Frost Dragon]], probably with at least one [[Frost Dragon Hatchling]] and maybe another one to two [[Frost Dragon]]s. As soon as you have killed them, walk west as shown in the picture and go down the hole. Once down, walk west to find the rope-spot and a frost dragon. Kill it and go up.

'''Beware''': this last room will have five [[Frost Dragon]]s in it and at least 4 of them will target you as soon as you go up. Lower level mages should use [[Mana Shield]]. [[Energy Wall Rune|Energy Wall]] and [[Energy Bomb Rune]]s can be used to prevent too many from dealing melee damage to you. Pick up your reward in the chest north-west.
