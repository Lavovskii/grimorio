== Background ==
The Thornfire [[World Change]] was introduced in the [[Summer Update 2011]].<br />

The elves of Shadowthorn lived like normal elves until one day the bog god's temple arose. Many came to worship the bog god. Some did not follow this god, like the others did, and tried to convince the followers. Soon the conflict turned to violence and the outsider elves set the follower's houses on fire.<br />
These firestarters were contained and imprisoned in three prisons underground. The watchful eyes of overseers and the magic of the bog keep them imprisoned. They could only be freed if the power of the bog is removed.

NPC [[Pyro Peter]], who lives in [[Venore]] and walks around [COORD x=32954 y=32095 z=6 | here], knows about the condition of Shadowthorn. You can ask him about the current state in '''Shadowthorn''', or alternatively ask any of the [[Guide NPCs]] about the '''thornfire''' world change.

== To Release the Firestarters ==
Start at the entrance of Shadowthorn (at the blue flag on the picture below). Go just east and down the stairs in the tower.<br />
Locate the three prisons underground marked by red Xs:<br />

[[File:Thornfire.png]]

Each prison is guarded by an [[Elf Overseer]]. The only way into each prison cell are [[Wooden Bars]] which are covered in [[Mossy Wall|moss]], by the magic of the bog god.<br />
You must kill:
* '''All three overseers''' (they will not respawn until the next time Shadowthorn stops burning)
* <span style="color:red;font-weight:bold">Every single elf</span> in Shadowthorn (they will respawn, but they should all be killed ''at least'' once).
When you kill the bog god's followers his power will weaken and the moss covering the bars will recede.<br />
Once the moss is gone the Wooden Bars can be broken with any weapon, so that the firestarters can be freed (the bars will reform and can be re-broken, but all that matters is that the moss goes away).

After the moss is removed from all three prisons (by killing ''all'' elves) and the firestarters are freed, the next day (after [[Server Save]]) the town of Shadowthorn will be on fire!

== To Stop Shadowthorn from Burning ==
When the firestarters have escaped they will set the village of Shadowthorn on fire. Considering the magical nature of the flames only water from the bog god's temple, east of the entrance, can quench the flames.<br />
The [COORD x=33125 y=32147 z=7 | bog god's temple] is marked on the above map with a cross.
If you walk on the bog water you see the message<br />
'''The water of the bog seems slippery and tries to escape even your clothes.'''<br />
'''A normal bucket won't be able to hold the bog unless prepared with something caulking but also enduring such as embalming fluid'''<br />

First get an ordinary [[Bucket]], then use a [[Flask of Embalming Fluid]] on it to make a [[Prepared Bucket]], which is sealed so it can hold the slippery bog water.<br />
[[File:Bucket.gif]] '''+''' [[File:Flask of Embalming Fluid.gif]] '''=''' [[File:Prepared Bucket.gif]]<br />

Then use the Prepared Bucket on the [[Bog Water]]. This gives a [[Bucket of Bog Water]] which you will take around Shadowthorn and use on the flames.<br />
[[File:Prepared Bucket.gif]] '''+''' [[File:Bog Water.gif]] '''=''' [[File:Bucket of Bog Water.gif]]<br />

After each use you have to refill the bucket. You may consider making multiple prepared buckets so you can put out multiple fires without going back. Also consider checking the market for prepared buckets, as you might be able to find them for a cheaper price than embalming fluid.

'''Once you put out 500 thornfires, you will receive the achievement [[Firefighter]].'''

=== Thornfire and Crystal Wolves ===

There is a '''small''' (0.1-1%) chance that a [[Thornfire Wolf]] will spawn each time you extinguish a thornfire. Regardless of where the fire is being put out, the wolf will spawn west of the lake, [COORD x=33095 y=32159 z=7 | here]. 

When a [[Thornfire Wolf]] spawns, an orange message will be broadcast across all of Shadowthorn:

{{Sound|Unleash the flaming wolves and burn the house of the enemy!}}

If your object is taming the [[Crystal Wolf]], '''do not kill''' the Thornfire Wolves. Lure them to the bog god's temple and they will be transformed into [[Crystal Wolf|Crystal Wolves]] as soon as the stand on the bog water. Then they can be tamed with a [[Diapason]].

Players who want to kill the wolves for their [[Bestiary]] entry can use a trick to get one kill of each wolf with a single spawn. Start by using a weak attack on the [[Thornfire Wolf]] so it gets to red health. Lure it to the bog water, and as soon as the [[Crystal Wolf]] appears you must deal a final blow to the Thornfire Wolf (an attack rune or spell is recommended because [[Melee]] attacks may have a delay). If you do it correctly, the Thornfire Wolf will die, being counted for the bestiary, at the same time the Crystal Wolf spawns, allowing you to kill both. 

Once enough fires are put out the firestarters can be contained again and the city will return to normal at the next server save. Note, however, that the Fire will reset twice during the day, 8 and 16 hours after the [[Server Save]], in order to give other players the opportunity to put them out and face the creatures exclusive to the World Change.
