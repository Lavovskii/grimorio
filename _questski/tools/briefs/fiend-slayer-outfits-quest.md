== Addon 1 ==
[[File:Outfit Fiend Slayer Male Addon 1.gif|link=|Male Fiend Slayer Addon 1]]
[[File:Outfit Fiend Slayer Female Addon 1.gif|link=|Female Fiend Slayer Addon 1]]

== Addon 2 ==
[[File:Outfit Fiend Slayer Male Addon 2.gif|link=|Male Fiend Slayer Addon 2]]
[[File:Outfit Fiend Slayer Female Addon 2.gif|link=|Female Fiend Slayer Addon 2]]

Use a [[Demon Skull]] {{Ilink|Demon Skull}} and you will receive the second addon, this can be looted from [[Arbaziloth]], '''when killed on hard mode'''.

* Upon receiving both addons, you'll receive the achievement [[Fiend Slayer]].
