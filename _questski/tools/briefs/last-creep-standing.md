## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Last Creep Standing}}
'''This event starts on the 20th of each month (unless there is a [[World Quest]] active or about to be launched that same day) and runs until the 25th.'''

== Required Equipment ==
*[[Shapechanger]] (provided by [[Casper]]).
*[[Speed]]-boosting items such as [[Time Ring]]s and [[Boots of Haste]] are recommended, if not essential, for lower levels.
*A supply of [[Mana Potion]]s to cancel the [[Slime Puddle]]s' [[Paralysis]] is also recommended.

== Method ==
*There will be a new round every four hours (starting from [[Server Save]]) once the event has started. Every round will be announced fifteen minutes in advance by the message {{Message|type=server|The darashian journey will begin in 15 minutes!}}.<br /> 
[[Image:LCS_cast.png]]
*In order to participate you are required to go north of [[Darashia]] and down the stairs twice [COORD x=33262 y=32364 z=7 | here].<br />[[image:Shapeshifter map.png]]<br /> 
*This room contains [[Casper]], two [[Last Creep Standing (Book)|books]] containing event instructions, and a [[Zombie Dummy]], [[Nightstalker Dummy]], [[Pharaoh Dummy]], [[Carniphila Dummy]], [[Hydromancer Dummy]], [[Lost Soul Dummy]], [[Blightwalker Dummy]] and [[Mutated Rat Dummy]]. Ask Casper for a "[[Shapechanger|Shape changer]]" and you should receive one. This is the only one he'll ever give you, so don't lose it. It is advised to bind it to a hotkey in order to save time during the challenge. Stay where you are and wait until the event starts.<br /> 
[[File:Last_creep_standing_1.jpg]]
*When it does and you are teleported to the challenge area, use your [[Shapechanger]]. Doing this will disguise you as one of the following creatures:<br /> 
**a [[Lost Soul]] {{Ilink|Lost Soul}}<br /> 
**one of the unclothed [[Pharaohs]] {{Ilink|Thalas}}
**a [[Carniphila]] {{Ilink|Carniphila}}<br /> 
**a [[Quara Hydromancer]] {{Ilink|Quara Hydromancer}}<br /> 
**a [[Blightwalker]] {{Ilink|Blightwalker}}<br /> 
**a [[Mutated Rat]] {{Ilink|Mutated Rat}}<br /> 
**a [[Zombie]] {{Ilink|Zombie}}<br /> 
**a [[Nightstalker]] {{Ilink|Nightstalker}}<br /> 
*You now have to make your way to the teleporter that is associated with the creature you are disguised as. More of the dummies which you already saw in [[Casper]]'s room will indicate which teleporter belongs to which disguise.
[[File:Last_Creep_Standing_Creatures.jpg|600px]]
*Transforming tiles and the [[Paralysis]] inflicted on you by the several [[Slime Puddle]]s in the room can slow you down. [[Items]] and [[Spells]] that increase [[Speed]] are therefore highly recommended.<br /> 
[[File:Last_creep_standing_2.jpg]]
*Entering the right teleporter in time will transport you to a smaller room where you will have to wait a bit before the next cycle begins. Failing to do so means you can no longer participate in the current round.
*Seven more cycles will follow, each granting you less time to reach the right teleporter than the previous.
*Make it through all eight cycles to win this round for the entire server and be teleported into the reward room. A certain amount of the total of 30 rounds needs to be won in order for the server to win the entire event.<br /> 
[[Image:LCS_Prize_room.png]] [[Image:LCS_proom.png]]<br />

== Rewards ==
*[[Shapeshifter Ring]] (from the chest located in the reward room);
*[[Reward Box]] (from [[Casper]] upstairs by saying ''reward''), which can contain some [[Chameleon Rune]]s or a [[Time Ring]].

Anyone who manages to do every disguise with the [[Shapechanger]] will also receive the [[Master Shapeshifter]] [[achievement]] during the event, and any server that wins enough rounds will get a temporary 20% increase in [[respawn]] rates.
