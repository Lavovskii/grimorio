## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Isle of the Mists Quest}}

Go to [[Plains of Havoc]] and enter the [[Magic Forcefield]] located [COORD x=32831 y=32295 z=7 | here], near some [[Orc]]s. You may find [[Giant Spider]]s on the way. You will be teleported to the [[Isle of the Mists]], there you will find a [[Box]] and a [[Drawer]] on the ground floor, containing 2 [[Small Emerald]]s and a [[Book]]. Some times, [[Fernfang]] may spawn in the upper floor.
