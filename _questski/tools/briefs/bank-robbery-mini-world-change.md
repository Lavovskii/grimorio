== Method ==
Occasionally, thieves will rob a [[bank]] in Tibia, prompting the [[Banker NPCs|bankers]] in [[Ab'Dendriel]], [[Carlin]], [[Thais]], or [[Venore]] to request adventurers for help. When the event begins, the [[Towncryer]] will announce the robbery, and the [[Adventurers' Guild|Adventurer’s Board]] in the [[Adventurers' Guild]] will also mention it.

As players learn a bank has been robbed, they can immediately check which of the four cities have been affected, then right after look for thief. Once you find and defeat the thief, retrieve the {{Ilink|Bag With Stolen Gold}} [[Bag With Stolen Gold]] and return to the corresponding banker to give them the money back.

:''Note that players may loot more than one bag during [[Double Loot Events]] or when using the {{Ilink|Wealth Duplex}} [[Wealth Duplex]] potion.

The bag return must be done before the next [[Server Save]], otherwise the event is terminated and the bag disappears from your inventory. Additionally, the bank services in the affected city will be unavailable until the bag is returned.

It is unknown how often this event can happen or what could affect its occurrence, however it is known that the event has a chance to happen hourly, based on the [[Server Save#Server Boot|Server Boot]] time. If the robbery has not yet happened or the gold has already been returned, the [[Towncryer]] and [[Adventurers' Guild|Adventurer’s Board]] will not mention the event.

Returning the [[Bag With Stolen Gold|stolen goods]] rewards the [[File:Achievement_Grade_Symbol.gif]] [[Honest Finder]] [[achievement]] and 10 [[Platinum Coin]]s. By returning five bags, players earn the achievement [[File:Achievement_Grade_Symbol.gif]] [[Goldhunter]].

:''In case two or more bags were looted during an event, different players can return the bags to the banker if they return them at the same time. That means players must sync their dialogue with the NPC and ideally time the messages. By doing so the achievement progress for all involved players will advance by one.
