## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Vampire Hunter Quest}}

=== Requirements ===
* Completed missions 1-3 from [[The Inquisition Quest]];
* 100 [[Vampire Lord Token]]s.

=== Notes ===
* You need to give [[Vampire Lord Token]]s to [[Storkus]] in order to advance in rank. All tokens required to advance to a higher rank need to be given at once.
* You don't have to slay the [[Vampire Lords]] yourself to get their tokens, so you can also just buy them from other players.
