== Method ==
A small [[Orc Encampment]] (a handful of [[Orc Warrior]]s outside the palisade, and inside two [[Orc Berserker]]s, an [[Orc Leader]] and three [[Orc Warlord]]s) can be found:
*north of the [[Jakundaf Desert]] ([COORD x=32671 y=32049 z=7 | here])
*north of [[Carlin]] ([COORD x=32341 y=31731 z=7 | here])
*east of the [[Femor Hills]] ([COORD x=32622 y=31827 z=7 | here])

After killing all of the [[orcs]] inside the camp, [[Bibby Bloodbath]] will spawn. Kill her and you will be rewarded with the [[achievement]] [[Bibby's Bloodbath]].

You can kill Bibby every '''24 hours''', however, if you visit her again before your cooldown is over it will reset, so make sure to only go up the stairs after 24 hours have past since your last encounter.
