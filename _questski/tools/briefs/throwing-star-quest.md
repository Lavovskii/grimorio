## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Throwing Star Quest}}

==Required Equipment==
*[[Pick]]
*[[Rope]]
*Recommended [[Antidote]], [[Antidote Rune]], or [[Destroy Field]] for [[Poison Field]]s

==Method==
* Go to the [[Ancient Temple]], north of [[Thais]] ([COORD x=32356 y=32171 z=7 | here]).
* Go down two levels, and head as far north as you can go.
* Go down to the level with the [[Rotworm]]s.
* Follow this path to the east and south, and go down the ladder:
[[Image:Route Mintwallin 1.png|400px]]

* On this level, there will be a lot of [[Poison Field]]s. You can either run through them and then use [[Antidote]], or you can use [[Destroy Field]] to clear a path.
* Go down another level.
* Follow this path to the east and north. Be careful of [[Minotaur]]s. Go down the hole that is circled on the map. You will face a couple [[Cyclops|Cyclopes]] down this hole:
[[Image:Route Mintwallin 2.png]]

* Follow this path around to the underground park and use the [[Pick]] where indicated:
[[Image:Throwing Star Quest Map 03.png]]

* The quest box will be just south-east of the ladder.
