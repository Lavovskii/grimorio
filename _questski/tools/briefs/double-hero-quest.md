## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Double Hero Quest}}
===Required Equipment===
* [[Rope]]

===Method===
First of all, you have to go to the Edron Cave, which is here, be prepared to face some [[Hunter]]s and [[Wild Warrior]]s outside:
 
[[Image:TripleUHRune2.jpg]]

When you enter the undergrounds, you have to kill some [[Wild Warrior]]s.
Go to the next hole.

[[Image:TripleUHRune3.jpg]]

Now you have to kill some [[Demon Skeleton|Demon Skeletons]], [[Wild Warrior|Wild Warriors]], 1 [[Priestess]] and [[Bonelord]]s. Go with that way, and go downstairs:

[[Image:TripleUHRune4.jpg]]

Now you have to go with that way, killing some [[Monk|Monks]], [[Demon Skeleton]]s and [[Wild Warrior|Wild Warriors]]. Go downstairs:

[[Image:TripleUHRune5.jpg]]

Kill the 3 [[Dragon]]s and enter that hole:

[[Image:TripleUHRune6.jpg]]

Here, you will face 1 [[Hero]] and 2 or 3 [[monk|Monks]].

[[Image:Double_Hero.png]]

Down here you'll meet 2-3 [[Priestess]]es and 2 [[monk|Monks]].

[[Image:Double Hero Quest 6.png]]

Travel south and once you start to get close to the room begin to move slowly. In the room there are 
*2 [[Hero]]es  
*1 [[Priestess]]
*2 [[Demon Skeleton]]s
*1 [[Orc Shaman]]

In the south of the room there are 2 chests open both to recieve the [[Club Ring]] and the [[Red Gem]].
