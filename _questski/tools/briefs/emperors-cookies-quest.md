## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Emperor's Cookies Quest}}
===Required Equipment===
None, (Maybe a [[rope]])

===Method===

*First you need to go to [[Emperor Kruzak]]'s chambers in his private room, to the north, get [[Key 3800]] which can be found at the end of the west "secret passage" exit, in a [[Chest (Quest)]] [COORD x=32605 y=31909 z=3 | here].

*Do not take the exit route down the hole, but go back to the king's room; you have to open the door of the small room in the emperor's chamber with the [[Key 3800|key]]. In the chest in the room you opened will be 7 and 20 [[Cookie]]s and [[Key 3801]].

*To get to the ([[Key 3802|3802]]) you can go in 2 ways:
*# You can go down several levels from the "secret passage" until you come out under [[Kazordoon]] (ground level). To get to the next key you need a [[rope]] and have to go to the barracks. How to get to Kazordoon entrance can be seen [[Route: Kazordoon|here]].
*# (faster way) Go back to the barracks.

*When at the barracks ([COORD x=32601 y=31927 z=6 | here]) you can open the door with [[Key 3801]], in the [[Chest (Quest)]] will be the key, [[Key 3802]], this key is needed to open [[Dwacatra]] Prison and a specific room in the [[Dwarf Mines]], the [[Mine Hub]].
