## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name= Inukaya Quest}} 

Walk into the [[dog sled]] just east of where you enter from [[Svargrond]] to travel from [[Nibelor]] to [[Inukaya]]. You must do the [[the Ice Islands Quest|Ice Islands Quest]] up to the mission 'Nibelor 5: Cure the Dogs' to be able to use the sled. 
Walk to the Chakoya villiage, and go in the igloo marked on the following map:<br />
[MINIMAP x=32482 y=31108 z=7 marks=none]

Then, search the barrel marked on this map for a fur bag with your rewards:<br />
[[Image:Chakoya2.gif]]

*Note: In order to access [[Inukaya]] and leave, you must carry one piece of [[ham]] with you for each trip, otherwise the dogs won't take you. Also, they will only take you if you have completed the five first missions of the [[Ice Islands Quest]].
