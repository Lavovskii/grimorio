== Method ==
This MWC revolves around the war between [[Hunters]] and [[Dworcs]] over some holy grounds in [[Tiquanda]], [COORD x=32900 y=32792 z=7 | here]. One day, you might find [[Hunter]]s and [[Poacher]]s roaming here in a [[Hunter Camp]], but on another the [[Dworcs]] might be on the winning hand and dominating the grounds in a [[Dworc Camp]]. Depending on who is dominating at the moment you might also encounter the boss of that clan, namely [[Arthom the Hunter]] or [[Oodok Witchmaster]].
