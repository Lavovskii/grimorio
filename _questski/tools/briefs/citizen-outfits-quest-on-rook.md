== Outfit ==
* All players have this outfit available. Only the addons are part of this quest.

== Feather Hat ==
[[Image:Outfit Citizen Male Addon 2.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit Citizen Female Addon 2.gif]]
=== Required Equipment ===
* 100 [[Chicken Feather]]s
* 50 [[Honeycomb]]s
* 1 [[Legion Helmet]]

=== Method ===
* Both males and females need to visit [[Norma]] ([COORD x=32097 y=32180 z=7 | here]) at her shop right north the Academy.
* [[Norma]] will make the hat for you, but she needs certain materials first.
* Give her the items you have collected, and she will make the hat for you.
* Collect [[Chicken Feather]]s by killing chickens.
* Get a [[Legion Helmet|legion helmet]] from [[Seymour]] in the Rookgaard academy (you will need a [[present]] to change for [[Legion Helmet|legion helmet]]).

== Backpack ==
[[Image:Outfit Citizen Male Addon 1.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit Citizen Female Addon 1.gif]]
=== Required Equipment ===
* 100 [[Minotaur Leather]]s

=== Method ===
* Go to [[Amber]] ([COORD x=32103 y=32183 z=8 | here]) in the Resting Room under the Academy.
* Ask about her "outfit" (the backpack). She will tell you it is not for sale, but she can make one for you if you bring the leather that she needs.
* Give her the [[Minotaur Leather]]s you collected.
* Come back to her in two hours and she will give you the backpack addon.
