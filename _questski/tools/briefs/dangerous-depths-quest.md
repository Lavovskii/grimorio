## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Dangerous Depths Quest}}

= Method =

This quest will give you access to three new Warzones. Unlike the [[Bigfoot's Burden Quest]] quest, you do not have to do them in order and can start all tasks from the beginning. In order to get access to the Warzones, you must help the Gnomes, Dwarves and Scouts, each group being responsible for [[Warzone 4]], [[Warzone 5]] and [[Warzone 6]], respectively. You can repeat the tasks every 20 hours, and you can also deliver [[Suspicious Device]]s in exchange for one task point with the NPC of your choice. You need 15 points to be granted access to the Warzones (10 if you have already defeated the bosses of the [[Bigfoot's Burden Quest]]: Warzones [[Warzone 1|1]], [[Warzone 2|2]] and [[Warzone 3|Warzone 3]]).

In addition to the point, you will also be rewarded with a [[Surprise Jar]] after finishing a task. Some tasks have an extra objective, if you also complete those you will receive an additional point and [[Surprise Jar]].

You can check your current amount of points by asking any of the task NPCs ([[Gnomus]], [[Klom Stonecutter]] or [[Lardoc Bashsmite]]) about '''Gnomes''', '''Dwarves''' and '''Scouts'''.

To enter the Warzones, you have to destroy [[Fragile Lava Funnel]]s, 3 of these can be found in 3 different areas (one for each warzone) that can be accessed only if you have enough points for the corresponding faction. After destroying a funnel your points will be deducted and when you enter the warzone for the first time you have 8 hours of access, so if you want to enter any other time after that time lapse you will have to destroy another funnel.

The bosses have a 4-hour cooldown, so they can be killed a maximum of 3 times during the 8-hour access for their corresponding warzone (for the last time you will have to enter the warzone before your access expires and wait for the boss cooldown to end). Here's how the cooldown works:

* If the boss is defeated successfully, the cooldown starts when the boss dies.
* If a team enters the boss room but exits without killing the boss, the cooldown starts. It hasn't been confirmed whether it starts when leaving the room (as an individual cooldown), or when the last person leaves the room (as a team-wide cooldown), or some other mechanic.
* If a team enters the boss room, doesn't kill the boss, and waits to get kicked from the boss room due to time running out (after ~17 minutes), there is no cooldown, and the team can immediately enter the boss teleport to try again.

There is no lever and thus no limit to the number of players that can participate in the fight.

Any fire damage will heal the bosses, so mages should avoid any spells of this element and paladins should not summon their [[Emberwing]]s.

== Daily Tasks ==

=== Gnomes (Warzone 4) ===

Find the NPC [[Gnomus]] [COORD x=33750 y=32167 z=14 | north] of the [[Gnome Deep Hub]]'s entrance and offer him your '''help''', he has three different tasks you can take.

'''Warning''': some of the [[Lava]] around this dungeon is walkable, but it will cause you 500 [[Fire Damage]] when stepping on it. One or two steps are enough to kill mages and paladins. Fire protection equipment can be used to lower the damage taken, but [[Firewalker Boots]] will not be effective. It is possible to avoid all damage-dealing lava when doing the tasks, but one wrong step can be fatal.<br />
'''Warning''': on some parts of the cave there is a constant danger of rocks falling from the ceiling when a [[Lost Gnome]] is nearby. These will cause 1500 [[Physical Damage]]! If you do not have this amount of hitpoint it's extremely recommended to use [[Magic Shield]].

==== Ordnance ====

[[Gnomus]] wants your help rescuing some [[Lost Gnome]]s that are bringing new ordnance to the base, as well as their pack animals which are their companions. Go to the eastern most part of the hub and go down [COORD x=33838 y=32178 z=14 | here], then up the next ramp. You should find one [[Lost Gnome]] and up to 3 [[Gnome Pack Crawler]]s there. The [[Lost Gnome]] will follow you, which means you can just walk back to the entrance while protecting it from the [[Lava Lurker]]s around. The [[Gnome Pack Crawler]]s will run away from you, so you have to guide their path by going to the opposite direction you want them to go.

==== Gnomal Warming Measurements ====

[[File:Dangerous Depths Measuring.png|right]]
[[Gnomus]] is interested in monitoring the temperature around this area. Grab a [[Gnome Trignometre]] from the [[Old Machine]] near the boxes just nort-east of him and go to the tasks area on the eastern part of the hub. You have to measure the temperature of 5 spots, all of which are pools of [[Lava]]. If you go to all of them from the center room (as shown to the right), you can avoid stepping on all lava and their damage. You will not be able to avoid the rockfall area (marked with the skull) though, so take care when walking around that area.

==== Uncharted Territory ====

Help the Gnomes getting information on ancient structures around the [[Gnome Deep Hub]]. Take a [[Gnome Chart]] from the [[Torn Out Page]] next to him on top of a [[Crate]]. You have to chart at least 3 structures, and charting all 6 of them will give you the right to bonus rewards. There are 3 structures (''Lost Ruin'', ''Old Gate'', ''The Gaze'') on the cave where you also do the Ordnance and Measurements missions, as seen on the image above. There are two structures (''Outpost'' and ''Bastion'') on the Scouts tasks area, which are also part of the '''Explosive Growth''' task. The final structure (''Broken Tower'') is located at the southern most part of the Dwarves area.

=== Dwarves (Warzone 5) ===

Find the NPC [[Klom Stonecutter]] [COORD x=33777 y=32205 z=14 | south-east] of the [[Gnome Deep Hub]]'s entrance and offer him your '''help''', he has two different tasks you can take.

===== Home Improvement =====

You help is required to kill 20 [[Lost Exile]]s in the dungeon nearby. These creatures, however, have built [[Makeshift Home]]s around the cave and will flee there before dying (they vanish and you don't get loot, experience of the task point). Hence, in order to force them to fight you, you must first destroy the [[Makeshift Home]]s nearby. If there are no Homes close to the [[Lost Exile]], it will fight you to death. On the second image below you can check the Home's radius of action. In some areas (for example north-east), destroying just one home is enough to create a zone where you can kill the [[Lost Exile]]s. Luring them to the entrance or the southernmost areas is also a good strategy.

This task also has a bonus objective. After destroying a [[Makeshift Home]], you can use its rubble to try and make a prisoner, a [[Captured Dwarf]]. If you succeed, you must then lure it out of the dungeon. The [[Captured Dwarf]] is immune to damage and will follow you, but you must escort it back within a time limit or it will escape your grasp. You have to capture 3 prisoners to receive the bonus rewards for this task.

==== Subterranean Life ====

To complete this task, you simply have to go to the task area and kill 50 [[Diremaw]]s and [[Deepworm]]s, so it's advised to do this while also doing the [[Dangerous Depths Quest/Spoiler#Home Improvement|Home Improvement]] task. Killing these creatures on other areas (such as the Warzone 6 task area) also counts, so make sure you take this task before doing those. There is no bonus objective.

=== Scouts (Warzone 6) ===

Find the NPC [[Lardoc Bashsmite]] [COORD x=33839 y=32122 z=14 | north-east] of the [[Gnome Deep Hub]]'s entrance and talk to him about his '''work''', he has two different tasks you can take.

'''Warning''': some of the [[Lava]] around this dungeon is walkable, but it will cause you 500 [[Fire Damage]] when stepping on it. One or two steps are enough to kill mages and paladins. Fire protection equipment can be used to lower the damage taken, but [[Firewalker Boots]] will not be effective. It is possible to avoid all damage-dealing lava when doing the tasks, but one wrong step can be fatal.

==== Explosive Growth ====

You have to drag [[Explosive Barrel (Quest)|Explosive Barrels]] to burn down sponges that serve as food for the [[Diremaw]]s. You have to explode at least 3 sponge areas out of a total of 5. Go down the ramp north of [[Lardoc Bashsmite]] (be careful with the [[Lava]]) and then go up again. Pull the lever to take a Barrel out of the explosive barrel depot, then drag it to one of the 5 locations. With the barrel inside, use it to set it on fire and it will explode after 5 seconds. If you explode barrels on all 5 locations you will also get bonus rewards.

Note: The [[Explosive Barrel (Quest)|Explosive Barrels]] can deal 1000 [[Fire Damage]] when exploding if you stand too close (however, the radius of danger is much smaller than the explosion animation might suggest).

==== Pesticide ====

Another way to fight the [[Diremaw]] infestation is by preventing their eggs from hatching. These creatures lay their eggs on half-decaying corpses of their own siblings. Pick up [[Gnomish Pesticides]] inside a [[Chest]] near [[Lardoc Bashsmite]] and go into the cave. You have to use the pesticide on 20 dead [[Diremaw]]s, but it will only work 10 seconds after the creature has been killed (as soon as it has become movable). When using the pesticides, an angry [[Diremaw]] might appear to protect the eggs.

Once you have the required number of points with each NPC you are eligible to enter their respective Warzones. Before being able to walk through the teleport, however, you have to do one final mission: destroy a [[Fragile Lava Funnel]] that is being used by Those Below to flood the surround areas. Enter the energy portal near the task area and destroy one of the three funnels to gain access to the Warzone.

'''Note'''¹: Every player must do this for each Warzone in order to receive access to it.<br />
'''Note'''²: Breaking the Lava machine grants you access to the Warzone, and this access will not expire until you enter the Warzone. This means that the players can (and should) do this part before the time scheduled with the team, otherwise there will be some waiting time until everyone has destroyed the lava pumps.<br />
'''Note'''³: After walking through the Warzone's teleport, you'll have 8 hours of access before your task points are deducted and you lose access (you can leave and re-enter as many times as you like). If your 8 hours run out while you are inside the Warzone, you will not be kicked out, but should you leave it won't be possible to return unless you break the pump again and spend more task points.

Inside the Warzone area you'll find large blue crystals that you must mine (use them) to obtain [[Heavy Crystal Fragment]]s. There are small, medium and large crystals, and you have a bigger chance to mine crystals from the larger ones. Your team needs 30 [[Heavy Crystal Fragment]]s to access the boss room.

'''Note''': The 3 Warzones bosses always drop 5 [[Heavy Crystal Fragment]]s for each player involved in the fight. Thus, if your team is going from one boss to the next, it's wise to pick these up from the boss corpse and avoid the mining on the following Warzones. Also, even though the crystals vanish after 24 hours, they can be kept inside the depot to be used at the first Warzone the next time the team decides to do them.

=== Warzone 4: The Baron from Below ===

[[File:Dangerous Depths Quest - Warzone 4|right|300px]]
After gathering all 30 [[Heavy Crystal Fragment]]s, find the [[Hollow Geode]] south-west of the dungeon, [COORD x=33461 y=32268 z=15 | here]. Use the [[Heavy Crystal Fragment]]s on it, and the [[Stalagmites]] on the ground next to into will become a teleport. '''Attention''': the teleport will only be active for 8 seconds, after which it will disappear and it will not be possible to open it again as long as there's a player inside the boss room. Make sure your whole team is ready and standing by.

Inside the room, you will find [[The Baron from Below]]. Start the fight by attacking the boss. After some seconds, it will change into [[The Hungry Baron from Below]] and teleport itself to the center of the room (meaning trapping it on corners is futile), and an [[Organic Matter]] will spawn. At this moment, the whole team should kill the [[Organic Matter]] as fast as possible, otherwise [[The Hungry Baron from Below]] will devour it and recover half of its maximum health. When the [[Organic Matter]] is destroyed, 4 [[Aggressive Matter]]s will spawn. Kill them fast as well (AoE recommended) so you can focus back on [[The Baron from Below]]. Note that you'll have to target the boss again after every shape change.

Watch out for the 4 [[Lava Hole]]s in this room as they will become active (just one at the time) for about 4 seconds and explode dealing ~1500 hp fire damage, it has the same area as a [[Great Fireball Rune]].

Inside the room, you'll also find [[Aggressive Lava]]s which use an area fire spell on themselves. These must be kept far from the boss, because it gets healed by [[Fire Damage]]. This also means that other Fire spells and runes (such as [[Hell's Core]] and [[Great Fireball Rune]]) shouldn't be used, as well as [[Emberwing]] which deals Fire Damage.

After killing the boss, exit by the teleporter to a room with his Lava Pump. Destroy (use) it to obtain the [[Buried the Baron]] achievement.

=== Warzone 5: The Count of the Core ===

[[File:Dangerous Depths Quest - Warzone 5|right|300 px]]
After gathering all 30 [[Heavy Crystal Fragment]]s, find the [[Hollow Geode]] north-east of the dungeon, [COORD x=33324 y=32109 z=15 | here]. Use the [[Heavy Crystal Fragment]]s on it, and the [[Stalagmites]] on the ground next to into will become a teleport. '''Attention''': the teleport will only be active for 8 seconds, after which it will disappear and it will not be possible to open it again as long as there's a player inside the boss room. Make sure your whole team is ready and standing by.

[[File:Glowing Obsidian Pipes.gif|right]]
Inside the room, you will find [[The Count of the Core]]. [[The Count of the Core]] is also healed by [[Fire Damage]], and inside its room there are 5 sets of aligned [[Obsidian Pipes]] that will throw a Fire Wave towards the other side of the room, healing the boss and dealing massive damage. The easiest way to avoid this healing is to block of the [[Obsidian Pipes]] by throwing [[Magic Wall Rune]]s in front of them. Using this strategy, the boss can be trapped on the south-east corner of the room and one or two team members can be responsible for renewing the Magic Wall protection when the Pipes are about to fire (they glow for a few seconds before that).

The team must also watch out for [[Snail Slime]]s which will constantly spawn as the boss gets hit. They should be killed far from the boss, otherwise [[The Count of the Core]] will also be healed upon their death and the players will take about 1000 damage upon each Slime death. The easiest way to do that is to have a second blocker use [[Challenge]] on them and keep them away, and then the team can attack the Slimes from distance with [[Avalanche Rune]]s.

Finally, you will have to deal with [[Ember Beast]]s as well. Those apparently make [[The Count of the Core]] stronger upon dying, regardless of them being close to the boss or not ({{Message|type=sound|The count absorbs the power of the beast and becomes stronger}}). Thus, you can try to block and avoid damaging them, but they will likely die when the team attacks the [[Snail Slime]]s, though this shouldn't be a problem.

After killing the boss, exit by the teleporter to a room with his Lava Pump. Destroy (use) it to obtain the [[His Days are Counted]] [[achievement]].

=== Warzone 6: The Baron From Below ===

[[File:Dangerous Depths Quest - Warzone 6|right|300 px]]
After gathering all 30 [[Heavy Crystal Fragment]]s, find the [[Hollow Geode]] at the center of the dungeon, [COORD x=33275 y=32315 z=15 | here]. Use the [[Heavy Crystal Fragment]]s on it, and the [[Stalagmites]] on the ground next to into will become a teleport. '''Attention''': the teleport will only be active for 8 seconds, after which it will disappear and it will not be possible to open it again as long as there's a player inside the boss room. Make sure your whole team is ready and standing by.

Inside the room, you will find [[The Duke Of The Depths]]. During this battle the duke will turn invincible ([[The Fire Empowered Duke]]) and [[Fiery Heart (Creature)|Fiery Hearts]] will appear in 5 fixed at the center of the room. The team must kill all of them quickly will make the boss vulnerable again. If the Fiery Hearts aren't killed fast enough they will become a [[Fiery Blood]]s, very strong Fire Elementals that you will also have to kill. One possible strategy is to split the mages of the team in two groups which should take turns in using their ultimate spells (Energy/Ice) to hit all [[Fiery Blood]]s and destroy them quickly, while the other shooters attack them with AoE spells and runes.

The boss can be trapped and blocked by only 1 knight in the east of the room near the teleport, but this blocker should be a high level because during the time [[The Duke Of The Depths]] becomes invincible it also becomes much stronger, dealing combos of more than 4000 hp, so at least two Druids should focus their healing on the main blocker. The Duke will use its strong waves when its attacked while invulnerable, so this should be avoided at all costs. Other knights in the team can help blocking the [[Aggressive Lava]]s that will be spawning in the room during the battle. Beware that the boss' strong Beam and Wave have a large area, so all players should mind their positioning to avoid taking this high damage.

''Note:'' The faster your team gets rid of the fiery hearts, the longer the boss will be vulnerable until summoning them again.

After killing the boss, make sure you destroy his Lava Pump to obtain the [[Duked It Out]] achievement.

Once you defeated [[The Baron From Below]], [[The Count of the Core]], [[The Duke Of The Depths]], and destroyed their lava pumps you will automatically gain access to the [[Powerful Featherweight]] imbuement. You will also receive the achievement [[Death in the Depths]] for defeating all three bosses.

After defeating all 3 Warzones bosses, you will have the chance to battle the [[Ancient Spawn of Morgathla]]. The recommended team size is between 15 and 30 players, depending on the team strength. To start the fight, you will need the three pieces of a {{Ilink|Strange Mallet}}[[Strange Mallet]]:

* {{Ilink|Mallet Head}}[[Mallet Head]], dropped by [[The Baron from Below]] during [[Warzone 4]];
* {{Ilink|Mallet Handle}}[[Mallet Handle]], dropped by [[The Count of the Core]] during [[Warzone 5]];
* {{Ilink|Mallet Pommel}}[[Mallet Pommel]], dropped by [[The Duke of the Depths]] during [[Warzone 6]].

Note that only one player needs the Mallet for the whole team. With the pieces in hand, use the [[Huge Red Crystal]] on the floor just under [[Gnomus]], [COORD x=33749 y=32160 z=15 | here], to be teleported to a room with [[NPC]] [[Gnominimus]]. The player in possession of the Mallet pieces should talk to the NPC and have him build you a complete [[Strange Mallet]].

[[File:Dangerous Depths Quest - Ancient Spawn of Morgathla|right|300 px]]
To open the entrance to the boss, the player who has the [[Strange Mallet]] must use it on the [[Gong]] and you will see the message {{Message|type=sound|BOOOOOOOONG!}} ('''Attention''': the [[Strange Mallet]] will break and disappear once used, so make sure your team is ready to go before doing so). The stone next to it will turn into a [[Magic Forcefield]] and all players should enter it quickly. Similarly to the other Warzone bosses, this teleport will only stay open for a short period (14 seconds), and all players must enter it quickly.

This battle happens in 4 stages, each one in a different room. Despite that, all stages consists of pretty much the same thing with increasing difficulty. Initially, many [[Burrowing Beetle]]s will spawn in the room. They are much stronger than regular [[Scarabs]] but can be killed quite easily (they do deal considerable damage, so watch your health and mana). The recommended strategy here is that the team positions in one of the corners of the room in a way that the most vulnerable players (lower level paladins and mages) can be protected (see the image gallery below). On the last room, [[Wild Growth Rune]]s can be used to also protect the team due to the high amount of beetles.

[[File:Ancient Spawn of Morgathla.gif|left]] Once the [[Burrowing Beetle]]s stop spawning, the [[Ancient Spawn of Morgathla]] will appear. Initially, the boss will be immune and heal if attacked. In order to make it vulnerable, lure the boss onto one of the crystals inside the room while they are shining in a darker blue color. After doing that, the boss will start taking damage again. Mages can use [[Sudden Death Rune]]s and everyone should focus only on the boss.

During the first 3 rooms, you have to attack Morgathla until it flees to the next room, and on the last room it will finally die. Only damage done to it on this last stage will count towards [[Bosstiary]].

[[File:Glowing Obsidian Pipes.gif|right]]
Note¹: Inside all rooms there will be [[Sulphur Geysir]]e which will constantly deal [[Earth Damage]] around them (the area increases every turn) and can hit up to 1500, and from the second room on there will also be [[Obsidian Pipes]] that, once they start [[Glowing Obsidian Pipes|glowing]], explode dealing 2500 [[Fire Damage]]. This damage, combo'ed with the damage from the boss, can instantly deal over 5000 damage, making the fight dangerous.

After defeating the boss, enter the teleport to the north (be careful if you linger in the boss room because the Geysers keep exploding). Open the chest to receive the [[Scourge of Scarabs]] [[achievement]]. There are three different reward rooms:

* Left room: Most of the team will be sent to this room. The rewards are: a [[Bag]] with 5 [[Platinum Coin]]s, 1 [[Gnomish Supply Package]], 1 [[Rusted Armor]] and 1 [[Piggy Bank]].
* Center room: Three players will be sent here. The rewards are: a [[Bag]] with 50 [[Platinum Coin]]s, 2 [[Gnomish Supply Package]], 1 [[Slightly Rusted Armor]] and 1 [[Piggy Bank]].
* Right room: One player will be sent here. The rewards are: a [[Bag]] with 2 [[Crystal Coin|Crystal Coins]], 4 [[Gnomish Supply Package]], 1 [[Slightly Rusted Armor]], 1 [[Slightly Rusted Legs]] and 1 [[Piggy Bank]].
