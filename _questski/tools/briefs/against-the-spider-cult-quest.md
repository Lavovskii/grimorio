## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Against the Spider Cult Quest}}

==Required Equipment==
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Rope]]
* A melee [[weapon]] to destroy the spider webs

==Method==
Go to [[Daniel Steelsoul]] and ask him for a '''mission''' (and accept it with '''yes'''). Your mission is to infiltrate the [[Edron Orc Cave]] and destroy 4 [[Spider Egg]]s.

In order to reach the [[Edron Orc Cave]], go to the [[Edron Flats]], which is just south of the [[Edron Castle]], and find a [[Sewer Grate]]. Follow the sewer to the surface on the other side.

On the surface you will face [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Orc]]s, [[Orc Spearman|Orc Spearmen]] and a few [[Orc Warrior]]s. Go south and find the entrance to the [[Edron Orc Cave]] ([COORD x=33173 y=31900 z=7 | here]). Check your minimap if you can't find it; it is concealed beneath a mountain.

<gallery mode="nolines">
File:Edron Orc Cave Sewer Grate.png
File:Edron Orc Cave Ramp.png
</gallery>

On the first floor of the cave you will face the same monsters but with more [[Orc Warrior]]s. When you enter, your path may be blocked by a rock. In this case, use the [[Lever]] to remove the rock. Fight your way to the south end of the floor and go down the hole.

<gallery mode="nolines">
File:Edron Orc Cave Lever.png
File:Dark Armor Quest Map 01.jpg
</gallery>

On the next floor you will face the same monsters and maybe 1-2 lured [[Orc Berserker]]s. Go to the west until you find a hole. Instead of going down the hole, go north and then east in the first room. Go down the hole in this room.

<gallery mode="nolines">
File:Dark Armor Quest Map 02.jpg
File:Against the Spider Cult Hole.png
</gallery>

As soon as you drop down the hole you will be surrounded by several [[Orc Shaman]]s and their summons. Clear the room and head west, turning north when you can and follow the path north across [[Black Marble Floor]]. Drop down the hole.

'''Tip''': use area attack runes like [[Great Fireball]] to clear the [[Orc Shaman]]s and their summons.

<gallery mode="nolines">
File:Dark Armor Quest Map 03.jpg
</gallery>

You will be greeted by many [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Orc Shaman]]s and even [[Orc Leader]]s. Go north and you will find a large pit with [[Giant Spider]] in it. Go down the ramp on the north-east side of the pit to enter, and kill the [[Giant Spider]]. Now use a melee [[weapon]] or a [[Firebug]] on the [[Spider Webs]] on the wall to destroy them and open a path to the [[Spider Egg]]s. '''Use''' the [[Spider Egg]]s to destroy them. When you destroy them, a [[Giant Spider]] will spawn a fair distance behind you. Kill the giant spider and proceed to destroy the other spider eggs. Destroy all 4 eggs and your mission is done. If you are not alone, you will have to wait for the [[Spider Egg]]s to regenerate as there are only enough eggs for 1 player.

<gallery mode="nolines">
File:Dark Armor Quest Map 04.jpg
File:Against the Spider Cult Web.png
File:Against the Spider Cult Egg.png
</gallery>

You can exit the cave now, or you can do the other quests that are in the same cave. These quests are:
* [[Dark Armor Quest]]
* [[Barbarian Axe Quest]]
* [[Berserker Treasure Quest]]
* [[Poison Daggers Quest]]
* [[Shaman Treasure Quest]]

When done, talk to [[Daniel Steelsoul]] about your '''mission''' and he will reward you.
