== Method ==
[[Noodles]], who lives in the [[King Tibianus|King]]'s throneroom sometimes disappears. Go to [[King Tibianus]] in the [[Rain Castle]] and ask him about [[Noodles]]. He will then hand you a [[Dog Collar]] to bring his dog back. [[Noodles]] has a '''very large''' walking area that stretches over all of the [[Thais|Thaian]] peninsula. The quicker you go look for him after [[server save]], the more likely you are to find him. Known places where he spawns at [[server save]] are:

* Inside the kitchen of his home castle, [COORD x=32313 y=32171 z=7 | here].
* Around his home castle, [COORD x=32314 y=32184 z=7 | here].
* North west of [[Thais]], just behind the Castle's river, [COORD x=32284 y=32165 z=7 | here].
* North west of [[Thais]], [COORD x=32224 y=32116 z=7 | here].
* West of [[Greenshore]], around [COORD x=32237 y=32070 z=7 | here].
* North-west of [[Greenshore]], around [COORD x=32221 y=32030 z=7 | here].
* South of [[Thais]], near the [[Thais Wolf Dungeon|Wolf Dungeon]], [COORD x=32362 y=32326 z=7 | here].
* South of [[Thais]], near the [[Minotaur Camp]], [COORD x=32287 y=32402 z=7 | here].
* The southern peninsula with the [[Cyclops Camp]], [COORD x=32402 y=32386 z=7 | here] or [COORD x=32429 y=32391 z=7 | here].
* Near the [[White Flower Temple]], around [COORD x=32361 y=32360 z=7 | here].
* North-east of the [[Snake Tower]] guildhall, [COORD x=32458 y=32326 z=7 | here].
* East of [[Thais]], [COORD x=32487 y=32241 z=7 | here].

Once you've used the [[dog collar]] on [[Noodles]], return to the [[King Tibianus|King]] and request your '''reward'''. Note that you cannot repeat this task for 2 weeks &mdash; the King will not give you another dog collar and you cannot use another collar on Noodles during this time.

=== Rewards ===
* [[Achievement]] [[Dog Sitter]]
* 1,000 [[experience]] points
* One of the following:
** [[File:Platinum Coin.gif]] 5 [[Platinum Coin]]s
** [[File:Fan Doll of Queen Eloise.gif]] [[Fan Doll of Queen Eloise]] (rare)

If you are lucky, a [[File:Dog Lady.gif]][[Dog Lady]] might appear in the [[Rain Castle]] after the next [[server save]]. While the Dog Lady is in the castle, you might encounter a [[Basket (Puppies)|Basket of Puppies]] on the floor of the throne room. The basket will appear at [[Server Save]] but it can also respawn if there's a floor reset in the area.
