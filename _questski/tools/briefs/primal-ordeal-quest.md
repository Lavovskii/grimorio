== Requirements ==
* Completed the [[Within the Tides Quest]] (Missions: Coastal Concerns, Mountainous Matters, Of Feathers and Flowers, Star-Crossed Lovers)

== Missions ==

This quest can be split in two parts. The first part consist of helping the Gnomes in different tasks until you defeat the first boss, [[Magma Bubble]]. Defeating this boss will grant you access to the second part, the Hazard System, which allows you to eventually get a piece of the [[Primal Set]] as well as increase your gains in the Gnomprona hunting areas. 

You can use the map below to check important locations and the overall visual of the quest area.

[[File:Gnomprona Map.png|center]]

=== The Island that time forgot! ===

Find [[Gnomcruiter]] at the [[Noodles Academy of Modern Magic]] in [[Edron]] and ask him to '''join''' the efforts of the Gnomes into investigating a location deep under the surface of the world.

Then, go to the [[Steamboat]] in [[Cormaya]] and ask for a passage to '''[[Gnomprona]]'''.

=== Fairy Trails! ===

Talk to [[Gnominer]] just south of the steam boat in Gnomprona and ask him about {{KW|challenger}} (this is the keyword you'll use to report missions with him throughout the quest). Then go south where you'll find [[Gnomfurry]], buy one [[Candy Lure]] {{Ilink|Candy Lure}} from him by asking for a '''trade'''.

You'll have to use the Candy Lure on 7 different [[Lazyfairy|Lazyfaries]] that are in the underground area but also in the areas filled with creatures. To get to them, go into the cave near [[Gnomfurry]] and then walk south, where you'll find ore wagons to three different places. Three of the Lazyfaries are in the stone passages without any creatures:

=== Raiders of the Lost Flame! ===

[[File:Primal Ordeal Quest - Raiders of the Lost Flame|right|350px]]
Gnominer tasks you with collecting flame charges at excavation site Moss, located [COORD x=33618 y=32952 z=15 | here]. While up to 5 players may enter, mission progress is individual.

Your task is to reach the other side of the room. Kill the [[Blocking Stalagmite]]s blocking your path while avoiding [[Raging Monster]]s and [[Sulphur Menace]]s. Contact with their AoE attacks will not only push the player back but it will also consume obtained flame charges.

Step on the flame to obtain a charge, then make your way back to '''energy extractor''' device at the start. Player must obtain 3 charges to continue. Once done report back to Gnomimer.

'''Note''': If you stand on a tile where a [[Blocking Stalagmite]] spawns, it will spawn south of you, allowing you to walk past it by moving diagonally instead of having to kill it every turn. This strategy will make this task a lot faster. 

You have 10 minutes to complete this task, otherwise you will be kicked from the room and will need to wait 20 hours to attempt it again.

=== Not Catching Fire! ===

[[File:Primal Ordeal Quest - Not Catching Fire|right|350px]]
Gnominer sends you to excavation site Firefoot, [COORD x=33556 y=32883 z=15 | here] (use the right wagon to get there).

The task here is simple enough. Player must reach the structure at the end without stepping on big fire fields. Fields will turn gradually from ash into small fires and eventually big fires. If you're caught by a big fire field you'll be sent back to the entrance and you can keep trying.

After reaching the end you should see a message on the screen about your success. You can them step on a big fire field to return to the entrance and leave using the teleport. Don't forget to report the task to Gnominer.

=== In the Heat of the Fight ===

[[File:Primal Ordeal Quest - In the Heat of the Fight|right|350px]]

=== Magmageddon! ===

 |{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Fire}}
 |{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Physical}}

[[File:Primal Ordeal Quest - Magma Bubble|350px|thumb]]
With the artifact in place and all tests concluded the gnomes are ready to strike back.

Enter the volcano [COORD x=33660 y=32896 z=14 | here] to face off [[Magma Bubble]] with up to 5 players. Players will have to use some of the strategies developed in the tasks during fight.

Firstly, [[Gnomish Crystallomancer]] must be protected from enemies. If he dies, then the team will fail the fight, so creatures should not be allowed to attack the Gnome.

'''Stage 1: Magma Crystals'''

[[File:Lava Creature.gif|thumb|Lava Creature]]
Initially, players must destroy 3 '''Magma Crystals''' in the arena. However, the crystals are in an overheated area and characters stepping on the fire fields without protection will take 60% of their maximum [[HP|health]] every second as [[Agony Damage]], meaning this damage cannot be mitigated, which makes it basically impossible to survive. To get protection, players must attack [[The End of Days]] in the room until they reach half health, at which point they will heal back and several [[Lava Creature]]s will spawn. Unless the team can handle all the damage from the Lava Creatures, it is recommended to focus on a single End of Days at a time, to control the rate of spawn.

When the Lava Creatures die, [[Charged Flame]]s {{Ilink|Charged Flame}} will appear on the floor. Players must pickup the flames quickly and use them on the Crystals around the room when they become '''green''', and then step on the purple flame that appears. The crystals randomly change from red to green, so players must run around looking for a green crystal before the flame vanishes. Doing this correctly will give a player 5 shield points, displayed under their character's name.

[[File:Magma Crystal.gif|thumb|Magma Crystal]]
The team must collect a few rounds of points. Depending on the strength of the team, at least 20 shield points on 4 players (or more points on less players) should be gathered before going to the overheated area. There, players should attack the [[Magma Crystal]] until it is destroyed. It is important to save at least 5 shield points for the way out of the overheated zone! If Crystals are left alive, they will slowly will heal back while the team gathers more points.

This process should be repeated until all three Magma Crystals are destroyed. It is also an option to gather all necessary shield points in advance, and then just going into the three separate overheated areas destroying the Crystals.

'''Stage 2: The End of Days'''

[[File:The End of Days.gif|thumb|The End of Days]]
Once again, players will have to fight [[The End of Days]], but this time they will not produce Lava Creatures nor heal. On the other hand, there will be six of them instead of only three. A good strategy here is to bring them to place in the room in a position the blocker can tank all six while they face the wall, or to block them south of the crystal in the middle of the room, so none of them will be facing the shooters and the mages can wave safely.

'''Stage 3: Magma Bubble'''

[[File:Magma Bubble.gif|thumb|Magma Bubble]]
Once the team kills all six End of Days, the [[Magma Bubble]] will spawn. It will also constantly summon four [[Unchained Fire]]s, so single target attacks can be used on the boss during this stage. It should be faster than the previous stage and relatively simple, but the knight should be careful not to let Unchained Fires facing the boss, or their fire waves can heal it significantly.

During this stage, be careful with the [[Unchained Fire]]s high [[Fire Damage]] area attacks. Shooters should stack a lot of fire protection and try to stay diagonally to lower the chances of being hit by them.

=== Rewards ===

Once the boss is defeated, return to [[Gnominer]] to receive your [[Fire-Fighter Outfits]]. You should also talk to [[Gnomadness]] to gain access to [[#Hazard System|Hazard System]] and the [[The Primal Menace]], located [COORD x=33714 y=32798 z=14 | here].

== Hazard System ==

Once you have defeated the [[Magma Bubble]] you can access the hazard system to spice up your hunts in Gnomprona.

To be able to set the difficulty to a higher hazard level, you have to earn the respective hazard qualification (i.e. your maximum achieved hazard) by killing [[The Primal Menace]], which has a cooldown of 20 hours.

* Each kill increases your hazard qualification by +1, but only if you kill the boss on the highest hazard level you have unlocked at that point.
* A hazard level will not be applied automatically. You can select your active hazard level with the help of Gnomadness by saying the number of Hazard you want ('''1''', '''2''', etc). This means you can also choose a hazard level below the current maximum you have unlocked. There's no cooldown or other limitations to change your current hazard level.
** In a party, the lowest hazard level will count for the whole party no matter your personal choice. This effective hazard level can be seen on a counter under the character's name while in Gnomprona.

=== Upside of increasing your hazard level ===
* Players earn more loot and experience (+4% per hazard);
* The chance to spawn a [[Plunder Patriarch]] when killing a monster increases. When killed, a Plunder Patriach always drops at least one random item from the [[Arboreal Set|Arboreal]], [[Arcanomancer Set|Arcanomancer]], [[Spiritthorn Set|Spiritthorn]], [[Alicorn Set|Alicorn]] or [[Ethereal Set|Ethereal]] sets.

=== Downsides of increasing your hazard level ===

* All damage dealt by creatures increases;
* The amount of critical extra damage dealt by creatures increases:
** All creatures have a 7.5% chance to land a critical hit. This is the same across all hazard levels and creatures;
** On hazard level 1 they will deal 50% critical extra damage;
** A player can never be hit by more than one critical hit from these creatures within 2 seconds. This limit was added to reduce extreme damage spikes, as the critical extra damage scales to very high numbers.
* The creature's chance to dodge attacks increases:
** On hazard level 1 they will dodge 0.85% of attacks. Starting at hazard 5, the chance scales up faster
* The chance to generate [[Primal Pod]]s when killing a creature increases. In addition the damage when stepping onto a Primal Pod in its second stage increases.
** Primal Pods have three stages. The first stage lasts 2 seconds, during which you can safely step on them to destroy them. In their second stage (also 2 seconds?) you will take damage when stepping on them, but still destroy them. In their final stage they turn into a [[Fungosaurus]].
** The amount of Primal Pods spawned is randomized, but increases with your group size;
** On hazard level 1 you have a 0.875% chance to spawn a Primal Pod and they deal damage equal to 5% of your current [[HP]] plus 500 as [[Ice Damage]].

=== The Primal Menace ===

{{FightTips

|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Death|text=Specially the Knight.}}
|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Energy|text=Specially the shooters.}}
|{{FightTip|type=use|element=Fire|text=GFBs are the best AoE rune.}}
}}

To fight [[The Primal Menace]], go to the [[Monster Graveyard]], up the ramp and all the way north until you find a teleport [COORD x=33714 y=32798 z=14 | here].

[[File:Primal Ordeal Quest - The Primal Menace - Hazard 5 to 6|thumb|200px]]
The mechanics of the fight include the spawn of [[Primal Pod]]s and [[Primal Pack Beast]]s. Pods that are not handled will turn into new [[Fungosaurus]], and primal beasts must be killed within 20 seconds, otherwise they become a Fungosaurus with full health. The higher your hazard level during the fight, the harder it will be, since you'll take more damage, spawn more pods and the boss will dodge attacks more often.

The recommended strategy is to always focus on the Pack Beasts, since one single Fungosaurus can make the fight snowball out of control. The Primal Menace will slowly die from AoE damage or attacks dealt when no Pack Beasts are alive. Beware that the fight gets increasingly harder as the boss' health lowers, because Pack Beasts will spawn more often which will also lead to more Pods appearing.

[[File:Primal Ordeal Quest - The Primal Menace - Hazard 11 to 12|thumb|200px]]
Since your main focus is to kill Pack Beasts as quickly as possible, every team member must be focused on making sure the Beasts are near the Knight so they hit by everyone's attacks. This should be done with a combination of the Knight moving to [[Challenge]] them, but also by shooters helping to position the creatures for that. If the Knight moves too much, there's a good chance some of the Beasts will switch targets during this movement, whereas not moving at all will make the shooters' life much more difficult. 

It's of utmost importance that mages use their waves as often as they can. Even though the boss is immune to [[Earth Damage]], Druid's [[Terra Wave]] does a lot more damage to Pack Beasts than GFBs. Sorcerers' [[Great Fire Wave]] and [[Energy Wave]]  also deal a lot of damage, both to the Beasts and to the boss. Some teams even bring two Sorcerers instead of two Druids, which should increase the team's damage output considerably. Of course, this increases the risk for the Knight and is generally only possible if both the blocker and the remaining Druid are considerably strong (around level 1200+). 

For the final hazard levels, it's worth considering the use of [[Tibiadrome]] potions for all team members. By using the Amplification and Resilience potions, as well as [[Strike Enhancement]], the team can counter the high damage dealt by MegaCrit hits as well as the higher chance of Beasts and the boss dodging your attacks. Obviously, the downside here is that failing the fight after such a big investment will be a considerable waste for the team. The shooters should also be using [[Shockwave Amulet]]s {{Ilink|Shockwave Amulet}} since it provides a lot of protection against two of the three elements they'll be taking damage of.

=== Rewards ===

* {{Ilink|Ripptor}} After reaching the Hazard Level 6, which means defeating the Primal Menace at Hazard 5, you can ask [[Gnomadness]] for a '''mount''' to receive your [[Ripptor]]. 
* When you reach the Hazard Level 12, which means defeating the Primal Menace at Hazard 11, you can ask [[Gnomadness]] for a '''reward''' to receive a [[Primal Bag]] which will instantly transform into any of the [[Primal Set]] items.
