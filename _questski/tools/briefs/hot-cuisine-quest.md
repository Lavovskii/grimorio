=== Method ===
This quest consists of preparing 10 dishes with [[Jean Pierre]]. For every dish you will need to bring a list of ingredients and [[Jean Pierre]] will cook them into a dish, which you will receive. After finishing all dishes you will also get [[Jean Pierre's Cookbook I]] and [[Jean Pierre's Cookbook II]]. Then you can make every dish again, once a year, but you will have to bring the ingredients again.

CipSoft held a [https://www.tibia.com/news/?subtopic=newsarchive&id=1335 Gourmet Competition] in May 2010 where players were asked to design some new recipes for [[Jean Pierre]] to cook. There were 4 winning recipes which was introduced into the game with the summer update 2010. (Be aware that some details about these winning recipes might be changed a bit when they were introduced) The winners and those dishes are as follows:

* Best Starter: [[Coconut Shrimp Bake]] by [https://www.tibia.com/community/?subtopic=characters&name=Natalya+Rimsky Natalya Rimsky], an exotic rice dish with a touch of white and brown mushrooms.
* Best Main Dish: [[Blackjack]] by [https://www.tibia.com/community/?subtopic=characters&name=Karolak Karolak], it's like a stew that looks rather awful, but it is pretty tasty and vitamin-rich.
* Best Dessert: [[Demonic Candy Ball]]s by [https://www.tibia.com/community/?subtopic=characters&name=Hitogedgy Hitogedgy], delicious dreams of sweetness with some unpredictable effects for those who dare to eat them.
* Best Drink: [[Sweet Mangonaise Elixir]] by [https://www.tibia.com/community/?subtopic=characters&name=Exaga Exaga], a kind of powerful magical liquid that works perfectly with rings.

With the introduction of the Monk vocation, a new dish [[Zaoan Sauce]] was added to complement the Monk's use of [[Fist Fighting]] skill.

'''Notes''':

* The first time you finish the quest you get 14 dishes, after that you can do any recipe again for a total of 28 dishes the first time.

* Most items can be bought in many different towns but if you only visit these towns: [[Edron]], [[Venore]], [[Darashia]], [[Ankrahmun]], [[Thais]], [[Carlin]] and [[Svargrond]] you will get them all (except items you can't buy) and do less traveling.

* The [[flour]] can be made by harvesting some [[Bunch of Wheat|bunches of wheat]] with a [[Scythe]] and used with a [[Millstone]] or buy the wheat in [[Thais]] from [[Donald McRonald]] for 1gp each.

* Annoying as it is, when he asks for a vial of something (e.g. milk), the container MUST be a vial, otherwise he won't take it, bottles, mugs, etc. will be rejected; to get a vial of anything, use the container with the liquid (for example, a mug of mead) on the empty vial and the liquid will be transfered. [[Vial]]s can be bought filled with [[Water (Liquid)|water]] in [[Edron]] for 8gp each, or [[oil]] in most other cities (Venore just to mention one) for 20gp each. Empty the [[vial]] of its contents and use it on a cask of wine/beer/mead which can be found in certain buildings all over Tibia.

* Hint: You can also parcel a freshly killed creature from another character, so you only have to run from Darashia depot.

* ''Important'': Each dish can only be used once every 10 minutes.

=== Starting the Quest ===

To start this quest, you must find Jean Pierre. He is a few steps to the west of the [[Blue Djinn Fortress]] ([COORD x=33068 y=32531 z=6 | here]) and tell him "''hi'', ''cook'', ''recipes'', ''apprentice'', ''yes''". He will ask you for ingredients to make a certain dish, bring them back to continue to the next dish. You can look for them as follows:<br />
<br />
==== Dish 1: [[Rotworm Stew]] ====
For this dish you have to bring:
*2 [[meat]] (buy in e.g. [[Darashia]])
*20 [[potatoe]]s (buy in e.g. [[Venore]])
*1 [[onion]] (buy in e.g. [[Ankrahmun]])
*1 [[Bulb of Garlic]] (buy in e.g. [[Ankrahmun]])
*5 ounces of [[flour]] (make it in [[Thais]])
*2 [[vial]]s of [[beer]]

==== Dish 2: [[Hydra Tongue Salad]] ====
*2 [[Hydra Tongue]]s (buy in e.g. [[Venore]])
*2 [[Tomato]]es (buy in e.g. [[Venore]])
*1 [[Cucumber]] (buy in e.g. [[Thais]])
*2 [[Egg]]s (buy in e.g. [[Venore]])
*1 [[Troll Green]] ([[Daily Respawns|12 respawns]] in [[Dusalk's Troll Clan Cave|here]])
*1 [[Vial]] of [[wine]]

==== Dish 3: [[Roasted Dragon Wings]] ====
*1 Fresh [[Dead Bat]] (run within 5 minutes from [[Port Hope]] into [[Tiquanda]])
*3 [[Jalapeno Pepper]]s (buy in e.g. [[Ankrahmun]])
*5 [[Brown Bread]]s (buy in e.g. [[Carlin]])
*2 [[Egg]]s (buy in e.g. [[Venore]])
*1 [[Powder Herb]] ([[Daily Respawns|3 respawns]] near [[Wyda]])
*5 [[Red Mushroom]]s (buy in e.g. [[Edron]])

==== Dish 4: [[Tropical Fried Terrorbird]] ====
*1 Fresh [[Dead Chicken]]
*2 [[Lemon]]s (buy in e.g. [[Venore]])
*2 [[Orange]]s (buy in e.g. [[Venore]])
*2 [[Mango]]s (buy in e.g. [[Venore]])
*2 [[Vial]]s of [[Coconut Milk]] (use [[Juice Squeezer]] on [[Coconut]]s while having a empty vial in the backpack, Coconut can be looted from [[Sibang]]s)
*1 [[Stone Herb]] (buy in e.g. [[Edron]])

*2 [[Vial]]s of [[milk]] (buy in e.g. [[Carlin]] (cheapest) or in [[Darashia]])
*1 [[Sling Herb]] ([[Daily Respawns|1 respawns]] near [[Wyda]], 12 respawns in [[Greenshore]] farm and 6 respawns in [[Thais]] farm. Or loot it from [[Spit Nettle]]s or [[Carniphila]]s.)

*10 [[Shrimp]]s (loot from [[Quaras]])

*5 [[Sandcrawler Shell]]s.
*2 [[Vial]]s of [[Water (Liquid)|Water]] (buy in e.g. [[Edron]])
*20 [[Carrot]]s (buy in e.g. [[Carlin]])
*10 [[Potato]]es (buy in e.g. [[Venore]])
*3 [[Jalapeno Pepper]]s (buy in e.g. [[Ankrahmun]])

==== Dish 13: [[Demonic Candy Ball]]s ====
*3 [[Candy|Candies]] (Dropped by [[The Mutated Pumpkin]], can also be obtained as a reward of the [[Barbarian Arena Quest]].)
*3 [[Candy Cane]]s (Candy Canes can be looted in [[Grynch Clan Goblin]]s and gifted by [[Santa Claus]] during [[Christmas]].)
