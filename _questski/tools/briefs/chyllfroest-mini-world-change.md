== Method ==
This is a special kind of MWC. From April 1 to May 1 every year, the frosty island of [[Chyllfroest]] will be accessible. Be prepared to face a large variety of [[creatures]], and even the occasional [[Yeti]]. This MWC provides you with the only possibility of taming an [[Ursagrodon]].
