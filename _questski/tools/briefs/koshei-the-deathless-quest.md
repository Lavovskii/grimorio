## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Koshei the Deathless Quest}}

== Requirements ==
* [[Rope]], or equivalent
* [[Shovel]], or equivalent
* 5,000 gold
*: If you plan to complete the quest you will receive 5,000 gold back. Note that you will '''not''' receive this if you decide to exit the quest with [[Koshei's Ancient Amulet]], i.e. choose not to fight [[Koshei the Deathless]].

=== Method ===
==== Parts of Koshei's Amulet ====
First, you have to find all four [[Piece of a Broken Amulet|broken amulet pieces]]:
* The [[Piece of a Broken Amulet (Mouth)]] can be found under a [[Loose Board]] in the [[Bath of Dreams]], the building west of [[Darashia]]'s [[depot]] ([COORD x=33194 y=32459 z=7 | here]);
* The [[Piece of a Broken Amulet (Hanger)]] is located in a [[Stone Coffin]] on the third floor underground of the [[Dark Pyramid]], in the far northeastern part of [[Darama]] ([COORD x=33306 y=32278 z=10 | here]);
* The [[Piece of a Broken Amulet (Nose)]] is buried in the [[Sand (Quest)|sands]] of the [[Kha'labal]], above the [[Ancient Ruins Tomb]] ([COORD x=33212 y=32593 z=7 | here]). [[Use]] the sand to obtain the amulet piece, don't dig for it;
* The [[Piece of a Broken Amulet (Eye)]] lies hidden in a [[Stone Coffin]] some floors down in southern [[Drefia]], in the room with the single [[Vampire]] ([COORD x=33054 y=32468 z=11 | here]).

After you have found all four pieces, go to [[A Sweaty Cyclops]] in [[Ab'Dendriel]] ([COORD x=32711 y=31682 z=7 | here]). To reach him go through the hole in the wheat field east of depot. Ask [[A Sweaty Cyclops]] about an '''amulet''', pay him 5,000 gold and wait 24 hours. Go back and ask about the '''amulet''' again. You will receive [[Koshei's Ancient Amulet]].

You can exit the quest here to keep the amulet, or you can continue with the quest. '''Note that by continuing the quest you forfeit the amulet.'''

==== Catacombs ====
[[File:Koshei Quest Entrance.jpg|thumb|right|Light the north and eastern torches and then use the [[Ancient Sundial]] in the center.]]
:Note: This part will involve killing [[Koshei the Deathless]]. It is strongly recommended to have at least 2 level 40 players although higher levels should have no problems with it alone.

Go to the [[Darashia]] [[Deeper Catacombs]], just east of [[Darashia]] ([COORD x=33281 y=32445 z=7 | here]).

You must open the staircase to go deeper. To do so, light the 2 torches (statues) to the north and east and ensure the others are turned off, before using the [[Ancient Sundial]] by the stairs. You will hear a "{{Message|type=sound|CLICK}}" sound in the south-west room. Go down the staircase that has been revealed in the coffin in that room.

{{Clear}}
[[File:Koshei Quest Map.png|thumb|right]]

Follow the cave south and turn east when you can. Descend the stairs. Go down the ladder. Use the [[Box]] to obtain 100 [[Gold Coin]]s and fall down the covered pitfall at the south part of the small room. Walk all the way north and find a level 40 [[Gate of Expertise]]. [[Koshei the Deathless]] awaits inside. Kill [[Koshei the Deathless|Koshei]] and use [[Koshei's Ancient Amulet]] on the Remains of Koshei quickly because his corpse will disappear after a few seconds and you'll see the the messagee "{{Message|type=sound|At least I have it back, my precious amulet. I am glad you didn't use it! I allow you to ....ahh....enter door ..... ahh}}".

:Koshei will only spawn if you have [[Koshei's Ancient Amulet]]. If you step outside [[Koshei the Deathless|Koshei's]] room he will despawn. If you fail to use the amulet on his corpse before it disappears he will immediately spawn to the north by the altar with the text "{{Message|type=sound|YOU FOOL! NOW DIE AND SERVE ME IN HELL!}}".

When you have finally slain [[Koshei the Deathless]], go to the south and enter the western [[Door (Sealed)|sealed door]] to claim your prize.
