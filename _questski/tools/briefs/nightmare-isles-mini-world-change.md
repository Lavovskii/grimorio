== Method ==
This MWC is active every day and features a teleporter which leads to the dreaded [[Nightmare Isles]]. The teleporter can be located:
*near the [[Plague Spike]] (''river near Drefia/Drefia's mountains'', [COORD x=33037 y=32404 z=7 | here])
*in northern [[Devourer (Geography)|Devourer]] (''northernmost coast/Darashia's coast'', [COORD x=33216 y=32273 z=7 | here])
*or in [[Kha'labal]] (''Ankrahmun tar pits/Ankrahmun's desert'', [COORD x=33265 y=32653 z=7 | here]).

On these isles, fearsome creatures protecting a [[Nightmare Teddy]] lurk. Do not access them unprepared.
