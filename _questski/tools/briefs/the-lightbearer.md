## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

== Introduction ==
The '''Lightbearer''' event is an event occurring in November in which participants must keep basins lit. It was first introduced in 2009 ({{OfficialNewsArchive|1114|Link to Official News}}) but already tested in the test server preceding the summer update of 2009 and again in the test server preceding the autumn patch of 2009.

The event consists of keeping 10 different [[Lightbringer's Basin]]s burning for 96 hours.<br />
Each player can only light each basin once per year. The [[Magical Torch]] is used to keep the [[Lightbringer's Basin]]s burning and vanishes at the end of November.

When you use the torch on a basin, the time of the basin will reset, for example if it was 98/180 minutes, and you use it, you will reset it to 180/180 minutes, that way you can do all the basins and still keep them lit. Also you don't have to kill the respawns to make it count as if you lit the basin. This means everybody get to use the basins if they can, and if they do it, they won't affect the schedulers of the other organized players, but still you should collaborate with your fellow world partners.

After using your [[Magical Torch]] on the basins you have a chance to get 5 minutes of respawn. Creatures that respawn depends on what basin you are and they can come in packs from 1 to 5. The respawns increase in difficulty up to the 3rd or 4th respawn, and then decrease again. For the Forbidden Lands and Magician Quarter locations the spawned monsters are particularly difficult, and even high level players may want a 2nd player there to assist with killing them.

[[Magical Torch]]es are not assigned to players. You can share your torch with other players. This means that [[Free Account]] players can participate in the event if someone lets them use their torch.

The event requires a lot of teamwork and organization, especially on servers with few people. There are online tools available to make it easier. TibiaWiki's own [[TibiaWiki:Tools/Lightbearer Basin Monitor|Lightbearer Basin Monitor]] records the amount of time on each basin; [[TibiaRoyal]] has a timetable tool for event manager (requires registration).

== Method ==
Seek [[Lucius]] in the [[Yalahar]] [[Magician Quarter]]. He is in the north-eastern area (around [COORD x=32892 y=31088 z=7 | here]). Say '''hi''', and '''yes'''. He will bring you to the [[Temple of Light]]. Go upstairs and find him again, say '''hi''' to him and ask him for a '''mission''', say '''yes''' to get your [[Magical Torch]].

This magical torch will be used to light [[Lightbringer's Basin]] all around the world, '''which can be done in any order'''. They are:

===Basin 1: [[Formorgar Glacier]]===

<div style="float: right;">[MINIMAP x=32080 y=31122 z=1 marks=none]</div>
Leave [[Svargrond]] from the north and walk west. Walk along the outside of the mountain (avoiding Svargrond Frost Trolls) until you find a place to levitate up three times. Levitate up to the top and walk around clockwise to the south side of the mountain. Levitate up again and follow the path west until you find the basin.

===Basin 10: [[Magician Quarter]]===

<div style="float: right;">[MINIMAP x=32824 y=31032 z=8 marks=none]</div>

Go to the [[Hellspawn]] area of [[Magician Quarter]]. Go down the east ramp. Look to the west and see if there is an overspawn of [[Duskbringer]]s. If there is, fight your way through the cave to the other side. Otherwise, simply go up the ramp and down the west ramp. The basin is just north-west of the west ramp.

The hardest basin to guard due to the location and basin creature strength. Luring and overspawning is serious problem at this basin. It's a good idea to go down the '''east ramp''' first to check if there are basin creatures near the other ramp. If you see a lot of [[Duskbringer]]s then you should kill your way through to the other side. '''Do not attempt clearing a full spawn of Duskbringers from the west ramp.''' It is much, much safer to kill your way through the cave. Consider using [[Energy Wall Rune]]s to kill Duskbringers in more manageable numbers if you must go through the cave.

Yalahar might seem to be the most convenient option for players who only do 1 basin (being that it's so close to [[Lucius]]) but for the basin managers' sake it is strongly recommended to light a different basin.

== Consequences ==

Failing leads to raids of [[Essence of Darkness|Essences of Darkness]] in every city. The following message appears:

''Strong demonic creatures have passed the border to our world and invade the Temple of Light! Run, as long as you still can - or fight them, if you dare!''

Winning leads to raids of [[Spirit of Light|Spirits of Light]] in every city, and the possibility to get rewards.
