=== Requirements ===
* The first 8 missions of [[The New Frontier Quest]] completed to be able to trade 6 [[Tome of Knowledge|Tomes of Knowledge]] with NPC [[Cael]].
* [[The New Frontier Quest]] completed to trade more [[Tome of Knowledge|Tomes of Knowledge]] with NPC [[Cael]].
* [[Parcel]]s or [[Levitate]] spell.

=== Notes ===
* You can take the [[Tome of Knowledge|Tomes]] in any order.
* Some Tomes are given as a reward for completing quest missions like the fourth and eighth ones.
* Some Tomes can '''ONLY''' be found during quest missions like the ninth [[Tome of Knowledge|Tome]].
* The [[Zzaion]] Tome can '''ONLY ''' be found during the [[Zao Raids#Orcs raid on Zzaion|Orcs raid on Zzaion]].

* [[Zao Palace]], go all the way to the chest located [COORD x=33157 y=31229 z=15 | here] and grab the tome from it.
