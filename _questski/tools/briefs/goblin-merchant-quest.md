## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler|name=Goblin Merchant Quest}}

== Required ==
* [[Rope]], or equivalent
* [[Shovel]], or equivalent
* [[Mouldy Cheese]]

The following items you will be given by NPC [[Rafzan]]. If you lose them before completing the task you will need to purchase more from him.
* 3 [[Advertisement Sign]]s
* [[Perfume Gatherer]]
* [[Guardcatcher]]
* [[Bottle with Rat Urine]]
* [[Trunkhammer]]

== Tips ==
Here are some tips for frequent taskers, to be more efficient in completing these tasks.

* Unless you need the money, you can purchase [[Perfume Gatherer]]s from [[Rafzan]]. The slug cave has 27-28 [[Slug]]s in it at full spawn; you can do a full circuit to supply yourself for the whole month, or however many you choose. This will save a lot of time. Note that you only need to purchase for the initial supply, as you will receive another empty one each time you accept the task.
* The tasks [[#Destroy|Destroy]] and [[#Busy|Busy]] both take you to the [[Venore Corym Cave]]. Save time by doing both tasks at once.
* The tasks [[#Guards|Guards]], [[#Kill|Kill]], [[#Advertising|Advertising]] can be done together as the quest targets are all over the surface of the swamp. However, it's a bad idea to roam the swamp randomly. The [[Venore Daily Tasks Quest]] are additional tasks designed for lower levels. The reward isn't that great by itself, but the most important thing is that they provide direction. You will be going all over the swamp anyway, you might as well do the additional tasks. On doing the 3 Venore daily tasks, you should have no problems finding enough objectives for these tasks.
* Consider digging for a [[Leech]] if you go to the northern construction site. You can do this once per week.

== Method ==
Leave [[Venore]] from the eastern exit (go down [COORD x=33004 y=32095 z=6 | here]). Leave the building and go east a few steps to find a ladder and/or ramp. This is the central construction site ([[Venore Daily Tasks Quest]]). Go to the north-eastern platform and down the ladder. Go east to find a fancy building. On the south side of the building there is an elevator ([COORD x=33051 y=32099 z=7 | here]). Use the [[Pulley]] if the elevator is up, to bring it down. Now step on the elevator and use the pulley to go up.

Ask [[Rafzan]] if he needs '''help'''. He will offer you 6 '''tasks'''. In order to receive the tasks you must say '''get''' and then the task name. In order to report, say '''report''' and then the task name. For future references, you don't need to say "help", "tasks", "get" or "report", just the mission's keyword.

The following tasks are available:

You can repeat tasks every 20 hours. After 500 tasks you will receive the [[Task Manager]] achievement. You can do all six of the tasks of the Goblin Merchant Quest at the same time. It is highly recommended to accept all tasks you will do, to save time.

=== {{Anchor|Destroy}} Business Opportunities ===
'''Keyword''': destroy<br />
'''Reward''': 1200 experience and 3 [[Platinum Coin]]s<br />
'''Notes''': this is one of the harder tasks, involving exploration of the Corym caves.

Go to the [[Venore Corym Cave|Corym Caves]], through a house north-east of Rafzan's building ([COORD x=33072 y=32089 z=7 | here]). Look for [[Junk Trunk]]s (they look like [[Large Trunk]]s) and use your [[Trunkhammer]] on 3 of them. They will turn into [[Smashed Trunk]]s. After completion, your [[Trunkhammer]] will disappear. When done, go back to [[Rafzan]] and report about your task.

=== {{Anchor|Guards}} Guardian Angel - Kind Of ===
'''Keyword''': guards<br />
'''Reward''': 600 experience and 1 [[Platinum Coin]]<br />

You will be given a [[Guardcatcher]] which looks like a [[Bow]]. All around the swamp you will find [[Adventurer]]s. Use the guardcatcher on at least three adventurers. When you use the guardcatcher, one of two things can happen: the adventurer can vanish (saying "{{Sound|Arrrgh! Not my knee!}}"), or the adventurer can become an [[Angry Adventurer]] (saying "{{Sound|You fiend! I will get you, servant of evil!}}"). When you've kneecapped three Adventurers, your guardcatcher will break (with the message "{{Server Message|Luckily the makeshift bow breaks as you hit the last adventurer you needed and not earlier!}}"). Report back to Rafzan when you are done.

=== {{Anchor|Kill}} Hungry Birds ===
'''Keyword''': kill<br />

=== {{Anchor|Perfume}} Rattraction ===
'''Keyword''': perfume<br />
'''Reward''': 1000 experience and 2 [[Platinum Coin]]s<br />

You need to get some odor samples. You will receive a [[Perfume Gatherer]] from [[Rafzan]] to collect the samples with.

* The first odor you're looking for is special snail slime. Find the Venore [COORD x=32967 y=32144 z=7 | Slug Cave], kill a slug, wait 10 seconds and use the perfume gatherer on it. A message will appear: {{Message|type=server|You gather the first part of the rat perfume!}}
* The second odor you're looking for is from [[Black Swamp Gas]], which looks like [[smoke]], or [[Poison Gas]] colored grey. You can find these in a few places in the swamp, two such places are [COORD x=33096 y=32064 z=7 | here] and [COORD x=33065 y=32120 z=7 | here]. Use your perfume gatherer on it. A message will appear: {{Message|type=server|You gather the second part of the rat perfume!}}
* The third odor you're looking for is a [[Rotworm]] fart. Go to any cave with [[Rotworm]]s, [COORD x=33028 y=32088 z=7 | such as here] Use the perfume gatherer on the rotworm repeatedly until you receive a rotworm fart. On failure, you will receive a message: {{Message|type=server|Darn! It didn't fart!}}, and on success: {{Message|type=server|You gather the third part of the rat perfume!}}. On average it will take about 3.85 attempts, but the rotworm does not vanish when you fail or succeed.
* The fourth and final odor you need is that of [[Mouldy Cheese]]. Get mouldy cheese from [COORD x=32934 y=32217 z=7 | Venore Swamp Trolls], [[Goblin]]s (rare), [[Corym Caves]] and many other places (daily respawns). The mouldy cheese can be obtained before the quest.

Report back to [[Rafzan]] with the complete [[Perfume Gatherer]] (its description: ''It is filled with smelley goo.'').

=== {{Anchor|Busy}} To Piss Off Some Rats ===
'''Keyword''': busy<br />
'''Reward''': 1200 experience and 3 [[Platinum Coin]]s<br />
'''Notes''': this is one of the harder tasks, involving exploration of the Corym caves.

Go north-east of his building to the [[Venore Corym Cave]]. Once in the cave, go east and down the hole, then go far east to a pond ([COORD x=33137 y=32094 z=10 | here]). '''Use''' the rat urine vial in the center of the pond (from a distance, like you would a fishing rod). A message will appear: ''The keen noses of the ratmen will smell the mark of their rivals!''. Report back to [[Rafzan]] and claim your reward.
