== Requirements ==
* Access to [[Vengoth]] (completed up to mission 4 of the [[Blood Brothers Quest]]);
* At least 17 {{Ilink|Norcferatu Talisman}} [[Norcferatu Talisman]]s;

== Into the Grave ==
Go to [[Edron]] and talk to [[An Ominous Bat]] ([COORD x=33264 y=31786 z=7 | here]). It tells you the grave is calling you. Go upstairs, just north of where [[Telas]] is, then levitate down [COORD x=33270 y=31787 z=6 | here] to reach  the spot where an ominous grave is located.

Use the grave and you will be brought to a room with [[Anaztassja Moroia]]. She will tell you she is glad you finally answered her {{KW|call}}, and informs you [[Vladrukh]] and his army of vile orcs obtained some preserved blood of an ancient {{KW|entity}}.

She grants you her magic to pierce their protection and enter their lair.

== Through the Mist ==
Head to [[Vengoth]] then walk north towards the [[Norcferatu Fortress]]. Do not go up the stairs. Instead, levitate up a bit to east from stairs after the mist, [COORD x=32914 y=31451 z=7 | here].

You will find Anaztassja again, and she wants to talk about {{KW|business}}. She asks you to find the ancestral cave and find a way to communicate to the ancestor spirits.

== The Wrath of the Ancestors ==
Walk through the quest door next to Anaztassja, walk east to another another quest door [COORD x=32951 y=31419 z=6 | here], then talk to [[An Orchish Ancestor Spirit]].

The spirit tells you you need to {{KW|appease}} them in order to gain their support.

== Dance of Death ==
You learn that you must dance to honor the rites of the the spirit's kind. By 'dancing' and performing the Ritual Dance of the Red Moon you will earn their blessing.

Walk in the room and stand on top of an omnious mark, then rotate your character as fast as you can. A counter next to your character will start increasing its value until 50. Avoid standing next to [[Angry Orc Ancestor Spirit]]s, they will often make your [[drunk]], [[rooted]], or [[feared]]. When you reach 50, a message appears to inform the ritual is concluded ({{Server Message|YOU HAVE APPEASED THE ORCISH ANCESTORS!}}).

Report back to Anaztassja by talking to her about {{KW|business}}.

== Bloodbath ==
Anaztassja tells you need to undergo a baptism of blood to fool the wards in the deeper dungeons. Go one floor up and enter a room protected by a quest door, [COORD x=32909 y=31432 z=5 | here].

This room is protected by a {{Ilink|Blind Monstrosity}} [[Blind Monstrosity]], be quick or use a good strategy to walk through without getting hit by its attacks until you reach the next room to the east. Throw a bomb near the {{Ilink|Blood Totem}} [[Blood Totem]], and get hit by its spells until your blood sacrament is finished (a counter will rise until you reach 10). Server messages will appear to inform about the progress ({{Server Message|Argh!}}, {{Server Message|That Pain!!}}, {{Server Message|<gasp>}}, {{Server Message|You gained the sacrament of blood!}}).

Report back to Anaztassja.

== Breaking in ==
:'''''Note:''' This mission can be done as a team, thus work together to collect the talismans needed.''

Anaztassja can smell the orcish blood on you, and confirms everything is ready. Your last obstacle before reaching underground levels is to walk past a [[Blood Guardian]], but Anaztassja has a plan: use {{ilink|Norcferatu Talisman}} [[Norcferatu Talisman]]s to lure a [[Behemoth]] to break their guard.

Walk a bit northwest, then enter a narrow corridor where you can see the guardian downstairs. Follow the corridor all the way to east, then start tossing the talismans downstairs, starting from the most east tile, until you lure the behemoth all the way to west. This must be done tile by tile, so you need at least 17 talismans to finish this mission.

Once the behemoth break the wall, you and any other player wanting to advance in the quest, have 5 minutes to run downstairs and enter the unprotected teleport.

=== Vladrukh ===
[[File:Vladrukh.gif|right]]
{{FightTips
|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Life Drain}}
|{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Earth}}
}}
The fight is essentially split into two different phases: one where Vladrukh is actually present in the room and you attack him, and a second one where you need to kill essences to bring the boss back to the fight. These phases repeat themselves alternately until the end of the fight.

In the first phase (also the starting phase as you enter the room), Vladrukh will be in the room and shortly after three [[Blood Pool]]s will spawn. Different teams handle this phase differently, depending what is more feasible for their level, or preference but in general the [[Knight]] will want to lure the boss to a corner, facing a direction where the boss doesn't hit shooters with his frequent waves. In this phase you and your team have about 2 minutes and 20 seconds to attack the boss.

As for the pools, shooters can choose to tank them, but a safer approach is luring them away from the boss and having a mage summon [[Demon Skeleton]]s to block them. Pools attack exclusively with [[Life Drain]] damage, and since demon skeletons are immune to it, it makes them perfect summons for the task.

After the 2 minutes and 20 seconds mark, Vladrukh will disappear and new creatures will appears in the room: four [[Vampire Bat]]s and four [[Vampiric Essence]]s (the [[Blood Pool]]s don't disappear!). Your goal now is to kill the four Vampiric Essences as fast as you can so you can go back to the first phase where you can attack Vladrukh. It's also important to deal with this second phase fast because Vladrukh heals himself while you're busy dealing with the essences.

Be aware that [[Vampire Bat]]s can be quite dangerous. They are immortal, make you [[drunk]], cause [[Feared|fear]], reflect all damages inflicted to them, and also don't respect [[Knight]]'s [[Challenge]]. Their weakness is that they don't sense invisibility, so prepare to put [[Stealth Ring]]s or cast [[Invisible]]]] as soon as they appear.

Repeat these phases until the boss reaches red [[HP]]. Now Vladrukh gets an additional temporary form that turns him into a poison field for few seconds. In that form it becomes immortal and deals considerably more damage than normal. [[Knight]]s can use these few seconds to run the boss around (as long as it's safe for him and shooters; don't forget the boss waves very frequently!) then trap him again. This is essentially an additional mechanic to delay your progress when the boss is low in health.

Keep on dealing damage until the boss is dead.

== Reward ==
[[File:Gloom Maw (Mount).gif|right]]
After defeating [[Vladrukh]], report a last time to [[Anaztassja Moroia]] and you will receive the [[Gloom Maw (Mount)]] and [[File:Achievement_Grade_Symbol.gif]] [[Bat Person]] [[achievement]]. Also, you're now allowed to use the Mysterious Machine on the highest floor, which teleports you to the entrance of the fortress.
