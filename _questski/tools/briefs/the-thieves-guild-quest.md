## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=The Thieves Guild Quest}}

==Required Equipment==
*10 Elephant [[Tusk]]s
*2,000 [[gp]]s
*6,000 [[gp]]s OR Archpostman of the Postman Guild and a Postman Hat
*A lot of travel money (~2210 [[gp]])

==Method==
(You can get the 10 elephant tusks before the mission to save you some time)

Talk to [[Dorian]] in [[Thais]] and say "hi" then "thieves". He will then invite you to join the Thieves Guild. ([[Dorian]] is located just a few squares west of [[Gamel]], underneath the docks just east of the [[boat]], ([COORD x=32317 y=32209 z=8 | here]).)

===Mission 1: Ivory Poaching===
Collect 10 elephant [[tusk]]s and report back to [[Dorian]].

===Mission 2: Burglary===
Buy some [[Lock Pick]]s from [[Dorian]], its recommended to buy from 3 to 10. Now travel to Carlin and visit [[Sarina]]'s general store ([COORD x=32335 y=31808 z=7 | here]). Use the vines on the side of her house to travel into her locked room. Use the [[Lock Pick|lock picks]] to break open her chest. Steal the [[Valuable vase|vase]] and report back to [[Dorian]]

[[Image:The Thieves Guild Quest mission 2 Burglary.jpg]]

===Mission 3: Invitation===
[[Dorian]] would like you to earn his guild an invitation to the King's ball. You can achieve this by visiting [[Oswald]] ([COORD x=32379 y=32221 z=7 | here]), east of the temple in [[Thais]], say "invitation". It will cost you 1000 gold. Report back to [[Dorian]] when you receive the [[Invitation]].

===Mission 4: Bridge Robbery===
Go to [[Outlaw Camp]] and find [[Snake Eye]] ([COORD x=32657 y=32190 z=8 | here]). Say 'documents' and he will tell you to go see [[Ahmet]] in [[Ankrahmun]] (north of depot, [COORD x=33126 y=32810 z=6 | here]).<br />
Go to [[Ahmet]] and say 'documents'. Sometimes, he will hand over the documents for the gold with no fuss. However, he may tell you that [[Nomad (Basic)|Nomads]] from the oasis north of Ankrahmun ([COORD x=33132 y=32656 z=7 | here]) have killed someone dear to him, and asks you to kill at least one of them. When complete this task he will forge the [[Deed of Ownership]] for you for a fee of 1000 [[gp]].

The next step is to go find [[Percybald]] who is normally east of [[Carlin]] ([COORD x=32380 y=31785 z=7 | here])temple near the east gate. Speak to him and say 'disguise' and he will ask you to go take a [[Theatre Script|copy of the script]] in the prop room at the theatre (east of the temple). Once you get it go back to him, speak to him and say 'test'. He will say lines from the script and you'll have to say the line which follows according to the script. Eventually he will give you the [[Disguise Kit (Dwarf)|disguise kit]]. '''DONT USE IT YET!!'''

Go to [[Venore]] and find [[Nurik]] under the [[depot]] on the eastern side -1 ([COORD x=32931 y=32072 z=8 | here]). Disguise yourself with the [[Disguise Kit (Dwarf)|disguise kit]] (After using the disguise kit, you will have 5 minutes before you return to your normal appearance). Greet him and say 'dwarven bridge'. He will trade you the [[Deed of Ownership]] for the [[The Famous Mina Losa Painting]]. Report to [[Dorian]].

[[Image:The Thieves Guild Quest mission4.jpg]]

===Mission 5: Enforcing Debts===
Go to the smugglers cave ([COORD x=32554 y=32678 z=7 | here]), north west of [[Port Hope]]. When -1 go down the ladder North-east, then go along untill you can head south, follow that path down the ladder, below you will find a quest door with a chest. Retrieve the [[Stolen Golden Goblet]] and report to [[Dorian]].

[[Image:Thieves Quest Goblet.jpg]]

[[Image:The Thieves Guild Quest mission5.jpg]]

===Mission 6: Fishnapping===

There are 2 methods to complete this quest.

'''First method''': Go to [[Liberty Bay]]. Go the Jewel Shop, north east corner of Liberty Bay. Talk to [[Chantalle]] ([COORD x=32361 y=32794 z=7 | here]) and ask her for "key". She says it is not possible to bribe her, she also says that [[Herbert]], the postman in Liberty Bay, is easy to bribe.
Now go to [[Herbert]] (inside the depot) and say "hi", "letter", "gold", "yes". He will take 1,000 [[gps]]. from you in exchange for a [[Letter to Chantalle|letter]].
Now, head [[Venore]] and find NPC [[Carina]] (bank, east depot [COORD x=33015 y=32054 z=6 | here]). She will sell you a [[Precious Necklace]] for 5,000 [[gps]]. Once you have the precious necklace, go back to [[Liberty Bay]] and talk to [[Chantalle]] again. Say "Key", and she will give you the [[Theodore Loveless' Key|key]].

'''Second method''': You wear a [[Post Officers Hat]] in your helmet slot and talk to [[Bertram]] ([COORD x=32357 y=32797 z=7 | here]) if your character is '''female''' or [[Chantalle]] ([COORD x=32361 y=32794 z=7 | here]) if your character is '''male''' to get the key and complete this mission. ('''Note:''' ''You must be Arch Postman rank for this to work!''). Ask about a key and he/she will mention how handsome you look in your uniform and then give you the [[Theodore Loveless' Key|key]] for the door upstairs.

When you have the key, go upstairs of [[Chantalle]]'s Jewel Shop and open the door. Inside, '''use''' the [[Goldfish Bowl (Object)]] and a [[Fishnapped Goldfish]] will appear in your backpack. Report back to [[Dorian]].

[[Image:The Thieves Guild Quest mission6.jpg]]

===Mission 7: Blackmail===
Go to the guard's barracks in [[Venore]] (first road you come by when heading east from south west gate). Go two floors up (to [COORD x=32898 y=32145 z=4 | this room]) and 'use' the [[Chest of Drawers (Quest)]] in the room. Once you get the [[Compromising Letter]], report back to [[Dorian]].

[[File:The Thieves Guild Quest mission7.jpg]]

===Mission 8: Message===
*Go to the second ground floor in [[Dark Cathedral]] ([COORD x=32615 y=32473 z=9 | here]). Switch all six levers in the circle and then go through the [[Teleport]] in the Middle of the Floor.

<u>The switches are where the blue dots are.</u>

[[Image:Thieves Guild Quest Mission 8 a.jpg]]

*Once you have switched the first lever, head '''fast''' to the other levers as they reset after approximately 1 minute. Once all levers are enabled, the teleporter in the middle will be active.

'''''Be prepared to face: '''''[[Assassin]]s, [[Dark Monk]]s, [[Witch]]es, [[Dark Apprentice]], [[Dark Magician]]s, [[Demon Skeleton]]s, [[Stone Golem]]s and [[Hunter]]s.

*When you are in the 3rd walk north and 'use' the [[Note from the Thieves Guild|note]] between the demon trophies on the wall. The white circle is where you must place the warning.

[[Image:Thieves Guild Quest Mission 8 b.jpg]]

[[Image:Thieves Guild Quest Mission 8 c.jpg]]

If you are with a team, it may take some time for the [[Note from the Thieves Guild|note]] to reset once it is placed upon the wall.

Finally, report to [[Dorian]] and claim your reward in the room next to him.
