== Method ==
=== The Way to Yalahar ===
First make sure you are a member of the [[Tibian Explorer Society|Explorer Society]] and have the rank of novice or higher, otherwise you will not be able to talk to [[Captain Max]] in [[Liberty Bay]].

Go to [[Wyrdin]] in the centre of the [[Ivory Towers]] in [[Edron]] and talk to him about a mission. He will explain to you that a member of the [[Tibian Explorer Society]] is in [[Yalahar]] and would like his [[Research Notes]] of the unknown city. He will also tell you that [[Captain Max]] in [[Liberty Bay]] will take you to [[Yalahar]].

Alternatively, as a free account player, head to [[Wyrdin's Apprentice]] to the west of [[Kazordoon]] and talk to him about Yalahar. You will need to pay him 250 gold for the password ({{KW|whizzkid}}), then you can travel from Thais to Yalahar from [[Captain Bluebear]] on Thais' boat.

Once in Yalahar, find [[Timothy]] just north of [[depot]] and ask him for his research notes. He will give them to you and talk about a native friend who is in need of help. 

To finish the mission, go back to [[Wyrdin]] or [[Wyrdin's Apprentice]] and talk to him about the mission. You will be rewarded 500 [[gold]]. It is not mandatory to go to [[Wyrdin]] to continue the quest.

=== Searoutes around Yalahar ===
Finishing this mission is not mandatory either, however it is recommended as it will save you a lot of time and money. It allows you to travel from different cities to Yalahar rather than only from Liberty Bay.

Go to captain [[Karith]] in [[Yalahar]] east of [[Captain Cookie]], and ask him for a passage. You must give Karith '''only five''' (out of 8 possible) of the items described in the [[In Service of Yalahar Quest/Spoiler#Required Equipment|Required Equipment]] section to prove that traveling to other cities will be worth while. It does not matter which ones you handle, the 8 destinations will be unlocked.

Having the 5 items, you must handle them to [[Karith]] naming the city from which you acquired them and answering {{KW|yes}} to each of them. The items do not need to be handed in at once, but it saves money and time. You will now be able to travel with [[Karith]] to these cities: [[Ab'Dendriel]], [[Ankrahmun]], [[Carlin]], [[Darashia]], [[Liberty Bay]], [[Port Hope]], [[Thais]], and [[Venore]]. Karith respects the postmen, so the fare will be 10 gp cheaper for those who completed [[The Postman Missions Quest]].

First, talk to [[Palimuth]], east of [[Timothy]], and ask him for a {{KW|mission}}. He will ask you to help with some sewer malfunctions. You will need either a {{Ilink|Crowbar}} [[Crowbar]] or a {{Ilink|Whacking Driller of Fate}} [[Whacking Driller of Fate]]. There will be 4 locations where you need to go that have been marked with an X on your map. They are not very hard to find.

=== Mission 02: Watching the Watchmen ===
You have to find all 7 [[Yalahar Guard NPCs|guards]]. This is a good mission to explore the [[Quarters]] of [[Yalahar]].

It is faster by starting on the [[Foreigner Quarter]] (south-west), although you can also start at the [[Magician Quarter]] (north-east). You need to find the guards in the walls between the [[Quarters]] and ask them for their {{KW|report}}. They will then give you their report and you can pass through into the next quarter to find the next guard.

The guards' locations are shown on the map below (inside the [[Quarters]] and marked with blue flags):

[MINIMAP x=32792 y=31196 z=7 marks=(32695,31257) (32688,31192) (32741,31114) (32802,31103) (32883,31158) (32896,31231) (32854,31302)]

Recommended order: Foreign > Arena > Alchemist > Cemetery > Magician > Sunken > Factory > Trade.

After having all reports from the guards, go back to Palimuth. You can leave through the [[Magician Quarter]], which has no mechanism at the gate.

=== Mission 03: Death to the Deathbringer ===
From here onwards you will be able to do the mission following [[Palimuth]] (good side) or [[Yalahari (NPC)|Yalahari (Azerus)]] (bad side). The difficulty is the same for both sides but achievements differ. You will still need to ask mission of [[Palimuth]] even if you choose to not help him, otherwise sealed doors will not open.

* Doing all missions following [[Palimuth]] side will give you the achievement [[Follower of Palimuth]]. 
* Following [[Azerus]] (Yalahari), you will get the achievement [[Follower of Azerus]].
* If you do at least one mission from the opposite side, you will get the [[Turncoat]] achievement.

Which addon you will get for the outfit in the end is decided on ''Mission 09: Decision''.

[[Palimuth]] will give you [[History of the Augur I (Book)|two notes]] to read (in the small room on the left) and then send you to his superior for this mission. [[Yalahari (NPC)|Yalahari (Azerus)]] will also ask you to read [[Manifest_of_the_Yalahari_I_(Book)|two notes]] he gives you and request you help him with a problem in the [[Alchemist Quarter]] — he wants you to retrieve an [[The Alchemists' Formulas]] as well as kill the 3 plague bearers. Palimuth does npt like the idea of Azerus getting a hold of the formulas and asks you to burn them by using them on an [[oven]]. You will now be able to use the [[Mechanism (Teleporter)|mechanism]] to the [[Alchemist Quarter]].

During this mission, you will face [[Death Blob]]s, [[Mutated Human]]s, [[Mutated Rat]]s, [[Acid Blob]]s, [[Slime]]s, [[Mutated Tiger]]s and [[Bog Raider]]s. First, you will need to kill the three plague bearers and then get [[The Alchemists' Formulas]]. Then, head back to either [[Palimuth]] (good side) or [[Yalahari (NPC)|Yalahari (Azerus)]] (bad side). Note that only the player gaining the most experience from each boss will complete that part. So if you are doing this mission with a team, you will need to kill each boss several times.

Additionally, since the [[Summer Update 2012]], the hitpoints and spell damage of the diseased bosses have been reduced ({{Post|33221962|announced here}}), and a shortcut to [[Diseased Fred]] was added.

Players can find [[Diseased Dan]] [COORD x=32707 y=31189 z=8 | here], [[Diseased Bill]] [COORD x=32671 y=31123 z=7 | here], and [[Diseased Fred]] [COORD x=32704 y=31124 z=7 | here].

=== Mission 05: Food or Fight ===
On this mission you are asked to find a druid by the name of [[Tamerin]], on the [[Arena Quarter]], [COORD x=32659 y=31213 z=8 | here]. You will get permission to use the gates [[Mechanism (Teleporter)|mechanism]].

[[Yalahari (NPC)|Yalahari (Azerus)]] wants you to get [[Tamerin]] to breed warbeasts to help them take back control of all the [[Quarters]]. Whereas [[Palimuth]] begs for you to request that [[Tamerin]] breeds cattle for food. But [[Tamerin]] will only listen to your mission after you fulfill 2 favors for him:
* First: bring him a flask of [[Animal Cure]]. It can be bought from [[Siflind]] on [[Nibelor]]
** You need to have started this mission in order to buy it, as well as the [[Ice Islands Quest]] started for access to [[Nibelor]]. If you are unable to go to Nibelor, you can ask someone else who has the quest and access to get it for you, as long as they are on this current mission.
* Second: kill [[Morik the Gladiator]] and bring his [[Helmet (Morik)|helmet]] as proof. He is located [COORD x=32685 y=31242 z=6 | here], at the last room going around clockwise on the first floor.

Report back to Tamerin as he will listen to your request and you can now make your choice:
* Asking for {{KW|Cattle}} will help [[Palimuth]] (good side)
* Asking for {{KW|Warbeasts}} will help [[Yalahari (NPC)|Yalahari (Azerus)]] (bad side).

=== Mission 06: Frightening Fuel ===
[[File:Cemetery.png|thumb]] [[Yalahari (NPC)|Yalahari (Azerus)]] orders you to travel to the [[Cemetery Quarter]] and find the [[Strange Carving]]. He gives you a [[Ghost Charm]] and tells you to charge it with the tormented souls of the ghosts there to be used as an energy source. [[Palimuth]] wants the [[Charged Ghost Charm]] in order to free those souls. You can use the [[Cemetery Quarter]] [[mechanism]] now.

Go to the big building in the [[Cemetery Quarter]] and use the [[Ghost Charm]] on the [[Strange Carving]] at the back of the room. Kill [[Tormented Ghost]]s and use the [[Ghost Residue]] on the charm thrice — this will make 2 more [[Tormented Ghost]]s appear. At this point the [[Ghost Charm]] will then change into a [[Charged Ghost Charm]].

[[File:Yalahar Quest Mission 6.jpg]]

* When choosing the Good side: go to [[Palimuth]], ask him about the {{KW|mission}}, and hand in the charm.
* When choosing the Bad side: go to [[Yalahari (NPC)|Yalahari (Azerus)]], ask him about the {{KW|mission}}, and give it back.

=== Mission 07: A Fishy Mission ===
* Bad side: [[Yalahari (NPC)|Yalahari (Azerus)]] will send you for a new {{KW|mission}} — to go to the [[Sunken Quarter]] and kill the [[Quara]] Leaders, [[Inky]], [[Splasher]] and [[Sharptooth]]. These bosses can sometimes do a lot of damage.
* Good side: rather than fighting any Quara leaders, [[Palimuth]]'s {{KW|mission}} will send you to find the cause for the Quaras aggressive behavior. Find [[Maritima]] ([COORD x=32905 y=31167 z=13 | here]), ask her about the {{KW|Quara}}, and she will explain what their problem is.

You will now need a [[Helmet of the Deep]] or a [[Depth Galea]] as you are going underwater on the [[Sunken Quarter]]. Many [[Quara]] will be faced during this mission. Talk to [[Tarak]] ([COORD x=32917 y=31199 z=7 | here]), ask him for a {{KW|trip}} there. Wear your helmet and jump into the water.

The maps below are a suggestion, they indicate the way to navigate while encountering less of the strong quara. Until the 2nd map, there will only be [[Quara Constrictor]]s and [[Quara Mantassin]]s. On the last floor, there are also [[Quara Hydromancer]]s, [[Quara Pincher]]s, and [[Quara Predator]]s.

=== Mission 09: Decision ===
You now need to decide between supporting [[Palimuth]] or the [[Yalahari]]'s goal. You must first say {{KW|mission}} to both of them, to which they will both tell you you need to decide between themself or the other.
* Good side: to choose [[Palimuth]]'s side, go to him, and simply ask him for a {{KW|mission}} again.
* Bad side: to choose [[Yalahari (NPC)|Yalahari (Azerus)]]' side, go to him and say {{KW|mission}}. 

Whomever you choose will ask you if you are choosing them, and then just say {{KW|yes}} to confirm your decision. This will complete mission 9. 

Depending on your previous choices, you will obtain one of the following achievements:
* [[Follower of Azerus]] - when the bad side was chosen for all missions.
* [[Follower of Palimuth]] - when the good side was chosen for all missions.
* [[Turncoat]] - when a mix of good and bad side missions were done.
* None: if all missions were done on one side, but at this mission it was decided to choose the other side (i.e., did all Palimuth's good missions and joined Azerus, or vice versa).

You can now ask for the next mission.

=== Mission 10: The Final Battle ===
Depending on the player's level, a team or stronger helpers might be needed. Lower level players will have issues due to damage and mana drain. Be aware that there is a time limit of around 12 minutes to kill [[Azerus]].

Someone who has not done the quest yet has to click on the machine at the center of the room to start the fight:
* [[Azerus]] will first appear with [[Rift Worm]]s — kill the [[Rift Worm]]s and ignore Azerus as he is extremely hard to kill at this point.
* Azerus will then teleport to another place in the room and summon [[Rift Brood]]s, use [[Earth Damage]] on them while still ignoring Azerus.
* Azerus will then summon [[Rift Scythe]]s, use [[Fire Damage]].
* Azerus will then summon [[War Golem]]s, but now it will be much easier to damage Azerus, so focus on Azerus while ignoring the golems.
* Once [[Azerus]] is dead, his body will turn into a [[Magic Forcefield]]. All players must step in the forcefield and be teleported to a room where many rift monsters can be seen from above. The message {{Sound|You have a very bad feeling about this...}} will be displayed.
* Walk to the most northern point in the room (as shown in the image below). The quest is finished for the character once the message {{Sound|It seems by defeating Azerus you have stopped this army from entering your world! Better leave this ghastly place forever.}} is displayed.
