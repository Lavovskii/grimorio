==Required Equipment==
*[[Rope]]
*[[Nomad Parchment]]
*[[Scimitar]]
*[[Water (Liquid)|Water]]

==Method==
Before starting this quest you will need to attain a [[Nomad Parchment]] from [[Nomad (Basic)|Nomad]]s and talk to [[Arito]] ([COORD x=33069 y=32886 z=6 | here]) before you try to enter the cave.<br />'''Note: '''If you do not do this before attempting to enter, you will be unable to enter and will lose your scimitar and water.

==Deathlist==
Before you begin this quest, you need to find a [[Nomad Parchment]] of the [[Nomad (Basic)|Nomad]]s' deathlist. This can be very tedious as the drop rate is incredibly low. As an alternative, you can purchase the parchment from other players. Once you have the deathlist, travel to [[Arito]] in [[Ankrahmun]] and tell him about the {{KW|nomads}} list you discovered. He reveals himself to be an ex-nomad and wants you to help him make peace with the nomads. He tells you how to get inside the [[Nomad Cave]]:

The entrance is located east of the mountain pass between [[Darashia]] and [[Ankrahmun]] ([COORD x=33207 y=32544 z=7 | here]), in a little grotto. There is a crack in the surface. Place a [[Scimitar]] on this tile before pouring [[Water (Liquid)|Water]] on the tile 2 steps to the right of it. A gate appears.<br />
'''Note: '''Once you have entered the gate with one player, the gate will close and you will need a new scimitar to enter with another player. This means that if you are doing this quest in groups, you will need one scimitar for each player.

==Nomads' Cave==
[[Image:Aritos_Entrance.jpg|400px]]

[[Image:Nomads_cave_entrace.JPG]]

Through the gate you will find yourself in a small cave with a teleport. Walk through this and you will be in a much larger cave just below you, full of [[Nomad (Basic)|Nomad]]s and [[Scorpion]]s. There are also many [[Rotworm]]s and [[Carrion Worm]]s further in the cave.

[COORD x=33279 y=32526 z=9 | Deep in the cave] is the man in charge, [[Muhad]]. Tell him about {{KW|Arito}}, and say {{KW|yes}}. [[Muhad]] will accept Arito's wish, and asks you to help the Nomad tribe with another mission (see [[Nomads Land Quest]]). You can leave up the stairs, via the back entrance.

Go back to [[Arito]]. Say {{KW|yes}} and {{KW|acquitted}}. He will reward you with 50 [[Platinum Coin]]s.<br>
After completing this quest, you can reenter the nomad cave through the back entrance to hunt [[Nomad (Basic)|Nomad]]s '''only''' if you agreed to help [[Muhad]] (see [[Nomads Land Quest]]).<br>
