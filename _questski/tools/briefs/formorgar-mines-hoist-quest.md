## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=Formorgar Mines Hoist Quest}}

== Requirements ==
* Access to the [[Formorgar Mines]] ([[Ice Islands Quest]] up to ''Formorgar Mines 1: The Mission'')

== Method ==
In order to gain access to the hoist, you must find the [[Paper]] and the [[Map (Brown)|Map]] within the [[Formorgar Mines]]. You do not need to carry the items with you to use it, only to open the box containing them.

=== Obtaining the Map ===
{{Box Right|content=
{{:Floor Plan of the Formorgar Mines (Book)|Display}}<br />
The [[Map (Brown)|Map]] you will receive.
}}

Follow [[The Ice Islands Quest/Spoiler#Formorgar Mines 1: The Mission|The Ice Islands Quest - Formorgar Mines 1: The Mission]] until you reach the [[Formorgar Mines]].

On the first floor you will find [[Ice Golem]]s, [[Novice of the Cult|Novices of the Cult]], [[Mammoth]]s, [[Stone Golem]]s, [[Gargoyle]]s, a few [[Bandit]]s and a single [[Hunter]]. From the entrance, head south and stop on the third turn west. Go west until the second turn south. Go south until you hit the wall. Go east until the second turn south. Go south and turn east when you can. Continue east all the way to a [[Ramp]] down. An alternate way to save time is through the [[Ice Portal]] in the [[Halls of Hope]] if you have met the requirements. In the first map, the blue flag is the starting point coming from the [[Formorgar Glacier]], after entering you will find [[Adept of the Cult|Adepts]], [[Acolyte of the Cult|Acolyte]]s and [[Novice of the Cult|Novices of the Cult]], as well as [[Ice Golem]]s. The star mark on the second map shows the starting point from the Halls of Hope and the path continues from the star mark in the first map. The [[Lion Trophy Quest]] is also north-east from the blue flag, and the Hoist is east from it.

[MINIMAP x=32179 y=31206 z=9 marks=(32146,31181) (32109,31183) (32110,31235) (32173,31237) (32146,31121) (32174,31278) (32227,31277) (32190,31153) (32189,31238)]

[MINIMAP x=32216 y=31097 z=10 marks=(32209,31036) (32192,31045) (32192,31155) (32251,31044) (32241,31042)]

On the second floor you will find [[Acolyte of the Cult|Acolyte]]s and [[Novice of the Cult|Novices of the Cult]], as well as [[Ice Golem]]s. Go west from the ramp just a little until you find a [[Pile of Bones (Quest)]] on the northern side of the mine. Open this to obtain the [[Floor Plan of the Formorgar Mines (Book)|Floor Plan of the Formorgar Mines]].

Now that you have received the [[Map (Brown)|Map]] you can use the hoist to go from floor -2 to floor -3.

=== Obtaining the Paper ===
{{Box Right|content={{:Instructions to the Formorgar Mines Hoist (Book)|Display}}<br />The [[Paper]] you will receive.}}

Continuing from the ''[[#Obtaining the Map|Pile of Bones (Quest)]]'', go back east and up the ramp you came from.

Now, go the star mark on the first map and go down [COORD x=32190 y=31153 z=9 | there].

This floor is the one from the second map, return to the star mark but continue to the east a little more until you reach the cross mark and go down [COORD x=32241 y=31044 z=10 | here]. On this floor you will face [[Enlightened of the Cult|Enlightened]], [[Adept of the Cult|Adepts]], [[Acolyte of the Cult|Acolytes]], [[Nightmare]]s, [[Destroyer]]s, a [[Lost Soul]], [[Ice Golem]]s, and [[Crystal Spider]]s, and possibly other lured creatures. From the ramp you will immediately be ambushed by 2-3 [[Enlightened of the Cult|Enlightened]] and 1-2 [[Crystal Spider]]s. Go west until the second turn south; you will find 1-2 [[Nightmare]]s and a [[Destroyer]] with a [[Nightmare]] at the southern turn. Go south until you hit a dead end; you will encounter 1-2 [[Nightmare]]s, a few [[Crystal Spider]]s, a [[Lost Soul]] at the hoist, and many [[Enlightened of the Cult|Enlightened]]. A bit north of the dead end, head west and turn south when you can; you will find [[Crystal Spider]]s and a [[Nightmare]]. Go south all the way; there are lots of [[Enlightened of the Cult|Enlightened]] here, 1-2 [[Crystal Spider]]s and 1-2 [[Nightmare]]s. Go east all the way and then south until a dead end; just [[Enlightened of the Cult|Enlightened]] here and possibly a [[Nightmare]] at the end. A bit north of the dead end, turn west and proceed until the ramp up; there are [[Enlightened of the Cult|Enlightened]], [[Adept of the Cult|Adepts]], [[Crystal Spider]]s, and 1-2 [[Nightmare]]s. Go up [COORD x=32106 y=31237 z=11 | here]. 
This Third map show the way starting from the cross mark. The exclamation mark is the location of the hoist, but for the moment you can only use it to go up.

[MINIMAP x=32155 y=31143 z=11 marks=(32242,31047) (32152,31046) (32152,31162) (32111,31162) (32112,31204) (32151,31204) (32151,31236) (32107,31239) (32160,31128)]

This floor is more relaxed. You will face [[Enlightened of the Cult|Enlightened]], [[Adept of the Cult|Adepts]], [[Acolyte of the Cult|Acolytes]], [[Novice of the cult|Novices]], [[Ice Golem]]s, [[Crystal Spider]]s, and if followed exactly, a single [[Destroyer]]. From the ramp, go west until the second turn north. Go north and turn west when possible. Continue west until you can turn north. Go north until you find a ramp; at the end of the cave you will encounter a bunch of [[Crystal Spider]]s and [[Ice Golem]]s with the single [[Destroyer]]. Go down the ramp [COORD x=32025 y=31147 z=10 | here]. Fourth map:

[MINIMAP x=32070 y=31202 z=10 marks=(32067,31236) (32067,31192) (32026,31193) (32026,31148) (32105,31237)] 

This is the final floor; you will encounter some [[Enlightened of the Cult|Enlightened]], a [[Nightmare]] and 1-2 [[Plaguesmith]]s. Go north from the ramp until you hit a dead end [COORD x=32029 y=31110 z=11 | here]. The dead end contains the [[Box]], open it to obtain the [[Instructions to the Formorgar Mines Hoist (Book)|Instructions to the Formorgar Mines Hoist]]. Fifth and final map:
[MINIMAP x=32027 y=31129 z=11 marks=(32029,31148) (32028,31108)]
<gallery mode="nolines">
File:Formorgar Mines Hoist Box.png
</gallery>

Now that you have received the [[Paper]], you can use the hoist to:
* Go from floor 3 to 2,
* Go from floor 2 to 3,
* Go from floor 2 to 1,
* Change the destination of the hoist on floor 2.

Note that you '''cannot''' go from floor 1 to 2 if you haven't obtained the map.

To leave the mines, backtrack to the blue flag on the fourth and fifth map and to the exclamation mark on the third, to get to the hoist ([COORD x=32155 y=31126 z=11 | here]) if you want to return through the [[Formorgar Glacier]] or to the cross mark and then to the star on the second map to quickly return to the [[Halls of Hope]].
