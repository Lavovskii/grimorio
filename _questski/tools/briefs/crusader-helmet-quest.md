## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler|name=Crusader Helmet Quest}}
==Required Equipment==
*[[Rope]]
*Any [[melee]] [[weapon]] or a [[firebug]] to break the [[spider web]]s on the way.

==Method==
There are two routes to the Crusader Helmet, the original route is through the [[Dwarf Mines]], but since [[Updates/8.1]] it is also possible to go through [[Mount Sternum]] and its [[Mount Sternum/Undead Cave|Undead Cave]], which is faster.

=== Mount Sternum Route ===
*Starting from [[Thais]], head to [[Mount Sternum]] and go down [COORD x=32450 y=32070 z=7 | here].
*Walk north-west, you will face quite some [[Cyclops]]es and possibly some [[Cyclops Drone]]s. Go down [COORD x=32418 y=32041 z=8 | here].
*You are now in the [[Mount Sternum/Undead Cave]] and will face [[Skeleton]]s, walk a little bit north and go down a hole [COORD x=32416 y=32024 z=9 | here].
*You will meet [[Skeleton]]s and [[Ghoul]]s. Walk north-east and go down a hole [COORD x=32427 y=31989 z=10 | here].
*You have now entered the [[Dwarf Mines]], you will face 3 [[Dwarf Soldier]]s when going down and more [[Dwarf Soldier]]s and [[Dwarf Guard]]s when walking further. Walk north and north-east and go down a hole [COORD x=32438 y=31967 z=11 | here].
*Continue with the [[#Last_Part|last part]].

=== Dwarf Mines Route ===
*Head to [[Dwarf Mines|Pick 'N Shovel Mine]] west of [[Kazordoon]], located [COORD x=32548 y=31987 z=7 | here].
*Head east and down the hole.
[[File:Crusader_Helmet_Quest_Map_01.gif]]
*Immediately head down another hole and then head far to the west. 
[[File:Crusader_Helmet_Quest_Map_02.gif]]
*Head to the north and west and continue down another hole.
[[File:Crusader_Helmet_Quest_Map_03.gif]]
*Continue with the [[#Last_Part|last part]].

=== Last Part ===
Regardless if you took the ''Mount Sternum Route'' or the ''Dwarf Mines Route'', the last part is the same.

*You will meet [[Dwarf Guard]]s. Head east continuing further down in the mine, [COORD x=32469 y=31972 z=12 | here].
[[File:Crusader_Helmet_Quest_Map_04.gif]]
*There will be a lot of [[Dwarf Guard]]s. Go north and through the [[level]] 35 gate of expertise.
[[File:Crusader_Helmet_Quest_Map_05.gif]]
*Head down the hole [COORD x=32477 y=31943 z=13 | here] and prepare to face [[Giant Spider]]s (there are 4 in the entire place, but they come one by one, and you should only meet 3 on the way to where the crusader helmet is). There are some [[spider web]]s blocking the way. Use a [[melee]] [[weapon]] or a [[firebug]] to break it and pass through.
*The reward (a [[Crusader Helmet]]) is located in a skeleton body at the far west end of the tunnel, [COORD x=32426 y=31940 z=14 | here].
