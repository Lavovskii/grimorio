'''Method:'''

Talk to NPC [[Hector The Mentor|Hector the Mentor]] in the Thais penisula area (here) and take the mission he is offering you. 

After taking the mission head down the Rabid Wolf Cave. 
[[File:2024-09-15 162127668 King Waver Hotkey.png|left|frameless|109x109px]]

Kill 20 x [[Rabid Wolf]] and head back to [[Hector The Mentor]]. [This step is optional]

After reporting your mission head back into the cave to fight the cause of the rabid disease.
[[File:Ravnous Madness Quest 2.png|left|frameless]]
[[File:Ravnous Madness Quest 3.png|left|frameless]]
[[File:Ravnous Madness Quest 4.png|left|frameless]]
[[File:Ravnous Madness Quest 5.png|left|frameless]]

You will encounter one [[Rabid Wolf]].

You will encounter two [[Cave Rat]]s.

You will encounter many [[Cave Rat]]s and many [[Rabid Wolf]]. Opening the chest marked with X will reward you 2x [[Small Health Potion]].

You will encounter many [[Rabid Wolf]]. If the gate around X is open you also might encounter up to two [[Ragged Rabid Wolf]].

After crossing the quest door you will encounter the mini boss [[Trollwut]]. Fight him with all your power! (Note: Killing him while standing inside the quest door will not count and you will have to kill him again.) 
[[File:Ravnous Madness Quest 6.png|left|600x600px]]

After killing [[Trollwut]] head to the north and enter the quest gate. Use the corpse (red circle) to obtain a Maddening Mushroom (Quest Item).
[[File:Ravnous Madness Quest 7.png|left|600x600px]]

To obtain your vocation bound reward enter the corresponding quest gate: 

Blue – '''Knight''': [[Protection Amulet]]

Purple – '''Paladin''': 25 x [[Poison Arrow]] and 25 x [[Burst Arrow]]

Orange – '''Sorcerer''': 10 x [[Fire Field Rune]], 10 x [[Energy Field Rune]] and 10 x [[Poison Field Rune]]

Green – '''Druid''': 10 x [[Intense Healing Rune]], 10 x [[Cure Poison Rune]] and 10 x [[Destroy Field Rune]]

To complete the Quest report back to [[Hector The Mentor]] ([COORD x=32476 y=32286 z=7 | here]). He will ask you to bring the Maddening Mushroom to [[Benjamin]] in Thais depot ([COORD x=32350 y=32219 z=7 | here]) so he can send it to the Academy in Edron for further investigations.
