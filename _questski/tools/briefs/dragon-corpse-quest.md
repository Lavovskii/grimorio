## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name= Dragon Corpse Quest}}

==Required Equipment==
*[[Shovel]]
*[[Scythe]]
*2-4 [[Small Health Potion]]s
*Food (It is a good idea to bring extra food so you don't get stuck at the dragon cave with too few hit points. [[Egg]]s, [[Cookie]]s, [[Cherry|Cherries]] and [[Blueberry|Blueberries]] are a good [[Capacity|cap]]-saving [[food]])

==Method==
'''Note:''' You need 102 [[oz]] ([[Copper Shield]] + [[Legion Helmet]] + [[Bag]]) for this quest.

* First, get a [[Shovel]], a [[Scythe]] and 2-4 [[Small Health Potion]]s. Now go to the Bear Cave located east of [[Rookgard]], [COORD x=32146 y=32208 z=7 | here].
[[File:Dragon Corpse Quest Map 1.png|400px|Entrace]]<br />

*Now go down the hole; you will find many [[bear]]s here. Run past them or try to kill them and do not get trapped. The cave splits in two ways: Choose the southern path. There's a small narrow passage, stand there if you are planning to kill the [[bear]]s, so only that two can attack you at any given time. Do '''not''' use the [[Small Health Potion]]s yet. At the end, there's a pool of water and a hole, open it with your [[Shovel]] and go down.
[[File:Dragon Corpse Quest Map 2.png|400px|Enter the hole]]

*Here, it might be advisable to eat [[food]] and wait to have full [[HP]]. Now use your [[Scythe]] to cut the [[Wheat]].<br />[[File:Dragon Corpse Quest Map 3.png|400px|Use Scythe]]

*Now you have to walk over 4 [[Fire|Large Fire Fields]] and 1 [[Fire|Medium Fire Field]], and back. Run through and do not stop until you find the [[Dead Dragon]]. Drink potions when your [[HP]] drops into red. Inside the dead dragon you will find a [[bag]] containing a [[Copper Shield]] and a [[Legion Helmet]].
[[Image:Dragon_corpse_quest_loot.jpg|300px|You have found a bag.]]

*Take these, and '''do not wait''' for your [[HP]] to fill back up, return quickly to the entrance instead, you will save potions doing so. If you do it fast enough you will need only 2 [[Small Health Potion]]s and some [[food]]. If you are level 7 or higher you can finish this quest without using any potions, just get there and loot the dragon corpse, wait till full hp and get out.
