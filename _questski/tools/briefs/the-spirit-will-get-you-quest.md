## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=The Spirit Will Get You Quest}}
=== Method ===
'''Note: To be able to do this quest you must have access to [[Northern Zao]], that is you need to have accepted the [[Wrath of the Emperor Quest]].'''

Coming from either the west or the east head towards the stairs which lead up into the [[Souleater Mountains]]. On the way there you might face all different kinds of monsters.
[[File:The_Spirit_Will_Get_You_Quest_Map_1.png]]

Now go to the southwest but watch out not to get trapped since there are lots of monsters in these mountains. Here you will find [[Cranky Lizard Crone]] (marked by the blue circle in the picture). Ask her for a '''mission''' and accept it. She wants you to find three [[Faded Last Will]]s. Follow the arrow and go upstairs.
[[File:The_Spirit_Will_Get_You_Quest_Map_2.png]]

The first [[Faded Last Will]] is located in a [[Box (Brown Quest)]] upstairs near the ramp (blue circle), [COORD x=33177 y=31021 z=5 | here]. To get to the second [[Faded Last Will]] you have to head south until you reach some stairs. Go up, kill or run all monsters and collect it from a [[Box (Quest)]] (blue circle), [COORD x=33161 y=31056 z=4 | here].<br>
[[File:The_Spirit_Will_Get_You_Quest_Map_3.png]]

Go back downstairs to find the third [[Faded Last Will]]. Go a few steps to the south until you reach another ramp and go downstairs. Again kill or run all monsters and collect it from a [[Chest (Quest)]] (blue circle), [COORD x=33166 y=31056 z=6 | here].
[[File:The_Spirit_Will_Get_You_Quest_Map_4.png]]

Once you collected all [[Faded Last Will]]s head back to [[Cranky Lizard Crone]] and tell her about your '''mission'''.
