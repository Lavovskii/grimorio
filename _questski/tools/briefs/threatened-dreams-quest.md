=== Troubled Animals ===
====Creation of Legends====

<div style="float:left;padding-right:5px;">[MINIMAP x=32754 y=31928 z=7 marks=none]<br />The Poacher Caves</div>
Your first mission is to enter the [[Poacher Caves]] ([COORD x=32754 y=31928 z=7 | here]) and find a {{Ilink|Book with Old Legends}}[[Book with Old Legends]]. Go through the southern passages and descend to floor -4. Open the chest behind a door in the southern part of the room, [COORD x=32787 y=31975 z=11 | here].

Once you have the book, travel to [[Ankrahmun]] and ask the shopkeeper [[Ahmet]] (next to the bank) about your '''book'''. He will offer to help as long as you are willing to pay 5,000 gold. Agree to receive {{Ilink|Book with Old Legends (Doctored)}}[[Book with Old Legends (Doctored)]].

Return to the room in Poacher Caves where you first found the book, and '''use''' it on the table, right next to the dripping candles. If done correctly, the book will vanish and your quest log will be updated with the message {{Server Message|You are placing the book on the table, hopefully the poachers will discover it soon.}}. Report your success to Alkestios. He will send you to [[Ikassis]] for your next mission.

=== The Fairy Treasure ===
==== The Tooth Fairy ====
Go to [[Tooth Fairy]] south of the [[Suntower (Blessing)|Suntower]] outside [[Ab'Dendriel]] and ask her for a '''mission'''. She will ask you to deliver some [[Present (Fairy)|Presents]] to several children who have recently lost a [[Milk Tooth]]. You have to visit [[Quero]]'s daughter's room in [[Thais]] ([COORD x=32390 y=32220 z=6 | here]), [[Allen]]'s son's room in [[Venore]] ([COORD x=33000 y=32061 z=5 | here]), and [[Rowenna]]'s daughter's room in [[Carlin]] ([COORD x=32325 y=31794 z=6 | here]). In each room, [[use]] the [[Chest of Drawers (Quest)]] to obtain a [[Milk Tooth]] (''You have found a milk tooth.'') and [[use]] one of the [[Present (Fairy)|Presents]] on a [[Bed]] (Quest) near it (''You are placing the present for Quero's/Rowenna's daughter/Allen's son on the pillow.''). Report back to [[Tooth Fairy]] by saying '''mission''' and she will give you a [[Part of an Old Map (Upper Left)]].

In addition, [[Tooth Fairy]] has an '''optional''' task for you that consists of obtaining several sorts of teeth for her collection. She wants an [[Orc Tooth]], [[Shark Teeth]], [[Vampire Teeth]], a [[Perfect Behemoth Fang]], a [[Carrion Worm Fang]], and [[Werewolf Fangs]]. She will reward you with a [[White Pearl]] for each of these items, and once you've brought her all of them and you've finished the main mission above you'll receive the [[Toothfairy Assistant]] [[achievement]].

====Tired Trees====
Find the [[Tired Tree]] north of the Fields of Glory, [COORD x=32309 y=31651 z=7 | here], and ask for a '''map'''. The tree will tell you that it wants a bedtime story about Dryads and will ask you for your help. The story is held within a book called [[The Seeds of Life (Book)|The Seeds of Life]], which is located in Ab'Dendriel's [[Ab'Dendriel Libraries|library]], but you don't have to go there. Ask for a '''mission''' or '''map''' again, confirm that you have found the story and then say the books name, '''The Seeds of Life'''. You will receive a [[Part of an Old Map (Bottom Left)]].

These Fae need your help scratching their backs since they are stuck in this form and their stony backs are itching, but they cannot scratch themselves. After asking for a '''mission''', use the [[rake]] on all 5 sentient stones around the Grumpy Stone. Report your '''mission''' to proceed and receive a [[Part of an Old Map (Upper Right)]]

=== A Dream Come True ===
'''Required equipment:'''
* Any [[Melee]] Weapon
* 2x {{Ilink|Small Enchanted Amethyst}} [[Small Enchanted Amethyst]]
* 2x {{Ilink|Small Enchanted Emerald}} [[Small Enchanted Emerald]]
* 2x {{Ilink|Small Enchanted Ruby}} [[Small Enchanted Ruby]]
* 2x {{Ilink|Small Enchanted Sapphire}} [[Small Enchanted Sapphire]]

Find the NPC [[Maelyrra]] at the [COORD x=33485 y=32237 z=7 | center] of [[Feyrist]], and offer your help to combat the invasion of nightmare creatures by rebuilding the magical barrier that protects Feyrist from [[Roshamuul]].
Your first '''mission''' is to kill 200 [[Enfeebled Silencer]]s and [[Weakened Frazzlemaw]]s in cave near the mountain [COORD x=33546 y=32267 z=7 | here], as well as the boss [[Kroazur]], which is accessible from the portal [COORD x=33619 y=32303 z=9 | here]
(''Note: Kroazur's lair is free access. You can kill it once every two real life hours. It drops good loot''). Report your '''mission''' back to Maelyrra and ask for the next one.

Your next '''mission''' is to free 5 fairies that have been taken prisoner by evil creatures (using a melee weapon to break the energy barrier and then using the fairy) and also retrieve the [[Dark Moon Mirror]]. They are located on three different caves, all filled with Feyrist dark creatures:
* [COORD x=33424 y=32215 z=7 | This small cave], north-west from the town area. There is one fairy here.
*[COORD x=33492 y=32292 z=7 | This small cave], south-west from the town area. There is one fairy here.
* [COORD x=33554 y=32211 z=7 | This large cave], south-east from the earth shrine. This is the largest cave, with two floors. The remaining three fairies and the Dark Moon Mirror are located here. On floor -1, there are two fairies: [COORD x=33577 y=32185 z=8 | here] and [COORD x=33622 y=32214 z=8 | here]. On floor -2, the fairy is [COORD x=33559 y=32202 z=9 | here] and the [[Dark Moon Mirror]] is [COORD x=33593 y=32214 z=9 | here], inside a [[Tree Stump (Feyrist)|Tree Stump]].

''Note: After being used, it will take one minute for the fairy to appear again so it can be freed by another player''. Report your mission back to [[Maelyrra]].

When you ask for your next mission, Maelyrra will tell you to obtain an [[Empty Starlight Vial]] from [[Aurita]] and a [[Dark Sun Catcher]] from [[Taegen]]. You can ask them for these items after you complete the [[Threatened Dreams Quest/Spoiler#An Unlikely Couple|An Unlikely Couple]] mission. 
After you finish this mission, talk to Taegen and give him 2 of each small enchanted gems (Small [[Small Enchanted Amethyst|Amethysts]], [[Small Enchanted Emerald|Emeralds]], [[Small Enchanted Ruby|Rubies]] and [[Small Enchanted Sapphire|Sapphires]]). Once you have the items, go back to [[Maelyrra]] and ask for a '''mission'''.

Now you need to collect three different light types using the items you just received:
* To collect Sun light, go to the beach shaped like a snake head on the northeast of the island ([COORD x=33513 y=32165 z=7 | here]), during [[Time|day]], and use the [[Dark Sun Catcher]] so it becomes a [[Shining Sun Catcher]].
* To collect Moon light, go to the glade north-east of town ([COORD x=33523 y=32211 z=7 | here]) and use the [[Dark Moon Mirror]] during [[night]], it will change into a [[Lit Moon Mirror]].
* To collect Star light, go up the mountain to the [[Energy Shrine]] ([COORD x=33529 y=32301 z=4 | here]) and use the [[Empty Starlight Vial]] during [[night]], it will change into a [[Gleaming Starlight Vial]].

After charging all three light items go back to Maelyrra and report your mission.

Your final mission will be to use the light sources in specific places around [[Feyrist]] to repair the magical barrier. Each item must be used 5 times, meaning you will have to visit 15 different places.
* Find the 5 Stone Mosaics and use the [[Shining Sun Catcher]] on them during the [[Time|day]] ([COORD x=33495 y=32234 z=7 | map] [MINIMAP x=33495 y=32234 z=7 marks=(33420,32245,7) (33483,32192,7) (33545,32156,7) (33546,32207,7) (33566,32244,7)]).
* Find the 5 Dream Bird Trees and use the [[Gleaming Starlight Vial]] on them at [[night]] ([COORD x=33496 y=32234 z=7 | map] [MINIMAP x=33496 y=32234 z=7 marks=(33457,32298,7) (33442,32199,7) (33529,32187,7) (33540,32246,7) (33562,32258,7)]).
* Find the 5 Moon Sculptures and use the [[Lit Moon Mirror]] on them at [[night]] (four on ground level [COORD x=33495 y=32233 z=7 | here] [MINIMAP x=33495 y=32233 z=7 marks=(33388,32216,7) (33517,32193,7) (33595,32184,7) (33549,32219,7)], one on mountain [COORD x=33511 y=32319 z=6 | here] [MINIMAP x=33511 y=32319 z=6 marks=(33511,32319,6)]).

<div style="float: center; clear: right; padding: 0 0 0.5em 0.5em; text-align: center;">[MINIMAP x=33495 y=32234 z=7 marks=(33420,32245) (33483,32192) (33545,32156) (33546,32207) (33566,32244)] 
[MINIMAP x=33496 y=32234 z=7 marks=(33457,32298) (33442,32199) (33529,32187) (33540,32246) (33562,32258)] 
[MINIMAP x=33497 y=32234 z=7 marks=(33388,32216) (33517,32193) (33595,32184) (33549,32219) (33519,32305)]</div>

After repairing all 15 spots, go back to [[Maelyrra]] to report your success. She will inform you that the barrier is strong once again thanks to your efforts and gift you a [[Blossom Bag]]. Once the items have been completed don't recharge them before delivering the quest otherwise you will get the quest version of the item which has no defensive stat and there is currently no way of fixing it (bug).

'''Note:''' After repairing all magical barriers, your light sources will be changed to their empty/dark version again. After completing the quest, you can go back to the charging places during the correct time of the day and charge them once more, turning them permanently into the versions with elemental protection which can be equipped in the Extra Slot ([[Sun Catcher]], [[Starlight Vial]] and [[Moon Mirror]]).

=== Sweet Dreams ===
==== Access to Candia ====
Go to [[Feyrist]] and walk to the east part of the island, around ([COORD x=33574 y=32222 z=7 | here]). When you spot a big donut, walk under it, and then meet with NPC [[Maelyrra]] ([COORD x=33484 y=32237 z=7 | here]).

Talk to her about the {{KW|donut}} and she tells you it leads you to [[Candia]] but in order to use it you need a {{Ilink|Gingerbread Key}} [[Gingerbread Key]]. Unfortunately all have been eaten and the recipe was stolen by a forest fury that escape to [[Vandura]].

Go to [[Liberty Bay]] and enter the [[Corym Black Market]] ([COORD x=32268 y=32837 z=7 | here]). Walk through the area until you reach the -4 floor and meet [[A Captive Forest Fury]] ([COORD x=32296 y=32837 z=11 | here]). It will ask your {{KW|help}} to leave their prison and tell you that in order to do that you need a spell to make it invisible as well as make an illusion of it so Coryms will still think it's in there.

Go another two floors down and get an {{ilink|Intricate Cage Key}} [[Intricate Cage Key]] ([COORD x=32295 y=32794 z=13 | here]). Right after, walk all the way back to the surface floor and talk to [[Charlotta]] on the {{KW|mirror image}} to create the forest fury's illusion. Pay her the requested 1,000 [[gp]]s and return to the forest fury while invisible (use [[Invisible]] spell or equip a {{ilink|Stealth Ring}} [[Stealth Ring]]).

Use the {{ilink|Intricate Cage Key}} [[Intricate Cage Key]] on the cage and leave again to surface level. Make sure to do the entire process while invisible. Once again back to surface level, you will receive the "[[A Friend in Need]]" [[achievement]] and the {{ilink|Gingerbread Key Recipe}} [[Gingerbread Key Recipe]].

The instructions in the recipe tell you you need a soft chocolate dough, shape it into a key format, glaze it with {{ilink|Moon Melon}} [[Moon Melon]], {{ilink|Raspberry}} [[Raspberry]] and {{ilink|Lemon}} [[Lemon]] sirups, and bake with the baking tins in [[Feyrist]].

In order to make the chocolate dough, you need to use {{ilink|Flour}} [[Flour]] on a container with {{ilink|Milk}} [[Milk]] to obtain {{ilink|Lump of Cake Dough}} [[Lump of Cake Dough]], then use this dough on a {{ilink|Bar of Chocolate}} [[Bar of Chocolate]] to obtain a {{ilink|Lump of Chocolate Dough}} [[Lump of Chocolate Dough]].

To make the sirups you will need the previously mentioned fruits and {{ilink|Sugar (Quest)}} [[Sugar (Quest)]]:
* {{ilink|Moon Melon}} [[Moon Melon]] is obtained on [[Feyrist]] surface level but can only be obtained during the [[night]]
* {{ilink|Raspberry}} [[Raspberry]] and {{ilink|Lemon}} [[Lemon]] can be bought from NPCs [[Talila]] or [[Valindara]], also in Feyrist
* The sugar cubes are located in three distinct underground caves:
** Northwest cave ([COORD x=33423 y=32214 z=8 | here])
** South cave ([COORD x=33495 y=32285 z=8 | here])
** Northeast cave ([COORD x=33570 y=32186 z=9 | here])

Use the sugar cubes on each fruit and you'll get {{ilink|Flask with Lemon Sirup}} [[Flask with Lemon Sirup]], {{ilink|Flask with Moon Melon Sirup}} [[Flask with Moon Melon Sirup]] and {{ilink|Flask with Raspberry Sirup}} [[Flask with Raspberry Sirup]].

Go to the oven in [[Feyrist]] ([COORD x=33517 y=32231 z=6 | here]) and use your chocolate dough on it. Note that the key will appear on top of the oven and anyone around you could pick it up. Then use the sirups (in order: moon melon, raspberry and lemon) to finally obtain {{ilink|Gingerbread Key (Moon Melon, Raspberry and Lemon)}} [[Gingerbread Key (Moon Melon, Raspberry and Lemon)]] and be granted access to [[Candia]].

==== Talking to the NPCs ====
When you first arrived to Candia, you can't speak to the NPCs and they will use gestures, trying to tell you what you need to do.
To gain the ability to speak to the NPCs, you must find the [[File:Candy Lipstick.gif|frameless]] [[Candy Lipstick]] at the top of the Gingerbread Castle ([COORD x=33433 y=32143 z=4 | here]) and then use it. Note that you will not be able to retrieve the lipstick if you did not greet the NPC downstairs first.

==== Stolen Sweets ====
After gaining the ability to speak with the NPCs, you should ask [[Sugar Plum Fairy]] just below you for a '''mission'''. She will tell you about her favorite sweets that were stolen by [[Kroazur]] and she wants you to bring them back.

You now need to go to Kroazurs lair in Feyrist ([COORD x=33619 y=32305 z=9 | here]) and kill the boss. From Kroazur you will loot the first sweet, and a note telling you that he has sold the other sweets to other bosses. 

Those bosses being, [[Katex Blood Tongue]], [[The Flaming Orchid]] and [[Gorga]]. You will need to kill these bosses to get sweets and bring them back to Sugar Plum Fairy.

Kroazur will drop [[Matcha Turtle]], Katex drops the [[Rainbow Waffles]], Flaming orchid drops the [[Rose Milk Cake]], Gorga drops the [[Nightsky Cupcake]], 

When you have all the items, return to Sugar Plum Fairy, say the name of the sweet to complete this part of the quest.

She will thank you and send you to help out in the chocolate mines.

==== Chocolate Mines ====
To enter the Chocolate mines, you enter this hole ([COORD x=33369 y=32167 z=7 | here]). Be careful, as there will be [[Nibblemaw]] and [[Candy Horror]] in the chocolate mines.

Find [[Candis]] ([COORD x=33364 y=32140 z=8 | here]) and ask them for a '''mission.''' They will tell you about a portal that their workers have been told to stay away from, but they think some workers have gone too close out of curiosity. You will need to go in to the portal ([COORD x=33399 y=32201 z=9 | here]) and in there you will have to fight [[Sugar Daddy]] to free Candis' workers. 

[[Sugar Daddy]] will occasionally spawn minor harmless candy fae, you will have to kill them so you can continue dealing damage to [[Sugar Daddy]]. If you do not kill them in a certain period of time, you will get a intense hex debuff, which will make you deal low damage to the boss and heal less.

After killing the boss, you can leave the room and you'll see [[Sugar Cube Worker]] running away as you exit the bossroom. You can kill these for bestiary with no consequences. 

Return to [[Candis]] and say '''mission,''' they will thank you for your help and ask for your help again, with stabilizing the mines. There is a chest just north of [[Candis]] with candy canes, grab those and use them at three locations in the mine. Two of the locations are on the -1 floor of the mine and the third one is on the -2 floor. To repair the structures, simply use the candy canes on the broken piece on the wall.

-1 [MINIMAP x=33381 y=32152 z=8 marks=(33359,32181) (33408,32145)]-2[MINIMAP x=33411 y=32174 z=9 marks=(33411,32174)]After repairing all three locations, go back to [[Candis]] and say '''mission''' to report. Ask him for '''mission''' again, and he will give you 5 jars. Go down to -2 and catch 5 [[Honey Elemental|Honey Elementals]] into the jar. Once you got that then go back to the NPC and report your mission.  

He will thank you for your help and send you to [[Sugar Plum Fairy]]. When you return to her, say '''mission''' and she will also thank you and say that the Candy Carnival is open again! 

You can now trade with [[Coco]] and [[Toffee]] for candy things, they are located at the Candy Carnival just south of the Chocolate Mine entrance.

==== Family Feud ====
Go and talk with [[Dulcineo]] ([COORD x=33349 y=32164 z=7 | here]) and he will be happy that the Candy Carnival is open again, but he is worried that the feud between [[Sugar Plum Fairy]] and her sister, the [[Tooth Fairy (Candia)|Tooth Fairy]] is wearing his lady, Sugar Plum Fairy, down. He wants you to help solve this by going and talking with the [[Tooth Fairy]] which you can find [COORD x=33514 y=32249 z=7 | here]. 

When talking with the Tooth Fairy she is worried about the children's teeth because of all the candy so she wants you to bring toothbrushes to three children and placing it on their pillows.

[[Rowenna]]'s child in Carlin [COORD x=32324 y=31795 z=7 | here], simply use the toothbrush on the headpart of the bed on the floor above Rowenna.

[[Quero]]'s child in Thais [COORD x=32390 y=32221 z=7 | here], simply use the toothbrush on the headpart of the bed on the floor above Quero.

And finally [[Allen]]'s child in Venore [COORD x=33008 y=32060 z=5 | here], simply use the toothbrush on the headpart of the bed.

Return to the [[Tooth Fairy (Candia)|Tooth Fairy]] and report your success by saying '''mission''' she will be happy and give you a [[Pegasus Feather]] for your help.

Now return to [[Dulcineo]] and say '''mission''' to finish the quest. 

=== Put the Cherry in the Cage ===

'''Note:''' This part doesn't have any rewards, but it's required to mark the whole quest as completed.

Go to [[Sugar Plum Fairy]] and say '''taffy bunny'''. She'll tell you that one of her taffy bunnies, Cherry, is lost and she asks you to keep an eye out for Cherry on your travels through Candia. Go downstairs to the [[Dessert Dungeons]] and you'll receive the following message, indicating Cherry might be close: {{Message|type=server|You discover a tuft of spun pink sugar on the floor. Could the missing taffy bunny Cherry be down here?}} 

Run southwest, [COORD x=33414 y=32189 z=8 | here], and you'll see [[A Taffy Bunny]] walking around. When you try to interact with her, you'll receive some messages saying that she's too fast for you to catch her. Go back and report '''bunny''' to [[Sugar Plum Fairy]].
