== Method ==
Every day, one of the portals to the [[Spirit Grounds]] will be open. These portals are located near [[Darashia]] ([COORD x=33161 y=32561 z=5 | here]), at the [[Ghostlands]] ([COORD x=32176 y=31788 z=7 | here]) and at [[Vengoth]] ([COORD x=32909 y=31540 z=7 | here]). No objectives for this Mini World Change are known, and neither are any associated [[achievements]]. It is simply a [[Hunting Places|place to hunt]]. The [[monsters]] inside the [[Spirit Grounds]] vary independently of the portal's location, making it like two Mini World Changes in one.
