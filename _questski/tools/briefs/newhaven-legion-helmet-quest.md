## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler|name=Newhaven Legion Helmet Quest}}

=== Prerequisites ===

* Complete the '''Protect the Village''' mission from NPC [[Gustavo, The Guard]].
* Complete the '''Lost Wisdom''' mission from NPC [[Viola]].

=== Required Equipment ===

* [[Rope]] or the [[Magic Rope]] spell (''requires level 9'').

=== Optional Equipment ===

* 21 [[Gold Coin]]s to mark the path.
* [[Food]].

== Method ==
Travel to the northeastern edge of [[Newhaven]] and take the stairs [COORD x=32598 y=32418 z=7 | here] to the second floor of the [[Corrupted Mines]].

From there, head southwest and climb through the hole [COORD x=32580 y=32445 z=6 | here]. The [[Rope Spot]] is somewhat difficult to see, so take care when locating it.

After climbing up, head east until you reach and pass through the [[Poison Gas]] wall. The image below shows the route through the cave; the [[Gold Coin]]s mark the path to the eastern end of the cave.

[[File:Newhaven Legion Helmet Quest Room.png|750px|Newhaven Legion Helmet Quest Room]]

At the end of the cave, [[Use]] the [[Dead Human]] to obtain the reward.

After claiming the reward, step into the nearby [[Magic Forcefield]]. It will teleport you back to the western end of the cave.
