## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=Rest in Hallowed Ground Quest}}

== Requirements ==
* Travel money
* Completed [[The White Raven Monastery Quest]]

== Method ==
Ask [[Amanda]] in [[Edron]] [[Temple]] for a mission. She will ask you to bring her a [[Special Flask (Holy Water)|vial of holy water]] from the [[White Raven Monastery]]. Travel to the [[Isle of Kings]] and ask [[Costello]] about holy water and tell him [[Amanda]] sent you.

Take the [[Special Flask (Holy Water)|water]] back to [[Amanda]]. She will ask you to put some on every grave in the [[Ghoul hill|Unholy Cemetery]], north of [[Edron]] ([COORD x=33196 y=31695 z=7 | here]). Be prepared to face [[Poacher]]s and [[Hunter]]s on your way, and [[Ghoul]]s on the cemetery.

Use the [[Special Flask (Holy Water)|vial]] on every grave there. There are 15 graves, after using all graves, your [[vial]] will be empty. Report back to [[Amanda]] to get your reward.

''You see a [[Package of Potions|package of potions]]. It weighs 36.00 oz. It contains 5 health and 5 mana potions.''
