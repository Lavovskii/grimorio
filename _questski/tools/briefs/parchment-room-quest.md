## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler| name= Parchment Room Quest}}
==Required equipment==
* [[Pick]]
* [[Rope]]

==Getting there==
* First you have to enter the [[Edron]] [[Hero Cave]] ([COORD x=33164 y=31638 z=7 | here]).
* Then follow the way described on the picture below.
[[Image:P_way.JPG]]
* Use a pick to open the hole.
[[Image:P_hole.JPG]]
* Now go down and head to the west. Here you will find a single gravestone and a teleporter to a big room with one [[Demon]] inside, the Parchment Room. The text on the gravestone says:

<pre>
De.th to those who disturb ..e slumber of .ath..
</pre>

This reads as:<br />

<pre>
Death to those who disturb the slumber of Father
</pre>

Get prepared to face the demon and enter the teleporter.

==The Quest Room==
* After you have killed the [[Demon]], go to the coffin in the middle of the room.
* When you push the [[Yellow Parchment]] ([[Buried Forever (Book)|Buried Forever]]) 4 [[Demon]]s will spawn around you.
[[Image:P_room.gif]]
* Now you can decide to kill the demons or to grab the reward and run away. You have to open the coffin to get the reward.
