## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=The Tainted Souls Quest}}
== Requirements ==
* [[Antlers]]
* [[Special Flask (Holy Water)|Holy Water]]
* [[Star Herb]]

== Method ==

=== Obtaining the Items ===
First, you will need to obtain a [[Sacred Antler Talisman]]. To make one, you will need some [[Antlers]], looted from [[Deer]]; a [[Star Herb]], looted from [[Witch]]es or bought from [[Luna]] in [[Edron]]; and [[Special Flask (Holy Water)|Holy Water]], able to be bought from [[Henricus]] in [[Thais]] after reaching mission 4 of the [[The Inquisition Quest]].

When you have obtained all three of these items, use the [[Star Herb]] on the [[Antlers]] to obtain [[Antler Talisman]].<br />
{{Ilink|Star Herb}} + {{Ilink|Antlers}} = {{Ilink|Antler Talisman}} ({{Message|type=server|You entwine the antler with fresh star herb.}})

Then use [[Special Flask (Holy Water)|Holy Water]] on the [[Antler Talisman]] to obtain [[Sacred Antler Talisman]].<br />
{{Ilink|Special Flask (Holy Water)}} + {{Ilink|Antler Talisman}} = {{Ilink|Sacred Antler Talisman}} ({{Message|type=server|You besprinkle the antler talisman with holy water. It glitters faintly.}})

=== Freeing the Souls ===
With the [[Sacred Antler Talisman]], head to the [[Tainted Caves]] in the north of the [[Green Claw Swamp]], [COORD x=32711 y=31939 z=7 | here]. In this cave you will encounter [[Tainted Soul]]s, [[Toad]]s, [[Gloom Wolf|Gloom Wolves]], and [[Witch]]es. The cave has 4 floors with the same monsters on each floor. Explore the caves and use the [[Sacred Antler Talisman]] on all the [[Tainted Soul]]s that you find. Do this until you have freed 100 souls. Note that you can free the summons as well. By luring two Tainted Souls on you and freeing their summons you can collect the required 100 in less than 10 minutes.

When freeing a soul, a [[Redeemed Soul]] will spawn and the [[Tainted Soul]] on which the talisman was used will despawn shortly afterwards. You don't have to kill the [[Redeemed Soul]]s since they will despawn 30 seconds after spawning. However, if you're interested on completing their [[Bestiary]] entries, you will have those 30 seconds to kill each Redeemed Soul and a single turn to kill each Tainted Soul after successfully using the talisman it. Note that the [[Redeemed Soul]] might not spawn if there isn't enough room around your character, but it still counts as a freed soul for the quest.

{{Ilink|Sacred Antler Talisman}} + {{Ilink|Tainted Soul}} = {{Ilink|Redeemed Soul}}

When you have freed 100 souls, go to the 4<sup>th</sup> floor and enter the {{Ilink|Mystic Flame}} [[Mystic Flame]]. You will be teleported to a nearby room with [[Ekatrix]]. Kill her. After entering the boss room, the quest will immediately restart and you can start freeing souls again. Ekatrix can only be killed once every 20 hours, however.
