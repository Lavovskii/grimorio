## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=The Way of the Monk Quest}}
== Requirements ==
* Level 275 is required for full completion.
* [[Rope]], [[Shovel]], and [[Machete]]; or [[Secret Agent Tools]] with similar functionalities.
* [[Levitate]] spell, or parcels.
* Started the '''Nibelor 2: Ecological Terrorism''' mission of [[The Ice Islands Quest]] (for access to [[Tyrsung]]).
* Reached the '''The Levers''' mission of [[The Pits of Inferno Quest]] (requires all five vocations, or players with a high health pool to cross the purple flames).
* Completed [[The New Frontier Quest]].
* Handed in eight {{Ilink|Tome of Knowledge}} [[Tome of Knowledge]]s to NPC [[Cael]] (access to [[Corruption Hole]]; see [[Tomes of Knowledge Quest]]).
* Reached the '''The Powerful Relic''' stage of the Barkless mission in the [[Cults of Tibia Quest]].

== The Three-Fold Path ==
=== Blue Valley ===
:'''''Note:''' If you are playing any vocation other than [[Monk]], begin the quest from this section.''

Meet [[Enpa-Deia Pema]] in the [[Blue Valley]] and ask him about {{KW|pilgrimage}}. You can reach the [[Blue Valley]] via the [[Adventurer's Guild]]. On the upper floor, next to the [[Reward Shrine]], you will find an [[Enchanted Guarded Crystal Sphere]]. Use it to be teleported to the island.

He will explain that all are welcome to visit the ancient Merudri shrines, though the journey is arduous and requires endurance. Use one of the nearby [[Guarded Crystal Sphere]]s to begin your pilgrimage.  

The shrines are scattered across different locations and **must be visited in the correct order**, from lowest to highest level. Skipping a shrine will prevent you from progressing further. Each shrine has a minimum level requirement, grants rewards, and offers additional unique rewards to [[Monk]]s.
