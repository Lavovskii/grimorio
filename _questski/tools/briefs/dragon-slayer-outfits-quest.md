== Obtaining the Dragon Slayer Outfit ==
As of server save on {{OfficialNewsArchive|7582|October 10th, 2023}}, a new world event has been introduced, which occurs every six months on each world [[Nimmersatt's Lair]]. During this event, players have a chance to acquire a unique and [[Bottled Dragon Breath|rare item]] from the world boss that is crucial for obtaining the [[Dragon Slayer Outfits]].

=== Base Outfit ===
'''Requirements:'''

* Pay 75,000,000 gold to [[A Sweaty Cyclops]].
* Killed at least 5,000 [[Dragon]]s and 5,000 [[Dragon Lord]]s, as recorded in the [[Bestiary]].
* Achieved the [[Hoard of the Dragon]], [[Reason to Celebrate]], and [[Treasure Hunter]] achievements.
* 1x {{Ilink|Bottled Dragon Breath}} [[Bottled Dragon Breath]] from the world boss.
* 500x {{Ilink|Green Dragon Scale}} [[Green Dragon Scale]]s.
* 100x {{Ilink|Green Dragon Leather}} [[Green Dragon Leather]].
* 500x {{Ilink|Red Dragon Scale}} [[Red Dragon Scale]]s.
* 100x {{Ilink|Red Dragon Leather}} [[Red Dragon Leather]].

To obtain the Dragon Slayer base outfit you must speak with [[A Sweaty Cyclops]] by saying {{KW|Dragon Slayer Attire}}.

=== First Addon ===
'''Requirements:'''

* Already obtained the base outfit.
* Pay 25,000,000 gold to [[A Sweaty Cyclops]].
* Killed at least 6,250 [[Dragon]]s and 6,250 [[Dragon Lord]]s, as recorded in the [[Bestiary]].
* 15x {{Ilink|Albino Dragon Leather}} [[Albino Dragon Leather]]
* 25x {{Ilink|Piece of Draconian Steel}} [[Piece of Draconian Steel]].
* 10,000 [[Tibiadrome|Drome Points]].

To unlock the first addon for the Dragon Slayer outfit, you must speak with [[A Sweaty Cyclops]] by saying {{KW|Dragon Slayer Attire}}.
=== Second Addon ===
'''Requirements:'''

* Already obtained the base outfit and the first addon.
* Pay 25,000,000 gold to [[A Sweaty Cyclops]].
* Killed at least 7,500 [[Dragon]]s and 7,500 [[Dragon Lord]]s, as recorded in the [[Bestiary]].
* Obtained the [[Cyclopedia#Titles|Competent Beastslayer]] title, by accumulating and redeeming 320,000 [[Prey_System#Prey_Hunting_Tasks|Hunting Task Points]].
* 25x {{Ilink|Albino Dragon Leather}} [[Albino Dragon Leather]]s
* 10x {{Ilink|Dragon Lord Trophy}} [[Dragon Lord Trophy|Dragon Lord Trophies]]
* 10x {{Ilink|Dragon Trophy}} [[Dragon Trophy|Dragon Trophies]]

To unlock the second addon for the Dragon Slayer outfit, you must speak with [[A Sweaty Cyclops]] by saying {{KW|Dragon Slayer Attire}}.

== Additional Information ==

* You only need to kill a '''total''' of 7,500 [[Dragon]]s and 7,500 [[Dragon Lord]]s.
* The Dragon Slayer outfit obtained through this method can only be worn by the character who unlocked it. 
* The Tournament version of the outfit remains account-wide and can be used by players who obtained it through Tournaments for all their characters.
