## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler|name=Afflicted Outfits Quest}}

Note that you do not need to obtain the required equipment through looting and doing the quest by youself. '''You may buy the items and simply use them to obtain the outfit and its addons.'''

This quest may be dependant on a [[World Change]]. For more information, see [[Swamp Fever]].

==Basic Outfit==
[[File:Outfit Afflicted Male.gif]] [[File:Outfit Afflicted Female.gif]]

===Required Equipment===
*{{Ilink|Luminous Piece of Cloth}} [[Luminous Piece of Cloth]]
*{{Ilink|Obvious Piece of Cloth}} [[Obvious Piece of Cloth]]
*{{Ilink|Dubious Piece of Cloth}} [[Dubious Piece of Cloth]]
*{{Ilink|Voluminous Piece of Cloth}} [[Voluminous Piece of Cloth]]
*{{Ilink|Ominous Piece of Cloth}} [[Ominous Piece of Cloth]]
*{{Ilink|Ludicrous Piece of Cloth}} [[Ludicrous Piece of Cloth]]

===Method===
These pieces of cloth can be looted from [[Feverish Citizen]]s wandering [[Venore]] [[Streets of Venore|streets]] during [[Swamp Fever|special raids]]. Once you have all pieces, use one of them to obtain the outfit. Note that you must have the pieces of cloth in your inventory to let this work, not in e.g. your [[Depot]] chest.

==Bell and Mask Addons==
[[File:Outfit Afflicted Male Addon 3.gif]] [[File:Outfit Afflicted Female Addon 3.gif]]

===Required Equipment===
*{{Ilink|Plague Bell}} [[Plague Bell]]
*{{Ilink|Plague Mask}} [[Plague Mask]]

===Method===
Loot [[Medicine Pouch]]es from [[Swamp Troll]]s and trade them with [[Ottokar]] for [[Belongings of a Deceased]]. By using it you have a chance to get a [[Plague Mask]] and a [[Plague Bell]]. Once you have one of these items, use it to obtain the respective addon. After using both items and thus completing the outfit, you will get the [[Achievement]] [[Beak Doctor]].
