## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name= Lone Medusa Quest}}

== Method ==
Go to [[Vandura Mountain]] northeast of [[Liberty Bay]]. Here, you will face several [[Wyrm]]s and a couple of [[Energy Elemental]]s. On the fourth floor, go inside the cave and up the southern ramp. Here is the single [[Medusa]]. Kill her and grab the reward in the pile of bones in the northwest corner.
