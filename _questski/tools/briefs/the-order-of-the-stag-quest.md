== Method ==

=== General ===
The Isle of Ada can be quite confusing for players unfamiliar with the map. There are lots of stairs, doors, and levitate spots to navigate through the place. Additionally, there are a lot of monsters, and the [[Night Harpy|Night Harpies]] raids can be active during the nights, which makes the place even more crowded with enemies. 

=== Etzel Writ ===
Find [[Kesar the Younger]] during the day, and speak with him about {{KW|help}}. Kesar asks you to retrieve a family writ from below a [COORD x=32402 y=32468 z=4 | cathedral] located in [[Bounac]]. You need to open the hole using a [[Frozen Starlight]], by placing it on the stand. The writ is inside a frozen chest. Bring the writ to Kesar.

=== Isle of Ada ===
Kesar asks you to travel to the [[Isle of Ada]]. Go to the shore south of the castle, and click on the [COORD x=32394 y=32513 z=7 | rope] inside a small fishing boat. You are transferred to the Isle of Ada.

Speak to [[NPC]] [[Second In Command Demeron]] and say {{KW|hi}}, {{KW|mission}}, {{KW|tell}}, {{KW|yes}}, {{KW|yes}}, {{KW|yes}}, he will grant you permission to enter the mines to the north west. By exploring the mines, your quest log should update when reaching this area [COORD x=33872 y=32193 z=7 | here].

Go back to the entrance, and you should have access to the stairs. Make your way to the castle entrance:
* up the stairs [COORD x=33858 y=32237 z=7 | here]
* [[Levitate|levitate up]] [COORD x=33887 y=32202 z=6 | here]
* go up the stairs [COORD x=33888 y=32156 z=5 | here]
* go to the gates [COORD x=33888 y=32114 z=4 | here]
** if the gates are open: go inside
** if the gates are closed: [[Levitate|levitate down]] twice, [COORD x=33896 y=32114 z=4 | here] and [COORD x=33901 y=32112 z=5 | here], go up the stairs [COORD x=33872 y=32117 z=6 | here], again [COORD x=33861 y=32115 z=5 | here], then go inside the NPC area through a door [COORD x=33882 y=32105 z=4 | here]
*** if you can not open the doors at this stage, or if you want to open the gates: levitate down south from the gates, [COORD x=33887 y=32141 z=4 | here], levitate up three times [COORD x=33883 y=32141 z=5 | here], [COORD x=33880 y=32139 z=4 | here], and [COORD x=33880 y=32136 z=3 | here]. Levitate down [COORD x=33890 y=32130 z=2 | here], go up the stairs, and click the two chain mechanisms that lock the gate shut. A player that is fast enough can do this alone, lower levels might be too slow and require more than one person, since both mechanism need to be clicked almost at the same time
* reach [[NPC]] [[Guard Captain Hartford]] [COORD x=33882 y=32113 z=4 | here]

=== Night Harpy Beaks ===
[[NPC]] [[Guard Captain Hartford]] will ask for your help fighting against the [[Night Harpy]] scourge. By saying {{KW|hi}}, {{KW|business}}, {{KW|explain}}, {{KW|yes}}, he asks you to bring back 20 {{ilink|Night Harpy Beak}} [[Night Harpy Beak]]s as proof of help. Players will need to [[Skinning|skin]] dead [[Night Harpy|night harpies]] corpses with an {{ilink|Obsidian Knife}} [[Obsidian Knife]] for a chance to obtain a beak. Note that purchasing them from the market or other players is not enough to advance the quest. Each character will need to skin the items themselves. 

Night Harpies only spawn at [[night]] time, as part of a [[Raid]] mechanic. They do not spawn every night. Once they start spawning, the raid will last for several hours, but they still only appear at night. Players should kill and skin their corpses until 20 beaks have been skinned. Return to [[Guard Captain Hartford]] and show it as proof by saying {{KW|hi}}, {{KW|news}}, {{KW|yes}}, and access will be granted to the upper floor where [[Eldoran the Ambitious]] is. The beaks will not be lost.

=== Cleaning up the castle ===
To reach [[NPC]] [[Eldoran the Ambitious]], players must go through a door that is initially hidden behind a tapestry. Someone will need to break the tapestry by using a weapon on it. The door can be found by going up the stairs [COORD x=33875 y=32114 z=4 | here] and [COORD x=33870 y=32114 z=3 | here], the door will be [COORD x=33868 y=32115 z=2 | here]. 

Talk to [[Eldoran the Ambitious]] and say {{KW|hi}}, {{KW|word}}, {{KW|yes}}, {{KW|yes}}, and he will ask you to clean up some corpses in the castle. There are three corpses, "use" each one to progress in this part. The corpses are nearby burning piles and they face to the right instead of the regular dead bodies facing west. They can be found [COORD x=33858 y=32118 z=5 | here], [COORD x=33869 y=32114 z=5 | here], and [COORD x=33869 y=32135 z=5 | here]. Once you have done it, return to [[Eldoran the Ambitious]] and say {{KW|hi}}, {{KW|news}}, {{KW|yes}}.

=== Court Warlock ===
Ask [[Eldoran the Ambitious]] for another {{KW|mission}} and he will ask you to take care of [[Court Warlock]], the final part of the quest. Head down a few floors until you are in the basement with the [[Tamed Frazzlemaw]]s, [COORD x=33870 y=32132 z=6 | here], and you should have access to the doors. Make your way through the Blood Gorge and up into the west tower by going up [COORD x=33834 y=32146 z=8 | here]. Speak to [[NPC]] [[Captain Marie-Denise Banner]] and say {{KW|hi}}, {{KW|wall}}, and you will be granted access to the teleport by clicking on the wall [COORD x=33833 y=32140 z=4 | here]. The teleport will take you to the lever room that brings players to the fight against the [[Court Warlock]].

==== The Fight ====
It is important to note that the first player to enter the lever room will have Raubritters continuously spawn on their position for the duration of the fight. It is recommended to make sure the [[Knight]] is the first player in the lever room.

The initial part of the fight will consist of fighting several [[Raubritters]]:
* [[Poisoned Raubritter]] - immune to [[Earth Damage]]
* [[Frozen Raubritter]] - immune to [[Ice Damage]]
* [[Raging Raubritter]] - immune to [[Fire Damage]]
* [[Energised Raubritter]] - distance fighters immune to [[Energy Damage]]

At intervals, all players will be teleported around the room. The room is divided into regular floor and "special" terrain. These special areas are visibly different, and players should avoid walking on them, because each step will deal high [[Death Damage]]. 

[[Raubritters]] will continuously spawn, and players must keep killing them. If area damage is not strong enough, it is recommended to focus on single targets in order to avoid having four of the same Raubritter type alive at the same time. If this happens, they will change form into a stronger version of themselves, which can deal significantly more damage. Since [[Chivalrous Challenge]] cannot be used in boss rooms, the [[Energised Raubritters]] can become an issue as they will keep their distance from the [[Knight]] and be more difficult to hit. 

After a few waves, the room will fill up with [[Falcon Paladin]]s and [[Falcon Knight]]s, and all "special" terrain will disappear, allowing you to move around the room freely. Once all of the minions have been dealt with, the [[Court Warlock]] will spawn. It spawns with less [[HP]], depending on how the fight went until now. Finish off the boss, return to [[Eldoran the Ambitious]], and say {{KW|hi}}, {{KW|news}}, and you will receive the {{ilink|The Broken Sword of Eldoran Etzel}} [[The Broken Sword of Eldoran Etzel]] and the {{Ilink||Achievement Grade Symbol}} [[Bastard]] [[achievement]].

=== Optional 1: Supplies for the Steadfast ===
On the Isle of Ada you will need to make your way through the mines and into the first encampment on your first visit, Speak to NPC [[Lieutenant Harlan]] {{KW|hi}}, {{KW|yes}}, {{KW|yes}}, {{KW|no}}, and he will open a path to the north east. Offer to help and he will ask you to provide his camp with 20x {{ilink|Tender Venison}} [[Tender Venison]]s, and reward you with 10,000 [[XP|experience points]].

=== Optional 2: Emmanuelle's Boat Repair ===
[[Second In Command Demeron]] asks you to bring him spare parts to repair the boat:

* 30x {{Ilink|Salvaged Iron Parts}} [[Salvaged Iron Parts]]
* 20x {{Ilink|Salvaged Bronze Parts}} [[Salvaged Bronze Parts]]
* 10x {{Ilink|Salvaged Silver Parts}} [[Salvaged Silver Parts]]

In return, he will give you {{Ilink|The Mariner's Compass From the Antoinette}} [[The Mariner's Compass From the Antoinette]].

=== Optional 3: King's Council ===
Go to [[NPC]] [[Kesar the Younger]] and say {{KW|hi}}, {{KW|help}}, and he will grant you the {{ilink||Achievement Grade Symbol}} [[King's Council]] [[achievement]], and the second addon of the [[Image:Outfit Royal Bounacean Advisor Male Addon 2.gif]] [[Image:Outfit Royal Bounacean Advisor Female Addon 2.gif]] [[Royal Bounacean Advisor Outfits]].
