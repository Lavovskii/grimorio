## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=Pilgrimage of Ashes Quest}}

=== Spoiler ===
* Start this quest by talking to any [[Guide NPCs|city guide]].
* Travel to the original 6 [[Blesser NPCs]], the keyword is '''mission'''. You must visit blessers in correct order: [[Norf]] → [[Humphrey]] → [[Edala]] → [[Kawill]] → [[Pydar]] → [[Eremo]] to retrieve [[Blessings]] for the quest.
* The nearest cities to each NPC are: [[Thais]] (Norf) → [[Carlin]] (Humphrey) → [[Ab'Dendriel]] (Edala) → [[Kazordoon]] (Kawill & Pydar) → [[Cormaya]] (Eremo)
* Pay attention that you will have to walk on [[Fire]] in order to reach (and left) Pydar. Since the area is a [[Protection Zone]], eating food won't regen your health and mana. It is advised to have potions with you or, if you are at least level 17 and have a magic level of at least 3, [[Destroy Field Rune]]s to remove the fire.
* Travel to any [[Guide NPCs|city guide]], say '''mission''' and get your 2k reward. (Past level 25 you no longer receive the 2k reward from the guide.)
* If you feel you're strong enough, you can also buy the two Enhanced Blessings, [[Blood of the Mountain]] and [[Heart of the Mountain]], but they are not part of this quest.
