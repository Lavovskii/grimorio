== Method ==
The [[Formorgar Glacier|glacier]] north of [[Svargrond]] will begin to melt uncovering [[Grass (Tile)|grass]] and other [[plants]].

There will be 15 [[Ice Flower]]s which have a small chance (~20%) of giving [[Ice Flower Seeds]] when used. It takes 4 hours for the ice flowers to regrow (counting from the last time they were harvested). They can be found:

*north of the path that leads to [[Frost Manor]] ([COORD x=32256 y=31079 z=5 | here])
*south of the [[Sleeping Bear]]'s cave ([COORD x=32254 y=31067 z=5 | here])
*west of [[Romir]]'s hut ([COORD x=32204 y=31056 z=5 | here])
*farther northwest of [[Romir]]'s hut ([COORD x=32192 y=31049 z=5 | here])
*east of the immovable [[Mammoth]] ([COORD x=32162 y=31032 z=5 | here])
*south of the immovable [[Mammoth]] ([COORD x=32152 y=31031 z=5 | here])
*southwest of the immovable [[Mammoth]] ([COORD x=32145 y=31037 z=5 | here])
*far southwest of the immovable [[Mammoth]] ([COORD x=32135 y=31050 z=5 | here])
*north of the path to the westernmost point ([COORD x=32122 y=31062 z=5 | here])
*on the path to the westernmost point ([COORD x=32112 y=31073 z=5 | here])
*north of [[A Starving Dog]] ([COORD x=32120 y=31083 z=5 | here])
*northwest of the entrance to [[Formorgar Glacier]] ([COORD x=32150 y=31061 z=5 | here])
*west of the entrance to [[Formorgar Glacier]] ([COORD x=32155 y=31086 z=5 | here])
