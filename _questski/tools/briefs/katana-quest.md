## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Katana Quest}}

==Required Equipment==
*[[Rope]]
*[[Shovel]]
*[[Small Health Potion]]s (plenty for your own safety)
*2 [[Antidote Potion]]s or (more economical) about 3 [[Small Health Potion]]s to survive the [[Poison Gas]]

==Method==
*Go to the graves to the north-east of the island ([COORD x=32151 y=32151 z=7 | here]), and open the middle down one with a [[Shovel]].
[[Image:Katana1.jpg]]
*Go down the hole, there might be some [[Spider]]s and [[Poison Spider]]s in this area. Follow the path west to a hole and jump into it.
[[Image:Katana2.jpg]]
*In this area there are some [[Skeleton]] spawns. It is recommended that you kill them all before proceeding, as they will follow you and even block your way back. A safe way to kill them is to go to the south of the room and kill them one by one while standing in the narrow passage.
[[Image:Katana3.jpg]]
*Go east until you find a ropespot. Get prepared, as the next room might take time and skills.
[[Image:Katana4.jpg]]
*Use your [[Rope]] to climb up the hole and into the [[Rotworm]]s' room. This room has a lot of rotworms, so it might be dangerous even for the most skilled player. Best thing to do is to rush to the other hole and go down as soon as you can, avoiding getting [[trap]]ped. Also, use defensive mode to get a minimum of damage.
[[Image:Katana5.jpg]]
*Go through the [[Poison Gas|poison fields]].
[[Image:Katana6.jpg]]
*(If you have [[Key 4603]], skip this point) To get [[Key 4603]] face north. It is recommended to use [[Defensive Fighting]] mode here while running from the rotworms. [[Key 4603]] will be hidden in one of the dead bodies. When you find it, go back south.
*Go east from the [[Poison Gas|poison fields]], into a hole. Down the hole resides a single [[Skeleton]]. Slay it if you want, open the door with [[Key 4603]], and take the stairs down.
[[Image:Katana7.jpg]]
*'''Watch Out!''' If the door is unlocked there could be two [[skeleton]]s and a [[rotworm]] in this room! There is a hidden lever behind the northern white pillar. Use it to unlock the door (if it hasn't been done already). It is best to wait for all creatures to get out of room, then run into the room and close the door. However, you can also kill the creatures one by one at the door if you have friends helping.
[[Image:Katana8.jpg]]
*If someone closes the door with the lever from the outside, go to the teleport for you to get out!
[[Image:Katana Quest Room.gif]]
*The treasure is hidden inside the fresh human corpses. Grab your [[Viking Helmet]] and [[Katana]] from them. Go back all the way in order to leave the cave.
