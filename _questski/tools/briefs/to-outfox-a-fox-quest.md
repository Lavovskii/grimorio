===Required Equipment===
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Rope]]

===Method===

First you need to find [[Budrik]] who is the mine foreman for the [[Dwarf Mines]]. From the main entrance of [[Kazordoon]] go from the red X mark in the top right, following the white line to a rope hole, here there are some [[spider]]s. Go north and exit down a ramp where there are [[Poison Spider]]s and some [[Dwarf|Dwarves]] on the ground level. The second mine with the red circled down arrow mark, is where Budrik is.

[[File:Way to Minotaur Tower.png|500px]]

Talk to [[Budrik]] one floor below ground in a mine. Ask him about a '''mission'''. He will ask you to get his helmet in a minotaur mountain nearby.<br />
Now keep following the white line south across two bridges and past other mine entrances. You may encounter [[Snake]]s, [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Wolf|Wolves]], and [[Bear]]s. Here use your [[shovel]] on a [[Loose Stone Pile]] to go down.

The mining helmet is behind a sealed door ([COORD x=32467 y=31971 z=5 | here]) on the second floor of the tower.

The [[Steel Helmet Quest]] is also located here, so save some room for those rewards as well.

Go back to [[Budrik]] and report your mission.
