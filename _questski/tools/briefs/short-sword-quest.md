## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Short Sword Quest}}
=== Method ===
* Head to the dock east of [[Rookgaard]] town ([COORD x=32172 y=32197 z=7 | here]). Be careful of [[Bug]]s, [[Spider]]s, and especially [[Poison Spider]]s near the dock.
* "Use" the chest on the dock to receive a black book.
* Take the book back to the [[Rookgaard Academy]], and talk to [[Amber]] ([COORD x=32103 y=32183 z=8 | here]) in the Resting Room under the Academy. 
* Tell her you found her notebook, and she will trade you for a [[Short Sword]]. The book is only the book you get in the chest not one she gives you.
* Say: {{KW|Hi, Book, Yes}}.

'''NOTE''': If you find black books that other people have thrown on the ground, you can trade them to [[Amber]] for more [[Short Sword]]s. This is especially handy because [[Obi]] gives you some [[gp|extra cash]] for it.
