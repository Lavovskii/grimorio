== Tips ==
The tasks are supposed to be additional objectives to complete while normally playing the game. However, some players will want to focus on points to use the features that are available to high-ranked citizens. The following tasks are reasonably completable during a hunt:
* [[#The Powder of the Stars|The Powder of the Stars]]
* [[#To Take Roots|To Take Roots]]

The following tasks are the fastest to complete for votes:
* [[#Communication Breakdown|Communication Breakdown]]
* [[#Combinatorics|Combinatorics]] (requires strong coordination)
* [[#The Ancient Sewers|The Ancient Sewers]]
* [[#Cooking Gloo|Cooking Gloo]] (requires ''Citizen'' rank and ''The Gloud Ship'' mission)

The following tasks are kill-tasks and require almost no effort outside of usual hunting:
* [[#Taking the Rot Out|Taking the Rot Out]]
* [[#The Minor Taurs|The Minor Taurs]]
* [[#The Name Of The Beast|The Name Of The Beast]]
* [[#Nature's Balance|Nature's Balance]]

The following task has limited repeats per day and is impractical for farming:
* [[#Tending Gloothworms|Tending Gloothworms]] ([[#Calibrating the Irrigation Funnels|Calibrating the Irrigation Funnels]], [[#Feeding the Worms|Feeding the Worms]], [[#Rake Away|Rake Away]])

The following task could be completed to buy your way through voting points assuming the items are available:
* [[#Glooth Capsules|Glooth Capsules]] (requires ''Citizen'' rank and ''Glooth Bandits'' and ''Four Demanding Statues'' mission)

The following tasks are simply not worth the hassle just for the points:
* [[#Probing|Probing]]
* [[#Rathleton Turnip Party|Rathleton Turnip Party]]
* [[#Sabotage|Sabotage]]
* [[#Dr. Merlay's Misery|Dr. Merlay's Misery]]

== Missions ==
Each mission can be completed once following the descriptions in this section. However, many of these missions will unlock corresponding [[#Tasks|tasks]], allowing you to revisit and repeat specific parts of the mission to earn additional [[Rathleton_Quest#Method|voting points]].

=== The Glooth Bandits ===
To begin "The Glooth Bandits" mission, start by speaking with [[Elbert]], who can be found [COORD x=33586 y=31986 z=12 | here]. When you speak with him ask him about a {{KW|Mission}}; he will enlist your help in investigating a recent surge in glooth bandit attacks.

Your task is to retrieve 10 [[Glooth Capsule]]s, which have been stolen by the [[Glooth Bandit]]s and [[Glooth Brigand]]s. You can obtain these capsules by hunting [[Glooth Bandit]]s and [[Glooth Brigand]]s or by purchasing them through [[The Market (Object)|The Market]] or from a friend. It's important to note that [[Elbert]] will not accept [[Tainted Glooth Capsule]]s. If you happen to loot [[Tainted Glooth Capsule]]s, you can use a [[Filled Glooth Converter]] acquired in the course of the [[Oramond Quest/Spoiler#The_Mutated_Glooth_Plague|Oramond Quest - The Mutated Glooth Plague]] to purify them into [[Glooth Capsule]]s.

Report back to [[Elbert]] to complete this mission, you will earn 2 [[Rathleton_Quest#Method|voting rights]] in the [[Rathleton Magistrate]]. If you have more than 10 [[Glooth Capsule]]s, you will receive 2 additional voting rights for every 10 capsules you hand in. Requires completing Four Demanding Statues mission before turning in [[Glooth Capsule]].

<div style="float: right; padding: 0 0 0.5em 0.5em;">

* 5 [[Bronze Gear Wheel]]s these can be looted from [[Glooth Golem]]s, [[Metal Gargoyle]]s, and [[Rustheap Golem]]s.
* 1 [[Copper Valve (Item)|Copper Valve]] can be obtained by using a [[Copper Valve]].
* 1 [[Calibrated Indicator Gauge]] found in the box [COORD x=33394 y=31915 z=12 | here].
* 1 [[Glass Tube]] received by asking [[Elbert]] about a {{KW|Glass Tube}} after starting the mission.
* 1 [[Strange Device]] supplied by [[Georgia Rainbird]] once accepting the mission.

To begin this mission, approach [[Georgia Rainbird]] located [COORD x=33585 y=31906 z=12 | here]. Ask her for a mission by using the keywords {{KW|Hi}} - {{KW|Mission}} - {{KW|Yes}} - {{KW|No}}. Upon your request, she will supply you with a [[Strange Device]] and instructions regarding the specific items required to assemble a [[Glooth Converter]].

To continue, speak with [[Georgia Rainbird]] once again, she will inform you that she needs an antidote to eliminate the harmful influence of the mutated glooth. To do this you will need to gather a [[Crimson Crest Mushroom]], these can be found throughout the [[Underground Glooth Factory]]. You are likely to find them [COORD x=33476 y=32001 z=12 | here], [COORD x=33685 y=31884 z=12 | here], and [COORD x=33717 y=31876 z=12 | here], among other areas around the [[Slime (Purple)|Purple Slime]]. To collect a [[Crimson Crest Mushroom]], all you need to do is use the [[Crimson Crest Mushroom (Immobile)|Crimson Crest Mushroom]]. Keep in mind that it takes '''5 minutes''' for them to regrow after being picked.

Now you must go make your way back to [[Georgia Rainbird]]'s laboratory and use the [[Crimson Crest Mushroom]] on the [[Alchemical Apparatus]] to create a [[Vial of Mushroom Distillate]]. Now you will need to use the [[Vial of Mushroom Distillate]] on the [[Glooth Converter]] to finish creating a [[Filled Glooth Converter]].

You will now need to use the [[Filled Glooth Converter]] on eight pools of [[Slime (Purple)|Purple Slime]]. To use it effectively, use it similarly to a fishing rod. Aim for the center of the pools to avoid obstruction by the ground borders near the edges. Once you've completed this procedure '''eight times''', the [[Filled Glooth Converter]] will break, leaving you with a [[Strange Device]], 5 [[Bronze Gear Wheel]]s, a [[Copper Valve]], a [[Calibrated Indicator Gauge]], and a [[Glass Tube]]. With these components, you can easily restart the task by reconstructing the device. Use another [[Vial of Mushroom Distillate]] on it to finish rebuilding the [[Filled Glooth Converter]]. 

Once you've completed the mission speak with [[Georgia Rainbird]] to receive 5 rights to [[Rathleton_Quest#Method|vote]] in the [[Rathleton Magistrate]].

'''Notes:'''
* You can request a replacement [[Strange Device]] by saying {{KW|Lost Glooth Converter}} to [[Georgia Rainbird]].
* You can collect a [[Calibrated Indicator Gauge]] every 2 minutes.
* You can request a replacement [[Glass Tube]] from [[Elbert]] by asking about a {{KW|Glass Tube}}.
* Requesting replacement items can only be done if you're carrying them.
* You can create multiple [[Filled Glooth Converter]]s, allowing you to use one for the quest and one for cleansing [[Tainted Glooth Capsule]]s.

==== Strong Cloth ====
Go to [[Nicholas]] inside [[Upper Rathleton]]. He is located above the post office, [COORD x=33640 y=31897 z=5 | here]; to reach him, you must use the [[Ladder (Oramond)]] on the south side of the building. [[Nicholas]] will ask you for 20 [[Piece of Crocodile Leather|Pieces of Crocodile Leather]] in return for secret information. Give him the [[Piece of Crocodile Leather|Crocodile Leather]] and ask again about the '''very strong cloth'''. [[Nicholas]] will create [[Strong Cloth]] for you if you bring him the material required.

First, [[Nicholas]] requires 15 pieces of [[Steel Spider Silk]]. To get them, you must find special [[Spider (Steel)|Steel Spiders]] which crawl around many [[Rathleton]] landmarks. Note that you must find the spiders, not [[Spider Webs]]. It is recommended to go to the [[Glooth Factory]] ([COORD x=33543 y=31930 z=7 | here]) as it has a higher spider density, but you can find them in the [[Rathleton Sewers]] and possibly other places as well. Use the [[Milking Fork]] that [[Nicholas]] provided on the [[Spider (Steel)|Steel Spiders]]. Note that some "spiders", even around [[Rathleton]], which look similar are actually [[Something Crawling]] which cannot be used.
:{{Message|type=server|With much care, you extracted some steel spider silk.}}

When you have obtained 15 pieces of [[Steel Spider Silk]], you must find a [[Yarn-Winding Machine]] and use the [[Steel Spider Silk]] on the [[Yarn-Winding Winch]] to make a [[Spool of Steel Silk Yarn]]. You can find one on the west side of the [[Workshop Quarter]], [COORD x=33614 y=31960 z=7 | here].
:{{Message|type=server|You have spun the yarn on a spool. Now you can weave a cloth.}}

Now go to the room below [[Zeronex]] and use your [[Spool of Steel Silk Yarn]] with a [[Weaving Loom]] to obtain [[Strong Cloth]]. The following message will appear:
:{{Message|type=server|You have woven a fine strong cloth.}}

==== Bottle of Airtight Gloo ====
Go back up the ladder and talk to [[Zeronex]]. He will take the [[Strong Cloth]] from you and instruct you to speak with [[Yeo]] about obtaining '''strong glue''', or '''gloo'''. Find [[Yeo]] by the [[Rathleton]] harbour and exchange the 10 [[Shrimp]] for a [[Bottle of Gloo]].

Now you must concoct a [[Bottle of Airtight Gloo]]. To do so, go to the Rathleton pharmacy. It is located in the western part of the [[Rathleton Workshop Quarter]], west of the stairs ([COORD x=33609 y=31949 z=7 | here]). On the second floor, open the [[Bookcase (Rathleton)|Bookcase]] to obtain [[Glooth Farina]]. Go up the stairs again and open the northern [[Bookcase (Rathleton)|Bookcases]] to obtain [[Pink Gloud Essence]] and [[Glooth Vinegar]]. You may not need all of these items or you may need more of them. If you need more of an item after using the one you obtain, you can open the respective [[Bookcase (Rathleton)|Bookcase]] to obtain another one.

Now that you have the ingredients, find a [[Glooth Cauldron]]. Conveniently, there are a few located just east of the pharmacy ([COORD x=33622 y=31945 z=7 | here] and upstairs). Use the [[Bottle of Gloo]] on a cauldron and a message appears:
:{{Server Message|<gloop> <gloop gloop>}}

Now you must use the [[Glooth Cauldron]] until you obtain the [[Bottle of Airtight Gloo]]. If you receive one of the following server messages then you must perform something else before continuing to use the cauldron:

{{Message|type=server|The concoction is too thick. Liquefy it with glooth vinegar.}}
:&mdash;Use [[Glooth Vinegar]] on the [[Glooth Cauldron]].
{{Message|type=server|The concoction does not stick! Sequester it with glooth farina.}}
:&mdash;Use [[Glooth Farina]] on the [[Glooth Cauldron]].
{{Message|type=server|?}}
:&mdash;Use [[Pink Gloud Essence]] on the [[Glooth Cauldron]].
{{Message|type=server|You have retrieved some glooth plasma. Maybe you should shake it to see if it settles down?}}
:&mdash;Use [[Glooth Plasma]]. A few things can happen:
:{{Message|type=server|The mix decomposes. Try to cook up some gloo at the cauldrons again.}}
::&mdash;You obtain [[Rubbish]] and lose the [[Glooth Plasma]]. Continue using the [[Glooth Cauldron]].
:{{Message|type=server|Nothing happens. Maybe if you shook it a little more?}}
::&mdash;Nothing happens. You can use the [[Glooth Plasma]] again.

If you require more of the three items, you can receive more from the pharmacy after using the ones you took.

When you are done, you will have received the message:
:{{Message|type=server|You have retrieved some airtight gloo.}}

Report back to [[Zeronex]] about your '''mission'''. He will give you an [[Airtight Cloth]] to keep, and a [[Gas Bag]] for your next mission.

'''Optional side-mission''': [[Zeronex]] will give you [[Airtight Cloth]] if you ask for it. You can give 6 [[Airtight Cloth]]s to the NPCs within [[Rathleton]]: [[Yeo]], [[Nicholas]], [[Barazbaz]], [[Alaistar]], [[Fayla]] and [[Christine]] will accept [[Airtight Cloth]]s. When you hand one in to each of these NPCs, you will receive the achievement ''[[Publicity]]''.

==== Filled Gas Bag ====

Your next mission is to go to [[Zeronex]]'s laboratory and fill his [[Gas Bag]]. The laboratory is in the [[Underground Glooth Factory]]. Go to the [[Underground Glooth Factory]] by entering the [[Slime Slide]] in the building by NPC [[Terrence]] who is east of the stairs to [[Upper Rathleton]]. Go to the furthest west-southwest of the cave to find [[Zeronex]]'s laboratory, [COORD x=33396 y=31982 z=12 | here].

When you find [[Zeronex]]'s laboratory, use the three [[Glooth Data Machine]]s that are against the walls. You must use the east side of the machines '''repeatedly''' until the calibration is complete for that machine. You will receive the following server messages when you're done with that machine, not necessarily in this order:
:{{Message|type=server|+++ 01 REPORT --- CALIBRATION COMPLETE --- VALUES IDEAL +++}}
:{{Message|type=server|+++ 02 REPORT --- CALIBRATION COMPLETE --- VALUES IDEAL +++}}
:{{Message|type=server|+++ 03 REPORT --- CALIBRATION COMPLETE --- VALUES IDEAL +++}}

You will receive 3 voting points for completing this mission and you can now accept 3 daily tasks.

== Tasks ==

You may complete [[Oramond_Quest/Spoiler#Tasks|tasks]] as many times as you'd like. While certain tasks may have daily limits, others do not impose any such restrictions. 

=== Combinatorics ===
[[File:Combinatorics Consecutive Chains.png|thumb|right|Example of 14 Consecutive Chains]]

You can initiate this task through [[Doubleday]], but it's not mandatory, as the task automatically activates when you eliminate specific monsters in the [[Rathleton New Sewers]]. Voting rights can accumulate rapidly in this task. Once you've slain 9 creatures in the correct order, you'll start building a '''Consecutive Chain''' for every additional 3 creatures you defeat in the correct sequence you'll gain an additional [[Rathleton_Quest#Method|voting right]].

To proceed, venture into the [[Rathleton New Sewers]] and kill monsters in the sequence indicated by your [[Quest Log|quest log]] or [[Server Log Channel|server log]]. The eligible [[Creatures|creatures]] for this task are:
* [[Glooth Golem]]
* [[Devourer]]
* [[Glooth Anemone]]

As you eliminate each correct creature, your streak increases. '''Beware that killing the wrong creature will reset your consecutive chains to zero.''' If you're not aiming for a high score or attempting to achieve the 10 consecutive chains required for the achievement, it's advisable to periodically report your progress to avoid losing it due to a single mistake. The number of '''Consecutive Chains''' recorded in your [[Quest Log|quest log]] can later be exchanged for voting points through [[Doubleday]]. To report your progress, speak with [[Doubleday]] using the keywords '''{{KW|Hi}} - {{KW|Combinatorics }} - {{KW|Yes}}'''. When you're ready to exchange your chains for voting points, use the keywords '''{{KW|Hi}} - {{KW|Exchange}} - (Enter Number) - {{KW|Yes}}'''. Your rewards will be awarded as [[Rathleton_Quest#Method|voting points]] in the [[Rathleton Magistrate]].

Note, the unpredictable [[Varying Monster Spawn|dynamic spawns]] within [[Rathleton New Sewers]] make it challenging to anticipate where specific creatures will appear. 

Upon successfully completing 10 consecutive chains within a single task, you'll earn the [[Combo Master]] [[Achievements|achievement]].

'''Note:''' To ascend back to the surface, you must use the base of the [[Rungs In The Wall|rungs in the wall]] as you would any [[Ladder]].

[[File:Generator (Big).gif|thumb|right|Massive Generator]]
In the sewers, your objective is to locate one of the [[Generator (Big)|Massive Generators]]. On the floor you're currently on, there are a total of '''3''' generators; one to the [COORD x=33664 y=31984 z=9 | east], which is closest, and two to the north [COORD x=33622 y=31961 z=9 | here], and [COORD x=33615 y=31929 z=9 | here]. When using the valve [[Coloured Steam]] will appear over your character, and orange text will display above the valve, indicating the colour of the steam released, such as "{{Sound|Yellow steam emerges...}}". [[Image:Green Smoke Effect.gif]] {{Sound|Green steam emerges...}} <br />
[[Image:Red Smoke Effect.gif]] {{Sound|Red steam emerges...}} <br />

{{Message|type=server|You fill some of the coloured glooth in a vessel. It seems to affect it, making it fragile. Better carry it somewhere safe.}}

To report back to [[Doubleday]], simply say '''{{KW|Hi}} - {{KW|Communications}} - {{KW|Yes}}'''. As a reward, you'll earn 1 favor with [[Doubleday]], which you can later '''{{KW|Exchange}}''' for [[Rathleton_Quest#Method|voting rights]] in the [[Rathleton Magistrate]].

=== Probing ===
[[File:Oramond Quest Probing Task.png|thumb|500px|right|Probing Location]]
To begin the probing task, locate [[Doubleday]] in the [[Workshop Quarter]] [COORD x=33640 y=31961 z=6 | here]. Speak to him about helping him probe part of the sewers using the keywords '''{{KW|Hi}} - {{KW|Probe}} - {{KW|Yes}}'''. [[Doubleday]] will give you a [[Mono Detector]] and a [[Strange Probing Device]]. 

Avoid any unnecessary interactions with the [[Strange Probing Device]]. Moving it within a container or placing it in the wrong location will cause it to disappear. (However, you can freely move '''the container'''.) In the event that such an incident occurs, you can request a replacement device from [[Doubleday]] by inquiring about the {{KW|Detector}}. However, please be aware that there is a mandatory waiting period of '''30 minutes''' before you can receive a new [[Strange Probing Device|device]], starting from the time you initially obtained it. If you complete this task before 30 minutes have passed, you can obtain a new [[Strange Probing Device]] after you've handed in the previous successful printout.

To complete this task, you must utilize the [[Mono Detector]] to pinpoint specific '''abnormal spots''' within the [[Rathleton New Sewers]]. These locations can be found [COORD x=33639 y=31896 z=11 | here]. Be well-prepared to encounter a variety of challenging creatures in this area, including [[Glooth Anemone]]s, [[Glooth Golem]]s, [[Devourer]]s, [[Rustheap Golem]]s, [[Humongous Fungus|Humongous Fungi]], and [[Hideous Fungus|Hideous Fungi]]. It is strongly advised to bring a [[Dwarven Ring]] to avoid becoming [[Drunk]] from the [[Humongous Fungus|Humongous Fungi]], and [[Hideous Fungus|Hideous Fungi]].

When you have located an '''abnormal spot''', take the [[Strange Probing Device]] from your inventory and place it onto the ground at the abnormal spot. It will transform into an [[Active Probing Device]]. Next, quickly use the [[Active Probing Device]] while it is on the ground. If completed, you will receive a [[Crude Primitive Printout]] in your backpack.

Finally, take the [[Crude Primitive Printout]] back to [[Doubleday]], he will acknowledge the task's completion. To report back to [[Doubleday]], simply say '''{{KW|Hi}} - {{KW|Probe}}'''. As a reward, you'll earn 1 favor with [[Doubleday]]. He will also take the [[Crude Primitive Printout]] and, if you have it with you, the [[Mono Detector]]. These favors can be '''{{KW|Exchanged}}''' for [[Rathleton_Quest#Method|voting rights]] in the [[Rathleton Magistrate]] at a later time. Note: if you happen to have multiple [[Strange Probing Device]]s, you can use all of them at once for multiple favors; handing these [[Crude Primitive Printout]]s is not actually required.

Upon successful completion of this task, you will be eligible to acquire a [[Necrometer]] from [[Jondrin]] as part of the [[Dark Trails Quest]]. Otherwise, he will inform you that you are too inexperienced to wield such valuable government equipment.

=== The Ancient Sewers ===
[[File:Oramond Quest Sewers Task.png|thumb|500px|right|4 Highlighted Repair Locations]]
To begin The Ancient Sewers task, locate either of the Glooth Brothers: [[Elliott]] found [COORD x=33570 y=31910 z=8 | here], or [[Jacob]] found [COORD x=33572 y=31983 z=9 | here] by saying '''{{KW|Mission}} - {{KW|Yes}}'''.

You will need to repair 20 [[Smoking Generator|smoking generators]] or [[Leaky Tube|leaky tubes]]. If you repair more than 20 before reporting back, they will count for the next time you take the task. In return, you will receive 1 [[Rathleton_Quest#Method|voting right]] in the [[Rathleton Magistrate]] for every 20 repairs you make.

These repairs are needed all throughout the sewers, but certain spots are better than others for this task. Look for [[Smoking Generator|generators]] and [[Leaky Tube|tubes]] that have dark smoke coming out of them; these are the ones needing repairs. You can find a group of 4 needing repairs just a bit away from [[Elliott]], [COORD x=33570 y=31917 z=9 | here]. To repair them, simply use them. It takes '''2 minutes''' for a leaky tube or smoking generator to become broken again.

If you're successful at repairing a [[Leaky Tube|leaky tube]], one of the following messages will appear:

=== Dr. Merlay's Misery ===
[[File:Dr Merlay's Misery Laboratory.png|thumb|500px|right|Entering the Laboratory]]
To begin the Dr. Merlay's Misery task, locate [[Roswitha]] in the [[Rathleton Slum]] found [COORD x=33584 y=31942 z=7 | here]. Speak to her to order a [[Pint of Glooth]] using the keywords '''{{KW|Hi}} - {{KW|Pint}} or {{KW|Drink}}'''. You will receive a [[Pint of Glooth]] that you will require shortly. Once you've obtained the [[Pint of Glooth]], you will have a time limit of '''10 minutes''' to reach the entrance of [[Dr Merlay|Dr. Merlay's]] laboratory before the [[Pint of Glooth]] transitions into a [[Pint of Glooth (Green)]] as it begins to go stale.

Now head north from [[Roswitha]], pass by [[Fillias]], and descend into the [[Rathleton Ancient Sewers]] located [COORD x=33569 y=31921 z=7 | here]. You may need to use the sewer cover to access the sewer entrance. Proceed by walking east and then north. As you pass by [[Elliott]], proceed to descend into the sewer entrance situated just to the north [COORD x=33571 y=31901 z=8 | here]. Keep going to the east until you reach the platform along the western wall with the [[Ladder (Oramond)|ladder]]. From there, head north to the location marked as [COORD x=33594 y=31893 z=9 | here]. Descend down the stairs, and then proceed north towards the sewer entrance [COORD x=33586 y=31885 z=10 | here]. Go ahead and descend into the sewer from there.

At this point, you should use the [[Pint of Glooth]] on yourself and it will transform you into a [[Rot Elemental]], this effect will last for '''30 seconds'''. Continue by walking north through the [[Strange Metal Statue]]s into the laboratory. As you do so, you will earn the [[Elementary, My Dear]] [[Achievement]]. He will instruct you that he needs you to hand out [[Bundle of Letters|letters]] to some of the citizens of [[Rathleton]].

* Continue by heading to [[Rathleton]]s temple to speak with [[Azalea]] '''{{KW|Hi}} - {{KW|Letter}} - {{KW|Yes}}''' she will hand you a [[Soothe Bloom]]. 
* Proceed to [[Fyodor]] at the Rathleton Post Office, again say '''{{KW|Hi}} - {{KW|Letter}} - {{KW|Yes}}'''. 
* Finally proceed to visit [[Dr Merlay]]s wife [[Emily]], and again say '''{{KW|Hi}} - {{KW|Letter}} - {{KW|Yes}}'''.

* If you wish to keep or loose the [[Soothe Bloom]] simply drop or deposit the one you had received and return back to [[Azalea]] and say {{KW|Lost}}, you can repeat this every hour to obtain as many as you wish.

Return to the laboratory and converse with [[Dr Merlay]]. Inform him about the '''{{KW|Cool Gloothy Mixture}}'''. Next, utilize the [[Cool Gloothy Mixture]] on the [[Particle Accelerator]], located in the southwest corner of his laboratory. This action will transform the [[Cool Gloothy Mixture]] into an [[Ominously Glowing Pill]]. If you wish to keep the [[Ominously Glowing Pill]], you can either drop it or deposit it into your depot prior to reporting back to [[Dr Merlay]].

Finally, speak to [[Dr Merlay]] once again to report your success by saying '''{{KW|Yes}}'''. In return, you will receive 1 [[Rathleton_Quest#Method|voting right]] in the [[Rathleton Magistrate]].

This is an extension of [[#The Glooth Bandits|The Glooth Bandits]] mission. You can hand in 10 [[Glooth Capsule]]s at any time to [[Elbert]] by saying '''hi''' > '''yes'''. Do note that the mission "Glooth Bandits" needs to be completed before he accepts the capsules.

[[Elbert]] will only accept [[Glooth Capsule]]s, but if you remove the taint from the [[Tainted Glooth Capsule]] he will also accept those. If you have 10 [[Glooth Capsule]]s, you can give them to [[Elbert]].

In order to convert [[Tainted Glooth Capsule]]s into [[Glooth Capsule]]s, you must create a [[Filled Glooth Converter]] following the steps of [[#The Mutated Glooth Plague|The Mutated Glooth Plague]] mission. Unlike during [[#The Mutated Glooth Plague|The Mutated Glooth Plague]] mission, the [[Filled Glooth Converter]] will '''not''' break when you convert the [[Tainted Glooth Capsule]]s, thus it's a good idea to keep a [[Filled Glooth Converter]] stored in your depot to convert all your capsules.

You'll get 1 voting right with the Rathleton Magistrate for every 5 capsules you deliver.
