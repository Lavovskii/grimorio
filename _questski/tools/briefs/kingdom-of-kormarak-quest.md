## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Kingdom of Kormarak Quest}}

This quest is a [[Daily Respawns|daily respawn]]. This means only one [[Character]] can get the rewards per [[Floor Reset]].<br />
It is recommended to do this quest after [[Server Save]], so there is a higher chance nobody have gotten the reward before.

== Recommended equipment ==
* [[Rope]] or equivalent, to avoid getting stuck if you go the wrong way
* [[Great Fireball]] and [[Heavy Magic Missile]] [[Runes]] for lower level [[mage]]s
* [[Health Potion|Health]] and [[Mana Potion]]s

== Method ==
[[File:Ancient Temple-4.jpg|thumb|400px]]

* Go down to the [[Ancient Temple]], near the [[Thais]] northern entrace ([COORD x=32356 y=32171 z=7 | here]).

* Go down the [[Ladder (Wood)|ladder]] to the north.

* Follow the white line through the dungeon and climb down the ladder. You will likely encounter [[Rotworm]]s, [[Poison Spider]]s and weaker [[Orcs]].

* There is a room with lots of [[Poison Gas]] but you only have to go through four of them. Cast the [[Antidote]] spell if you don't want to receive further damage and climb down.

[[File:Way to Kingdom of Kormarak.jpg|thumb|200px]]

* Down this ladder there are 3 [[Minotaur]]s and a few Poison Spiders. Go north following the line. You can also go by a room with [[Skeleton]]s and do the [[Ghoul Room Quest]] now.

:{{Message|type=look|You see a sign.<br />You read: Home of the dead Kingdom of Kormarak<br />Enter and die!}}

* Go up the stairs and you may have to kill three [[Ghoul]]s that spawn there.

* Go through the [[Magic Forcefield]]. Be prepared to face 11 Ghouls spread throughout the room and a [[Demon Skeleton]] that spawns at the far end of the room. Use some [[Great Fireball]]s to take the Ghouls out quick and kill the [[Demon Skeleton]] by shooting [[Heavy Magic Missile]]s while running away.

[[File:Kingdom of Kormarak respawn.jpg|400px]]

* Open the [[Dead Human]] near the [[Wooden Coffin]] to find a [[Brass Armor]], a [[Brass Helmet]], a [[Hatchet]] and 13 [[Throwing Star]]s.
