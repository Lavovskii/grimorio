## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Rise of Devovorga}}
This World Quest starts on September 1, and lasts for up to 7 days (until the server save on the 7th). During this time players on a whole world have to co-operate in order to be successful. If the world succeeds, it benefits from +50% health and mana regeneration until the end of September. 

== Required Equipment ==
*Hunting supplies
*Team work

== Method ==

=== Stage 1 ===
After the server save on September 1, travel to [[Vengoth]] and enter the energy gate on the surface by the eastern mountain ([COORD x=32948 y=31521 z=7 | here]) to [[Devovorga]]'s lair. You will find the NPC [[A Beautiful Girl]] - Devovorga in her human form. To wake Devovorga you must talk to her an uncertain amount of times with uncertain intervals between them.

When [[A Beautiful Girl]] wakes, [[Devovorga]] will spawn in the lair. She can be damaged in this form, but she will not die. 30 minutes after [[Devovorga]] spawns, the server will broadcast a message:
:{{Message|type=server|Something is rumbling deep underground all over the world.}} 

At this point, [[Devovorga]] becomes immune, and the 5 portals that have appeared all over the Tibia continent will now function, leading to Devovorga's incarnations' lairs.

=== Stage 2 ===
Stage 2 begins with the server message "{{Server Message|Devovorga has awoken.}}". The immortal version of [[Devovorga (Invincible)|Devovorga]] spawns immediately, and the five mini-bosses ([[Devovorga]]'s incarnations) spawn exactly 30 minutes later.

The server must slay all five of [[Devovorga]]'s incarnations at once and leave the room to complete Stage 2. This can potentially already be done on the first day of the event — however, the world won't progress to Stage 3 until the next server save. This is to allow more players to participate by killing the incarnations and claim rewards.

The incarnations respawn 5 minutes after being killed, so this is the time span the world has to kill all of them.

The server message "{{Server Message|The incarnations of Devovorga have been thrown back into their abyss! The threat ain't over yet. Watch out!}}" triggers once your world manages to kill all incarnations at the same time span. At this point Stage 2 is completed.

If the world failed to slay all incarnations at the same time during the whole day, Stage 2 will not be completed and the world will go back to Stage 1 at server save, with [[A Beautiful Girl]] needing to be woken up again.

=== Stage 3 ===
Once Stage 2 is {{Post|37134085|completed}}, Stage 3 will begin between 8 and 14 hours after the next server save. (The earliest this can possibly happen is on September 2, i.e. the second day of the event, at 8 hours after server save.)

Stage 3 starts with the server message "{{Server Message|The mutation of Devovorga goes on! Fight her back!}}".

[[Bloom of Doom|Blooms of Doom]] will now spawn in [[Devovorga]]'s lair below [[Vengoth]]. Kill these with distance attacks because they have very high melee damage (9500+). [[Spawn of Devovorga|Spawns of Devovorga]] will appear when the blooms are slain. Kill these and everyone in the area will be teleported to the previous area. Go down the hole again and kill the [[Spawn of Devovorga|Spawns of Devovorga]] once more.

The server message "{{Server Message|The mutation of Devovorga has been stopped - but her essence was shattered once again and formed incarnations!}}" now appears. The five incarnations start spawning in their respective lairs again. (For each incarnation that is killed, a new [[Spawn of Devovorga]] spawns in Devovorga’s lair.)

Just like in Stage 2, the world must slay the five incarnations at the same time. When this is done, the server message "{{Server Message|The incarnations of Devovorga have been thrown back into their abyss!}}" appears.

All players in [[Devovorga]]'s lair are teleported to the area above. They must go back and, after 5 minutes, slay the [[Bloom of Doom|Blooms of Doom]] and the [[Vulnerable Cocoon]]. When this is done, the server message "{{Server Message|You destroyed Devovorga's cocoon - but not in time to stop her transformation. Kill her - or the world as you know it will end.}}" appears. Finally, [[Devovorga]] spawns in a vulnerable form.

Focus all your firepower on [[Devovorga]]. When [[Devovorga]] is slain, the energy gate in her lair and all the incarnation portals will lead to the reward room for those who have participated in the event.

During the quest messages will appear for the entire world to see such as:
== Log-in Messages ==
These messages will appear in your Server Log when you log in. They can be used to determine which stage of [[Devovorga]] your world is currently on.

* {{Server Message|Something is rumbling deep underground all over the world.}} - Indicates that the quest is beginning and [[Devovorga]] has appeared.
* {{Server Message|An unknown evil is dwelling deep under Vengoth and strange portals have appeared all over the world...}} - Indicates that [[Devovorga]] minibosses are spawning on the first day of the event.
* {{Server Message|You might still be able to stop the evil entity that has awoken deep under Vengoth - but there is not much time left!}} - Indicates that [[Devovorga]] minibosses are spawning from the second day onward.
* {{Server Message|The shattered incarnations of Devovorga reassemble step by step. They will rise again sooner or later. Be prepared!}} - Indicates that the third stage will begin today or has already begun.

Once your world has defeated [[Devovorga]], you will receive all of the following messages:

* {{Server Message|Thanks to the mighty and fearless Tibians who drove Devovorga back into her abyss, your regeneration and your experience gain from extra-dimensional creatures have been improved.}}
* {{Server Message|You made it - Devovorga, the incredibly powerful weapon sealed deep below Vengoth, was destroyed.}}
* {{Server Message|Upon her death, energy was released all over the world.}}
* {{Server Message|Until the month ends, it will boost your health and mana regeneration and help your fight against extra-dimensional creatures.}}
* {{Server Message|Congratulations, Tibians!}}

= Individual Rewards =
[[File:Reward_room.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Inside the reward room.]]
To gain access to the reward room, players must sufficiently contribute to slaying [[Devovorga]] or one of her incarnations during the final 60 seconds of the fight. The quest log entry for ''Rise of Devovorga'' will indicate whether the player has contributed enough to be rewarded. Note that even if a player receives the achievement for killing a boss it is not necessarily the case that that player will be eligible for the rewards.

To reach the reward room after your world successfully banished [[Devovorga]], simply enter the portal where you killed one of the incarnations or Devovorga herself. These will teleport you to the reward room, where nothing but [[Treasure Chest (Blue)|treasure chests]] await you.

The reward room is only accessible until the server save on September 30th.

= World Rewards =
Worlds who succeed on killing [[Devovorga]] will receive 50% extra regeneration per hour until the end of September.

Additionally, creatures in the [[Bestiary/Classes#Extra Dimensional|Extra Dimensional]] Bestiary category will grant 15% extra experience points.
