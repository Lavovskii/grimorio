=== Required Equipment ===

* [[Rope]]
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Fishing Rod]]
* 1 [[Worm]]

* [[Antidote Rune]], if high enough level

== Method ==
This quest consists of 7 missions. You do ''not'' need to first ask an NPC but can just begin killing or collecting things.

Starting with the NPC you must find [[Mr Morris]] on the second floor of the [[Adventurer's Outpost]], right above [[Inigo]]. He offers 3 "fetch" missions and 3 "kill" missions. In addition, he also needs a '''key''' to be found.

=== Fetch ===
==== The Lost Amulet ====
Keyword: '''amulet'''<br />
Reward: 50 [[Gold Coin]]s<br />
Notes: Go to the cave directly below the Outpost, accessible from holes near the western Sorcerer ramp or eastern Paladin ramp. Around the far-south side of the cave is a human [[Skeleton (Item)|Skeleton]] on top of a [[Hiding the Amulet (Book)|Piece of Paper]] ([COORD x=32047 y=31914 z=7 | here]).

The paper indicates that the amulet was hidden on the beach somewhere. Go back upstairs and take the south exit (Druid side) from the outpost. Once on ground level walk east a bit then south to the beach (you can swim here too). Dig on the tile ([COORD x=32089 y=31937 z=7 | here]) with a [[shovel]] 3 times to retrieve a [[Strange Amulet]]. Take it back to [[Mr Morris]] to be rewarded with 50 [[gp]].

==== The Stolen Log Book ====
Keyword: '''log book'''<br />
Reward: 50 [[Gold Coin]]s<br />
Notes: You must find a [[Torn Log Book]] for [[Mr Morris]]. Leave the Outpost either south (Druid) or east (Paladin). Paladins should go south of the ramp to find a cave, druids should go west to find it. Go down to the final floor and fight your way to the north-west end of the cave. You may encounter a [[Troll Marauder]] at the end. Do not go up the ladders; instead, walk east across what seems to be a bridge ([COORD x=32060 y=31800 z=10 | here]). Open the chest to find the [[Torn Log Book]]. Go back to [[Mr Morris]] and report your mission.

You can do this task alongside the '''kill trolls''' task, which you receive automatically when you kill a troll or by talking to [[Mr Morris]].

==== The Rare Herb ====
Keyword: '''herbs'''<br />
Reward: 50 [[Gold Coin]]s<br />
Notes: Find a rare herb on the west side of Dawnport, ground floor. This is near [[Woblin]]s cave and one entrance to the Minotaur cave. Use the [[Dawnfire Herb Plant]] to obtain some unseen flower buds, and you will receive the following message: ''You harvested some of the herb's flower buds. Bring them to Morris for a reward.'' This ''could'' be done in tandem with the ''Plus Minos A Few'' mission. The herb looks like [[Jungle Grass]] [[File:Jungle Grass.gif]]
[MINIMAP x=32028 y=31882 z=7 marks=none]

==== The Dorm Key ====
Keyword: '''key'''<br />
Reward: [[Key 0010]] or 50 [[Gold Coin]]s<br />
Notes: Find [[Woblin]] in his own cave in western Dawnpost. First bring a [[Fishing Rod]] and 1 [[worm]]; consider sharing the Rod with a friend but you each need a worm. Exit the outpost going west (Sorcerer ramp) then go down to ground floor from the next western ramp. Walk north to a ladder and bridge, go down to ground floor again and go to a ramp near the water, ''not'' the shovel hole. Walk through the cave until you meet Woblin.<br />
Ask about his '''precious''' and a '''reward'''. He wants you to fish for his arch enemy, [[Old Nasty]]. Use the fishing rod on the lake next to [[Woblin]]. Once you fish out Old Nasty he will exchange it for [[Key 0010]]. You can use this key to unlock the south east bedroom on the top floor of the Outpost. There you can find another [[Key 0010]] inside the drawers under the book. You can then give one of the keys to [[Mr Morris]] to complete the mission for 50 gold (drop one on the floor before talking to him, or he will take both).
[MINIMAP x=32021 y=31886 z=7 marks=none]

=== Kill ===
==== A Toll on Trolls ====
Keyword: '''trolls'''<br />
Reward: 50 [[Gold Coin]]s<br />
Notes: Kill 20 [[Mountain Troll]]s. These can be found all over the surface of [[Dawnport]] and in a large cave accessible from 2 holes just outside the Outpost but each closer to the southern Paladin ramp than the western Sorcerer or eastern Paladin ramps - there are 2 other holes closer to those ramps.
* Note: this cave is mostly [[Mountain Troll]]s and [[Troll-Trained Salamander]]s but there is a solo [[Troll]] near the end and ladders to 2 rooms: one has two [[Skeleton]]s, and one has more Salamanders and a [[Salamander Trainer]], a high-defense and fast boss.
The two cave entrances are just outside the outpost
[MINIMAP x=32057 y=31900 z=6 marks=none] [MINIMAP x=32074 y=31894 z=6 marks=none]

==== The Goblin Slayer ====
Keyword: '''goblins'''<br />
Reward: 50 [[Gold Coin]]s<br />
Notes: Kill 20 [[Muglex Clan Footman]]. These Goblins are found with 2 other kinds, [[Muglex Clan Scavenger]] and [[Muglex Clan Assassin]], in their own cave with 2 entrances. The closest entrance is directly below the Outpost on the side nearest to the eastern Paladin ramp: go down the hole near the ramp then down another hole a few tiles south-east. 
* Note: [[Lesser Fire Devil]]s, [[Crazed Dwarf|Crazed Dwarves]], [[Troll Marauder]]s, [[Poison Spider]]s, and [[Spider]]s.
There are two cave entrances: one below outpost and one to the east
[MINIMAP x=32087 y=31888 z=7 marks=none] [MINIMAP x=32111 y=31903 z=7 marks=none]

==== Plus Minos A Few ====
Keyword: '''minotaur'''<br />
Reward: 50 [[Gold Coin]]s<br />
Notes: Kill 20 [[Minotaur Bruiser]]s. These can be found primarily with [[Minotaur Poacher]]s in a cave with 2 entrances. The closest entrance is directly below the Outpost. You can take either hole, so the one by western Sorcerer ramp, then walk north-east, or the one by the eastern Paladin ramp, then walk north-west. The hole has a [[Warning Sign]] [[File:Warning Sign.gif]]. 
* Note: the first floor down has just Bruisers and Poachers but the next floor has the same and [[Minotaur Occultist]]s, [[Dawn Scorpion]]s, [[Brittle Skeleton]]s, [[Poison Spider]]s, and [[Spider]]s.
There are two cave entrances: one below outpost and one to the west near [[Woblin]]'s cave and near the [[Dawnfire Herb Plant]].
[MINIMAP x=32050 y=31874 z=7 marks=none] [MINIMAP x=32035 y=31890 z=7 marks=none]
