== Method ==
This MWC is active every day and features a tug of war between [[Poachers]] and the game they hunt over [[Poacher Caves|some caves]] located at [[Ferngrims Gate]], [COORD x=32756 y=31928 z=7 | here]. Due to these struggles, there are three variations possible of these caves:
* [[Poacher Caves/Game]] (when game dominates).
* [[Poacher Caves/Poachers]] (when [[Poacher]]s dominate).
* [[Poacher Caves/Gloomy]] (when [[Ghost Wolf|Ghost Wolves]] & [[Gloom Wolf|Gloom Wolves]] dominate).

It all depends on your actions who comes out victorious and dominates the cave for a while.
