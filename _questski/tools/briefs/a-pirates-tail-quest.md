## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=A Pirate's Tail Quest}}

= Overview =

This quest has two separate mission-lines. The main mission revolve around helping [[Eustacio]] in the fight against Pirats. First you have to prove yourself to him by fighting Pirat Raids to gain access to [[Rascacoon]]. On the island, you must also gain the trust of the [[Rascoohan]], the island inhabitants, to be able to reach [[The Wreckoning]], the Pirats hideout, and fight their leaders. 

Parallel to that, you can start a search for a ''Hidden Treasure'' lost by some Coryms, which will eventually require access to Rascacoon as well. 

== Pirat Raids ==

Speak with [[Eustacio]] at the harbor in [[Venore]] (ground floor) – [COORD x=32937 y=32031 z=7 | here].  
Greet him with {{KW|hi}} > {{KW|mission}} > {{KW|yes}} to offer your help against the [[Pirats]].

To earn his trust, you must participate in multiple Pirat Raids across Tibia. Raid locations and types are random, but you can ask Eustacio about the current {{KW|location}} to check if/where a raid is active.

Before heading out, ask him for an {{KW|Orb}} {{Ilink|Magical Water Orb}} and a {{KW|Flintstone}} {{Ilink|Flintstone}}, as both are required during the raids.

You need a total of '''1,500 points''' to proceed.

=== Raid Types ===

There are three different raid types, and any of them can occur at any of the listed locations. A new raid begins '''30 minutes''' after the previous one ends.

==== Spyrats ====

[[File:A Pirate's Tail Quest - Spyrats Raid.png|thumb]]

[[Spyrat]]s spawn in the water near the shore. To reach them, use the [[Magical Water Orb]] {{Ilink|Magical Water Orb}} on the water to create an underwater platform. Build a path of platforms toward the Spyrats, then defeat them.

* Each Spyrat attacked grants '''5 points'''.
* You do '''not''' need to deal the most damage to receive points.

The raid ends after '''60 Spyrats''' have been killed. You will then see: {{Sound|The pirats used the big wave to flee!}}
{{Clear}}

==== Ship Attack ====

[[File:A Pirate's Tail Quest - Ship Raid.png|thumb]]

A large ship anchors near the shore. To repel the attack:
# Use a [[Pick]] on nearby stones to obtain [[Ballistic Boulder]]s {{Ilink|Ballistic Boulder}}.
# Load the boulders into the catapults.
# Ignite them using the [[Greenish Flintstone]] {{Ilink|Greenish Flintstone}}.
# Pull the lever to fire.

There is a 5-second cooldown between shots, but you can reload and ignite immediately after firing.
* Loading a Catapult: '''2 points'''
* Igniting a Boulder: '''2 points'''
* Firing a shot: '''2 points'''

The raid ends after '''50 shots''' are fired. You will then see: {{Sound|You fended off the attack! The ship is gone.}}
{{Clear}}

==== Land Invasion ====

Pirats invade the surrounding area and must be defeated.
* Each Pirat attacked grants '''3 points'''.
* You do '''not''' need to deal the most damage to receive points.

The raid ends after '''100 Pirats''' have been killed. You will then see: {{Sound|You pushed them back! The pirats ran away.}}
{{Clear}}

== Rascacoon ==

After getting 1500 points with [[Eustacio]] (you can check by asking about your '''status'''), he will tell you about a secret passage that he believes leads to the Pirats hideout. The passage leads, in fact, to an island [[Rascacoon]], which is also suffering with Pirats attacks. To reach this island, travel to [[Issavi]] and use the small shell north of the depot on the location shown below. Follow the path in this cave until you reach the surface of the island.

BE VERY CAREFUL as near the location north of the temple there is a pit-fall hole that (map)walking over will drop you into harms way. This hole leads to a group of [[Burning Gladiator|Burning Gladiators]], which can very easily kill a lvl 100 mage in no time.

=== Isle Events ===

On the island, find [[Tik'hi Tak'he]] to the west. Offer him your '''help''' and he will ask you to assist the Rascacoon villagers in different tasks to grain his trust. Each task will give you a certain amount of points, which you can check with Tik'hi Tak'he by asking about '''trust points'''. You need 1200 trust points to be able to advance to the Tentugly fight. 

'''Note''': After visiting the island for the first time and talking to Tik'hi Tak'he you'll gain access to a shortcut between Venore and Rascacoon. To travel to the island, ask [[Eustacio]] about '''shortcut'''. To return, simply use the small boat on the deck where you arrive from Venore. 

==== Memory Test ====

Find the large room inside the mountain at the center of the island. A few seconds after entering the room it will be locked and the test will start.

[[File:A Pirate's Tail Quest - Memory Test|thumb|300px]]
At the center, a certain pattern of Pieces of Shells, Spiral Shells and Starfishes will appear. Your goal is to memorize them. Once this pattern disappears, several patterns of the same objects will appear around the center. You have to quickly find the pattern previously displayed and stay near  it waiting for the round to end. The area corresponding to a pattern is delimited by the debris on the floor - you don't have to stay exactly on top of it. 

A good strategy is to focus only on two of the three types of objects and their positions. For instance, there will always be a diagonal with 3 Starfishes or Spiral Shells. If you memorize that part and the remaining 2-objects diagonal, you'll end up with a unique pattern. 

If you get the pattern right, you'll be allowed another chance, otherwise you'll be kicked out of the test area. You can win up to 10 times in a row, and then you'll be forced out of the room. Each successful round grants you 5 Trust Points for a total of 50, and you can repeat the Test every 2 hours.

Note that the options never change and will always be the same patterns on the same positions. You can see the patterns in the image below.

==== Stealth Mission ====

[[File:A Pirate's Tail Quest - Stealth Mission|300px|thumb]]
[[File:Stealth Task Guide - A Pirate's Tail Quest - Fastest Solo Task for Ratmiral and Pirat Ship Access|300px|thumb]]
To start this mission talk to [[Eustacios Spy Rat]] inside the Pirats cave, [COORD x=33857 y=31334 z=9 | here]. Ask him about '''advantage'''. After getting the instructions from him, enter the teleport. You can go with two other players during the mission, but this doesn't change your chances of succeeding or failing. 

Your objective is to cross the room undetected by the [[Pirat Guard]]s and [[Pirat Patrol]]s. If you walk on a tile adjacent to them you'll be automatically kicked from the room. The Guards don't move and are easier to avoid, but the Patrols walk back and forth on a predefined path, so you have to watch their movement. Finally, in order to pass undetected by the Guards on top of towers, you can only walk near the tower when the Wall Lamp is off. The Lamps start blinking before turning on and off, so make sure to keep an eye on them. Note that on some spots you'll have to [[Levitate]] up and down.

You can use the map below as a reference on the path you must take, with the coins representing the path. [[Platinum Coin]]s are used to show parts of the path on which you must watch out for the Wall Lamps. 

You have 5 minutes to complete the mission, and this time is displayed as a countdown in seconds below the character name. Completing the mission successfully will grant you 200 Trust Points, and it can be repeated every 20 hours. 

[[File:A Pirate's Tail Quest - Stealth Map.png|850px]]

==== Supply Mission ====

This task happens just north of [[Tik'hi Tak'he]]. Find [[Om'Wake Naha]] nearby [COORD x=33744 y=31332 z=7 | here] and ask him how you could be '''helping'''. Enter the teleport next to him, where you'll find a lever with spots for 5 players. A full team is recommended for this mission.

When you enter the room, use the chest at your side and pick up the [[Racoohan Rat Revealer]] that will appear on the floor. Your objective is to push the Cheese down the open trapdoor at the center, while also protecting them from the rats that will spawn. To deal with the rats, you first have to use the Revealer on them, which makes them vulnerable to your attacks.You can also lure them over the stone piles to make them disappear. 

The best strategy here is to only push the cheese near the trapdoor and have one or two players doing that. The rest of the team should stay around the room dealing with the rats, since they will quickly "eat" the cheese, which make you lose points. You can follow your score on the counter under your character's name.

Completing the mission successfully will grant you 200 Trust Points and it can be repeated every 20 hours.

==== The Journey ====

{{FightTips|
{{FightTip|type=use|element=Energy}}
}}

[[File:A Pirate's Tail Quest - The Journey|right|300px]]

''Note¹'': The Journey can only be taken if you have progressed up until the ''Queso'' mission of the ''Hidden Treasure'' part of the quest.<br/>
''Note²'': To be able to open the chest after the Journey, you'll need to have a [[Cheesy Key]] {{Ilink|Cheesy Key}}, obtained from the fight against [[Tentugly's Head]].

Enter the teleport north of the island, [COORD x=33734 y=31275 z=7 | here]. The Journey can be taken by up to 5 players together. Your objective is to protect the raft for 10 minutes from attacks and obstacles. The health of the Raft is displayed as a small number under your character's health bar. If the raft takes too many hits and loses all its health, you'll fail.

Succeeding in the Journey yields 200 Trust Points with [[Tik'hi Tak'he]].

Note that since it takes 10 minutes to complete this event, many of the same events will repeat. Generally, the sequence follows the pattern outlined below, although slight variations may occur.

'''Gusts of Wind'''

{{Sound|Captain Coohan: WIND AHOY! CATCH THE BUGGERS WITH THE WIND CATCHER!}}

A few seconds after entering the raft, several [[Gust of Wind|Gusts of Wind]] will appear. Be cautious, as they inflict considerable [[Energy Damage]]. The Gusts must be lured to the Grille near the mast. Doing so adds health to the raft, which can be crucial. To simplify luring, use [[Magic Wall]]s and [[Wild Growth]]s to the north and south of the Grille, and then walk east and west to guide the Gusts over the specified tile.

'''Trash'''

{{Sound|Captain Coohan: HAZARD AHEAD! MAN THE COOHAN CANNONS! BLAST IT TO PIECES!}}

Small pieces of trash will begin floating towards the raft from the west. Use the Cannons on the raft to destroy them. The cannons are not automatic; you must wait until the trash approaches before clicking again. This task continues throughout the event, so ensure team members take turns monitoring and clearing debris on the left side of the raft.

'''Angry Elementals'''

{{Sound|Captain Coohan: WATER ELEMENTALS! GET RID OF THEM}}

[[Angry Elemental]]s will appear on the raft. Eliminate them using [[Energy Damage]] or [[Death Damage]], as they are resistant to most other elements.

'''Sea Serpent'''

{{Sound|Captain Coohan: SEA SERPENT STARBOARD! AHOY! BRACE YOURSELF!}}

A [[Sea Serpent (Journey)|Sea Serpent]] will swim alongside the raft, either north or south. It periodically launches a strong wave that deals 300-600 [[Physical Damage]] towards the raft. Be ready to dodge or block these waves. Use magic walls to protect the [[Raccoon Supplies]], as the serpent's waves can damage them and reduce the Raft's HP.

'''Quara Saboteurs'''

{{Sound|Captain Coohan: QUARAS AHOY! PROTECT ME SUPPLIES!}}

[[Quara Saboteur]]s will spawn on the raft and aim to destroy the [[Raccoon Supplies]]. Since [[Energy Bomb Rune]]s are ineffective, Knights must quickly use [[Challenge]] to distract them. Focus the group's attacks on eliminating them swiftly, as they often retarget the supplies.

'''Mean Termites'''

When [[Mean Termite]]s spawn, eliminate them promptly, prioritizing those near the mast. Each larva bite reduces the Raft's HP by 5, so vigilance is essential.

All of these events will repeat over the 10-minute duration, so remain attentive to the captain's warnings!

=== Tentugly ===

[[File:A Pirate's Tail Quest - Tentugly|right|300px]]{{FightTips|
{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Energy}}
}}Once you have accumulated 1,200 [[Trust Points]] or more, speak with [[Tik'hi Tak'he]] about your mission again. He will grant you access to Tentugly. Head to the teleport located south-east of the island, [COORD x=33800 y=31359 z=7 | here]. Note that 1,200 Trust Points will be deducted to provide you with permanent access to this teleporter. Proceed up one floor to talk to [[Captain Jack Rat]], who will invite you on a journey to the Pirats' hideout, with the risk of encountering the formidable seamonster, Tentugly. Up to five players can join this challenge. You have 8 minutes to defeat Tentugly before being ejected from the battle.

After pulling the lever, you will find yourself at the center of a boat facing [[Tentugly's Head]]. During the battle, [[Servant of Tentugly|Servants of Tentugly]] will spawn around the boat. Ignore them or eliminate them before focusing your attacks on [[Tentugly's Head]] until it temporarily retreats. Shortly after, many [[Tentacle]]s will emerge around the boat.

'''Warning: Beware that standing in areas where the tentacles are sticking out likely indicates that they will be spawning there, which could result in receiving the following message before being teleported back to the temple.'''

{{Message|type=server|You had to leave the area because of strange events.}}

You must defeat the tentacles one by one. Each defeated tentacle will retreat by one tile. Continue this process until all the tentacles have been eliminated. Be aware that there are additional tentacles on the upper level as well.

Once all tentacles have been vanquished, you will have another opportunity to engage [[Tentugly's Head]]. Defeat it to claim your reward.

Upon your first victory over Tentugly, you will earn the [[Krakoloss]] mount and the [[Release the Kraken]] achievement.

After exiting the boat, you will arrive at [[The Wreckoning]]. You may leave via the teleport or explore the island further if desired.

=== Ratmiral Blackwhiskers ===
{{FightTips|
  |{{FightTip|type=equip|element=Life Drain}}
}}

After defeating [[Tentugly's Head|Tentugly]] you'll arrive at [[The Wreckoning]]. If you're ready for the fight you can proceed to the teleport [COORD x=33892 y=31197 z=7 | here]. If you decide to leave the island, you can return to The Wreckoning by going to Captain Jack Rat's ship and saying '''sail'''/'''safe'''/'''yes'''. 

[[File:A Pirate's Tail Quest - Ratmiral Blackwhiskers|right|300px]]

This fight happens in four stages, the first one being the most dangerous part of the fight. During the first 3 stages there's a miniboss that will disappear before it dies. On the final stage they all return to aid the main boss in the fight. 

'''First Stage'''

Initially your goal is to destroy the [[Weak Spot]] on the gate north of the room. In order to do that, you must drag the [[Rum Barrel]] that spawns north-east of the room near the Weak Spot and wait until it dies. Note that both the Barrel and the gate only take considerable damage from [[Life Drain]] damage. The [[Pirat Artillerist]]s that are spread across the room shoot a GFB-like Life Drain attack which will hit both the gate and the Barrel. 

While trying to breach the wall, you'll have to fight [[Rateye Ric]] as well as several [[Elite Pirat]]s. The blocker should challenge them, but beware that [[Rateye Ric]] attacks from distance. Furthermore, avoid walking around the [[Smelly Cheese]]s because they deal constant damage around themselves.

The fastest strategy is to have all players near the gate while one of them pushes the Barrel. Using this strategy, only one [[Rum Barrel]] is necessary since the [[Weak Spot]] will also take damage from the Artillerists. This method can be very dangerous for lower level players and specially mages, however. A safer strategy is to have each player stay away from each other (2 east, 2 west and the blocker at the center). This way, the damage taken by each player will be much lower, but you'll likely need to push 2 [[Rum Barrel]]s (it respawns shortly after exploding). Regardless of that, [[Life Drain]] protection is extremely helpful here, especially [[Necklace of the Deep|Necklaces of the Deep]] {{Ilink|Necklace of the Deep}}. 

'''Second Stage'''

After breaching the wall, move north to the ship. Here you'll fight [[Mister Catkiller]]. This part is very fast and should only last about 30 seconds, after which a ladder to the upper floor will appear. During this part you also have to watch out for [[Elite Pirat]]s and [[Smelly Cheese]]s. 

'''Third Stage'''

Going up the ladder to the ship's main floor you'll find [[1st Mate Ratticus]]. It will also disappear not long after the fight begins, and the final stage will start.

'''Fourth Stage'''

You'll now finally fight [[Ratmiral Blackwhiskers]]. During this fight, the only special mechanic is a way to avoid the boss' fire waves. North-west of the room there's a Bath Tub. By using it, each player gets 6 "water charges" which will protect you from the fire damage from the boss. In order to get the charges, Ratmiral can't be near the Tub, so the knight must lure it to the east and quickly run to the tub to recharge whenever necessary.

When [[Ratmiral Blackwhiskers]] reaches 50% of his health, all the previously fought minibosses will reappear. You may single-target some of them to bring them down quickly and make the fight safer, but your main goals remains being to kill the Ratmiral. 

As during the other stages, you'll still have to deal with [[Smelly Cheese]]s, [[Elite Pirat]]s and [[Life Drain]] damage from [[Pirat Artillerist]]s during this fight.

After successfully fighting Ratmiral, ask [[Tik'hi Tak'he]] about your '''mission''' and you will get the basic [[Rascoohan Outfits|Rascoohan Outfit]]. The addons of this outfit are obtained by using [[Soap]] and [[Scrubbing Brush]] which are dropped by [[Ratmiral Blackwhiskers]]. You will also receive 100 Trust Points every time you defeat Ratmiral.

== Hidden Treasure ==

Note: even though you can start this mission before getting access to [[Rascacoon]], you'll need a [[Cheesy Key]] obtained from the fight against Tentugly to take the treasure. 

=== Rita ===

Travel to the [[Corym Black Market]] in [[Liberty Bay]]. Go down to the 4th floor and go to the room north, where you'll find [[Rita's Letter]] in a chest. On this letter, Rita explains about a treasure she found with her brothers on a small island somewhere. Each brother holds part of a Rhyme you need to pass a magic portal that protects the Treasure. 

To learn about the whereabouts of the brothers, go to the room south-west of the chest and look for the parrot [[Larry]]. Ask him about '''Rita''' and you'll receive a [[Quest Log]] update. Larry will tell you that one of the brothers, [[Sniff]], is on [[Venore]].

=== Sniff ===

[[Sniff]] can be found on the ground floor of the eastern exit of [[Venore]], down the ladder, [COORD x=33001 y=32096 z=7 | here]. Offer him your {{KW|Help}} and he will ask you to recover some of his belongings that he lost in the sea south of the [[Plains of Havoc]]. 

<div style="float:right;>[MINIMAP x=32757 y=32384 z=7 marks=none]</div>
Travel to the Plains and go to a small beach all the way south on the location shown to the right. To recover Sniff's belongings you'll have to swim in the bay, however, there are sharks swimming in it and you'll need to be very careful with the path you take. You have to follow a specific route, and if you make a mistake you'll take 500 damage and be forced back to the shore. Follow the map below to reach the Cask, and then use it to retrieve Sniff's belongings.

[[File:A Pirate's Tail Quest - Shark path.png]]

Return to Sniff with the [[File:Bag of Smuggled Goods.gif]] [[Bag of Smuggled Goods]]. Hand it in to him by saying {{KW|HI}} - {{KW|Help}} - {{KW|Yes}}. In return, he will tell you where to find his sister [[Ra'Clette]] and provide the second part of the poem ''''Mould is blue!''''.

=== Ra'Clette ===

Leave Venore towards the eastern Swamp. You'll find [[Ra'Clette]] above the [[Little Corym Charlatan]]s cave, [COORD x=33030 y=32139 z=5 | here]. Offer her your '''help'''. Your task will be to retrieve a personal item of [[Eustacio]] so she can make a voodoo doll of him. If you have already earned Eustacio's trust by helping in the Pirat raids, you can talk to him about his '''house''' and he will grant you access and add a marker to your minimap. In his house ([COORD x=32921 y=32138 z=6 | here]), open the [[Chest of Drawers]] to find a [[Lace-Trimmed Handkerchief]] {{Ilink|Lace-Trimmed Handkerchief}}. Deliver this to [[Ra'Clette]] and she will give you her part of the rhyme, as well as send you to [[Thais]] to find the last brother, Queso.

=== Queso ===

Travel to Thais and go to the Prison (where [[Henricus]] stays). Inside a cell to your left you'll find some inscriptions on a wall. Use the inscriptions to discover Queso's part of the rhyme.

[[File:A Pirate's Tail Quest - Queso.png]]

=== The Treasure ===

To finally get the treasure, you'll have to complete [[#The Journey|The Journey]] mission in [[Rascacoon]]. Make sure you bring a [[Cheesy Key]], which can be obtained from the fight against [[Tentugly's Head]]. 

After completing the Journey, you'll arrive at a small island. Use the portal to cross to the protected area and the open the chest. You'll receive a new Familiar and its corresponding figurine, depending on your vocation:

* Druids get [[Mossmasher]] {{Ilink|Mossmasher}} and a [[Mossmasher Figurine]] {{Ilink|Mossmasher Figurine}};
* Paladins get [[Sandscourge]] {{Ilink|Sandscourge}} and a [[Sandscourge Figurine]] {{Ilink|Sandscourge Figurine}};
* Knights get [[Snowbash]] {{Ilink|Snowbash}} and a [[Snowbash Figurine]] {{Ilink|Snowbash Figurine}};
* Sorcerers get [[Bladespark]] {{Ilink|Bladespark}} and a [[Bladespark Figurine]] {{Ilink|Bladespark Figurine}};
* Monks get [[Moonhunter]] {{Ilink|Moonhunter}} and a [[Moonhunter Figurine]] {{Ilink|Moonhunter Figurine}}.
