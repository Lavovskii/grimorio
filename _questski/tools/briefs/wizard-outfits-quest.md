== Outfit ==
The [[Wizard Outfits]] are available to all [[Premium Account]] players. Only the addons are included in this quest.

== Skull Mask (male)/Snake Tiara (female) ==
[[Image:Outfit Wizard Male Addon 2.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit Wizard Female Addon 2.gif]]

=== Required Equipment ===
* [[Medusa Shield]]
* [[Dragon Scale Mail]]
* [[Crown Legs]]
* [[Ring of the Sky]]

=== Method ===
* Go to [[Lugri]] underground north of [[Thais]] ([COORD x=32389 y=32118 z=8 | here]). Watch out for [[Bonelord]]s and [[Ghoul]]s.
* Ask him about his "outfit". He will tell you that only true followers of [[Zathroth]] can wear the skull mask, and ask you if you are willing to prove your loyalty.
* After you have proven your loyalty by sacrificing all of the items, then you will be allowed to wear the skull mask.

== Skull Shoulder Pad (male)/Wings (female) ==
[[Image:Outfit Wizard Male Addon 1.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit Wizard Female Addon 1.gif]]

=== Required Equipment ===
* Must have completed the first addon (skull helmet/snake tiara)
* 50 [[Holy Orchid]]s

=== Method ===
* You can collect the items above before starting this quest. To save time, you are advised to collect the items before talking to the npc.
** Collect [[Holy Orchid]]s from [[Elf Arcanist]]s
* Go to [[The Queen Of The Banshees]] under the [[Ghostlands]]
* Ask her for an "addon"
* She will tell you that she is tired of the stench of death, and would like to smell something nice for once.
* Bring her 50 [[Holy Orchid]]s and she will reward you with the addon.
