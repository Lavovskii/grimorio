== Required Equipment ==
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Rope]]

== Method ==
''Note: You may also want to do the [[To Outfox a Fox Quest]] at the same time, since it is in the same place.''<br />

Travel west of [[Kazordoon]] past the [[Dwarf Mines]] and use your [[shovel]] to dig a [[Loose Stone Pile]] hidden behind a [[Plum Tree]] located [COORD x=32489 y=31965 z=7 | Here]. You may encounter [[Snake]]s, [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Wolf|Wolves]], [[Bear]]s, [[Bug]]s and [[Dwarf|Dwarves]]. Once you go down the hole, walk west and rope up into the [[Secret Hideout]] [COORD x=32466 y=31966 z=8 | here]

Creatures on each of the floors:<br />
'''ground floor''': 2 [[Minotaur Guard]]s, 2 [[Minotaur Archer]]s<br />
'''first floor''': 4 [[Minotaur Guard]]s, 4 [[Minotaur Archer]]s<br />
'''floor 2''': 2 [[Minotaur Guard]]s in each room (north and south rooms)<br />
'''floor 3''': North side: 2 [[Minotaur Guard]]s, 4 [[Minotaur Archer]]s<br />
South side: 2 [[Minotaur Mage]]s, 4 [[Minotaur Archer]]s<br />
'''floor 4''': North side:4 [[Minotaur Archer]]s<br />
South side: 1 [[Minotaur Mage]], 3 [[Minotaur Archer]]s

The rewards are found in different locations across the hideout.
*56 [[Gold Coin]]s can be found in a [[Chest of Drawers (Quest)|Chest of Drawers]] next to a table [COORD x=32460 y=31951 z=5 | here]
*[[Steel Helmet]] can be found in a [[Box (Quest)|Box]] next an anvil [COORD x=32461 y=31947 z=4 | here]
*47 Gold Coins can be found in a [[Chest (Quest)|Chest]] between two beds [COORD x=32464 y=31957 z=5 | here]
*[[The Horned Fox]]'s [[The Fox Is Out (Book)|scroll]] can be found in a [[Chest (Quest)|Chest]] between a bed and a table [COORD x=32467 y=31962 z=4 | here]
