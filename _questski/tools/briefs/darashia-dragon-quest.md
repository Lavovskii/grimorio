{{Spoiler|name= Darashia Dragon Quest}}

===Method===

Go into the [[Darashia Dragon Lair]], north of [[Darashia]] through the level 40 [[Gate of Expertise]]. The entrance looks like this:<br />
[[File:Darashia_Dragons.png|200px]]

On the first floor you will find only [[Dragon]]s and [[Dragon Hatchling]]s, go south to find the stairs for the second hunting level. Going this way you will maximal meet 2 [[Dragon]]s.

On the second level there are [[Dragon]]s as well as [[Dragon Lord]]s, go north-west to find some more stairs which will take you to the third level. On this way you will face 1-3 [[Dragon Lord]]s one by one.

The third hunting level has 4 [[Dragon Lord]]s, you will find your reward inside the dead dragon's body.
