## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Troll Cave Quest}}
==Required Equipment==
* [[Rope]]

==Method==
* From the [[Edron]] [[depot]], go down the drain in the south-west corner of the castle ([COORD x=33165 y=31828 z=7 | here]).
* There is only one way to go, so follow this cave down and up until you emerge in a grassy area ([COORD x=33129 y=31811 z=7 | here]).
* Go to the Troll Cave to the north-west ([COORD x=33117 y=31776 z=7 | here]), and go down the hole. Be prepared to face up to 20 [[Troll Champion]]s if no one has been down there for a while. For low level players it's advised to move in slowly, there's a small passage where only one of the [[Troll Champion]]s can attack you.

* Follow this path to the north and east, and go down the hole:
[[Image:Troll Cave Quest Map 01.jpg]]

* Follow this path to the room to the north and go down the hole:
[[Image:Troll Cave Quest Map 02.jpg]]

* Here you will face a number of [[Troll]]s, [[Bear]]s, [[Troll Champion]]s, and a [[Frost Troll]]:
[[Image:Troll Cave Quest Map 03.jpg]]

* The reward is in two boxes on the east side of the room.
