## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=Small Ruby Quest}}
This quest is a [[Daily Respawns|daily respawn]]. This means only one [[Character]] can get the rewards per [[Floor Reset]].<br />
It is recommended to do this quest after [[Server Save]], so there is a higher chance nobody have gotten the reward before.
==== Required Equipment ====

* [[Shovel]]

* [[Rope]] or [[Magic Rope]]

==Method==

* Follow the road to [[Route:Mintwallin]].

* When you have come to [[Mintwallin]] you go south and up the stairs ([COORD x=32425 y=32136 z=15 | here]) to the '''throne room''' and you take the ladder slightly southwest from the stairs in there.

* When you have gone down the ladder ([COORD x=32435 y=32172 z=13 | here]) in to '''the pit''', you will face 1 [[dragon]] and when you go down the hole ([COORD x=32436 y=32189 z=14 | here]) you will face 2 [[dragon]]s.

* Browse the field on the fire located north of the room.
