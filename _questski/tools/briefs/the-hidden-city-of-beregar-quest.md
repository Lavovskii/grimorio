==Method==

*Required Equipment : [[Pick]].
*Reward : Access to the mines in [[Beregar]].
===For Going Down and Justice for All===
*Required Equipment : 3 [[Gear Wheel]]s or 3 [[Iron Ore]]s that can be traded with [[Sweaty Cyclops]] in Ab'dendriel for 3 [[Gear Wheels]]. (you have to start the mission Going Down first)
*Reward : [[Gold Ingot]], [[Small Topaz]]es and the ability to buy [[Rust Remover]].
===For Sweet as Chocolate Cake===
*Required Equipment : [[Chocolate Cake]].
*Reward : [[Crystal of Focus]].

===For Royal Rescue===
Required Equipment:
*6 [[Nail]]
*4 [[Wood]] (3 for "Repairs" part of quest, 1 to make [[Wooden Ties]])
*[[Saw]]
*[[Wooden Ties]] (obtainable by using [[Saw]] on [[Wood]])
*[[Firebug]]
*[[Hammer]]
*2 [[Metal Fitting]]s
*[[Bricklayers' Kit]]
*[[Rope]]
*[[Shovel]]
*A [[Melee]] weapon (in order to break a wall).
*[[Levitate]] spell
Reward : [[Dwarven Legs]]

===For Good Guard===
Required Equipment:
*[[Dwarven Ale]] (Can be purchased from [[Boozer]] in Venore for 3000 [[gp]]s, can only be bought during mission and 1 per person, it weighs 300[[oz]].)

*Reward : [[Rusted Armor]].

===For Pythius the Rotten===
Required Equipment:
* Level 100
* [[Golden Mug]]
*Reward : [[Firewalker Boots]].

==The Way to Beregar==
===Requirements===
*[[Pick]]
====Method====

*Find NPC [[Maris]] at the docks in the [[Foreigner Quarter]] ([COORD x=32650 y=31293 z=6 | here]).
*Ask [[Maris]] for a passage to [[Mistrock]].
*While still on [[Mistrock]], ask [[Maris]] about [[Mistrock]]. It's important to do, or you can't use the pick on the crack later (See Photo 2).
*Then ask [[Maris]] for a passage to [[Fenrock]].
*Enter the mountain, go down to level -2 with [[Tortoise]]s and [[Crocodile]]s. You will there find a crack in the stone floor in the south-eastern corner ([COORD x=32564 y=31338 z=9 | here]).
*Use your pick on it. (If you have spoken to [[Maris]] about [[Mistrock]] you will now be teleported down.)
*Follow the path down on which you won't encounter more than [[Skeleton]]s. (You will find an ore wagon which you can't use yet),[[Goblin]]s and [[Rotworm]]s until you see [[Beregar NPCs|an NPC]]. if you take a wrong turn down the path You may also face dangerous creatures there like [[Diabolic Imp]]s and [[Dragon Lord]]s. If you encounter them you'll know you've went wrong. 
<br />[[Image:The_Hidden_City_of_Beregar.jpg]]

*When you find the [[Dwarf]] [[NPC]] [[Nokmir]] go a little south you will find another [[Ramp (Stone)|ramp]], go up and you will find an ore wagon on the yellow ground, activate it by using it, now you will be able to use the ore wagon at the beginning. (See Photo 4) {This will save you a lot of time in the future.} 

*You may now proceed to speak to the NPCs on the main floor about missions.

==Going Down==
===Requirements===
*3 [[Gear Wheel]]s
====Method====
*Ask [[Xorlosh]] at the elevator ([COORD x=32611 y=31503 z=14 | here]) for a 'mission'.
*He will tell you to bring him [[Gear Wheel]]s. (These may be looted from [[Diamond Servant|Diamond]], [[Iron Servant|Iron]] and [[Golden Servant]]s or [[Worker Golem]]s, You may also obtain them by giving [[Iron Ore]] to the [[Sweaty Cyclops]].)
<br />
'''Note : You have to ask [[Xorlosh]] for the mission and finish [[The Sweaty Cyclops Quest]] before you can trade [[Iron Ore]]s for [[Gear Wheel]]s. You can only trade 3 per person. and only during the mission.'''
*Deliver the [[Gear Wheel]]s to [[Xorlosh]].
=====Reward=====
''Note : It is better if you take the reward ([[Gold Ingot]]) on your next mission, so you can save time and efforts by getting them at the same time. also, You can't get the ring until you take the "Justice for all" mission. '' ''''
*You will be able to use the elevator (teleport) to enter the eastern mine, (where you can find a [[Gold Ingot]] in a skeleton to the north-west in the mine). The lost [[Rerun's Ring]] that is needed for the '''Justice for All''' mission can be found in a crystal east of the skeleton (by using it). Both items are very close to each other. Be ready to face many [[Dwarf Soldier]]s, [[Dwarf Guard]]s and [[Dwarf Geomancer]]s in the mines (also note that [[Dwarf Geomancer|Geomancers]] drain a lot of mana)

==Justice for All==
===Method===
*Speak to [[Nokmir]] at the entrance to the northern mine.
*Say: '''Hi > Mission > Ring > Grombur > Rerun > Rehal.'''
*Go up one floor, to the floor with the ore wagon, and then up the three stairs (just keep going up until you found the Emperor at the north of the main room, Speak to [[Emperor Rehal]], saying : Hail Emperor > Nokmir > Grombur. Then leave him again and head to Grombur.
*Speak to Grombur at the entrance to the Southern mine, say : '''Hi > Nokmir > Rerun. '''
*Enter the eastern mine (go the teleport near [[Xorlosh]]), '''''you need to have completed the 'Going Down' before'''''.
*Find the beer cask in the north-western corner of the mine and click the crystals right next to it.
*You will find [[Rerun's Ring]]. To be sure, check your inventory and backpack. (Don't forget to take the [[Gold Ingot]] with you, which you can find in the skeleton to the west of the beer cask. It is not necessary for the quest, but as you already are beside it, it saves a long way).
[[Image:HiddenRing.jpg|400px|ring]]

*Bring the ring to the emperor, saying: '''Hail Emperor > Nokmir.'''
'''Note : You should start "The Royal Rescue" mission now to save time, by asking the emperor for a mission, so you won't have to do this trip twice.'''
*Speak to [[Nokmir]] again about the ''Mission''.
*[[Nokmir]] will now grant you access to the northern mine there you can find 3 [[Small Topaz]]es at the north of the mine.
<br />
[[Image:Hidden3Topazs.jpg|300px|3 [[Small Topaz]]es]]

'''You are now able to buy [[Rust Remover]]s from [[Harog]] ([COORD x=32592 y=31445 z=9 | here]).'''

==Sweet as Chocolate Cake==
===Requirements===
*1 [[Chocolate Cake]] (See: [[Baking#Baking Chocolate Cake|Baking a Chocolate Cake]]).

====Method====
*Ask [[Frafnar]] at the entrance to the western mine for a 'mission'.
*[[Baking|Bake]] a [[Chocolate Cake]] (use [[Flour]] on a [[Bucket]] of [[Milk]] to create [[Lump of Cake Dough]], use the [[Lump of Cake Dough|Dough]] on a [[Bar of Chocolate]] to get [[Lump of Chocolate Dough]] and bake it on an [[Oven]]).
*Go up the all the stairs in the main room, halfway to the emperor (west of the stairs which takes you to the Emperor) and find the bar. The door may be closed, just use the lever to open it, go down then south and you will find the bar.
[[File:THCOB_Tavern.png]]
*Speak to [[Bolfona]], say: '''Hi > Chocolate cake > Yes > Frafnar.'''
*Report back to [[Frafnar]] by telling him about the mission.
*[[Frafnar]] will now grant you access to the western mine, where you can find a [[Crystal of Focus]] in a treasure chest in a room to the north in the mine.

*'''''Please note that if you, for any given reason, say the wrong name to [[Bolfona]] and lose your chocolate cake, it doesn't mean the quest has ended, just when you speak to Frafnar for the second time (after giving the cake to Bolfona), he will ask you if you said it was from him, just lie and say yes.'''''

== The Royal Rescue ==
===Requirements===

*6 [[Nail]]s
*4 [[Wood]] (3 [[Wood]],and another 1 to make the [[Wooden Ties]])
*a [[Saw]]
*[[Wooden Ties]] (created after using a [[Saw]] on [[Wood]])
*a [[Firebug]]
*[[Hammer]]
*2 [[Metal Fitting]]s (buy-able during mission from [[Harog]])
*1 [[Bricklayers' Kit]]
*a [[Rope]]
*a [[Shovel]]
*A [[Melee]] weapon.
*Going Down mission completed (requires 3 [[Gear Wheel|Gear Wheels]])

====Method====
Go to [[Emperor Rehal]] inside Beregar and talk with him to start the quest.
*'''(if you already asked him before when you finished the "justice for all" mission so you don't have to talk to him again)'''

====The Book====
*After talking to the emperor head to the [[Xorlosh]] and ask him about the "tunnel", he will tell you to go to Beregar's library and find a [[Tunnelling Guide (Book)|book about tunnelling]]. The book is in the bookshelf second row from the bottom at the far end, here:<br />[[File:Royalrescue4.png|Library]]
'''The book contains all you need to know about repairing tunnels, if you forget what you need to do while on mission you can always read the instructions written in the book'''

===Repairs===
At this mission you will be doing many repairs, and after most you will use a wagon to progress to the next part. These wagons are one-way. If you try to go back you will be taken all the way back to the main hall of Beregar. However, this is not a problem. If you use the wagon in the main hall again, you will be taken back to the point you last completed. So, if you forget something and have to go back, it saves your progress.

====First Wagon====
*First you have to buy 2 [[Metal Fitting]]s from [[Harog]].
*For this part you must have 3 [[Wood]], 2 [[Metal Fitting]], 1 [[Wooden Ties]] and 6 [[Nail]]s
*The wagons are located in the main hall of Beregar ([COORD x=32581 y=31483 z=9 | here]).
[[File:THCOB_Inactive Wagons.png]]
*Use the bottom one to go to the deeper mines (you can find which way the wagon takes you by looking at the wall tags, this one's says "Deeper Mines")
*You will be taken into a room with a rail that's been divide by a gap that separates it.
*'''Make sure you have all the needed items in your inventory'''.
*Use your [[Hammer]] on the gap between the rails to lay down the [[Wood]].
*Then, use your [[Hammer]] a second time to lay down the rails,it should look like this:
[[File:THCOB_fix1.png]]
*Now use the wagon to get to the other side.

====Second Wagon====
*In this room, the tunnels arch has fallen, and now that nothing holds the tunnel gravel falls from above and blocks the rails
*Use your [[Bricklayers' Kit]] on the missing part of the wooden structure in order to hold the tunnel from collapsing.
*Then use your [[Pick]] on the rock that's blocking the rails to break it and remove it.
[[File:Brick here02.jpg]]
*You can now use the wagon.

====Third Wagon====
*To pass the third wagon you you must cross the bridge, since its been broken you have to walk back through the tunnel in the left and you will be teleported to the other side, go down the stairs.
[[File:1.jpg]]
*Now you fill fight some minor creatures like [[Gozzler]]s and [[Stone Golem]]s on the way to the broken bridge. After going down the staircase you go left following the north wall. Go down the hole, then head to the right following the north wall. When you reach the broken bridge go up the ladder and use the wagon.
[[File:Foto2.jpg]]<br />

====Inactive Wagon Room====

*You will find yourself in a room with some wagons lifted above the rails. You will have to fire the Big Oven to get the crane to drop a wagon for you.
*The crane needs steam power to be working, in order to do that you will need to fill it with Coal and fire it up:
* Push the empty ore wagon east until you reach the end of it's track where there are stacks of coals.
* Use your [[Shovel]] to fill the wagon up with coal.
[[File:THCOB coal.png]]
* Push the now filled ore wagon back to the crane.
[[File:THCOB coal2.png]]
* Use your shovel to unload the coal into the crane's engine.
* Use a [[Firebug]] to start the crane's engine, and start walking over the [[File:Bellow.gif]] [[Bellow]] with your feet over and over, to get the crane working.
'''Note that if you are doing it alone this must be done fast since the flame alone couldn’t sustain the engine without air'''<br />
[[File:THCOB_bellow.png]]
*Once the crane is working and you can see the steam you'll be able to use the lever that operates it and get an ore wagon on track.

'''Wagon destinations from left to right:'''
*First wagon: Takes you to a room with lava
*Second wagon: Takes you to the wagon maze (This the one you need to use in order to proceed with the quest)
[[File:THCOB_CW.png]]
*Third wagon: Takes you to a room with a small quest, you can get pick 10 [[Brown Mushroom]]s from a [[File:Glimmer_Cap_Mushrooms.gif]] [[Glimmer Cap Mushrooms|Witches' Cap Mushrooms]].
*Forth wagon: Takes you to a small tunnel with 4 [[Stone golem]]s

====Wagon Maze====
In this part you will have to use the different levers in order to change the railways so you can get to the different tunnels with the ore wagon. You can work your way back from the end of the tunnel to the beginning if you are not sure that you are doing it in the right order, here's a map that specify the locations:<br />
[[File:THCOB_WMM.png]]<br />

'''Note:''' You can optionally use the levers here in a different way in order to earn the [[File:Achievement Grade Symbol.gif|link=]] [[Unlikely Pathfinder]] secret achievement.

'''Tunnel's destinations from bottom left clockwise:'''
*The first tunnel to the bottom left takes you back to the room with the coal (by using the first ore wagon).
*The second tunnel takes you to a tavern infested with [[Spider]]s, [[Poison Spider]]s and 2 [[Giant Spider]]s behind webs.
*The third tunnel takes you back to [[Beregar]].
*In this Maze you have to use the levers in order to get the wagon to the north tunnel in the center. The center tunnel leads to [[Tehlim]], Use the north ore wagon at the beginning in the room, Once you enter the center tunnel, head north (if you go east there are some [[Fire Elemental]]s and [[Fire Devil]]s as well as single [[Furious Troll]]s on the way), go up the stairs and you'll meet [[Tehlim]], tell him about your ''mission'', he'll tell you that his brother was captured by trolls and held captive.
[[File:THCOB_1M.png]]<br />

*The Right tunnel takes you to the trolls hideout
*After you talked to Tehlim go back to the maze room again by using the ore wagon to enter the room.
*Use the levers in order to go the right tunnel which takes you to the troll's hideout.
[[File:335.jpg]]<br />

===The troll tribe===
Around the area many spots on the floor have traps, they will summon 2 to 4 [[Mechanical Fighter]]s when you step on them. The traps are unavoidable, as they are rows of invisible switches that will trigger the summons.
*There you'll find some [[Furious Troll]]s, after killing them go up the stairs and find your way through them to get to a teleport.
*After going into the teleport you will appear one floor up. Now follow the first two steps in the below picture to find a [[Chest (Quest)]] from which to take the [[Prison Cell Key]].
[[File:987.jpg|380px]][[File:Key.jpg|297px]]
*Go back to the portal and now follow steps 3-6 of the above picture. You will encounter many [[Troll Legionnaire]]s, [[Furious Troll]]s, [[Mechanical Fighter]]s and at times [[Lavahole]]s shooting at you, so don't dawdle. It is recommended to wear a [[Dragon Necklace]] to reduce the fire damage.
*On the bridge you will meet some [[Furious Troll]]s and [[Troll Legionnaire]]s, some of them will be invisible. They block your path till you kill them. If you're a mage it could be a good idea to summon one [[Demon Skeleton]] so he absorbs the fire from the lava holes while you shoot some area runes to kill the trolls. The damage from the lava holes is significant.
*Go down the hole and take the right passage until you get to a jail with a berserker in it. One part of the wooden rail is weak, break it and use levitate to get down.
*Kill the berserker and go through the passage killing trolls and Mechanical Fighters until you get to the jail, there talk to the NPC asking for mission and give him the key.
*Now go back to the emperor to claim your reward ([[Dwarven Legs]]).

==The Good Guard==
===Requirements===
*[[The Ultimate Booze Quest]] Completed.
*[[Dwarven Ale]] (Can be purchased from [[Boozer]] in Venore for 3000 [[gp]]s).
'''Note:''' You can't buy the Dwarven Ale before accepting the mission, and you can only buy 1 per person.

====Method====
You can leave this mission as last one as you have to travel to Venore to buy the dwarven ale. You need to have at least 300 cap for [[Dwarven Ale]].
*Ask Grombur at the entrance to the southern mine for a 'mission'. He will tell you he needs [[Dwarven Ale]].
*Go to Boozer in Venore and ask for 'Dwarven Brown Ale' (or just say 'mission').
*If you haven't done the [[The Ultimate Booze Quest]], you have to do it first (say mission to start it).
*Buy the [[Dwarven Ale]] from [[Boozer]] for 3,000 [[gp]]s.
*Deliver the cask of ale to Grombur by saying 'mission'.
*You will find a [[Rusted Armor]] in a skeleton in the farthest north branch of the -2 level from Grombur.

==Pythius the Rotten==
===Requirements===

* Level 100
* [[Golden Mug]]

====Method====
Remember: You need [[level]] 100 to open [[Pythius The Rotten (Creature)|Pythius the Rotten]] chamber. If you are under level 100, you will be able to enter the dragon lair but not to open then the boss chamber. (When you use your pick on the stone nothing will happen).

You can obtain [[Firewalker Boots]] by defeating the gold-crazy [[Pythius The Rotten]].

[[File:Fenrock Dragon Lair 1.png|600px]]

# Start at the Ore Wagon.
# Head South and go down the stairs.
# Head south-east and take the stairs down. Up leads to some [[Fire Elemental]]s.
# Go west and take the stairs up and to the south.
# Go up the stairs and you'll meet two [[Elder Bonelord]]s.
# Take the stairs down to the west.
# Walk straight west and take first stairs south. At the eastern end of this passage are stairs down to a pair of [[Giant Spider]]s.
# Continue directly south and up the stairs at the south end of the cave. There are just a few [[Skeleton]]s in this cave.
# Go up 2 levels. You will encounter [[Goblin]]s. Continue east and go down the stairs.
# Continue east and north as the passage curves. More [[Goblin]]s will be found here. Take the stairs up at the end of the passage. 
# You will now encounter a number of [[Dragon]]s and [[Dragon Hatchling]]s. The passage will curve away to the west. Follow it until the end and go down the stairs.
# Continue down 2 sets of stairs. At the bottom will be a chamber with a teleport to the north. Pythius can be seen at the west end of the chamber guarding his chamber of treasures. He does not attack.

[[Image:Pythias_the_Rotten.jpg]]

Talk to Pythius. He doesn't do anything unless you pay him. Offer the greedy bonebeast a golden mug for his horde. He will grant access to the teleport to the north of the chamber for a few minutes. Go through the teleport and down two levels. Here you will encounter [[Dragon Lord]]s and [[Dragon Lord Hatchling]]s. Head west and follow the yellow path until you find a rock in the corner of the last chamber to the south-west.

[[Image:Dragon_Lord_Cave.jpg]]

Use your pick on the stone to uncover a teleport which will take you to the final chamber.

[[Image:Dragon_Lord_Cave_Entrance_Stone.jpg]]

The teleport is opened ....

[[Image:Portal_open.jpg]]

You are transported to a large chamber. An escape teleport beckons at the west side of the chamber near you but you launch into the fight ...

'''Note: Be careful because Pythius The Rotten may curse you.'''

[[Image:Battling_Pythias.jpg]]

After defeating the greedy old bonebag (he doesn’t die, the hp just goes black and yells: "Fighting little worm, your victory shall be rewarded) you are teleported to the reward room
