Failed: {{Server Message|As soon as you reach out for the stunned rabbit it comes to its senses and flees.}}<br />

Now, make a {{Ilink|Large Snowball}}[[Large Snowball]] by using one regular [[Snowball]] on another one that is on the ground. You can obtain infinite [[Snowball]]s from {{Ilink|Snow Heap}}[[Snow Heap]]s around the island.

If you try to create it before the cooldown is over, you'll get the following message: {{Server Message|You're still exhausted from building your previous snowman.}} If you try to do it somewhere else, you'll get the following message: {{Server Message|Nothing happens.}} Just like taking the frozen carrot, this step may also fail.

Failed: {{Sound|A gust of wind blows your snowman away. Unlucky!}}<br />

* Walk to the south-west and enter the teleport [COORD x=33752 y=31083 z=9 | here].

'''Note''': The disguise grants you access to the lever room. If you didn't fight the [[The Percht Queen]] (succeeded or failed) you do not need a new disguise.

== The Percht Queen ==

You can fight the queen with up to 5 players, once every 20 hours. The fight is very easy and there is pretty much no danger of dying, a team of players around level 50 should be enough to do it. 

In order to defeat the Percht Queen, you must first unfreeze it. To do so, you'll have to do the following steps:

[[File:Winterlight Solstice Queen 1.png|thumb]]
1. As soon as you pull the lever, you'll be in a small room with a [[Warm Bonfire]] {{Ilink|Warm Bonfire}} and [[Vortex of Nature]] {{Ilink|Vortex of Nature}}, [COORD x=33698 y=31056 z=8 | here]. Use the bonfire and a [[Percht Warding Torch]] {{Ilink|Percht Warding Torch}} will appear in front of it, pick it up. 

2. Step on the Vortex and your appearance will change to a [[Leaf Golem]].

[[File:Winterlight Solstice Queen 2.png|thumb]]
3. Go down, and then quickly go all the way to the other side of the dungeon, [COORD x=33721 y=31056 z=9 | here]. '''Do not go north'''. When you step near the wall, a [[Rush Wood|Rush Wood (Quest)]] {{Ilink|Rush Wood}} (tree) will appear. Use it 5 times, each time a [[Branch (Quest)|Branch]] {{Ilink|Branch}} will appear in front of the tree, pick them all up.

[[File:Winterlight Solstice Queen 3.png|thumb]]
4. Go to the south where there will be a [[Burn Out Bonfire]] {{Ilink|Burn Out Bonfire}}, [COORD x=33711 y=31067 z=9 | here]. If it's not lit, use your torch on it and light it up, so it changes into a [[Smoldering Bonfire]] {{Ilink|Smoldering Bonfire}}. Start using the wood on the bonfire. 

5. After using all the wood, go back to the west side, transform yourself into the Leaf Golem and repeat this process until the bonfire is so big that it's a [[Blazing Bonfire]] {{Ilink|Blazing Bonfire}} and a [[Warm Fire]] {{Ilink|Warm Fire}} appears (20 [[Branch (Quest)|Branches]] are necessary). 

[[File:Winterlight Solstice Queen 4.png|thumb]]
6. When the Warm Fire appears, the bonfire will stop burning, use a torch on it again (the torch only lasts for 40 seconds, so you'll also need to constantly pick up a new one). Then, use the bonfire and you'll start burning yourself {{Ilink|Burning Man}}.

7. Lure the [[Warm Fire]] to the north, but be careful because the [[Percht Minion]]s will try to kill it. Also avoid the Ice beams that come from the north of the room. The [[Warm Fire]] will explode and deal damage to [[The Percht Queen]]. 

Repeat the process above until [[The Percht Queen]] is not frozen anymore (required 4 [[Warm Fire]]s) and can be killed by the players. 

'''Important notes''':

[[File:Winterlight Solstice Queen 5.png|thumb]]
* You can use creatures that don't walk over Fire to draw the attention of the [[Percht Minion]]s. Two creatures between the stairs and the center are enough. This can also be done by Paladins and Knights using [[Animate Dead Rune]]s (bring yourself a corpse).
* Never walk to the north of the room if you're not transformed into a burning man. You'll instantly "freeze" and be teleported out of the battle, with no way to come back. 
* If you don't go near the bonfire to the west every few minutes, you'll start feeling cold ({{Server Message|You are cold.}}). If this message appears, quickly go west and walk near the bonfire to warm yourself. 
* In order to receive loot, the player must deal Elemental damage to [[The Percht Queen]]. Knights should use weapons with elemental damage or runes such as [[Great Fireball Rune]]s, even if the damage is low it's enough to get the reward. 

== Rewards ==

After the Winterlight Solstice ends, all worlds receive a week of increased respawn rates (2x) while mana & health points regenerate twice as fast. This reward applies to all worlds, as it's not possible to "fail" the event.

{{Server Message|The Percht Queen has been defeated, rejoice in the majesty of light and let its renewed grace heal your body and soul!}}

=== Percht Raider Outfits ===
* After defeating [[The Percht Queen]] for the first time, you'll receive the [[Percht Raider Outfits]].
* You'll receive the first addon of the outfit after participating in the event 2 times (2 years).
* You'll receive the second addon of the outfit and the achievement [[Raider in the Dark]] after participating in the event 3 times (3 years).

=== Sleigh Mounts ===
* After participating in the event once, you can deliver 3 [[Percht Skull]]s {{Ilink|Percht Skull}} to get the [[Dark Percht Sleigh]], [[Bright Percht Sleigh]], and [[Cold Percht Sleigh]] mounts.
* By participating twice, you can upgrade these mounts to the [[Dark Percht Sleigh Variant]], [[Bright Percht Sleigh Variant]], and [[Cold Percht Sleigh Variant]] versions by delivering one [[Percht Skull]] for each.
* After participating three times, you'll gain access to the final mount upgrades: [[Finished Dark Percht Sleigh]], [[Finished Bright Percht Sleigh]], and [[Finished Cold Percht Sleigh]], by handing in one [[Percht Skull]] per mount.
