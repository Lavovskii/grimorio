## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=The Isle of Evil Quest}}

=== Required Equipment ===
* [[Rope]] [or equivalent];
* [[Shovel]] [or equivalent];
*10 [[Vial]]s of [[Rum]];
*1 [[Vial]] of [[Fruit Juice]];
*3 to 30 [[Nail]]s or [[Mechanical Fish]];
*Access to [[Yalahar]];
*Travel money.

=== Method ===
*Visit [[Rapanaio]] in [[Kazordoon]] (one floor above the [[Steamship]] ([COORD x=32683 y=31987 z=14 | here]) and ask him about the '''Isle of Evil'''. He will give you access to the room to the west, where you can find a [[Box (Quest)]] containing [[Isle of Evil (Book)]]. Obtaining it is optional.
[[File:IoE_paper.png]]

====  Mission 1: Just A Fishing Trip ====
*Ask [[Rapanaio]] for your first '''mission''' and he will give you a [[Mechanical Fishing Rod]]. He wants you to fish a [[Mechanical Fish]] from the sewers of [[Yalahar]], a fish you can only catch by using [[Nail]]s as bait. Note that you can give him a [[Mechanical Fish]] bought in the [[The Market (Object)|Market]].
[[File:IoE_rod.png]]
*Go to the sewers of [[Yalahar]] carrying the rod and bait and start fishing like you normally would. It's a good idea to bring plenty of [[Nail]]s, as they can disappear in unsuccessful fishing attempts. If this happens, you will get harmed for 35 [[energy]] damage and turn into a [[Skeleton]] for some seconds. Keep fishing until you're successful, return to [[Rapanaio]] with your catch, and ask for your next mission.
[[File:IoE_2.png]]

==== Mission 2: A Potent Fuel ====
*[[Rapanaio]] wants you to bring him 10 [[Vial]]s of [[Rum]]. The closest [[Rum Cask]] is located in the tavern of [[Liberty Bay]] ([COORD x=32298 y=32831 z=7 | here]), near which you can also buy the vials from [[Red Lilly]] (it's a good idea to buy an extra vial in preparation for the next mission). Hand them in and ask for your next mission.

==== Mission 3: Something Sweet ====
*[[Rapanaio]] is now in need of a [[Vial]] of [[Fruit Juice]]. This is obtained by using a [[Juice Squeezer]] on any piece of fruit while carrying a vial. Hand it in and ask for your next mission.

==== Mission 4: The Technomancer's Machine ====
*The next thing [[Rapanaio]] needs is a special machine. Go to [[Scutty]] ([COORD x=32576 y=31916 z=12 | here]) and ask him about the '''machine'''. He will ask for your help and hand you a [[Worm Punisher]]. Go through the door on the south.
*Use the [[Worm Punisher]] on the [[Crucible]] while it's red to turn it into a [[Heated Worm Punisher]] for ten seconds. You can [[use]] it on the [[Wormhole (Carrion Worm)|Carrion Worm]]s to hit them when they appear (like in a game of Whack-a-Mole). You will need to hit 6 Carrion Worms in order to finish the task and earn the achievement [[Worm Whacker]].
**'''Note''': If you hit a drilling worm (appears as a [[Wormhole (Rotworm)|Rotworm]]) rather than a [[Wormhole (Carrion Worm)|Carrion Worm]], you'll need to pay [[Scutty]] a fee of 500 [[gp]] and restart the task completely.
[[File:IoE_3.png]] [[File:IoE_4.PNG]]
*Report back to [[Scutty]] and he will give you a [[Machine Crate]]. Bring it to [[Rapanaio]] and ask for your next mission.

==== Mission 5: The Map to Evil ====
*[[Rapanaio]] won't be able to arrive at the island safely without some sort of navigational aid. Go to [[Mortimer]] in [[Northport]] ([COORD x=32500 y=31626 z=7 | here]) and ask for a '''nautical map'''.
*Visit the beach to the east and dig [COORD x=32531 y=31628 z=7 | right here] between the two stones to reveal the entrance to [[The Cave]].
[[File:ScreenShot1047.jpg|300px]]
*Enter it and drop down another level. Move to the northeastern room and go down the ladder. Now head north to find a [[Box (Brown Quest)]] containing a [[Nautical Map]] ([COORD x=32576 y=31572 z=10 | here]).
*Open the box and bring the map to [[Rapanaio]] before asking for another mission.

==== Mission 6: The Secret ====
*[[Rapanaio]] wants you to meet him on his ship. Reach it by going down the stairs behind the lvl 35 [[Gate of Expertise]] to the south ([COORD x=32681 y=31994 z=14 | here]). 
*Greet Rapanaio and he will tell you that he is ready to go when you are. Ask for your next mission once you've gathered the supplies you need to fight evil.

==== Mission 7: The Master of Evil ====
*Fight your way to the stairs [COORD x=32741 y=31492 z=7 | here] and go up. Approach the castle with caution and do '''not''' pull the lever, as it will cause you to drop down a level and land amongst some [[Infernal Frog]]s.
*Once you're inside the castle, there are six bosses to kill (one per floor). These are (in order) [[Doctor Perhaps]], [[Dirtbeard]], [[Mephiles]], [[Boogey]], [[Monstor]], and [[Evil Mastermind]]. Slaying them will grant you the right to [[use]] their corpses and obtain a unique item. Using the corpse of the final boss makes you unable to use any of the other bosses' corpses, so make sure to use those first. Take your time and don't get trapped. 
*After you've slain all six bosses, use the corpse of [[Evil Mastermind]] to obtain a [[Fan Club Membership Card]]. Mention it to [[Rapanaio]] and he'll tell you to show it to [[King Tibianus]].
*Go to [[King Tibianus]] and show him the card. He will reward you with 6666 [[Experience Point]]s and a [[Fan Doll of King Tibianus]]. You will also receive the [[achievement]] [[King Tibianus Fan]].
