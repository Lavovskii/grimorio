## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Captain Iglues Treasure Quest}}

==Required Equipment==
* [[Rope]]
* Recommend two [[Antidote Potion]]s for [[Poison Gas]]es, or enough [[Small Health Potion]]s to withstand the [[Poisoned|poison]]

==Method==
'''Note:''' There are many ways to reach this quest (the caves under [[Rookgaard]] are almost all connected somehow). This spoiler will show the most direct route, not necessarily the safest.

* Go to the poison spider tower in northern [[Rookgaard]] ([COORD x=32058 y=32091 z=7 | here]), and down one level.
* Go to the south, and down the first hole you come along. Then go up at the spot east of you and head north to the next hole:
[[File:Captain Iglues Treasure Quest Map 2.png]]
* On this floor are many [[Poison Spider]]s along with 2 [[Poison Gas]]es. Follow the way to the north-west walking through the Poison Gases. Using an [[Antidote Potion]] is recommended, but [[Small Health Potion]]s are more economical:
[[File:Captain Iglues Treasure Quest Map 3.png]]
* Go down another level, here you will find 3 Poison Spiders:
[[File:Captain Iglues Treasure Quest Map 4.png]]
* Follow this passage and then go down a level. There are no creatures here:
[[File:Captain Iglues Treasure Quest Map 5.png]]
* You will now be in an area with stone walls, go down the [[Stairs (White)|stairs]]. Watch out for [[Skeleton]]s, you will find two chests:
[[File:Captain Iglues Treasure Quest Map 6.png]]
* These two chests are guarded by two Skeletons. The left chest contains 12 [[Salmon]] and a [[Stamped Letter]] but is a [[Daily Respawns|daily spawn]]. The right chest is a [[Chest (Quest)]] and contains 2 Salmon:
[[File:Captain Iglues quest rewards.png]]

'''Note:''' You may take the Salmon to [[Amber]] in the [[Rookgaard Academy]], she will trade it for some information about the [[Orc Language]].
