## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Dream Warrior Outfits Quest}}

== Base Outfits ==

Finish [[The Dream Courts Quest]] and report about your '''task''' to either [[Vanys]] at the [[Court of Summer]] ([COORD x=33691 y=32184 z=8 | here]) or [[Undal]] at the [[Court of Winter]] ([COORD x=33710 y=32113 z=4 | here]).

== First Addon ==

Ask for an ''addon'' and deliver 5 [[Pomegranate]]s to either [[Vanys]] or [[Undal]].

== Second Addon == 

Ask for an ''addon'' and deliver an [[Ice Shield]] to either [[Vanys]] or [[Undal]].
