== Required Equipment ==
* [[Rope]]

== Method ==
* Travel to [[Mount Sternum]] and find a hole in the centre of the mountain, and go down the staircase [COORD x=32441 y=32008 z=13 | here].

* Follow this path past the [[Graveyard of the Doomed]] to the west and north and go down a level:<br />[[Image:Geomancer Quest Map 01.jpg]]

* Head directly north west and go down the hole:<br />[[Image:Geomancer Quest Map 02.jpg]]

* Move north and take first west and go down the hole:<br />[[Image:Geomancer Quest Map 03.jpg]]

* Go east and down a hole:<br />[[Image:Geomancer Quest Map 04.jpg]]

* Here you will face [[Mummy|Mummies]], [[Crypt Shambler]]s and [[Ghost]]s. Kill them and head east, and go down one more level:<br />[[Image:Geomancer Quest Map 05.jpg]]

* The quest box is on the east side of this room, behind a couple [[Dwarf Guard]]s, and a [[Dwarf Geomancer]].
