First of all, you have to go to the Edron Hero Cave, which is here:
 
[[File:TripleUHRune2.jpg]]

When you enter to the undergrounds, you have to kill some wild warriors.
Go to the next hole.

[[File:TripleUHRune3.jpg]]

Now you have to kill some Demon Skeletons, Wild Warriors, Prietesses and Bonelords. Go with that way, and go downstairs:

[[File:TripleUHRune4.jpg]]

Now you have to go with that way, killing some Monks, Demon Skeletons and Wild Warriors. Go downstairs:

[[File:TripleUHRune5.jpg]]

Kill the 3 [[Dragon]]s and 2 [[Dragon Hatchling]]s and enter that hole:

[[File:TripleUHRune6.jpg]]

Now you have to kill one [[Hero]] and two [[Monk (Creature)|Monk]]s. Go with that way and again, go downstairs:

[[File:TripleUHRune7.jpg]]

Now you have to kill some Hunters, Demon Skeletons and Prietesses. Go downstairs. 

[[File:TripleUHRune8.jpg]]

This is the last floor, it containts 5 [[Monk (Creature)|Monk]]s:

[[File:TripleUHRune9.jpg]]

The [[Silver Rune Emblem (Ultimate Healing)|Silver Rune Emblem]] is in the box.
