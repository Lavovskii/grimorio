## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{spoiler| name=Time Ring Quest}}
==Required equipment==
*[[Machete]]

==Method==
*Find your way to [[Shadowthorn]] and enter the hole [COORD x=33060 y=32182 z=7 | here].
*When you come down, head west.
[[Image:Time_Ring_Quest_Map.gif]]
*Down the ramp you will find 4 [[Elf Arcanist]]s, at least 4 [[Elf Scout]]s, two [[Ghoul]]s and one [[Demon Skeleton]].
*Occasionally [[Dharalion]] spawns in the area.
*The three quest boxes are located in the north end of this room.

'''Suggestion.'''
Get a high level [[knight]] with enough hitpoints and [[Health Potions]] to run the creatures around while [[paladin]]s can shoot [[bolt]]s and [[Heavy Magic Missile|HMMs]] runes at [[Demon Skeleton]]s, while [[mage]]s can fire [[Sudden Death Rune|SDs]] at the [[Elf Arcanist]]s.

If you are a mage or a paladin, you should stand at the ramp and lure the monsters slowly because it is a big room and the monsters are spread out.
