=== Shoulder Spike ===
[[Image:Outfit Warrior Male Addon 1.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit Warrior Female Addon 1.gif]]

==== Required Equipment ====
* 100 [[Hardened Bone]]s
* 100 [[Turtle Shell]]s
* 1 [[Spirit Container]]
* 1 [[Dragon Claw]]

==== Method ====
* All of the items listed above can be obtained before you start the quest. You are advised to collect the items before you speak to the NPC
** Collect the [[Hardened Bone]]s from [[Bonebeast]]s;
** Collect the [[Turtle Shell]]s from [[Tortoise]]s;
** Collect a [[Dragon Claw]] from [[Demodras]];
** Exchange 2 [[Royal Helmet]]s for a [[Spirit Container|Fighting Spirit]] in Djinn fortress or collect [[Spirit Container]]s from [[Souleater]]s.
* Once you have the items, go to the Knight's Guild in [[Carlin]] and talk to [[Trisha]].

=== Sword ===
[[Image:Outfit Warrior Male Addon 2.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit Warrior Female Addon 2.gif]]

==== Required Equipment ====
* 100 [[Iron Ore]]
* 1 [[Piece of Royal Steel]]

==== Method ====
* All of the items listed above can be obtained before you start the quest. You are advised to collect the items before you speak to the NPC
** Collect [[Iron Ore]] from [[Dwarves]], [[Worker Golem]]s, or [[War Golem]]s.
** Obtain [[Piece of Royal Steel]] by trading a [[Crown Armor]] to [[A Sweaty Cyclops]] in [[Ab'Dendriel]] ([[The Sweaty Cyclops Quest]]), note however that you must first give him 3 [[Bast Skirt]]s. The [[Piece of Royal Steel]] is called "Uth'Kean".
* Once you have the items, speak to [[Morgan]] in [[Sabrehaven]] (on the island of [[Meriana]]).
* Ask him about "weapons", and tell him you want the "warrior's sword".
* Give him the items and he will forge the sword addon for you.
