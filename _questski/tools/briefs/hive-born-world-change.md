## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Hive Born World Change}}

If enough actions against the [[Hive Born]] are taken, its defenses may be breached and access to the inner Hive will be opened. In order to change the Hive stage, tasks of the [[War Against the Hive Quest]] must be completed. This World Change have three possible stages which change at the [[Server Save]] of the [[Game Worlds|Game World]]:

== Stage 1 ==

This is the default stage and the Hive will remain this way unless actions against it are taken. There are 4 different [[War Against The Hive Quest#First Stage|tasks]] which can be done, each one being worth one point. The server needs 200 total points in a single day to advance to the second stage. 

Players can check the current stage by asking [[Guide NPCs]] about the '''hive''' or [[Orockle]] about '''status''':
{{Infobox Transcript|
Player: hive<br/>
Guide NPC: The hive is well defended and prepared for war. ...<br/>
Guide NPC: ''X'' actions have been taken against the Hive Born. 200 actions are necessary to advance further into the hive.
}}

== Stage 2 ==

During the second stage, the [COORD x=33588 y=31235 z=7 | eastern part] of the inner Hive becomes accessible. During this stage there are two other [[War Against The Hive Quest#Second Stage|tasks]] that can be completed and are worth 2 points each. In order to advance to the next and final stage, another 200 actions are necessary. If this is not done after 3 days, the Hive will return to the first stage. 

At this stage it's also possible to enter some reward rooms to obtain [[Gooey Mass]]es from [[Insectoid Cell]]s. Note that in order to enter the rooms you need to speak to [[Orockle]] (''reward'', ''yes'') and spend 50 Favour Points of the quest (gained through completing the tasks). Orockle will use a special pheromone on you that allows you to go through the [[Hive Gate]]s on all floors for 7 days. 
{{Infobox Transcript|
Player: hive<br/>
Guide NPC: The defences of the hive are breached. The hive structure to the east is open. ...<br/>
Guide NPC: ''X'' actions have been taken against the Hive Born. 200 actions are necessary to advance further into the hive.
}}

Or if the number of actions taken is at least 200:
{{Infobox Transcript|
Guide NPC: The defences of the hive are breached. The hive structure to the east is open. ...<br/>
Guide NPC: ''X'' actions have been taken against the Hive Born. Enough to advance even further into the hive.
}}

== Stage 3 ==

During this stage, the Hive is completely exposed and the western side of the inner Hive is also accessible. If you have the pheromone from Orockle, you'll have access to other reward rooms with [[Gooey Mass]]es as well as the room of the [[Insectoid Outfits Quest]]. The western side is also home of the [[Hive Born Bosses]] [[Chopper]], [[Fleshslicer]], [[Maw]], [[Mindmasher]], [[Rotspit]] and [[Shadowstalker]] which may drop [[Dung Ball (Quest)|special Dung Balls]] worth 10 War Exp. of the [[War Against the Hive Quest]] each. Finally, [[Ladybug]]s may also be found on the ground floor of the Hive during this stage.

'''This stage lasts 5 days''', after which it reverts to stage 1. If at least 200 actions were taken during the final day of stage 3, it will revert to stage 2 instead. 
{{Infobox Transcript|
Player: hive<br/>
Guide NPC: The hives defences have fallen. Its armies are confused and in shambles. All structures are open for invaders. ...<br/>
Guide NPC: ''X'' actions have been taken against the Hive Born. This position will hold for a while.
}}

On the last day of stage 3:
{{Infobox Transcript|
Player: hive<br/>
Guide NPC: The hives defences have fallen. Its armies are confused and in shambles. All structures are open for invaders. ...<br/>
Guide NPC: ''X'' actions have been taken against the Hive Born. It is impossible to hold this position any longer, however.
}}
