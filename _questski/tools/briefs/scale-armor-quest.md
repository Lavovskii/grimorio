## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=Scale Armor Quest}}
==Required Equipment==
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Rope]]
* Recommended [[Antidote]], [[Antidote Rune]] or [[Silver Amulet]] for facing [[Scorpion]]

==Method==
* Enter the cave that is directly west of the [[Ancient Temple]] entrance ([COORD x=32337 y=32172 z=7 | here]). You will need to use a [[Shovel]] to open the hole.

* Follow this path to the north and east until you find the room with a well in it:
[[Image:Scale Armor Quest Map.jpg]]

* "Use" the lower-right corner of the well to go down into it. Be prepared to face one or more [[Scorpion]]s.

* Open the chests to receive the [[Scale Armor]]
