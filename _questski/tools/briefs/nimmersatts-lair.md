## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name = Nimmersatt's Lair}}

At the north of [[Carlin]], [COORD x=32259 y=31642 z=7 | here], [[Rascalio]] will bring players to Nimmersatt's Lair when prompted with {{KW|hi}}, {{KW|passage}}, {{KW|yes}}.

The place only has [[Dragon Bride]]s, which have the visual form of [[Dragon]]s, [[Dragon Lord]]s, and [[Frost Dragon]]s, but have none of the lethality. Each of the brides have different abilities though, that they use in area around themselves and can afflict [[Feared|fear]], [[Drunk|drunkenness]], and [[Rooted|frozen state]].

The goal of the raid is to pick up [[Snowball]]s at the north of the dungeon and throw them at the [[Lava Wall]] near the entrance. Each Snowball does some damage to the wall and the raid is finished once it is brought down.
