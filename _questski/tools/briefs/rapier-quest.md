==Required equipment==
*None, a [[Shields|shield]] is recommended.

==Method==
*Go into the [[Rookgaard]] sewer ([COORD x=32094 y=32204 z=7 | here]).
*After entering, make your way west, and go down a [[Ladder]], you will encounter a few rats on your way
*Once you go down, you will encounter 3-7 rats, which you can kill or simply run past through
*The quest-box is in the north-east part of the room, use it to obtain a [[Rapier]].<br /><gallery mode="nolines">
