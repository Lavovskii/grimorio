## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=The Annihilator Quest}}

=== Requirements ===
* This quest needs a team of '''four players'''. It mandates a full team of four individuals who have not previously completed this quest.

=== Preparation ===
It is recommended that you be over [[Level]] 130 to succeed. You cannot leave the Annihilation room until you clear a path blocked by two [[Angry Demon]]s. You can only enter the quest room in a team of 4 players who have not previously completed the quest. Be prepared to have a huge supply of potions as well as other aids like several [[Might Ring]]s and a few [[Necklace of the Deep|Necklaces of the Deep]]. The blocker may consider using [[Stone Skin Amulet]]s.

A [[Level]] 100 mage can now complete this [[quest]] without having an insane amount of luck. A full magma set, a few [[Elven Amulet]]s, maybe 5-10 (they last much longer than one would think), and about one backpack of might rings, in use with great mana potions, will allow any mage to gain more [[mana]] than is being drained by fire and energy damage. The key is to kill the first [[Angry Demon]] as fast as possible. The difference between 6 and 5 demons is very important and much greater than one may think. It is also best if this mage goes with other mages and perhaps one [[knight]] to block, because a team of all knights and one mage would be too slow for a mage to even cap the amount of great mana potions s/he would need.

=== Starting the Quest ===
Depending on how long the [[Game Worlds|world]] has been around relates to the amount of other level 100's. It's good if each player is capable of handling a [[Demon]] on their own.

The quest is located in the central cave of north [[Edron]], the [[Hero Cave]]. First encounter is [[Hunter]]s outside the cave. Three levels down of [[Wild Warrior]]s, [[Demon Skeleton]]s and [[Priestess]]es. The fourth level has 3 [[Dragon]]s. Fifth has 2 [[Monk (Creature)|Monk]]s and a [[Hero]]. Sixth floor first corridor contains an assortment of the [[Demon Skeleton]]s, [[Wild Warrior]]s, [[Priestess]]es, [[Monk (Creature)|Monk]]s, and [[Minotaur Guard]]s. Take a right at the bottom, encounter the last three [[Creatures]] named at the north and a [[Hero]] spawn south. Go on to the next passages that goes south, across from the [[Fire Elemental]] room. [[Hunter]]s and such are here, and at the end is an [[Gate of Expertise|level 100 gate of expertise]] and a [[Door (Sealed)|quest door]] that leads to the [[Portal|teleport room]] [COORD x=33211 y=31672 z=13 | here].

The four [[level]] 100's line up on the designated squares and the foremost pulls the lever. You are sent to a nearby room with six [[Angry Demon]]s. Two north, two south and two dead ahead blocking the door. They will attack until dead and once they are dead and you succeed, you can enter the [[door]] to the reward.

=== Directions and Tips ===

== The Setup ==

[[File:Annihilator Quest 1.jpg|700px]]

The four members of the team must each stand on the four tiles. Once all four members are in place, the lever must be pushed in order to teleport the entire team instantaneously to the dungeon with [[Angry Demon]]s. Remember, the blocker or highest level should be farthest to the right.

== The Dungeon ==

[[File:Annihilator Quest 2.jpg]]

Upon entering you will see 6 [[Angry Demon]]s. Attack only the two staying between the team and the door that leads to the reward room to the east. Make sure the entire team attacks the first of the two demons at the same time in order to kill it as fast as possible. Also Mages below Level 130 are recommended to use [[Blessed Steak]] to give a extra full mana bar when the first one is gone to greatly increase the survival rate. Also not a bad idea for Mages is to get close to leveling before entering the Dungeon (within a few exp from leveling) so when the first demon dies you will level up and receive another full mana bar.

== Leader Tip ==

[[File:Annihilator Quest 3.jpg]]

The leader, or head member, can put the second demon on follow, so that as soon as the first demon is killed, they will move forward one map field. This will reduce the amount of potential Great Energy Beams used in a combo on this player, greatly increasing their chances of survival. With this trick they will have a smaller chance to get blocked by the [[Fire Elemental|Demons' summons]].

== Quick Escape ==

[[File:Annihilator Quest 4.jpg]]

Once the two demons are dead, immediately run east through the door which will not be locked. Close the door behind you so that stray Fire Elementals and great fireball attacks do not make it through the door.<br />
'''Note:''' If there are people still inside the room 10 minutes after the lever was pulled, they will be moved to the reward room, regardless if the angry demons blocking the way are still alive or dead.

== Final Departure ==

[[File:Annihilator Quest 5.jpg]]

After choosing your reward, go through the teleport that is east of the reward choices. This will teleport you in front of the level 100 door at the beginning of the quest.

=== The treasure room ===
You can only enter this door once, so choose your reward wisely.<br />
*[[Demon Armor]] (Arm:16) (The best armor without [[Vocation]] restrictions)
*[[Magic Sword]] (Atk:48 Def:35 +3) ''It's the Sword of Valor.''
*[[Stonecutter's Axe]] (Atk:50 Def:30 +3) ''You feel the power of this Mighty Axe.''
*[[Annihilation Bear]] '' I braved the Annihilator and all I got is this lousy teddy bear.''

=== Notes ===
*Attacks from one [[Angry Demon]] can take from 20 to 450 [[hitpoints]] per turn. Six of them at any time could attack with [[Great Fireball|GFB]] which may cause around 800 [[hitpoints]] of [[damage]]. Each player has three demons that can [[Great Energy Beam|GEB]]. This means most mages are not likely to survive a powerful combo (2 GEBs and some GFBs for example, which means you have to be lucky to get through, not considering that you might take a very powerful combo causing up to 1800 [[hitpoints]] of damage which could kill the whole team)

*A recommendation for the leader person ([[knight]] is strongly recommended of course), is to have a weapon [[Imbuing|imbued]] with [[Powerful Frost]], or even better, an ice weapon such as [[Winterblade]], [[Resizer]] or [[Cobra Axe]], however, these weapons require a higher level to use (usually 200+).

*Mages could get a [[Sudden Death|SD]] shot ready before they are getting teleported to the room, so they can shoot it while their [[Mana Shield]] is still blocking.

*For a good chance of survival all the members of the team can use [[Might Ring]]s and [[Elven Amulet]]s that make the damage taken from the demons drop by 25%. You could also consider wearing [[Necklace of the Deep]], which reduces the damage of the life drain beam by 50%.

*Note: Watch out for not getting exhausted while healing, as you may not attack using [[melee]] or ranged ([[crossbow]], [[bow]], etc..) [[weapon]] as long as you are exhausted.

*The normal strategy is the following order: 3 shooters and [[Knight]] blocking (never forget it, the knight must be in the first square, to use the melee)

*Another strategy is when the team consists into 2 or more [[knight]]s, is that the "not blocking" [[knight]]s, should use [[Assassin Star]]s to attack, because they would get exhausted while using potions and [[Exori Hur]].

*Another strategy can be applied by [[knight]]s, but never tested with [[paladin]]s and [[mage]]s. All 4 [[knight]]s should bring 1 [[Backpack|bp]] of [[Icicle (rune)|icicles]].
[[Blocker]] may bring fewer because he will be healing more than the other knights, the trick consists on shooting the demons with [[Icicle (rune)|icicles]] while you heal, always shoot the [[Icicle (rune)|icicle]] when you are full [[health]], with this a team of 4 [[knight]]s shooting [[Icicle (rune)|Icicles]] can complete the [[quest]] in less than 2 minutes with a waste of about 350 [[Great Health Potion]]s on average.
