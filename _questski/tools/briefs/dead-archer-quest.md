## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Dead Archer Quest}}
==Required Equipment==
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Rope]]
* Recommended [[Antidote]], [[Antidote Rune]], or [[Destroy Field]] for [[Poison Field]]s, and if it's necessary some healing runes (Intense or Ultimate) for the [[Slime]]s.

==Method==
* Go the [[Thais]] troll cave east of town ([COORD x=32491 y=32260 z=7 | here]). You will need to use a shovel to open the hole.
* After entering the cave, go east and go down a hole:
[[Image:Dead Archer Quest Map 01.jpg]]

* Follow this path east and go down into the hole. When down this hole, you will find [[Orc Warrior]]s, [[Orc]]s and possibly [[Luring|lured]] [[Scorpion]]s.
[[Image:Dead Archer Quest Map 02.jpg]]

* Follow this path south to find the Dead Archer. On either side of the [[Slime]] spawn, you will have to pass through some [[Poison Field]]s:
[[Image:Dead Archer Quest Map 03.jpg]]

* "Use" the body on the north end of this room to get your reward.
