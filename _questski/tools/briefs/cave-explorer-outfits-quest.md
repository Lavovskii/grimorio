== Requirements ==
* 100 [[Spike Tasks Quest|fame points]] within [[The Spike|The Spike construct]]. You can check your current amount of fame points by asking [[Gnommander]] about '''reward''' and then checking your questlog. 
* 6,000 [[gp]]
* Level 50+ for the First Addon (Headware)
* Level 80+ for the Second Addon (Grappling Hook)
