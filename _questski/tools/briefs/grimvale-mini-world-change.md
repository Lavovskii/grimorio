## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

== Messages ==
{{MWC Messages|The full moon has a strange impact on the island of Grimvale. The small forest there seems darker, filled with nightly howls.|The full moon pale has risen over Grimvale. Help lift the curse, you won't be off worse!}}

[[Feroxa]] always spawns on the 13th of each month. [[Feroxa]] messages:
# {{Message|type=server|Grimvale drowns in werecreatures as the full moon reaches its apex and ancient evil returns.}}
# {{Message|type=server|Half of the current full moon is visible now, there are still a lot of clouds in front of it.}}<br />''(This will be triggered between 16 and 18 minutes after the first message.)''
# {{Message|type=server|The full moon is completely exposed: Feroxa awaits!}}<br />''(This will be triggered exactly 4 minutes after the previous message.)''
# All characters in Feroxa's antechamber will be teleported and the boss battle will begin.<br />''(This will be triggered exactly 10 minutes after the previous message.)''

'''Note:''' Every time before facing Feroxa you have to fulfill the prerequisites of delivering 3 [[Purple Nightshade Blossoms]] to [[Maeryn]] and kill 50 were-creatures as described in {{Spoiler Section|Grimvale Quest|The Curse of the Full Moon}}.

== Method ==
From the [[Server Save]] of the 12<sup>th</sup> to the server save of the 15<sup>th</sup> of each month, the full moon will rise over [[Grimvale]]. This means that the harmless [[Bandits]] inhabiting the island will turn into fearsome [[Were-Creatures]]! Take this opportunity to do the [[Grimvale Quest]] and rid the normally peaceful island from this awful curse.
