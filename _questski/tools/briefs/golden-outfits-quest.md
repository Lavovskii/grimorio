## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Golden Outfits Quest}}
=== Outfit (Armor) ===
[[File:Outfit Golden Male.gif]] [[File:Outfit Golden Female.gif]] {{Ilink|The Golden Outfit Display (Basic)}}
==== Required Equipment ====
* 500,000,000 [[gp]]

==== Method ====
* Go to [[King Tibianus]] ([[Thais]]) '''or''' [[Queen Eloise]] ([[Carlin]]) '''or''' [[Emperor Kruzak]] ([[Kazordoon]]).
* Greet the chosen NPC properly: ''hail king'', ''hail queen'' or ''hail emperor''.
* Ask about either ''outfit'' or ''addon'', and say ''yes''.
* Then ask about ''armor'' and say ''yes''.

==== Transcripts ====
{{Infobox Transcript|
''Player'': '''hail queen'''<br />
Queen Eloise: I greet thee, my loyal '''subject'''.<br />
''Player'': '''outfit'''<br />
Queen Eloise: In exchange for a truly generous donation, I will offer a special outfit. Do you want to make a donation?<br />
''Player'': '''yes'''<br />
Queen Eloise: Excellent! Now, let me explain. If you donate 1.000.000.000 gold pieces, you will be entitled to wear a unique '''outfit'''. ...<br />
Queen Eloise: You will be entitled to wear the '''armor''' for 500.000.000 gold pieces, '''boots''' for an additional 250.000.000 and the '''helmet''' for another 250.000.000 gold pieces. ...<br />
Queen Eloise: What will it be?<br />
''Player'': '''armor'''<br />
Queen Eloise: So you would like to donate 500.000.000 gold pieces which in return will entitle you to wear a unique armor?<br />
''Player'': '''yes'''<br />
Queen Eloise: Take this armor as a token of great gratitude. Let us forever remember this day, my friend.
}}
