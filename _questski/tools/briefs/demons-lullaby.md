## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Demon's Lullaby}}
== Needed Equipment ==
* One or more [[Mouldy Cheese]]
* One or more [[Dragon Ham]]
* 3 or more [[Worm]]s
* a [[Fishing Rod]]
* a [[War Drum]]
* a [[Hammer]]
'''Note:''' The wishes are random so you don't have to buy everything before you start.<br>
'''Note:''' You will have to complete 6 tasks before you can return the [[Demon Infant]], which means you don't have to start the quest on the first day to finish it and you can miss a few days as long as you complete 6 tasks.

== Method ==
Travel to the [[Jakundaf Desert]] and locate the [[Demon Mother]], found [COORD x=32634 y=32121 z=7 | here] on the surface, south-west of the entrance to the [[Desert Dungeon]] and west of [[Adrenius]]' temple.<br />
[[File:Demon Mother location.jpg|500px|center|Location of Demon Mother in the Jakundaf Desert.]]

Speak with her using {{KW|Hi}}, {{KW|Challenge}}, and {{KW|Yes}} to receive a [[Demon Infant]].

Your objective is to care for the baby by fulfilling its requests. To hear what it wants, '''use''' the [[Demon Infant]]. The baby will cry and request a specific item, creature, or location interaction. Once the request is fulfilled successfully, the infant will fall asleep for 20 hours.

After the sleeping period ends, use the infant again to wake it up, then use it once more to hear its next request. Each completed request counts toward the event progress.

You must successfully fulfill '''6 requests''' in total. After completing the sixth request, the infant will become pleased. Return the pleased [[Demon Infant]] to the [[Demon Mother]] to finish the event participation.

'''Important Notes:'''
* You may only care for '''one''' [[Demon Infant]] at a time.
* Some requests may require traveling to dangerous areas or interacting with hostile creatures.
* If the infant is neglected for too long, it will return to the [[Demon Mother]] automatically and you will fail the event for that character.
* If you lose your infant, you can only continue the event that year by obtaining another infant from a different character.

== Tasks ==
'''Use''' the [[Demon Infant]] to get its attention and hear what it currently wants. After 15 seconds the infant will begin crying again, and you can use it once more to repeat its request.

'''Note:''' To complete a request successfully, you must first use the infant to get its attention. While the infant is attentive, use it on the required creature, item, or location to fulfill the task. If the infant starts crying again before you complete the task, you must calm it down by using it again before attempting to satisfy the request.

== Rewards ==

=== Individual Rewards ===

These are obtained when returning a pleased Demon Infant:

* [[Achievement]] [[Baby Sitter]];
* [[Achievement]] [[I Did My Part]] (if you didn't have it already);
* [[Achievement]] [[Nanny from Hell]], after successfully completing the event 5 years;
* A random baby demon doll ({{Ilink|Evilina}}[[Evilina]], {{Ilink|Impward}}[[Impward]], {{Ilink|Meandi}}[[Meandi]], {{Ilink|Whinona}}[[Whinona]], {{Ilink|Badbara}}[[Badbara]], {{Ilink|Cryana}}[[Cryana]] or {{Ilink|Tearesa}}[[Tearesa]]).

=== [[Game World]] Reward ===

If {{Post|38675804|at least 500}} babies are returned until May 14:

* [[Experience]] bonus of 50% until May 21.
* Login message: {{Server Message|Your world has pleased the demon mother. Your experience gain is increased!}}

If less than 500 babies are returned until May 14:
* Login message: {{Server Message|The demon mother is disappointed. You were not able to help her with all of her babies.}}
