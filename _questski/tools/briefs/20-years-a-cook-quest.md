== Requirements ==
* 1x {{Ilink|Spike Sword}} [[Spike Sword]];
* Access to [[Meriana]] (completed "The Errand" mission of [[The Shattered Isles Quest]]);
* Some {{Ilink|Stone Skin Amulet}} [[Stone Skin Amulet]]s (recommended);
* Some {{Ilink|Might Ring}} [[Might Ring]]s (recommended);

== Meeting Cheaty and The Draccoon ==
<div style="float:right;">[MINIMAP x=32849 y=32136 z=7 marks=none]</div>
Start by traveling to [[Venore]] and make your way towards the southwest exit. Descend the stairs and head just south of where [[Appaloosa]] is situated. There, you'll find a ramp leading up to [[Cheaty]].

{{KW|Talk}} to him and he'll tell you [[The Draccoon]] has just returned after a long absence and could use some help from talented individuals. Accept the mission and head to Venore's dumpster (going down [COORD x=32964 y=32110 z=6 | here]).

When you get to the dumpster, walk a bit to the east and step on the {{Ilink|Ominous Trashcan}} [[Ominous Trashcan]] found [COORD x=32974 y=32111 z=7 | here] to get teleported to [[The Draccoon]]'s room. Ask him for a {{KW|mission}} and he'll tell you [[Nimmersatt]] entrapped him there with the intention to devour him but he managed to prolong his life by becoming one of Nimmersatt's cooks. But now he wants revenge!

:'''Note:''' All tasks in this quest have a 20 hour cooldown.

== An Old Friend ==
By accepting the mission Draccoon will lead you to [[Kazordoon]] to inform an old friend of his of his return.

In Kazordoon, go three floors up from depot (same floor where [[Sigurd]] is) and walk [COORD x=32596 y=31938 z=5 | here] where you'll find a light blue teleport. Step on it and you'll get teleported in a room with [[The Rest of Ratha]]. Walk and stand close to the skeleton until you get notified to head back to Draccoon to tell him about his friend's state. Say "mission" to update the quest.

== Ghost Hunt ==
:''This task has a maximum duration of 600 seconds (10 minutes). This makes doing it alone hard to finish in time.''
[[File:The Rest of Ratha.gif|right]]
After informing Draccoon about Ratha, he believes a communication with the skeleton is still possible with the help of another friend who has tools that can "extract" Ratha.

Return to Kazordoon and this time you will access a lever room when stepping in the light blue teleport, leading to this quest's first task.

Your goal here is to position the [[Ghost Duster (Ratha)]] in a way that its beam hits Ratha. When hit by the beam Ratha will drop essences on the floor which you will collect with a [[Spirit Container (Ratha-Empty)]] {{Ilink|Spirit Container (Ratha-Empty)}} and deposit in the bigger spirit container. You find the small container in a box near the Ghost Duster. All equipment needed (except the harp) are in the south-east corner of the room.

Note that the duster only beams when a player deals damage to it, so when Ratha is close to it, it is recommended that mages and paladins attack it from distance. It is also recommended to use spells that cause damage over time to it, like [[Inflict Wound]], [[Ignite]], [[Curse]], [[Envenom]], and [[Holy Flash]]. Additionally, since Ratha teleports a lot around the room, it is possible to play the {{Ilink|Harp}} [[Harp]] in the middle of the room to lure it back there. Note that this only works 3 times.

Repeat the process of collection and depositing essences until your container reaches a sum of 50 and the task will be concluded.

== Almost a Seance ==
Report back to [[The Draccoon|Draccoon]] and he says that he received the vial with [[Ratha]]'s essences from the postman guild as well as a potion of spirit talking, a potion that allows humans to talk with human spirits. He asks you to grab the potion from his rack and drink it.

By using the rack, you accidentally drink Ratha's essences instead of the potion of spirit talking, and thus start "hearing" Ratha while he is able to read your mind.

Inform Draccoon of the current situation and he will let you know that this will get sorted, but he has other things to care for first.

Note: To get rid of Ratha's messages, use the rack a second time to "extract Ratha from your mind". This is possible once you received your reward.

== Revenge Best Served Fried! ==
:''This task has a maximum duration of 600 seconds (10 minutes).''
[[File:The Fryclops.gif|right]]
[[The Draccoon|Draccoon]] wants you to remove Nimmersatt's master cook, [[The Fryclops]], from the picture. He says his friend and guildmate [[Bozo]] informed him the cyclops now resides on a small island north of [[Carlin]] where he tries to sink ships as a hobby.

Draccoon then sets an {{Ilink|Ominous Trashcan}} [[Ominous Trashcan]] in the [[Ghostlands]] that is a secret passage to Fryclops' isle ([COORD x=32247 y=31832 z=7 | here]).

In the isle you'll have another lever task. In the first part of the task you need to lure Fryclops to beam the [[Locked Door]] in the north wall of the room. A total of four beams are needed to destroy the door.

Once the door is destroyed, walk out in the yard, get the {{Ilink|Fryclops Axe}} [[Fryclops Axe]] from the chest then position Fryclops on the catapult. Once that happens, use the axe to cut the rope and launch the cyclops far away.

== In Charge ==
{| style="float: right;"
| [[File:Orshabaal.gif|right]] || [[File:Morshabaal.gif|right]]
|}

By defeating Fryclops, Draccoon and his mates now have access to his machine in his cellar but the machine needs to be refueled with infernal power crystals which you can steal from unsuspecting demons.

Head to [[Edron]] and near the [[Stone Circle]], next to an abandoned tower, you will find an {{Ilink|Ominous Trashcan}} [[Ominous Trashcan]] to the next task ([COORD x=33117 y=31672 z=7 | here]).

:<small>'''Note:''' Even though nothing in the room attacks you, you still receive damage reflection from [[Morshabaal]] and [[Ominous Pillar]]. Just ignore everything in the room.</small>

:''This task has a limit of 200 seconds as you enter, then extends 10 seconds for each crystal. The timer is personal and you see the countdown on yourself.''

In this task your goal is to steal 50 {{Ilink|Immensely Powerful Crystal}} [[Immensely Powerful Crystal]] from the demons. During the task you are under the effect of [[Cheaty]]'s spell thus disguised as something harmless and uninteresting.

Run to the northwest part of the room, use the crates with crystals, then run back to the entrace to store the crystal you got in the [[Crystal Collection Box]]. 

When you pick up the crystal you will be "drunk with power" and will act as drunk until you have dropped off the crystal. Each time you drop off a crystal, your disguise is extended. Dwarven Ring have no effect.

Once you reach the goal of 50 crystals return to Venore and report to Draccoon.

== Charged Machine ==
:''This task has a maximum duration of 600 seconds (10 minutes).''
Now that Draccoon can recharge the machine he'll tell you to go back to [[Ghostlands]] once again and use the left trashcan to Fryclops' cellar.

In this task you and the other players need to use the lever next to Draccoon to get things started, then use the ten other levers in the centre of the room to use the machine. To use the machine properly you need to use the lever that triggers green sparkles and makes a {{message|type=sound|<Tick>}} sound. It is recommend to assign a player for the top row and one for the bottom row to avoid various players misclicking the same lever at the same time.

During the process the machine will generate the following items, in order:
* {{Ilink|Worn Leather Boots}} [[Worn Leather Boots]]
* {{Ilink|Fire Sword}} [[Fire Sword]]
* {{Ilink|Crown}} [[Crown]]
* {{Ilink|Ferumbras' Hat}} [[Ferumbras' Hat]]
* {{Ilink|The Famous Sword of Fury}} [[The Famous Sword of Fury]]
* {{Ilink|The Broken Sword of Fury}} [[The Broken Sword of Fury]]

Using the machine makes it so you accidently break the [[Sword of Fury]]. Bring a new {{Ilink|Spike Sword}} [[Spike Sword]] to Draccoon for a replacement.

== Bonelord Language ==
:''This task has a maximum duration of 540 seconds (9 minutes).''

Draccoon paid a hefty sum of money to a mad scientist to crack the code of the [[469|Bonelord Language]]. Travel to [[Quirefang]] ([COORD x=33492 y=31317 z=7 | here]) and use the {{Ilink|Ominous Trashcan}} [[Ominous Trashcan]] to meet the scientist in the next task and get the secret of the language for Draccoon.

Once you start the task, the scientist, [[Professor Rudebert Evul]], will ask you to prove you are worth knowing the secret by clearing his cellar. In total, there are three floors to be cleared. Every time a floor is cleared, at least one player needs to rush back upstairs and walk close to the professor to grant access to the next floor for all players.

The first floor has some [[Cellar Rat]]s which should not be a problem to kill if you come prepared.

Second floor has lots of [[Adventurers Bane]]s, creatures that look like [[Crate]]s with very high damage reflection. It is recommended to attack them slowly while wearing {{Ilink|Stone Skin Amulet}} [[Stone Skin Amulet]]s and {{Ilink|Might Ring}} [[Might Ring]]s, and adapt depending on whether your character deals well with the reflection. 

If it feels like you are taking too long, rather abandon the attempt and regroup after 20 hours cooldown. You have to fight through 3 levels within the time limit.

[[File:20 Years a Cook Quest - Adventurers Bane Floor.png|center|450px]]

The third floor is similar to the second one but with [[Adventurers Nemesis]], creatures that look like [[Barrel]]s with slightly lower damage reflection but more health points. Because of a lower damage reflection it is maybe viable to use {{Ilink|Gill Necklace}} [[Gill Necklace]]s and {{Ilink|Might Ring}} [[Might Ring]]s instead but be careful and make sure to do what is safest for your character.

Now that you cleared all three floors, go back to the surface once again and as the professor is about to tell you the secret of the Bonelord language, Fryclops falls from the skies on top of him, killing both. Report to Draccoon.

== Pirate Ship ==
:''This task has a maximum duration of 600 seconds (10 minutes).''
[[File:Pirate Ship.gif|right]]
Draccoon tells you his informants learned Nimmersatt has a hidden base where he is conducting breeding experiments with dragons. There he created [[Titano Dragon]], the ultimate dragon that is bigger than ships and that is almost ready to make flight and bring doom and destruction to the civilised world.

Go to [[Meriana]] ([COORD x=32302 y=32538 z=7 | here]) and talk to [[Rascalio]] who will arrange you a ship to face the titanic dragon.

Use the lever to start the task and use the docks to get a {{Ilink|Balista Bolt}} [[Balista Bolt]]. Sail west and find a [[Pirate Ship]]. Use your bolt (the same way you'd use a rune) and destroy the ship. Loot the ship and you'll acquire a {{Ilink|Enchanted Balista Bolt}} [[Enchanted Balista Bolt]].

Now go north and repeat the process with your new bolt to kill Titano Dragon. Avoid the dragon waves and also standing close to [[Storm]]s.

Return to Draccon lair.

== The Dragon Hoard ==
With the quest completed and at the breeding ground you can face three kinds of dragons: [[Wardragon]]s, [[Dragolisk]]s and [[Mega Dragon]]s. Make your way through the hunting area until you reach the lever to face the Dragon Hoard.

In this boss fight you will face a total of seven dragons, each one with a special feature. They are, using the lever order in the bossfight room:

Your goal is to use the levers and defeat each of these dragons individually in 15 minutes. The main mechanic of this fight is that the order in which you choose to defeat the dragons directly affects the next dragons. Once a dragon is defeated, its feature will be available to all dragons thereafter. For example, if you choose to kill [[Vengar]] first, a dragon that casts [[Feared|Fear]] to players, then all next six dragons in the fight will also cast Fear, etc. Resistances are not inherited, so for example if you decide to kill [[Greedok]] first, you can still use death damage on all remaining bosses.

For that reason it is important to analyse the vocations of your team and plan ahead which order you want to use. For example if your team features a lot of [[Paladin|Royal Paladins]] it is probably not a good idea to end the fight with [[Bruton]], who is immune to physical damage, or perhaps ending the fight with [[Greedok]] when your team features lower level [[Druid|Elder Druids]] and [[Sorcerer|Master Sorcerers]] who would be safer standing further from the boss and attacking with [[Sudden Death Rune]]s.

It is generally recommended to finish the fight with [[Bruton]] to avoid having to deal with multiple bosses with inherited self-healing. Since he's immune to physical damage, Royal Paladins should consider imbuing a cheap secondary crossbow such as [[Rift Crossbow]] with [[Powerful Reap]] or other powerful elemental imbuing.

With all seven dragons defeated you can access the treasure chest located south in the room and loot your reward.

Note that for the purposes of [[Cyclopedia#Bosstiary|Bosstiary]] and daily boosted boss, [[Dragon Pack]] is used with [[Despor]]'s picture, instead of ''Dragon Hoard'', but they will both boost the reward regardless of which dragon was killed last.
