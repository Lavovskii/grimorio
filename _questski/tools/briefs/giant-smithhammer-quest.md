## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Giant Smithhammer Quest}}
==Method==
* Go to the [[Cyclops]]/[[Minotaur]] camp in the [[Plains of Havoc]], and be prepared to face many [[Cyclops|Cyclopes]] and various types of [[Minotaurs]].
* Go down the stairs in the north structure ([COORD x=32779 y=32235 z=7 | here]). Once you go down these stairs, you will be faced with numerous (read: MANY) [[Cyclops|Cyclopes]], [[Minotaur]]s, [[Minotaur Guard]]s, [[Minotaur Mage]]s, and even some [[Orc]]s. Even a level 30 will lose more than half of his/her life immediately if going down without being invisible.
* The quest box is located in the room directly to the south.
