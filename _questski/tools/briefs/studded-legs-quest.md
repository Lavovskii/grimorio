==Required equipment==
*[[Rope]]
*Optional [[Small Health Potion]]s and [[Food]]

== Method ==
*Go to the [[Wasp]] tower in North-west [[Rookgaard]] ([COORD x=31999 y=32140 z=7 | here]).
*Rope yourself up killing or simply ignoring the [[Wasp]]s you will face.
*At the top simply "use" on the [[Honeyflower Patch]] to the right and you will get a [[Honey Flower]].
*Head to [[Lee'Delle]]'s Shop ([COORD x=32024 y=32196 z=7 | here]), on '''premium account area''', and trade your [[Honey Flower]] for a pair of [[Studded Legs]].
**'''Note:''' If you find more [[Honey Flower]]s you can trade those too, there is not a once only limit.
