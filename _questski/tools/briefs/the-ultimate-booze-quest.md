## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=The Ultimate Booze Quest}}

===Method===
Go to [[Boozer]] in [[Venore]], above the [[depot]], and ask him for a '''mission'''. He will tell you to go to [[Kazordoon]] Brewery and fill a [[Empty Beer Bottle|Bottle]] with a [[Beer Bottle|special beer]].

Go to Kazordoon. After you pass the part with the lava to enter the city, while you are in the part that has some Rats, go left instead of north, and follow the way up to the mountains.<br>
[[Image:The Ultimate Booze Quest.png]]

<br>
On the top floor, on the south-east corner, there will be a [[Cask of Brown Ale]]. Fill the bottle with it and bring it back to Boozer to get your reward.
[[File:UltimateBoozeCask.png|none|thumb|332x332px]]
