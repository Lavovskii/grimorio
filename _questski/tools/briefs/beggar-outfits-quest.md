== Outfit ==
[[File:Outfit Beggar Male.gif]]
[[File:Outfit Beggar Female.gif]]

=== Required Equipment ===
*50 [[Minotaur Leather]]s
*30 [[Heaven Blossom]]s
*20 [[Brown Piece of Cloth|Brown Pieces of Cloth]]
*10 [[Bat Wing]]s

=== Method ===
* You can collect all of the items above before you begin the quest. To save time, you are advised to collect the items before you talk to the npc.
** Collect [[Brown Pieces of Cloth]] from [[Ghouls]], [[Barbarians]].
** Collect [[Minotaur Leather]]s from [[Minotaurs]].
** Collect [[Bat Wing]]s from [[Bat]]s, or [[Mutated Bat]]s.
** Collect [[Heaven Blossom]]s from [[Elf|Elves]] and [[Elf Scout]].
* Talk to Hugo in Venore about a new "outfit".
* He will become inspired to create a new fashion, but he needs materials.
* Give him the items and he will make the new outfit for you.
* Come back after he has finished making the outfit (24 hours in real time, not in tibian time), and you will be able to wear it.

== Beard (male), Dress (female) ==
[[File:Outfit Beggar Male Addon 1.gif]] [[File:Outfit Beggar Female Addon 2.gif]]
=== Required Equipment ===
* 100 [[Ape Fur]]
* 20,000 [[gp]]
=== Method ===
* To save time, it is a good idea to collect the items above before you begin the quest. Collect [[Ape Fur]]s from [[Apes]] and make sure you have 20K gp.
* Visit [[Simon the Beggar]] south of [[Fibula]] ([COORD x=32187 y=32473 z=7 | here]), say "hi" and ask him about his "outfit". He will tell you he can make a magic potion to make your beard grow, but it will cost you 100 ape furs and 20,000 gp.
* Give him the 100 ape furs and 20,000 gp.
* Return to Simon and say "Hi" followed by "Addon". He will sprinkle the magic potion on you.
* For male characters: Wait '''at least''' 120 real hours (5 real days) for the potion to "take effect". Although, it can take more than that if Simon "forgets" about your addon (this is random), in this case you must remind him about it.<br /> For Female characters: You will receive the gypsy dress immediately.
* Return to [[Simon the Beggar]] and mention the "addon". He will tell you how wonderful your beard looks, and you will be able to select the addon.

== Staff ==
[[File:Outfit Beggar Male Addon 2.gif]]
[[File:Outfit Beggar Female Addon 1.gif]]

=== Required Equipment ===
* [[Staff (Simon)|Simon the Beggar's Favorite Staff]]

=== Method ===
* You must complete the "beard" addon before you can get the staff addon
* Talk to [[Simon the Beggar]] south of [[Fibula]]
* First say "addon" or "beard", then ask him about his "staff"
* He will tell you that you cannot have his staff, but that he will give you this staff if you retrieve his lost [[Staff (Simon)|"favourite staff"]]
* Go to the [[Hero Cave]] north of [[Edron]] and follow path to the main hunting level where [[Demon]]s can be accessed:
[[File:TripleUHRune3.jpg]]<br />

[[File:TripleUHRune4.jpg]]<br />

[[File:TripleUHRune5.jpg]]<br />

[[File:TripleUHRune6.jpg]]<br />

[[File:TripleUHRune7.jpg]]<br />

[[File:SimonsStaffRoom.jpg]]<br />

Go south just a little way from the hole on this level and go into the room on your right. (Room on left is to [[Triple UH Rune Quest]].)
Down the stairs two levels is the final room where you can retrieve his lost staff. There are [[Monk (Creature)|Monk]]s, [[Stone Golem]]s, and [[Priestess]]es in these rooms. (In the final room you can also retrieve a bag with 5 [[Strong Mana Potion]]s from a chest.)
[[File:Simon_the_Beggar_staff.JPG]]

* Return the staff to Simon, and he will give you his current staff as an addon for you.
