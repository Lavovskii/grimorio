## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Swamp Fever World Change}}

Find [[Ottokar]] in [[Venore]]'s poor house [COORD x=32880 y=32117 z=7 | here]. Go out the south-west exit and walk east below the bridge, then north along the path.

He will inform you about his fight against Swamp Fever. Only the poorest of Venore get the disease, but the cure is not hard to obtain.
[[Feverish Citizen]]s may appear throughout Venore and may attack you - they suffer from Swamp Fever (spawn time can be either thirty minutes or an hour and more depending on the amount of [[Medicine Pouch]]es given to [[Ottokar]]). If you kill them, they may drop certain pieces of cloth needed for the [[Afflicted Outfits Quest]].

In order to prevent the fever from spreading a certain number of [[Medicine Pouch]]es must be given to Ottokar each day. You can go south of Venore to the [[Swamp Troll Cave]] and hunt them for these medicine bags. You could also go to the [[Port Hope Swamp Trolls]]. In exchange for [[Medicine Pouch]]es, he will give you the [[Belongings of a deceased|Belongings of a Deceased]] patient. These bags may contain a number of things, including [[Slug Drug]], which can be used to [[Tiger Slug|tame]] wild [[Slug]]s.
