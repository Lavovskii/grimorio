== Messages ==
{{MWC Messages|Sharpen your sword! The witch Wyda seems to be bored so pay her a visit!|Hear ye! Hear ye! The witch Wyda seems to be bored. Pay her a visit but sharpen your sword. She might come up with a terrible surprise, are you brave enough to believe your eyes?}}

== Method ==
Visit [[Wyda]] in the [[Green Claw Swamp]]. When [[Wyda]] is bored she will react different to keywords. If after saying "quest" she tells you that she's bored, you can bring her [[Blood Herb]]s and you have the chance to get a [[Torn Teddy]] and the [[achievement]] [[Torn Treasures]]. It's also possible to earn the [[achievement]] [[Someone's Bored]] by killing one of the [[Giant Spider (Wyda)|fake Giant Spiders]] which spawn around [[Wyda]]'s house.
