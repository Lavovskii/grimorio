## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Spirithunters Quest}}

== Required Equipment ==
*Completed [[Research and Development Quest]].
*Travel money.
*It might be a good idea to take some [[Food]] and [[Health Potions]] with you on missions 2 & 3.

== Method ==
Find [[Spectulus]] and join the Spirithunters in the Edron Academy.

=== Mission 1 ===

Find [[Spectulus]], join the Spirithunters, and then help him use the [[Magical Measurement Device]]
in the graveyard northeast of Edron, [COORD x=33217 y=31670 z=7 | here]. Use the [[Magical Measurement Device]] on all the graves (upon use, a [[Squidgy Slime]] is spawned, this used to be a normal [[Slime]] before [[Updates/11.02]]). Report back to [[Spectulus]] when you're done.

=== Mission 2 ===

Speak with [[Spectulus]] about your '''mission'''. Agree on helping with the new [[Spirit Cage]] the Spirithunters have invented and calibrated, then go down to [[Sinclair]] and talk to him about his '''research'''. He will ask you to find a [[Ghost]], kill it, and then use the [[Spirit Cage]] on the dead [[Ghost]]. Report back to [[Sinclair]] when you're done.

=== Mission 3 ===

Speak to [[Sinclair]]. He will ask you to take the [[Spirit Cage]] and hunt some more [[Ghost]]s. You need to capture 5 [[Ghost]]s, OR 5 [[Ghost]]s and 5 [[Nightstalker]]s, OR 5 [[Ghost]]s, 5 [[Nightstalker]]s, and 5 [[Souleater]]s, in order to complete this mission. The more spirits you capture, the greater the reward will be in [[gold]] and [[experience]]. To get the higher rewards, you must capture 5 of each spirit - any less will not be counted when you return to [[Sinclair]].<br />

*Completing this quest gives you the [[Afraid of no Ghost!]] [[achievement]], even if you only bring 5 ghosts.

=== Rewards ===
For capturing 5 [[Ghost]]s - 1,000 exp and 5 [[Platinum Coins]]<br /> 
For capturing 5 [[Ghost]]s and 5 [[Nightstalker]]s - 3,000 exp and 15 [[Platinum Coins]]<br /> 
For capturing 5 [[Ghost]]s, 5 [[Nightstalker]]s and 5 [[Souleater]]s - 10,000 exp and 60 [[Platinum Coin]]s<br />
