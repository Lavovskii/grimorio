## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Royal Costume Outfits Quest}}
=== Outfit (Armor) ===
[[File:Outfit Royal Costume Male.gif]] [[File:Outfit Royal Costume Female.gif]]
==== Required Equipment ====
* {{Ilink|Silver Token}} 15,000 [[Silver Token]]s
* {{Ilink|Gold Token}} 12,500 [[Gold Token]]s

==== Method ====
* Go to [[Percybald]] ([[Carlin]]) '''or''' [[Alyxo]] ([[Issavi]]).
* Greet the chosen NPC.
* Ask about either ''outfit'' or ''addon'', and say ''yes''.
* Then ask about ''armor'' and say ''yes''.

==== Transcripts ====
{{Infobox Transcript|
''Player'': '''hi'''<br />
Alyxo: Greetings, dear art lover! Welcome to the Seaside '''Theatre'''!<br />
''Player'': '''outfit'''<br />
Alyxo: In exchange for a truly generous donation of gold and silver tokens, I will offer a special outfit. Do you want to make a donation?<br />
''Player'': '''yes'''<br />
Alyxo: Excellent! Now, let me explain. If you donate 30,000 silver tokens and 25,000 gold tokens, you will be entitled to wear a unique '''outfit'''. ...<br />
Alyxo: You will be entitled to wear the '''armor''' for 15,000 silver tokens and 12,500 gold tokens, and the '''shield''' and the '''crown''' for additional 7,500 silver tokens and 6,250 gold tokens each. ...<br />
Alyxo: What will it be?<br />
''Player'': '''armor'''<br />
Alyxo: So you would like to donate 15,000 silver tokens and 12,500 gold tokens, which in return will entitle you to wear a unique red armor?<br />
''Player'': '''yes'''<br />
 
}}
