== Requirements ==
* [[Cobra Tongue]] (mission only)
* [[Small Ruby]] (daily task)
* [[Small Sapphire]] (daily task)
* [[Small Amethyst]] (daily task)
* [[Small Topaz]] (daily task)

== Mission ==
Go to the [[Lion's Rock]] west by north-west of [[Darashia]], near the Darashian portal to [[Gnomebase]], here:

[MINIMAP x=33143 y=32362 z=7 marks=none]
[[File:Lion's Mane Flower (Immobile).gif|thumb]]
Start by exploring the above ground rock; climb the staircases inside and outside the mountain. On the upper floors you can find 1) [[Lion's Mane Flower]]: use to obtain a [[Lion's Mane Flower]], and 2) a [[Well With a Lion-like Face|fountain]]; use to obtain an [[Ewer with Holy Water]]; note: you cannot take holy water from the well more than once in a short time (1 day?).
:{{Message|type=server|You took some holy water from the sacred well.}}
[[File:Dead Human3.gif|right|Dead Explorer]]
Once you have both items, make your way to the underground cave. Not far from the start you will find a [[Dead Explorer]], use the corpse to obtain an [[Lion's Rock Three Tests (Book)|Old Parchment (Brown)]]. here:

[MINIMAP x=33145 y=32342 z=8 marks=none]

=== <u>Three Trials</u> ===

<div style="float: right; padding: 0 0 0.5em 0.5em;">{{:Lion's Rock Three Tests (Book)|Display}}
</div>

This floor has a series of tunnels which lead to small rooms filled with semi-weak undead monsters. You must complete 3 trials to progress, marked on the map below. It doesn't really matter in which order.[MINIMAP x=33151 y=32328 z=8 marks=(33136,32291) (33154,32279) (33136,32369)]
==== The Lion's Strength ====
(Marked 1 in mini map, North West)

You will find a [[Slightly Feline Statue]], [COORD x=33136 y=32290 z=8 | here]. Use the [[Cobra Tongue]] on this statue. The [[Small Pagoda]] will light up signifying that you have passed the trial. You will also receive a server message:
:{{Message|type=server|You have sacrificed a cobra tongue at an ancient statue. The light in the small pyramid nearby begins to shine.}}

==== The Lion's Tears ====
(Marked 2 in mini map, North East, just east of the first trial)

You will find an [[Ornamented Stone Pedestal]], [COORD x=33154 y=32280 z=8 | here]. Pour the [[Ewer with Holy Water]] onto the [[Ornamented Stone Pedestal]] and the [[Small Pagoda]] will light up.
:{{Message|type=server|You have purified a sacred pedestal with holy water. You have now passed the last test to  enter the inner sanctum.}}

==== The Lion's Beauty ====
(Marked 3 in mini map, South West)

You will find a [[Coal Basin]], [COORD x=33136 y=32369 z=8 | here]. Burn the [[Lion's Mane Flower]] on the [[Coal Basin]] and the [[Small Pagoda]] will light up.
:{{Message|type=server|You burnt a lion's mane flower. The light in the small pyramid nearby begins to shine.}}

Completing the 3 trials will enable you to enter the '''Inner Sanctum''', through the [[Mystic Flame]] on the west side of this floor. You can enter as many times as you like without redoing the trials.
:{{Message|type=server|You have passed the Lion's Tests and are now worthy to enter the inner sanctum!}}

Go through the [[Mystic Flame]] ([COORD x=33128 y=32308 z=8 | here]) to continue the quest.

=== <u>Scroll Translation</u> ===
On the second floor you will face [[Ancient Scarab]]s and [[Roaring Lion]]s. The floor is split into 3 parts, which are each connected by a small tunnel on the third floor. You start in the middle part. This part of the quest involves getting a Translation Scroll (marked 1 on the map), then walking by 4 areas to translate the inscriptions (marked with blue flags.)

'''Getting the translation scroll (marked 1 in the map):''' On the second floor, go west and north and down a hole [COORD x=33114 y=32296 z=9 | here]. Then walk north and go up [COORD x=33122 y=32281 z=10 | here]. Now walk north and run past a [[Large Amphora]] in the middle of the room, [COORD x=33119 y=32247 z=9 | here]. It will crumble, and along with a [[Translation Scroll]] you will receive the following message:
:{{Message|type=server|As you pass incautiously, the ancient amphora crumbles to shards and dust. Amidst the debris you discover an old scroll.}}

Now that you have the [[Translation Scroll]], make your way to the 4 areas marked blue, the scroll will automatically activate giving you a white server message when you're in the right spot.

== Task ==
After completing the mission to grant access to the [[Lion's Rock Inner Sanctum]] and translate the scroll you can do this task to obtain a random reward. In the north-west area of the Inner Sanctum, find the southern rectangular room to the south-west, here: 

[MINIMAP x=33074 y=32300 z=9 marks=none]

In this room you will find 4 "sun" floor inscriptions as well as a rock in the center. The sun inscriptions represent gem slots.

Here you must use gems corresponding to the slot's representation. You can see the slot's representation by looking at the tile.

[[File:Lion's Rock Quest Fountain.png|thumb|300px|Each gem must be used in a specific socket to light it up.]]
The north-west slot represents a hyaena's maw. Use a [[Small Ruby]] (hyaena blood) on the [[Stone Tile with a Socket (Hyaena's Maw)|Stone Tile with a Socket]]. A [[Red Flame]] will momentarily light up the slot.
:{{Message|type=server|You place the ruby on the small socket. A red flame begins to burn.}}

The south-west slot represents a nest. Use a [[Small Sapphire]] (lizard egg) on the [[Stone Tile with a Socket (Nest)|Stone Tile with a Socket]]. A [[Blue Flame]] will momentarily light up the slot.
:{{Message|type=server|You place the sapphire on the small socket. A blue flame begins to burn.}}

The north-east slot represents a snake's mouth. Use a [[Small Topaz]] (snake eye) on the [[Stone Tile with a Socket (Snake's Mouth)|Stone Tile with a Socket]]. A [[Yellow Flame]] will momentarily light up the slot.
:{{Message|type=server|You place the topaz on the small socket. A yellow flame begins to burn.}}

The south-east slot represents a scorpion's pincer. Use a [[Small Amethyst]] (scorpion poison) on the [[Stone Tile with a Socket (Scorpion's Pincer)|Stone Tile with a Socket]]. A [[Violet Flame]] will momentarily light up the slot.
:{{Message|type=server|You place the amethyst on the small socket. A violet flame begins to burn. Something happens at the center of the room ...}}

Once you have sacrificed all 4 of the gems, a bubbling [[Ornamented Fountain]] will appear in the middle of the room where the rocks were. Use the fountain to get your reward, which will be random out of the items listed below.
:{{Message|type=server|Something sparkles in the fountain's water. You draw out a beautiful and certainly precious amulet.}}
Possible rewards from the fountain are:
* [[The Lion's Heart]]
* [[Gold Ingot]]
* [[Yellow Gem]]
* [[Red Gem]]
* [[Giant Shimmering Pearl (Brown)]]
* [[Emerald Bangle]]

You can repeat this every 24 hours (not 20 hours, like most other tasks) to obtain another random reward.

{{Seealso|Lion's Rock Quest/Statistics|usage=article}}

If you have obtained the [[Noble Lion (Mount)|Noble Lion mount]], you cannot obtain [[The Lion's Heart]], even if you never obtained [[The Lion's Heart]] from the fountain. Be aware that you will be able to use your small gems on the sockets (to reveal the fountain) even if you cannot obtain a reward. In that case, using the fountain results in the following message: 

''At the moment there is neither a treasure nor anything else in the fountain. Perhaps you might return later.''

'''Also note that other players can use the fountain that you revealed, so it's not wise to do it if somebody is around that you do not trust.'''

You can leave now if you choose to. If you obtained [[The Lion's Heart]] you can tame a [[Noble Lion]]. To reach the [[Noble Lion]]s you must go to the lion temple. Go north of the gem room and follow the tunnel west until you reach an intersection. Turn east at the intersection and go down the stairs. On the first time going down here you will receive the server message:
:{{Message|type=server|You enter a temple area which is gorgeously decorated and mysteriously unaffected by the course of time.}}

In the lion temple you will only find [[Roaring Lion]]s and [[Noble Lion]]s.
