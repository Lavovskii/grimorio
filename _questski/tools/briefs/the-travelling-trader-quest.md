## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=The Travelling Trader Quest}}

== Requirements ==

Players must start the quest on Monday (after [[server save]]).

=== Item Requirements ===
* 1 {{ilink|Deer Trophy}} [[Deer Trophy]]
* 3 {{ilink|Iron Ore}} [[Iron Ore]]s
* 1 {{ilink|Goldfish Bowl}} [[Goldfish Bowl]]
* 1500 [[gp]] + travel money

== Method ==

After each mission, players must wait until the next day (after server save) to continue with the next part.

=== First Mission, Monday ===

[[Rashid]] will be in [[Svargrond]], at [[Dankwart]]'s tavern ([COORD x=32210 y=31158 z=7 | here]). Talk to him, ask for a {{KW|mission}}, and he will ask if you are interested in becoming a recognized trader. He warns you that you will have to travel a lot, and asks if you are willing to try. Say {{KW|yes}}, and he will explain the details to you, and ask if you understood. Answer {{KW|yes}}, and he will ask for a {{ilink|Deer Trophy}} [[Deer Trophy]]. Once you obtain one, come back to him and say {{KW|mission}}, {{KW|yes}}, and the quest log will update.

=== Second Mission, Tuesday ===

For this mission you will need 600 [[oz]] of free [[capacity]] to carry a {{ilink|Heavy Package}} [[Heavy Package]]. Rashid will be in [[Liberty Bay]] on Tuesday, in [[Lyonel]]'s tavern ([COORD x=32303 y=32835 z=7 | here]). Ask him for a {{KW|mission}}, say {{KW|yes}}, {{KW|yes}} and he will ask you to get him a package from [[Willard]], on [[Edron]] ([COORD x=33212 y=31795 z=6 | here]). Go to Edron, where Willard is the weapon dealer. Find him and ask him about the {{KW|package for Rashid}}. He will tell you that he forgot about it, and ask if you can keep it a secret from Rashid, and pick up the package from [[Snake Eye]] in [[Outlaw Camp]] ([COORD x=32657 y=32190 z=8 | here]). Answer {{KW|yes}}. Go to Snake Eye, and ask about the {{KW|package for Rashid}}, and he will let you open the [[Door (Sealed)|flashing door]]. Get the package and deliver to Rashid in Liberty Bay by saying {{KW|mission}}, {{KW|yes}}, and the quest log will update.

=== Third Mission, Wednesday ===

Rashid will be in [[Port Hope]] on Wednesdays, in [[Clyde]]'s tavern ([COORD x=32577 y=32753 z=7 | here]). Ask [[Rashid]] for a {{KW|mission}}, say {{KW|yes}}, {{KW|yes}}, and he will ask you to buy a {{ilink|Scarab Cheese}} [[Scarab Cheese]] from [[Miraia]] on [[Darashia]] ([COORD x=33239 y=32484 z=7 | here]). Travel to Darashia and buy the cheese for 100 [[gp]], by saying {{KW|scarab cheese}}, {{KW|yes}} to Miraia. You must be fast in delivering the cheese to Rashid, because after 30 minutes it turns into a {{ilink|Mouldy Cheese}} [[Mouldy Cheese]]. Also, beware that the cheese is edible. If you lose the cheese somehow, you can buy another one from [[Miraia]]. You cannot use boats, so you must make your way to Port Hope on foot following this [[Route:Ankrahmun to Port Hope|path]] from [[Ankrahmun]] to Port Hope.

Rashid '''will not''' accept the cheese if you:
* buy it before speaking to him
* send the cheese by parcel
* use [[Temple Teleport Scroll]] to [[Port Hope]]

After reaching Rashid, say {{KW|mission}}, {{KW|yes}}, to finish the delivery, and the quest log will update. If you have previously finished the Ice Delivery mission of [[The Explorer Society Quest]], you will also receive the [[Just in Time]] [[achievement]].

=== Fourth Mission, Thursday ===

Rashid will be in [[Ankrahmun]] on Thursdays, in [[Arito]]'s tavern, one floor above the post office. Talk to Rashid, say {{KW|mission}}, {{KW|yes}}, {{KW|yes}}, and he will ask you to buy him a {{ilink|Fine Vase}} [[Fine Vase]]. Travel to [[Ab'Dendriel]] and find [[Briasol]] ([COORD x=32634 y=31667 z=8 | here]) and buy the vase from him by saying {{KW|fine vase}}, {{KW|yes}}. You will need 1,000 [[gp]] to buy one. Note that this vase is very fragile, you must not touch it (do not move it on your [[Backpack]]). If the vase breaks, you can buy another. Go back to Rashid and deliver the vase to him by saying {{KW|mission}}, {{KW|yes}}, and the quest log will update.

=== Fifth Mission, Friday ===

Rashid will be in [[Darashia]] on Fridays, in [[Miraia]]'s tavern. Ask Rashid for {{KW|mission}}, say {{KW|yes}}, {{KW|yes}}, and he will ask you to use your trading skills to buy a {{ilink|Crimson Sword}} [[Crimson Sword]] from [[Uzgod]], on [[Kazordoon]] ([COORD x=32661 y=31895 z=9 | here]). He wants you to buy it for 400 [[gp]] or less, it must not be cheap, and the quality must be perfect. Go to Kazordoon and find Uzgod, the weapon dealer. Ask him for a {{KW|Crimson Sword}}, he will say he does not have any to sell. Ask him to {{KW|forge}} one for you, he will ask if you are joking with him, answer {{KW|no}}. He will set a high price, and ask you if you accept, answer {{KW|no}}. He will offer you a cheap sword for 300 [[gp]], answer {{KW|no}} again. He will then ask you to give him 3 {{Ilink|Iron Ore}} [[Iron Ore]]s and 250 gps for a good Crimson Sword, now you can answer {{KW|yes}}. Take back the sword to Rashid on Darashia, saying {{KW|mission}}, {{KW|yes}}. The quest log will be updated. If you make a mistake, you are able to reset the mission by buying a Crimson Sword and offering it to Rashid. This will allow you to reset the mission and try again right after.

=== Sixth Mission, Saturday ===

Rashid will be in [[Edron]] on Saturdays, in [[Mirabell]]'s tavern, one floor above the post office and bank. Ask him for {{KW|mission}}, say {{KW|yes}}, {{KW|yes}}, and he will ask you for a {{ilink|Goldfish Bowl}} [[Goldfish Bowl]]. You can obtain this before you talk to him, and just deliver it. If you do not have one, you can buy an empty {{ilink|Goldfish Bowl (Empty)}} [[Goldfish Bowl (Empty)|bowl]] on any [[Furniture Shopkeeper NPCs|furniture shopkeeper]]. After that, you must go to [[Calassa]] or the [[Sunken Quarter]] in [[Yalahar]] ([COORD x=32981 y=31190 z=13 | here], [COORD x=32932 y=31187 z=10 | here], or [COORD x=32941 y=31134 z=13 | here]) to get a goldfish inside your bowl. If you cannot enter Calassa or access Yalahar, you can ask a friend to get it to you, or buy from another player. After you have the item, talk to Rashid and say {{KW|mission}}, {{KW|yes}}, to deliver the bowl and the quest log will be updated.

=== Final Mission, Sunday ===

Rashid will be in the [[Carlin]] depot on Sundays, one floor above. If you have done all his previous tasks, just ask him about {{KW|mission}}. He will declare you as one of his recognized traders, and you will be able to trade with him anytime. You will also receive the achievement [[Recognised Trader]].
