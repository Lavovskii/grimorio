## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Shards of a Broken Moon Quest}}

== Requirements; ==
* Completed: [[Within the Tides Quest]]
* Lost Paradise of the [[Grimvale Quest]]

Optional rewards:
* [[Red Piece of Cloth]]
* [[Perfume Flacon]]

== Mission 1: Working for the Maharani ==
Speak to [[Saraki]] ({{KW|Hi}}, {{KW|moon}}), and she will open the quest up and ask players to find 3 hints to the ongoing weird stuff happening on the Naga islands, indicating [[Sundara]] and [[Tarisu]] might have clues.

=== Yukti Hint ===
Speak to [[Sundara]]: {{KW|Hi}}, {{KW|Yukti}}

Note: if you began the quest by speaking to [[Nilavarna]] instead of [[Saraki]], you should get this hint from [[Nipuna]] instead of [[Sundara]].

=== Tides Hint ===
Speak to [[Tarisu]], {{KW|Hi}}, {{KW|Tides}}, and he will hand you a [[Shell Gauge]] and ask you to mark 3 locations, 1 in the Sparkling Lagoon [COORD x=33901 y=32841 z=7 | here], and 2 in the Emerald Gardens [COORD x=33907 y=32787 z=7 | here] and [COORD x=33897 y=32765 z=7 | here], use the Shell Gauge on the Tide Markers to complete this part of the hint.

=== Plants Hint ===
Speak to [[Dhira]]: {{KW|Hi}}, {{KW|plants}}, and he will ask you to inspect a [[Sample of Bluish Tide Veil]] and [[Sample of Bluish Whisper Reed]] which will unlock the plant hint. Head to the Sparkling Lagoon and use a tool on the plants ([COORD x=33881 y=32824 z=7 | here], and [COORD x=33861 y=32838 z=7 | here]), and take the items back to the laboratory and use them on the refiner of magic [COORD x=33775 y=32854 z=7 | here].

Return to Saraki and say {{KW|Hi}}, {{KW|moon}}.

== Mission 2: Search for the Crater ==
Speak to [[Udu]] in the Observation tower: {{KW|Hi}}, {{KW|moon}}.

Udu will instruct you to find the piece of the moon that fell off the east coast of the Emerald Gardens. Head north east of the Emerald Gardens, and you will encounter the [[NPC]] [[Sharai]], say {{KW|Hi}}, {{KW|moon}}.

Sharai will give you access to the hidden cave south east, use the stones [COORD x=33894 y=32714 z=7 | here] to be transported into the hunting grounds. Take a few steps and you will have a questlog update asking you to explore the area and investigate the Asuri.

== Mission 3: Investigating the Asuri ==
Head north and you will find 2 doors, attempt to use the "stone door" on the right and you will find it is locked. Use the red door to the left and it will be open. Head north and speak to [[Javala]] ({{KW|hi}}, {{KW|moon}}), and she will give you the key to the stone door.

Head down a floor and towards the south west and you will enter a laboratory looking room. Inspect everything in the room, specially the note on the wall and the Crystal Plinth, until you get a quest log update. Enter the room to the north, it has a piece of moonshard in the middle of the room, explore the area and use one the Large Crystal Aparatus containers in the room to get a questlog update. Return to Javala to obtain your next mission.

== Mission 4: Talk to Niral ==
Head to [[Oskayaat]] and speak to [[Niral]] ({{KW|hi}}, {{KW|moon}}). He will ask for your help in dispatching a moon infused werecreature, [[Rakesh Moonfang]]. Head back to the floor above and go south east and into the portal. Defeat Rakesh and return to Niral. He will give you instructions on how to create Blue Lava, which requires you to return to the surface and head to the south west of Oskayaat, reaching the shipwreck and to search for an empty flask in the pile of flasks. Take this flask to the active volcano on the east side of Oskayaat during the night time. Have a {{ilink|Moon Mirror}} [[Moon Mirror]] on you and use the [[Empty Crystal Flask]] on the lava, making it become {{ilink|Crystal Flask with Blue Lava}} [[Crystal Flask with Blue Lava]]. Go back and report to Niral ({{KW|hi}}, {{KW|moon}}), he will bless it for you.

==Mission 5: Sabotage==
Return to the [[Asura Citadel]] and head to the lowest floor and west, through the door into the room with the Moon shard. There are 4 Large Crystal Apparatus, use the {{ilink|Crystal Flask with Blessed Blue Lava}} [[Crystal Flask with Blessed Blue Lava]] on the tile south of each apparatus to reveal a fallen star glyph. After you have done it for all 4 rune glyphs, you will be granted access to [[The Moonsnow Magnolia]] located south east on -2.

While fighting the boss, some portals around the room will spawn, which need to be stepped on to prevent additional monsters joining the fight. Once the boss is at red health, it will feign death, then reset its [[HP]]. Once it is dead, go south through the teleport into a new area. You will be locked inside a prison. Use the skeleton in the east to obtain a Torch, then use the torch on the north wall. Use the icicle in the south west to obtain an {{ilink|Icicle Chisel}} [[Icicle Chisel]], use the chisel on the wall and you will be free from this icy tomb.

Return to Saraki ({{KW|hi}}, {{KW|moon}}), and the quest will be completed. You will receive a {{ilink|Skewered Fish}} [[Skewered Fish]], which can be used to tame a [[Jaracal]] to get it as a [[Jaracal (Mount)|mount]].
