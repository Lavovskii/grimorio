## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Troll Sabotage Quest}}
===Required Equipment===
*[[Helmet of the Deep]] or [[Depth Galea]]
*[[Boots of Haste]]

===Method===
Find [[Jerom]] north-east of [[Edron]] ([COORD x=33239 y=31760 z=7 | here]), near [[Avar Tar]].

Ask him about "Mission" and he will tell you about the troll who ruined his life.

Go to the other side of the mountain ([COORD x=33236 y=31744 z=7 | here]) and jump into the river, put on your [[Helmet of the Deep]] or [[Depth Galea]] and swim under the mountain.
*'''You must have either helmet equipped in order to enter the mountain cave.'''
[[Image:TrollSabotage_1.png]][[Image:TrollSabotage_2.png]]<br />
You should fall down a floor, swim to the water vortex and you are another floor down. Go out of the water and follow the way up as far as you can go. Walk immediately north from where the last set of stairs take you.
[[Image:TrollSabotage_Map.png]]<br />
you will end up at the top of the mountain, wear your [[BoH|boots of haste]] and run the full distance in order to make the leap.<br />
[[Image:TrollSabotage_3.png]][[Image:TrollSabotage_4.png]]<br />

Go up and you will find [[Chief Grarkharok]], talk to him. 

1. Threaten him with 'kill', 2. Offer him a 'lady', 3. Provide him the name of any female player on your server.
He will give you [[Jerom's Family Necklace]].

Now Go back to [[Jerom]] and ask him about '''mission'''.

'''Note that if you've lost your amulet and you talk to Jerom about your mission he will give you 200gp and will tell you that he got it.'''

If you check your Quest Log, you can read that [[Chief Grarkharok]] is still waiting for a troll lady. Maybe there's another reward if you can help him.

==== Further information ====

*[[Jerom]] will tell you about his dream to open up a '''pet''' store. and about his parent's '''farm'''. he will also tell you about varieties of pets (spider, snake, dog, rat, cat etc.)
*[[Chief Grarkharok]] will tell you about his hobby of throwing '''stones''' at humans, and will also tell you about his love for '''snakes''' and '''frogs''' and will eat any dead frog or snake you will give him.
