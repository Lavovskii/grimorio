== Ability to deal with Marina ==

=== Method ===

[[Marina]], the mermaid from [[Meriana]] will help you to create yarn from spider silk, but first you have to bring her 50 [[honeycomb]]s. You can get them by hunting [[Wasp]]s and [[Bear]]s.

=== Transcripts ===
{{Infobox Transcript|
''Player'': '''silk yarn'''<br>
Marina: Um. You mean, you really want me to touch that gooey spider silk just because you need yarn? Well... do you think that I'm pretty?<br>
''Player'': '''yes'''<br>
Marina: Well, everyone would say that in your position. Do you think that I'm really, absolutely the most stunning being that you have ever seen?<br>
''Player'': '''yes'''<br>
Marina: <giggles> It's funny how easy it is to get humans to say what you want. Now, proving it will be even more fun! ...<br>
Marina: You want me to touch something gooey, so you have to touch something gooey for me too. <giggles> ...<br>
Marina: I love honey and I haven't eaten it in a while, so bring me 50 honeycombs and worship my beauty a little more, then we will see.<br>
''Player'': '''50 honeycombs'''<br>
Marina: Did you bring me the 50 honeycombs I requested and do you absolutely admire my beauty?<br>
''Player'': '''yes'''<br>
Marina: Oh goodie! Thank you! Okay... I guess since my fingers are sticky now anyway, I will help you. From now on, if you bring me 10 [[Spider Silk|pieces of spider silk]], I will create one [[Spool of Yarn|spool of yarn]].
}}
