## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=Marlin Trophy Quest}}

In order to obtain a [[Marlin Trophy]] you need to bring a [[marlin]] to the NPC [[Pemaret]] on [[Cormaya]] Ship. He will be happy to exchange it for you.
