==Required Equipment==
* [[Rope]]

==Method==
* From the [[Edron]] [[depot]], go down the drain in the south-west corner of the castle ([COORD x=33165 y=31828 z=7 | here]).
* There is only one way to go, so follow the cave until you emerge in a grassy area ([COORD x=33129 y=31811 z=7 | here]).
* Go south-west until you find the goblin hill ([COORD x=33107 y=31848 z=7 | here]). Go down the hole, and be prepared to face up to 10 [[Goblin]]s at once if no one has been down there for a while.

* Follow this path to the north-west and go down the hole:

* Follow this path to the hole in the far north-east corner. Go down this hole, and watch out for more goblins and 2 [[wasp]]s:
[[Image:Edron Goblin Quest Map 02.jpg]]

* The reward is in two chests in the throne room to the north:
