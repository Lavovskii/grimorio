## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

== Outfit ==
The [[Druid Outfits]] are available to all [[Premium Account]] players, only the addons are included in this quest.

== Bear Paws (Male) Wolf Paws (Female) ==
[[Image:Outfit Druid Male Addon 1.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit Druid Female Addon 1.gif]]
=== Required Equipment ===
* 50 [[Bear Paw]]s
* 50 [[Wolf Paw]]s

=== Method ===
* All of the items above can be collected before you begin the quest. To save time, you are advised to collect the items before you talk to the NPC.
* Go to Port Hope and see [[Ustan]] (upstairs [COORD x=32576 y=32754 z=7 | here]) and ask him about his "addon".
* He will tell you to bring him 50 [[Bear Paw]]s '''and''' 50 [[Wolf Paw]]s and he will fit them on you.
* Give him the items, and he will give you the paws addon.

== Bear Fur (Male) Wolf Fur (Female) ==
[[Image:Outfit Druid Male Addon 2.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit Druid Female Addon 2.gif]]

=== Required Equipment ===
* Sample of a Blooming [[Griffinclaw]] flower.
* Sample of [[Magic Water|Water]] from a [[hydra]] cave.
* 100 ounces of [[Demon Dust]].
* [[Ceiron's Wolf Tooth Chain]] from [[Orc Fortress]]

=== Method ===
In order to get this addon, you must prove yourself trustworthy to [[Ceiron]] and then [[A Majestic Warwolf]]. However first you must learn the language of wolves.
====Mission 1====
* You must start this quest by going to [[Ab'Dendriel]] and talking to [[Ceiron]] the druid ([COORD x=32669 y=31715 z=7 | here]). Ask him about his ''outfit''. He will tell you that you must earn his respect before you can be given a wolf skin to wear. He will ask you to bring him a sample of a [[Blooming Griffinclaw]], and give you a [[Botanist's Container (Empty)]] to collect it with.
* If you lose your [[Botanist's Container (Empty)]], [[Ceiron]] tells you to buy another one from a representative of [[The Explorer Society]].
* The griffinclaw flower can be found on [[Nargor]] on the 3rd floor to the north-east [COORD x=32024 y=32831 z=4 | here]. The flower is usually [[Dry Griffinclaw|dry]] and of no use to you. It blooms approximately once every month, and when this happens it stays blooming between two [[Server Save]]s. You may need to return many times before you find the flower blooming. When you finally succeed in doing so, [[use]] the container on the flower to receive a [[Griffinclaw Container]]. 
* If you try to collect more than one sample the following message will pop-up in your game window: <code>You just collected a fragile griffinclaw. At least wait for the rest of the plant to recover a bit before gathering more.</code>
* Note that if you already got this addon with your character, you will not be able to interact again with a [[Blooming Griffinclaw]] even if you have a [[Botanist's Container (Empty)]] to collect it.
[[Image:Blooming griffinclaw.PNG]]

====Mission 2====
* The second mission is to collect a sample of ''The purest and cleanest water in tibia''. Ceiron provides you with [[Ceiron's Waterskin]] which you must fill with water from the [[Hydra]] spawn [COORD x=32987 y=32681 z=7 | here] from the most-eastern pond [COORD x=33024 y=32673 z=6 | here]. (Note: You must use the waterskin directly on the "trickle" as using it on the pond will do nothing.)
(Note: When you get the water its from the pond in the very middle of the new [[Hydra]] spawn. when it works you will see the {{Ilink|Ripple Effect}} [[Ripple Effect]].)

====Mission 3====
* The third mission is to bring him "100 ounces of [[Demon Dust]]" to prove that "you're on the right side, against Zathroth".
These can be obtained by purchasing from other players, or by using a [[Blessed Wooden Stake]] on the fresh corpse of a [[Demon]].

====Mission 4====
* The last mission is to find and retrieve [[Ceiron's Wolf Tooth Chain]] lost inside the [[Orc Fortress]]. It is located in a concealed room which is located west of the stairs on the same floor as the [[Orc Fortress Quest]] is found [COORD x=32939 y=31776 z=9 | here]. Since the [[Updates/12.62.10592|patch on February 09, 2021]], you can now buy the item from [[Black Bert]].
* After completing all of Ceiron's tasks, ask him about ''Faolan'' and you'll be able to speak the language of the wolves. Head over to [[Cormaya]], find [[A Majestic Warwolf]]'s Cave (down [COORD x=33364 y=31975 z=7 | here]) and ask her about an "addon".
