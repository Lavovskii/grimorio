== Pen and Sword ==
=== Inkwing ===
Go to [[Carlin]] and meet [[Phillip]] ([COORD x=32369 y=31764 z=7 | here]). Talk to him about his {{KW|book}} and he will tell you a monster came out of it and is now roaming around.

He asks if you can take care of it, and once you accept the challenge, he informs you the monster left some traces of ink which you can use to track it.

Following the ink will lead you to [[Carlin]] surroundings, around [COORD x=32510 y=31744 z=7 | here]. Go down, follow the corridors until the end, and step on a ink pool on the floor.

Defeat [[Inkwing]] and report back to [[Phillip]] by talking about '''book'''. He will tell you he is still concerned that more of these monsters would be around, so he suggests that you talk to [[Wyrdin]] in [[Edron]] to investigate this further.

=== Wyrdin ===
Head to the [[Noodles Academy of Modern Magic]] in [[Edron]] and meet [[Wyrdin]]. Tell him about the {{KW|book}} and he will tell you the same situation is also happening in the academy and the castle of Edron.

He tells you some book pages are working as portals to bring more and more of these creatures, and tells you to go to the basements to find three of these pages to break the portal spell.

Go three floors down and enter the quest door to the east. Be ready to face [[Cinder Wyrmling]]s and [[Shell Drake]]s. In their hunting area you will find the pages hidden in the following objects and locations:
* {{ilink|Illuminated Book Page (A)}} [[Illuminated Book Page (A)]]: [COORD x=33364 y=31812 z=10 | here] in a dead tree
* {{ilink|Illuminated Book Page (B)}} [[Illuminated Book Page (B)]]: [COORD x=33382 y=31835 z=10 | here] in a tree stump
* {{ilink|Illuminated Book Page (C)}} [[Illuminated Book Page (C)]]: [COORD x=33432 y=31838 z=10 | here] in some mushrooms

=== Chapter I ===
Find and talk to [[Quill (NPC)|Quill]], and you will learn your familiar was kidnapped by the crusaders, half knight and half lion creatures, and that their leader, [[Sir Leonard]], is holding your friend captive.

Quill advises you to get a {{ilink|Dip Pen}} [[Dip Pen]] to write down your thoughts or needs on the parchment floor. To do so, you need to harvest a pen out of parchment (by using it), fill it with ink from ink pools or fountains, then write down on '''any blank space''' on the parchment.

Get your {{KW|mission}} from Quill, then use your {{ilink|Dip Pen with Ink}} [[Dip Pen with Ink]] on a blank space to obtain a {{ilink|Painted Torch}} [[Painted Torch]]. Use the torch on the stylized fire flower tile to burn a hole and go downstairs.

:'''''Note:''' The missions provided by Quill must be done in different chapters depending on your [[vocation]]. If your vocation is not mentioned in a chapter, just assume you do not need to do anything in that chapter and skip to the next one.''

=== Chapter III ===
:'''''Note:''' In this chapter you can access a treasure room to reach the east area in a shorter route. To do so, you must complete the mission provided by [[Quill]]. Alternatively, you can access the east area of this chapter through the northwest corridor.''

The enemies here are [[Lion Hydra]]s and [[Bluebeak]]s. The leader of the crusaders is still not in this chapter, so your goal here is essentially advance to the next chapter by using the boat located to the east. However, you can only access it if you have a {{ilink|Boat Ticket}} [[Boat Ticket]]. To do that, use your {{ilink|Dip Pen with Ink}} [[Dip Pen with Ink]] close to the boat (around [COORD x=32593 y=32568 z=10 | here]), and you will obtain the ticket.

Additionally, the following vocations can progress with [[Quill]]'s mission in this Chapter:
* [[Knight]]s can defeat 30 [[Lion Hydra]]s
* [[Monk]]s need to gain more insight on the nature of life and death.
** This knowledge is granted by [[Death (NPC)]], which is located [COORD x=32572 y=32574 z=11 | here]
** Follow the bold keywords in the dialogue, and when he asks you for questions, talk to him about {{KW|loss}} and {{KW|acceptance}}

=== Chapter IV ===
The final chapter, and you finally find [[Crusader]]s and [[Headwalker]]s. If you are a [[Sorcerer]], you finish your mission from [[Quill]] here:
* [[Sorcerer]]s need to learn a magical spell to open magically sealed doors
** to learn the spell you need to light up candles in three locations, using your {{ilink|Painted Torch}} [[Painted Torch]]: [COORD x=32586 y=32517 z=11 | here], [COORD x=32645 y=32520 z=11 | here], and [COORD x=32616 y=32557 z=11 | here]
** '''Warning!''' When lighting up candles you will receive 44.15% of your current [[HP]] as damage

=== Familiar Saving ===
Walk through Chapter IV, and enter the teleport [COORD x=32631 y=32560 z=11 | here]. 

(Required for [[Paladin]]) You need to have the quest items from earlier steps in you possession, e.g. [[Paladin]] needs {{ilink|The Sacred Writings of Getema}} [[The Sacred Writings of Getema]], otherwise you won't be teleported to the special room after the fight. (possibly a bug and could be fixed later)

This will lead to [[Sir Leonard]]'s boss fight.

The fight should be straightforward, without any special mechanics. Defeat the boss and leave the room to the south.

Once you leave the boss room, you will find yourself in a new room where your familiar is locked in. Here you will need to use a different item on the sealed door depending on your [[vocation]]:
* [[Knight]]s: {{ilink|Ink-Coated Sword}} [[Ink-Coated Sword]]
* [[Paladin]]s: {{ilink|Painted Bow}} [[Painted Bow]] (requires to have 3 {{ilink|Painted Arrow}} [[Painted Arrow]] in the backpack as well)
* [[Druid]]s: {{ilink|Deer Fur Brush}} [[Deer Fur Brush]]
* [[Sorcerer]]s: {{ilink|Phoenix Feather Quill}} [[Phoenix Feather Quill]]
* [[Monk]]s: {{ilink|Dip Pen with Ink}} [[Dip Pen with Ink]]

There is a pen sticking out the ground in the room, as well as ink and space to write on - you can get some of the above items in the room itself.

Talk to your familiar, follow the dialogue by clicking the bold keywords, and you will be able to use [[Summon Familiar]] spells after {{KW|saving}} your buddy. Pick up the familiar figurine from the chest. Leave the room by clicking on the door and then enter a teleporter on the floor. You will have to go back all the way through the chapters and exit the [[Book World|book world]] to [[Edron]].

== Reward ==
Return to [[Wyrdin]] to report a last time and finish the quest. As a reward you will receive two parts of the [[Painting of Thais]], the base [[Illuminator Outfits|Illuminator Outfit]] and [[File:Achievement_Grade_Symbol.gif]] [[Power of Words]] achievement.
* {{Ilink|Illuminator Outfits|Outfit Illuminator Male}} [[Illuminator Outfits|Illuminator Outfit - Male]]
* {{Ilink|Illuminator Outfits|Outfit Illuminator Female}} [[Illuminator Outfits|Illuminator Outfit - Female]]

Additionally, you can now use the shortcuts to different chapters in the [[Book World]].
