## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=To Appease the Mighty Quest}}

'''Warning! You CANNOT do this quest if you have started or completed either [[Blue Djinn Quest|Blue]] or [[Green Djinn Quest]]!'''

===Method===
==== Djinn Greeting ====
Before you begin, talk to [[Melchior]] to be able to speak with the Djinn. He is a wandering NPC that lives in [[Ankrahmun]]. Ask him about {{KW|greeting}}.<br />
It may take a while to find [[Melchior]] but he cannot walk outside the shaded area and may not be too far from where he starts.<br />
[[File:Melchior's_boundaries.png|300px]]

==== Visit The Djinn ====
Talk to [[Kazzan]] in [[Darashia]] ([COORD x=33231 y=32389 z=6 | in the castle, one floor up]) and ask about a {{KW|mission}}. He will tell you to offer an appeasement treaty to the Djinn races.

You may talk to the Djinn in any order, none of them will accept.

Head to either the [[Blue Djinn Fortress]] or the [[Green Djinn Fortress]]. Greet the Djinn NPC ([[Umar]] in blue, [[Ubaid]] in green) by saying {{KW|DJANNI'HAH}}. Ask about a {{KW|mission}}. Say {{KW|Kazzan}} sent you, and say he wants an {{KW|appeasement}}.

After doing this, head to the other Djinn Fortress and say the same to the Djinn NPC there.

==== Shorter Alternative ====
Alternatively you can go to [[Umar]] in the [[Blue Djinn Fortress]] and offer {{KW|peace}}. He will agree and you won't have to visit the green Djinn fortress.

==== Reward ====
Return to [[Kazzan]] to collect your reward.
