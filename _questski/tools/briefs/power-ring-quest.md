## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Power Ring Quest}}
==Required Equipment==
* [[Rope]]

==Method==
* On [[Femor Hills]], follow along the South side of the river that flows into the mountain (just north of the [[Magic Carpet]] [COORD x=32575 y=31797 z=7 | here]), and rope up one level.

* Head East into the mountain, and go down the ladder to the north:
[[Image:Power Ring Quest Map 01.jpg]]

* Follow the path to the north-east, and go down the hole.  There may be a rotworm here, so be prepared.
[[Image:Power Ring Quest Map 02.jpg]]

* Here you will find up to 11 [[Goblin]]s and four [[Wolf|Wolves]] (which will attack you the moment you enter the room), and there will be [[Fire Field]]s scattered throughout the room. Go to the south-west and drop down one more level:
[[Image:Power Ring Quest Map 03.jpg]]

* Here will be up to 11 [[Goblin]]s (again, they will all attack you the moment you enter the room), and a pen to the south with up to five [[Pig]]s in it. The Quest boxes are on the north end of this room, next to the [[Beer Cask]]s:
[[Image:Power Ring Quest Map 04.jpg]]
