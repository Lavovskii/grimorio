## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Griffin Shield Quest}}

This quest is located through the [[Maze of Lost Souls|MoLS]] (Maze of Lost Souls). Exploring this place without a map can be very dangerous, as you may encounter [[Giant Spider]]s, [[Dragon Lord]]s and more!

If you want to do this quest don't forget to have a [[shovel]] in your backpack and some [[food]] because the way to the quest and back is too long.

Well, so let's go, it begins in a cave near [[Northport]] [COORD x=32488 y=31610 z=7 | here], a small village near [[Carlin]].
Use the shovel to open a hole [COORD x=32512 y=31624 z=7 | here] and get into it.

Follow the blue line until the next hole.

[[Image:Route Demona.png]]

Keep following the blue line and you'll find a small forest inside the cave. In the red spot, behind the middle tree, there is a hole and inside that hole there is a switch that you must turn right to open the entrance to the [[Maze of Lost Souls]]. A sign next to the switch says:<br />
''You read: Greetings fellow brother!''<br />
''Turn the switch to the right and follow the blue sunshine to get to Demona.'' ''You know the rest of the way.''<br />
''Zhandramon''<br />
Note: if it is already turned right, then turn it left and right again.

[[Image:Route Demona (2).png]]

Get out the hole and follow this way to get to the entrance of the [[Maze of Lost Souls]]:

[[Image:Route Demona (3).png]]

Now you are inside of the [[Maze of Lost Souls]] and it is suggested you follow the next map step by step or you'll face [[Dragon]]s, [[Dragon Lord]]s and [[Giant Spider]]s. In this path you'll face all the creatures in the list above ([[Rat]]s ~ [[Orc Leader]]s). Keep in mind: don't get out of the path if you can't face dangerous creatures.

Ok, here you are in the final room. There are 2 [[Dwarf Guard]]s, 2 [[Minotaur Guard]]s, 2 [[Stone Golem]]s and 3 [[Elf Arcanist]]s here. Kill them and take the reward ([[Griffin Shield]], [[Dwarven Axe]] and [[Obsidian Lance]]) that is inside two [[Pile of Bones (Quest)|piles of bones]] and a dead body. The final room:

[[Image:Mols5.jpg]]

If your level is higher than 60 you can even visit Demona to do [[Double SD Quest]] once you are at its gates. Anyhow you'll have to get out of [[Maze of Lost Souls]], so you'll have to follow the path in the next map. Note: there are other ways to get out of [[Maze of Lost Souls]], but this one is the safest and shortest one.
