## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler| name=Hunter Outfits Quest}}
=== Outfit ===
* The [[Hunter Outfits]] are available to all players. Only the addons are included in this quest.

=== Hooded Cloak/Winged Tiara ===
[[Image:Outfit Hunter Male Addon 1.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit Hunter Female Addon 2.gif]]

==== Required Equipment ====
* 1 [[Elane's Crossbow|Engraved Crossbow]], or
**Access to deeper [[Cult Cave]], by learning all verses of the [[Hymn]] (from [[Music Sheet]]s))
* 100 Pieces of [[Lizard Leather]]
* 100 Pieces of [[Red Dragon Leather]]
* 5 [[Enchanted Chicken Wing]]s (Trade [[Boots of Haste]])
* 1 [[Piece of Royal Steel]] (Trade [[Crown Armor]])
* 1 [[Piece of Draconian Steel]] (Trade [[Dragon Shield]])
* 1 [[Piece of Hell Steel]] (Trade [[Devil Helmet]])

==== Method ====
All items listed above except the crossbow can be obtained before starting this quest. You are advised to collect these items before you visit the NPC.
* Collect the [[Lizard Leather]]s from [[Lizards]]
* Collect the [[Red Dragon Leather]]s from [[Dragon Lord]]s
* Collect the [[Enchanted Chicken Wing]]s, they can be traded for [[Boots Of Haste]] at [[Haroun]] and [[Yaman]].
* Obtain the three metals by trading items to [[A Sweaty Cyclops]] in [[Ab'Dendriel]]
* Obtain [[Elane's Crossbow]] from the [[Liberty Bay]] deeper cult quest area. You need to have learned all 4 verses of the hymn '''and''' have the mission to get the crossbow from Elane, in order to reach the quest room.
** Verses of the hymn are very rare drops from each cultist. [[Novice of the Cult|Novices]] drop the first verse, [[Acolyte of the Cult|acolyte]]s drop the second, [[Adept of the Cult|adept]]s the third and [[Enlightened of the Cult|enlightened]]s drop the fourth. You can buy the music sheets instead of hunting them.

==== Obtaining [[Elane's Crossbow]] ====

First you must talk to [[Elane]] in [[Thais]]' Paladins Guild and accept the '''task''' to obtain the items and the crossbow so you'll be able to open the sealed door. You must have learned all the four verses of the hymn from [[Music Sheet]]s.

(Consider on all images, the '''flag''' icon is where you start and the '''star''' icon is where you must go)

[[Image:Elane Crossbow Way1.GIF]]<br>
First you must go to the [[cult cave (liberty bay)|hidden cult cave]], north from [[Liberty Bay]].

[[Image:Elane Crossbow Way2.GIF]]<br>
After you enter, follow the path as it's shown on the mini map. Be prepared to face some [[Tarantula]]s, several [[Novice of the Cult]] and [[Witch|Witches]].

[[Image:Elane Crossbow Way3.GIF]]<br>
Continue through following the map and you'll face more cults, Bonelords and witches. At the end of the room you'll find a piano. If you have learned all the verses of the hymn, you can use the piano to teleport to the other side of the room.

[[Image:Elane Crossbow Way4.GIF]]<br>
In this room you will have to face [[Novice of the Cult]], [[Acolyte of the Cult]], and [[Dark Monk]]s. Area runes such as [[Thunderstorm Rune|Thunderstorms]] and [[GFB]]s are useful here. Follow the path to get to the chest. Note that you must pass by a sealed door, and if you didn't receive the task from [[Elane]] you won't be able to open it.

==== Delivering the items ====

Go back to [[Elane]] and give her her '''crossbow'''. Then proceed to deliver the '''leathers''', '''enchanted chicken wings''' and '''steel''' pieces. After delivering all items you will receive your addon.

=== Sniper Gloves ===
[[Image:Outfit Hunter Male Addon 2.gif]]
[[Image:Outfit Hunter Female Addon 1.gif]]

==== Required Equipment ====
* 1 pair of [[Sniper Gloves]]

==== Method ====
* Kill [[Hunter]]s until you loot a pair of [[Sniper Gloves]].
* Take the sniper gloves to [[Elane]] in [[Thais]] or to [[Legola]] in [[Carlin]] and you'll receive the addon.
