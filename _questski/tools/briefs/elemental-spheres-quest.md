## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Elemental Spheres Quest}}
=== Requirements ===
* One character of each [[vocation]] level 80+: [[Sorcerer]], [[Druid]], [[Knight]], [[Paladin]], [[Monk]].
* 20 Enchanted Gems, depending on vocation:
* [[Sorcerer]]: 20 {{Ilink|Small Enchanted Ruby}} [[Small Enchanted Ruby|Small Enchanted Rubies]]
* [[Druid]]: 20 {{Ilink|Small Enchanted Emerald}} [[Small Enchanted Emerald]]s
* [[Knight]]: 20 {{Ilink|Small Enchanted Amethyst}} [[Small Enchanted Amethyst]]s
* [[Paladin]]: 20 {{Ilink|Small Enchanted Sapphire}} [[Small Enchanted Sapphire]]s
* [[Monk]]: 5 {{Ilink|Small Enchanted Emerald}} [[Small Enchanted Emerald]]s, 5 {{Ilink|Small Enchanted Amethyst}} [[Small Enchanted Amethyst]]s, 5 {{Ilink|Small Enchanted Sapphire}} [[Small Enchanted Sapphire]]s, 5 {{Ilink|Small Enchanted Ruby}} [[Small Enchanted Ruby|Small Enchanted Rubies]].

'''Important Note''': When beginning the quest, your character must not have any of the elemental soil or corresponding boss items in their inventory (Glimmering Soil, Natural Soil, Energy Soil, Iced Soil, Spirited Soil, or their related boss drops). If any of these items are present, Arkulius will refuse to proceed and claim they are "fake".

=== The Elemental Sphere ===
Once you have collected the 20 enchanted gems corresponding to your vocation, proceed to speak with [[Arkulius]]. He can be found beside the frozen Alverus on the top floor of the south-eastern Ivory Tower in Edron: [COORD x=33280 y=31845 z=3 | here].

When talking to him, ask about:
* {{KW|Alverus}}
* {{KW|Shrines}}
* {{KW|Sphere}}
* {{KW|Mission}}

Confirm with {{KW|Yes}} to begin the quest.

After receiving the mission, head to the four elemental shrines located on the lowest floor of the central tower in the Ivory Tower: [COORD x=33267 y=31835 z=10 | here].

=== {{Ilink|Mysterious Machine}} Activating the Machine ===
To the north of the room, you will find a [[Mysterious Machine]].<br />
1. Be sure to turn the machine <span style="color:red;"><b>OFF</b></span>.<br />
2. Each player must use all 20 enchanted gems on the machine individually (one at a time per character).<br />
3. Once all 20 gems have been used, turn the machine <span style="color:green;"><b>ON</b></span>.<br />

If done correctly you will be teleported to your vocation’s elemental realm.

''Note'': If you attempt to use more than 20 gems, the following message will appear:
{{Message|type=sound|Turn on the machine now!!}}

[[File:Elemental Spheres Quest - Machine.png|500px]]<br />
''Note: After entering your elemental realm, you may leave at any time. However, re-entry will require another set of 20 enchanted gems.''

The creatures in each realm are:

* [[Sorcerer]]s: [[Blazing Fire Elemental]]s & [[Blistering Fire Elemental]]s.
* [[Druid]]s: [[Jagged Earth Elemental]]s & [[Muddy Earth Elemental]]s.
* [[Knight]]s: [[Charged Energy Elemental]]s & [[Overcharged Energy Elemental]]s.
* [[Paladin]]s: [[Slick Water Elemental]]s & [[Roaring Water Elemental]].
* [[Monk]]s: [[Psychic Spirit Elemental]]s [[Flickering Spirit Elemental]]s.

In the realm you need to kill the monsters until you have 8 soils:

* [[Sorcerer]]s: [[Glimmering Soil]] {{Ilink|Glimmering Soil}}
* [[Druid]]s: [[Natural Soil]] {{Ilink|Natural Soil}}
* [[Knight]]s: [[Energy Soil]] {{Ilink|Energy Soil}}
* [[Paladin]]s: [[Iced Soil]] {{Ilink|Iced Soil}}
* [[Monk]]s: [[Spirited Soil]] {{Ilink|Spirited Soil}}

After getting the soils, head to the part of your realm that has no elementals in it, but have a circle formed by 8 pillars (varies for each vocation/realm). 

[[File:Elemental Spheres Quest - Pillar.png|center]]

[[File:Elemental Spheres Quest Fire Overlord (Sorcerer)|thumb|300px]]
Place each soil on the floor beside each of the 8 holes, ice cubes, mushrooms or lava hole. After the soils are at the side of the place you will use them, lure 2 monsters next to the circle and kill both '''out of it'''. Then you can quickly activate your 8 soils by using them on the 8 marked places, followed by throwing 1 single body of a slain elemental into the circle. This will summon the overlord of your element.

''Attention'': Upon using the first soil, you will have 1 minute and 40 seconds to kill the boss. If you kill it before this time ends, you can throw another dead body inside the circle, making another boss appear. By repeating this procedure, it is possible to spawn more bosses during this 1 minute and 40 seconds. Keep in mind you cannot spawn more than one boss at a time. It is important to emphasize that the 1 minute and 40 seconds start to count by the moment you use the first soil, so if you take long to use the other soils you will waste precious time.

When the boss spawns, kill it and he will drop one of the following items:

* [[Sorcerer]]s: [[Fire Overlord]] drops [[Eternal Flames]] {{Ilink|Eternal Flames}}
* [[Druid]]s: [[Earth Overlord]] drops [[Mother Soil]] {{Ilink|Mother Soil}}
* [[Knight]]s: [[Energy Overlord]] drops [[Pure Energy]] {{Ilink|Pure Energy}}
* [[Paladin]]s: [[Ice Overlord]] drops [[Flawless Ice Crystal]] {{Ilink|Flawless Ice Crystal}}
* [[Monk]]s: [[Spirit Overlord]] drops [[Reality Splinter]] {{Ilink|Reality Splinter}}

'''Note''': Only the player that deals most damage to the boss will complete the mission. If you are not the killer of the boss, it will count as if you have not completed the mission given to you by [[Arkulius]], and he will reject you when performing the next step.

After you have the boss item, go to the exit of the realm and go through the teleport. Report back to [[Arkulius]]. You will now be ready to do the final mission with the other four [[vocation]]s.

===Lord of the Elements===

Before you do the final mission, it is recommended that each player has their own [[Obsidian Knife]].
[[Image:Killing loe strategy.jpg|thumb|right|Lord of elements]]
* Go to the Edron Ivory Tower, on the lowest floor of the middle tower. There will be 4 [[Strange Carving]]s on the floor, similar to the [[Desert Dungeon Quest|Desert Quest]]. A player of each [[vocation]] must stand at their shrine. ([[Fire]] - [[Sorcerer]]s, [[Earth]] - [[Druid]]s, [[Ice]] - [[Paladin]]s and [[Energy]] - [[Knights]]). Players need to have their boss items, or they will be unable to teleport.
* Once you hit the switch, you will spawn into an "x" shaped room and will need to follow the signs for each [[vocation]] (there are no monsters here as of yet).
* Walk to the end of your [[vocation]]s pathway, where each player must use their boss item on the machine at the end of the hallway. '''Do not go into the teleport. '''
* Walk back to the middle room to meet with the team.
* Each player must now stand on one of the 4 tiles in the middle room with switches next to them. Hit the switches at the same time and the '''[[Lord of the Elements]]'''<span style="line-height: 21px;">''' '''</span>will spawn. The walls will block you in to the middle room only. You have 10 minutes from now on until you get teleported out.
* Kill the boss using [[Ice Strike|Ice]]/[[Energy Strike]], [[Energy Wave]] and [[Shiver Arrow]]s and then use your [[Obsidian Knife]] on his [[Overlord's Ashes|ashes]].
** '''Note''': After the Lord of the Elements is dead you only have 10 seconds to loot it or the body will close. After that it is possible to skin.
** '''Note''': All players will get the [[Neutral Matter|neutral matter]].
* Exit through the teleport and report back to [[Arkulius]] to receive your reward.
* You will be able to enter the room again with your team after 10 minutes have passed.
