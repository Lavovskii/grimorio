==Required equipment==
None, a [[Shields|shield]] is recommended.
'''Note:''' To take the one on the premium side, 3 [[box]]es are needed.

==Method==
*Head to the [[Bananapalm]] located in [COORD x=32172 y=32169 z=7 | East] [[Rookgaard]], or north-west premium Rookgaard.
*Alternatively, go to the premium side and up the wolf hill, [COORD x=31988 y=32199 z=7 | here]. Take the 3 boxes to the flat part and jump up to the [[Bananapalm]] there.
*"Use" the [[Bananapalm]] to get the [[Banana]].
*Head to [[Willie]] in the town ([COORD x=32067 y=32205 z=7 | here]) and trade the '[[Banana]]' for a [[Studded Shield]].
