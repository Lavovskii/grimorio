## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=Ancient Aucar Outfits Quest}}

== Outfit == 

Complete the '''Galthen and the City of Gold''' mission of the [[Adventures of Galthen Quest]] by defeating the boss [[Ahau]]. The first time you kill the boss you'll loot [[The Living Idol of Tukh]] {{Ilink|The_Living_Idol_of_Tukh}}. Use the Idol to receive your outfit.

== Addon 1 ==

To obtain this addon, you'll first need some Broken Iks equipment, in particular a [[Broken Iks Headpiece]], a [[Broken Iks Cuirass]], some [[Broken Iks Faulds]], and finally [[Broken Iks Sandals]]:
{{Equips_Set
|head      = Broken Iks Headpiece
|torso     = Broken Iks Cuirass
|legs      = Broken Iks Faulds
|feet      = Broken Iks Sandals
}}

'''''Note''''': [[Broken Iks Spear]] and [[Broken Macuahuitl]] are not needed.

You will have to kill the Iks boss [[Ahau]], even if you've already done it. Before pulling the lever, equip the Broken Iks items mentioned above (and keep them equipped). Exactly two minutes after starting the fight, you should get a message informing you that you have received the addon. If the boss dies before that, just wait in the room for the message.

{{Server Message|A challenger worthy of the aucar warrior attire would now bear the right to carry a macuahuitl as well.}}

''Note¹'': The Broken Iks items will be destroyed during this process, so they cannot be reused by other players.<br/>
''Note²'': Only characters wearing the set during the fight will receive the title and the addon.

You will also receive the '''Challenger of the Iks''' [[Cyclopedia#Titles|title]] by doing this.
