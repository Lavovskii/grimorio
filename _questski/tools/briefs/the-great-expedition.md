== Day 1: Worm Hunt ==
Meet [[Proprietor Piers]] and he will tell you woodworms are eating the construction materials. Offer to {{KW|help}} dealing with them.

Next to Piers, and also one floor below you can find different flasks of pesticides. Use the flask to collect it in your backpack, then use it on a woodworm with the same colour.

Repeat the process until you succeed. You'll receive a server message ({{Server Message|SUCCESS! You repelled the worms! A new hunt is about to begin!}}), as well as a server log update.

After succeeding, return to Piers and cast your vote for the first reward, as well as 5 Event Points.
* Option {{KW|A}}: 40% chance for double Bestiary progress
* Option {{KW|B}}: Double respawn rate

For a game world to succeed and advance to Day 2, a total of 50 votes must be cast, and at least 5,000,000 gold coins must be donated.

== Day 2: Whack-A-Mole ==
Meet [[Proprietor Piers]] and he will tell you moles are popping up everywhere and destroying their resources. Offer to {{KW|help}} dealing with them.

Next to Piers, and also in other locations on surface, you can find mole hammers. Use them to collect it in your backpack, then use it on moleholes to destroy them.

Repeat the process until you succeed. You'll receive a server message ({{Server Message|SUCCESS! The moles have been driven off!}}), as well as a server log update.

After succeeding, return to Piers and cast your vote for the second reward, as well as 5 Event Points.
* Option {{KW|A}}: +5% experience
* Option {{KW|B}}: +5% critical rate

For a game world to succeed and advance to Day 3, a total of 50 votes must be cast, and at least 5,000,000 gold coins must be donated.

== Day 3: Sauerkraut! ==
Meet [[Proprietor Piers]] and he will tell you all sauerkraut needs to be moved to Estrela. Offer to {{KW|help}} dealing with them.

Go downstairs, then walk southwest, and you'll find some [[Sauerkraut Barrel]]s. Push them to the centre but coordinate with other players so there are 4 barrels in the centre at the same time.

If done correctly, you'll receive a server message, as well as a server log update.

After succeeding, return to Piers and cast your vote for the third reward, as well as 5 Event Points.
* Option {{KW|A}}: +5% life leech
* Option {{KW|B}}: +3% mana leech

For a game world to succeed and advance to Day 4, a total of 50 votes must be cast, and at least 5,000,000 gold coins must be donated.

== Day 4: Express Delivery ==
Meet [[Proprietor Piers]] and he will tell you to speed up the deliveries with a new catapult system. Offer to {{KW|help}} dealing with them.

Stay in the southern area next to [[Proprietor Piers]] until the event begins with the message {{Server Message|CATAPULT DELIVERY SYSTEM IS STARTING!}} . The event cooldown is '''2 minutes'''.

Now you have '''50 seconds''' to deliver at least '''7 boxes''' to the box area on the other side of the wall, north of  [[Proprietor Piers]] .

To deliver a box, you must stand on the red ''throwing target'' circles and wait until you transform into a box. Then, run in box form to the box area (green area in the image), where you must step.
[[File:Event-The Great Expedition wiki.png|none|thumb|450x450px]]
If done correctly, you'll receive a server message {{Server Message|EXCELLENT PERFORMANCE!}} , as well as a server log update.

After succeeding, return to Piers and cast your vote for the fourth reward, as well as 5 Event Points.
* Option {{KW|A}}: +20% XP for Bounty Tasks
* Option {{KW|B}}: +10% Shared XP Bonus

For a game world to succeed and advance to Day 5, a total of 50 votes must be cast, and at least 5,000,000 gold coins must be donated.

== Day 5: Pull Your Weight ==
Meet [[Proprietor Piers]] and he will tell you to move the heavy stuff cluttering up the wharf. Offer to {{KW|help}} dealing with it.

Head downstairs, where you'll find the wharf in the '''northeast''' section. To complete this part of the event, a minimum of '''4 players''' is required.

Each player must stand in one of the spots next to the wooden fence and pull the central rope (click “'''Use'''”).
[[File:Event-The Great Expedition day5.png|alt=The Great Expedition Day 5|none|thumb|793x793px]]
To move the cargo successfully, at least 4 players must pull the rope within a '''5-second''' window, starting from the moment the first player begins pulling.

You will need to repeat this process until the cargo reaches the final platform, where it will disappear.
[[File:Event-The Great Expedition day5 2.png|alt=The Great Expedition Day5 2|none|thumb|793x793px]]
If done correctly, you'll receive a server message {{Server Message|EXCELLENT! NEXT DELIVERY!}} , as well as a server log update.

After succeeding, return to Piers and cast your vote for the fifth reward, as well as 5 Event Points.
* Option {{KW|A}}: +10% XP
* Option {{KW|B}}: +15% Loot

For a game world to succeed and advance to Day 6, a total of 50 votes must be cast, and at least 5,000,000 gold coins must be donated.

== Day 6: Connections ==
Meet [[Proprietor Piers]] and he will tell you to get proper illumination aboard the ship by using an abundant source. Offer to {{KW|help}} dealing with it.

Head downstairs, where you'll find the power room in the '''northwest''' section.

To complete this part of the event, you must '''route the electricity''' from the source point next to the east wall of the room (Energizer) to the target in the middle of the room (Generator). To do this, you will need to form a '''human chain''' with several players, leaving a maximum of '''3 sqm''' between each player.

[[File:The Great Expedition Day6 2.png|alt=The Great Expedition Day 6|none|thumb|781x781px]]
The power can be transmitted through a chain of '''up to 12 players (5 recommended)''', also being viable for solo players using high speed characters.

'''In order to succeed, you have to be standing on the iron floor near the generator in skeleton form when the blue glow appears.'''[[File:Event-The Great Expedition Day6 3.png|alt=The Great Expedition Day6 3|none|thumb]]
'''Note:''' You can run as a skeleton to the Power-hungry Ship-generator, and do it with less players.

If done correctly, you'll receive a server message {{Server Message|SUCCESS! Charge Delivered!}} , as well as a server log update.

After succeeding, return to Piers and cast your vote for the sixth reward, as well as 5 Event Points.
* Option {{KW|A}}: +20% XP for Bounty Tasks
* Option {{KW|B}}: +10% Shared XP Bonus

For a game world to succeed and advance to Day 7, a total of 50 votes must be cast, and at least 5,000,000 gold coins must be donated.

== Day 7: Fire! ==
Meet [[Proprietor Piers]] and he will tell you the wharf is on fire!. Offer to {{KW|help}} dealing with it.

After the message {{Server Message|FIRE! Extinguish the flames!}} pop up, all around [[Proprietor Piers]] area, spots with ashes will appear'''.''' The event cooldown is '''3 minutes'''.

Now you have another '''3 minutes''' to put out at least '''30 fires''', using a bucket of water that you will find south of [[Proprietor Piers]].

Keep in mind that the fire spots can change between three states: ashes, glowing ashes, and burning fire. '''Only the last two states can be extinguished with the bucket of water.'''
[[File:Event-The Great Expedition Day7 2.png|alt=The Great Expedition Day 7|none|thumb|277x277px]]
'''Note:''' You can carry multiple buckets of water with you to save travel time, but keep in mind that they will disappear from your inventory if they are not used within 2.5 minutes.

If done correctly, you'll receive a server message {{Server Message|SUCCESS! The fires are under control for now!}} , as well as a server log update.

After succeeding, return to Piers and cast your vote for the seventh reward, as well as 5 Event Points.
* Option {{KW|A}}: +15% Loot
* Option {{KW|B}}: +10% XP

For a game world to succeed, a total of 50 votes must be cast, and at least 5,000,000 gold coins must be donated.
