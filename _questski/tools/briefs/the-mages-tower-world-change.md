## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=The Mage's Tower World Change}}

==Legend==
South-west of [[Zao Steppe]], a vicious [[Raging Mage]] has created a dimensional portal on top of a tower in order to access the energy buzzing there. The wizard himself is keeping the dimensional rift open. So as long as he is alive you are able to enter Another Dimension where you will encounter [[Yielothax]], strange and tough creatures with rare [[Loot Statistics:Yielothax|loot]]. Also, you might come across the [[Uniwheel]], a very specific [[Mount]].
Killing '''yielothax''' will also weaken the '''raging mage''' and if a certain number of them has been slain, the '''raging mage''' can be defeated and you can get your hands on his unique loot. However, the dimensional rift will break down soon after killing the mage and players will have to leave the dimension until the raging mage will return and restore the portal.

== Requirements ==
* You must have completed the mission 2 of [[The New Frontier Quest]] to access the Mage's Tower.

==Method==

First, ask a [[Guide NPCs|guide]] about the '''Mage Tower'''. If the '''Raging Mage''' is at his tower, you can proceed. Be aware that the dimensional portal will open at a random time after the [[Server Save]], so you have to check with a guide or at the tower.

Travel to the [[Zao Steppe]] and go to the ramp [COORD x=33133 y=31511 z=7 | here]. Climb it until you reach the top level, where you will meet [[Death Blob]]s on each of the levels except the top where you will meet the mage and find the portals.

The [[Energized Raging Mage]] will be on the top floor. While he can be killed, he does not drop loot and will resurrect '''immediately''', unless his power is weakened. You can ignore him and go through the portal to [[Yielothax Dimension|another dimension]] where you will encounter [[Yielothax]]es and may find a [[Uniwheel (Mount)|Uniwheel]] to tame. In order for the [[Raging Mage]] to spawn, your [[Game Worlds|world]] will have to kill 2000 [[Yielothax]]es. Then, the [[Energized Raging Mage]] will be weakened and, when killed, will resurrect once more as the weaker [[Raging Mage]]. Once killed, the portal to another dimension will become destabilized and will collapse in five minutes, until the next [[Server Save]].

== Details ==
* In order to be able to fight with the [[Raging Mage]], you need to have killed at least 250 [[Yielothax]]es.
** If you did not kill the required 250 [[Yielothax]]es, you will get a message when trying to enter the tower while the [[Raging Mage]] has spawned: {{Server Message|You have not proven worthy in the dimensional struggle against the yielothax.}}
* After you kill the [[Energized Raging Mage]] and he changes into the [[Raging Mage]], a message appears: {{Sound|GNAAAAAHRRRG!! WHAT? WHAT DID YOU DO TO ME!! I... I feel the energies crawling away... from me... DIE!!!!}}
* Once the [[Raging Mage]] is dead, another message appears: {{Sound|I WILL RETURN!! My death will just be a door to await my homecoming, my physical hull will be... my... argh...}}
* When hitting the [[Raging Mage]], the 250 [[Yielothax]] kills are reset, and players will need to kill them again for the next access. If a player with access fails to hit the [[Raging Mage]], their kills are kept and they will still have access the next time it spawns.

== History ==
