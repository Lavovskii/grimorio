## SPOILER PAGE (walkthrough source)

{{Spoiler|name=An Interest In Botany Quest}}

=== Required Equipment ===
* A [[Fire Bug]] or a [[Melee]] Weapon able to cut through [[Spider Web]]s.
* [[Obsidian Knife]]
* [[Rope]], or equivalent
* [[Shovel]], or equivalent
* [[Levitate]], 6 [[Parcel]]s or equivalent.

=== Method ===

:''Before even attempting it, please note that '''this mission is not required to unlock the potion store'''. The potion store will be unlocked by completing [[The New Frontier Quest]] only.''

Visit [[Rabaz]] in [[Farmine]] and ask him for a '''mission'''. He needs you to collect samples from rare plant specimen in [[Zao]]. Go to the storage room to the west and receive the [[Botany Almanach]]. Make sure you have the [[Botany Almanach]] in your inventory before you continue.

Leave [[Farmine]] to the [[Zao Steppes]]. Find the small north-western [[Lancer Beetle Cave]] and in it, the [[Giant Dreadcoil]]. It's about half way through the cave, [COORD x=33132 y=31396 z=8 | here]. When you find it, use your [[Obsidian Knife]] on it to obtain a sample. The samples do not exist as an item for you to confirm, so watch for the orange text that appears above your character.

<gallery mode="nolines">
File:An Interest In Botany Quest Map 1.png|[[Lancer Beetle Cave]] entrance.
File:An Interest In Botany Quest Dreadcoil.png|[[Giant Dreadcoil]].
</gallery>

Now you must find the second plant, a [[Giant Verminous]]. Go to the [[Zao Wailing Widow Cave]], located slightly east of the [[Zao Orc Land]] ([COORD x=33223 y=31397 z=7 | here]).
Inside it, go north-east to find [[Spider Web]]s. Use your [[Fire Bug]] or a melee weapon on the spider webs to cut through them. Now go north and follow the path north-west and then north-east to find a large pit. On the edge there is a [[Giant Verminous]]. Use your [[Obsidian Knife]] on this plant and exit the cave. Note that there is a [[Giant Dreadcoil]] very close to the giant verminous. You cannot use this dreadcoil.

Now go back to [[Rabaz]] and turn in your '''mission''' to claim your [[Sedge Hat]].
